Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information BookMCQ handles when you browse learning materials, use quizzes, adjust cookie preferences, or contact us.

Last updated: 28 November 2026

1. About this policy

BookMCQ provides educational explanations, topic and chapter resources, practice activities, quizzes, and related study tools. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you visit www.bookmcq.com or use its features.

Some services are provided by third-party platforms. Their handling of information is also subject to their own privacy terms and settings. This policy should be read together with the choices shown in the cookie banner.

2. Information BookMCQ receives

Information you submit

When you use the Contact Us form, BookMCQ asks for your name, email address, message category, and message. The submitted information is sent to the site's database service so that the message or book request can be received and answered. The form also records a submission timestamp and an internal status value used to manage incoming messages.

Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in a contact message.

Information stored in your browser

BookMCQ uses browser storage for limited site functions. The cookie preference setting is saved in local storage under the key bookmcqCookieConsentV1. It records whether analytics and advertising were accepted or rejected and an expiry timestamp. The preference is set to expire after 180 days. The quiz page may also use local storage entries with the bookmcq_ prefix to temporarily cache learning or quiz data; the quiz cache uses a one-hour default lifetime. A quiz certificate flow may place temporary certificate details in session storage for the current browser session.

These browser-storage items are not the same thing as an account profile. The ordinary learning and practice pages do not require you to create an account to browse their content.

Technical and usage information

When a browser requests a page or third-party resource, the relevant hosting, content, analytics, or advertising provider may receive technical information such as IP address, browser and device characteristics, requested URL, referral information, timestamps, and diagnostic data. Google Analytics may process usage and interaction information when analytics consent is granted. Advertising services may process device, browser, and ad-interaction information subject to your advertising choice and the providers' own settings.

3. Cookies, local storage, and consent choices

BookMCQ uses essential browser storage and may use cookies or similar technologies through Google and other integrated services. Essential functionality and security storage are enabled to support core site operation. Analytics and advertising storage are initially set to denied in the site's Google Consent Mode v2 configuration.

When you make a choice in the cookie banner, the site applies that choice to the relevant Google consent signals. You can accept all optional categories, reject optional categories, or customize analytics and advertising separately. Advertising consent controls signals including ad storage, ad user data, and ad personalization. Analytics consent controls analytics storage.

Your preference is saved in local storage for up to 180 days, where the browser allows it. You can reopen the controls using Cookie settings in the site footer. You can also clear site data through your browser settings; clearing it may cause the banner to appear again. Blocking storage or scripts may affect some website features.

4. Google Analytics and advertising

BookMCQ integrates Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and Google AdSense. Google Tag Manager can manage the loading of website tags. Google Analytics helps understand website use and performance. Google AdSense may display advertisements and measure their delivery or performance.

These services are subject to the consent signals described above. Where consent is required, analytics and advertising storage are not granted by default; the site updates the applicable Google consent signals after you make a selection. Google may process information according to its own privacy policy and service terms. Your interactions with advertisements may also be governed by Google's ad settings and controls.

For details about Google's data practices and available controls, consult Google's Privacy Policy and My Ad Center.

5. Service providers and sharing

BookMCQ uses service providers to operate and improve the website. The contact form sends submissions to a Supabase-hosted database endpoint. The quiz experience uses Supabase services to retrieve quiz content. The topic library may retrieve its published data file from GitHub-hosted content. Google services described above may receive information when their tags or features operate.

These providers process information under their own service arrangements and privacy documentation. BookMCQ does not sell the personal information submitted through the contact form. Information may also be disclosed where necessary to respond to lawful requests, protect the site and its users, investigate abuse, or address security incidents.

6. Why information is used

Information may be used to deliver educational pages and quiz features, retrieve and temporarily cache content, handle questions and book requests, reply to messages, maintain and secure the website, diagnose errors, understand aggregate site usage where analytics consent is granted, and support advertising where advertising consent is granted.

BookMCQ does not describe quiz cache data as a permanent learner profile. Browser-based cache and session information is used to support the relevant on-page feature.

7. Retention and security

Contact messages may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to review the request, respond, maintain relevant records, and protect the website. The exact retention period may depend on the nature of the message and operational or legal requirements. Browser preferences remain until their stated expiry or until you clear them; quiz cache entries are designed to expire after their configured lifetime, and session storage is generally cleared when the browser session ends.

BookMCQ and its providers use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive details through the contact form.

8. International processing and external links

Third-party providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Their applicable safeguards and terms are described in their own documentation. BookMCQ also links to external websites and resources. We do not control the privacy practices or content of independent third-party sites, and recommend reviewing their policies before providing information to them.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage optional analytics and advertising consent through the Cookie settings link in the footer, and you can clear browser storage using your browser controls. You may choose not to submit the contact form, although that means BookMCQ may be unable to respond to your request.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To submit a privacy request, email bookmcq@hotmail.com and describe the request. We may need enough information to identify the relevant message and verify the request before acting.

10. Children's privacy

BookMCQ is an educational resource for a general audience and is not designed to request sensitive personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information through the contact form may contact us at bookmcq@hotmail.com so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date. Please review this page periodically for the latest information.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the way BookMCQ handles information, contact us at bookmcq@hotmail.com or use the Contact Us page.
