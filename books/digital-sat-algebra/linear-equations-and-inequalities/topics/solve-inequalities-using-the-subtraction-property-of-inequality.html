What is Solve inequalities using the Subtraction Property of Inequality?

Definition:
The Subtraction Property of Inequality states that subtracting the same number from both sides of an inequality preserves the inequality direction. For \(a < b\), \(a - c < b - c\). This is used to eliminate added constants on the variable side.

Working:
For \(x + 7 > 12\), subtract 7 from both sides: \(x + 7 - 7 > 12 - 7\), giving \(x > 5\). No sign reversal occurs.

Example:
Solve \(m + 4 \leq 10\). Subtract 4: \(m \leq 6\). The solution is all numbers less than or equal to 6.

Reason:
This property allows us to isolate the variable by removing constants, just like in equations, but without changing the inequality.

Q1. Solve \(x+7>15\) by subtracting the same number from both sides. Which solution set is correct?

A. \(x>8\) ✅
B. \(x>22\)
C. \(x<8\)
D. \(x\geq8\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Subtracting 7 from both sides preserves the direction of the inequality because the same quantity is removed from each side. Thus \(x+7>15\) becomes \(x>8\), so all values greater than 8 satisfy the inequality.

Q2. A student solves \(y+12\leq20\) and writes \(y\leq32\). Which correction best explains the error?

A. Add 12 to both sides instead of subtracting it
B. Subtract 12 from both sides, giving \(y\leq8\) ✅
C. Reverse the inequality sign, giving \(y\geq8\)
D. Divide both sides by 12, giving \(y\leq\frac{20}{12}\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The student incorrectly added 12 instead of removing it from the left side. Subtracting 12 from both sides gives \(y\leq8\). Since subtraction does not reverse an inequality, the sign remains \(\leq\).

Q3. A temperature must remain below 18°C. If the temperature is modeled by \(T+5<18\), which condition describes the allowable values of \(T\)?

A. \(T<13\) ✅
B. \(T>13\)
C. \(T<23\)
D. \(T>23\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

To isolate \(T\), subtract 5 from both sides: \(T+5<18\) becomes \(T<13\). Therefore, any temperature strictly below 13°C meets the stated requirement.

Q4. Which inequality is equivalent to \(3+x\geq11\), and why?

A. \(x\geq8\), because 3 is subtracted from both sides ✅
B. \(x\geq14\), because 3 is added to both sides
C. \(x\leq8\), because the inequality reverses
D. \(x\leq14\), because subtraction reverses the inequality

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Subtracting 3 from both sides isolates \(x\): \(3+x\geq11\) becomes \(x\geq8\). The inequality direction does not change because subtracting the same quantity from both sides preserves the original ordering.

Q5. A club has already collected 15 points and needs more than 40 points in total. If \(p+15>40\), what is the minimum whole-number value of \(p\)?

A. 24
B. 25
C. 26 ✅
D. 55

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Subtracting 15 gives \(p>25\). Because \(p\) represents a whole number of additional points, the smallest possible value strictly greater than 25 is 26. This distinguishes an inequality boundary from its first integer solution.

Q6. A student claims that \(a-9<4\) becomes \(a<-5\) after subtracting 9 from both sides. What is the correct reasoning?

A. Add 9 to both sides, obtaining \(a<13\) ✅
B. Subtract 9 again, obtaining \(a<-5\)
C. Reverse the sign, obtaining \(a>13\)
D. Multiply by \(-1\), obtaining \(a>5\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The expression \(a-9\) already has 9 subtracted from \(a\). To isolate \(a\), add 9 to both sides: \(a-9<4\) becomes \(a<13\). The student's second subtraction moves the variable farther from isolation.

Q7. A number line represents all values to the left of 10, with 10 shown by an open circle. Which inequality matches the graph?

A. \(x\leq10\)
B. \(x<10\) ✅
C. \(x>10\)
D. \(x\geq10\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

An open circle means the boundary value 10 is excluded, while shading to the left represents values smaller than 10. Therefore, the graph corresponds precisely to \(x<10\), not \(x\leq10\).

Q8. A number-line graph shows a closed circle at 6 and shading to the left. Which original inequality could produce this solution after subtracting 4 from both sides?

A. \(x+4\leq10\) ✅
B. \(x+4<10\)
C. \(x-4\leq10\)
D. \(x+4\geq10\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The graph represents \(x\leq6\). Adding 4 to both sides reverses the subtraction step, giving \(x+4\leq10\). The closed circle confirms that 6 is included, so a strict inequality would be incorrect.

Q9. Two students solve \(m+18>30\). Student A subtracts 18 from both sides. Student B subtracts 30 from both sides. Who obtains an equivalent inequality?

A. Only Student A, because \(m>12\) isolates the variable
B. Only Student B, because \(m-12>0\) is simpler
C. Both, because both operations preserve equivalence ✅
D. Neither, because subtraction always reverses \(>\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Student A obtains \(m>12\). Student B obtains \(m-12>0\), which is also equivalent to \(m>12\) after rearranging. Subtracting the same quantity from both sides preserves the inequality, so both methods are valid.

Q10. A delivery service charges a fixed fee of 8 dollars plus an amount \(c\) for the package. The total must be at most 35 dollars. Which inequality describes the allowable package charge?

A. \(c\leq27\) ✅
B. \(c<27\)
C. \(c\geq27\)
D. \(c\leq43\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The cost condition is \(c+8\leq35\). Subtracting 8 from both sides gives \(c\leq27\). The phrase 'at most' includes the boundary value, so 27 is allowed and the correct symbol is \(\leq\).

Q11. Suppose \(q+14\geq22\). A student says \(q\geq36\) because the numbers 14 and 22 should be combined. Which statement best evaluates the student's approach?

A. Correct, because numbers on opposite sides should be added
B. Incorrect; subtract 14 from both sides to get \(q\geq8\) ✅
C. Incorrect; subtract 22 from both sides to get \(q\geq-8\)
D. Correct, because \(14+22=36\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The goal is to isolate \(q\), not combine unrelated constants. Subtracting 14 from both sides gives \(q\geq8\). The student's 36 comes from an operation that changes the equation's meaning rather than preserving equivalence.

Q12. A solution must satisfy \(r+6>2\) and \(r+10\leq15\). Which interval contains exactly the values satisfying both conditions?

A. \(r>4\)
B. \(r\leq5\)
C. \(4<r\leq5\) ✅
D. \(r<4\) or \(r>5\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Subtracting 6 from the first inequality gives \(r>-4\), while subtracting 10 from the second gives \(r\leq5\). Combining both conditions produces \(-4<r\leq5\). This requires solving and intersecting two inequalities rather than treating them separately.

Q13. For the inequality \(x+9<16\), a student obtains \(x<7\). Another student obtains \(x<25\). Without testing many values, which result must be correct and why?

A. \(x<25\), because addition requires increasing 16 by 9
B. \(x<7\), because subtracting 9 reverses the inequality
C. Both are correct because \(7<25\)
D. \(x<7\), because subtracting 9 isolates \(x\) without changing the sign ✅

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Subtracting 9 from both sides gives \(x<7\). The inequality direction stays unchanged because the same quantity is subtracted from both sides. The result \(x<25\) comes from incorrectly adding 9 instead of removing it.
