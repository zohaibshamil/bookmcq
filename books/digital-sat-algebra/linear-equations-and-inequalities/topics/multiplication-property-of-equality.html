What is Multiplication Property of Equality?

Definition:
The Multiplication Property of Equality states that multiplying both sides of an equation by the same number preserves equality. Formally, if \(a = b\), then \(a \cdot c = b \cdot c\). It is used to eliminate division by a constant, especially when the variable is in the numerator of a fraction.

Working:
To solve \(x/6 = 9\), multiply both sides by 6: \((x/6) \cdot 6 = 9 \cdot 6\), which simplifies to \(x = 54\). The multiplication cancels the division by 6.

Example:
Solve \(a/5 = 12\) using Multiplication Property. Multiply both sides by 5: \((a/5) \cdot 5 = 12 \cdot 5\), so \(a = 60\). Verify: \(60/5 = 12\).

Reason:
This property is the inverse of division, allowing us to undo division and solve equations where the variable is divided by a constant.

📝 All Multiplication Property of Equality MCQs

Q1. What is the solution to the equation \( \frac{x}{5} = -3 \)?

A.\( x = -15 \) ✅
B.\( x = 15 \)
C.\( x = -\frac{3}{5} \)
D.\( x = \frac{3}{5} \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Multiplication Property of Equality states that if you multiply both sides of an equation by the same nonzero number, the sides remain equal. To isolate \( x \), multiply both sides by 5: \( 5 \cdot \frac{x}{5} = -3 \cdot 5 \), which gives \( x = -15 \). Option B is a common sign error, while C and D come from incorrectly multiplying or dividing.

Q2. Which equation has the solution \( x = 12 \)?

A.\( \frac{x}{4} = 3 \)
B.\( 4x = 3 \)
C.\( \frac{x}{3} = 4 \)
D.Both A and C ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Both A and C yield \( x = 12 \). For A, multiply both sides by 4: \( x = 12 \). For C, multiply both sides by 3: \( x = 12 \). Option B gives \( x = \frac{3}{4} \). This question tests whether you understand that different equations can have the same solution and that the multiplication property works in both directions.

Q3. A recipe calls for \( \frac{3}{4} \) cup of sugar to make 12 cookies. How much sugar is needed for 30 cookies if the proportion is \( \frac{3}{4} = 12x \)?

A.\( \frac{15}{8} \) cups ✅
B.\( \frac{5}{8} \) cups
C.\( \frac{45}{4} \) cups
D.\( \frac{3}{16} \) cups
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Start with \( \frac{3}{4} = 12x \). To solve for \( x \), multiply both sides by the reciprocal of 12, which is \( \frac{1}{12} \): \( x = \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{12} = \frac{3}{48} = \frac{1}{16} \) cup per cookie. For 30 cookies, multiply by 30: \( 30 \cdot \frac{1}{16} = \frac{30}{16} = \frac{15}{8} \) cups. Option B is per-cookie amount for 10 cookies, C uses 12 as multiplier, D is per-cookie amount.

Q4. A student solves \( -4x = 20 \) and writes \( x = 5 \). What error did they make?

A.They added 4 instead of multiplying
B.They divided by -4 instead of 4
C.They divided by 4 instead of -4 ✅
D.They multiplied by -4 instead of dividing
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The student correctly identified that division is needed, but they divided by positive 4 instead of negative 4. Since the coefficient is -4, you must divide both sides by -4 to isolate \( x \): \( x = \frac{20}{-4} = -5 \). Option B would give \( x = -5 \) if they had divided by -4, but they didn't. Option A and D are procedural errors not shown.

Q5. The graph of \( y = 3x \) passes through the point \( (4, 12) \). If you solve \( 3x = 12 \), what does the solution represent on the graph?

A.The x-coordinate where the line crosses y=12 ✅
B.The y-coordinate when x=4
C.The slope of the line
D.The x-intercept of the line
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The equation \( 3x = 12 \) asks: for what x-value does the line \( y = 3x \) have a y-value of 12? Solving gives \( x = 4 \), which is the x-coordinate of the point where the horizontal line \( y = 12 \) intersects the graph. Option B reverses the variables, C confuses slope with solution, and D is the x-intercept (where y=0).

Q6. Solve for \( x \): \( \frac{2}{3}x = 8 \). Which method is correct?

A.Multiply both sides by \( \frac{2}{3} \)
B.Multiply both sides by \( \frac{3}{2} \) ✅
C.Divide both sides by \( \frac{3}{2} \)
D.Subtract \( \frac{2}{3} \) from both sides
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: To isolate \( x \), you must multiply by the reciprocal of \( \frac{2}{3} \), which is \( \frac{3}{2} \), because \( \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 1 \). This gives \( x = 8 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 12 \). Option A would leave \( \frac{4}{9}x \), C is division by reciprocal which is same as multiplying by \( \frac{2}{3} \), and D is not a multiplicative operation.

Q7. If \( \frac{x}{-7} = 9 \), what is the value of \( -x \)?

A.\( -63 \)
B.\( 63 \) ✅
C.\( -9 \)
D.\( 9 \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: First solve for \( x \): multiply both sides by -7 to get \( x = -63 \). The question asks for \( -x \), so \( -(-63) = 63 \). Option A is the value of \( x \), not \( -x \). This tests if you can solve and then apply an additional operation, a common two-step reasoning task.

Q8. Compare the equations \( 5x = 20 \) and \( \frac{x}{5} = 20 \). Which statement is true?

A.Both have the same solution
B.\( 5x = 20 \) has a larger solution
C.\( \frac{x}{5} = 20 \) has a larger solution ✅
D.The solutions are opposites
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Solve \( 5x = 20 \) gives \( x = 4 \). Solve \( \frac{x}{5} = 20 \) gives \( x = 100 \). Since 100 > 4, option C is correct. This question requires solving both equations and comparing the results, integrating multiplication and division properties in one problem.

Q9. The equation \( 0.25x = 10 \) can be solved by multiplying both sides by 4. Which property justifies this?

A.Multiplication Property of Equality ✅
B.Division Property of Equality
C.Multiplicative Inverse Property
D.Commutative Property
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Multiplication Property of Equality says you can multiply both sides by the same nonzero number. Here, multiplying by 4 (the reciprocal of 0.25) isolates \( x \). While the Multiplicative Inverse Property tells us that \( 0.25 \times 4 = 1 \), the action of doing it to both sides is justified by the Multiplication Property of Equality. Option B is division, C is a property of numbers, D is order of operations.

Q10. A car travels at a constant speed and covers \( d \) miles in \( t \) hours. The formula is \( d = 60t \). If the car travels 240 miles, which equation and solution are correct?

A.\( 60t = 240 \), \( t = 4 \) ✅
B.\( 60t = 240 \), \( t = 14400 \)
C.\( \frac{t}{60} = 240 \), \( t = 4 \)
D.\( 60t = 240 \), \( t = 180 \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Substitute \( d = 240 \) into \( d = 60t \) to get \( 60t = 240 \). Divide both sides by 60 (or multiply by \( \frac{1}{60} \)): \( t = 4 \) hours. Option B incorrectly multiplies 240 by 60, C uses division incorrectly, and D adds 60 to 240. This models real-world distance-rate-time relationships.

Q11. A student claims that \( \frac{x}{3} = 5 \) and \( 3x = 5 \) have the same solution because both involve 3 and 5. Is the student correct?

A.Yes, both give \( x = 15 \)
B.No, the first gives 15, the second gives \( \frac{5}{3} \) ✅
C.No, the first gives \( \frac{5}{3} \), the second gives 15
D.Yes, both give \( \frac{5}{3} \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The student has a conceptual misunderstanding. For \( \frac{x}{3} = 5 \), multiply by 3: \( x = 15 \). For \( 3x = 5 \), divide by 3: \( x = \frac{5}{3} \). These are different operations (multiplication vs. division) and yield different solutions. This question tests the ability to recognize and explain the error in reasoning.

Q12. The line \( y = -2x \) is graphed. Which equation represents the x-value where the line crosses \( y = 10 \)?

A.\( -2x = 10 \), \( x = -5 \) ✅
B.\( -2x = 10 \), \( x = 5 \)
C.\( 2x = 10 \), \( x = 5 \)
D.\( -2x = -10 \), \( x = 5 \)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The line \( y = -2x \) crosses the horizontal line \( y = 10 \) when \( -2x = 10 \). Divide both sides by -2 (or multiply by \( -\frac{1}{2} \)): \( x = -5 \). Option B has sign error, C uses wrong coefficient sign, D has wrong constant sign. This connects graphical interpretation with algebraic solution.

Q13. If \( \frac{3x}{4} = 9 \), what is the value of \( \frac{x}{4} \)?

A.\( 12 \)
B.\( 3 \) ✅
C.\( 9 \)
D.\( 4 \)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: First solve \( \frac{3x}{4} = 9 \). Multiply both sides by 4: \( 3x = 36 \), then divide by 3: \( x = 12 \). The question asks for \( \frac{x}{4} \), which is \( \frac{12}{4} = 3 \). Option A is \( x \), C is the original RHS, D is the denominator. This requires solving and then substituting into a different expression, a higher-order reasoning task.

Q14. Which of the following is NOT a valid first step to solve \( \frac{x}{-5} = 15 \)?

A.Multiply both sides by -5
B.Multiply both sides by 5 ✅
C.Divide both sides by \( \frac{1}{-5} \)
D.Multiply both sides by \( \frac{-1}{5} \)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: To solve \( \frac{x}{-5} = 15 \), you must isolate \( x \). Since \( x \) is divided by -5, you multiply by -5. Multiplying by 5 would give \( \frac{5x}{-5} = 75 \) or \( -x = 75 \), which is not a direct isolation and introduces an extra step. Option C is equivalent to multiplying by -5 (since dividing by \( \frac{1}{-5} \) is multiplying by -5), and D is the reciprocal of -5, so it’s also valid. This tests understanding of equivalent operations.
