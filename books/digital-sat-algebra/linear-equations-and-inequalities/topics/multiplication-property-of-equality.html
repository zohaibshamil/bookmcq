What is Multiplication Property of Equality?

Definition:
The Multiplication Property of Equality states that multiplying both sides of an equation by the same number preserves equality. Formally, if a=ba = ba=b, then a⋅c=b⋅ca \cdot c = b \cdot ca⋅c=b⋅c. It is used to eliminate division by a constant, especially when the variable is in the numerator of a fraction.

Working:
To solve x/6=9x/6 = 9x/6=9, multiply both sides by 6: (x/6)⋅6=9⋅6(x/6) \cdot 6 = 9 \cdot 6(x/6)⋅6=9⋅6, which simplifies to x=54x = 54x=54. The multiplication cancels the division by 6.

Example:
Solve a/5=12a/5 = 12a/5=12 using Multiplication Property. Multiply both sides by 5: (a/5)⋅5=12⋅5(a/5) \cdot 5 = 12 \cdot 5(a/5)⋅5=12⋅5, so a=60a = 60a=60. Verify: 60/5=1260/5 = 1260/5=12.

Reason:
This property is the inverse of division, allowing us to undo division and solve equations where the variable is divided by a constant.

Q1. What is the solution to the equation x5=−3 \frac{x}{5} = -3 5x​=−3?

A.x=−15 x = -15 x=−15 ✅
B.x=15 x = 15 x=15
C.x=−35 x = -\frac{3}{5} x=−53​
D.x=35 x = \frac{3}{5} x=53​
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Multiplication Property of Equality states that if you multiply both sides of an equation by the same nonzero number, the sides remain equal. To isolate x x x, multiply both sides by 5: 5⋅x5=−3⋅5 5 \cdot \frac{x}{5} = -3 \cdot 5 5⋅5x​=−3⋅5, which gives x=−15 x = -15 x=−15. Option B is a common sign error, while C and D come from incorrectly multiplying or dividing.

This question tests whether you understand that different equations can have the same solution and that the multiplication property works in both directions.

Q3. A recipe calls for 34 \frac{3}{4} 43​ cup of sugar to make 12 cookies. How much sugar is needed for 30 cookies if the proportion is 34=12x \frac{3}{4} = 12x 43​=12x?

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Start with 34=12x \frac{3}{4} = 12x 43​=12x. To solve for x x x, multiply both sides by the reciprocal of 12, which is 112 \frac{1}{12} 121​: x=34⋅112=348=116 x = \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{12} = \frac{3}{48} = \frac{1}{16} x=43​⋅121​=483​=161​ cup per cookie. For 30 cookies, multiply by 30: 30⋅116=3016=158 30 \cdot \frac{1}{16} = \frac{30}{16} = \frac{15}{8} 30⋅161​=1630​=815​ cups. Option B is per-cookie amount for 10 cookies, C uses 12 as multiplier, D is per-cookie amount.

Q6. Solve for x x x: 23x=8 \frac{2}{3}x = 8 32​x=8. Which method is correct?

Option A would leave 49x \frac{4}{9}x 94​x, C is division by reciprocal which is same as multiplying by 23 \frac{2}{3} 32​, and D is not a multiplicative operation.

Q9. The equation 0.25x=10 0.25x = 10 0.25x=10 can be solved by multiplying both sides by 4. Which property justifies this?

A.Multiplication Property of Equality ✅
B.Division Property of Equality
C.Multiplicative Inverse Property
D.Commutative Property
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Multiplication Property of Equality says you can multiply both sides by the same nonzero number. Here, multiplying by 4 (the reciprocal of 0.25) isolates x x x. While the Multiplicative Inverse Property tells us that 0.25×4=1 0.25 \times 4 = 1 0.25×4=1, the action of doing it to both sides is justified by the Multiplication Property of Equality. Option B is division, C is a property of numbers, D is order of operations.

Q14. Which of the following is NOT a valid first step to solve x−5=15 \frac{x}{-5} = 15 −5x​=15?

This tests understanding of equivalent operations.
