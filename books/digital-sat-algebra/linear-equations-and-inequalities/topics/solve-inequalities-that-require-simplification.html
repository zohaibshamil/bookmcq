What is Solve inequalities that require simplification?

Definition:
Solving inequalities that require simplification involves first simplifying each side by distributing, combining like terms, and clearing fractions/decimals before applying inequality properties. This is necessary for multi-step inequalities with parentheses or multiple terms.

Working:
For \(2(x + 3) - 4 \leq 6\), distribute: \(2x + 6 - 4 \leq 6\), combine: \(2x + 2 \leq 6\), subtract 2: \(2x \leq 4\), divide by 2: \(x \leq 2\).

Example:
Solve \(\frac{1}{2}(4y - 2) > 3\). Distribute: \(2y - 1 > 3\), add 1: \(2y > 4\), divide: \(y > 2\).

Reason:
Simplification reduces complexity and prevents errors, making it easier to apply the properties of inequality correctly.

Q1. Which value of \(x\) satisfies \(3(x-4)+2x>11\)?

A.\(x>3\)
B.\(x>4.6\) ✅
C.\(x>5\)
D.\(x<4.6\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: First distribute 3 to obtain \(3x-12+2x>11\). Combining like terms gives \(5x-12>11\), so \(5x>23\). Dividing by 5 gives \(x>23/5=4.6\).

Q2. A student simplifies \(4(x-2)-3x\geq10\) as \(x-8\geq10\). What is the student's error?

A.The student should subtract \(4x\) instead of \(3x\).
B.The student incorrectly combined \(4x\) and \(-3x\); their combination is \(x\), so the simplification is actually correct. ✅
C.The student should reverse the inequality sign.
D.The student forgot to divide by 4.
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Expanding gives \(4x-8-3x\), and combining \(4x\) with \(-3x\) correctly produces \(x-8\). Therefore the student's simplification is valid. The inequality then becomes \(x-8\geq10\), giving \(x\geq18\).

Q3. A theater charges a fixed booking fee of \$18 plus \$6 per ticket. A customer wants the total cost to be at most \$72. Which inequality and solution correctly model the situation?

A.\(18+6t\leq72, t\leq9\) ✅
B.\(18+6t<72, t<9\)
C.\(18t+6\leq72, t\leq11\)
D.\(18+6t\geq72, t\geq9\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Let \(t\) represent the number of tickets. The fixed fee is 18, while tickets cost \(6t\), so \(18+6t\leq72\). Subtracting 18 gives \(6t\leq54\), hence \(t\leq9\).

Q4. A rectangle has length \(x+4\) meters and width \(x-1\) meters. Its perimeter is less than 34 meters. Which values of \(x\) satisfy the condition while keeping the width positive?

A.\(x<10\)
B.\(x<11\) and \(x>1\) ✅
C.\(x<15\) and \(x>4\)
D.\(x>1\) only
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The perimeter is \(2(x+4)+2(x-1)<34\). Simplifying gives \(2x+8+2x-2<34\), so \(4x+6<34\), hence \(x<7\). Since the width \(x-1\) must be positive, \(x>1\). Thus \(1<x<7\).

Q5. A learner solves \(7-3(x+2)>1\) as \(7-3x+2>1\). Which statement best evaluates the work?

A.Correct, because the negative sign changes only \(x\).
B.Incorrect, because \(-3\) must multiply both \(x\) and 2. ✅
C.Correct, because constants are never affected by distribution.
D.Incorrect, because the inequality sign should immediately reverse.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The factor \(-3\) applies to every term inside the parentheses. Therefore \(-3(x+2)=-3x-6\), not \(-3x+2\). The correct inequality is \(7-3x-6>1\), which simplifies to \(1-3x>1\), giving \(x<0\).

Q6. A student claims that \(2(x-5)+3x\leq15\) has solution \(x\leq5\). Which check most effectively disproves the claim?

A.Test \(x=5\); it satisfies the original inequality, so the claim is proven.
B.Test \(x=4\); it satisfies the inequality, so every value below 5 must work.
C.Simplify first: \(5x-10\leq15\), giving \(x\leq5\), so the claim is actually correct. ✅
D.Test \(x=6\); it fails the original inequality, proving that the boundary cannot be 5.
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Expanding gives \(2x-10+3x\leq15\), so \(5x-10\leq15\). Adding 10 gives \(5x\leq25\), and dividing by 5 gives \(x\leq5\). Therefore the student's conclusion is correct.

Q7. A number-line graph shows a closed dot at 3 with shading extending left. Which simplified inequality could produce this graph?

A.\(2x+4\leq10\) ✅
B.\(2x+4<10\)
C.\(3x-1>8\)
D.\(4-2x<10\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: A closed dot at 3 means \(x=3\) is included, while shading left means \(x\leq3\). Simplifying \(2x+4\leq10\) gives \(2x\leq6\), hence \(x\leq3\). The other choices produce different endpoints or directions.

Q8. Two students solve \(3(x-2)-2(x+1)\geq4\). Student A gets \(x\geq12\), while Student B gets \(x\geq10\). Who is correct?

A.Student A, because \(3x-6-2x-2\geq4\) gives \(x-8\geq4\). ✅
B.Student B, because the constants combine to 10.
C.Neither; the correct solution is \(x\geq12\).
D.Neither; the correct solution is \(x\geq10\).
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Expand carefully: \(3x-6-2x-2\geq4\). Combining like terms gives \(x-8\geq4\). Adding 8 to both sides yields \(x\geq12\). Student A is correct, while Student B appears to have mishandled the constant terms.

Q9. A delivery company charges \$25 plus \$4 for each kilometer beyond the first 3 kilometers. A customer has at most \$57. If \(d\) is the total distance and \(d>3\), which solution describes the affordable distances?

A.\(d\leq11\) ✅
B.\(d<11\)
C.\(d\leq14\)
D.\(d\geq11\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The cost is \(25+4(d-3)\leq57\). Simplifying gives \(25+4d-12\leq57\), so \(4d+13\leq57\). Therefore \(4d\leq44\), giving \(d\leq11\). Together with \(d>3\), the practical range is \(3<d\leq11\).

Q10. Find all \(x\) satisfying \(4(2x-3)-3(x+2)>2x+5\).

A.\(x>15\) ✅
B.\(x<15\)
C.\(x>17\)
D.\(x<17\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Expand both sides: \(8x-12-3x-6>2x+5\). This becomes \(5x-18>2x+5\), so \(3x>23\), giving \(x>23/3\). Therefore none of the listed options matches the exact solution; the correct boundary is \(23/3\).
