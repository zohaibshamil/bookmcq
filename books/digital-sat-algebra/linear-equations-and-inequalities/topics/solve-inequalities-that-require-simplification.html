What is Solve inequalities that require simplification?

Definition:
Solving inequalities that require simplification involves first simplifying each side by distributing, combining like terms, and clearing fractions/decimals before applying inequality properties. This is necessary for multi-step inequalities with parentheses or multiple terms.

Working:
For 2(x+3)−4≤62(x + 3) - 4 \leq 62(x+3)−4≤6, distribute: 2x+6−4≤62x + 6 - 4 \leq 62x+6−4≤6, combine: 2x+2≤62x + 2 \leq 62x+2≤6, subtract 2: 2x≤42x \leq 42x≤4, divide by 2: x≤2x \leq 2x≤2.

Example:
Solve 12(4y−2)>3\frac{1}{2}(4y - 2) > 321​(4y−2)>3. Distribute: 2y−1>32y - 1 > 32y−1>3, add 1: 2y>42y > 42y>4, divide: y>2y > 2y>2.

Reason:
Simplification reduces complexity and prevents errors, making it easier to apply the properties of inequality correctly.

Q1. Which value of xxx satisfies 3(x-4)+2x&gt;11?

A.x&gt;3
B.x&gt;4.6 ✅
C.x&gt;5
D.x&lt;4.6
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: First distribute 3 to obtain 3x-12+2x&gt;11. Combining like terms gives 5x-12&gt;11, so 5x&gt;23. Dividing by 5 gives x&gt;23/5=4.6.

📖 Explanation: Expanding gives 4x−8−3x4x-8-3x4x−8−3x, and combining 4x4x4x with −3x-3x−3x correctly produces x−8x-8x−8. Therefore the student's simplification is valid. The inequality then becomes x−8≥10x-8\geq10x−8≥10, giving x≥18x\geq18x≥18.

Q4. A rectangle has length x+4x+4x+4 meters and width x−1x-1x−1 meters. Its perimeter is less than 34 meters. Which values of xxx satisfy the condition while keeping the width positive?

A.x&lt;10
B.x&lt;11 and x&gt;1 ✅
C.x&lt;15 and x&gt;4
D.x&gt;1 only
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The perimeter is 2(x+4)+2(x-1)&lt;34. Simplifying gives 2x+8+2x-2&lt;34, so 4x+6&lt;34, hence x&lt;7. Since the width x−1x-1x−1 must be positive, x&gt;1. Thus 1&lt;x&lt;7.

Q5. A learner solves 7-3(x+2)&gt;1 as 7-3x+2&gt;1. Which statement best evaluates the work?

A.Correct, because the negative sign changes only xxx.
B.Incorrect, because −3-3−3 must multiply both xxx and 2. ✅
C.Correct, because constants are never affected by distribution.
D.Incorrect, because the inequality sign should immediately reverse.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The factor −3-3−3 applies to every term inside the parentheses. Therefore −3(x+2)=−3x−6-3(x+2)=-3x-6−3(x+2)=−3x−6, not −3x+2-3x+2−3x+2. The correct inequality is 7-3x-6&gt;1, which simplifies to 1-3x&gt;1, giving x&lt;0.

Q6. A student claims that 2(x−5)+3x≤152(x-5)+3x\leq152(x−5)+3x≤15 has solution x≤5x\leq5x≤5. Which check most effectively disproves the claim?

D.Test x=6x=6x=6; it fails the original inequality, proving that the boundary cannot be 5.
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Expanding gives 2x−10+3x≤152x-10+3x\leq152x−10+3x≤15, so 5x−10≤155x-10\leq155x−10≤15. Adding 10 gives 5x≤255x\leq255x≤25, and dividing by 5 gives x≤5x\leq5x≤5. Therefore the student's conclusion is correct.

Q7. A number-line graph shows a closed dot at 3 with shading extending left. Which simplified inequality could produce this graph?

A.2x+4≤102x+4\leq102x+4≤10 ✅
B.2x+4&lt;10
C.3x-1&gt;8

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: A closed dot at 3 means x=3x=3x=3 is included, while shading left means x≤3x\leq3x≤3. Simplifying 2x+4≤102x+4\leq102x+4≤10 gives 2x≤62x\leq62x≤6, hence x≤3x\leq3x≤3. The other choices produce different endpoints or directions.

Q8. Two students solve 3(x−2)−2(x+1)≥43(x-2)-2(x+1)\geq43(x−2)−2(x+1)≥4. Student A gets x≥12x\geq12x≥12, while Student B gets x≥10x\geq10x≥10. Who is correct?

A.Student A, because 3x−6−2x−2≥43x-6-2x-2\geq43x−6−2x−2≥4 gives x−8≥4x-8\geq4x−8≥4. ✅
B.Student B, because the constants combine to 10.
C.Neither; the correct solution is x≥12x\geq12x≥12.
D.Neither; the correct solution is x≥10x\geq10x≥10.
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Expand carefully: 3x−6−2x−2≥43x-6-2x-2\geq43x−6−2x−2≥4. Combining like terms gives x−8≥4x-8\geq4x−8≥4. Adding 8 to both sides yields x≥12x\geq12x≥12. Student A is correct, while Student B appears to have mishandled the constant terms.
