What is How to Solve Linear Inequalities?

Definition:
Solving linear inequalities is similar to solving linear equations, but with inequality symbols (<,>,≤,≥<, >, \leq, \geq<,>,≤,≥). The goal is to isolate the variable on one side. However, a key difference is that multiplying or dividing by a negative number reverses the inequality sign.

Working:
For 3x−5>103x - 5 > 103x−5>10, add 5: 3x>153x > 153x>15, divide by 3: x>5x > 5x>5. For −2x≤6-2x \leq 6−2x≤6, divide by -2 and reverse the sign: x≥−3x \geq -3x≥−3. The solution set is often represented on a number line or in interval notation.

Example:
Solve 4y+2<184y + 2 < 184y+2<18. Subtract 2: 4y<164y < 164y<16, divide by 4: y<4y < 4y<4. This means all numbers less than 4 are solutions.

Reason:
Inequalities are used to describe ranges, limits, and constraints in real-life scenarios like budgets, speed limits, and minimum requirements.

Q1. Which value of xxx satisfies the inequality 4x-7&gt;13?

A.x=4x=4x=4
B.x=5x=5x=5
C.x=6x=6x=6 ✅
D.x=7x=7x=7
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Add 7 to both sides to obtain 4x&gt;20, then divide by the positive number 4 without changing the inequality direction. This gives x&gt;5, so among the choices only x=6x=6x=6 and x=7x=7x=7 satisfy it; therefore option C is correct.

Q2. A student solves −3x+8≤20-3x+8\leq20−3x+8≤20 and writes x≤−4x\leq-4x≤−4. What is the most important error in the student's reasoning?

A.The student should add 8 instead of subtracting 8.
B.The student forgot to divide by 3.
C.The inequality direction should reverse after division by −3-3−3. ✅
D.The constant 20 should become negative.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Subtracting 8 gives −3x≤12-3x\leq12−3x≤12. Dividing by −3-3−3 requires reversing the inequality symbol, producing x≥−4x\geq-4x≥−4. The student's numerical boundary is correct, but the direction is reversed incorrectly, which changes the entire solution set.

Q3. A delivery service charges a fixed fee of 6 dollars plus 2.50 dollars per mile. A customer has at most 31 dollars. Which inequality and solution correctly represent the greatest possible distance mmm?

A.6+2.50m≥31,m≥106+2.50m\geq31, m\geq106+2.50m≥31,m≥10
B.6+2.50m≤31,m≤106+2.50m\leq31, m\leq106+2.50m≤31,m≤10 ✅
C.2.50m−6≤31,m≤14.82.50m-6\leq31, m\leq14.82.50m−6≤31,m≤14.8
D.6+31m≤2.50,m≤06+31m\leq2.50, m\leq06+31m≤2.50,m≤0
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The phrase 'at most' means the total cost cannot exceed 31, so 6+2.50m≤316+2.50m\leq316+2.50m≤31. Subtracting 6 gives 2.50m≤252.50m\leq252.50m≤25, and dividing by 2.50 gives m≤10m\leq10m≤10. Thus the customer can travel no more than 10 miles.

Q4. A teacher claims that 5-2x&gt;17 gives x&gt;-6. Which explanation best evaluates the claim?

A.Correct, because subtracting 5 gives -2x&gt;12.
B.Incorrect, because dividing by −2-2−2 reverses the inequality, giving x&lt;-6. ✅
C.Correct, because negative coefficients never affect inequality signs.
D.Incorrect, because the answer must be x&gt;-12.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Subtracting 5 from both sides produces -2x&gt;12. Dividing by −2-2−2 changes the direction from greater than to less than, giving x&lt;-6. The student's proposed x&gt;-6 includes values that do not satisfy the original inequality.

Q5. A graph on a number line shows an open circle at 3 with shading extending to the left. Which inequality does the graph represent?

A.x&gt;3
B.x≥3x\geq3x≥3
C.x&lt;3 ✅
D.x≤3x\leq3x≤3
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: An open circle means the boundary value 3 is excluded from the solution. Shading to the left represents numbers smaller than 3. Therefore the graph corresponds to x&lt;3, rather than either inequality that includes 3 or shades toward larger values.

Q6. A school club has 120 dollars and must reserve 30 dollars for supplies. Each membership packet costs 15 dollars. If ppp packets are purchased, which solution represents the maximum number of packets?

A.p≤6p\leq6p≤6 ✅
B.p≥6p\geq6p≥6
C.p≤8p\leq8p≤8
D.p≥8p\geq8p≥8
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The available amount for packets is 120−30=90120-30=90120−30=90 dollars. Therefore 15p≤9015p\leq9015p≤90. Dividing by 15 gives p≤6p\leq6p≤6. Since packets are counted in whole numbers, six is the maximum possible purchase while still preserving the required 30 dollars.

Q7. A student solves 2(x-4)&lt;3x+5 as follows: 2x-8&lt;3x+5, then -13&lt;x, so x&gt;-13. What went wrong?

A.The distributive property was applied incorrectly.
B.The student should divide by 2 before expanding.
C.The inequality should reverse when subtracting 3x3x3x, giving x&gt;-13, so nothing is wrong.
D.The student made an arithmetic error when combining constants. ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The expansion 2(x−4)=2x−82(x-4)=2x-82(x−4)=2x−8 is correct. Subtracting 3x3x3x gives -x-8&lt;5, then adding 8 gives -x&lt;13. Dividing by −1-1−1 reverses the inequality, producing x&gt;-13. Therefore the student's final answer is actually correct, so the claim that an error occurred is false.

Q8. Which inequality has the same solution set as 7−3x≥167-3x\geq167−3x≥16?

A.x≥−3x\geq-3x≥−3
B.x≤−3x\leq-3x≤−3 ✅
C.x≥3x\geq3x≥3
D.x≤3x\leq3x≤3
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Subtracting 7 gives −3x≥9-3x\geq9−3x≥9. Dividing by −3-3−3 reverses the inequality, resulting in x≤−3x\leq-3x≤−3. The negative coefficient is the key feature because failing to reverse the inequality would produce the opposite and incorrect solution set.

Q9. A number xxx must satisfy both x&gt;2 and 3x−4≤113x-4\leq113x−4≤11. Which description gives all possible values of xxx?

A.x≤2x\leq2x≤2
B.x&gt;5
C.2&lt;x\leq5 ✅
D.2\leq x&lt;5
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Solving 3x−4≤113x-4\leq113x−4≤11 gives 3x≤153x\leq153x≤15, so x≤5x\leq5x≤5. Combining this with x&gt;2 requires values greater than 2 but no greater than 5. Thus the intersection of the two solution sets is 2&lt;x\leq5.

Q10. A student says the inequality 4-5x&lt;19 has solution x&gt;-3. Another student says it has solution x&lt;-3. Which conclusion is correct, and why?

A.The first student is correct because the coefficient of xxx is positive after simplification. ✅
B.The second student is correct because dividing by a negative coefficient reverses the inequality.
C.Both are correct because inequalities can have two solution intervals.
D.Neither is correct; the solution is x&lt;3.
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Subtracting 4 gives -5x&lt;15. Dividing by −5-5−5 reverses the inequality, producing x&gt;-3. Therefore the first student's answer is actually correct, while the second student's answer incorrectly ignores the reversal caused by dividing by a negative number.
