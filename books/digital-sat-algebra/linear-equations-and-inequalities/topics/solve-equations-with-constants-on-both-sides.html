What is Solve equations with constants on both sides?

Definition:
Solving equations with constants on both sides means equations where there are numbers (constants) on both the left and right sides, alongside the variable. The goal is to isolate the variable by moving all constant terms to one side using the Addition or Subtraction Property.

Working:
First, move all variable terms to one side (if they are on both sides), then move all constants to the opposite side by adding or subtracting. Finally, divide by the coefficient. For example, solve \(2x + 5 = x - 3\): subtract \(x\): \(x + 5 = -3\), subtract 5: \(x = -8\).

Example:
Solve \(4n + 7 = 2n + 15\). Subtract \(2n\): \(2n + 7 = 15\), subtract 7: \(2n = 8\), divide by 2: \(n = 4\). Check: \(4(4)+7 = 16+7=23\), \(2(4)+15 = 8+15=23\).

Reason:
This approach ensures all constant terms are grouped together, simplifying the equation to a standard form \(ax = b\), which is easy to solve.

Q1. A student solves \(3x + 7 = 5x - 3\) by subtracting \(3x\) from both sides to get \(7 = 2x - 3\), then adds 3 to get \(10 = 2x\), and finally divides to get \(x = 5\). Which step contains the first error if any?

A. Subtracting \(3x\) ✅
B. Adding 3
C. Dividing by 2
D. No error

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The student's steps are mathematically correct. Subtracting \(3x\) isolates the variable term on the right, adding 3 isolates the constant, and dividing by 2 gives \(x=5\). A common misconception is that the order of operations is wrong, but here the inverse operations are applied correctly in sequence.

Q2. Which equation has the same solution as \(4x - 9 = 2x + 7\)?

A. \(2x = 16\) ✅
B. \(4x = 2x + 16\)
C. \(6x = -2\)
D. \(x - 9 = 7\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The original equation simplifies to \(2x = 16\) after adding 9 and subtracting \(2x\). Option A is exactly that simplified form. Option B is equivalent but not simplified; option C has incorrect sign; option D ignores the \(4x\) term. Recognizing equivalent forms tests understanding of equation transformation.

Q3. A rectangle has perimeter 48 cm. Its length is \(2x + 5\) cm and its width is \(x - 3\) cm. Write and solve an equation to find \(x\).

A. \(2(2x+5 + x-3)=48 \Rightarrow x=8\) ✅
B. \(2x+5 + x-3 = 48 \Rightarrow x=15.33\)
C. \(2(2x+5) + 2(x-3)=48 \Rightarrow x=7\)
D. \(2(2x+5)+2(x-3)=48 \Rightarrow x=22/3\).

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The perimeter formula is \(2(L+W)=48\). Substituting gives \(2(3x+2)=48 \Rightarrow 6x+4=48 \Rightarrow 6x=44 \Rightarrow x=7.33\)

Q4. Given the equation \(7 - 3x = 4x + 14\), a student claims \(x = -1\). Is this correct? If not, what is the error?

A. Correct, \(x=-1\) ✅
B. Incorrect; should be \(x=1\)
C. Incorrect; should be \(x=-3\)
D. Incorrect; should be \(x=3\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Actually \(7 - 3(-1) = 7+3=10\) and \(4(-1)+14 = -4+14=10\), so \(x=-1\) is correct. The question tests if student can verify by substitution. Many students might make sign errors when moving \(-3x\) or adding constants, but here the solution is valid.

Q5. The graph of \(y = 5x - 2\) and \(y = 3x + 8\) intersect at point \(P\). What are the coordinates of \(P\)?

A. \((5, 23)\) ✅
B. \((3, 13)\)
C. \((5, 23)\)
D. \(( -5, -27)\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Setting \(5x - 2 = 3x + 8\) gives \(2x = 10 \Rightarrow x=5\). Substituting into either equation gives \(y=5(5)-2=23\). So point is \((5,23)\). This tests the connection between equation solving and intersection of linear graphs, a key conceptual bridge.

Q6. A mobile phone plan charges a fixed fee of \$20 plus \$0.10 per text. Another plan charges \$12 plus \$0.15 per text. For how many texts are the costs equal? Solve \(20 + 0.10t = 12 + 0.15t\).

A. \(t = 160\) ✅
B. \(t = 80\)
C. \(t = 200\)
D. \(t = 40\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Subtract \(0.10t\) from both sides: \(20 = 12 + 0.05t\). Subtract 12: \(8 = 0.05t\). Divide by 0.05: \(t = 160\). This models a real-world break-even scenario. A common mistake is to subtract 20 first, leading to \(0.10t = -8 + 0.15t\) then \( -0.05t = -8 \Rightarrow t=160\) still correct, but sign errors are frequent.

Q7. Compare the two methods for solving \(2x + 9 = 6x - 7\): Method A: Subtract \(2x\) then add 7. Method B: Add 7 then subtract \(6x\). Which statement is true?

A. Both methods yield \(x=4\) ✅
B. Method A gives \(x=4\), Method B gives \(x=-4\)
C. Method A gives \(x=-4\), Method B gives \(x=4\)
D. Both methods yield \(x=-4\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Method A: \(9 = 4x - 7 \Rightarrow 16=4x \Rightarrow x=4\). Method B: \(2x+16 = 6x \Rightarrow 16=4x \Rightarrow x=4\). Both are valid; order of inverse operations doesn't change the solution. This tests understanding that equation solving is flexible as long as operations are legal.

Q8. What is the value of \(x\) in the equation \(\frac{2x+5}{3} = \frac{x-1}{2}\)?

A. \(x = -13\) ✅
B. \(x = 7\)
C. \(x = -7\)
D. \(x = 13\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Cross-multiply: \(2(2x+5) = 3(x-1) \Rightarrow 4x+10 = 3x-3 \Rightarrow x = -13\). This involves fractions and constants on both sides, requiring distribution and careful integer arithmetic. A common error is to forget to distribute the 3 or 2 correctly.

Q9. A student writes the equation \(8x - 3 = 5x + 9\) and solves it as follows: \(8x - 5x = 9 + 3 \Rightarrow 3x = 12 \Rightarrow x = 4\). Is this correct? If not, identify the misconception.

A. Correct ✅
B. Incorrect; should be \(x=6\)
C. Incorrect; sign error when moving constants
D. Incorrect; should divide by 3 first

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The student moved all variable terms to left (subtract \(5x\)) and constants to right (add 3). That yields \(3x = 12\) which is correct. Then dividing by 3 gives 4. This is a perfectly valid method. The distractor 'sign error' is common when students incorrectly add/subtract, but here it's done correctly.

Q10. The equation \(4(x-2) = 3(x+5) - 2x\) simplifies to which of the following before isolating \(x\)?

A. \(4x - 8 = 3x + 15 - 2x\) ✅
B. \(4x - 8 = 3x + 5 - 2x\)
C. \(4x - 2 = 3x + 15 - 2x\)
D. \(4x - 8 = 3x + 15 + 2x\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Distribute correctly: \(4(x-2) = 4x - 8\). Right side: \(3(x+5) = 3x+15\), then subtract \(2x\) gives \(3x+15-2x = x+15\). So equation becomes \(4x - 8 = x+15\). Option A shows the intermediate distribution step before combining like terms, which is correct. This tests the order of operations in multi-step equations.

Q11. A car rental company A charges \$30 per day plus \$0.20 per mile. Company B charges \$50 per day with no mileage fee. For a one-day rental, how many miles must you drive for the costs to be equal?

A. \(m = 100\) ✅
B. \(m = 250\)
C. \(m = 400\)
D. \(m = 150\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Set \(30 + 0.20m = 50\). Subtract 30: \(0.20m = 20\). Divide by 0.20: \(m=100\). This is a simpler case where constant on one side is zero after moving. Students might incorrectly set \(30 = 50 - 0.20m\) and get negative sign errors, but the solution is straightforward.

Q12. The solution to \(3x + 4 = 2x - 5\) is \(x = -9\). Which of the following equations has a solution that is the reciprocal of this?

A. \(6x + 8 = 4x - 10\) ✅
B. \(\frac{3}{x} + 4 = \frac{2}{x} - 5\)
C. \(3x + 4 = -(2x - 5)\)
D. \(-3x - 4 = -2x + 5\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Reciprocal of \(-9\) is \(-1/9\). Solving \(\frac{3}{x} + 4 = \frac{2}{x} - 5\): multiply by \(x\): \(3+4x = 2 - 5x \Rightarrow 9x = -1 \Rightarrow x = -1/9\). This combines reciprocal concept with equation solving, testing deeper understanding of variable in denominator and inverse relationships.

Q13. A student solves \(5x - 8 = 3x + 12\) and gets \(x = 10\). Another student solves \(5x - 8 = 3x + 12\) by first adding 8 to both sides, then subtracting \(3x\), and gets \(x = 10\) as well. Which property justifies that both methods are valid?

A. Commutative property of addition ✅
B. Associative property of addition
C. Addition and subtraction properties of equality
D. Distributive property

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Both methods use the addition property of equality (adding or subtracting the same quantity from both sides). The order doesn't matter because these properties allow us to perform any sequence of legal operations. This distinguishes between properties of numbers (commutative/associative) and properties of equations (equality properties).

Q14. Given the equation \(2(3x - 1) = 4 - 2(x + 3)\), a student expands to get \(6x - 2 = 4 - 2x - 6\). They then simplify to \(6x - 2 = -2x - 2\). What is the next correct step to solve for \(x\)?

A. Add \(2x\) to both sides, then add 2 ✅
B. Add 2 to both sides, then subtract \(6x\)
C. Subtract \(6x\) from both sides, then add 2
D. Add \(2x\) to both sides, then subtract 2

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

After simplification, we have \(6x - 2 = -2x - 2\). To isolate \(x\), add \(2x\) to both sides to get \(8x - 2 = -2\), then add 2 to get \(8x = 0 \Rightarrow x=0\). Option A is correct order. Option D would give \(8x - 4 = -2\) which is unnecessary. This tests the strategic sequence of inverse operations.
