What is Make the coefficient of the variable equal to 1?

Definition:
Making the coefficient of the variable equal to 1 means transforming an equation of the form ax=bax = bax=b into x=b/ax = b/ax=b/a by dividing both sides by the coefficient aaa (or multiplying by its reciprocal). This final step isolates the variable completely, giving the solution. This is achieved using the Division or Multiplication Property of Equality.

Working:
Given 7x=427x = 427x=42, divide both sides by 7 to get x=6x = 6x=6. If the coefficient is a fraction, like 23x=8\frac{2}{3}x = 832​x=8, multiply both sides by the reciprocal 32\frac{3}{2}23​: x=8⋅32=12x = 8 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 12x=8⋅23​=12.

Example:
Solve 5y=455y = 455y=45. Divide both sides by 5: 5y/5=45/55y/5 = 45/55y/5=45/5, so y=9y = 9y=9. Verify: 5(9)=455(9) = 455(9)=45.

Reason:
This step is essential because the solution of an equation is the value of the variable alone, so making the coefficient 1 gives the direct answer in its simplest form.

📖 Explanation: The action of dividing both sides by 7 is an application of the Multiplication Property of Equality (specifically, multiplying by 1/71/71/7). The primary goal is to transform the coefficient of the variable from 7 to 1, thereby isolating the variable to find its value.
