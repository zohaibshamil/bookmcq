What is Equations with fraction coefficients?

Definition:
Equations with fraction coefficients have variables multiplied by fractions, such as \(\frac{2}{3}x = 8\) or \(\frac{1}{4}y + 2 = 5\). These are solved by multiplying both sides by the reciprocal of the fraction coefficient or by clearing all fractions using the LCD.

Working:
For \(\frac{3}{4}x = 9\), multiply both sides by the reciprocal \(\frac{4}{3}\): \(x = 9 \cdot \frac{4}{3} = 12\). Alternatively, multiply by the LCD of all denominators if there are multiple terms.

Example:
Solve \(\frac{2}{5}m = 14\). Multiply both sides by \(\frac{5}{2}\): \(m = 14 \cdot \frac{5}{2} = 35\). Check: \(\frac{2}{5}(35) = 14\).

Reason:
This method directly isolates the variable when it has a fractional multiplier, avoiding complex fraction operations and providing a clean solution.

📝 All Equations with fraction coefficients MCQs

Q1. Which value of \(x\) satisfies \( \frac{3}{4}x - \frac{1}{2} = 7 \)?

A. 8
B. 9
C. 10 ✅
D. 11

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Adding \(\frac{1}{2}\) to both sides gives \(\frac{3}{4}x = \frac{15}{2}\). Multiplying by \(\frac{4}{3}\) produces \(x = 10\). The key idea is to preserve equality while eliminating the fractional coefficient.

Q2. An equation is written as \( \frac{2}{3}x + 5 = 17 \). Which first step most efficiently removes the fraction without changing the solution?

A. Multiply only \(x\) by 3
B. Subtract 5 from both sides
C. Multiply every term by 3 ✅
D. Divide both sides by \(\frac{2}{3}\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Multiplying every term by 3 clears the denominator while preserving equality: \(3(\frac{2}{3}x+5) = 3(17)\). This gives \(2x+15 = 51\), making the remaining solution process simpler.

Q3. A student solves \( \frac{5}{6}x - 4 = 11 \) by multiplying only the fraction term by 6, obtaining \(5x - 4 = 11\). Why is this reasoning invalid?

A. The coefficient should be divided by 6
B. The constant term must also be multiplied by 6 ✅
C. The variable cannot have a fractional coefficient
D. The right side should be divided by 6 instead

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

When multiplying an equation by 6, every term on both sides must be multiplied by 6. The correct transformation is \(5x - 24 = 66\). Multiplying only one term changes the equation and therefore can change its solution.

Q4. A recipe uses \( \frac{3}{4} \) cup of sugar per batch. A baker has 6 cups and uses 2 cups for another recipe. If \(b\) represents the number of complete batches possible, which equation correctly models the situation?

A. \(3b - 2 = 6\)
B. \(\frac{3}{4}b + 2 = 6\)
C. \(\frac{3}{4}b = 8\)
D. \(\frac{3}{4}b = 6 - 2\) ✅

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

After reserving 2 cups, \(6-2=4\) cups remain. Each batch requires \(\frac{3}{4}\) cup, so the model is \(\frac{3}{4}b = 4\). Solving gives \(b = \frac{16}{3}\), meaning 5 complete batches can actually be made.

Q5. A student claims that \( \frac{1}{2}(x-6) = 10 \) has solution \(x = 14\). Which reasoning identifies the error most precisely?

A. The student should subtract 6 after multiplying
B. The student should divide 10 by \(\frac{1}{2}\), giving \(x-6=5\)
C. The factor \(\frac{1}{2}\) must be distributed first, giving \(\frac{x}{2}-3=10\) ✅
D. The student forgot that negative numbers cannot occur

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Distributing gives \(\frac{x}{2}-3 = 10\), so \(\frac{x}{2} = 13\) and \(x = 26\). Alternatively, multiplying both sides by 2 gives \(x-6 = 20\), again producing \(x=26\).

Q6. A taxi company charges a fixed fee of $3 plus \( \frac{5}{2} \) dollars per kilometer. A passenger pays $18. Which equation can be used to find the distance \(d\), and what is \(d\)?

A. \(3 + \frac{5}{2}d = 18; d = 6\) ✅
B. \(3 + \frac{5}{2}d = 18; d = 7.5\)
C. \(5 + \frac{3}{2}d = 18; d = 8\)
D. \(\frac{5}{2}(d+3) = 18; d = 4.2\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The fixed fee is $3 and the distance charge is \(\frac{5}{2}d\), so \(3 + \frac{5}{2}d = 18\). Subtracting 3 gives \(\frac{5}{2}d = 15\), and multiplying by \(\frac{2}{5}\) gives \(d = 6\) kilometers.

Q7. Consider the equation \( \frac{4}{5}x + 2 = 10 \). Which sequence of operations correctly isolates \(x\)?

A. Subtract 2, then multiply by \(\frac{4}{5}\)
B. Subtract 2, then multiply by \(\frac{5}{4}\) ✅
C. Multiply by \(\frac{4}{5}\), then subtract 2
D. Divide by 5, then multiply by 4

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

First subtract 2 from both sides to obtain \(\frac{4}{5}x = 8\). Since \(x\) is multiplied by \(\frac{4}{5}\), multiply both sides by its reciprocal \(\frac{5}{4}\). Therefore \(x = 10\).

Q8. A graph of \(y = \frac{2}{3}x + 4\) and the horizontal line \(y = 10\) intersect at one point. What \(x\)-coordinate should the intersection have?

A. 6
B. 8
C. 9 ✅
D. 12

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

At the intersection, the two \(y\)-values are equal, so \(\frac{2}{3}x + 4 = 10\). Subtracting 4 gives \(\frac{2}{3}x = 6\). Multiplying by \(\frac{3}{2}\) gives \(x = 9\), so the intersection has \(x\)-coordinate 9.

Q9. Two students solve \( \frac{3}{4}x + 2 = \frac{1}{2}x + 8 \). Student A multiplies the entire equation by 4 first. Student B subtracts \(\frac{1}{2}x\) first. Which statement is correct?

A. Only Student A can obtain the correct solution
B. Only Student B can obtain the correct solution
C. Both methods are valid if every operation is applied to both sides ✅
D. Neither method is valid because fractions cannot be removed

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Both approaches preserve equality. Student A obtains \(3x+8=2x+32\), while Student B obtains \(\frac{1}{4}x+2=8\). Both lead to \(x=24\), demonstrating that different valid sequences can produce the same solution.

Q10. A student transforms \( \frac{7}{8}x - 3 = \frac{1}{4}x + 9 \) into \(7x - 3 = 2x + 9\) by multiplying by 8. What should the corrected equation be, and what solution follows?

A. \(7x - 24 = 2x + 72, x = 19.2\) ✅
B. \(7x - 3 = 2x + 9, x = 2\)
C. \(7x - 24 = 2x + 9, x = 6.6\)
D. \(7x - 24 = 2x + 72, x = 16\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Multiplying the original equation by 8 requires multiplying every term, including the constants. Thus \(7x - 24 = 2x + 72\). Subtracting \(2x\) gives \(5x = 96\), so \(x = \frac{96}{5} = 19.2\).

Q11. A number is increased by \( \frac{2}{3} \) of itself, and the result is 40. Another student argues that the number must be 24 because \( \frac{2}{3} \times 40 = 24 \). What is the actual number?

A. 15
B. 20
C. 24 ✅
D. 30

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Let the number be \(x\). The statement means \(x + \frac{2}{3}x = 40\), not \(\frac{2}{3}(40) = x\). Combining terms gives \(\frac{5}{3}x = 40\), and multiplying by \(\frac{3}{5}\) yields \(x = 24\). Therefore option C is actually the correct value, while the distractor reasoning happens to reach it by coincidence.

Q12. What is the most useful first step for solving \( \frac{x}{3} + \frac{5}{6} = 4 \) by clearing fractions?

A. Multiply every term by 6 ✅
B. Multiply only the fractions by 6
C. Multiply every term by 3
D. Add the denominators 3 and 6

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The denominators are 3 and 6, so their least common denominator is 6. Multiplying every term by 6 removes both fractions while preserving equality, producing a simpler linear equation.
