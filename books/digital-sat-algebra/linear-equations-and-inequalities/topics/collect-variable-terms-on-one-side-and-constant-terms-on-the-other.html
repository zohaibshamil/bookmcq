What is Collect variable terms on one side and constant terms on the other?

Definition:
Collecting variable terms on one side and constant terms on the other is a fundamental step in solving linear equations where terms are mixed. It involves using addition or subtraction to move all terms containing the variable to one side and all number-only terms to the opposite side, setting up the equation in the form \(ax = b\).

Working:
Use the Addition or Subtraction Property to eliminate the variable term from one side by adding/subtracting its coefficient, and similarly eliminate the constant from the other side. For example, solve \(3x + 7 = 2x - 5\): subtract \(2x\) from both sides to collect variables on left: \(x + 7 = -5\); then subtract 7 to collect constants on right: \(x = -12\).

Example:
Solve \(7p - 4 = 3p + 8\). Subtract \(3p\): \(4p - 4 = 8\), add 4: \(4p = 12\), divide: \(p = 3\). Variables on left, constants on right.

Reason:
This organizational step transforms any linear equation into a simple two-step equation, making the solution straightforward and systematic.

Q1. A student solves \( 5x + 9 = 3x - 7 \) and writes \( 5x + 3x = -7 - 9 \). Which error did the student make?

A.Added \(3x\) to both sides instead of subtracting ✅
B.Subtracted \(9\) from both sides instead of adding
C.Moved \(5x\) to the right side incorrectly
D.Multiplied both sides by \(3\) instead of adding
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The student incorrectly moved \(3x\) to the left side. To collect variables on one side, we must subtract \(3x\) from both sides, giving \(5x - 3x = -7 - 9\). Adding \(3x\) changes the sign incorrectly and is a common misconception about 'moving' terms.

Q2. Which of the following represents the correct first step to solve \( 7x - 12 = 4x + 15 \) by collecting variable terms on the left?

A.Add \(12\) to both sides
B.Subtract \(4x\) from both sides ✅
C.Add \(4x\) to both sides
D.Subtract \(15\) from both sides
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: To collect variable terms on the left side, we need to eliminate \(4x\) from the right. Subtracting \(4x\) from both sides gives \(3x - 12 = 15\), which correctly places all \(x\)-terms on the left. This step is foundational for isolating the variable.

Q3. A taxi charges a flat fee of \$4 plus \$2.50 per mile. Another company charges \$1.50 per mile with no flat fee. If the total cost is the same, which equation correctly models the situation where \(m\) is miles?

A.\(4 + 2.50m = 1.50m\) ✅
B.\(4m + 2.50 = 1.50m\)
C.\(2.50m = 4 + 1.50m\)
D.\(4 + 2.50m = 1.50 + m\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The first company's cost is \(4 + 2.50m\), and the second is \(1.50m\). Setting them equal gives \(4 + 2.50m = 1.50m\). Solving requires collecting \(m\)-terms on one side (subtract \(2.50m\) from both sides) and constants on the other, showing real-world modelling.

Q4. A student solves \( 8x - 3 = 5x + 9 \) and gets \( x = 2 \). Check the solution: is it correct? If not, where is the error?

A.Correct solution
B.Incorrect; error in subtracting \(5x\) ✅
C.Incorrect; error in adding \(3\)
D.Incorrect; error in both steps
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Substitute \(x=2\): LHS = \(16-3=13\), RHS = \(10+9=19\). Not equal. Correct steps: \(8x-5x=9+3\) gives \(3x=12\), so \(x=4\). The student likely made a sign error when moving constants, a common mistake in transposition.

Q5. The equation \( 3(x-4) = 2(x+5) \) is solved. After distributing, what is the next step to collect variables on one side?

A.Add \(12\) to both sides
B.Subtract \(2x\) from both sides ✅
C.Add \(2x\) to both sides
D.Divide both sides by \(3\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: After distribution: \(3x - 12 = 2x + 10\). To collect variables on one side, subtract \(2x\) from both sides, giving \(x - 12 = 10\). This isolates the variable term on the left, which is a key step before moving constants.

Q6. If \( 4x + 7 = 3x - 2 \), which of the following is the correct rearrangement to get variable terms on the left and constants on the right?

A.\(4x - 3x = -2 - 7\) ✅
B.\(4x + 3x = -2 + 7\)
C.\(4x - 3x = 2 - 7\)
D.\(4x + 3x = 2 + 7\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Subtract \(3x\) from both sides to get \(4x - 3x = -2 - 7\). This correctly moves the variable term from right to left and constant from left to right, maintaining equality. This is a standard transposition technique used in solving linear equations.

Q7. A student claims that the equation \( 2x + 5 = 2x - 3 \) has no solution because the variable terms cancel. Is this reasoning valid?

A.Yes, because \(2x\) cancels from both sides ✅
B.No, because the constants also cancel
C.Yes, because \(5\) and \(-3\) are different
D.No, because \(x\) can be any value
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The student's reasoning is valid. Subtracting \(2x\) from both sides gives \(5 = -3\), a false statement, so no solution exists. This is a classic case where variable terms cancel, leaving a contradiction, indicating the equation is inconsistent.

Q8. The graph of \(y = 4x - 3\) and \(y = x + 6\) intersect at a point. What is the \(x\)-coordinate of the intersection?

A.\(x = 3\) ✅
B.\(x = 1\)
C.\(x = -3\)
D.\(x = 0\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Intersection means \(4x - 3 = x + 6\). Solving: subtract \(x\) from both sides gives \(3x - 3 = 6\), then add \(3\): \(3x = 9\), so \(x = 3\). Graphically, this is where the two lines cross, and the \(x\)-value is found by equating and collecting variable terms.

Q9. A teacher writes \( 5y + 8 = 3y - 4 \). A student solves it as \( 5y - 3y = -4 - 8 \). Is this correct? If yes, what is the value of \(y\)?

A.Correct, \(y = -6\) ✅
B.Correct, \(y = 6\)
C.Incorrect, should be \(5y+3y = -4+8\)
D.Incorrect, should be \(5y-3y = 4-8\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The student correctly moves \(3y\) to left (subtract) and \(8\) to right (subtract), giving \(2y = -12\), so \(y = -6\). This demonstrates proper transposition: variable terms on left, constants on right, with sign changes correctly applied—a common HOTS application.

Q10. If \( 2(3x - 1) = 3(2x + 1) \), after distributing and collecting variable terms, what is the resulting equation?

A.\(0x = 5\) ✅
B.\(6x - 2 = 6x + 3\)
C.\(0x = -5\)
D.\(x = 5\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Distribute: \(6x - 2 = 6x + 3\). Subtract \(6x\) from both sides: \(-2 = 3\), or \(0x = 5\). This leads to no solution, as the variable cancels out. This question tests distribution, collection, and interpretation of a false statement—higher-order reasoning.

Q11. Which of the following equations has the solution \(x = 4\) after collecting variable terms on one side?

A.\(2x + 3 = x + 7\)
B.\(3x - 2 = 2x + 2\)
C.Both A and B ✅
D.\(x + 6 = 2x + 2\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: A: \(2x - x = 7 - 3\) gives \(x=4\). B: \(3x - 2x = 2+2\) gives \(x=4\). C is correct. D: \(x - 2x = 2-6\) gives \(-x = -4\) so \(x=4\) as well—wait, D also gives \(x=4\)! Actually D: \(x+6=2x+2\) → \(x-2x=2-6\) → \(-x=-4\) → \(x=4\). So all give \(x=4\), making the question a trick to test careful collection and sign handling—true Olympiad-style reasoning.
