What is Solving for a variable in two variable equation?

Definition:
Solving for a variable in a two-variable equation means expressing one variable explicitly in terms of the other. This is common in linear equations like \(2x + 3y = 12\). To solve for \(y\), isolate it: \(3y = 12 - 2x\), then \(y = (12 - 2x)/3\).

Working:
Given \(4x - 5y = 20\), to solve for \(x\), add \(5y\): \(4x = 20 + 5y\), divide by 4: \(x = (20 + 5y)/4\). To solve for \(y\), subtract \(4x\): \(-5y = 20 - 4x\), divide by -5: \(y = (4x - 20)/5\).

Example:
For \(3a + 2b = 18\), solve for \(a\): subtract \(2b\): \(3a = 18 - 2b\), divide by 3: \(a = (18 - 2b)/3\).

Reason:
This is foundational for graphing linear equations and substitution methods in systems of equations.

Q1. Which expression correctly solves \(3x+2y=14\) for \(y\)?

A.\(y=7-3x\)
B.\(y=7-\frac{3}{2}x\)
C.\(y=\frac{14-3x}{2}\)
D.Both B and C are equivalent ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: To isolate \(y\), subtract \(3x\) from both sides to obtain \(2y=14-3x\), then divide by 2. This gives \(y=7-\frac{3}{2}x\), which is algebraically identical to \(\frac{14-3x}{2}\).

Q2. For \(5x-y=12\), what is the most efficient expression for \(y\) in terms of \(x\)?

A.\(y=5x-12\) ✅
B.\(y=12-5x\)
C.\(y=5x+12\)
D.\(y=\frac{12}{5}-x\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Adding \(y\) to both sides and subtracting 12 gives \(5x-12=y\). Therefore \(y=5x-12\). The other choices result from reversing a sign, adding instead of subtracting, or incorrectly dividing by 5.

Q3. A student claims that \(4x+6y=18\) becomes \(y=3-4x\). Which reasoning best evaluates the claim?

A.It is correct because \(18\div6=3\)
B.It is incorrect because \(4x\) must first be subtracted before dividing by 6 ✅
C.It is incorrect because 18 should be divided by 4 first
D.It is correct because coefficients can be divided independently
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The coefficient of \(x\) must remain attached to \(x\) when it is moved. Subtracting \(4x\) gives \(6y=18-4x\), and dividing every term by 6 produces \(y=3-\frac{2}{3}x\), not \(3-4x\).

Q4. Which form of \(7x-4y=20\) makes the value of \(y\) easiest to determine when \(x\) is known?

A.\(y=\frac{7x-20}{4}\) ✅
B.\(y=\frac{20-7x}{4}\)
C.\(y=20-7x\)
D.\(y=\frac{20+7x}{4}\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Moving \(7x\) to the other side gives \(-4y=20-7x\). Dividing by \(-4\) changes both signs, yielding \(y=\frac{7x-20}{4}\). Wait carefully: this shows A is actually equivalent to the correct form, so the correct answer is A.

Q5. A line is described by \(2x+5y=25\). If \(x\) increases from 0 to 5, how does \(y\) change?

A.\(y\) increases by 2
B.\(y\) decreases by 2 ✅
C.\(y\) decreases by 5
D.\(y\) increases by 5
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Solving gives \(y=5-\frac{2}{5}x\). When \(x\) increases by 5, the term \(\frac{2}{5}x\) increases by 2, so \(y\) must decrease by 2. This connects algebraic isolation with the relationship between variables.

Q6. A theater charges \(x\) dollars for an adult ticket and \(y\) dollars for a student ticket. A group buys 3 adult and 4 student tickets for 54 dollars. Which equation correctly expresses the student price \(y\) in terms of \(x\)?

A.\(y=54-3x\)
B.\(y=\frac{54-3x}{4}\) ✅
C.\(y=\frac{54+3x}{4}\)
D.\(y=\frac{3x-54}{4}\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The total-cost equation is \(3x+4y=54\). Subtracting \(3x\) gives \(4y=54-3x\), and dividing by 4 gives \(y=\frac{54-3x}{4}\). This models the situation while preserving the meaning of each variable.

Q7. A rectangle has perimeter 46 units, with length \(x\) and width \(y\). Which expression represents \(y\) in terms of \(x\), and what width results when \(x=15\)?

A.\(y=46-2x; y=16\)
B.\(y=23-x; y=8\) ✅
C.\(y=23-2x; y=-7\)
D.\(y=\frac{46-x}{2}; y=15.5\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The perimeter equation is \(2x+2y=46\). Dividing by 2 gives \(x+y=23\), so \(y=23-x\). Substituting \(x=15\) gives \(y=8\), which is a realistic positive width.
