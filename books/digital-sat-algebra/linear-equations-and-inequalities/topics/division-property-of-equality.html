What is Division Property of Equality?

Definition:
The Division Property of Equality states that if you divide both sides of an equation by the same nonzero number, the equality is maintained. Formally, if \(a = b\) and \(c \neq 0\), then \(a/c = b/c\). It is specifically used to eliminate multiplication by a constant.

Working:
When you have an equation like \(4x = 20\), divide both sides by 4 to isolate \(x\): \(4x/4 = 20/4\), resulting in \(x = 5\). The division cancels out the coefficient 4.

Example:
Solve \(9z = 63\) using Division Property. Divide both sides by 9: \(9z/9 = 63/9\), so \(z = 7\). Check: \(9(7) = 63\).

Reason:
This property is the inverse of multiplication, allowing us to undo multiplication and solve for the variable when it has a coefficient.

📝 All Division Property of Equality MCQs

Q1. If \( -7x = 42 \), what is the value of \( x \) based on the division property of equality?

A.\( x = -6 \) ✅
B.\( x = 6 \)
C.\( x = -7 \)
D.\( x = 294 \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The division property states that if you divide both sides of an equation by the same nonzero number, the equality remains true. Here, dividing both sides by \(-7\) gives \( x = -6 \). It is a direct application.

Q2. Which equation correctly represents the statement: 'A number multiplied by 4 gives 28. Find the number.'?

A.\( 4x = 28 \) ✅
B.\( x/4 = 28 \)
C.\( 4 + x = 28 \)
D.\( 4 - x = 28 \)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Translating a verbal statement to an algebraic equation requires understanding that 'multiplied by' indicates multiplication. \( 4x = 28 \) is the correct model, and solving by division gives \( x = 7 \). The other options change the operation.

Q3. A taxi charges a fixed fare of \( \$5 \) plus \( \$2 \) per mile. If the total fare is \( \$25 \), which equation and solution correctly model the situation?

A.\( 2m + 5 = 25 \); \( m = 10 \) ✅
B.\( 2m = 25 \); \( m = 12.5 \)
C.\( 5m + 2 = 25 \); \( m = 4.6 \)
D.\( 2m + 5 = 25 \); \( m = 15 \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The fixed cost is added to the variable cost. So \( 2m + 5 = 25 \). Subtracting 5 gives \( 2m = 20 \), then dividing by 2 gives \( m = 10 \). This applies the division property after a subtraction step.

Q4. A student solved \( 3x = 15 \) as \( x = 5 \). Another solved \( -3x = 15 \) as \( x = -5 \). Which statement about their solutions is true?

A.Both are correct because they correctly divided by the coefficient. ✅
B.First is correct, second is incorrect because dividing by a negative changes sign.
C.Both are incorrect because division property doesn’t apply to negative numbers.
D.First is incorrect, second is correct because positive divided by positive.
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The division property applies regardless of sign. For \( 3x = 15 \), dividing by 3 gives 5. For \( -3x = 15 \), dividing by \(-3\) gives \(-5\). Both students correctly applied the property, showing understanding that sign matters in the result.

Q5. If the graph of \( y = 5x \) passes through the point where \( y = 20 \), what is the x-coordinate? Interpret using division property.

A.\( x = 4 \) ✅
B.\( x = 100 \)
C.\( x = 15 \)
D.\( x = 0.25 \)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: On the graph, \( y = 20 \) means \( 5x = 20 \). Dividing both sides by 5 gives \( x = 4 \). This represents the input value that produces the output 20 on the line, linking graphical representation with algebraic solution.

Q6. Compare the two solution methods for \( 8x = 64 \): Method A divides by 8, Method B multiplies by \( \frac{1}{8} \). Which is correct?

A.Both are correct and equivalent ✅
B.Only Method A is correct
C.Only Method B is correct
D.Neither is correct
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Dividing by 8 and multiplying by its reciprocal \( \frac{1}{8} \) are equivalent operations. Both yield \( x = 8 \). This question tests the conceptual understanding that division is multiplication by the reciprocal.

Q7. What is the solution of \( \frac{x}{-4} = 12 \)? Note: Division property is used differently here.

A.\( x = -48 \) ✅
B.\( x = -3 \)
C.\( x = 48 \)
D.\( x = 3 \)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Here \( x \) is divided by \(-4\). To isolate \( x \), multiply both sides by \(-4\) (which is the inverse operation, since division property can be extended). \( x = -48 \). This tests if students recognize that division property can be applied inversely.

Q8. A physics student knows that Force = mass × acceleration, or \( F = ma \). If \( F = 120 \) N and \( a = 15 \) m/s², what is the mass?

A.\( m = 8 \) kg ✅
B.\( m = 1800 \) kg
C.\( m = 105 \) kg
D.\( m = 135 \) kg
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Using \( F = ma \), substitute values: \( 120 = 15m \). Dividing both sides by 15 gives \( m = 8 \). This models real-world application of division property in a scientific formula.

Q9. A student writes \( 0x = 5 \) and claims division property gives \( x = 5 \). What is the error?

A.Division by zero is undefined, so no solution ✅
B.Division by zero gives zero, so \( x = 0 \)
C.Zero divided by zero is 1, so \( x = 1 \)
D.It is correct because any number divided by 0 is 0
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The division property requires dividing by a nonzero number. Since the coefficient is 0, dividing both sides by 0 is undefined. The equation \( 0x = 5 \) has no solution. This identifies the critical misconception about division by zero.

Q10. Given the equation \( 4x + 8 = 24 \), a student subtracts 8 first to get \( 4x = 16 \), then divides by 4 to get \( x = 4 \). What property is used in each step?

A.Subtraction property, then division property ✅
B.Division property, then subtraction property
C.Only division property used twice
D.Only subtraction property used
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: This question requires identifying two different properties. Subtracting 8 uses the subtraction property of equality; dividing by 4 uses the division property. It tests the sequence and conceptual understanding of multiple properties.

Q11. If \( -2x = -18 \), which of the following is the result of dividing both sides by -2?

A.\( x = 9 \) ✅
B.\( x = -9 \)
C.\( x = 36 \)
D.\( x = -36 \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Dividing both sides by \(-2\) gives \( x = \frac{-18}{-2} = 9 \). The signs cancel, producing a positive result. This is a straightforward application of the division property.

Q12. Which of the following is NOT a valid use of the division property of equality?

A.Dividing both sides by 2 in \( 2x = 10 \)
B.Dividing both sides by -3 in \( -3x = 12 \)
C.Dividing both sides by 0 in \( 0x = 0 \) ✅
D.Dividing both sides by 0.5 in \( 0.5x = 7 \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Dividing by zero is invalid in any equation, including \( 0x = 0 \), because division by zero is undefined. Although the equation is true for all \( x \), you cannot divide by 0 to solve it. This is a common error.

Q13. If \( \frac{x}{3} = 7 \), solve for \( x \) using the division property after multiplying both sides by 3. What is the result?

A.\( x = 21 \) ✅
B.\( x = \frac{7}{3} \)
C.\( x = 3 \)
D.\( x = 10 \)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Although the equation involves division, we multiply both sides by 3 to isolate \( x \). This is the inverse operation. The division property is used implicitly because multiplication and division are inverse operations. \( x = 21 \).
