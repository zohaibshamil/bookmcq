What is Linear equations in two variables?

Definition:
Linear equations in two variables are equations that can be written in the form Ax+By=CAx + By = CAx+By=C, where A,B,CA, B, CA,B,C are constants and x,yx, yx,y are variables. They graph as straight lines on a coordinate plane and have infinitely many ordered pair solutions (x,y)(x, y)(x,y) that satisfy them.

Working:
To find solutions, choose a value for one variable and solve for the other. For example, for 2x+y=62x + y = 62x+y=6, if x=1x = 1x=1, then 2(1)+y=62(1) + y = 62(1)+y=6, so y=4y = 4y=4, giving solution (1,4).

Example:
For x−3y=9x - 3y = 9x−3y=9, if y=0y = 0y=0, then x=9x = 9x=9, solution (9,0). If x=0x = 0x=0, then −3y=9-3y = 9−3y=9, so y=−3y = -3y=−3, solution (0, -3).

Reason:
Understanding two-variable equations is key to graphing, systems of equations, and modeling real-world relationships with two unknowns.

Q1. A student solves 6x−3y=216x-3y=216x−3y=21 as follows: 6x−21=3y6x-21=3y6x−21=3y, then y=2x−21y=2x-21y=2x−21. What is the specific error?

A.The student should add 21, not subtract it
B.The student divided only 6x6x6x by 3 and failed to divide 21 by 3 ✅
C.The student should divide by 6 first
D.The student incorrectly changed 3y3y3y into −3y-3y−3y
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: After rearranging, 3y=6x−213y=6x-213y=6x−21. Dividing the entire right side by 3 gives y=2x−7y=2x-7y=2x−7. The student's error is failing to divide the constant 21 by 3, a common misconception when isolating a variable.

Q2. Two students solve 8x+4y=328x+4y=328x+4y=32. Student A gets y=8−2xy=8-2xy=8−2x, while Student B gets y=2x−8y=2x-8y=2x−8. Which conclusion is correct?

A.Student A is correct because the xxx-term is subtracted before division ✅
B.Student B is correct because 4y4y4y is positive
C.Both are correct for different values of xxx
D.Neither is correct because yyy cannot be isolated
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Subtracting 8x8x8x gives 4y=32−8x4y=32-8x4y=32−8x. Dividing by 4 produces y=8−2xy=8-2xy=8−2x. Student B reversed the signs incorrectly. Substituting a simple value such as x=0x=0x=0 confirms that y=8y=8y=8, supporting Student A.

Q3. The graph of a linear equation crosses the yyy-axis at 6 and passes through the point (3,0)(3,0)(3,0). Which equation represents the line in solved-for-yyy form?

A.y=2x+6y=2x+6y=2x+6
B.y=−2x+6y=-2x+6y=−2x+6 ✅
C.y=−12x+6y=-\frac{1}{2}x+6y=−21​x+6
D.y=12x−6y=\frac{1}{2}x-6y=21​x−6
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The yyy-intercept is 6. Using the points (0,6)(0,6)(0,6) and (3,0)(3,0)(3,0), the slope is (0−6)/(3−0)=−2(0-6)/(3-0)=-2(0−6)/(3−0)=−2. Therefore the equation is y=−2x+6y=-2x+6y=−2x+6, matching both graph features.

Q4. A company models its remaining inventory with 4x+y=1204x+y=1204x+y=120, where xxx is the number of boxes sold and yyy is inventory remaining. Which interpretation follows from solving for yyy?

A.y=120+4xy=120+4xy=120+4x, so inventory rises as boxes are sold
B.y=120−4xy=120-4xy=120−4x, so each additional box sold reduces inventory by 4 units ✅
C.y=4x−120y=4x-120y=4x−120, so inventory starts negative
D.y=1204−xy=\frac{120}{4}-xy=4120​−x, so each box reduces inventory by 1
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Rearranging 4x+y=1204x+y=1204x+y=120 gives y=120−4xy=120-4xy=120−4x. The intercept 120 represents the starting inventory, while the coefficient −4-4−4 means every additional box sold reduces the remaining inventory by four units.

Q5. For 3x−2y=123x-2y=123x−2y=12, a learner substitutes x=2yx=2yx=2y and obtains y=6y=6y=6. Is this conclusion valid?

A.Yes, because 3(2y)−2y=123(2y)-2y=123(2y)−2y=12 gives y=6y=6y=6
B.No, because substituting x=2yx=2yx=2y gives 4y=124y=124y=12, so y=3y=3y=3 ✅
C.No, because x=2yx=2yx=2y cannot be substituted into a linear equation
D.Yes, because xxx and yyy must have equal coefficients
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Substituting x=2yx=2yx=2y gives 3(2y)−2y=123(2y)-2y=123(2y)−2y=12, so 6y−2y=126y-2y=126y−2y=12, which simplifies to 4y=124y=124y=12 and therefore y=3y=3y=3. The learner's arithmetic conclusion y=6y=6y=6 is incorrect.

Q6. A line has equation 9x+3y=279x+3y=279x+3y=27. Another student rewrites it as y=9−3xy=9-3xy=9−3x. Which equivalent form correctly preserves the line and best reveals its slope?

A.y=9−3xy=9-3xy=9−3x ✅
B.y=3−3xy=3-3xy=3−3x
C.y=9x−9y=9x-9y=9x−9
D.y=3x−9y=3x-9y=3x−9
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Subtracting 9x9x9x from both sides gives 3y=27−9x3y=27-9x3y=27−9x. Dividing every term by 3 produces y=9−3xy=9-3xy=9−3x. Therefore A is mathematically correct, while B incorrectly divides the constant term. The slope is −3-3−3.
