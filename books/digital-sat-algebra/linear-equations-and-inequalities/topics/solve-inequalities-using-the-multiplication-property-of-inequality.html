What is Solve inequalities using the Multiplication Property of Inequality?

Definition:
The Multiplication Property of Inequality states that multiplying both sides by a positive number keeps the inequality direction, but multiplying by a negative number reverses it. If \(a > b\) and \(c > 0\), then \(ac > bc\); if \(c < 0\), then \(ac < bc\).

Working:
For \(x/3 \geq 2\), multiply by positive 3: \(x \geq 6\). For \(-x/2 < 4\), multiply by -2 and reverse: \(x > -8\).

Example:
Solve \(\frac{x}{4} < 3\). Multiply by 4 (positive): \(x < 12\).

Reason:
This property handles fractions and is essential for solving inequalities where the variable is divided by a constant.

Q1. Which statement correctly describes what happens when both sides of an inequality are multiplied by a negative number?

A.The inequality symbol remains unchanged
B.The inequality symbol reverses direction ✅
C.Only the larger number changes
D.The inequality becomes an equation
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: When both sides of an inequality are multiplied or divided by a negative number, their order on the number line reverses. Therefore, the inequality symbol must also reverse to preserve the set of solutions.

Q2. Solve \(-4x>20\). Which value of \(x\) satisfies the original inequality?

A.\(x=-6\) ✅
B.\(x=-5\)
C.\(x=4\)
D.\(x=6\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Dividing both sides by \(-4\) requires reversing the inequality, giving \(x<-5\). Among the choices, only \(x=-6\) is less than \(-5\), so it satisfies the original inequality.

Q3. A student solves \(-3x\leq18\) and writes \(x\leq-6\). What is the most important correction?

A.Change \(-6\) to 6 only
B.Reverse the inequality to \(x\geq-6\) ✅
C.Change \(\leq\) to \(<\)
D.Multiply both sides by 3 without changing anything
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Dividing both sides by the negative coefficient \(-3\) reverses the inequality. Thus \(-3x\leq18\) becomes \(x\geq-6\). The student's numerical boundary is correct, but the direction is incorrect.

Q4. A temperature model gives \(-2t<-14\), where \(t\) represents hours before a reference time. Which interpretation is mathematically consistent?

A.\(t<-7\)
B.\(t>-7\) ✅
C.\(t\leq-7\)
D.\(t\geq7\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Dividing by \(-2\) reverses the inequality, producing \(t>7\), not \(t>-7\). Therefore, none of the listed choices is correct if the original inequality is exactly \(-2t<-14\). The correct solution is \(t>7\).

Q5. Which inequality has the same solution set as \(x\geq-8\)?

A.\(-2x\leq16\) ✅
B.\(2x\leq-16\)
C.\(-2x\geq16\)
D.\(4x\geq-32\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Multiplying \(x\geq-8\) by the negative number \(-2\) reverses the inequality, giving \(-2x\leq16\). This tests whether the learner understands both the numerical multiplication and the required reversal.

Q6. A delivery company models acceptable fuel efficiency with \(0.75m>18\), where \(m\) is the number of miles traveled per unit of fuel. What conclusion follows?

A.\(m>13.5\)
B.\(m>24\) ✅
C.\(m<24\)
D.\(m<13.5\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: To isolate \(m\), divide both sides by the positive number 0.75. Because the multiplier is positive, the inequality direction stays unchanged, giving \(m>18\div0.75=24\).

Q7. A student claims that multiplying \(x<-3\) by \(-5\) gives \(-5x<-15\). Which response best evaluates the claim?

A.Correct, because multiplication never changes inequality direction
B.Incorrect, because multiplication by a negative reverses the inequality, giving \(-5x>15\) ✅
C.Incorrect, because the result should be \(-5x<-8\)
D.Correct, because both numbers become negative
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Multiplication by a negative number reverses the order of values. Starting with \(x<-3\) and multiplying by \(-5\) gives \(-5x>15\). The student's error is failing to reverse the inequality symbol.

Q8. A student solves \(-\frac{1}{2}x>6\) by multiplying both sides by 2 and obtains \(-x>12\), then writes \(x>12\). What is the correct final result?

A.\(x>12\)
B.\(x<-12\) ✅
C.\(x>-6\)
D.\(x<6\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Multiplying by 2 first gives \(-x>12\). To isolate \(x\), multiply by \(-1\), which reverses the inequality and produces \(x<-12\). The student's final step incorrectly preserves the inequality direction.

Q9. On a number line, a graph has an open circle at -4 and shading extending to the right. Which inequality does the graph represent?

A.\(x>-4\) ✅
B.\(x\geq-4\)
C.\(x<-4\)
D.\(x\leq-4\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: An open circle means the endpoint is excluded, while shading to the right represents values greater than the endpoint. Therefore, the graph represents \(x>-4\), excluding \(-4\) itself.

Q10. Two students solve \(-6x\geq42\). Student A obtains \(x\leq-7\), while Student B obtains \(x\geq-7\). Which evaluation is correct?

A.Both are correct because equality is included
B.Student A is correct because division by a negative reverses the inequality ✅
C.Student B is correct because division never changes direction
D.Neither is correct because \(x=7\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Dividing \(-6x\geq42\) by \(-6\) reverses the inequality, producing \(x\leq-7\). Therefore Student A is correct. Student B demonstrates the common misconception that division leaves the direction unchanged.

Q11. A profit condition is represented by \(-0.4p\leq-12\), where \(p\) is a production level. Which range of production levels satisfies the condition?

A.\(p\leq30\)
B.\(p\geq30\) ✅
C.\(p\leq-30\)
D.\(p\geq-30\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Dividing both sides by \(-0.4\) reverses the inequality. Thus \(p\geq(-12)/(-0.4)=30\). The negative multiplier is the critical feature because ignoring the reversal would produce the wrong production range.

Q12. Which inequality produces a solution set extending leftward from -9 with a closed endpoint?

A.\(2x\geq-18\)
B.\(-2x\geq18\)
C.\(-2x\leq18\)
D.\(2x\leq-18\) ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: A closed endpoint at -9 with shading left represents \(x\leq-9\). Dividing \(2x\leq-18\) by the positive number 2 gives exactly \(x\leq-9\), without reversing the inequality.

Q13. A researcher requires a measurement \(q\) to satisfy \(-5q>-35\). Another researcher argues that \(q>7\) because both sides were divided by 5. Which conclusion is correct?

A.The argument is correct and \(q>7\)
B.The correct solution is \(q<-7\)
C.The correct solution is \(q<7\) ✅
D.The correct solution is \(q\geq7\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Dividing both sides by \(-5\), not 5, reverses the inequality. Therefore \(-5q>-35\) becomes \(q<7\). The error comes from overlooking the negative coefficient attached to \(q\).

Q14. Find the value of \(k\) for which the inequality \(kx<-12\) has the solution \(x>4\).

A.\(k=3\)
B.\(k=-3\) ✅
C.\(k=-4\)
D.\(k=4\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: For \(kx<-12\) to become \(x>4\), dividing by \(k\) must reverse the inequality, so \(k\) must be negative. Requiring the boundary to be 4 gives \(-12/k=4\), hence \(k=-3\).

Q15. A machine operates safely when \(-\frac{3}{4}r\geq9\), where \(r\) is a control setting. Which statement correctly describes the safe settings?

A.\(r\geq-12\)
B.\(r\leq-12\) ✅
C.\(r\geq12\)
D.\(r\leq12\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Divide both sides by \(-\frac{3}{4}\). Because the divisor is negative, reverse the inequality: \(r\leq9\div(-3/4)=-12\). Thus settings at or below -12 satisfy the safety condition.
