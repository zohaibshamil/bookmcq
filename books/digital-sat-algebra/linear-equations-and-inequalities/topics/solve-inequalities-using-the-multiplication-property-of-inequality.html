What is Solve inequalities using the Multiplication Property of Inequality?

Definition:
The Multiplication Property of Inequality states that multiplying both sides by a positive number keeps the inequality direction, but multiplying by a negative number reverses it. If a>ba > ba>b and c>0c > 0c>0, then ac>bcac > bcac>bc; if c<0c < 0c<0, then ac<bcac < bcac<bc.

Working:
For x/3≥2x/3 \geq 2x/3≥2, multiply by positive 3: x≥6x \geq 6x≥6. For −x/2<4-x/2 < 4−x/2<4, multiply by -2 and reverse: x>−8x > -8x>−8.

Example:
Solve x4<3\frac{x}{4} < 34x​<3. Multiply by 4 (positive): x<12x < 12x<12.

Reason:
This property handles fractions and is essential for solving inequalities where the variable is divided by a constant.

Q1. Which statement correctly describes what happens when both sides of an inequality are multiplied by a negative number?

A.The inequality symbol remains unchanged
B.The inequality symbol reverses direction ✅
C.Only the larger number changes
D.The inequality becomes an equation
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: When both sides of an inequality are multiplied or divided by a negative number, their order on the number line reverses. Therefore, the inequality symbol must also reverse to preserve the set of solutions.

Q2. Solve -4x&gt;20. Which value of xxx satisfies the original inequality?

A.x=−6x=-6x=−6 ✅
B.x=−5x=-5x=−5
C.x=4x=4x=4
D.x=6x=6x=6
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Dividing both sides by −4-4−4 requires reversing the inequality, giving x&lt;-5. Among the choices, only x=−6x=-6x=−6 is less than −5-5−5, so it satisfies the original inequality.

Q3. A student solves −3x≤18-3x\leq18−3x≤18 and writes x≤−6x\leq-6x≤−6. What is the most important correction?

📖 Explanation: Dividing both sides by the negative coefficient −3-3−3 reverses the inequality. Thus −3x≤18-3x\leq18−3x≤18 becomes x≥−6x\geq-6x≥−6. The student's numerical boundary is correct, but the direction is incorrect.

Q4. A temperature model gives -2t&lt;-14, where ttt represents hours before a reference time. Which interpretation is mathematically consistent?

Q7. A student claims that multiplying x&lt;-3 by −5-5−5 gives -5x&lt;-15. Which response best evaluates the claim?

📖 Explanation: Multiplication by a negative number reverses the order of values. Starting with x&lt;-3 and multiplying by −5-5−5 gives -5x&gt;15. The student's error is failing to reverse the inequality symbol.
