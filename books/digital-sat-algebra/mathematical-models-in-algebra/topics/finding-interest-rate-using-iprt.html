What is Finding interest rate using I=Prt?

Definition:
Finding the interest rate using I=PrtI = PrtI=Prt involves solving for rrr (rate) when interest earned, principal, and time are known. Rearrange the formula to r=I/(Pt)r = I / (P t)r=I/(Pt). Convert the decimal result to a percentage by multiplying by 100.

Working:
For example, if an investment of \500 earns \75 in 3 years, r=75/(500×3)=75/1500=0.05=5%r = 75 / (500 \times 3) = 75 / 1500 = 0.05 = 5\%r=75/(500×3)=75/1500=0.05=5%.

Example:
Problem: 'A loan of \2000 earned \400 interest over 5 years. Find the rate.' r=400/(2000×5)=400/10000=0.04=4%r = 400 / (2000 \times 5) = 400/10000 = 0.04 = 4\%r=400/(2000×5)=400/10000=0.04=4%.

Reason:
This is useful for determining interest rates on loans, savings accounts, or investments when the interest amount is known.
