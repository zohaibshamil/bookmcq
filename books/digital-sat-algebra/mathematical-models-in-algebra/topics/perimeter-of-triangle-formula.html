What is Perimeter of triangle formula?

Definition:
The perimeter of a triangle is the total distance around the outside of the triangle, calculated by summing the lengths of all three sides, with the formula P=a+b+cP = a + b + cP=a+b+c, where a,b,a, b,a,b, and ccc are the lengths of the sides, and this formula applies to any triangle regardless of its type (scalene, isosceles, or equilateral).

Working:
To find the perimeter, simply measure or know the lengths of the three sides and add them together, ensuring all sides are in the same unit, and for special triangles like equilateral, where all sides are equal, the formula simplifies to P=3sP = 3sP=3s, where sss is the side length, and for isosceles with two equal sides, P=2a+bP = 2a + bP=2a+b.

Example:
If a triangle has side lengths of 5 cm, 7 cm, and 9 cm, its perimeter is P=5+7+9=21P = 5 + 7 + 9 = 21P=5+7+9=21 cm, and if it is an equilateral triangle with side 6 cm, the perimeter is 3×6=183 \times 6 = 183×6=18 cm.

Reason:
The perimeter formula is widely used in real-life applications such as fencing a triangular field, framing a triangular structure, calculating the border length of a triangular garden, and in various construction and design projects.

Q1. A triangle has side lengths 777 cm, 111111 cm, and 151515 cm. What is its perimeter?

A.28 cm
B.31 cm
C.33 cm ✅
D.35 cm
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The perimeter of a triangle is found by adding the lengths of all three sides. Therefore, 7+11+15=337+11+15=337+11+15=33 cm. The other choices result from arithmetic mistakes or incorrectly omitting or altering one of the side lengths.

Q3. Two triangles have perimeters of 363636 cm. Triangle A has sides 101010, 121212, and 141414 cm. Triangle B has two sides of 999 cm and 131313 cm. What must the third side of Triangle B be?

A.12 cm
B.13 cm
C.14 cm ✅
D.15 cm
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Since Triangle B has the same perimeter as Triangle A, its perimeter must be 363636 cm. Its known sides total 9+13=229+13=229+13=22 cm, so the missing side must be 36−22=1436-22=1436−22=14 cm. This also demonstrates that equal perimeters do not require equal side lengths.

Q8. A triangular banner has perimeter 484848 cm. Two sides are 171717 cm and 141414 cm. The manufacturer can use only a third side that is a whole number. Which value is possible?

The student's approach measures only part of the boundary and therefore cannot represent the perimeter.
