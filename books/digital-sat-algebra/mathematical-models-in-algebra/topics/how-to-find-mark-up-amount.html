What is How to find mark up amount?

Definition:
The mark up amount is the monetary difference between the cost price (the amount paid by the seller to acquire or produce an item) and the selling price, representing the profit added to the cost, and it is calculated by subtracting the cost price from the selling price, with the formula Mark Up Amount=Selling Price−Cost Price\text{Mark Up Amount} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{Cost Price}Mark Up Amount=Selling Price−Cost Price.

Working:
To find the mark up amount, simply subtract the cost price from the selling price, ensuring both are in the same units, and if the mark up is given as a percentage of cost, then the mark up amount can also be found by multiplying the cost price by the mark up rate, such that Mark Up Amount=Cost Price×Mark Up Rate\text{Mark Up Amount} = \text{Cost Price} \times \text{Mark Up Rate}Mark Up Amount=Cost Price×Mark Up Rate.

Example:
If a retailer buys a television for \<span class="katex-error" title="ParseError: KaTeX parse error: Can&#x27;t use function &#x27;' in math mode at position 5: 300 \̲)̲ and sells it f…" style="color:#cc0000">300 \) and sells it for \</span>420\</span>420\</span>420, the mark up amount is 420 - 300 = \<span class="katex-error" title="ParseError: KaTeX parse error: Can&#x27;t use function &#x27;' in math mode at position 5: 120 \̲)̲, meaning the r…" style="color:#cc0000">120 \), meaning the retailer adds \</span>120\</span>120\</span>120 to the cost to cover expenses and profit.

Reason:
Calculating the mark up amount is essential for businesses to determine profit margins, set competitive prices, and ensure financial viability, as well as for consumers to understand the cost structure of products.
