What is Translate English sentences into algebraic equations?

Definition:
Translating English sentences into algebraic equations is the process of converting verbal statements into mathematical expressions using variables, operations, and equality signs. Key words: 'is' or 'equals' becomes \(=\), 'sum' becomes \(+\), 'difference' becomes \(-\), 'product' becomes \(\times\), and 'quotient' becomes \(\div\).

Working:
For 'The difference between a number and 8 is 4', write \(x - 8 = 4\). Solve: \(x = 12\). For 'Five times a number plus 3 is 28', write \(5x + 3 = 28\), solve: \(5x = 25\), \(x = 5\).

Example:
Translate 'A number divided by 6 equals 9' to \(x/6 = 9\). Multiply by 6: \(x = 54\).

Reason:
This is the cornerstone of solving word problems, as it bridges the gap between language and mathematics.

Q1. Which algebraic equation correctly represents the sentence: "Seven less than three times a number is 20"?

A. \(3x-7=20\) ✅
B. \(7-3x=20\)
C. \(3(x-7)=20\)
D. \(3x+7=20\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The phrase "three times a number" translates to \(3x\), while "seven less than" means subtract 7 from that quantity. Therefore, the correct translation is \(3x-7=20\). The other choices reverse the subtraction or incorrectly distribute the phrase.

Q2. A teacher says, "The sum of a number and 12 is twice the number." Which equation best models the statement?

A. \(x+12=x/2\)
B. \(x+12=2x\) ✅
C. \(12x=2+x\)
D. \(2(x+12)=x\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The word "sum" indicates addition, so a number \(x\) and 12 give \(x+12\). The phrase "is twice the number" means this sum equals \(2x\). Hence \(x+12=2x\) accurately preserves the relationship.

Q3. A parking lot charges a fixed entry fee plus Rs. 50 per hour. A driver pays Rs. 350 after \(h\) hours. Which equation represents the situation?

A. \(50h+350=0\)
B. \(350h+50=350\)
C. \(50+50h=350\) ✅
D. \(50+350h=350\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The fixed entry fee is represented by 50, while the hourly cost is \(50h\). Since the total payment is Rs. 350, the correct model is \(50+50h=350\). This requires identifying which quantity is fixed and which depends on time.

Q4. A rectangle has a length that is 4 cm greater than its width. Its perimeter is 36 cm. If the width is \(w\), which equation correctly models the situation?

A. \(w+(w+4)=36\)
B. \(2w+4=36\)
C. \(2[w+(w+4)]=36\) ✅
D. \(2w+4w=36\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

If the width is \(w\), the length is \(w+4\). A rectangle's perimeter includes two lengths and two widths, so the total is \(2[w+(w+4)]\). Setting this equal to 36 produces the correct equation.

Q5. A student translates "Five times the difference between a number and 8 is 45" as \(5x-8=45\). What is the best assessment of the student's equation?

A. It is correct because multiplication always comes before subtraction.
B. It is incorrect because the correct equation is \(5(x-8)=45\). ✅
C. It is incorrect because the correct equation is \(x-5(8)=45\).
D. It is correct because \(5x-8\) means five times the difference.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The phrase "difference between a number and 8" creates the grouped quantity \(x-8\). The phrase "five times" applies to the entire difference, so parentheses are required. Thus \(5(x-8)=45\), not \(5x-8=45\).

Q6. A school club has 8 more members than another club. Together they have 54 members. If \(x\) represents the smaller club's membership, which equation should be used?

A. \(x+(x+8)=54\) ✅
B. \(8x=54\)
C. \(x+(x-8)=54\)
D. \(2x+54=8\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The smaller club has \(x\) members, so the larger club has \(x+8\) members. Because their combined membership is 54, adding the two expressions gives \(x+(x+8)=54\). This model correctly connects the verbal relationship to the total.

Q7. A phone plan costs Rs. 600 per month plus Rs. 8 for each additional gigabyte used. One month the bill is Rs. 792. If \(g\) is the number of additional gigabytes, which equation represents the bill?

A. \(8(g+600)=792\)
B. \(600g+8=792\)
C. \(600+8g=792\) ✅
D. \(600-8g=792\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The monthly charge of Rs. 600 is fixed, while Rs. 8 is added for every additional gigabyte. Therefore, the variable charge is \(8g\), and the total bill is \(600+8g=792\). The other choices confuse fixed and variable quantities.

Q8. A number is increased by 25% and then the result is 10 greater than the original number. Which equation correctly expresses this relationship using \(x\) for the original number?

A. \(x+25=10x\)
B. \(1.25x=x+10\) ✅
C. \(25x=x+10\)
D. \(1.25+x=10\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Increasing \(x\) by 25% produces \(x+0.25x=1.25x\). The statement says this new value is 10 greater than the original, so it equals \(x+10\). Therefore, \(1.25x=x+10\) correctly models both steps.

Q9. A graph shows a line crossing the \(y\)-axis at 6 and passing through \((2,14)\). Which verbal statement could reasonably be represented by its equation?

A. A number multiplied by 4 is 6
B. A starting amount of 6 increases by 4 for each unit ✅
C. A starting amount of 4 increases by 6 for each unit
D. Six less than four times a number is 14

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The graph has \(y\)-intercept 6, representing an initial amount of 6. From \((0,6)\) to \((2,14)\), the change is 8 over 2 units, giving a rate of 4. Thus \(y=6+4x\), matching the second statement.

Q10. A student hears: "Eight more than twice a number equals five less than three times the number." Which equation should the student write before solving?

A. \(2x+8=3x-5\) ✅
B. \(8x+2=5x-3\)
C. \(2(x+8)=3(x-5)\)
D. \(2x-8=3x+5\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

"Twice a number" is \(2x\), and "eight more than" gives \(2x+8\). On the other side, "three times the number" is \(3x\), and "five less than" gives \(3x-5\). Therefore, \(2x+8=3x-5\).

Q11. A worker receives a base payment of Rs. 900 and a bonus of Rs. 75 for every completed task. After completing some tasks, the total payment is Rs. 2,100. Which equation and interpretation are both correct?

A. \(900+75t=2100\), where \(t\) is completed tasks ✅
B. \(75+900t=2100\), where \(t\) is completed tasks
C. \(900t+75=2100\), where \(t\) is the bonus
D. \(75(t+900)=2100\), where \(t\) is completed tasks

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The base payment remains Rs. 900 regardless of the number of tasks. Each task adds Rs. 75, represented by \(75t\). Therefore, total earnings are \(900+75t\), and setting this equal to Rs. 2,100 correctly models the situation.

Q12. A student says the sentence "The difference between twice a number and 9 is equal to 15" must be written as \(2(x-9)=15\). What error has the student made?

A. They treated 2 as an addition instead of multiplication.
B. They incorrectly applied 2 to both terms inside the difference. ✅
C. They should have divided 15 by 9 first.
D. They reversed the entire equation.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The expression "twice a number" is \(2x\), and then 9 is subtracted from that result. Therefore, the correct equation is \(2x-9=15\). Writing \(2(x-9)\) incorrectly makes 9 part of the quantity being doubled.

Q13. Two consecutive integers have a sum of 91. If \(x\) represents the smaller integer, which equation correctly models the situation and why is it better than \(2x+1=91\)?

A. \(x+(x+1)=91\), because consecutive integers differ by exactly 1 ✅
B. \(x+(x+2)=91\), because two integers are involved
C. \(2x+1=91\), because the second integer is \(x+1\)
D. \(x(x+1)=91\), because consecutive numbers are multiplied

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

If \(x\) is the smaller consecutive integer, the next integer must be \(x+1\). Their sum is therefore \(x+(x+1)\). Although \(2x+1=91\) happens to simplify to the same equation, the explicit form better represents the original relationship.
