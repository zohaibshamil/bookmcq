What is How to Solve Percent Applications?

Definition:
Solving percent applications involves using the percent equation: Part=Percent×Whole\text{Part} = \text{Percent} \times \text{Whole}Part=Percent×Whole, often written as P=R⋅BP = R \cdot BP=R⋅B (Part = Rate × Base). Convert percentages to decimals (e.g., 25% = 0.25) and solve for the unknown using algebra.

Working:
To find 20% of 50, write P=0.20×50=10P = 0.20 \times 50 = 10P=0.20×50=10. To find what percent 15 is of 60, write 15=R×6015 = R \times 6015=R×60, so R=15/60=0.25=25%R = 15/60 = 0.25 = 25\%R=15/60=0.25=25%. To find the whole when 12 is 40%, write 12=0.40×B12 = 0.40 \times B12=0.40×B, so B=12/0.40=30B = 12/0.40 = 30B=12/0.40=30.

Example:
Problem: 'What is 15% of 200?' P=0.15×200=30P = 0.15 \times 200 = 30P=0.15×200=30.

Reason:
Percent applications are widely used in finance, statistics, and everyday situations like discounts, taxes, and grades.
