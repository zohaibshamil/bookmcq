What is Work hours inequality problems?

Definition:
Work hours inequality problems involve constraints on the number of hours worked, where the total hours must be within a certain range (e.g., minimum and maximum), expressed using inequalities like Minimum≤Hours≤Maximum\text{Minimum} \le \text{Hours} \le \text{Maximum}Minimum≤Hours≤Maximum, and these are common in labor laws, shift scheduling, and project management to ensure fair and legal working conditions.

Working:
To solve, define the variable for hours, set up the inequality based on given limits (e.g., at least 20 hours" means ≥20\ge 20≥20 and "at most 40 hours" means ≤40\le 40≤40) combine into a compound inequality and solve for the variable; if there are multiple workers or days use sums to model total hours and round to appropriate values.

Example:
A part-time employee can work between 15 and 25 hours per week; if they already worked 10 hours and xxx hours remain then 15≤10+x≤2515 \le 10 + x \le 2515≤10+x≤25 so 5≤x≤155 \le x \le 155≤x≤15 meaning they can work between 5 and 15 more hours this week.

Reason:
Work hours inequalities are essential for ensuring compliance with labor regulations fair scheduling and efficient time management and they help both employers and employees plan work hours effectively."
