What is How Use a Problem Solving Strategy?

Definition:
A problem-solving strategy is a systematic step-by-step approach used to tackle word problems and real-world scenarios in algebra. It typically involves: 1) Read and understand the problem, 2) Assign a variable to the unknown, 3) Translate the situation into an equation, 4) Solve the equation, and 5) Check if the answer is reasonable in the original context.

Working:
This strategy works by breaking down complex sentences into manageable parts, identifying relationships between quantities, and converting those relationships into algebraic expressions. For example, if a problem states 'the sum of a number and 5 is 12', we assign xxx as the number, write x+5=12x + 5 = 12x+5=12, solve to get x=7x = 7x=7, and verify by substitution.

Example:
Problem: 'A number increased by 9 gives 20.' Use the strategy: read, let xxx be the number, translate to x+9=20x + 9 = 20x+9=20, subtract 9: x=11x = 11x=11. Check: 11+9=2011 + 9 = 2011+9=20, correct.

Reason:
This structured approach reduces confusion, minimizes errors, and ensures that all aspects of the problem are considered, making it essential for solving application problems.

Q1. A school plans to buy notebooks for 240240240 students. Each student needs 333 notebooks, but the school already has 185185185 usable notebooks. If notebooks are sold in packs of 202020, which strategy correctly determines the minimum number of packs needed?

A.Multiply 240240240 by 333, subtract 185185185, then divide by 202020 and round down
B.Multiply 240240240 by 333, subtract 185185185, then divide by 202020 and round up ✅
C.Add 240240240, 333, and 185185185, then divide by 202020
D.Subtract 240240240 from 185185185, then divide the result by 202020
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A sound problem-solving strategy first identifies the total requirement, subtracts the available supply, and then accounts for whole packages. Since a partial package cannot be purchased, the quotient must be rounded upward. This sequence preserves the meaning of each quantity.
