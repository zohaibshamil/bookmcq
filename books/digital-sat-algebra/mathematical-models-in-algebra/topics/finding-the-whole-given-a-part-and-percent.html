What is Finding the Whole Given a Part and Percent?

Definition:
Finding the whole given a part and percent means determining the total amount when you know a portion and its corresponding percentage. Use the formula Whole=Part/Percent (as decimal)\text{Whole} = \text{Part} / \text{Percent (as decimal)}Whole=Part/Percent (as decimal). For example, if 24 is 40% of a number, find the number: 24/0.40=6024 / 0.40 = 6024/0.40=60.

Working:
Step 1: Convert the percent to a decimal. Step 2: Divide the part by this decimal. For example, '15 is 25% of what number?' 15/0.25=6015 / 0.25 = 6015/0.25=60.

Example:
Problem: '18 is 30% of what number?' 18/0.30=6018 / 0.30 = 6018/0.30=60. The whole is 60.

Reason:
This is essential for finding original values from discounts, tax calculations, or determining total populations from sample data.

D.0.30+18=x0.30+18=x0.30+18=x, because the whole combines the percent and part
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The percentage relationship is part = percent × whole. Since 30% is 0.300.300.30 and the known part is 18, the correct model is 0.30x=180.30x=180.30x=18. Solving this equation identifies the unknown whole.

Q2. A school reports that 42 students represent 35% of the students enrolled in a program. Without immediately calculating, which estimate is most reasonable for the total enrollment?

A.About 60
B.About 90
C.About 120 ✅
D.About 150
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Because 35% is slightly more than one-third, the whole must be slightly less than three times 42. Three times 42 is 126, so an answer near 120 is reasonable. The other choices are inconsistent with the given percentage.

Q7. A graph shows four bars representing portions of different groups: Group A has 18 units at 30%, Group B has 24 units at 40%, Group C has 21 units at 35%, and Group D has 32 units at 50%. Which group has the largest whole?

B.Group B
C.Group C
D.Group D ✅

📖 Explanation: For each group, divide the known part by its percentage: A gives 18÷0.30=6018\div0.30=6018÷0.30=60, B gives 24÷0.40=6024\div0.40=6024÷0.40=60, C gives 21÷0.35=6021\div0.35=6021÷0.35=60, and D gives 32÷0.50=6432\div0.50=6432÷0.50=64. Therefore, Group D actually has the largest whole, making option D correct.
