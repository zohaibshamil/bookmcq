What is Translate and solve basic percent equations?

Definition:
Translating and solving basic percent equations means converting percent statements like 'What is 30% of 80?' into the equation P=R⋅BP = R \cdot BP=R⋅B, where PPP is part, RRR is rate (decimal), and BBB is base. Then solve for the unknown using multiplication or division.

Working:
For 'What percent of 40 is 10?' write 10=R×4010 = R \times 4010=R×40, solve R=10/40=0.25=25%R = 10/40 = 0.25 = 25\%R=10/40=0.25=25%. For '20% of what number is 14?' write 14=0.20×B14 = 0.20 \times B14=0.20×B, so B=14/0.20=70B = 14/0.20 = 70B=14/0.20=70.

Example:
Problem: 'Find 40% of 120.' Equation: P=0.40×120=48P = 0.40 \times 120 = 48P=0.40×120=48.

Reason:
This provides a direct method to handle any percent question, making it a versatile tool for percentage calculations.
