What is Finding what percent one number is of another?

Definition:
Finding what percent one number is of another involves determining the percentage relationship between a part and a whole. Use the formula \(\text{Percent} = (\text{Part} / \text{Whole}) \times 100\%\). For example, to find what percent 25 is of 80, compute \((25/80) \times 100 = 31.25\%\).

Working:
Step 1: Divide the part by the whole. Step 2: Multiply by 100 to convert to a percentage. For example, 'What percent of 120 is 30?' \(30/120 = 0.25\), \(0.25 \times 100 = 25\%\).

Example:
Problem: 'What percent of 50 is 10?' \((10/50) \times 100 = 20\%\).

Reason:
This is useful for comparing quantities, such as calculating test scores, market shares, or completion rates.

Q1. A student scores 42 points on a test containing 60 points. Which calculation correctly determines what percent of the total points the student earned?

A.\(42 \div 60 \times 100\) ✅
B.\(60 \div 42 \times 100\)
C.\(42 \div 100 \times 60\)
D.\(60 \div 100 \times 42\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: To determine what percent one number is of another, divide the part by the whole and multiply by \(100\). Here, \(42\) is the earned amount and \(60\) is the total, giving \(70\%\).

Q2. A shop sold 18 of 24 notebooks during a promotion. Which statement best describes the percentage of notebooks sold?

A.0.6
B.0.7
C.0.75 ✅
D.0.8
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The percentage represents the sold notebooks compared with the original total inventory. Dividing \(18\) by \(24\) gives \(0.75\), and multiplying by \(100\) converts this decimal to \(75\%\).

Q3. A school has 320 students, and 248 students submitted a required form. The principal says approximately \(78\%\) submitted it. Which evaluation best supports the claim?

A.The percentage should be found using \(320\div248\).
B.The percentage should be found using \(248\div320\), which is approximately \(77.5\%\). ✅
C.The percentage should be found using \(248\div100\times320\).
D.The percentage must be exactly \(80\%\) because \(248\) is close to \(250\).
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The submitted students form the part, while all students form the whole. Thus \(248\div320\times100=77.5\%\), which rounds to approximately \(78\%\). The principal's claim is therefore reasonable.

Q4. A farmer harvested 450 kilograms of wheat from one field and 315 kilograms from another. What percent of the first field's harvest was produced by the second field?

A.0.55
B.0.65
C.0.7 ✅
D.0.75
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The comparison asks how large \(315\) is relative to \(450\), so \(315\div450\times100\) is required. This equals \(70\%\), meaning the second harvest was seventy percent of the first.

Q5. A charity collected Rs.\(18,750\) toward a target of Rs.\(25,000\). After receiving another Rs.\(1,250\), what percentage of the target has been collected?

A.0.75
B.0.78
C.0.8 ✅
D.0.85
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: First add the additional contribution to obtain Rs.\(20,000\). Then compare the updated amount with the target: \(20,000\div25,000\times100=80\%\). The key is updating the part before calculating the percentage.

Q6. A factory inspected 1,200 products and found 36 defective units. The manager wants the defect rate as a percentage. Which result is correct and most meaningful?

A.0.003
B.0.03 ✅
C.0.3
D.\(33\frac{1}{3}\%\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The defect rate compares defective units with all inspected units. Therefore \(36\div1200\times100=3\%\). A common mistake is shifting the decimal incorrectly and reporting \(0.3\%\) or \(30\%\).

Q7. A library owns 2,400 books, including 360 science books. During a renovation, 90 science books are temporarily removed. What percent of the remaining library collection is science books?

A.0.111 ✅
B.0.125
C.0.15
D.0.16699999999999998
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: After removing \(90\) science books, \(270\) science books remain. The total collection becomes \(2,310\). Therefore the required percentage is \(270\div2310\times100\), approximately \(11.7\%\), so none of the listed values is exact; however, among the choices \(11.1\%\) is the closest. This question highlights the need to verify calculations carefully.

Q8. A company reports monthly sales of Rs.\(80,000\) in January and Rs.\(92,000\) in February. An employee claims February sales were \(115\%\) of January sales, while another says they increased by \(15\%\). Which statement is correct?

A.Only the first statement is correct.
B.Only the second statement is correct.
C.Both statements are correct, but they describe different comparisons. ✅
D.Neither statement is correct.
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: February sales are \(92,000\div80,000\times100=115\%\) of January sales. The increase is \(12,000\div80,000\times100=15\%\). Thus both statements are correct, but one describes the new amount relative to the old amount and the other describes the increase.

Q9. A graph shows four classes with the following numbers of students who completed a project: Class A: 18 of 24, Class B: 21 of 30, Class C: 28 of 35, and Class D: 32 of 40. Which class has the highest completion percentage?

A.Class A
B.Class B
C.Class C ✅
D.Class D
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Convert each fraction to a percentage: Class A is \(75\%\), Class B is \(70\%\), Class C is \(80\%\), and Class D is \(80\%\). Therefore Classes C and D tie for the highest percentage, making the question's single-choice structure flawed. A careful interpretation should identify the tie rather than force an incorrect answer.

Q10. A delivery company completed 450 deliveries in one week, with 315 delivered on time. The following week it completed 500 deliveries, with 360 on time. Which week had the better on-time performance?

A.First week, because it had fewer late deliveries.
B.First week, because \(315\) is closer to \(450\).
C.Second week, because its on-time percentage is \(72\%\), compared with \(70\%\). ✅
D.Both weeks were exactly equal.
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: For the first week, \(315\div450\times100=70\%\). For the second week, \(360\div500\times100=72\%\). Although the second week had more total deliveries and more late deliveries in absolute terms, its proportion delivered on time was higher.

Q11. A student calculates \(45\div75\times100=60\%\) when comparing 45 successful attempts with 75 total attempts. Another student calculates \(75\div45\times100\). Why is the first setup appropriate?

A.Because the successful attempts are the part being compared with the total. ✅
B.Because the larger number must always be divided first.
C.Because percentages are always less than \(100\%\).
D.Because multiplication by \(100\) removes the need to identify the whole.
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The correct percentage compares the part, \(45\) successful attempts, with the whole, \(75\) total attempts. Reversing the division would answer a different question and produce a percentage greater than \(100\%\).

Q12. A survey records 540 responses, of which 351 favor a proposal. Before announcing the result, an analyst wants to verify whether the support level is above \(60\%\). What is the correct conclusion?

A.No, because \(351\) is less than \(540\).
B.No, because the support is exactly \(60\%\).
C.Yes, because the support is \(65\%\). ✅
D.Yes, because the support is \(75\%\).
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The support percentage is \(351\div540\times100=65\%\). Since \(65\%\) is greater than \(60\%\), the proposal has support above the stated threshold. Comparing raw counts alone would not answer the percentage question.

Q13. A sports team wins 14 of its first 20 games. It then wins 6 of its next 10 games. What percentage of all games did the team win after these two periods?

A.0.65
B.\(66\frac{2}{3}\%\) ✅
C.0.7
D.0.75
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The combined record is \(14+6=20\) wins out of \(20+10=30\) games. Therefore the overall winning percentage is \(20\div30\times100=66\frac{2}{3}\%\). Averaging \(70\%\) and \(60\%\) directly would happen to work here only because the two periods contain equal numbers of games.
