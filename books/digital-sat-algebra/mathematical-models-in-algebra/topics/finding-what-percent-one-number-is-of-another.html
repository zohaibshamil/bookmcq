What is Finding what percent one number is of another?

Definition:
Finding what percent one number is of another involves determining the percentage relationship between a part and a whole. Use the formula Percent=(Part/Whole)×100%\text{Percent} = (\text{Part} / \text{Whole}) \times 100\%Percent=(Part/Whole)×100%. For example, to find what percent 25 is of 80, compute (25/80)×100=31.25%(25/80) \times 100 = 31.25\%(25/80)×100=31.25%.

Working:
Step 1: Divide the part by the whole. Step 2: Multiply by 100 to convert to a percentage. For example, 'What percent of 120 is 30?' 30/120=0.2530/120 = 0.2530/120=0.25, 0.25×100=25%0.25 \times 100 = 25\%0.25×100=25%.

Example:
Problem: 'What percent of 50 is 10?' (10/50)×100=20%(10/50) \times 100 = 20\%(10/50)×100=20%.

Reason:
This is useful for comparing quantities, such as calculating test scores, market shares, or completion rates.
