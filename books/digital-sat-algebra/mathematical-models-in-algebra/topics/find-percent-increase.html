What is Find percent increase?

Definition:
Percent increase measures the relative growth from an original amount to a new, larger amount. The formula is \(\text{Percent Increase} = \frac{\text{New Amount} - \text{Original Amount}}{\text{Original Amount}} \times 100\%\). It quantifies how much a quantity has grown.

Working:
If a price rises from $50 to $65, the increase is \(65 - 50 = 15\). Percent increase = \((15/50) \times 100 = 30\%\).

Example:
Problem: 'A population grew from 200 to 250. Find the percent increase.' Increase = 50, so \((50/200) \times 100 = 25\%\).

Reason:
Percent increase is widely used in economics, population studies, and business to track growth rates.

📝 All Find percent increase MCQs

Q1. A quantity changes from 80 to 100. Which expression correctly represents the percent increase?

A. \(20/100\times100\%\)
B. \(20/80\times100\%\) ✅
C. \(80/100\times100\%\)
D. \(100/20\times100\%\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Percent increase compares the change with the original amount, not the final amount. The change is \(100-80=20\), and dividing by the original \(80\) gives \(20/80=0.25\), or \(25\%\).

Q2. A town's population rises from 24,000 to 30,000. A student says the increase is \(20\%\) because \(6,000\) is \(20\%\) of \(30,000\). What is the correct interpretation?

A. The increase is \(15\%\)
B. The increase is \(20\%\)
C. The increase is \(25\%\) ✅
D. The increase is \(30\%\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The absolute increase is \(30,000-24,000=6,000\). Percent increase must use the original population as the reference, so \(6,000/24,000\times100\%=25\%\), not \(20\%\).

Q3. A monthly electricity bill increases from Rs. 4,500 to Rs. 5,175. Before calculating, which estimate best predicts the percent increase?

A. About \(5\%\)
B. About \(10\%\)
C. About \(15\%\) ✅
D. About \(25\%\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The increase is Rs. \(675\). Since \(675\) is approximately \(15\%\) of Rs. \(4,500\), the exact percent increase is \(675/4500\times100\%=15\%\). Estimation therefore supports the result.

Q4. A machine originally produces 160 units per hour. After an adjustment, it produces 200 units per hour. Which statement best explains the percentage change?

A. Production increased by \(20\%\) because 40 is \(20\%\) of 200.
B. Production increased by \(25\%\) because 40 is \(25\%\) of 160. ✅
C. Production increased by \(40\%\) because the output rose by 40 units.
D. Production increased by \(125\%\) because 200 is \(125\%\) of 160.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The output change is \(200-160=40\) units. Because percentage increase is measured relative to the original output, calculate \(40/160\times100\%=25\%\). The final output being \(125\%\) of the original does not mean the increase is \(125\%\).

Q5. A store raises the price of a backpack from Rs. 2,400 to Rs. 2,760. The manager wants the percentage increase for a report. What should be reported?

A. \(12\%\)
B. \(15\%\) ✅
C. \(18\%\)
D. \(20\%\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The price increase is Rs. \(360\). Dividing the change by the original price gives \(360/2400=0.15\). Multiplying by \(100\%\) gives a \(15\%\) increase. Using the final price as the denominator would produce a misleading percentage.

Q6. A company's online orders rise from 1,200 in January to 1,500 in February and then to 1,800 in March. Which statement correctly compares the two monthly percent increases?

A. Both increases are \(25\%\).
B. January-to-February is \(25\%\), while February-to-March is \(20\%\). ✅
C. January-to-February is \(20\%\), while February-to-March is \(25\%\).
D. Both increases are \(20\%\).

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

From January to February, the increase is \(300\), so \(300/1200=25\%\). From February to March, the increase is also \(300\), but the original comparison value is now \(1,500\), giving \(300/1500=20\%\). Equal absolute increases can produce different percentages.

Q7. A water tank holds 600 liters initially and later contains 690 liters. A technician computes \(90/690\times100\%\). What is wrong with the calculation, and what is the correct result?

A. Nothing is wrong; the increase is about \(13.0\%\).
B. The denominator should be 600; the increase is \(15\%\). ✅
C. The denominator should be 690; the increase is \(15\%\).
D. The change should be \(690+600\); the increase is \(215\%\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The technician used the final amount as the reference. Percent increase compares the change with the starting amount. Since the increase is \(690-600=90\), the correct calculation is \(90/600\times100\%=15\%\).

Q8. A student's score improves from 64 to 76. The student reasons: "The score increased by 12 points, so the percent increase is \(12\%\)." Which response best evaluates the reasoning?

A. Correct, because every point equals one percent.
B. Incorrect, because \(12\) must be divided by the original score \(64\). ✅
C. Incorrect, because \(12\) must be divided by the new score \(76\).
D. Correct, because the final score is less than 100.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The student correctly identifies the absolute increase as \(12\), but percentage increase requires comparison with the original value. Thus \(12/64\times100\%=18.75\%\). The number of percentage points and percent increase are different ideas.

Q9. A graph shows a company's sales rising from Rs. 2.0 million at Point A to Rs. 2.5 million at Point B. Without relying on the visual height alone, what percent increase does the data represent?

A. \(20\%\)
B. \(25\%\) ✅
C. \(50\%\)
D. \(125\%\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The graph's values give an increase of Rs. \(0.5\) million. Relative to the original Rs. \(2.0\) million, the increase is \(0.5/2.0=0.25\), so the percent increase is \(25\%\). The vertical appearance of a graph can be misleading.

Q10. A company's number of employees changes from 320 to 400. Another department changes from 500 to 600. Which department has the larger percent increase?

A. The first department, because it gained 80 employees. ✅
B. The second department, because it gained 100 employees.
C. Both departments have the same percent increase.
D. There is not enough information to compare them.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The first department increases by \(80/320\times100\%=25\%\). The second increases by \(100/500\times100\%=20\%\). Although the second department gains more employees in absolute terms, the first experiences the greater proportional increase.

Q11. A farmer's crop yield increases from 2,400 kg to 3,000 kg after using a new method. The farmer pays an additional Rs. 12,000 for the method. Which pair correctly describes the yield increase and the additional cost per extra kilogram?

A. \(20\%\) and Rs. 10 per kg
B. \(25\%\) and Rs. 20 per kg ✅
C. \(25\%\) and Rs. 12 per kg
D. \(30\%\) and Rs. 20 per kg

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The yield increases by \(600\) kg, and \(600/2400\times100\%=25\%\). The additional cost per extra kilogram is \(12,000/600=\) Rs. 20. Solving both quantities requires distinguishing percent increase from unit cost.

Q12. A graph records website visitors as 8,000 in Week 1, 9,600 in Week 2, and 11,520 in Week 3. Which conclusion is supported by the data?

A. Visitors increased \(20\%\) each week.
B. Visitors increased \(20\%\) from Week 1 to Week 2 and \(20\%\) from Week 2 to Week 3. ✅
C. Visitors increased \(16\%\) each week.
D. The second increase is larger because the graph rises more steeply.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

From Week 1 to Week 2, the increase is \(1,600\), and \(1,600/8,000=20\%\). From Week 2 to Week 3, the increase is \(1,920\), and \(1,920/9,600=20\%\). The absolute increases differ, but the proportional increases are equal.

Q13. A product's price increases from Rs. 800 to Rs. 920. A second product's price increases from Rs. 1,500 to Rs. 1,710. Which product experienced the greater percent increase, and by how many percentage points?

A. The first by \(1\) percentage point
B. The second by \(1\) percentage point
C. The first by \(2\) percentage points ✅
D. Both are equal

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The first product increases by Rs. \(120\), giving \(120/800\times100\%=15\%\). The second increases by Rs. \(210\), giving \(210/1500\times100\%=14\%\). Therefore, the first product's percent increase is larger by \(1\) percentage point, not \(2\).

Q14. A researcher reports that a measurement rose from 50 units to 65 units. One method calculates the increase as \(65/50\times100\%=130\%\), while another calculates \((65-50)/50\times100\%=30\%\). Which method correctly describes the percent increase?

A. The first method, because 65 is the final value.
B. The second method, because it measures the change relative to the original value. ✅
C. Both methods, because they describe the same percentage.
D. Neither method, because percent increase cannot exceed \(100\%\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The first calculation shows that the final value is \(130\%\) of the original, not that it increased by \(130\%\). Percent increase specifically measures the added amount, \(65-50=15\), relative to 50, giving \(30\%\).
