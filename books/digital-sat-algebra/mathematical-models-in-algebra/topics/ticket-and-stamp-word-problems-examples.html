What is Ticket and stamp word problems examples?

Definition:
Ticket and stamp word problems are algebraic applications where the total cost of tickets or stamps is calculated by summing the products of the number of each type and their respective prices, often using a table to organize the information, and these problems are solved by setting up equations based on the total number of items and the total cost.

Working:
To solve ticket and stamp problems, create a table with rows for each type (e.g., adult/child tickets, 3-cent/5-cent stamps), columns for quantity, price per item, and total cost, then define variables for unknowns, use the relationship Quantity×Price=Total Cost\text{Quantity} \times \text{Price} = \text{Total Cost}Quantity×Price=Total Cost, and form two equations: one for the total number of items and one for the total cost, then solve the system using substitution or elimination.
