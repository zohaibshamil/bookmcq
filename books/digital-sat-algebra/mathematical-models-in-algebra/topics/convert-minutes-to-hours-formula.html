What is Convert minutes to hours formula?

Definition:
Converting minutes to hours is essential when working with rate-time problems, as rates are often given in per hour units, and the formula is Hours=Minutes60\text{Hours} = \frac{\text{Minutes}}{60}Hours=60Minutes​, since there are 60 minutes in an hour, and this conversion ensures that units are consistent for calculations using D=rtD = rtD=rt.

Working:
To convert minutes to hours, divide the number of minutes by 60; for example, 30 minutes is 3060=0.5\frac{30}{60} = 0.56030​=0.5 hours, and 90 minutes is 9060=1.5\frac{90}{60} = 1.56090​=1.5 hours; if you need to convert hours to minutes, multiply by 60, and this conversion is critical when rate is in miles per hour and time is in minutes, as you must convert to hours before multiplying.

Example:
A train travels at 120 miles per hour for 45 minutes; first convert 45 minutes to hours: 4560=0.75\frac{45}{60} = 0.756045​=0.75 hours, then distance is 120×0.75=90120 \times 0.75 = 90120×0.75=90 miles, and if a runner takes 2 hours and 30 minutes, that is 2+3060=2.52 + \frac{30}{60} = 2.52+6030​=2.5 hours.

Reason:
Converting minutes to hours is a necessary skill for accurately solving motion, work, and rate problems, ensuring all units are consistent, which is crucial in science, engineering, and everyday scheduling.
