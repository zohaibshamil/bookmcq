What is Finding unknown numbers in algebra?

Definition:
Finding unknown numbers in algebra involves solving equations where the unknown is represented by a variable. The problem gives a relationship (e.g., sum, difference, product) involving the unknown, and you use algebraic techniques to isolate the variable and determine its value.

Working:
For example, 'A number plus 7 is 20.' Let the number be xxx. Equation: x+7=20x + 7 = 20x+7=20. Subtract 7: x=13x = 13x=13. The unknown number is 13.

Example:
Problem: 'When a number is multiplied by 4, the result is 32.' Let xxx be the number. Equation: 4x=324x = 324x=32. Divide by 4: x=8x = 8x=8.

Reason:
This skill is fundamental because it allows students to solve for missing values in various mathematical and real-world contexts.

Q1. A number is increased by 7 and the result is three times the original number minus 9. Which equation correctly models the situation if xxx represents the unknown number?

A.x+7=3x−9x+7=3x-9x+7=3x−9 ✅
B.x+7=3(x−9)x+7=3(x-9)x+7=3(x−9)
C.7x=3x−97x=3x-97x=3x−9
D.x−7=3x+9x-7=3x+9x−7=3x+9
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The phrase increased by 7 means x+7x+7x+7, while three times the original number minus 9 means 3x−93x-93x−9. Translating both expressions carefully gives x+7=3x−9x+7=3x-9x+7=3x−9, preserving the intended order of operations.

Q5. A number line shows two unknown numbers equally spaced around 25. Their distance from 25 is 9 units on each side. What is the sum of the two unknown numbers?

A.16
B.25
C.41
D.50 ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The two numbers are 25−9=1625-9=1625−9=16 and 25+9=3425+9=3425+9=34. Their sum is 16+34=5016+34=5016+34=50. The equal spacing means the midpoint is 25, so the sum is twice the midpoint, 2(25)=502(25)=502(25)=50.
