What is How to find slope and y-intercept from equation?

Definition:
Finding slope and y-intercept from an equation means rewriting the equation in slope-intercept form \( y = mx + b \), where the coefficient of \( x \) is the slope \( m \) and the constant term is the y-intercept \( b \), and if the equation is already in this form, the values are read directly, while if it is in another form such as standard form \( Ax + By = C \), the equation is solved for \( y \) first, and the resulting coefficient and constant give the slope and y-intercept.

Working:
The procedure works by isolating \( y \) on one side: for \( Ax + By = C \), subtract \( Ax \) from both sides to get \( By = -Ax + C \), then divide by \( B \) to get \( y = -\frac{A}{B}x + \frac{C}{B} \), so the slope is \( m = -\frac{A}{B} \) and the y-intercept is \( b = \frac{C}{B} \); for an equation already in \( y = mx + b \), read \( m \) and \( b \) directly, and the y-intercept point is \( (0, b) \), which can be verified by substituting \( x = 0 \) into the original equation.

Example:
For \( 4x - 2y = 8 \), subtract \( 4x \) to get \( -2y = -4x + 8 \), divide by \( -2 \) to get \( y = 2x - 4 \), so the slope is \( m = 2 \) and the y-intercept is \( b = -4 \); for \( y = -\frac{3}{4}x + 5 \), the slope is \( -\frac{3}{4} \) and the y-intercept is 5, and substituting \( x = 0 \) gives \( y = 5 \), confirming the intercept.

Reason:
The reason for ease of grasping how to find slope and y-intercept from an equation is that a single algebraic step, solving for \( y \), converts any linear equation into a form where the slope and y-intercept are plainly visible, so the same procedure works for every linear equation and always yields the two values needed for graphing and analysis.

Q1. For the equation \(y=4x-7\), which pair correctly identifies the slope and y-intercept?

A.Slope \(4\), y-intercept \(-7\) ✅
B.Slope \(-7\), y-intercept \(4\)
C.Slope \(7\), y-intercept \(4\)
D.Slope \(-4\), y-intercept \(7\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: In slope-intercept form \(y=mx+b\), \(m\) represents the slope and \(b\) represents the y-intercept. Comparing \(y=4x-7\) with this form gives slope \(4\) and y-intercept \(-7\).

Q2. A line is represented by \(y=-\frac{3}{5}x+8\). What are its slope and y-intercept?

A.Slope \(\frac{3}{5}\), y-intercept \(8\)
B.Slope \(-\frac{3}{5}\), y-intercept \(8\) ✅
C.Slope \(8\), y-intercept \(-\frac{3}{5}\)
D.Slope \(-\frac{5}{3}\), y-intercept \(8\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The coefficient of \(x\) in slope-intercept form is the slope, while the constant term is the y-intercept. Therefore, \(m=-\frac{3}{5}\) and \(b=8\).

Q3. A student sees \(y=2-6x\) and says the slope is \(2\) because it appears first. Which interpretation is correct?

A.The slope is \(2\) and the y-intercept is \(-6\)
B.The slope is \(6\) and the y-intercept is \(2\)
C.The slope is \(-6\) and the y-intercept is \(2\) ✅
D.The slope is \(-2\) and the y-intercept is \(6\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The equation can be rearranged as \(y=-6x+2\), making the roles clear. The coefficient of \(x\) is \(-6\), so that is the slope, while the constant \(2\) is the y-intercept.

Q4. Two equations are given: \(y=5x+1\) and \(y=5x-4\). Without graphing, what relationship between their graphs can be predicted?

A.They have the same y-intercept but different slopes
B.They have the same slope but different y-intercepts ✅
C.They have opposite slopes and the same y-intercept
D.They have both the same slope and y-intercept
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Both equations have the same coefficient of \(x\), namely \(5\), so their slopes are equal. Their constant terms are \(1\) and \(-4\), so their y-intercepts differ, producing distinct parallel lines.

Q5. A delivery company's cost is modeled by \(C=12d+75\), where \(d\) is distance in kilometers. What do \(12\) and \(75\) represent?

A.\(12\) is the starting cost and \(75\) is the cost per kilometer
B.\(12\) is the cost per kilometer and \(75\) is the starting cost ✅
C.Both values represent costs per kilometer
D.\(12\) represents distance and \(75\) represents the slope
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Writing the model as \(C=12d+75\) matches \(y=mx+b\). Thus \(12\) is the slope, meaning cost increases by \(12\) units per kilometer, while \(75\) is the initial cost when distance is zero.

Q6. A line is given by \(3x+2y=14\). Which pair gives its slope and y-intercept after solving for \(y\)?

A.Slope \(\frac{3}{2}\), y-intercept \(7\)
B.Slope \(-\frac{3}{2}\), y-intercept \(7\) ✅
C.Slope \(-2\), y-intercept \(14\)
D.Slope \(3\), y-intercept \(-7\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Solving \(3x+2y=14\) for \(y\) gives \(2y=-3x+14\), so \(y=-\frac{3}{2}x+7\). Therefore the slope is \(-\frac{3}{2}\) and the y-intercept is \(7\).

Q7. A savings model is \(S=25w+300\), where \(w\) is the number of weeks. What does the slope tell you, and what does the y-intercept tell you?

A.Savings start at \(25\) and increase by \(300\) each week
B.Savings increase by \(25\) per week and begin at \(300\) ✅
C.Savings decrease by \(25\) per week and begin at \(300\)
D.Savings increase by \(300\) per week and begin at \(25\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: In \(S=25w+300\), the coefficient \(25\) is the rate of change, so savings increase by \(25\) units each week. The constant \(300\) is the initial savings when \(w=0\).

Q8. A student converts \(4x-2y=10\) into \(y=2x-5\) and concludes the slope is \(-2\) because the original \(x\)-coefficient was positive. What is the error?

A.The y-intercept should be \(5\), not \(-5\)
B.The slope should be \(4\), because \(x\) remains unchanged
C.The student must first isolate \(y\), which changes the slope to \(2\) ✅
D.The equation has no slope because it is not originally written with \(y\) alone
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: From \(4x-2y=10\), subtracting \(4x\) gives \(-2y=10-4x\). Dividing by \(-2\) gives \(y=2x-5\). Therefore the slope is \(2\), not \(-2\).

Q9. A graph of a line crosses the y-axis at \(6\) and falls \(2\) units for every 1-unit increase in \(x\). Which equation matches the graph?

A.\(y=2x+6\)
B.\(y=-2x+6\) ✅
C.\(y=6x-2\)
D.\(y=-6x+2\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The y-axis crossing gives the y-intercept \(b=6\). Because the line falls \(2\) units whenever \(x\) increases by \(1\), its slope is \(-2\). Substituting these values into \(y=mx+b\) gives \(y=-2x+6\).

Q10. A temperature model is \(T=-3h+28\), where \(h\) represents hours after a measurement begins. What can be concluded from the equation?

A.Temperature starts at \(-3\) and rises \(28\) degrees per hour
B.Temperature starts at \(28\) and decreases \(3\) degrees per hour ✅
C.Temperature starts at \(3\) and decreases \(28\) degrees per hour
D.Temperature starts at \(28\) and rises \(3\) degrees per hour
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The constant \(28\) is the y-intercept, representing the temperature at \(h=0\). The coefficient \(-3\) is the slope, showing that temperature decreases by \(3\) degrees for every additional hour.

Q11. Which equation represents a line with slope \(-4\) and y-intercept \(9\), and also has a negative value of \(y\) when \(x=3\)?

A.\(y=-4x+9\) ✅
B.\(y=4x-9\)
C.\(y=-9x+4\)
D.\(y=4x+9\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The required slope and y-intercept directly produce \(y=-4x+9\). Checking \(x=3\) gives \(y=-12+9=-3\), which is negative. The other equations do not satisfy both required features.

Q12. Two students analyze \(6x-3y=12\). Student A says the slope is \(6\) and y-intercept is \(-4\). Student B says the slope is \(2\) and y-intercept is \(-4\). Who is correct?

A.Student A only
B.Student B only ✅
C.Both students
D.Neither student
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Rearranging gives \(-3y=12-6x\), then \(y=2x-4\). Thus the slope is \(2\) and the y-intercept is \(-4\). Student B correctly accounts for division by \(-3\) when isolating \(y\).

Q13. A line has equation \(2y+8x=-16\). Another line has equation \(y=-4x+5\). What can be concluded about their slopes and y-intercepts?

A.They have equal slopes and equal y-intercepts
B.They have different slopes but equal y-intercepts
C.They have equal slopes but different y-intercepts ✅
D.They have opposite slopes and different y-intercepts
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The first equation becomes \(y=-4x-8\), so its slope is \(-4\) and y-intercept is \(-8\). The second has slope \(-4\) and y-intercept \(5\), so slopes match while intercepts differ.

Q14. For a line written as \(y=mx+b\), suppose increasing \(m\) from \(2\) to \(5\) while keeping \(b=-3\) unchanged. Which statement describes the resulting change?

A.The line keeps the same y-intercept but becomes steeper ✅
B.The line keeps the same slope but moves upward
C.The line changes its y-intercept from \(-3\) to \(5\)
D.The line becomes horizontal while keeping the same y-intercept
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The value \(b\) determines where the line crosses the y-axis, so keeping \(b=-3\) preserves that intercept. Increasing the slope from \(2\) to \(5\) makes the line rise more rapidly as \(x\) increases.
