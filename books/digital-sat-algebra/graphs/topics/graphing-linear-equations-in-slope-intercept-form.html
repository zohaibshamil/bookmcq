📝 Graphing Linear Equations in Slope-Intercept Form (36 MCQs)

The Mathematical Structure of Slope-Intercept Form

Graphing a linear equation in slope-intercept form represents the complete set of ordered pairs satisfying an equation of the form \(y=mx+b\). This representation belongs to coordinate geometry and the study of linear functions. The coefficient \(m\) specifies the constant rate of change, while \(b\) identifies the vertical-axis intercept. Together, these quantities determine the direction and position of a nonvertical straight line, connecting algebraic expressions with geometric representations.

Why Slope and Initial Position Determine a Straight Line

Constructing a graph by repeatedly selecting values and calculating corresponding coordinates requires unnecessary computation when the equation already contains information about its structure. A table can generate points, but it does not immediately identify the line's direction or starting position. Slope-intercept form addresses this limitation by providing both quantities directly. Since a straight line is uniquely determined by two distinct points, graph construction becomes substantially more systematic.

Plotting the Intercept and Applying the Rise-to-Run Ratio

Begin with \(y=mx+b\). Plot the vertical intercept at \((0,b)\). Express the slope as a ratio \(m=\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}\), then use its numerator for vertical movement and denominator for horizontal movement. A positive slope rises toward the right, whereas a negative slope falls. Locate a second point using this ratio and draw the straight line through both coordinates.

Worked Construction of the Graph \(y=2x-3\)

Consider \(y=2x-3\). Its vertical intercept is \(-3\), giving the point \((0,-3)\). Its slope is \(2=\frac{2}{1}\), so move two units upward and one unit right to obtain \((1,-1)\). Draw a line through these points. Substituting \(x=2\) gives \(y=1\), providing another coordinate on the same graph.

Linear Graphs as Models of Constant Rates

Slope-intercept graphs represent relationships involving a fixed initial quantity and a constant rate of change. Examples include a service charge combined with a cost per unit and an account balance increasing through regular deposits. The model assumes that the rate remains constant throughout the interval considered. Situations involving changing rates require a different model, while practical constraints may restrict the permissible values of the independent variable.

Distinguishing Slope, Intercept, and Direction

'The slope is the vertical intercept' confuses the rate of change with the value at \(x=0\); these are separate quantities. 'A slope of \(2\) means moving right two and upward one' reverses rise and run; the correct movement is upward two and right one. 'A negative slope requires a negative intercept' is incorrect because the direction of a line does not determine its vertical starting position.

Interpreting Linear Graphs in Examination Problems

Problems commonly require identifying coefficients from equations, matching equations to graphs, or interpreting changes in slope and intercept. Examine the vertical intercept first, then establish the direction and steepness using the slope. Check negative signs and coordinate scales carefully. Desmos can verify a constructed graph and reveal intersections, although plotting two points manually often provides a more efficient mathematical justification.

Terminology of Slope-Intercept Graphs

  • Slope - vertical change per horizontal change.
  • Y-intercept - graph location where \(x=0\).
  • Rise - vertical displacement between points.
  • Run - horizontal displacement between points.
  • Ordered pair - coordinates identifying a position.
  • Linear function - function with a straight-line graph.

📝 All Graphing Linear Equations in Slope-Intercept Form MCQs

Q1. In the equation \(y=-\frac{3}{4}x+5\), what is the slope of the line?

A.\(5\)
B.\(-\frac{3}{4}\) ✅
C.\(\frac{3}{4}\)
D.\(-5\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: In slope-intercept form, \(y=mx+b\), the coefficient of \(x\) is the slope. Here, \(m=-\frac{3}{4}\). The tempting choice A is the y-intercept, not the rate used to move between points. This coefficient determines each vertical change per horizontal unit.

Q2. What is the y-intercept of the line represented by \(y=6x-7\)?

A.\((0,6)\)
B.-7
C.(0,-7) ✅
D.70
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The y-intercept occurs when \(x=0\), so the point is \((0,-7)\). The tempting choice D reverses the coordinates and gives an x-intercept location, not where the line crosses the vertical axis.

Q3. Which value is the slope of the line \(y=-x+4\)?

A.\(4\)
B.\(1\)
C. \(-4\)
D. \(-1\) ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The coefficient of \(x\) is understood to be \(-1\), so the slope is \(-1\). The tempting choice A is the constant term, which identifies the y-intercept rather than the line's steepness.

Q4. The equation \(y=\frac{3}{5}x\) is in slope-intercept form. At which point does its graph cross the y-axis?

A.\((0,0)\) ✅
B.\((0,\frac{3}{5})\)
C.\((\frac{3}{5},0)\)
D.\((0,5)\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: There is no constant term, so \(b=0\) and the y-intercept is \((0,0)\). The tempting choice B treats the slope as the intercept, even though slope describes change between points. A line with this intercept passes through the origin.

Q5. The equation \(2y=-6x+8\) is rewritten in slope-intercept form. What are its slope and y-intercept?

A.Slope \(-6\); y-intercept \(8\)
B.Slope \(-3\); y-intercept \(4\) ✅
C.Slope \(3\); y-intercept \(4\)
D.Slope \(-3\); y-intercept \(8\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Divide every term by \(2\) to obtain \(y=-3x+4\). Thus, the slope is \(-3\) and the intercept is \((0,4)\). The tempting choice D leaves the constant undivided. Each term must be divided to preserve the equation's balance.

Q6. Which statement correctly describes the graph of \(y=-2x+5\)?

A.It crosses the y-axis at \((0,-2)\).
B.It rises 5 units for each 1-unit move right.
C.It crosses the y-axis at \((0,5)\). ✅
D.It crosses the x-axis at \((5,0)\).
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Setting \(x=0\) gives \(y=5\), so the vertical-axis crossing is \((0,5)\). The tempting choice B confuses the intercept with the slope; the line actually falls 2 units per unit right. That change is measured for every one-unit increase in x.

Q7. A delivery service models its charge by \(C=1.5d+4\), where \(d\) is distance in kilometers and \(C\) is the charge in dollars. What does 4 represent?

A.The charge for each kilometer
B.The charge when the distance is zero ✅
C.The distance covered for 4 dollars
D.The increase in charge for every 4 kilometers
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: At \(d=0\), the model gives \(C=4\), so 4 is the starting charge. The tempting choice A describes the coefficient \(1.5\), which is the additional cost for each kilometer traveled. It is paid even before any distance is traveled.

Q8. The lines \(y=3x+1\) and \(y=3x-4\) have the same slope. How do their graphs differ?

A.They have different slopes but the same intercept.
B.They cross the y-axis at the same point.
C.One is horizontal and the other is vertical.
D.They have the same steepness, but different y-intercepts. ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Both equations have slope \(3\), while their y-intercepts are \(1\) and \(-4\). Their graphs therefore have the same steepness but different vertical starting positions; choice B incorrectly treats the intercepts as equal.

Q9. Two lines are represented by \(y=2x+1\) and \(y=-2x+1\). Which point lies on both graphs?

A.\((0,1)\) ✅
B.\((1,1)\)
C.\((0,2)\)
D.\((1,-1)\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: When \(x=0\), both equations give \(y=1\), so both graphs contain \((0,1)\). The tempting choice B substitutes a nonzero x-value without checking that it satisfies both equations. The y-intercept is the shared point where each graph meets the vertical axis.

Q10. A ride costs a fixed \(\$4\) plus \(\$2\) per mile. If \(x\) is miles traveled and \(y\) is total cost, which description matches the graph?

A.It begins at \((0,2)\) and rises 4 dollars per mile.
B.It begins at \((0,4)\) and rises 2 dollars per mile. ✅
C.It begins at \((4,0)\) and rises 2 dollars per mile.
D.It begins at \((0,4)\) and falls 2 dollars per mile.
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The fixed fee gives the y-intercept \(4\), and the per-mile fee gives slope \(2\), so \(y=2x+4\). Choice A swaps these roles, confusing the starting cost with the rate. This is the amount charged before the ride begins.

Q11. A freezer's temperature is modeled by \(T=18-2.5h\), where \(h\) is hours after it is switched on. What does the slope indicate?

A.The initial temperature is 2.5 degrees.
B.The temperature increases 18 degrees each hour.
C.The temperature decreases 2.5 degrees each hour. ✅
D.The temperature decreases 18 degrees each hour.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Rewriting gives \(T=-2.5h+18\), so the slope is \(-2.5\) degrees per hour. The tempting choice D uses the initial temperature as the rate; 18 is the starting value. The negative sign signals a decrease rather than an increase.

Q12. Which point is on the graph of \(y=2x-3\) when moving 1 unit right from its y-intercept?

A.\((1,3)\)
B.\((1,-3)\)
C.\((1,-1)\) ✅
D.\((2,1)\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The y-intercept is \((0,-3)\). With slope \(2=\frac{2}{1}\), move right 1 and up 2 to reach \((1,-1)\). The tempting choice B moves right without applying the rise. The y-value must change by the slope for that one-unit run.

Q13. The graph of \(y=-\frac{3}{2}x+1\) starts at its y-intercept. What point is reached by moving 2 units right along the line?

A.\((2,4)\)
B.\((2,-2)\) ✅
C.\((-2,-2)\)
D.\((2,1)\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The intercept is \((0,1)\), and slope \(-\frac{3}{2}\) means right 2 and down 3. This reaches \((2,-2)\). The tempting choice A reverses the slope's direction and moves upward. The sign of the slope determines that the vertical movement is downward.

Q14. A student graphs \(y=3x-2\) by plotting \((0,3)\) and then moving right 1 and up 2. Which correction produces the correct graph?

A.Plot \((0,-2)\), then move right 1 and up 3. ✅
B.Plot \((0,3)\), then move right 1 and down 2.
C.Plot \((0,-2)\), then move right 1 and down 3.
D.Plot \((-2,0)\), then move right 3 and up 1.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The student used the slope as the intercept and the intercept as the rise. The correct graph starts at \((0,-2)\) and uses slope \(3=\frac{3}{1}\); choice B still begins incorrectly. The coefficient of x controls rise, not the starting location.

Q15. A student graphs \(y=-\frac{2}{5}x+4\) from \((0,4)\), then moves right 5 and up 2. What should the student do instead?

A.Move left 5 and down 2.
B.Move right 2 and down 5.
C.Move right 5 and down 2. ✅
D.Move left 5 and up 2.
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The slope \(-\frac{2}{5}\) means a run of 5 and a rise of \(-2\). From \((0,4)\), move right 5 and down 2. The tempting choice D reverses both directions. A negative rise requires downward movement when the run is positive.

Q16. A line passes through \((0,-1)\) and \((2,3)\). Which equation represents the line?

A.\(y=2x-1\) ✅
B.\(y=-2x-1\)
C.\(y=2x+1\)
D.\(y=\frac{1}{2}x-1\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The y-intercept is \(-1\), and the slope is \(\frac{3-(-1)}{2-0}=2\). Therefore, \(y=2x-1\). The tempting choice D reverses the rise-to-run ratio. The intercept fixes the starting point, and the slope fixes the direction and steepness.

Q17. A line crosses the y-axis at 4 and passes through \((3,-2)\). Which equation describes the line?

A.\(y=2x+4\)
B.\(y=-2x+4\) ✅
C.\(y=-\frac{1}{2}x+4\)
D.\(y=-2x-4\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Using \((0,4)\) and \((3,-2)\), the slope is \(\frac{-2-4}{3-0}=-2\). Thus, \(y=-2x+4\). The tempting choice C divides the coordinate changes in the wrong order. The intercept supplies the starting value, while the computed slope determines the direction.

Q18. A student claims that the graph of \(y=-4x+6\) crosses the x-axis at \((0,6)\), because 6 is the constant term. What is the correct interpretation?

A.The x-intercept is \((0,6)\), and the y-intercept is \((\frac{3}{2},0)\).
B.Both intercepts are \((0,6)\).
C.The x-intercept is \((6,0)\), and the y-intercept is \((0,-4)\).
D.The y-intercept is \((0,6)\), and the x-intercept is \((\frac{3}{2},0)\). ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The constant 6 gives the y-intercept \((0,6)\). Setting \(y=0\) gives \(4x=6\), so \(x=\frac{3}{2}\). Choice A swaps the intercept labels, the student's central mistake. An x-intercept must have a zero y-coordinate, unlike the y-intercept.

Q19. A water tank contains 25 liters initially and fills at 7 liters per minute. How many liters are in the tank after 3 minutes?

A.\(21\)
B.\(32\)
C.\(46\) ✅
D.\(75\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The model is \(V=7t+25\). Substituting \(t=3\) gives \(V=21+25=46\) liters. The tempting choice A counts only the water added and omits the initial 25 liters. The positive slope represents the tank's constant filling rate over time.

Q20. A worker earns \(\$3\) for each item assembled, with no starting payment. Which statement about the graph of \(E=3x\) is correct?

A.It crosses the y-axis at \((0,3)\).
B.It crosses the origin and rises 3 dollars for each additional item. ✅
C.It crosses the x-axis at \((3,0)\).
D.It decreases 3 dollars for each additional item.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: When no items are assembled, earnings are zero, so the graph passes through the origin. Its slope is 3 dollars per item. Choice A mistakes the rate for the initial payment.

Q21. A graph is represented by the table: \(x=0:y=6\) ; \(x=1:y=4\) ; \(x=2:y=2\) ; \(x=3:y=0\) ; \(x=4:y=-2\). Which equation matches the graph?

A.\(y=2x+6\)
B.\(y=-x+6\)
C.\(y=-2x-6\)
D.\(y=-2x+6\) ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The y-value decreases by 2 whenever x increases by 1, so the slope is \(-2\). At \(x=0\), y is 6. Therefore, \(y=-2x+6\); choice B uses half the rate. Checking additional rows confirms the same constant rate across the whole table.

Q22. The points on a line are given by the table: \(x=-2:y=1\) ; \(x=-1:y=3\) ; \(x=0:y=5\) ; \(x=1:y=7\) ; \(x=2:y=9\). What is the equation of the line?

A.\(y=2x+5\) ✅
B.\(y=5x+2\)
C.\(y=-2x+5\)
D.\(y=2x-5\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Each unit increase in x adds 2 to y, giving slope 2; the row with \(x=0\) gives intercept 5. Thus, \(y=2x+5\). Choice C incorrectly makes the slope negative. The constant change across rows confirms a linear relationship.

Q23. A fundraiser begins with \(\$18\) and earns \(\$4\) for each item sold. If \(x\) items are sold and \(y\) is the total amount, which point is on the graph after 5 items?

A.\((5,20)\)
B.\((5,22)\)
C.\((5,38)\) ✅
D.\((5,90)\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The amount is modeled by \(y=4x+18\). At \(x=5\), \(y=20+18=38\), so \((5,38)\) lies on the graph. Choice A omits the initial amount. The fixed starting amount remains in the total regardless of how many items are sold.

Q24. A student graphs \(y=\frac{1}{3}x-5\) by starting at \((0,-5)\) and moving right 1 and down 3. Which change is needed?

A.Start at \((-5,0)\), then move right 3 and up 1.
B.Keep the starting point, then move right 3 and up 1. ✅
C.Keep the starting point, then move left 3 and up 1.
D.Keep the starting point, then move right 1 and up 3.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The intercept \((0,-5)\) is correct, but slope \(\frac{1}{3}\) means rise 1 for run 3. The student reversed and changed the ratio; choice D uses the reciprocal slope. A positive slope requires upward movement for a rightward run.

Q25. For \(y=-4x+1\), a student substitutes \(x=1\) and writes \(y=-4+1=-5\). Which point should the student plot instead?

A.\((1,-5)\)
B.\((1,5)\)
C.\((1,-3)\) ✅
D.\((-1,-3)\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The substitution is correct, but the arithmetic is not: \(-4+1=-3\). The point is \((1,-3)\). The tempting choice A repeats the student's addition error rather than correcting it. The plotted x-coordinate remains 1 because that is the substituted input.

Q26. A phone plan costs \(\$12\) each month plus \(\$0.10\) per text message. What is the total monthly cost for 80 messages?

A.\(\$8\)
B.\(\$12.80\)
C.\(\$20\) ✅
D.\(\$92\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The cost model is \(C=0.10m+12\). For 80 messages, \(C=0.10(80)+12=20\) dollars. Choice B adds the fee incorrectly by treating 80 messages as 8 dollars total. The monthly fee is charged even when no messages are sent.

Q27. A line has slope \(-3\). If a point on the line is followed by a move 2 units to the right, how must its y-coordinate change?

A.Increase by 6.
B.Decrease by 3.
C.Increase by 3.
D.Decrease by 6. ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Slope is rise divided by run, so \(-3=\frac{-6}{2}\). A 2-unit move right requires a 6-unit decrease in y. The tempting choice B applies the slope to only one horizontal unit.

Q28. A car rental costs \(\$30\) per day plus \(\$0.25\) per kilometer. What total cost is represented by the point when 40 kilometers are driven in one day?

A.\((40,10)\)
B.\((40,30)\)
C.\((40,40)\) ✅
D.\((40,70)\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The cost model is \(C=0.25k+30\). For 40 kilometers, \(C=10+30=40\), so the graph includes \((40,40)\). Choice A excludes the fixed daily rental charge. The plotted output must include both the variable mileage charge and fixed daily fee.

Q29. At noon, the temperature is \(22^\circ\text{C}\) and then falls steadily by \(1.5^\circ\text{C}\) each hour. What temperature does the model predict 6 hours later?

A.\(9^\circ\text{C}\)
B.\(13^\circ\text{C}\) ✅
C.\(16^\circ\text{C}\)
D.\(31^\circ\text{C}\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The model is \(T=22-1.5h\). At \(h=6\), \(T=22-9=13^\circ\text{C}\). The tempting choice C subtracts only four degrees, not the six hourly decreases specified. The starting temperature is the intercept, while the hourly drop is the slope.

Q30. A school play group models its net earnings by \(P=8t-120\), where \(t\) is the number of tickets sold. How many tickets must be sold for net earnings to be zero?

A.\(8\)
B.\(12\)
C.\(15\) ✅
D.\(120\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Break-even occurs when \(P=0\). Solving \(8t-120=0\) gives \(8t=120\), so \(t=15\). The tempting choice D reports the setup cost rather than the number of tickets needed. The intercept represents the initial cost, and ticket sales offset it.

Q31. Which description matches the graph of \(y=-2\)?

C.A line with slope \(-2\) crossing the origin.
D.A horizontal line crossing the x-axis at \((-2,0)\).
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The equation fixes y at \(-2\) for every x, so its graph is horizontal and includes \((0,-2)\). The tempting choice A describes a vertical line, which would require a fixed x-value.

Q32. A line has a negative slope and a positive y-intercept. Which statement must describe its graph?

A.It falls from left to right and crosses the y-axis above the origin. ✅
B.It rises from left to right and crosses the y-axis above the origin.
C.It falls from left to right and crosses the y-axis below the origin.
D.It is horizontal and crosses the y-axis above the origin.
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: A negative slope means y decreases as x increases, while a positive intercept places the y-axis crossing above zero. Choice C correctly describes direction but reverses the intercept's sign. These signs determine separate graph features and cannot be interchanged.

Q33. A taxi charges a \(\$6\) pickup fee and \(\$1.80\) per kilometer. According to this model, what is the fare for a 9-kilometer trip?

A.\(\$16.20\)
B.\(\$21.80\)
C.\(\$22.20\) ✅
D.\(\$54.00\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The fare is \(F=1.8d+6\). For 9 kilometers, \(F=1.8(9)+6=16.2+6=22.2\) dollars. Choice A includes only the distance charge and omits the pickup fee. The intercept is the pickup cost, and the slope is the per-kilometer rate.

Q34. A student has \(\$45\) saved and adds \(\$15\) each week. How much will the student have after 4 weeks?

A.\(\$60\)
B.\(\$75\)
C.\(\$105\) ✅
D.\(\$180\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The savings model is \(S=15w+45\). After 4 weeks, \(S=15(4)+45=105\) dollars. The tempting choice B adds only two weekly deposits to the original savings. The slope represents each weekly deposit, while the intercept is the initial balance.

Q35. The line \(y=\frac{k}{2}x+3\) passes through \((4,-1)\). What is the value of \(k\)?

A.\(-4\)
B.\(-2\) ✅
C.\(2\)
D.\(4\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Substitute \((4,-1)\): \(-1=2k+3\), so \(2k=-4\) and \(k=-2\). The tempting choice A divides by 1 instead of isolating the coefficient correctly. The given point must satisfy the equation, so substitution uniquely determines the parameter.

Q36. The line \(y=(a-1)x+5\) passes through \((2,11)\). What is the y-value on this same line when \(x=-1\)?

A.\(-4\)
B.\(2\) ✅
C.\(5\)
D.\(8\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Substitution gives \(11=2(a-1)+5\), so \(a=4\) and the slope is 3. At \(x=-1\), \(y=3(-1)+5=2\). Choice C overlooks the slope's effect. Using the point first determines the slope, which then gives the requested output.
