What is How to graph a line with point and slope?

Definition:
Graphing a line with a point and slope uses the slope to locate a second point from a given point, where the slope \( m = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} \) indicates vertical change over horizontal change, and the procedure is to plot the given point, move vertically by the rise and horizontally by the run to find another point, plot the second point, and draw a straight line through both points, which produces the graph of the line.

Working:
The method works by starting at the known point \( (x_1, y_1) \), using the slope as a fraction \( \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} \), moving up for positive rise or down for negative rise, moving right for positive run or left for negative run, marking the new point, and drawing the line through both points; for example, with point \( (2, 3) \) and slope \( \frac{2}{1} \), move up 2 and right 1 to reach \( (3, 5) \), and the equation of the line can be written as \( y - y_1 = m(x - x_1) \) to verify the points.

Example:
Graph the line through \( (1, 2) \) with slope \( m = -3 \), which is \( \frac{-3}{1} \); plot \( (1, 2) \), move down 3 and right 1 to reach \( (2, -1) \), plot \( (2, -1) \), and draw the line through the two points, giving the graph of the line.

Reason:
The reason for ease of grasping this procedure is that the slope directly provides the movement pattern between points, so only one known point is needed to determine the entire line, and this connects the algebraic slope value to a visual graph in a single, repeatable step.

📝 All How to graph a line with point and slope MCQs

Q1. A line passes through the point \((2,5)\) and has slope \(3\). Which equation represents the line?

A.\(y=3x+1\)
B.\(y=3x-1\) ✅
C.\(y=2x+3\)
D.\(y=3x+5\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Using point-slope reasoning, substitute \((2,5)\) and \(m=3\): \(y-5=3(x-2)\). Expanding gives \(y-5=3x-6\), so \(y=3x-1\).

Q2. A line has slope \(-2\) and passes through \((4,1)\). Starting at the given point, which movement reaches another point on the line?

A.Move 2 units right and 1 unit up
B.Move 1 unit right and 2 units up
C.Move 1 unit right and 2 units down ✅
D.Move 2 units left and 1 unit down
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: A slope of \(-2\) means rise over run is \(-2/1\). Thus, for every 1 unit moved to the right, the line moves 2 units downward. From \((4,1)\), moving right 1 and down 2 gives another point on the line.

Q3. Two students graph a line through \((-3,4)\) with slope \(2\). Student A moves 1 unit right and 2 units up. Student B moves 2 units right and 1 unit up. Which student uses the correct slope and why?

A.Student A, because \(2/1=2\) ✅
B.Student B, because \(1/2=2\)
C.Both, because either movement gives slope \(2\)
D.Neither, because the slope must be negative
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The slope is \(2\), which can be written as \(2/1\). Therefore the correct movement is 1 unit horizontally and 2 units vertically in the same upward direction. Student B uses \(1/2\), which equals \(0.5\), not \(2\).

Q4. A line through \((1,-2)\) has slope \(4\). A student writes \(y+2=4(x+1)\). What error did the student make?

A.The slope should be \(-4\)
B.The \(x\)-coordinate should be subtracted, not added ✅
C.The \(y\)-coordinate should be written as \(y-2\)
D.The point cannot be used because its \(y\)-value is negative
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Point-slope form is \(y-y_1=m(x-x_1)\). For \((1,-2)\), this becomes \(y-(-2)=4(x-1)\), or \(y+2=4(x-1)\). The student incorrectly changed \(x-1\) into \(x+1\).

Q5. A line passes through \((5,7)\) with slope \(-\frac{1}{2}\). Which point could be obtained by moving exactly 4 units horizontally from the given point while staying on the line?

A.\((9,9)\)
B.\((9,5)\) ✅
C.\((1,9)\)
D.\((1,5)\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A slope of \(-1/2\) means that moving 4 units right produces a vertical change of \(-2\). Starting from \((5,7)\), the resulting point is \((9,5)\). This preserves both the horizontal change and the required negative slope.

Q6. A line through \((2,-3)\) has slope \(5/2\). Which pair of points can be used to graph the line without first finding its equation?

A.\((2,-3)\) and \((4,2)\) ✅
B.\((2,-3)\) and \((5,0)\)
C.\((2,-3)\) and \((4,7)\)
D.\((2,-3)\) and \((0,-8)\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Starting at \((2,-3)\), a run of 2 with a rise of 5 gives \((4,2)\). The slope between these points is \((2-(-3))/(4-2)=5/2\). Thus both points lie on the required line and can be connected to graph it.

Q7. A road on a coordinate map passes through \((3,6)\). Its grade is represented by a slope of \(\frac{1}{4}\). If the road extends 12 horizontal units to the right, what vertical change should be shown?

A.3 units upward ✅
B.3 units downward
C.48 units upward
D.12 units upward
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The slope \(1/4\) means a vertical change of 1 unit for every 4 horizontal units. For 12 horizontal units, the vertical change is \(12(1/4)=3\). Because the slope is positive, the vertical change is upward.

Q8. A line through \((-2,3)\) has slope \(-3\). Which equation should be used to graph it directly from the given point?

A.\(y-3=-3(x+2)\) ✅
B.\(y+3=-3(x-2)\)
C.\(y-3=3(x+2)\)
D.\(y+3=3(x-2)\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Point-slope form is \(y-y_1=m(x-x_1)\). Substituting \(x_1=-2\), \(y_1=3\), and \(m=-3\) gives \(y-3=-3(x-(-2))\), which simplifies to \(y-3=-3(x+2)\).

Q9. A graphing student starts at \((6,-1)\) and needs to graph a line with slope \(-\frac{3}{2}\). Which sequence of points correctly continues the line?

A.\((8,-4)\), then \((10,-7)\) ✅
B.\((8,2)\), then \((10,5)\)
C.\((4,-4)\), then \((2,-7)\)
D.\((9,-4)\), then \((12,-7)\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: For slope \(-3/2\), moving 2 units right requires moving 3 units down. Starting at \((6,-1)\), the next point is \((8,-4)\), followed by \((10,-7)\). Each consecutive pair maintains the same slope, confirming the sequence.

Q10. A student says, "If a line has slope \(2\) and passes through \((3,4)\), I can graph it by moving 2 units right and 1 unit up." How should this reasoning be corrected?

A.The student should move 1 unit right and 2 units up ✅
B.The student should move 2 units right and 2 units up
C.The student should move 1 unit right and 1 unit up
D.The student should move 2 units left and 1 unit down
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Slope \(2\) means rise/run equals \(2/1\), not \(1/2\). Therefore the correct basic movement is 1 unit right and 2 units up. The student's proposed movement has slope \(1/2\), so it would produce a different line.

Q11. A graph shows a line passing through \((-1,2)\) and \((3,10)\). A new point is given as \((5,14)\). Which conclusion is justified?

A.The new point lies on the same line because the slope remains \(2\) ✅
B.The new point lies on the line because its \(y\)-coordinate is positive
C.The new point cannot lie on the line because its coordinates are larger
D.The new point changes the slope to \(1/2\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The slope between \((-1,2)\) and \((3,10)\) is \((10-2)/(3-(-1))=8/4=2\). From \((3,10)\) to \((5,14)\), the slope is \((14-10)/(5-3)=4/2=2\). Since the slopes match, the three points are collinear.

Q12. Two methods are proposed for graphing a line through \((4,-5)\) with slope \(-2\). Method A uses point-slope form to find another point. Method B moves 1 unit left and 2 units up from the given point. Which statement correctly compares them?

A.Both methods work because Method B also gives slope \(-2\) ✅
B.Only Method A works because negative slopes cannot be graphed by moving left
C.Only Method B works because equations cannot determine points
D.Neither method works because the starting point has a negative \(y\)-coordinate
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Method A is valid because point-slope form represents the required line. Method B is also valid: moving 1 unit left and 2 units up gives rise/run \(=2/(-1)=-2\). Both methods therefore generate points on the same line.

Q13. A line passes through \((0,6)\) and another known point \((2,2)\). Without finding the full equation first, a student wants to graph the line from \((0,6)\). What movement should the student repeatedly use?

A.2 units right and 4 units down ✅
B.4 units right and 2 units down
C.2 units left and 4 units up
D.4 units left and 2 units down
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The slope from the two points is \((2-6)/(2-0)=-4/2=-2\). Therefore a convenient movement is 2 units right and 4 units down. Repeating that movement preserves the slope and allows the entire line to be constructed.

Q14. A delivery route is modeled by a straight line passing through \((10,30)\) with slope \(-\frac{3}{5}\). The route is extended 15 horizontal units to the right. What coordinate should represent the new location?

A.\((25,21)\) ✅
B.\((25,39)\)
C.\((13,27)\)
D.\((15,27)\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: A horizontal change of 15 corresponds to a vertical change of \((-3/5)(15)=-9\). Starting at \((10,30)\), the new coordinates are \((10+15,30-9)=(25,21)\). This requires combining slope interpretation with coordinate changes.
