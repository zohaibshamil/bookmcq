What is Slope intercept form y = mx + b?

Definition:
Slope-intercept form is the equation of a line written as \( y = mx + b \), where \( m \) is the slope of the line and \( b \) is the y-intercept, which is the point where the line crosses the y-axis, and this form is the most convenient for graphing because it immediately reveals the steepness and the starting point of the line on the y-axis, and it is obtained by solving a linear equation for \( y \).

Working:
The form works by reading \( m \) as the rise over run and \( b \) as the y-coordinate of the y-intercept \( (0, b) \); to graph, plot the point \( (0, b) \), use the slope to locate a second point, and draw the line; to convert from standard form \( Ax + By = C \), solve for \( y \) to obtain \( y = -\frac{A}{B}x + \frac{C}{B} \), so \( m = -\frac{A}{B} \) and \( b = \frac{C}{B} \), and the equation \( y = mx + b \) allows direct calculation of \( y \) for any chosen \( x \).

Example:
For the equation \( y = 2x + 3 \), the slope is \( m = 2 \) and the y-intercept is \( b = 3 \), so plot \( (0, 3) \), move up 2 and right 1 to reach \( (1, 5) \), and draw the line; for \( 2x + 3y = 6 \), solving for \( y \) gives \( y = -\frac{2}{3}x + 2 \), so the slope is \( -\frac{2}{3} \) and the y-intercept is 2.

Reason:
The reason for ease of grasping slope-intercept form is that it packages the two essential features of a line, steepness and starting height, into one compact equation, so graphing and comparing lines becomes a direct reading of \( m \) and \( b \) rather than a series of separate calculations.

📝 All Slope intercept form y = mx + b MCQs

Q1. Which equation is already written in slope-intercept form and has slope \(-3\) and y-intercept \(5\)?

A.\(y=5x-3\)
B.\(y=-3x+5\) ✅
C.\(3y=x+5\)
D.\(y+3x=5\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Slope-intercept form is \(y=mx+b\), where \(m\) is the slope and \(b\) is the y-intercept. Substituting \(m=-3\) and \(b=5\) gives \(y=-3x+5\), so option B satisfies both conditions.

Q2. A line has slope \(4\) and passes through the point \((0,-7)\). Which equation represents the line?

A.\(y=4x-7\) ✅
B.\(y=-7x+4\)
C.\(y=4x+7\)
D.\(y=-4x-7\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: In slope-intercept form, \(y=mx+b\). Because the point has \(x=0\), its y-coordinate directly gives the y-intercept. Thus \(m=4\) and \(b=-7\), producing \(y=4x-7\).

Q3. Two lines are \(y=2x+3\) and \(y=2x-5\). What can be concluded without graphing them?

A.They intersect at \((0,3)\)
B.They have different slopes but the same intercept
C.They have the same slope and different y-intercepts ✅
D.They have the same y-intercept and different slopes
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Both equations have the coefficient of \(x\) equal to \(2\), so their slopes are identical. Their constant terms are \(3\) and \(-5\), so their y-intercepts differ. Therefore, the lines are distinct and parallel.

Q4. A student says that \(y=-\frac{2}{3}x+4\) crosses the x-axis at \(4\) because \(4\) is the constant term. What is the correct interpretation?

A.The x-intercept is \(4\)
B.The y-intercept is \(4\), while the x-intercept is \(6\) ✅
C.The y-intercept is \(-4\), while the x-intercept is \(6\)
D.Both intercepts are \(4\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The constant term in slope-intercept form is the y-intercept, not the x-intercept. Setting \(y=0\) gives \(0=-\frac{2}{3}x+4\), so \(x=6\). Therefore, the y-intercept is \(4\) and the x-intercept is \(6\).

Q5. A line passes through \((2,7)\) and \((5,16)\). A student writes \(y=3x+7\). Which reasoning identifies the error?

A.The slope should be \(9\), not \(3\)
B.The slope is \(3\), but \(7\) is not the y-intercept ✅
C.The slope should be \(-3\), and the intercept should be \(13\)
D.The equation is correct because \(7\) is one of the y-values
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The slope is \((16-7)/(5-2)=3\), so the slope calculation is correct. However, \(7\) is the y-value when \(x=2\), not when \(x=0\). Using \(7=3(2)+b\) gives \(b=1\), so the equation is \(y=3x+1\).

Q6. A taxi company models its fare with \(y=2.5x+60\), where \(x\) is the distance traveled and \(y\) is the fare. What does the number \(60\) represent?

A.The cost per kilometer
B.The distance traveled before charging begins
C.The fixed starting charge ✅
D.The maximum possible fare
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: In \(y=mx+b\), the constant \(b\) represents the value of \(y\) when \(x=0\). Here, \(60\) is the fare when no distance has been traveled, so it represents the fixed starting charge.

Q7. A savings account is modeled by \(y=150+25x\), where \(x\) is the number of weeks and \(y\) is the balance in dollars. What will the balance be after \(8\) weeks?

A.1200
B.325
C.350 ✅
D.175
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Substitute \(x=8\) into the model: \(y=150+25(8)\). The weekly increase contributes \(200\) dollars, which is added to the initial \(150\) dollars. Therefore, the predicted balance after eight weeks is \(350\) dollars.

Q8. A water tank contains \(900\) liters and drains at \(35\) liters per minute. Which equation models the amount \(y\) remaining after \(x\) minutes, and what does its slope indicate?

A.\(y=35x+900\); the tank gains \(35\) liters per minute
B.\(y=900x-35\); the tank loses \(900\) liters per minute
C.\(y=-35x+900\); the tank loses \(35\) liters per minute ✅
D.\(y=-900x+35\); the tank loses \(900\) liters per minute
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The initial amount is the y-intercept, \(900\), while draining causes the amount to decrease, giving a slope of \(-35\). Thus \(y=-35x+900\), and the slope represents a loss of \(35\) liters each minute.

Q9. A line has equation \(y=-4x+12\). Another student claims that increasing \(x\) by \(3\) decreases \(y\) by \(4\). Which statement correctly analyzes the claim?

A.It is correct because the y-intercept is \(12\)
B.It is correct because the slope is \(-4\)
C.It is incorrect because increasing \(x\) by \(3\) decreases \(y\) by \(12\) ✅
D.It is incorrect because increasing \(x\) always increases \(y\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The slope \(-4\) means \(y\) changes by \(-4\) for every increase of \(1\) in \(x\). Therefore, increasing \(x\) by \(3\) changes \(y\) by \((-4)(3)=-12\), so the student understated the change.

Q10. A graph shows a line crossing the y-axis at \(6\) and passing through \((2,2)\). Which equation matches the graph?

A.\(y=-2x+6\) ✅
B.\(y=2x+6\)
C.\(y=-\frac{1}{2}x+6\)
D.\(y=2x-6\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The y-intercept is \(6\), so the equation must have \(b=6\). Using \((0,6)\) and \((2,2)\), the slope is \((2-6)/(2-0)=-2\). Therefore, the equation is \(y=-2x+6\).

Q11. A graph of a linear relationship passes through \((0,-3)\) and \((4,9)\). A second method starts with \(y=mx+b\). Which conclusion is obtained using either method?

A.The equation is \(y=3x-3\) ✅
B.The equation is \(y=-3x-3\)
C.The equation is \(y=3x+3\)
D.The equation is \(y=-3x+9\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The slope from the two points is \((9-(-3))/(4-0)=3\). Since the graph crosses the y-axis at \(-3\), the intercept is \(-3\). Substituting both values into \(y=mx+b\) gives \(y=3x-3\).

Q12. A delivery service charges a fixed fee plus a constant amount per kilometer. A customer pays $46 for 4 km and $70 for 8 km. What equation models the cost \(y\) for \(x\) kilometers?

A.\(y=6x+22\) ✅
B.\(y=4x+30\)
C.\(y=6x+46\)
D.\(y=24x+22\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The rate is \((70-46)/(8-4)=6\) dollars per kilometer. Using \(46=6(4)+b\) gives \(b=22\). Therefore, the model is \(y=6x+22\), where \(22\) is the fixed fee.

Q13. A line has positive slope and y-intercept \(-5\). It passes through \((3,1)\). Which equation must represent it?

A.\(y=2x-5\) ✅
B.\(y=-2x-5\)
C.\(y=2x+5\)
D.\(y=\frac{1}{2}x-5\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Using the known y-intercept gives \(y=mx-5\). Substituting the point \((3,1)\) gives \(1=3m-5\), so \(3m=6\) and \(m=2\). Therefore, the required equation is \(y=2x-5\).

Q14. Among all lines with integer slope and integer y-intercept that pass through both \((1,4)\) and \((3,10)\), which equation is possible?

A.\(y=3x+1\) ✅
B.\(y=2x+2\)
C.\(y=4x\)
D.\(y=3x-1\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The slope determined by the two points is \((10-4)/(3-1)=3\). Substituting \((1,4)\) into \(y=3x+b\) gives \(4=3+b\), so \(b=1\). Hence \(y=3x+1\), satisfying both points and the required integer conditions.
