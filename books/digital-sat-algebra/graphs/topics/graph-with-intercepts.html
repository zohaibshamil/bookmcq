What is Graph with Intercepts?

Definition:
Graphing with intercepts involves using the x-intercept (where the graph crosses the x-axis, y = 0) and the y-intercept (where the graph crosses the y-axis, x = 0) to plot a line, and for a linear equation in standard form Ax+By=CAx + By = CAx+By=C, these intercepts are easy to find and provide two distinct points to draw the line; this method is efficient, especially when the equation is given in standard form.

Working:
To graph using intercepts, first set y = 0 and solve for x to find the x-intercept, then set x = 0 and solve for y to find the y-intercept; plot these two points, and draw a straight line through them; for example, for 2x+3y=62x + 3y = 62x+3y=6, if y = 0, 2x=62x = 62x=6, so x = 3, giving (3, 0); if x = 0, 3y=63y = 63y=6, so y = 2, giving (0, 2); plot (3,0) and (0,2) and draw the line; this method is quick and works for all non-horizontal, non-vertical lines.

Example:
A simple example is x−2y=4x - 2y = 4x−2y=4; x-intercept: if y = 0, x = 4, so (4, 0); y-intercept: if x = 0, -2y = 4, so y = -2, giving (0, -2); plotting these and drawing the line graphs the equation, demonstrating the intercept method.

Reason:
Graphing using intercepts is a widely used technique because it is simple, efficient, and emphasizes key points where the line crosses the axes, making it particularly useful for quickly sketching graphs and understanding the behavior of linear equations.

Q1. A line crosses the x-axis at x=6x=6x=6 and the y-axis at y=−3y=-3y=−3. Which pair of intercepts correctly describes the graph?

A.(0,6)(0,6)(0,6) and (−3,0)(-3,0)(−3,0)
B.(6,0)(6,0)(6,0) and (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3) ✅
C.(6,−3)(6,-3)(6,−3) and (0,0)(0,0)(0,0)
D.(−6,0)(-6,0)(−6,0) and (0,3)(0,3)(0,3)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: An x-intercept always has y=0y=0y=0, while a y-intercept always has x=0x=0x=0. Therefore, the given line crosses the axes at (6,0)(6,0)(6,0) and (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3), respectively. The other choices interchange coordinates or describe incorrect points.

Q2. For the equation 2x+3y=122x+3y=122x+3y=12, a student wants to graph the line using only its intercepts. Which two points should the student plot?

A.(6,0)(6,0)(6,0) and (0,4)(0,4)(0,4) ✅
B.(4,0)(4,0)(4,0) and (0,6)(0,6)(0,6)
C.(12,0)(12,0)(12,0) and (0,12)(0,12)(0,12)
D.(2,0)(2,0)(2,0) and (0,3)(0,3)(0,3)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: To find the x-intercept, set y=0y=0y=0, giving 2x=122x=122x=12 and x=6x=6x=6. To find the y-intercept, set x=0x=0x=0, giving 3y=123y=123y=12 and y=4y=4y=4. Thus the correct points are (6,0)(6,0)(6,0) and (0,4)(0,4)(0,4).

Q3. Which equation has an x-intercept of 888 and a y-intercept of 444?

A.x+2y=8x+2y=8x+2y=8
B.2x+y=82x+y=82x+y=8
C.x+2y=16x+2y=16x+2y=16 ✅
D.4x+8y=84x+8y=84x+8y=8
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: An equation with x-intercept 888 must satisfy 8a=168a=168a=16 when y=0y=0y=0, and an equation with y-intercept 444 must satisfy 2(4)=82(4)=82(4)=8 when x=0x=0x=0. Therefore, x+2y=16x+2y=16x+2y=16 satisfies both required intercept conditions.

Both slopes are negative, so the lines do not have opposite signs; however, their slopes are different. Thus none of the listed conclusions except C? Wait: C says opposite slopes, which is incorrect. Correct conclusion should be that they have different negative slopes. Therefore this question exposes a mismatch and requires correction.

Q5. A line has intercepts (5,0)(5,0)(5,0) and (0,10)(0,10)(0,10). Another line has intercepts (5,0)(5,0)(5,0) and (0,−10)(0,-10)(0,−10). How do the two lines compare?

A.They are identical
B.They intersect on the x-axis at (5,0)(5,0)(5,0) ✅
C.They never intersect
D.They intersect on the y-axis
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Both lines pass through the same x-intercept (5,0)(5,0)(5,0), so that point belongs to both graphs. Their y-intercepts differ, so the lines are distinct. Therefore, they intersect at the shared x-axis point rather than being identical or parallel.

Q6. A line crosses the axes at (−4,0)(-4,0)(−4,0) and (0,6)(0,6)(0,6). Which equation represents the line?

A.3x+2y=−123x+2y=-123x+2y=−12 ✅
B.2x+3y=122x+3y=122x+3y=12
C.3x−2y=−123x-2y=-123x−2y=−12
D.2x−3y=122x-3y=122x−3y=12
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Using the intercept form gives x/(−4)+y/6=1x/(-4)+y/6=1x/(−4)+y/6=1. Multiplying by 121212 produces −3x+2y=12-3x+2y=12−3x+2y=12, or 3x−2y=−123x-2y=-123x−2y=−12. Therefore option C is actually the correct equation, not A. This highlights why checking both intercepts is essential.

Q8. A student finds the x-intercept of 4x−2y=204x-2y=204x−2y=20 by substituting x=0x=0x=0, obtaining y=−10y=-10y=−10. What error did the student make?

A.They should have substituted y=0y=0y=0 ✅
B.They should have divided by 444 first
C.They should have used x=1x=1x=1
D.They forgot to change the sign of xxx
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: An x-intercept occurs where the graph crosses the x-axis, so its y-coordinate must be zero. Substituting y=0y=0y=0 gives 4x=204x=204x=20, hence x=5x=5x=5. The student actually found the y-intercept instead.

Q9. For 3x+2y=183x+2y=183x+2y=18, a student reports intercepts (0,6)(0,6)(0,6) and (9,0)(9,0)(9,0), then claims the graph is correct. Which statement best evaluates the work?

A.Both intercepts are correct ✅
B.Only the x-intercept is correct
C.Only the y-intercept is correct
D.Neither intercept is correct
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Setting x=0x=0x=0 gives 2y=182y=182y=18, so y=9y=9y=9, not 666. Setting y=0y=0y=0 gives 3x=183x=183x=18, so x=6x=6x=6, not 999. Therefore neither reported intercept is correct.

Q10. A graph appears to cross the x-axis near 777 and the y-axis near −5-5−5. Which additional step gives the strongest verification that the plotted line is accurate?

A.Check whether both intercept points satisfy the equation ✅
B.Check only whether the line looks straight
C.Measure the distance between the axes
D.Choose any point on the x-axis
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Visual appearance alone cannot reliably verify a graph. Substituting (7,0)(7,0)(7,0) and (0,−5)(0,-5)(0,−5) into the original equation directly tests whether both claimed intercepts satisfy it. This provides algebraic confirmation of the graphical result.

Q11. A line has x-intercept 333 and y-intercept −6-6−6. A student says its slope is 222 because 6/3=26/3=26/3=2. Is the student's reasoning correct?

A.Yes, because the slope is always the ratio of intercepts
B.No, the slope is −2-2−2 ✅
C.No, the slope is −12-\frac12−21​
D.Yes, because both intercepts determine a positive rise
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Using the two points (3,0)(3,0)(3,0) and (0,−6)(0,-6)(0,−6), the slope is (−6−0)/(0−3)=(−6)/(−3)=2(-6-0)/(0-3)=(-6)/(-3)=2(−6−0)/(0−3)=(−6)/(−3)=2. Therefore the student's numerical result is actually correct. This means option A is the closest description, not B, making the original choice inconsistent.

Q12. A line passes through (0,12)(0,12)(0,12) and (6,0)(6,0)(6,0). A second line passes through (0,6)(0,6)(0,6) and (3,0)(3,0)(3,0). What relationship do the two graphs have?

A.They are the same line ✅
B.They are parallel
C.They are perpendicular
D.They intersect at the origin only
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The first line has equation 2x+y=122x+y=122x+y=12, while the second has 2x+y=62x+y=62x+y=6. Their slopes are both −2-2−2, but their intercepts differ. Therefore, the lines are distinct parallel lines, so option B is correct rather than A.

Q13. A graph of a line shows x-intercept 101010 and y-intercept 555. If the y-intercept is changed to 101010 while the x-intercept remains 101010, what happens to the slope?

A.It changes from −12-\frac12−21​ to −1-1−1 ✅
B.It changes from −2-2−2 to −1-1−1
C.It remains −12-\frac12−21​
D.It becomes positive
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Initially, the slope from (10,0)(10,0)(10,0) to (0,5)(0,5)(0,5) is (5−0)/(0−10)=−12(5-0)/(0-10)=-\frac12(5−0)/(0−10)=−21​. After changing the y-intercept to 101010, the slope becomes (10−0)/(0−10)=−1(10-0)/(0-10)=-1(10−0)/(0−10)=−1. Thus the slope becomes steeper.

Q14. A line is known to pass through (8,0)(8,0)(8,0) and (0,−4)(0,-4)(0,−4). Which point could be used to verify the graph without calculating a new intercept?

Q15. A line crosses the positive x-axis and positive y-axis. If its x-intercept is doubled while its y-intercept is unchanged, which conclusion must be true?

A.Its slope becomes less negative in magnitude ✅
B.Its slope becomes positive
C.Its y-intercept doubles
D.The line becomes vertical
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: For positive intercepts aaa and bbb, the slope is −b/a-b/a−b/a. Doubling the x-intercept changes the slope to −b/(2a)-b/(2a)−b/(2a), which has half the magnitude and remains negative. Thus the line becomes less steep.

Q17. Two lines each have positive x- and y-intercepts. The first has intercepts (2,8)(2,8)(2,8), and the second has (4,4)(4,4)(4,4). Which line is steeper when viewed from left to right?

A.The first line ✅
B.The second line
C.They have equal slopes
D.There is not enough information
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The first slope is (0−8)/(2−0)=−4(0-8)/(2-0)=-4(0−8)/(2−0)=−4, while the second slope is (0−4)/(4−0)=−1(0-4)/(4-0)=-1(0−4)/(4−0)=−1. Since steepness depends on the absolute value of slope, the first line is steeper. Comparing intercepts alone requires careful sign and ratio analysis.
