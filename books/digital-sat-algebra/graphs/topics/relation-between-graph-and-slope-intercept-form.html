What is Relation between graph and slope intercept form?

Definition:
The relation between a graph and slope-intercept form is that the graph of \( y = mx + b \) is a straight line whose steepness and direction are determined by \( m \) and whose vertical position on the y-axis is determined by \( b \), so the algebraic values in the equation correspond exactly to visual features of the line, with positive \( m \) producing an upward slope from left to right, negative \( m \) producing a downward slope, \( m = 0 \) producing a horizontal line, and changing \( b \) shifting the line up or down without changing its steepness.

Working:
The relation works by matching each part of the equation to the graph: \( b \) is the y-intercept, the point \( (0, b) \) where the line crosses the y-axis; \( m \) is the slope, the ratio of vertical change to horizontal change between any two points on the line; for example, in \( y = 3x - 1 \), the graph crosses the y-axis at \( (0, -1) \) and rises 3 units for every 1 unit to the right, and two lines with the same \( m \) but different \( b \) are parallel, while two lines with \( m \) values that are negative reciprocals are perpendicular.

Example:
Compare \( y = 2x + 1 \) and \( y = 2x - 3 \), which have the same slope \( m = 2 \) so their graphs are parallel lines, with the first crossing the y-axis at 1 and the second at \( -3 \); compare \( y = \frac{1}{2}x \) and \( y = -2x \), whose slopes are negative reciprocals, so their graphs are perpendicular lines intersecting at the origin.

Reason:
The reason for ease of grasping the relation between graph and slope-intercept form is that every symbol in the equation has a visible counterpart on the graph, so reading an equation becomes seeing a line and seeing a line becomes reading an equation, which unifies algebra and geometry into one understanding.

📝 All Relation between graph and slope intercept form MCQs

Q1. A line crosses the y-axis at \(5\) and rises \(2\) units for every \(1\) unit it moves right. Which equation matches the graph?

A.\(y=5x+2\)
B.\(y=2x+5\) ✅
C.\(y=-2x+5\)
D.\(y=2x-5\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The y-intercept is \(5\), so the constant term must be \(5\). The line rises as \(x\) increases, giving a positive slope of \(2\). Therefore the equation is \(y=2x+5\).

Q2. A graph represents a line with equation \(y=-3x+4\). Without calculating additional points, what two features of the graph can be identified directly from the equation?

A.It crosses the y-axis at \(4\) and decreases from left to right ✅
B.It crosses the x-axis at \(4\) and increases from left to right
C.It crosses the y-axis at \(-3\) and increases from left to right
D.It crosses the x-axis at \(-3\) and decreases from left to right
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: In slope-intercept form \(y=mx+b\), \(m\) determines the direction and steepness while \(b\) gives the y-intercept. Here \(m=-3\), so the line decreases, and \(b=4\), so it crosses the y-axis at \(4\).

Q3. Two lines are shown by their equations: \(y=4x-1\) and \(y=4x+6\). How should their graphs be related?

A.They have different slopes and the same y-intercept
B.They have the same slope and different y-intercepts ✅
C.They have opposite slopes and the same y-intercept
D.They have the same slope and the same y-intercept
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Both equations have slope \(4\), so the graphs rise at the same rate and are parallel. Their y-intercepts are \(-1\) and \(6\), so they cross the y-axis at different locations and are distinct lines.

Q4. A student sees a line crossing the y-axis at \(-2\) and writes \(y=-2x+3\). The graph rises from left to right at a rate of \(1\) unit vertically for every \(2\) units horizontally. What is the student's mistake?

A.The y-intercept should be \(3\)
B.The slope should be \(-2\)
C.The slope should be \(1/2\) ✅
D.The equation should have no constant term
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The graph rises, so its slope is positive. A rise of \(1\) for a run of \(2\) gives \(m=1/2\), while the y-intercept is \(-2\). The correct equation is \(y=(1/2)x-2\).

Q5. A taxi fare graph begins at \(8\) dollars when the distance is \(0\) miles and increases by \(2.50\) dollars per mile. Which equation models the fare, and what does each coefficient represent?

A.\(y=8x+2.50\); \(8\) is the rate and \(2.50\) is the starting fare
B.\(y=2.50x+8\); \(2.50\) is the rate and \(8\) is the starting fare ✅
C.\(y=8-2.50x\); \(8\) is the rate and \(2.50\) is the starting fare
D.\(y=2.50-8x\); \(2.50\) is the starting fare and \(8\) is the rate
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The starting fare is the value when \(x=0\), so it is the y-intercept \(8\). The fare increases by \(2.50\) dollars per mile, making \(2.50\) the slope. Thus \(y=2.50x+8\).

Q6. A line passes through \((0,-6)\) and \((3,0)\). A student claims its slope-intercept equation is \(y=2x-6\). Which reasoning correctly evaluates the claim?

A.It is correct because the y-intercept is \(-6\)
B.It is correct because the x-intercept is \(3\)
C.It is incorrect because the slope is \(2\) and the y-intercept is \(6\)
D.It is incorrect because the slope is \(2\) and the y-intercept is \(-6\) ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Using the two points, the slope is \((0-(-6))/(3-0)=2\). The line also crosses the y-axis at \(-6\). Therefore \(y=2x-6\) is actually consistent with both graph features, so the student's claim is correct; option D states those same features but incorrectly labels the claim as incorrect. This exposes why both slope and intercept must be checked together.

Q7. A graph of a line crosses the y-axis at \(7\) and crosses the x-axis at \(3.5\). Which slope-intercept equation represents the line?

A.\(y=2x+7\)
B.\(y=-2x+7\) ✅
C.\(y=2x-7\)
D.\(y=-2x-7\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The line moves downward from left to right because it goes from the positive y-intercept to the positive x-intercept, so the slope is negative. Using \((0,7)\) and \((3.5,0)\), the slope is \(-2\), giving \(y=-2x+7\).

Q8. A graph has a negative y-intercept and rises from left to right. Which combination of slope and y-intercept must its equation have?

A.Positive slope and negative y-intercept ✅
B.Negative slope and positive y-intercept
C.Positive slope and positive y-intercept
D.Negative slope and negative y-intercept
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: A line that rises from left to right has a positive slope. A line crossing the y-axis below the origin has a negative y-intercept. Therefore its slope-intercept form must contain a positive \(m\) and a negative \(b\).

Q9. A company's monthly cost is modeled by \(y=120x+500\), where \(x\) is the number of months. Which graph description is consistent with this model?

A.The graph starts at \(120\) and decreases by \(500\) each month
B.The graph starts at \(500\) and increases by \(120\) each month ✅
C.The graph starts at \(-500\) and increases by \(120\) each month
D.The graph starts at \(500\) and decreases by \(120\) each month
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The coefficient of \(x\), \(120\), is the rate of change, so the cost increases by \(120\) per month. The constant \(500\) is the initial value, so the graph crosses the y-axis at \(500\).

Q10. Line A has equation \(y=0.5x+4\), while Line B has equation \(y=2x+4\). If both are drawn on the same coordinate plane, what feature will they share?

A.They have the same slope
B.They have the same x-intercept
C.They cross the y-axis at the same point ✅
D.They are parallel
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The constant term is \(4\) in both equations, so both lines cross the y-axis at \((0,4)\). Their slopes differ, \(0.5\) versus \(2\), so they are not parallel and will diverge from their common y-intercept.

Q11. A graph shows a line passing through \((0,3)\) and \((4,11)\). Which equation should a student obtain after connecting the graph's two points and converting to slope-intercept form?

A.\(y=2x+3\) ✅
B.\(y=3x+2\)
C.\(y=2x-3\)
D.\(y=-2x+3\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The slope from the two points is \((11-3)/(4-0)=8/4=2\). Since the line crosses the y-axis at \(3\), the intercept is \(3\). Substituting both values into \(y=mx+b\) gives \(y=2x+3\).

Q12. A graph represents \(y=-\frac{1}{2}x+6\). Another student wants to draw the line using the intercept and slope. Which sequence correctly describes the graphing process?

A.Start at \((6,0)\), move down \(1\) and right \(2\)
B.Start at \((0,6)\), move down \(1\) and right \(2\) ✅
C.Start at \((0,-6)\), move up \(1\) and right \(2\)
D.Start at \((6,0)\), move up \(1\) and right \(2\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The y-intercept \(6\) places the starting point at \((0,6)\). The slope \(-1/2\) means that for every \(2\) units moved right, the graph moves \(1\) unit down. This produces the required line.

Q13. A water tank contains \(900\) liters and loses \(75\) liters each hour. A graph models the remaining amount of water. Which slope-intercept equation and graph behavior correctly represent the situation?

A.\(y=75x+900\); the graph rises
B.\(y=-75x+900\); the graph falls ✅
C.\(y=900x-75\); the graph falls
D.\(y=-900x+75\); the graph falls
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The initial amount, \(900\) liters, is the y-intercept because it occurs at \(x=0\). The tank loses \(75\) liters per hour, giving a slope of \(-75\). Therefore the graph decreases according to \(y=-75x+900\).

Q14. A student says, If two lines have the same y-intercept their equations must be identical. Lines \(y=3x+2\) and \(y=-x+2\) are used to test the statement. What conclusion follows?

A.The statement is correct because both lines start at \(2\)
B.The statement is correct because both slopes have the same absolute value
C.The statement is false because the slopes differ even though the y-intercepts match ✅
D.The statement is false because neither equation has a y-intercept
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Both equations have the same y-intercept \(2\), so their graphs meet the y-axis at the same point. However, their slopes are \(3\) and \(-1\), producing different directions and steepness. Therefore equal intercepts do not imply identical equations.

Q15. A graphing program displays a line that passes through \((0,1)\), \((2,5)\), and \((5,11)\). A proposed model is \(y=2x+1\). Why does the evidence from all three points support the model?

A.Only the first point gives the slope
B.The points show a constant increase of \(4\) for every \(2\) units of \(x\), confirming slope \(2\) and intercept \(1\) ✅
C.The points show an intercept of \(2\) and a slope of \(1\)
D.The points prove that every line must have a positive slope
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The first point establishes the y-intercept of \(1\). From \((0,1)\) to \((2,5)\), the rise is \(4\) over a run of \(2\), giving slope \(2\). From \((2,5)\) to \((5,11)\), the rise is \(6\) over a run of \(3\), again giving slope \(2\), so all points support \(y=2x+1\).

Q16. Two graphs have y-intercepts \(4\) and \(-2\), respectively. The first line has slope \(-3\), while the second has slope \(2\). Which comparison correctly predicts their directions and equations?

A.Both graphs rise; \(y=-3x+4\) and \(y=2x-2\)
B.The first graph falls and the second rises; \(y=-3x+4\) and \(y=2x-2\) ✅
C.The first graph rises and the second falls; \(y=3x+4\) and \(y=-2x-2\)
D.Both graphs fall; \(y=-3x-4\) and \(y=-2x+2\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A negative slope means the graph falls from left to right, while a positive slope means it rises. Combining each slope with its corresponding y-intercept gives \(y=-3x+4\) for the first line and \(y=2x-2\) for the second.
