What is Relation between graph and slope intercept form?

Definition:
The relation between a graph and slope-intercept form is that the graph of y=mx+by = mx + by=mx+b is a straight line whose steepness and direction are determined by mmm and whose vertical position on the y-axis is determined by bbb, so the algebraic values in the equation correspond exactly to visual features of the line, with positive mmm producing an upward slope from left to right, negative mmm producing a downward slope, m=0m = 0m=0 producing a horizontal line, and changing bbb shifting the line up or down without changing its steepness.

Working:
The relation works by matching each part of the equation to the graph: bbb is the y-intercept, the point (0,b)(0, b)(0,b) where the line crosses the y-axis; mmm is the slope, the ratio of vertical change to horizontal change between any two points on the line; for example, in y=3x−1y = 3x - 1y=3x−1, the graph crosses the y-axis at (0,−1)(0, -1)(0,−1) and rises 3 units for every 1 unit to the right, and two lines with the same mmm but different bbb are parallel, while two lines with mmm values that are negative reciprocals are perpendicular.

Example:
Compare y=2x+1y = 2x + 1y=2x+1 and y=2x−3y = 2x - 3y=2x−3, which have the same slope m=2m = 2m=2 so their graphs are parallel lines, with the first crossing the y-axis at 1 and the second at −3-3−3; compare y=12xy = \frac{1}{2}xy=21​x and y=−2xy = -2xy=−2x, whose slopes are negative reciprocals, so their graphs are perpendicular lines intersecting at the origin.

Reason:
The reason for ease of grasping the relation between graph and slope-intercept form is that every symbol in the equation has a visible counterpart on the graph, so reading an equation becomes seeing a line and seeing a line becomes reading an equation, which unifies algebra and geometry into one understanding.

Q1. A line crosses the y-axis at 555 and rises 222 units for every 111 unit it moves right. Which equation matches the graph?

A.y=5x+2y=5x+2y=5x+2
B.y=2x+5y=2x+5y=2x+5 ✅
C.y=−2x+5y=-2x+5y=−2x+5
D.y=2x−5y=2x-5y=2x−5
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The y-intercept is 555, so the constant term must be 555. The line rises as xxx increases, giving a positive slope of 222. Therefore the equation is y=2x+5y=2x+5y=2x+5.

Q2. A graph represents a line with equation y=−3x+4y=-3x+4y=−3x+4. Without calculating additional points, what two features of the graph can be identified directly from the equation?

A.It crosses the y-axis at 444 and decreases from left to right ✅
B.It crosses the x-axis at 444 and increases from left to right
C.It crosses the y-axis at −3-3−3 and increases from left to right
D.It crosses the x-axis at −3-3−3 and decreases from left to right
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: In slope-intercept form y=mx+by=mx+by=mx+b, mmm determines the direction and steepness while bbb gives the y-intercept. Here m=−3m=-3m=−3, so the line decreases, and b=4b=4b=4, so it crosses the y-axis at 444.

Q4. A student sees a line crossing the y-axis at −2-2−2 and writes y=−2x+3y=-2x+3y=−2x+3. The graph rises from left to right at a rate of 111 unit vertically for every 222 units horizontally. What is the student's mistake?

📖 Explanation: The graph rises, so its slope is positive. A rise of 111 for a run of 222 gives m=1/2m=1/2m=1/2, while the y-intercept is −2-2−2. The correct equation is y=(1/2)x−2y=(1/2)x-2y=(1/2)x−2.

Therefore y=2x−6y=2x-6y=2x−6 is actually consistent with both graph features, so the student's claim is correct; option D states those same features but incorrectly labels the claim as incorrect.
