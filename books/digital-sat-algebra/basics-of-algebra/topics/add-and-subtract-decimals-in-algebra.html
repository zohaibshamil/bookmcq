What is Add and Subtract Decimals in Algebra?

Definition:
Adding and subtracting decimals in algebra involves combining decimal numbers by aligning decimal points vertically, then adding or subtracting as with whole numbers, ensuring that the decimal point in the result is placed directly below the column of decimal points.

Working:
Write numbers with decimal points aligned; add zeros as placeholders if needed to make equal number of decimal places; add or subtract digits from right to left, carrying or borrowing as necessary; place the decimal point in the answer at the same position.

Example:
Subtract \( 15.6 - 3.75 \).
Solution: Align: \( 15.60 - 3.75 = 11.85 \).

Reason:
Decimal addition/subtraction is essential for solving algebraic problems involving money, measurements, and data analysis, and it reinforces place value understanding, which is critical for precise calculations.

📝 All Add and Subtract Decimals in Algebra MCQs

Q1. A shopkeeper records sales of \(125.75\), \(89.6\), and \(47.85\) dollars over three transactions, then refunds \(32.4\) dollars. What is the final amount kept?

A. 230.8 ✅
B. 231.2
C. 263.2
D. 295.6

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

First add the three sales: \(125.75+89.60+47.85=263.20\). Then subtract the refund: \(263.20-32.40=230.80\). The main misconception is forgetting to align decimal places or subtracting the refund before completing the total.

Q2. Two students calculate \(14.08-6.9\). Student A writes \(14.08-6.90=7.18\), while Student B writes \(14.08-6.9=7.98\). Which reasoning correctly identifies the error?

A. Student A is wrong because zeros cannot be added to decimals.
B. Student B is wrong because \(6.9=6.90\), giving \(7.18\). ✅
C. Both students are correct because decimal digits cannot be aligned.
D. Student A is wrong because subtraction must begin from the tenths place.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A trailing zero does not change a decimal's value, so \(6.9\) equals \(6.90\). Aligning decimal points gives \(14.08-6.90=7.18\). Student B incorrectly subtracts digits without preserving place value.

Q3. A runner completes three sections of a race measuring \(2.75\) km, \(3.6\) km, and \(1.85\) km. The planned distance is \(8.5\) km. How much farther must the runner travel?

A. \(0.20\) km
B. \(0.30\) km ✅
C. \(0.40\) km
D. \(0.50\) km

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The completed distance is \(2.75+3.60+1.85=8.20\) km. Comparing this with the planned \(8.50\) km gives \(8.50-8.20=0.30\) km. Therefore, the runner needs \(0.30\) km more. The realistic error is confusing \(0.30\) with \(0.40\) through poor decimal alignment.

Q4. A number-line display marks \(3.25\), \(3.7\), and \(4.05\). A student claims the distance from \(3.25\) to \(4.05\) is \(0.60\). What is the correct distance, and why?

A. \(0.60\), because only tenths matter
B. \(0.70\), because \(4.05-3.25=0.70\)
C. \(0.80\), because \(4.05-3.25=0.80\) ✅
D. \(1.20\), because the endpoints must be added

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Distance on a number line is found by subtracting the smaller coordinate from the larger. Writing \(4.05-3.25\) with aligned decimal places gives \(0.80\). The graph interpretation reinforces that distance measures the interval between two positions.

Q5. A container holds \(18.5\) liters of water. First, \(4.75\) liters are removed, then \(6.8\) liters are added. A student calculates \(18.5-4.75+6.8=19.55\) liters. Is the result correct?

A. Yes, because addition and subtraction can always be performed from left to right.
B. No, the correct amount is \(20.55\) liters. ✅
C. No, the correct amount is \(20.35\) liters.
D. No, the correct amount is \(17.55\) liters.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

After removing \(4.75\) liters, the container has \(18.50-4.75=13.75\) liters. Adding \(6.80\) liters gives \(20.55\) liters. The student's arithmetic is incorrect even though the expression setup is appropriate.

Q6. A budget starts at \(250.00\) dollars. Three expenses are \(48.75\), \(36.40\), and \(27.85\) dollars, while a refund of \(15.50\) dollars is received. Which expression correctly models the remaining budget?

A. \(250.00-48.75-36.40-27.85+15.50\) ✅
B. \(250.00+48.75+36.40+27.85-15.50\)
C. \(250.00-48.75+36.40-27.85-15.50\)
D. \(250.00-(48.75-36.40-27.85)+15.50\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Expenses reduce the starting budget, so each expense is subtracted. A refund increases the remaining budget, so it is added. Therefore, the correct model is \(250.00-48.75-36.40-27.85+15.50\).

Q7. Find two decimals \(x\) and \(y\) such that \(x+y=10.00\) and \(x-y=2.40\). Which pair satisfies both conditions?

A. \(x=5.80,\ y=4.20\)
B. \(x=6.20,\ y=3.80\) ✅
C. \(x=6.40,\ y=3.60\)
D. \(x=7.20,\ y=2.80\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The pair must satisfy both equations simultaneously. Testing \(x=6.20\) and \(y=3.80\), their sum is \(10.00\) and their difference is \(2.40\). This requires coordinating addition and subtraction rather than solving only one condition.

📖 Explanation:

Writing \(7.5\) as \(7.500\) and \(12.38\) as \(12.380\) does not change their values. The decimal points are vertically aligned, allowing each digit to occupy the correct place-value column during addition.

Q9. A recipe requires \(2.75\) cups of flour, \(0.8\) cups of sugar, and \(1.125\) cups of another ingredient. A student writes the numbers without adding zeros. What is the best setup?

A. \(2.75+0.8+1.125\) with decimal points aligned ✅
B. \(2.75+8.0+1.125\)
C. \(2.75+0.08+1.125\)
D. \(2.750+8.00+1.125\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The numbers may have different numbers of decimal digits, but their decimal points must occupy the same vertical position. Trailing zeros are optional, so \(2.75\), \(0.8\), and \(1.125\) can be aligned directly without changing their values.

Q10. A student subtracts \(15.2-7.86\) by placing the \(6\) directly under the \(2\), producing \(8.60\). Which statement best explains the mistake?

A. The student should subtract whole numbers before decimals.
B. The decimal points were not aligned, so the place values were mismatched. ✅
C. The student should always add zeros to the left of every decimal.
D. The answer must contain exactly one decimal digit.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The \(2\) in \(15.2\) represents tenths, while the \(6\) in \(7.86\) represents hundredths. Aligning the decimal points ensures tenths match tenths and hundredths match hundredths. The incorrect setup changes the place values.

Q11. A number line shows points at \(4.6\), \(4.08\), and \(4.75\). A student says \(4.08\) should appear to the right of \(4.6\) because \(8>6\). Which conclusion is correct?

A. The student is correct because \(8>6\).
B. The student is correct because hundredths are larger than tenths.
C. The student is incorrect because \(4.08<4.60\), so it lies to the left of \(4.6\). ✅
D. The points cannot be compared because they have different decimal lengths.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

To compare decimals, corresponding place values must be considered. Writing \(4.6\) as \(4.60\) makes the comparison clear: \(4.08<4.60<4.75\). Therefore, \(4.08\) appears to the left of \(4.6\) on the number line.

Q12. A store receipt lists prices of \(18.95\), \(6.7\), and \(3.085\) dollars. The cashier wants to verify the total manually. Which arrangement is most reliable for minimizing place-value errors?

A. \(18.95+6.7+3.085\) with decimal points vertically aligned ✅
B. \(18.95+67+3.085\)
C. \(1895+6.7+3085\)
D. \(18.950+67.000+3.085\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The safest arrangement keeps every decimal point in the same column. Although \(6.7\) could be written as \(6.700\), it must remain \(6.700\), not \(67.000\). Alignment preserves the original place values and prevents a tenfold error.

Q13. Two methods are proposed for calculating \(23.4-8.275\). Method 1 changes \(23.4\) to \(23.400\) and subtracts \(8.275\). Method 2 changes \(8.275\) to \(8.27\) and subtracts. Which comparison is correct?

A. Only Method 1 preserves the exact value of the subtraction. ✅
B. Only Method 2 preserves the exact value because trailing digits are irrelevant.
C. Both methods always produce different values.
D. Neither method can be used because decimals must have equal lengths.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Method 1 is valid because \(23.4=23.400\), so adding trailing zeros preserves its exact value. Method 2 changes \(8.275\) to \(8.27\), which changes the number itself. Therefore, Method 1 preserves the original subtraction.

Writing the numbers as \(9.070\), \(0.893\), and \(14.600\) creates matching decimal columns without changing their values. A careless setup can shift digits into different place-value columns, producing an answer that may look reasonable but is mathematically incorrect.

Q16. A student claims \(8.4+2.75=10.115\) because they added \(8+2\), then combined the decimal digits \(4+75\). What is the best evaluation of this reasoning?

A. The reasoning is correct because decimal digits can be combined separately.

The decimal parts must be aligned by place value. Writing \(8.4\) as \(8.40\) gives \(8.40+2.75=11.15\). The student's \(10.115\) treats the decimal digits as unrelated whole numbers rather than tenths and hundredths.

Q17. A water tank contains \(35.75\) liters. During the morning, \(8.6\) liters are used. Later, \(4.275\) liters are added. How much water is in the tank afterward?

Q18. A number line has points at \(2.35\), \(2.8\), and \(3.15\). What is the total distance traveled when moving from \(2.35\) to \(2.8\), then from \(2.8\) to \(3.15\)?

A. 0.45

📖 Explanation:

The first movement is \(2.80-2.35=0.45\), and the second is \(3.15-2.80=0.35\). Their sum is \(0.80\). The tempting \(1.25\) results from adding the coordinates instead of calculating distances between consecutive points.

Q19. A budget of \(100.00\) dollars is changed by three transactions: a payment of \(23.75\), a deposit of \(18.50\), and another payment of \(9.625\). Which expression correctly models the final balance?

A. \(100.00-23.75+18.50-9.625\) ✅
B. \(100.00+23.75-18.50+9.625\)

D. \(100.00+23.75+18.50-9.625\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Payments reduce the balance, while a deposit increases it. Therefore, the correct model subtracts \(23.75\), adds \(18.50\), and subtracts \(9.625\). This produces \(85.125\) dollars and demonstrates how decimal operations model sequential financial changes.

Q20. Two students solve \(17.05-9.8+2.375\). Student A obtains \(9.625\), while Student B obtains \(9.675\). Which result is correct, and what key step supports it?

A. Student A, because \(17.05-9.80=7.25\), then \(7.25+2.375=9.625\). ✅
B. Student B, because \(17.05-9.80=7.25\), then \(7.25+2.375=9.675\).
C. Student A, because decimal places should never be padded with zeros.
D. Student B, because \(9.8\) is larger than \(9.80\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Rewrite \(9.8\) as \(9.800\) and \(7.25\) as \(7.250\). Then \(17.050-9.800=7.250\), followed by \(7.250+2.375=9.625\). The error in Student B's work is a hundredths-place addition mistake.

Q21. Find the value of \(x\) if \(x+3.75-1.28=10.00\). Which value satisfies the equation?

A. 6.25
B. 7.53
C. 8.03 ✅
D. 8.53

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Combine the known decimal changes: \(3.75-1.28=2.47\). Therefore, \(x+2.47=10.00\), so \(x=10.00-2.47=7.53\). Thus option B is correct. This requires reversing the combined addition and subtraction rather than simply calculating forward.
