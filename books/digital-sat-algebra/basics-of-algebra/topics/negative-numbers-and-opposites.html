What is Negative numbers and opposites?

Definition:
Negative numbers are numbers less than zero, represented with a minus sign (\( - \)), while the opposite of a number is the number that is the same distance from zero on the number line but in the opposite direction, so the opposite of \( a \) is \( -a \), and the sum of a number and its opposite is zero.

Working:
To find the opposite, change the sign of the number: the opposite of \( 5 \) is \( -5 \), and the opposite of \( -3 \) is \( 3 \); on a number line, negative numbers lie to the left of zero, and the absolute value of a number is its distance from zero, ignoring sign.

Example:
Find the opposite of \( -7 \) and compare \( -4 \) and \( -9 \).
Solution: Opposite of \( -7 \) is \( 7 \); on number line, \( -4 \) is to the right of \( -9 \), so \( -4 > -9 \).

Reason:
Understanding negatives and opposites is essential for operations with integers, solving equations like \( x + 5 = 0 \), and interpreting directed quantities in physics, finance, and everyday contexts like debt or temperature below zero.

📝 All Negative numbers and opposites MCQs

Q1. A number line shows a point at \(-7\). Its opposite is represented by moving the same distance from zero in the other direction. Which value represents the opposite, and why?

A.\(-14\), because the distance doubles
B.\(7\), because opposites are equally distant from zero on opposite sides ✅
C.\(-7\), because the point does not change
D.\(0\), because zero is between all opposites
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The opposite of a number has the same distance from zero but lies on the other side. Since \(-7\) is seven units left of zero, its opposite is \(7\), seven units right of zero.

Q2. A student says, The opposite of \(-12\) is \(-(-12)=-12\), because two negative signs should keep the result negative." Which response best evaluates the student's reasoning?"

A.Correct, because negative signs always produce a negative result
B.Correct, because opposites must have the same sign
C.Incorrect, because applying the opposite operation changes \(-12\) to \(12\) ✅
D.Incorrect, because the opposite of every number is zero
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The student's mistake is treating two negative signs as automatically producing a negative value. The expression \(-(-12)\) means the opposite of \(-12\), which is \(12\).

Q3. A submarine is \(18\) meters below sea level, represented by \(-18\). It rises \(7\) meters and then moves to the position opposite its current position relative to sea level. What is its final position?

A.11 meters below sea level
B.11 meters above sea level ✅
C.25 meters above sea level
D.25 meters below sea level
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Starting at \(-18\), rising \(7\) meters gives \(-11\). The position opposite to \(-11\) is \(11\), so the submarine's final position is \(11\) meters above sea level.

Q4. A student evaluates \(-(-5)+(-3)\) as \(-8\). Which step contains the error, and what is the correct result?

A.The first term should remain \(-5\); the result is \(-8\)
B.The opposite of \(-5\) is \(5\); therefore the result is \(2\) ✅
C.The second term should become \(3\); therefore the result is \(8\)
D.Both negatives cancel, so the result is \(0\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The expression begins with \(-(-5)\), which means the opposite of \(-5\), giving \(5\). Then \(5+(-3)=2\). The error was failing to interpret the nested negative correctly.

Q5. On a number line, point \(P\) is at \(-6\), while point \(Q\) is at \(4\). A third point \(R\) is placed at the opposite of \(P\). Which statement correctly compares \(Q\) and \(R\)?

A.\(Q\) is 10 units left of \(R\)
B.\(Q\) and \(R\) are at the same position
C.\(Q\) is 2 units left of \(R\)
D.\(Q\) is 2 units right of \(R\) ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The opposite of \(P=-6\) is \(R=6\). Since \(Q=4\), it lies two units to the left of \(R=6\). Therefore \(Q\) is not at the same location and is not to the right.

Q6. A temperature changes from \(-9^\circ\text{C}\) to its opposite value. It then decreases by \(4^\circ\text{C}\). Which expression and result correctly model the situation?

A.\(-(-9)-4=5\) ✅
B.\(-9-(-9)-4=-4\)
C.\(-(-9)+4=13\)
D.\(9+(-4)=5\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The opposite of \(-9\) is \(9\). Decreasing by \(4\) gives \(9-4=5\). Thus \(-(-9)-4=5\) correctly models both transformations and produces the final temperature.

Q7. A game awards the opposite of a player's penalty. A player receives a penalty of \(-x\) points, where \(x>0\). The player then loses \(3\) more points. Which expression represents the final score change, and what happens when \(x=8\)?

A.\(-x-3=-11\)
B.\(x-3=5\) ✅
C.\(-x+3=-5\)
D.\(x+3=11\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The opposite of the penalty \(-x\) is \(x\), so the score change becomes \(x\). Losing \(3\) points gives \(x-3\). When \(x=8\), the result is \(5\), requiring both symbolic reasoning and substitution.

Q8. A bank account has a balance of \(-\$45\). The owner deposits \(\$70\) and then withdraws \(\$30\). What is the final balance, and what does its sign indicate?

A.\(-\$5\), meaning the account still owes money ✅
B.\(-\$5\), meaning the account has positive savings
C.\(\$5\), meaning the account has money available
D.\(\$155\), meaning all transactions increase the balance
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Starting with \(-45\), adding \(70\) gives \(25\). Subtracting \(30\) gives \(25-30=-5\), not \(5\). Therefore, the correct balance is actually \(-\$5\), indicating money is still owed. None of the listed choices correctly states both the calculation and interpretation, so the intended correct choice is A.

Q9. Which statement best explains why \(-8\) is greater than \(-12\), even though \(8\) is smaller than \(12\)?

A.Because negative numbers become greater as they move farther left
B.Because \(-8\) is closer to zero and therefore represents a greater value ✅
C.Because the larger absolute value is always the greater negative number
D.Because subtracting two negative signs makes every negative number positive
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: On a number line, values increase as they move to the right. Since \(-8\) lies to the right of \(-12\), it is greater. Its smaller distance from zero also helps explain why \(-8\) represents the greater negative value.

Q10. A temperature graph shows the temperature changing from \(-6^\circ\text{C}\) at 6 a.m. to \(-1^\circ\text{C}\) at noon, then to \(-4^\circ\text{C}\) at 6 p.m. During which interval did the temperature increase the most?

A.6 a.m. to noon ✅
B.Noon to 6 p.m.
C.Both intervals increased equally
D.Neither interval showed an increase
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: From 6 a.m. to noon, the temperature changes from \(-6\) to \(-1\), an increase of \(5^\circ\text{C}\). From noon to 6 p.m., it decreases by \(3^\circ\text{C}\). Therefore, the first interval has the greatest increase.

Q11. A student claims that \(-15+9=-24\) because both numbers should be combined using addition. Which explanation identifies the error and gives the correct result?

A.The student should add absolute values; the result is \(24\)
B.The positive number must be ignored; the result is \(-15\)
C.The numbers have opposite signs, so their absolute values are compared; the result is \(-6\) ✅
D.Both signs cancel automatically; the result is \(6\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: When adding numbers with opposite signs, compare their absolute values and subtract the smaller from the larger. Since \(15>9\), the result has the sign of \(-15\), giving \(-6\).

Q12. A hiker begins at an elevation of \(-20\) meters relative to a reference level. She climbs \(13\) meters, descends \(8\) meters, and then climbs \(6\) meters. What is her final elevation?

A.\(-9\) meters ✅
B.\(-3\) meters
C.\(1\) meter
D.\(7\) meters
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Starting at \(-20\), climbing \(13\) gives \(-7\). Descending \(8\) gives \(-15\), and climbing \(6\) gives \(-9\). Therefore, the mathematically correct final elevation is \(-9\) meters, making option A correct.

Q13. On a number line, point \(A\) is at \(-11\), point \(B\) is at \(-3\), and point \(C\) is at \(5\). Which comparison is correct?

A.\(A>B>C\)
B.\(C>B>A\) ✅
C.\(B>C>A\)
D.\(A>C>B\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Number-line values increase from left to right. Therefore, \(-11\) is the smallest, \(-3\) is next, and \(5\) is the greatest. The correct ordering from greatest to least is \(5>-3>-11\).

Q14. Three consecutive integers have a sum of \(-18\). Which set could they be?

A.\(-8,-6,-4\)
B.\(-7,-6,-5\) ✅
C.\(-6,-5,-4\)
D.\(-9,-6,-3\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Let the middle integer be \(n\). Three consecutive integers are \(n-1,n,n+1\), whose sum is \(3n\). Setting \(3n=-18\) gives \(n=-6\), so the integers are \(-7,-6,-5\). This requires modeling rather than simple calculation.

Q15. Which statement correctly describes the relationship between \(-9\) and \(4\) on a number line?

A.\(-9\) is greater because \(9>4\)
B.\(-9\) is less because it lies farther left than \(4\) ✅
C.Both numbers are equal because they are integers
D.\(4\) is less because it is closer to zero
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Integers are ordered by their positions on the number line, with values increasing from left to right. Since \(-9\) lies to the left of \(4\), \(-9\) is less than \(4\), regardless of its larger absolute value.

Q16. A player starts a game with \(6\) points, loses \(11\) points, gains \(8\) points, and then loses \(5\) points. What is the final score?

A.2
B.\(-2\) ✅
C.18
D.\(-18\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The score changes can be modeled as \(6-11+8-5\). First, \(6-11=-5\), then \(-5+8=3\), and finally \(3-5=-2\). Therefore, the player's final score is \(-2\).

Q17. A student calculates \((-7)+(-5)=2\) and explains that the two negative signs cancel. What is the best evaluation of this reasoning?

A.Correct, because any two negative integers produce a positive sum
B.Correct, because addition always removes negative signs
C.Incorrect, because numbers with the same negative sign combine to give \(-12\) ✅
D.Incorrect, because the answer must always be zero when both numbers are negative
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: When adding two negative integers, their distances from zero combine and the result remains negative. Thus \((-7)+(-5)=-(7+5)=-12\). Negative signs do not automatically cancel during addition.

Q18. A number line records four positions: \(A=-8\), \(B=-2\), \(C=3\), and \(D=7\). A student claims the distance from \(A\) to \(B\) is \(6\), while the distance from \(C\) to \(D\) is \(4\). Which conclusion is correct?

A.Only the first distance is correct
B.Only the second distance is correct
C.Both distances are correct ✅
D.Both distances are incorrect because negative integers cannot have distance
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The distance between two integer positions is the absolute difference. For \(A\) and \(B\), \(|-8-(-2)|=6\). For \(C\) and \(D\), \(|3-7|=4\). Therefore, both calculations are correct.

Q19. A temperature is \(-4^\circ\text{C}\). It falls \(7^\circ\text{C}\), rises \(12^\circ\text{C}\), and then falls \(5^\circ\text{C}\). What temperature is recorded at the end?

A.\(-4^\circ\text{C}\) ✅
B.\(0^\circ\text{C}\)
C.\(4^\circ\text{C}\)
D.\(6^\circ\text{C}\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The changes can be represented by \(-4-7+12-5\). Starting with \(-4\), falling \(7\) gives \(-11\), rising \(12\) gives \(1\), and falling \(5\) gives \(-4\). The final temperature is \(-4^\circ\text{C}\).

Q20. Three consecutive integers have a sum of \(15\). Which set of integers satisfies the condition?

A.\(3,4,5\) ✅
B.\(4,5,6\)
C.\(2,5,8\)
D.\(1,6,8\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Three consecutive integers can be represented as \(n-1,n,n+1\). Their sum is \(3n\), so \(3n=15\) gives \(n=5\). Therefore, the consecutive integers are \(4,5,6\), making option B the correct set.

Q21. A student compares \((-3)(-4)+(-2)\) with \((-3)[(-4)+(-2)]\) and says they must be equal because both expressions contain the same integers. Which comparison is correct?

A.Both equal \(10\)
B.Both equal \(-18\)
C.The first equals \(10\), while the second equals \(18\) ✅
D.The first equals \(10\), while the second equals \(18\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The first expression is \((-3)(-4)+(-2)=12-2=10\). The second is \((-3)[(-4)+(-2)]=(-3)(-6)=18\). The grouping changes which operations are performed together, so the expressions are not equal.

Q22. Which ordering lists the integers from least to greatest?

A.\(-2,-7,0,4\)
B.\(-7,-2,0,4\) ✅
C.\(4,0,-2,-7\)
D.\(0,-2,-7,4\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: On a number line, values increase as you move from left to right. Therefore, the integer farthest left is the smallest. Since \(-7\) is left of \(-2\), followed by \(0\) and \(4\), the correct order is \(-7,-2,0,4\).

Q23. Four locations have elevations represented by \(-12\), \(5\), \(-3\), and \(0\) meters. Which location has the second-lowest elevation?

A.The location at \(-12\) meters
B.The location at \(-3\) meters ✅
C.The location at \(0\) meters
D.The location at \(5\) meters
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Ordering the elevations from lowest to highest gives \(-12,-3,0,5\). The second value in this ordering is \(-3\), so the location represented by \(-3\) meters has the second-lowest elevation.

Q24. A student orders \(-15,-6,-10,2\) as \(-15,-6,-10,2\). What is the most important error in the student's reasoning?

A.The positive integer must always come first
B.Negative integers must be ordered by their distance from zero in reverse size ✅
C.Zero should always appear between every pair of negative integers
D.The largest absolute value must always be the greatest number
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: For negative integers, a number with greater distance from zero is actually smaller. Since \(-15<-10<-6\), the correct order is \(-15,-10,-6,2\). The student incorrectly treated larger absolute values as larger numbers.

Q25. A number line contains \(A=-9\), \(B=-4\), \(C=1\), and \(D=6\). A token starts at \(B\), moves \(7\) units left, and then \(12\) units right. Which labeled point does it reach?

A.\(A\)
B.\(B\)
C.\(C\) ✅
D.\(D\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The token starts at \(-4\). Moving \(7\) units left gives \(-11\). Moving \(12\) units right gives \(1\). Since \(C=1\), the token finishes at point \(C\). This requires tracking signed movement rather than simply comparing labels.

Q26. A weather station records temperatures of \(-8^\circ\text{C}\), \(-2^\circ\text{C}\), \(3^\circ\text{C}\), and \(-11^\circ\text{C}\). Which pair represents the greatest increase when moving from the colder temperature to the warmer temperature?

A.\(-11^\circ\text{C}\) to \(3^\circ\text{C}\) ✅
B.\(-8^\circ\text{C}\) to \(-2^\circ\text{C}\)
C.\(-11^\circ\text{C}\) to \(-2^\circ\text{C}\)
D.\(-8^\circ\text{C}\) to \(3^\circ\text{C}\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The increase between two temperatures is found by subtracting the lower value from the higher value. From \(-11\) to \(3\), the increase is \(14^\circ\text{C}\), which is greater than the other possible increases.

Q27. Which statement correctly compares \(-18\), \(-9\), and \(4\)?

A.\(-18>-9>4\)
B.\(-9>-18>4\)
C.\(4>-9>-18\) ✅
D.\(4>-18>-9\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Moving from left to right on the number line means values increase. Thus \(4\) is greatest, \(-9\) is next, and \(-18\) is smallest. Therefore, the correct comparison is \(4>-9>-18\).

Q28. Five consecutive integer positions are occupied by numbers whose sum is \(-35\). If the integers are equally spaced on a number line, which is the greatest integer?

A.\(-9\)
B.\(-7\)
C.\(-5\) ✅
D.\(-3\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Five consecutive integers can be represented as \(n-2,n-1,n,n+1,n+2\). Their sum is \(5n=-35\), so \(n=-7\). The five integers are \(-9,-8,-7,-6,-5\), making \(-5\) the greatest. Therefore, option C is correct.

Q29. What is the opposite of \(-17\), and what property do the two numbers share?

A.\(-17\); they are both negative
B.\(17\); they are the same distance from zero but on opposite sides ✅
C.\(0\); it lies between all opposite numbers
D.\(34\); their distances from zero are added
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The opposite of \(-17\) is \(17\). Both numbers are exactly \(17\) units from zero, but they lie on opposite sides of zero. This equal distance with reversed direction defines a pair of opposites.

Q30. A bank balance is represented by \(-\$60\). A transaction changes the balance to its opposite value. The customer then withdraws \(\$15\). What is the resulting balance?

A.45 dollars ✅
B.75 dollars
C.-45 dollars
D.-75 dollars
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The opposite of \(-60\) is \(60\). After withdrawing \(15\), the balance becomes \(60-15=45\), so the mathematically correct answer is \(45\) dollars. Therefore, option A is correct.

Q31. A student claims that the opposite of \(8\) is \(-8\), but then says the opposite of \(-8\) is also \(-8\). Which evaluation is most accurate?

A.Both statements are correct because opposites can repeat
B.The first statement is correct, but the second is incorrect because the opposite of \(-8\) is \(8\) ✅
C.The first statement is incorrect because \(8\) has no opposite
D.Both statements are incorrect because only zero has an opposite
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The opposite of a positive number is negative, so the opposite of \(8\) is \(-8\). Applying the same idea again reverses the sign, making the opposite of \(-8\) equal to \(8\), not \(-8\).

Q32. On a number line, point \(P\) is located at \(-6\). Point \(Q\) is placed at the opposite of \(P\). Point \(R\) is located \(4\) units to the left of \(Q\). Where is \(R\) located?

A.\(-10\)
B.\(-2\) ✅
C.2
D.-10
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The opposite of \(P=-6\) is \(Q=6\). Moving \(4\) units left from \(6\) gives \(6-4=2\). Therefore, the correct location is \(2\), making option C correct.

Q33. A temperature is \(-12^\circ\text{C}\). Its opposite value is recorded, and then the temperature decreases by \(5^\circ\text{C}\). What is the final value?

A.\(-17^\circ\text{C}\)
B.\(-7^\circ\text{C}\)
C.\(7^\circ\text{C}\) ✅
D.\(17^\circ\text{C}\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The opposite of \(-12\) is \(12\). Decreasing that value by \(5\) gives \(12-5=7\). Therefore, the final value is \(7^\circ\text{C}\), making option C correct.

Q34. Which pair of expressions produces the same result?

A.The opposite of \(9\) and \(-9\) ✅
B.The opposite of \(-14\) and \(14\)
C.The opposite of \(5\) and \(5\)
D.The opposite of \(0\) and \(1\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The opposite of \(9\) is \(-9\), so both expressions represent the same value. Similarly, the opposite of \(-14\) is \(14\), but those are different values. Zero is its own opposite, while \(1\) is not.

Q35. Two integers \(a\) and \(b\) are opposites. Their sum is then multiplied by \(25\). What must the final result be, regardless of the value of \(a\)?

A.\(-25\)
B.0 ✅
C.25
D.It depends on the value of \(a\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: If \(a\) and \(b\) are opposites, then \(b=-a\). Their sum is \(a+(-a)=0\). Multiplying this result by \(25\) still gives \(0\), so the final result does not depend on the particular integer chosen.
