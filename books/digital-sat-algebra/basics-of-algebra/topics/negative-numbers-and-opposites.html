What is Negative numbers and opposites?

Definition:
Negative numbers are numbers less than zero, represented with a minus sign (− - −), while the opposite of a number is the number that is the same distance from zero on the number line but in the opposite direction, so the opposite of a a a is −a -a −a, and the sum of a number and its opposite is zero.

Working:
To find the opposite, change the sign of the number: the opposite of 5 5 5 is −5 -5 −5, and the opposite of −3 -3 −3 is 3 3 3; on a number line, negative numbers lie to the left of zero, and the absolute value of a number is its distance from zero, ignoring sign.

Example:
Find the opposite of −7 -7 −7 and compare −4 -4 −4 and −9 -9 −9.
Solution: Opposite of −7 -7 −7 is 7 7 7; on number line, −4 -4 −4 is to the right of −9 -9 −9, so −4>−9 -4 > -9 −4>−9.

Reason:
Understanding negatives and opposites is essential for operations with integers, solving equations like x+5=0 x + 5 = 0 x+5=0, and interpreting directed quantities in physics, finance, and everyday contexts like debt or temperature below zero.
