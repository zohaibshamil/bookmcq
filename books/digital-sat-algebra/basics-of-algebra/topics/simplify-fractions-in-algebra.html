What is Simplify Fractions in Algebra?

Definition:
Simplifying fractions in algebra means reducing a fraction to its lowest terms by dividing the numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor (GCF), so that they have no common factors other than 1 1 1, resulting in a fraction that is easier to work with in expressions and equations.

Working:
Find the GCF of the numerator and denominator, then divide both by that number; for algebraic fractions, factor both numerator and denominator completely, then cancel any common factors (variables or numbers) from both.

Example:
Simplify 1824 \frac{18}{24} 2418​.
Solution: GCF of 18 and 24 is 6; divide both by 6: 18÷624÷6=34 \frac{18 \div 6}{24 \div 6} = \frac{3}{4} 24÷618÷6​=43​.

Reason:
Simplification makes fractions less cumbersome, helps in comparing sizes, and is crucial in algebra for solving equations, combining terms, and performing operations like multiplication and division of rational expressions.

Q1. A student simplifies 84126\frac{84}{126}12684​ by dividing both numbers by 666, obtaining 1421\frac{14}{21}2114​. What should the student do next to reach simplest form?

A.Divide numerator and denominator by 222 ✅
B.Multiply numerator and denominator by 333
C.Subtract 777 from both numbers
D.The fraction is already simplest
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The fraction 1421\frac{14}{21}2114​ still has a common factor of 777. Dividing both numerator and denominator by 777 gives 23\frac{2}{3}32​, so dividing by 222 is not the correct next step. The key is to continue reducing using a common factor.

C.Both methods can produce the same simplest fraction ✅

📖 Explanation: Both 121212 and 242424 are common factors of 969696 and 144144144. Dividing by 121212 gives 812\frac{8}{12}128​, which reduces to 23\frac{2}{3}32​, while dividing directly by 242424 gives 46\frac{4}{6}64​, which also reduces to 23\frac{2}{3}32​.
