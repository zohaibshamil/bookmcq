What is Conversion of U.S. and the Metric Systems of Measurement?

Definition:
Conversion between the U.S. and metric systems of measurement involves changing units from one system to another using approximate conversion factors (e.g., 1 inch=2.54 cm 1 \text{ inch} = 2.54 \text{ cm} 1 inch=2.54 cm, 1 mile≈1.609 km 1 \text{ mile} \approx 1.609 \text{ km} 1 mile≈1.609 km), which are essential for international communication and scientific work.

Working:
Set up a conversion factor (ratio) that relates the two units, arrange it to cancel the original unit, multiply to get the desired unit, and round to appropriate precision, often using dimensional analysis with multiple steps.

Example:
Convert 5 miles to kilometers.
Solution: 1 mile≈1.609 km 1 \text{ mile} \approx 1.609 \text{ km} 1 mile≈1.609 km; so 5 miles×1.609 km1 mile=8.045 km≈8.05 km 5 \text{ miles} \times \frac{1.609 \text{ km}}{1 \text{ mile}} = 8.045 \text{ km} \approx 8.05 \text{ km} 5 miles×1 mile1.609 km​=8.045 km≈8.05 km.

Reason:
This conversion skill is vital for global collaboration, travel, and interpreting data from different sources, and it applies proportional reasoning and algebraic manipulation to solve practical problems.
