What is Conversion of U.S. and the Metric Systems of Measurement?

Definition:
Conversion between the U.S. and metric systems of measurement involves changing units from one system to another using approximate conversion factors (e.g., \( 1 \text{ inch} = 2.54 \text{ cm} \), \( 1 \text{ mile} \approx 1.609 \text{ km} \)), which are essential for international communication and scientific work.

Working:
Set up a conversion factor (ratio) that relates the two units, arrange it to cancel the original unit, multiply to get the desired unit, and round to appropriate precision, often using dimensional analysis with multiple steps.

Example:
Convert 5 miles to kilometers.
Solution: \( 1 \text{ mile} \approx 1.609 \text{ km} \); so \( 5 \text{ miles} \times \frac{1.609 \text{ km}}{1 \text{ mile}} = 8.045 \text{ km} \approx 8.05 \text{ km} \).

Reason:
This conversion skill is vital for global collaboration, travel, and interpreting data from different sources, and it applies proportional reasoning and algebraic manipulation to solve practical problems.

Q1. A road sign shows a distance of 12 miles. Using \(1\text{ mile}\approx1.609\text{ km}\), which value is the best estimate of the distance in kilometers?

A. 18.1 km
B. 19.3 km ✅
C. 20.4 km
D. 21.7 km

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

To convert miles to kilometers, multiply by \(1.609\). Thus \(12(1.609)=19.308\), which rounds to about \(19.3\) km. The distractors reflect common errors such as using an incorrect conversion factor or adding instead of multiplying.

Q2. A student claims that \(5\) feet is about \(1.52\) meters because \(1\) foot is approximately \(0.3048\) meter. Another student says it should be \(0.06096\) meter. Which reasoning is correct?

A. Only the first student, because the conversion factor must be multiplied by 5 ✅
B. Only the second student, because the conversion factor must be divided by 5
C. Both students, because multiplication and division give equivalent conversions
D. Neither student, because feet cannot be converted into meters

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The first student correctly multiplies \(5\) by \(0.3048\), obtaining \(1.524\) meters. The second student incorrectly divides the conversion factor by 5, reversing the operation needed when converting five feet.

Q3. A recipe requires \(3\) cups of flour. Using \(1\) U.S. cup \(\approx236.6\) milliliters, a baker wants to prepare the same amount using a \(500\)-mL measuring container. How many full containers and approximately how much additional flour are needed?

A. 1 full container and 210 mL ✅
B. 1 full container and 420 mL
C. 2 full containers and 210 mL
D. 2 full containers and 420 mL

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Three cups contain approximately \(3(236.6)=709.8\) mL. Two full \(500\)-mL containers would contain \(1000\) mL, which is too much. Therefore one full container plus about \(209.8\) mL is sufficient, making option A correct; however, because the container must be filled completely only when measuring a full container, the practical interpretation requires one full container and about 210 mL. The best answer is A.

Q4. A student converts \(8\) inches to centimeters by calculating \(8\div2.54\). Another student calculates \(8(2.54)\). Which method correctly represents the conversion, and why?

A. Division is correct because centimeters are smaller units
B. Multiplication is correct because each inch represents \(2.54\) centimeters ✅
C. Both are correct because the two operations differ only by rounding
D. Division is correct because the number should decrease when changing units

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Since \(1\) inch equals \(2.54\) centimeters, every inch contributes \(2.54\) centimeters. Therefore \(8\) inches equals \(8(2.54)=20.32\) centimeters. Dividing by \(2.54\) would incorrectly make the measurement smaller.

Q5. A graph compares four package weights after conversion to kilograms. The plotted values are \(A=0.45\), \(B=0.91\), \(C=1.36\), and \(D=1.81\) kg. If the original weights were approximately \(1\), \(2\), \(3\), and \(4\) pounds respectively, what pattern does the graph support?

A. Each additional pound adds about \(0.45\) kg ✅
B. Each additional pound doubles the kilogram value
C. Each kilogram represents about \(0.45\) pounds
D. The relationship is nonlinear because the values increase unevenly

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The differences between consecutive kilogram values are approximately \(0.46\), \(0.45\), and \(0.45\) kg. This indicates a nearly constant conversion rate of about \(0.45\) kg per pound, consistent with multiplying pounds by approximately \(0.454\).

Q6. A cyclist travels \(15\) miles in the morning and \(8\) kilometers in the afternoon. Using \(1\text{ mile}\approx1.609\text{ km}\), which expression correctly represents the total distance in kilometers before rounding?

A. \(15+8(1.609)\)
B. \(15(1.609)+8\) ✅
C. \(15(1.609)+8(0.621)\)
D. \(15\div1.609+8\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The \(15\)-mile distance must first be converted to kilometers, giving \(15(1.609)\) km. The afternoon distance is already in kilometers, so it remains \(8\) km. Adding these quantities gives the total in one consistent unit.

Q7. A rectangular garden is \(10\) feet long and \(6\) feet wide. A designer wants dimensions in meters using \(1\text{ foot}\approx0.3048\text{ meter}\). Which approximate area is closest?

A. 1.83 square meters
B. 5.57 square meters ✅
C. 18.29 square meters
D. 56.69 square meters

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

First convert each dimension: \(10(0.3048)=3.048\) m and \(6(0.3048)=1.8288\) m. Multiplying gives approximately \(3.048(1.8288)=5.574\) square meters. Converting the area directly requires squaring the conversion factor, not using it only once.

Q8. A laboratory technician needs to convert \(5\) pounds of material into kilograms using \(1\text{ lb}\approx0.454\text{ kg}\). Which value is the closest reasonable estimate?

A. 1.10 kg
B. 2.27 kg ✅
C. 2.54 kg
D. 4.54 kg

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The appropriate conversion multiplies pounds by kilograms per pound: \(5(0.454)=2.27\) kg. A value near \(4.54\) would result from incorrectly using approximately \(0.908\) kg per pound, while dividing by the factor reverses the conversion.

Q9. A student remembers that \(1\) inch equals \(2.54\) centimeters and argues that \(10\) inches must equal \(2.54\) centimeters because the conversion factor does not change. What is the best evaluation?

A. Correct, because conversion factors are fixed
B. Incorrect, because \(10\) inches equals \(25.4\) centimeters ✅
C. Correct, because inches and centimeters measure different quantities
D. Incorrect, because \(10\) inches equals \(0.254\) centimeters

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The conversion factor remains fixed, but it must be applied to the entire measurement. Since \(1\) inch is \(2.54\) centimeters, \(10\) inches equals \(10(2.54)=25.4\) centimeters. The student's mistake is failing to scale the factor.

Q10. A runner completes \(3\) miles and then \(2\) kilometers. Using \(1\text{ mile}\approx1.609\text{ km}\), which calculation gives the total distance entirely in kilometers?

A. \(3+2(1.609)\)
B. \(3(1.609)+2\) ✅
C. \(3\div1.609+2\)
D. \(3(1.609)+2(1.609)\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The three-mile portion must be converted into kilometers, giving \(3(1.609)=4.827\) km. The additional \(2\) km already uses the desired unit, so it is added directly, producing \(6.827\) km before rounding.

Q11. A graph shows a straight-line relationship between a measurement in pounds on the horizontal axis and kilograms on the vertical axis. The graph passes near \((2,0.91)\) and \((6,2.72)\). Which conclusion best explains the graph?

A. Each pound corresponds to about \(0.45\) kilogram ✅
B. Each kilogram corresponds to exactly \(2\) pounds
C. The relationship cannot be represented by multiplication
D. The kilogram value decreases as pounds increase

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The ratio of kilograms to pounds is approximately \(0.91\div2=0.455\) and \(2.72\div6\approx0.453\). The nearly constant ratio shows a proportional relationship of about \(0.45\) kilogram for every pound.

Q12. A carpenter measures a board as \(8\) feet \(6\) inches. Using \(1\text{ foot}=0.3048\) meter and \(1\text{ inch}=0.0254\) meter, which method correctly finds its length in meters?

A. Convert only the feet, then ignore the inches
B. Convert feet and inches separately, then add the meter values ✅
C. Convert feet to inches first, then divide by \(0.3048\)
D. Multiply the feet by \(0.0254\) and inches by \(0.3048\), then add

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The mixed measurement contains two different units, so each part must be converted using its appropriate factor. \(8\) feet becomes \(8(0.3048)=2.4384\) meters, while \(6\) inches becomes \(6(0.0254)=0.1524\) meter. Their sum is \(2.5908\) meters.

Q13. Two students convert \(12\) inches to centimeters. Student A calculates \(12(2.54)=30.48\). Student B calculates \(12\div2.54\approx4.72\). Which statement best analyzes their methods?

A. Student A is correct because inches are multiplied by centimeters per inch ✅
B. Student B is correct because division is always used for unit conversion
C. Both are correct because multiplication and division produce equivalent units
D. Neither is correct because \(1\) inch equals \(2.54\) meters

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Because \(1\) inch corresponds to \(2.54\) centimeters, the conversion factor has units of centimeters per inch. Multiplying \(12\) inches by this factor cancels inches and leaves \(30.48\) centimeters. Dividing produces the wrong magnitude and unit relationship.

Q14. A delivery company charges based on a shipment's mass. A package weighs \(44\) pounds, and the company records masses in kilograms. Using \(1\text{ lb}\approx0.454\text{ kg}\), another employee estimates \(20\) kg without calculating. Which reasoning best justifies the estimate?

A. The estimate is reasonable because \(44(0.454)\) is close to \(20\) kg ✅
B. The estimate is impossible because kilograms must always be larger numerically
C. The estimate is wrong because \(44\div0.454\) is close to \(20\) kg
D. The estimate is reasonable only if \(1\) pound equals \(1\) kilogram

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The estimate can be checked efficiently: \(44(0.454)=19.976\) kg, which is essentially \(20\) kg. This demonstrates useful estimation with a conversion factor and confirms that multiplying by approximately one-half gives a sensible result.
