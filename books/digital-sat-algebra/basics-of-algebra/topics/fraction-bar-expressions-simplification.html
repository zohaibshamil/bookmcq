What is Fraction bar expressions simplification?

Definition:
Fraction bar expressions simplification involves reducing complex expressions where a fraction bar acts as a grouping symbol, indicating division of the entire numerator by the entire denominator, and simplifying requires applying order of operations to both top and bottom separately before dividing.

Working:
Simplify the numerator and denominator independently using PEMDAS (treating the fraction bar as division), then divide the simplified numerator by the simplified denominator; if possible, factor and cancel common terms, but note that the fraction bar groups terms.
