What is Fraction bar expressions simplification?

Definition:
Fraction bar expressions simplification involves reducing complex expressions where a fraction bar acts as a grouping symbol, indicating division of the entire numerator by the entire denominator, and simplifying requires applying order of operations to both top and bottom separately before dividing.

Working:
Simplify the numerator and denominator independently using PEMDAS (treating the fraction bar as division), then divide the simplified numerator by the simplified denominator; if possible, factor and cancel common terms, but note that the fraction bar groups terms.

Example:
Simplify 4+2×35−1 \frac{4 + 2 \times 3}{5 - 1} 5−14+2×3​.
Solution: Numerator: 4+6=10 4 + 6 = 10 4+6=10; denominator: 5−1=4 5-1=4 5−1=4; so fraction is 104=52 \frac{10}{4} = \frac{5}{2} 410​=25​.

Reason:
This skill is essential for simplifying rational expressions and complex fractions in algebra, where the fraction bar groups expressions, and it ensures correct evaluation of multi-step problems involving division.

Q1. A student simplifies 6x+123\frac{6x+12}{3}36x+12​ by dividing only the first term in the numerator by 333, obtaining 2x+122x+122x+12. What is the correct simplification, and why?

A.2x+42x+42x+4, because every term in the numerator must be divided by 333 ✅
B.2x+122x+122x+12, because only the first term contains xxx
C.6x+46x+46x+4, because 12÷3=412\div3=412÷3=4
D.2x+362x+362x+36, because 333 multiplies the numerator
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The fraction bar indicates that the entire numerator 6x+126x+126x+12 is divided by 333. Therefore, 6x+123=6x3+123=2x+4\frac{6x+12}{3}=\frac{6x}{3}+\frac{12}{3}=2x+436x+12​=36x​+312​=2x+4. The incorrect approach divides only one part of the numerator.
