What is Find Equivalent Fractions in Algebra?

Definition:
Finding equivalent fractions in algebra involves determining fractions that represent the same value as a given fraction, obtained by multiplying or dividing both the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero integer, which is essential for simplifying or comparing fractions in algebraic contexts.

Working:
Multiply or divide the numerator and denominator by the same number (e.g., ab=a×kb×k\frac{a}{b} = \frac{a \times k}{b \times k}ba​=b×ka×k​); to find equivalent fractions, choose any k≠0k \neq 0k=0; this process is also used to find common denominators for addition/subtraction.

Example:
Find two equivalent fractions for 23\frac{2}{3}32​.
Solution: Multiply numerator and denominator by 2: 2×23×2=46\frac{2 \times 2}{3 \times 2} = \frac{4}{6}3×22×2​=64​; multiply by 3: 69\frac{6}{9}96​.

Reason:
Equivalent fractions are fundamental for simplifying algebraic fractions, solving proportions, and adding/subtracting fractions with different denominators, ensuring that expressions are written in their simplest or most useful form.
