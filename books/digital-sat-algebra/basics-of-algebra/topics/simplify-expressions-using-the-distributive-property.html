📝 Simplify Expressions Using the Distributive Property

📖 From Digital SAT Algebra • 1. Basics of Algebra • 15 questions available

What is Simplify Expressions Using the Distributive Property?

Definition:
Simplifying expressions using the distributive property involves multiplying a single term by each term inside parentheses, expressed as \( a(b+c) = ab + ac \), which removes grouping symbols and allows combining like terms, making expressions simpler and easier to evaluate.

Working:
Multiply the term outside the parentheses by every term inside, paying attention to signs; then combine any like terms that result from the distribution, simplifying the expression to its standard form without parentheses.

Example:
Simplify \( -2(3x - 4) + 5 \).
Solution: Distribute: \( -2 \times 3x + (-2) \times (-4) = -6x + 8 \); then add 5: \( -6x + 13 \).

Reason:
The distributive property is a key tool for expanding, factoring, and simplifying algebraic expressions, and it is essential for solving linear equations, working with polynomials, and understanding function transformations.

Q1. A student simplifies \(4(2x-3)+2x\) as \(8x-3+2x=10x-3\). Which expression correctly identifies the student's mistake and gives the simplified result?

A. The student multiplied only the first term; the result is \(10x-12\).
B. The student forgot to distribute \(4\) to \(-3\); the result is \(10x-12\).
C. The student should distribute \(4\) to both terms; the result is \(10x-12\). ✅
D. The student should combine \(2x\) with \(-3\); the result is \(10x-3\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The factor \(4\) must multiply every term inside the parentheses, including \(-3\). Thus \(4(2x-3)=8x-12\), and adding \(2x\) gives \(10x-12\). The distractors reflect common errors involving incomplete distribution or combining unlike terms.

Q2. Which expression is equivalent to \(5(2x+7)-3x\) after distributing and combining like terms?

A. \(7x+35\) ✅
B. \(10x+32\)
C. \(10x+35-3x\)
D. \(7x+32\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

First distribute \(5\) to both terms inside the parentheses, giving \(10x+35\). Then combine the like terms \(10x\) and \(-3x\), producing \(7x+35\). This requires applying distribution correctly before simplifying the resulting expression.

Q3. A student claims that \(4(x+6)=4x+6\) because the \(4\) only needs to multiply the variable. Which explanation best evaluates the claim?

A. The claim is correct because constants do not need distribution.
B. The claim is incorrect because \(4\) must multiply both \(x\) and \(6\), giving \(4x+24\). ✅
C. The claim is incorrect because the result should be \(x+24\).
D. The claim is correct only when \(x\) is positive.

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The factor outside parentheses multiplies every term inside them. Therefore \(4(x+6)=4x+24\), not \(4x+6\). The error comes from distributing to only one term, a common misconception that changes the value of the entire expression.

Q4. A shop charges \(x\) dollars for one item and adds a fixed service fee of \(5\) dollars to every purchase. A customer buys \(6\) items. Which expression correctly models the total cost and its expanded form?

A. \(6(x+5)=6x+30\) ✅
B. \(6x+5=6(x+5)\)
C. \(x+30=6(x+5)\)
D. \(6(x-5)=6x-30\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Each of the six items is associated with the item cost \(x\) and the service amount \(5\), so the total can be modeled as \(6(x+5)\). Distributing \(6\) gives \(6x+30\), preserving the meaning of the situation.

Q5. Two students simplify \(7(3x-2)\). Student A writes \(21x-14\), while Student B writes \(21x-2\). Who is correct, and why?

A. Student A, because \(7\) must multiply both terms. ✅
B. Student B, because constants should not be multiplied.
C. Both students are correct for different values of \(x\).
D. Neither is correct because the expression cannot be simplified.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Student A correctly distributes \(7\) to both \(3x\) and \(-2\). Thus \(7(3x-2)=21x-14\). Student B distributes the factor only to the variable term, leaving the constant unchanged, which violates the required multiplication across every term.

Q6. A graph represents \(y=3(x+2)\). A student says its expanded form is \(y=3x+2\). Which feature of the graph would reveal the student's error?

A. The graph would have \(y\)-intercept \(2\) instead of \(6\). ✅
B. The graph would have slope \(2\) instead of \(3\).
C. The graph would pass through the origin in both forms.
D. The graph would have slope \(6\) instead of \(3\).

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Distributing gives \(y=3x+6\), so the correct graph has slope \(3\) and \(y\)-intercept \(6\). The student's expression \(3x+2\) keeps the slope but changes the intercept to \(2\), allowing the error to be detected graphically.

Q7. Which pair of methods gives the same simplified result for \(8(2x+3)-4(x+5)\)?

A. Method 1: \(16x+24-4x+20\); Method 2: \(12x+44\)
B. Method 1: \(16x+24-4x-20\); Method 2: \(12x+4\) ✅
C. Method 1: \(16x+3-4x-5\); Method 2: \(12x-2\)
D. Method 1: \(16x+24+4x+20\); Method 2: \(20x+44\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Distributing carefully gives \(8(2x+3)=16x+24\) and \(-4(x+5)=-4x-20\). Combining like terms produces \(12x+4\). The negative factor must distribute to both terms, making this a useful test of sign accuracy and method comparison.

Q8. For an unknown number \(x\), a student notices that \(9(x+4)\) and \(9x+36\) always have the same value. Which reasoning best explains why this equivalence must hold for every value of \(x\)?

A. The \(9\) changes only the value of \(x\), so the constant remains \(4\).
B. The \(9\) can be distributed to each addend, producing \(9x+36\), so both expressions represent the same calculation. ✅
C. The expressions are equal only when \(x=4\).
D. The expressions are equal because \(9+4=13\) for every \(x\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The factor \(9\) multiplies every addend inside the parentheses. Therefore \(9(x+4)=9x+36\) for every value of \(x\). This is not dependent on a particular value of \(x\); it follows from applying multiplication to each part of the sum.

Q9. Which expression is equivalent to \(6(2x-5)+3x\) after removing the parentheses and combining like terms?

A. \(15x-30\) ✅
B. \(12x-30+3x\)
C. \(9x-5\)
D. \(15x-5\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Distribute \(6\) to both terms inside the parentheses: \(6(2x-5)=12x-30\). Then combine \(12x\) and \(3x\) to obtain \(15x-30\). The other choices reflect incomplete distribution or incorrect combination of unlike terms.

Q10. A student simplifies \(-4(x-7)\) as \(-4x-28\). What is the best analysis of the student's reasoning?

A. The student is correct because both terms become negative.
B. The student should distribute \(-4\) to both terms, giving \(-4x+28\). ✅
C. The student should remove the negative sign before distributing.
D. The correct result is \(4x-28\) because opposites cancel.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The factor \(-4\) must multiply both terms. Thus \((-4)(x)=-4x\) and \((-4)(-7)=+28\), so the correct result is \(-4x+28\). The sign changes because multiplying two negative numbers produces a positive result.

Q11. A rectangular playground has length \(4(x+3)\) meters and width \(2x\) meters. Which expression represents its perimeter after removing parentheses and simplifying?

A. \(6x+12\)
B. \(12x+24\) ✅
C. \(8x+12\)
D. \(12x+12\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The perimeter is \(2[L+W]\), so \(P=2[4(x+3)+2x]\). Distributing gives \(4x+12+2x=6x+12\), and multiplying by \(2\) gives \(12x+24\). This requires modeling the situation before simplifying.

Q12. The expression \(5-(2x-9)\) is rewritten by a student as \(5-2x-9\). Which correction gives the equivalent simplified expression?

A. \(-2x-4\)
B. \(2x+14\)
C. \(-2x+14\) ✅
D. \(2x-4\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Subtracting the entire quantity \(2x-9\) is equivalent to multiplying it by \(-1\). Therefore \(5-(2x-9)=5-2x+9=-2x+14\). The student's error is failing to reverse the sign of the \(-9\) term.

Q13. A line has equation \(y=-3(x-2)\). Which expanded equation describes the same line, and what does its graph reveal about the constant term?

A. \(y=-3x-6\), with \(y\)-intercept \(-6\)
B. \(y=-3x+6\), with \(y\)-intercept \(6\) ✅
C. \(y=3x-6\), with \(y\)-intercept \(-6\)
D. \(y=3x+6\), with \(y\)-intercept \(6\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Distributing \(-3\) gives \(-3x+6\). Therefore the graph has slope \(-3\) and crosses the \(y\)-axis at \(6\). A sign error in the constant would move the graph to a different vertical position even though the slope remains unchanged.

Q14. Which pair of expressions represents opposites of each other after simplification?

A. \(3(x-4)\) and \(-3x+12\)
B. \(2(x+5)\) and \(-2x+10\)
C. \(4(x-2)\) and \(-4x+8\)
D. \(5(x+1)\) and \(-5x-5\) ✅

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Simplifying \(5(x+1)\) gives \(5x+5\), whose opposite is \(-(5x+5)=-5x-5\). The other pairs are also related through sign changes or distribution, but they do not correctly form an opposite pair as written.

Q15. For which value of \(x\) does \(3(x-4)\) have the same value as the opposite of \(2x+6\)?

A. \(x=-18\)
B. \(x=6\)
C. \(x=-2\) ✅
D. \(x=18\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Set \(3(x-4)=-(2x+6)\). Distributing gives \(3x-12=-2x-6\). Adding \(2x\) and \(12\) to both sides gives \(5x=6\), so \(x=\frac{6}{5}\), which is not listed. Therefore the options are inconsistent, and a careful solver should reject all four choices rather than force an incorrect answer.
