What is Reading and writing decimals?

Definition:
Reading and writing decimals involves interpreting the place value of digits after the decimal point, where each position represents a fraction with denominator a power of ten (tenths, hundredths, thousandths, etc.), and expressing the decimal in words or as a fraction, and vice versa.

Working:
To read a decimal, say the whole number part, then 'and' for the decimal point, then the fractional part as a whole number followed by the place value name of the last digit (e.g., 0.340.340.34 is 'thirty-four hundredths'); to write, place digits in their corresponding place values.

Example:
Write 0.2050.2050.205 in words and as a fraction.
Solution: In words: 'two hundred five thousandths'; as fraction: 2051000=41200\frac{205}{1000} = \frac{41}{200}1000205​=20041​.

Reason:
This skill is necessary for interpreting numerical data, making conversions, and ensuring accurate communication of decimal quantities in algebra, science, and everyday life, such as in measurements and currency.

Q1. A student writes the decimal 4.0704.0704.070 in words as “four and seventy hundredths.” Which explanation best identifies the error and gives the correct wording?

A.The zero in the tenths place is ignored; the correct wording is four and seven tenths.
B.The zero in the hundredths place is ignored; the correct wording is four and seven hundredths. ✅
C.The zero in the tenths place changes the place value of 7; the correct wording is four and seventy thousandths.
D.The final zero is irrelevant, so the correct wording is four and seventy tenths.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The digit 7 is in the hundredths place because the tenths position contains 0. Therefore, 4.0704.0704.070 is correctly named four and seventy hundredths. The zero preserves the place-value structure.
