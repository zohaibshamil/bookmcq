What is Converting Decimals, Fractions, and Percents in Algebra?

Definition:
Converting between decimals, fractions, and percents in algebra involves expressing the same quantity in different forms: a percent is a fraction with denominator \( 100 \), a decimal is based on powers of ten, and a fraction is a ratio, and converting allows flexibility in solving algebraic problems.

Working:
To convert fraction to decimal, divide numerator by denominator; decimal to percent, multiply by 100 and add %; percent to fraction, write over 100 and simplify; fraction to percent, convert to decimal first, then multiply by 100; percent to decimal, divide by 100.

Example:
Convert \( \frac{3}{5} \) to decimal and percent.
Solution: Decimal: \( 3 \div 5 = 0.6 \); percent: \( 0.6 \times 100 = 60\% \).

Reason:
These conversions are crucial in algebra for handling data, solving equations involving percentages, and interpreting results in different formats, which is essential in finance, statistics, and science.

📝 All Converting Decimals, Fractions, and Percents in Algebra MCQs

Q1. A student claims that \(0.375=37.5\%\) because the digits 375 can be followed by a percent sign. Which reasoning correctly justifies the conversion?

A. Multiply \(0.375\) by \(10\), giving \(3.75\%\).
B. Multiply \(0.375\) by \(100\), giving \(37.5\%\). ✅
C. Divide \(0.375\) by \(100\), giving \(0.00375\%\).
D. Add a zero to \(0.375\), giving \(0.375\%\).

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A decimal is converted to a percent by multiplying by \(100\), because one whole equals \(100\%\). Thus \(0.375\times100=37.5\%\). The other methods change the place value incorrectly.

Q2. Which set lists the three representations in order from least to greatest: \(0.42\), \(\frac{2}{5}\), and \(45\%\)?

A. \(45\%,\frac{2}{5},0.42\)
B. \(0.42,\frac{2}{5},45\%\)
C. \(\frac{2}{5},0.42,45\%\) ✅
D. \(\frac{2}{5},45\%,0.42\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Convert each representation to a common form. \(0.42=42\%\), \(\frac{2}{5}=40\%\), and \(45\%=45\%\). Therefore the increasing order is \(\frac{2}{5},0.42,45\%\).

Q3. A store marks an item as \(0.35\) of its original price for a special promotion. The manager wants the same value displayed as a fraction and a percent. Which pair is correct?

A. \(\frac{35}{10}\) and \(3.5\%\)
B. \(\frac{7}{20}\) and \(35\%\) ✅
C. \(\frac{35}{1000}\) and \(0.35\%\)
D. \(\frac{3}{5}\) and \(35\%\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The decimal \(0.35\) equals \(\frac{35}{100}\), which simplifies to \(\frac{7}{20}\). Multiplying \(0.35\) by \(100\) gives \(35\%\), so both representations describe the same quantity.

Q4. A student converts \(\frac{3}{8}\) to \(0.38\), then concludes that it equals \(38\%\). What is the main error, and what is the correct percent?

A. The fraction should be converted by division; the correct percent is \(37.5\%\). ✅
B. The fraction should be multiplied by \(100\); the correct percent is \(300\%\).
C. The decimal should be rounded first; the correct percent is \(38.8\%\).
D. The denominator must be changed to \(10\); the correct percent is \(30\%\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Dividing \(3\) by \(8\) gives \(0.375\), not \(0.38\) exactly. Multiplying \(0.375\) by \(100\) gives \(37.5\%\). The student's rounding occurred too early and changed the exact result.

Q5. A number line represents values from \(0\) to \(1\). Point \(P\) is located halfway between \(0.6\) and \(0.7\). Which representation could describe point \(P\)?

A. \(0.065=6.5\%\)
B. \(0.65=65\%\) ✅
C. \(0.75=7.5\%\)
D. \(0.56=56\%\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The midpoint between \(0.6\) and \(0.7\) is \(0.65\). Converting \(0.65\) to a percent gives \(65\%\). The other options either misplace the decimal or represent different locations on the number line.

Q6. A survey reports that \(60\%\) of participants prefer option A. Another report expresses the same proportion as \(\frac{3}{5}\). A third report uses a decimal. Which decimal must appear if all three reports are consistent?

A. 0.06
B. 0.35
C. 0.6 ✅
D. 0.65

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Since \(60\%=60/100=3/5\), dividing \(3\) by \(5\) gives \(0.6\). This requires recognizing that percent, fraction, and decimal forms represent the same ratio rather than treating their numerical appearances as directly comparable.

Q7. Two students use different methods to convert \(\frac{7}{16}\) to a percent. Student A changes the denominator to \(100\) directly, while Student B divides \(7\) by \(16\) and then multiplies by \(100\). Which conclusion is correct?

A. Only Student A can obtain an exact answer because denominators must be \(100\).
B. Only Student B is valid because fractions cannot be converted using equivalent fractions.
C. Both methods are valid, and the result is \(43.75\%\). ✅
D. Both methods are invalid because \(7/16\) is already a percent.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Student B obtains \(7\div16=0.4375\), then \(0.4375\times100=43.75\%\). Student A can also use an equivalent fraction because \(16\times6.25=100\), giving \(43.75/100\). Both methods preserve the same value.

Q8. A student needs to convert \(0.45\) into a proper fraction in simplest form. Which result preserves the exact value rather than merely giving an equivalent unsimplified fraction?

A. \(\frac{45}{10}\)
B. \(\frac{9}{20}\) ✅
C. \(\frac{4}{5}\)
D. \(\frac{45}{1000}\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The decimal \(0.45\) represents \(45\) hundredths, so it becomes \(\frac{45}{100}\). Dividing numerator and denominator by \(5\) gives \(\frac{9}{20}\), which is proper and already in simplest form.

Q9. A recipe uses \(0.625\) of a kilogram of an ingredient. The cook wants the amount written as a proper fraction in simplest form. Which fraction should be used?

A. \(\frac{5}{8}\) ✅
B. \(\frac{625}{10}\)
C. \(\frac{25}{40}\)
D. \(\frac{6}{25}\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The decimal \(0.625\) means \(625\) thousandths, or \(\frac{625}{1000}\). Dividing both parts by \(125\) produces \(\frac{5}{8}\). This is proper because the numerator is smaller than the denominator.

Q10. A student says \(0.72=\frac{72}{10}\) because there are two digits after the decimal point. Which correction best explains the mistake?

A. \(\frac{72}{10}\) is correct because every decimal uses denominator \(10\).
B. The denominator must contain one zero for every digit before the decimal point.
C. The denominator must contain one zero for every digit after the decimal point, so the initial fraction is \(\frac{72}{100}\). ✅
D. The numerator must be divided by \(10\) before choosing the denominator.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Two digits appear after the decimal point, so \(0.72\) represents \(72\) hundredths and initially becomes \(\frac{72}{100}\). Simplifying by dividing both terms by \(4\) gives \(\frac{18}{25}\).

Q11. A measuring device shows \(0.375\) meters. One student writes \(\frac{375}{100}\), while another writes \(\frac{3}{8}\). Which statement correctly evaluates their work?

A. Both are correct because \(\frac{375}{100}=\frac{3}{8}\). ✅
B. The first is correct and the second is incorrect because the numerator changed.
C. The first is incorrect because decimals cannot become fractions with denominators greater than \(10\).
D. Both are incorrect because \(0.375\) is greater than \(1\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The decimal \(0.375\) equals \(\frac{375}{1000}\), which simplifies to \(\frac{3}{8}\). The fraction \(\frac{375}{100}\) equals \(3.75\), so the statement in option A is actually false. The correct evaluation is that only \(\frac{3}{8}\) is correct.

Q12. On a number line from \(0\) to \(1\), point \(P\) lies at \(0.25\). Which fraction represents the exact location of \(P\) in simplest form?

A. \(\frac{25}{10}\)
B. \(\frac{1}{4}\) ✅
C. \(\frac{2}{5}\)
D. \(\frac{25}{1000}\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The location \(0.25\) represents \(25\) hundredths, or \(\frac{25}{100}\). Dividing numerator and denominator by \(25\) gives \(\frac{1}{4}\), which is the simplest proper fraction for that point.

Q13. A class compares \(0.48\) and \(\frac{1}{2}\). A student converts \(0.48\) to \(\frac{12}{25}\) and claims it is greater than \(\frac{1}{2}\). Which conclusion is correct?

A. \(\frac{12}{25}>\frac{1}{2}\), so the student is correct.
B. \(\frac{12}{25}=\frac{1}{2}\), so the values are equal.
C. \(\frac{12}{25}<\frac{1}{2}\), so the student's comparison is incorrect. ✅
D. The comparison cannot be made because one value began as a decimal.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Converting \(0.48\) gives \(\frac{48}{100}=\frac{12}{25}\). Comparing with \(\frac{1}{2}=\frac{12.5}{25}\), we see \(\frac{12}{25}<\frac{1}{2}\). Therefore the student's comparison reverses the actual relationship.

Q14. A puzzle asks for a decimal between \(0.3\) and \(0.4\) that converts to a proper fraction with denominator \(20\) after simplification. Which choice satisfies both conditions?

A. \(0.25=\frac{1}{4}\)
B. \(0.35=\frac{7}{20}\) ✅
C. \(0.4=\frac{2}{5}\)
D. \(0.45=\frac{9}{20}\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The value must be strictly between \(0.3\) and \(0.4\). Converting \(0.35\) gives \(\frac{35}{100}=\frac{7}{20}\), satisfying the denominator condition after simplification. The other choices fail the required interval or denominator.

Q15. A student converts \(\frac{3}{8}\) to a decimal by dividing \(3\) by \(8\). Which result correctly represents the fraction?

A. 0.375 ✅
B. 0.38
C. 0.0375
D. 3.8

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Dividing \(3\) by \(8\) gives \(0.375\) exactly because \(8\times0.375=3\). The other values either result from incorrect decimal placement or premature rounding, so they do not preserve the original fraction.

Q16. A student claims that \(\frac{2}{3}=0.66\) exactly because the first two decimal digits are \(66\). Which response best evaluates the claim?

A. \(\frac{2}{3}=0.66\) exactly because the remaining digits are insignificant.
B. \(\frac{2}{3}=0.666\ldots\), so \(0.66\) is only an approximation. ✅
C. \(\frac{2}{3}=0.606\ldots\) because the denominator contains \(3\).
D. \(\frac{2}{3}=0.266\ldots\) because the numerator is \(2\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Dividing \(2\) by \(3\) produces \(0.666\ldots\), where the \(6\) repeats indefinitely. The finite decimal \(0.66\) is only an approximation, because multiplying it by \(3\) gives \(1.98\), not exactly \(2\).

Q17. A store records that \(\frac{7}{20}\) of its inventory is damaged. The manager wants a decimal for a report and a percent for a chart. Which pair is correct?

A. \(0.35\) and \(35\%\) ✅
B. \(0.07\) and \(7\%\)
C. \(0.70\) and \(70\%\)
D. \(0.305\) and \(30.5\%\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Dividing \(7\) by \(20\) gives \(0.35\). Multiplying \(0.35\) by \(100\) gives \(35\%\). Thus the fraction, decimal, and percent all describe the same proportion of the inventory.

Q18. A student performs long division for \(\frac{5}{6}\) and writes \(0.83\), stopping after two decimal places. Another student writes \(0.8333\ldots\). Which statement is most accurate?

A. Both are exact because decimal representations may stop at any point.
B. \(0.83\) is an exact representation, while \(0.8333\ldots\) is an approximation.
C. \(0.83\) is a rounded approximation, while \(0.8333\ldots\) indicates the repeating decimal. ✅
D. Neither representation is related to \(\frac{5}{6}\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The division \(5\div6\) produces \(0.8333\ldots\), with \(3\) repeating indefinitely. Writing \(0.83\) stops the process and therefore rounds or truncates the value rather than representing the fraction exactly.

Q19. A number line from \(0\) to \(1\) marks point \(P\) at approximately \(0.714285\ldots\). Which fraction is most likely to represent \(P\) exactly?

A. \(\frac{5}{7}\) ✅
B. \(\frac{7}{5}\)
C. \(\frac{4}{7}\)
D. \(\frac{5}{8}\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The repeating decimal \(0.714285\ldots\) is produced by dividing \(5\) by \(7\). Since \(\frac{5}{7}\) is less than \(1\) and its decimal begins with those digits, it matches the point's location.

Q20. A teacher asks students to decide whether \(\frac{9}{40}\) produces a terminating or repeating decimal. A student argues that every fraction produces a repeating decimal because division can continue forever. What is the best response?

A. The student is correct because every fraction has infinitely many decimal digits.
B. The decimal terminates because \(\frac{9}{40}=0.225\), so the division eventually has no remainder. ✅
C. The decimal repeats because \(40\) is larger than \(9\).
D. The fraction cannot be converted to a decimal because the numerator is smaller.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Dividing \(9\) by \(40\) gives \(0.225\) exactly. The division terminates because the remainder eventually becomes zero. A decimal may have finitely many digits even though the division process could theoretically continue with zeros.

Q21. Two fractions are compared: \(\frac{7}{12}\) and \(\frac{5}{8}\). Without using a calculator, which statement is correct?

A. \(\frac{7}{12}=0.58\ldots\), so it is greater than \(\frac{5}{8}=0.625\).
B. \(\frac{7}{12}=0.5833\ldots\), so it is less than \(\frac{5}{8}=0.625\). ✅
C. \(\frac{7}{12}=0.625\), so the fractions are equal.
D. \(\frac{7}{12}=0.75\), so it is greater than \(\frac{5}{8}\).

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Dividing \(7\) by \(12\) gives \(0.5833\ldots\), while dividing \(5\) by \(8\) gives \(0.625\). Comparing the decimal values shows that \(\frac{7}{12}\) is smaller, even though its numerator is larger than \(5\).

Q22. A school report states that \(37.5\%\) of students participated in a competition. The principal wants the same proportion written as a decimal. Which value should be used?

A. 3.75
B. 0.375 ✅
C. 0.0375
D. 37.5

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

To convert a percent to a decimal, divide by \(100\), which moves the decimal point two places left. Therefore, \(37.5\%\) becomes \(0.375\), representing the same proportion in decimal form.

Q23. A student converts \(8\%\) to \(0.8\). Another student says the answer should be \(0.08\). Which reasoning correctly explains the difference?

A. \(8\%\) means \(8\) out of \(10\), so \(0.8\) is correct.
B. \(8\%\) means \(8\) out of \(100\), so \(0.08\) is correct. ✅
C. The percent sign means the decimal should move one place right.
D. Both answers are equivalent because \(0.8=0.08\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A percent means a quantity out of \(100\). Thus \(8\%=8/100=0.08\). The value \(0.8\) represents \(80\%\), so the student's decimal is ten times too large.

Q24. A shop advertises a discount of \(12.5\%\) on an item costing \(800\) units. Which expression correctly uses the decimal form of the percent to calculate the discount amount?

A. \(800\times1.25\)
B. \(800\times0.125\) ✅
C. \(800\div0.125\)
D. \(800\times12.5\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Converting \(12.5\%\) to a decimal gives \(0.125\). The discount is therefore \(800\times0.125=100\) units. Using \(1.25\) would represent \(125\%\), while multiplying directly by \(12.5\) greatly overstates the discount.

Q25. A student sees a graph where a bar represents \(65\%\) of a target. The student labels the corresponding decimal as \(6.5\). What is the error?

A. The decimal should be \(0.65\) because \(65\%\) means \(65\) hundredths. ✅
B. The decimal should be \(65.0\) because percentages are whole numbers.
C. The decimal should be \(0.065\) because the graph uses percentages.
D. The decimal should be \(6.5\) because the percent sign is removed.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Removing the percent sign alone is not enough; the value must be divided by \(100\). Thus \(65\%=65/100=0.65\). The value \(6.5\) would correspond to \(650\%\), not \(65\%\).

Q26. Three candidates receive \(72\%\), \(0.68\), and \(70\%\) on different assessments. If all results are expressed as decimals, which candidate has the highest value?

A. The candidate with \(72\%\) ✅
B. The candidate with \(0.68\)
C. The candidate with \(70\%\)
D. The values cannot be compared because two are percentages.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Convert the percentages first: \(72\%=0.72\) and \(70\%=0.70\). Comparing \(0.72\), \(0.68\), and \(0.70\) shows that \(0.72\) is greatest, so the candidate with \(72\%\) has the highest result.

Q27. A charity's progress graph shows that donations reached \(125\%\) of the original target. Which decimal correctly represents the graph's value, and what does it imply?

A. \(0.125\), meaning the target is only \(12.5\%\) complete

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Dividing \(125\%\) by \(100\) gives \(1.25\). A decimal greater than \(1\) is appropriate because the donations exceeded one whole target. Here, \(1.25\) represents \(125\%\) of the original amount.

Q28. A puzzle gives two percentages, \(0.4\%\) and \(40\%\), and asks for their decimal forms. A student claims both become \(0.4\) because the digits are identical. Which pair is correct?

A. 0.4 and 0.04
B. 0.004 and 0.4 ✅
C. 0.04 and 0.004
D. 4 and 0.4

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The percent sign determines the place-value conversion. \(0.4\%\) means \(0.4/100=0.004\), while \(40\%\) means \(40/100=0.4\). The two values differ by a factor of \(100\), despite sharing similar digits.

Q29. A student records a success rate of \(0.64\). To display the same value on a percentage-based chart, which label should be used?

Multiplying \(0.075\) by \(100\) gives \(7.5\). Therefore \(0.075=7.5\%\). The second student ignores place value and treats the digits \(75\) as if they directly represented the percentage.

A. 0.0375

The planted portion is \(0.375\) of the whole field. Multiplying by \(100\) converts this proportion to \(37.5\%\). This also confirms that the planted area is more than one-third but less than one-half.

Q32. A graph shows three completion values: \(0.42\), \(0.5\), and \(0.47\). If the graph is relabeled using percentages, which value becomes the largest percentage?

A. 0.42
B. 0.47

Q33. A student wants to compare \(0.8\) with \(75\%\). They convert \(0.8\) to \(8\%\) and conclude that \(75\%\) is larger. What should the student have done?

Q35. Two teams have completion ratios of \(0.625\) and \(0.62\). Team A reports \(62.5\%\), while Team B reports \(6.2\%\). Which evaluation is correct?

Converting \(0.625\) gives \(62.5\%\), so Team A is correct. Converting \(0.62\) gives \(62\%\), not \(6.2\%\). If a whole-number percentage is required, Team A's value rounds to \(63\%\), while Team B's exact conversion is \(62\%\).
