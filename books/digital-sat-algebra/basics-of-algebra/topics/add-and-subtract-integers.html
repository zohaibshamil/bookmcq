What is Add and Subtract Integers?

Definition:
Adding and subtracting integers involves combining positive and negative whole numbers, where addition of a positive increases the value, addition of a negative decreases it, and subtraction can be interpreted as adding the opposite (or additive inverse) of the number being subtracted.

Working:
For addition, if signs are same, add absolute values and keep the sign; if different, subtract smaller absolute value from larger and use sign of larger; for subtraction, change to addition of the opposite: \( a - b = a + (-b) \), then apply addition rules.

Example:
Calculate \( -5 + 3 \) and \( -2 - (-6) \).
Solution: For \( -5+3 \), signs differ, subtract \( 5-3=2 \), use sign of larger absolute value (negative), so \( -2 \); for \( -2 - (-6) \), rewrite as \( -2 + 6 = 4 \).

Reason:
Integer operations are foundational for algebra, as they apply to variable expressions, solving equations, and modeling real-life situations like temperature changes, financial transactions, and elevation differences.

📝 All Add and Subtract Integers MCQs

Q1. A submarine is at \(-18\) meters relative to sea level. It rises \(7\) meters and then descends \(12\) meters. What is its final position?

A. \(-23\) meters ✅
B. \(-5\) meters
C. \(1\) meter
D. \(23\) meters

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Representing upward movement as positive and downward movement as negative gives \(-18+7-12=-23\). The final position is therefore \(23\) meters below sea level. The distractors reflect common errors such as reversing a direction or combining magnitudes without signs.

Q2. A student claims that \(-14-(-9)=-23\) because subtracting always makes a number smaller. Which reasoning best evaluates the claim?

A. The claim is correct because subtraction always decreases the first number.
B. The claim is incorrect because subtracting a negative is equivalent to adding its positive, so the result is \(-5\). ✅
C. The claim is incorrect because two negative numbers always produce a positive answer.
D. The claim is correct because \(14+9=23\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The key idea is that subtracting a negative changes the operation: \(-14-(-9)=-14+9=-5\). The student's error comes from treating every subtraction as movement farther left, ignoring that subtracting a negative moves the value to the right.

Q3. A bank account has a balance of \(-\$35\). A deposit of \(\$50\) is made, followed by a withdrawal of \(\$18\). What balance remains, and what does it mean?

A. \(-\$103\), meaning the account is deeply overdrawn
B. \(-\$3\), meaning the account is still overdrawn ✅
C. \(\$3\), meaning the account has a positive balance
D. \(\$67\), meaning the account has a positive balance

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The balance can be modeled as \(-35+50-18=-3\). Thus, the account remains overdrawn by \(\$3\). The distractors represent errors such as treating the initial debt as positive or adding the withdrawal instead of subtracting it.

Q4. On a number line, a point starts at \(-6\), moves \(11\) units right, and then moves \(8\) units left. Where does it finish?

A. \(-25\)
B. \(-3\)
C. 3 ✅
D. -13

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Moving right corresponds to adding a positive integer, while moving left corresponds to adding a negative integer. Therefore, the position is \(-6+11-8=3\). The incorrect choices come from reversing one direction or combining all movements as positive distances.

Q5. Two students solve \(-8-(-13)\). Student A writes \(-8+13=5\). Student B writes \(-8-13=-21\). Which comparison is correct?

A. Student A is correct because subtracting a negative becomes addition. ✅
B. Student B is correct because both numbers have negative signs.
C. Both are correct because different methods can give different results.
D. Neither is correct because subtraction of integers cannot involve negative numbers.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Student A correctly rewrites \(-8-(-13)\) as \(-8+13\), giving \(5\). Student B incorrectly treats the second negative sign as if it should remain unchanged. The important reasoning is recognizing that subtracting a negative reverses the direction.

Q6. A temperature chart records changes during four hours as \(+6^\circ\), \(-9^\circ\), \(+4^\circ\), and \(-7^\circ\). If the starting temperature was \(-2^\circ C\), what is the final temperature?

A. \(-8^\circ C\) ✅
B. \(-4^\circ C\)
C. \(0^\circ C\)
D. \(2^\circ C\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The temperature changes must be combined with their signs: \(-2+6-9+4-7=-8\). Therefore, the final temperature is \(-8^\circ C\). A correct model must preserve each increase and decrease rather than simply adding the magnitudes of all changes.

Q7. A game awards \(+10\) points for a correct answer and \(-6\) points for an incorrect answer. A player answers five questions, getting three correct and two incorrect. Compared with a player who gets all five incorrect, how many more points does the first player earn?

A. 18 points
B. 30 points
C. 48 points ✅
D. 60 points

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The first player's score is \(3(10)+2(-6)=18\). The all-incorrect player's score is \(5(-6)=-30\). The difference is \(18-(-30)=48\) points. This requires modeling both scores and then subtracting a negative quantity correctly.
