What is Round Decimals in Algebra?

Definition:
Rounding decimals in algebra means reducing the number of decimal places based on a specified place value, by looking at the digit to the right of the target place: if it is \( 5 \) or more, round up (increase the target digit by 1), otherwise round down (keep target digit), and drop all digits to the right.

Working:
Identify the place to round to (e.g., tenths, hundredths), look at the next digit to the right; if it's \( \ge 5 \), add 1 to the target digit; if \( < 5 \), leave it; then truncate all digits after the target place, keeping the decimal point.

Example:
Round \( 4.567 \) to the nearest tenth.
Solution: Target is tenths place (5), next digit is 6 (which is \( \ge 5 \)), so round up: \( 4.6 \).

Reason:
Rounding decimals is used to estimate results, make numbers easier to work with, and report answers with appropriate precision in scientific measurements, financial calculations, and algebraic solutions.

📝 All Round Decimals in Algebra MCQs

Q1. A school records a runner's time as \(12.746\) seconds but reports times to the nearest tenth. Which value should appear in the final report?

A.\(12.7\) seconds ✅
B.\(12.8\) seconds
C.\(12.75\) seconds
D.\(13.0\) seconds
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: To round \(12.746\) to the nearest tenth, identify the tenths digit, which is \(7\), and examine the hundredths digit, which is \(4\). Since \(4\) is less than \(5\), the tenths digit remains unchanged, giving \(12.7\) seconds.

Q2. A measuring device gives three readings: \(4.263\), \(4.251\), and \(4.298\) meters. If all readings must be reported to the nearest hundredth, which sequence is correct?

A.\(4.26,\ 4.25,\ 4.29\)
B.\(4.27,\ 4.26,\ 4.30\)
C.\(4.26,\ 4.25,\ 4.30\) ✅
D.\(4.30,\ 4.25,\ 4.29\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: For each measurement, inspect the thousandths digit because the hundredths place is being retained. Thus \(4.263\) becomes \(4.26\), \(4.251\) becomes \(4.25\), and \(4.298\) becomes \(4.30\) because \(8\) rounds the hundredths digit upward.

Q3. A number line is marked at \(5.1,\ 5.2,\ 5.3,\) and \(5.4\). A point representing \(5.26\) lies between \(5.2\) and \(5.3\), slightly closer to \(5.3\). To the nearest tenth, where should the point be reported?

A.5.2
B.5.3 ✅
C.5.26
D.5
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The point \(5.26\) lies between \(5.2\) and \(5.3\). Its hundredths digit is \(6\), which is at least \(5\), so the tenths digit \(2\) increases to \(3\). Therefore, the rounded value is \(5.3\).

Q4. A carpenter measures a board as \(18.675\) cm. The measurement must be recorded to the nearest hundredth. Another worker claims the answer is \(18.67\) because the thousandths digit is \(5\) and therefore the number should remain unchanged. Which response is most accurate?

A.The worker is correct because \(5\) never changes a digit.
B.The worker is correct because only digits greater than \(5\) cause rounding.
C.The worker is incorrect because a following digit of \(5\) causes the hundredths digit to increase, giving \(18.68\). ✅
D.The worker is incorrect because the number must always be rounded to the nearest whole number.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: When rounding to the nearest hundredth, the hundredths digit is \(7\), and the next digit is \(5\). Under the standard rounding rule, a digit of \(5\) causes the retained digit to increase by one, producing \(18.68\).

Q5. A science experiment records a quantity as \(0.0496\). The teacher asks students to round it first to the nearest thousandth and then round that result to the nearest hundredth. What final value should they obtain?

A.0.04
B.0.05 ✅
C.0.05
D.0.06
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: First round \(0.0496\) to the nearest thousandth. The thousandths digit is \(9\), and the next digit is \(6\), so the result becomes \(0.050\). Rounding \(0.050\) to the nearest hundredth gives \(0.05\), since the hundredths digit is already \(5\).

Q6. Two students round \(9.949\) to the nearest tenth. Student A says \(9.9\) because the hundredths digit is \(4\). Student B says \(10.0\) because the final digit is \(9\). Which conclusion is correct?

A.Student A, because the hundredths digit determines rounding and \(4<5\). ✅
B.Student B, because any \(9\) forces the whole number upward.
C.Both students are correct because \(9.949\) is equally close to \(9.9\) and \(10.0\).
D.Neither student is correct because the number should be \(9.94\).
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: To round to the nearest tenth, inspect the hundredths digit, which is \(4\). Since \(4<5\), the tenths digit remains \(9\), producing \(9.9\). The final digit \(9\) is irrelevant because it is not immediately after the rounding place.

Q7. A delivery truck travels \(27.864\) km, but the report requires the distance to the nearest tenth. Which value should be recorded, and why?

A.\(27.8\), because \(4<5\)
B.\(27.9\), because the hundredths digit \(6\) increases the tenths digit ✅
C.\(28.0\), because the decimal contains more than two digits
D.\(27.86\), because two decimal places are always required
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The target place is the tenths position, which contains \(8\). The next digit is the hundredths digit \(6\), so the tenths digit must increase from \(8\) to \(9\). Therefore, the correctly rounded distance is \(27.9\) km.

Q8. A laboratory records a mass of \(14.375\) grams and requires measurements rounded to the nearest hundredth. A student reports \(14.37\). Which analysis best identifies the student's mistake?

A.The student should examine the thousandths digit \(5\), which causes the hundredths digit to increase, giving \(14.38\). ✅
B.The student should examine the tenths digit \(3\), giving \(14.40\).
C.The student should remove all digits after the hundredths place without changing anything.
D.The student should round to the nearest whole number first.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The hundredths digit is \(7\), and the digit immediately following it is \(5\). Because the next digit is \(5\), the retained hundredths digit increases from \(7\) to \(8\). Thus \(14.375\) rounds to \(14.38\).

Q9. A store lists a product price as \(8.496\) dollars but displays prices to the nearest cent. A customer argues that the displayed price should be \(8.49\) because the third decimal digit is \(6\), which is not part of the cents value. What should the cashier display?

A.8.49
B.8.5 ✅
C.8.496
D.9
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The nearest cent means rounding to the hundredths place. In \(8.496\), the hundredths digit is \(9\), and the thousandths digit is \(6\), so the value rounds upward. This creates a carry, changing \(8.49\) to \(8.50\).

Q10. A number-line point is located at \(3.74\), between \(3.7\) and \(3.8\). When rounding to the nearest tenth, which labeled value should represent the point?

A.\(3.7\), because \(3.74\) is less than \(3.75\) ✅
B.\(3.8\), because \(3.74\) has a hundredths digit of \(4\)
C.\(3.7\), because the tenths digit must never change
D.\(3.8\), because every decimal between \(3.7\) and \(3.8\) rounds upward
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The target is the nearest tenth, so the hundredths digit determines the rounding. Since \(3.74\) has \(4\) in the hundredths position, the tenths digit remains \(7\). Therefore, the point rounds to \(3.7\), not \(3.8\).

Q11. A student rounds \(6.2497\) first to the nearest hundredth and then independently to the nearest tenth. Which pair of results is correct?

A.\(6.24\) and \(6.2\)
B.\(6.25\) and \(6.2\) ✅
C.\(6.25\) and \(6.3\)
D.\(6.20\) and \(6.3\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: For the nearest hundredth, inspect the thousandths digit \(9\), so \(6.24\) becomes \(6.25\). For the nearest tenth, inspect the hundredths digit \(4\), so the tenths digit remains \(2\), giving \(6.2\). Therefore, the correct pair is \(6.25\) and \(6.2\).

Q12. A technician measures three wires as \(2.146\) m, \(2.154\) m, and \(2.149\) m. The measurements must be reported to the nearest hundredth. Which report preserves the required precision?

A.\(2.15,\ 2.15,\ 2.15\)
B.\(2.14,\ 2.15,\ 2.14\) ✅
C.\(2.15,\ 2.16,\ 2.15\)
D.\(2.1,\ 2.2,\ 2.1\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: For \(2.146\), the thousandths digit \(6\) makes the hundredths digit \(4\) become \(5\), giving \(2.15\). For \(2.154\), the next digit is \(4\), so it stays \(2.15\). For \(2.149\), the \(9\) raises \(4\) to \(5\), giving \(2.15\).

Q13. A contest compares two methods for rounding \(5.999\) to the nearest tenth. Method A looks only at the tenths digit and gives \(5.9\). Method B examines the hundredths digit before rounding and gives \(6.0\). Which method is mathematically justified?

A.Method A, because the digit being retained should never change.
B.Method A, because \(9\) in the tenths place means the value stays below \(6\).
C.Method B, because the hundredths digit \(9\) causes a carry through the tenths place, producing \(6.0\). ✅
D.Neither method, because decimals ending in \(9\) cannot be rounded.
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: To round \(5.999\) to the nearest tenth, the hundredths digit \(9\) requires the tenths digit \(9\) to increase. That creates a carry into the whole-number part, changing \(5.9\) into \(6.0\). Method B correctly accounts for this carry.
