What is Zero property of multiplication?

Definition:
The zero property of multiplication states that the product of any real number and zero is always zero, expressed as a×0=0 a \times 0 = 0 a×0=0 for any real number a a a, which is a fundamental property used in algebra to solve equations and simplify expressions.

Working:
Whenever you multiply a term or expression by zero, the entire product becomes zero; this property is often used in factoring equations, where setting a product equal to zero allows solving by setting each factor to zero (zero product property).

Example:
Simplify 7×0 7 \times 0 7×0 and solve (x−2)(x+3)=0 (x-2)(x+3)=0 (x−2)(x+3)=0.
Solution: 7×0=0 7 \times 0 = 0 7×0=0; for equation, by zero product property, set x−2=0 x-2=0 x−2=0 or x+3=0 x+3=0 x+3=0, so x=2 x=2 x=2 or x=−3 x=-3 x=−3.

Reason:
This property is crucial for solving polynomial equations by factoring, as it allows us to break down complex problems into simpler linear equations, and it reinforces the concept of zero as a unique number in arithmetic.
