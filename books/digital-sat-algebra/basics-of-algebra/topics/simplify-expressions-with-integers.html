What is Simplify Expressions with Integers?

Definition:
Simplifying expressions with integers means reducing an algebraic expression to its simplest form by performing all integer operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division) correctly, following the order of operations, and combining like terms to produce a cleaner, equivalent expression.

Working:
Apply PEMDAS: handle parentheses, then exponents (if any), then multiplication/division from left to right, then addition/subtraction from left to right, while always applying integer rules for signs; combine any like variable terms and constant terms at the end.
