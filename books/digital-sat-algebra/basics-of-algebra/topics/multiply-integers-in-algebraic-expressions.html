What is Multiply Integers in algebraic expressions?

Definition:
Multiplying integers in algebraic expressions means finding the product of signed integer coefficients and constants, where the result's sign depends on the signs of the factors: positive product for same signs and negative product for opposite signs, applied when simplifying or expanding expressions.

Working:
Multiply the absolute values of the integers, then assign the sign: if both factors have same sign (both positive or both negative), product is positive; if signs differ, product is negative; then combine with any variables present, multiplying coefficients as usual.

Reason:
