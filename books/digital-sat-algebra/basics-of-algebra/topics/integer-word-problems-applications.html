What is Integer word problems applications?

Definition:
Integer word problems applications involve using signed whole numbers to represent real-world situations such as gains/losses, temperature changes, elevations, or time differences, where positive integers indicate increase or gain and negative integers indicate decrease or loss, requiring translation into algebraic expressions.

Working:
Read the problem carefully, assign variables to unknowns, identify key phrases indicating positive or negative quantities, write an equation or expression using integer operations, then solve by evaluating the expression or solving the equation with integer rules.

Example:
A submarine is at −150 -150 −150 feet and descends 75 75 75 feet; find its new depth.
Solution: New depth = −150+(−75)=−225 -150 + (-75) = -225 −150+(−75)=−225 feet (or 225 225 225 feet below sea level).

Reason:
These applications demonstrate the practical use of integers in everyday contexts, helping students develop problem-solving skills and understand how algebra models real-life changes, which is essential for STEM fields.

Q3. A temperature graph shows the temperature changing from −6∘C-6^\circ\text{C}−6∘C at 6 a.m. to 3∘C3^\circ\text{C}3∘C at noon, then to −2∘C-2^\circ\text{C}−2∘C at 6 p.m. Which statement correctly compares the two changes?

A.The first change is 9∘C9^\circ\text{C}9∘C, and the second change is −5∘C-5^\circ\text{C}−5∘C. ✅
B.The first change is −9∘C-9^\circ\text{C}−9∘C, and the second change is 5∘C5^\circ\text{C}5∘C.
C.Both changes are increases because the final temperature is higher than the initial temperature.
D.The total temperature change is 14∘C14^\circ\text{C}14∘C.
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: From −6-6−6 to 333, the change is 3−(−6)=9∘C3-(-6)=9^\circ\text{C}3−(−6)=9∘C. From 333 to −2-2−2, the change is −2−3=−5∘C-2-3=-5^\circ\text{C}−2−3=−5∘C. The signs indicate warming and cooling, not simply whether temperatures are positive.

Q4. A student models a parking garage using floor numbers. The car starts on floor 222, goes down 555 floors, then up 333 floors. The student writes 2−5−3=−62-5-3=-62−5−3=−6. What is the student's error?

A.The starting floor should be negative.
B.Moving up 333 floors should be represented by +3+3+3, not −3-3−3. ✅
C.Moving down 555 floors should be represented by +5+5+5.
D.The final floor must always be positive.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The downward movement is −5-5−5, but upward movement is +3+3+3. Therefore the correct model is 2−5+3=02-5+3=02−5+3=0. The error comes from assigning the same negative sign to both directions of movement.

Q5. A delivery vehicle travels 121212 km east, then 777 km west, and finally 101010 km west. If east is represented by positive integers and west by negative integers, what is the vehicle's net displacement?

A.5 km east
B.5 km west ✅
C.9 km east
D.29 km west
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Using the chosen direction convention, the movements are +12,−7,−10+12,-7,-10+12,−7,−10. Their sum is 12−7−10=−512-7-10=-512−7−10=−5. The negative result means the vehicle ends 555 km west of its starting position.

Q6. A game awards 151515 points for a successful challenge and subtracts 888 points for an unsuccessful one. A player succeeds three times and fails twice. Another player succeeds twice and fails once. How many more points does the first player earn?

A.15 points ✅
B.23 points
C.31 points
D.7 points
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The first player's score is 3(15)+2(−8)=45−16=293(15)+2(-8)=45-16=293(15)+2(−8)=45−16=29. The second player's score is 2(15)+(−8)=222(15)+(-8)=222(15)+(−8)=22. Therefore, the first player earns 29−22=729-22=729−22=7 more points. Integer signs model gains and losses.

Q7. A submarine starts 181818 m below sea level, represented by −18-18−18. It rises 252525 m, dives 121212 m, and then rises 555 m. Which conclusion is correct?

A.It finishes 101010 m above sea level.
B.It finishes 000 m at sea level.
C.It finishes 000 m below sea level. ✅
D.It finishes 505050 m below sea level.
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The sequence is −18+25−12+5-18+25-12+5−18+25−12+5. This gives 000. Therefore, the submarine finishes exactly at sea level. Tracking each signed change prevents the common mistake of treating all movements as positive distances.

Q8. At 6 a.m., the temperature is −4∘C-4^\circ\text{C}−4∘C. It rises 9∘C9^\circ\text{C}9∘C by noon and then falls 7∘C7^\circ\text{C}7∘C by midnight. What is the final temperature and the overall change from 6 a.m.?

A.−2∘C-2^\circ\text{C}−2∘C, a decrease of 2∘C2^\circ\text{C}2∘C ✅

D.12∘C12^\circ\text{C}12∘C, an increase of 16∘C16^\circ\text{C}16∘C
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Represent the temperature changes with signs: −4+9−7=−2-4+9-7=-2−4+9−7=−2. Comparing the final temperature with the starting temperature gives −2−(−4)=2-2-(-4)=2−2−(−4)=2, so the temperature actually increased overall by 2∘C2^\circ\text{C}2∘C.
