What is Systems of Measurement in Algebra?

Definition:
Systems of measurement in algebra refer to standardized sets of units for quantifying physical quantities like length, mass, and volume, primarily the U.S. Customary System (feet, pounds, gallons) and the Metric System (meters, kilograms, liters), and they are used in word problems and conversions.

Working:
Measurements are converted using conversion factors (ratios equal to 1) within or between systems; in algebra, we set up proportions or multiply by conversion factors to change units, ensuring that units cancel appropriately.

Reason:
