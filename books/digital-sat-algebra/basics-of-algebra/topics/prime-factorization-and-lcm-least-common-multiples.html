What is Prime factorization and (LCM) Least Common Multiples?

Definition:
Prime factorization is expressing a composite number as a product of its prime factors (numbers greater than with only two factors: and itself), while the Least Common Multiple (LCM) is the smallest positive number that is a multiple of two or more given numbers.
