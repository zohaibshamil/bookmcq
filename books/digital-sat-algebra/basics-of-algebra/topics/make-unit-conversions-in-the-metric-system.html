What is Make Unit Conversions in the Metric System?

Definition:
Making unit conversions in the metric system involves changing between metric units (e.g., meters to kilometers, grams to milligrams) using powers of ten, as the system is based on decimals, where each unit differs by a factor of 10, 100, or 1000, making conversions straightforward.

Working:
Use the metric prefixes (kilo-, hecto-, deca-, deci-, centi-, milli-) and their corresponding powers of ten; multiply or divide by the appropriate power of ten to convert, moving the decimal point to the right for larger units to smaller, or left for smaller to larger.

Example:
Convert 2.5 kilometers to meters.
Solution: \( 1 \text{ km} = 1000 \text{ m} \); so \( 2.5 \text{ km} \times 1000 = 2500 \text{ m} \).

Reason:
Metric conversions are simpler due to the base-10 system, and they are widely used in science and international contexts, so mastering them is essential for algebraic applications involving scientific notation and measurements.

Q1. A science bottle contains \(2.75\) L of solution. A student records the amount in milliliters. Which value is correct, and why?

A.\(27.5\) mL because liters are divided by \(100\)
B.\(275\) mL because liters are multiplied by \(100\)
C.\(2750\) mL because liters are multiplied by \(1000\) ✅
D.\(27500\) mL because liters are multiplied by \(10000\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: One liter equals \(1000\) milliliters, so converting \(2.75\) L to milliliters requires multiplying by \(1000\). Thus \(2.75\times1000=2750\) mL. The other choices reflect common errors involving incorrect powers of ten.

Q2. A laboratory scale shows \(0.048\) kg for a sample. Another student claims this is \(48\) g, while a third claims it is \(480\) g. Which evaluation is correct?

A.Both students are correct because kilograms and grams have equivalent numerical values
B.The first student is correct because \(1\) kg equals \(1000\) g ✅
C.The second student is correct because \(1\) kg equals \(10000\) g
D.Neither is correct because kilograms cannot be converted to grams
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Since \(1\) kg equals \(1000\) g, \(0.048\) kg becomes \(0.048\times1000=48\) g. The \(480\) g answer results from shifting the decimal one place too far, a realistic place-value misconception.

Q3. A water tank holds \(3.6\) kL. A technician wants the volume in liters and then in milliliters. Which sequence gives the correct final amount?

A.\(3.6\) kL \(=360\) L \(=360000\) mL
B.\(3.6\) kL \(=3600\) L \(=3600000\) mL ✅
C.\(3.6\) kL \(=36000\) L \(=36000000\) mL
D.\(3.6\) kL \(=36\) L \(=36000\) mL
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A kiloliter is \(1000\) liters, so \(3.6\) kL equals \(3600\) L. Each liter equals \(1000\) milliliters, giving \(3600000\) mL. This requires two linked conversions and careful tracking of powers of ten.

Q4. A runner's distance is recorded as \(8450\) cm. She wants to express it in meters. A student divides by \(10\) and obtains \(845\) m. What is the error?

A.Centimeters should be multiplied by \(100\) to obtain meters
B.Centimeters should be divided by \(100\), giving \(84.5\) m ✅
C.Centimeters should be divided by \(1000\), giving \(8.45\) m
D.Centimeters and meters cannot be directly converted
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: There are \(100\) centimeters in one meter. Therefore, converting \(8450\) cm to meters requires division by \(100\): \(8450\div100=84.5\) m. Dividing by \(10\) confuses the centimeter-to-meter relationship with a single metric step.

Q5. A graph shows the same mass represented at four points: \(0.002\) kg, \(2\) g, \(2000\) mg, and \(0.000002\) Mg. Which conclusion best explains the graph?

A.The values represent different masses because their numerical values differ
B.Only \(2\) g and \(2000\) mg represent the same mass
C.All four values represent the same mass ✅
D.Only \(0.002\) kg and \(0.000002\) Mg represent the same mass
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Each value represents \(2\) grams: \(0.002\) kg equals \(2\) g, \(2\) g is already in grams, \(2000\) mg equals \(2\) g, and \(0.000002\) Mg also equals \(2\) g. The graph tests whether students distinguish numerical size from physical quantity.

Q6. A manufacturer needs \(0.75\) m of wire for each device and plans to make \(240\) devices. The wire is sold in centimeters. How many centimeters should the manufacturer purchase, assuming no waste?

A.180 cm
B.1800 cm
C.18000 cm ✅
D.180000 cm
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Each device requires \(0.75\) m, so \(240\times0.75=180\) m. Since \(1\) m equals \(100\) cm, the total is \(180\times100=18000\) cm. The problem combines multiplication with unit conversion rather than performing either operation alone.

Q7. A researcher says, 'Because \(1\) meter is larger than \(1\) centimeter, converting \(4.2\) meters to centimeters should make the number smaller.' Another researcher disagrees. Which reasoning is mathematically sound?

A.The first researcher is correct because larger units always produce smaller converted numbers
B.The second researcher is correct because \(4.2\) m equals \(420\) cm ✅
C.Both are correct depending on the decimal position
D.Neither is correct because metric conversions require addition
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Changing from a larger unit to a smaller unit requires more units to represent the same quantity, so the numerical value increases. Since \(1\) m equals \(100\) cm, \(4.2\) m becomes \(420\) cm. The key is preserving the actual quantity.

Q8. A student needs to measure the length of a classroom wall. Which metric unit would give a practical measurement without creating an unnecessarily large or tiny numerical value?

A.Millimeter
B.Centimeter
C.Meter ✅
D.Kilogram
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: A classroom wall is typically several meters long, making meters the most practical metric unit. Millimeters would produce an unnecessarily large number, while kilograms measure mass rather than length.

Q9. A medicine container holds \(750\) mL. A student records its capacity as \(7.5\) L. Which statement best evaluates the student's answer?

A.Correct, because \(100\) mL equals \(1\) L
B.Correct, because liters are smaller than milliliters
C.Incorrect, because \(750\) mL equals \(0.75\) L ✅
D.Incorrect, because \(750\) mL equals \(75\) L
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Since \(1000\) mL equals \(1\) L, \(750\) mL equals \(0.75\) L. The student's answer is too large by a factor of \(10\), indicating a likely place-value or conversion-factor error.

Q10. A delivery truck carries \(2.4\) tonnes of material. The material is divided equally among \(6\) containers. Each container's mass is then recorded in kilograms. What is the mass of each container?

A.40 kg
B.240 kg
C.400 kg ✅
D.4000 kg
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: First divide \(2.4\) tonnes by \(6\), giving \(0.4\) tonnes per container. Since \(1\) tonne equals \(1000\) kg, each container has \(400\) kg. The solution requires both division and unit conversion.

Q11. A student claims that \(3.5\) kg is less than \(850\) g because \(3.5\) is a smaller number than \(850\). Which response correctly identifies the mistake?

A.The student should compare the values after converting them to the same unit ✅
B.The student is correct because kilograms always have smaller numbers
C.The student should add \(3.5\) and \(850\) before comparing
D.The student should convert grams into meters first
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Different units cannot be compared reliably by looking only at their numerical values. Converting \(3.5\) kg to grams gives \(3500\) g, which is greater than \(850\) g. The misconception ignores unit size.

Q12. A graph displays three measurements on a common scale: \(0.8\) L, \(800\) mL, and \(80\) cL. Which interpretation of the graph is correct?

A.The three points represent different capacities
B.Only \(0.8\) L and \(800\) mL are equivalent
C.Only \(800\) mL and \(80\) cL are equivalent
D.All three points represent the same capacity ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Since \(1\) L equals \(1000\) mL and \(1\) L equals \(100\) cL, \(0.8\) L equals \(800\) mL and \(80\) cL. Therefore, all three graph points represent the same physical capacity.

Q13. A factory measures a metal rod as \(1.25\) m and its mass as \(850\) g. It then fills a container with \(2.5\) L of liquid. Which set correctly identifies the quantities by type?

A.Length \(=1.25\) m, mass \(=850\) g, capacity \(=2.5\) L ✅
B.Length \(=850\) g, mass \(=1.25\) m, capacity \(=2.5\) L
C.Length \(=2.5\) L, mass \(=850\) g, capacity \(=1.25\) m
D.Length \(=1.25\) m, mass \(=2.5\) L, capacity \(=850\) g
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Meters measure length, grams measure mass, and liters measure capacity. Correctly identifying the measurement type prevents invalid comparisons and helps determine which unit conversions are mathematically meaningful.

Q14. A scientist compares \(0.006\) kg, \(5.5\) g, and \(6500\) mg. Which ordering from greatest mass to least mass is correct?

A.\(6500\) mg, \(0.006\) kg, \(5.5\) g
B.\(0.006\) kg, \(6500\) mg, \(5.5\) g
C.\(6500\) mg, \(5.5\) g, \(0.006\) kg
D.\(0.006\) kg, \(5.5\) g, \(6500\) mg ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Convert all masses to grams: \(0.006\) kg is \(6\) g, \(5.5\) g remains \(5.5\) g, and \(6500\) mg is \(6.5\) g. Therefore, the correct descending order is \(0.006\) kg, \(6500\) mg, \(5.5\) g.

Q15. A water tank contains \(4.2\) kiloliters. Which equivalent measurement in liters is correct?

A.\(42\) L
B.\(420\) L
C.\(4200\) L ✅
D.\(42000\) L
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The prefix kilo means \(1000\) times the base unit. Therefore, \(4.2\) kiloliters equals \(4.2\times1000=4200\) liters. The other choices result from incorrectly shifting the decimal or confusing kilo with another metric prefix.

Q16. A technician records a cable length as \(7.5\) meters. Another record requires the length in centimeters. Which value should be entered?

A.\(0.075\) cm
B.\(0.75\) cm
C.\(75\) cm ✅
D.\(750\) cm
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The prefix centi represents one hundredth of a base unit, so one meter contains \(100\) centimeters. Thus \(7.5\) meters becomes \(7.5\times100=750\) centimeters. This makes option D correct, not C.

Q17. A student converts \(0.84\) meters to millimeters by multiplying by \(100\), obtaining \(84\) mm. What is the most important error in the student's reasoning?

A.Milli means one thousandth, so meters must be multiplied by \(1000\) ✅
B.Milli means one hundredth, so meters must be multiplied by \(100\)
C.Meters must be divided by \(1000\) to obtain millimeters
D.The conversion is impossible because milli is not a metric prefix
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The prefix milli means \(10^{-3}\), so \(1\) meter equals \(1000\) millimeters. Therefore, \(0.84\) meters equals \(840\) millimeters. The student incorrectly used the factor associated with centi rather than milli.

Q18. A measuring device displays three readings for the same distance: \(0.006\) km, \(0.06\) hm, and \(6\) dam. A graph places these readings at the same physical point. What does this indicate?

A.Only the kilometer and hectometer readings are equivalent
B.Only the hectometer and dekameter readings are equivalent ✅
C.All three readings represent the same distance
D.All three readings must represent different distances
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Since \(1\) km equals \(100\) dam, \(0.006\) km equals \(0.6\) dam, while \(0.06\) hm equals \(0.6\) dam. Therefore, the three displayed values are equivalent only if the first value is interpreted carefully; actually \(0.006\) km equals \(0.6\) dam, not \(6\) dam, so the graph cannot place all three at the same physical point.

Q19. A factory needs \(35\) dekameters of cable. The cable is sold by the meter. How many meters are needed?

A.\(3.5\) m
B.\(35\) m
C.\(350\) m ✅
D.\(3500\) m
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The prefix deka means \(10\) times the base unit. Therefore, \(35\) dekameters equals \(35\times10=350\) meters. A common misconception is treating deka as \(100\) or \(1000\), producing the larger incorrect choices.

B.\(6.5\) dekameters

Q22. A laboratory records a liquid volume of \(3.75\) L. The value must be entered in milliliters. Which decimal movement produces the correct result?

A.Move the decimal \(1\) place right to get \(37.5\) mL
B.Move the decimal \(2\) places right to get \(375\) mL
C.Move the decimal \(3\) places right to get \(3750\) mL ✅
D.Move the decimal \(3\) places left to get \(0.00375\) mL
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Moving from liters to milliliters changes from a larger unit to a unit that is \(1000\) times smaller. Therefore, the decimal moves three places to the right, giving \(3.75\times1000=3750\) mL.

📖 Explanation: There are \(100\) centimeters in one meter, so the decimal must move two places right. Thus \(0.064\) m equals \(6.4\) cm. The student's answer reflects an incorrect assumption about the number of decimal places.

Q25. A graph shows measurements of a rod recorded as \(0.45\) m, \(45\) cm, and \(450\) mm. All three points appear aligned at the same physical length. What conclusion is justified?

D.The graph must be incorrect because their numerical values differ
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Converting \(0.45\) m to centimeters gives \(45\) cm, while converting \(45\) cm to millimeters gives \(450\) mm. The different numerical values occur because the units have different sizes, not because the physical lengths differ.

Q27. A technician needs to compare \(0.0075\) km, \(7.5\) m, and \(750\) cm. Which statement correctly describes their relationship?

B.\(7.5\) m is greatest
C.\(750\) cm is greatest
D.All three measurements are equal ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Moving from kilometers to meters requires three places right, so \(0.0075\) km equals \(7.5\) m. Moving from meters to centimeters requires two more places right, so \(7.5\) m equals \(750\) cm. All represent the same length.

📖 Explanation: Converting kilometers to millimeters requires moving the decimal six places right because \(1\) km equals \(1,000,000\) mm. Thus \(0.00084\) km becomes \(840\) mm. Student A stops too early, while Student C moves the decimal too far.
