What is Locate Decimals on the Number Line in Algebra?

Definition:
Locating decimals on the number line in algebra involves placing decimal numbers at their precise positions between integers, where each decimal place corresponds to smaller subdivisions, allowing for accurate representation of decimal values and comparison of their magnitudes.

Working:
