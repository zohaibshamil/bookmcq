Q55. Which synchronization construct is most directly related to the condition variables in monitors?

Condition variables in monitors are most directly related to semaphores, as both provide waiting and signaling mechanisms. However, condition variables are specifically designed for use within monitors and have different semantics (no effect if no waiting process) compared to general semaphores.
