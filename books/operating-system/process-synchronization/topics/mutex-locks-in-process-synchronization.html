What is Mutex Locks in Process Synchronization?

A mutex lock is a simple synchronization primitive that protects a critical section by requiring a process to acquire the lock before entering and release it upon exit, where the lock state is represented as a boolean variable \( available \) that is atomically tested and modified to enforce mutual exclusion.
