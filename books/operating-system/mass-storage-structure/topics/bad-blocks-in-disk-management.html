Q1. A storage system receives logical block number 2500. The outer zone stores 140 sectors per track, the inner zone stores 100 sectors per track, and each cylinder contains 2 tracks. 🔹 A. 9
🔹 B. 10
🔹 C. 11
🔹 D. 12

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

Q2. Why does a disk with variable sectors per track require more complex logical‑to‑physical address translation than a disk with constant sectors per track?

🔹 A. Because the mapping must account for differing sector counts per track and zone
🔹 B. Because variable sectors cause the disk to spin at varying speeds
🔹 C. Because the controller needs extra memory to store sector sizes
🔹 D. Because the operating system cannot cache any data
