What is Introduction to Computing Environments in Operating Systems?

Definition:
Computing environments describe diverse platforms like mobile, cloud, or embedded systems where constraints vary, requiring tailored OS designs balancing power \( P \), performance, and connectivity.

Example:
Android OS optimizing for battery life and touch input on smartphones versus server-grade Linux prioritizing throughput and network stack efficiency in data centers.

Reason:
Different environments impose unique requirements; recognizing these allows selection or customization of appropriate OS features to meet specific user experience, cost, and reliability goals effectively.
