What is Maastricht Treaty EU enlargement and reform?

The Maastricht Treaty not only created the EU but also established accession criteria for new members and initiated institutional reforms necessary for enlargement, setting the stage for integrating post-communist Central and Eastern European states while deepening existing integration through monetary and political union provisions.
