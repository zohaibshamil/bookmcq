Q1. Which of the following best describes the primary economic characteristic of the communist systems in Eastern Europe during the 1970s?

🔹 A. Highly decentralized market mechanisms
🔹 B. Over‑centralized planning and state control
🔹 C. Predominantly private ownership of enterprises
🔹 D. Liberalized trade with the West

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The communist economies relied on central planning where the state dictated production, pricing, and distribution. This over‑centralized approach limited market signals, reducing efficiency and innovation, which contributed to the overall economic failure despite abundant natural resources.

Q2. What was a direct consequence of requiring Eastern Bloc countries to trade mainly within the communist bloc?

🔹 A. Increased competition leading to higher productivity
🔹 B. Reduced exposure to technological innovations from the West
🔹 C. Diversification of export markets
🔹 D. Improved balance of payments

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Limiting trade to the communist bloc isolated these economies from Western technological advances and competitive pressures. This reduced their ability to modernize industries and improve productivity, reinforcing economic stagnation and lower living standards.

Q3. According to Misha Glenny, what did communist leadership refuse to acknowledge about the working class?

🔹 A. That wages were higher than in the West
🔹 B. That living conditions were comparable to Western standards
🔹 C. That they lived in more squalid conditions with polluted air and water
🔹 D. That they had superior healthcare services

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Glenny reported that communist leaders denied the deteriorating living conditions of their workers, including poorer housing, contaminated air, and unsafe water, contrasting sharply with the realities faced by the population and undermining credibility of the regime.

Q4. Country X, a former Eastern Bloc state, decides to liberalize its trade in 1988. Which immediate effect is most likely?

🔹 A. A surge in foreign direct investment within the first month
🔹 B. A rapid increase in domestic productivity without any external assistance
🔹 C. Short‑term shortages of consumer goods due to supply chain adjustments
🔹 D. Immediate improvement in environmental standards

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Opening trade after decades of isolation often leads to transitional disruptions. Domestic producers may struggle to meet new demand, causing temporary shortages while the economy adjusts to market mechanisms and import flows.

Q5. How did the over‑centralization of economic decision‑making contribute to inefficiency in communist economies?

🔹 A. It allowed local managers to respond quickly to consumer demand
🔹 B. It ensured optimal allocation of resources through market signals
🔹 C. It suppressed feedback loops, leading to mismatched production and demand
🔹 D. It encouraged competition among enterprises, raising quality

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Centralized planning limited the flow of information from producers to planners, preventing adjustments based on real‑time demand. This mismatch caused overproduction of unwanted goods and underproduction of needed items, a classic inefficiency of command economies.

Q6. Which metric most directly illustrates the lower standard of living in Eastern Europe compared to Western Europe during the 1980s?

📖 Explanation:

GDP per capita reflects average economic output per person and is a standard indicator of living standards. Eastern Europe’s GDP per capita lagged significantly behind Western Europe, highlighting the disparity in wealth and consumption capacity.
