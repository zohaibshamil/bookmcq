What is Britain joins EEC 1973?

After two failed applications vetoed by France, Britain successfully joined the European Economic Community in 1973 alongside Ireland and Denmark, marking a historic shift toward continental integration despite ongoing domestic debate about sovereignty and economic benefits versus costs.
