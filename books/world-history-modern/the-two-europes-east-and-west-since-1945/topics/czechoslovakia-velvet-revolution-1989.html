What is Czechoslovakia Velvet Revolution 1989?

Mass demonstrations and civic activism forced Czechoslovakia’s communist regime to resign peacefully in late 1989 without violence, leading to Václav Havel’s election as president and free elections in 1990, exemplifying how civil society could achieve democratic transition through nonviolent resistance.
