What is Slobodan Milošević downfall 2000?

Serbian President Slobodan Milošević was ousted in October 2000 after losing elections and refusing to concede, triggering mass protests that ended his decade-long authoritarian rule responsible for Yugoslav wars, enabling democratic transition and his eventual transfer to The Hague tribunal for war crimes prosecution.
