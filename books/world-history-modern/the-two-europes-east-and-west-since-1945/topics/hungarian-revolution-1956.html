What is Hungarian Revolution 1956?

Hungarians rose against Soviet domination in October 1956 demanding democratic reforms, neutrality, and withdrawal from the Warsaw Pact, but Soviet tanks crushed the uprising in November, killing thousands and reinstalling hardline communist rule while demonstrating Moscow’s willingness to use force to maintain bloc integrity.

📝 All Hungarian Revolution 1956 MCQs

Q1. Which leader replaced Rákosi as a more moderate communist after Stalin's death?

A.Imre Nagy ✅
B.János Kádár
C.Władysław Gomułka
D.Georgy Malenkov
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Imre Nagy succeeded the hard‑line Rákosi in the early 1950s, representing a shift toward moderation within the Hungarian Communist Party. This change occurred after Stalin’s death in 1953, when the Soviet leadership encouraged softer policies, allowing Nagy to assume a leading role before his later removal.

Q2. What year did the Soviet tanks enter Budapest to suppress the uprising?

A.1955
B.1956 ✅
C.1957
D.1958
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Soviet military intervened on 3 November 1956, deploying tanks around Budapest to crush the Hungarian revolt. This decisive action marked the end of the brief period of autonomy that the revolutionaries had hoped to achieve, leading to a two‑week conflict before Soviet control was re‑established.

Q5. What was the primary reason the Soviet Union opposed Hungary withdrawing from the Warsaw Pact?
