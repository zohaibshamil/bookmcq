What is Why Britain hesitated to join European Community?

Britain initially refused to join the European Community due to its preference for Commonwealth trade ties, belief in parliamentary sovereignty over supranational authority, special relationship with the United States, and skepticism about continental integration, only applying after realizing its economic isolation in the 1960s.
