What is France after World War 2 Fourth Republic Fifth Republic?

After World War II, France established the Fourth Republic in 1946, which was characterized by political instability and weak coalition governments until it collapsed during the Algerian Crisis, leading to the creation of the Fifth Republic in 1958 under Charles de Gaulle with a strong presidential system that provided lasting stability.

Q1. Who became President of France in 1969 after Georges Pompidou's death?

B. Valéry Giscard d’Estaing

Q2. Which Republic introduced a constitution that gave the French president significantly more powers?

The Fifth Republic, established in 1958 under Charles de Gaulle, created a strong executive branch, granting the president extensive authority, unlike the weak presidential role in the Fourth Republic. Recognizing this shift is essential for understanding modern French political structure.
