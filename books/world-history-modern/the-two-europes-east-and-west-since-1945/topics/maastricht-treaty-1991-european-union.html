Q1. In which month and year was the summit held in Maastricht that produced the treaty?

🔹 A. Jun-90
🔹 B. Dec-91
🔹 C. Mar-92
🔹 D. Oct-93

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The summit that drafted the Maastricht Treaty took place in December 1991, when the heads of state of the twelve member nations convened in the Dutch city of Maastricht to negotiate a deeper political and economic union, marking a pivotal moment in European integration.

Q2. What common currency was envisaged to be adopted by the end of the 20th century under the Maastricht Treaty?

🔹 A. Pound sterling
🔹 B. Swiss franc
🔹 C. Euro
🔹 D. Danish krone

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The treaty set out a plan for a single European currency, the euro, to be introduced by the close of the 1990s. This monetary union was intended to facilitate trade, reduce exchange‑rate uncertainty, and strengthen the economic cohesion of the member states.

Q3. Which part of the treaty was removed after strong British objections?

🔹 A. Economic Chapter
🔹 B. Social Chapter
🔹 C. Foreign Policy Chapter
🔹 D. Monetary Chapter

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Britain objected to the Social Chapter, which contained regulations on workplace safety, gender equality, and redundancy protections. Because of these objections, that entire section was stripped from the final text, leaving implementation of those measures to the discretion of individual national governments.

Q4. Which three governments supported the treaty strongly to contain Germany’s power?

🔹 A. France, Italy, Spain
🔹 B. France, Netherlands, Belgium
🔹 C. Germany, United Kingdom, Denmark
🔹 D. Portugal, Greece, Sweden

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The French, Dutch and Belgian governments championed the Maastricht Treaty because they believed a deeper European Union would provide a framework to limit the influence of a reunified Germany, ensuring balanced political and economic power among the member states.

The ratification process was completed by October 1993, when every national parliament of the twelve EU members gave its approval to the Maastricht Treaty, confirming the political commitment to the new Union despite ongoing domestic debates and the removal of the Social Chapter.

🔹 B. By averaging inflation across all member states
🔹 C. By measuring wage growth only
🔹 D. By using exchange‑rate fluctuations

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The inflation rate \( \pi \) would be derived by taking the price level in the year of euro introduction (\(P_t\)) and dividing the difference from the previous year’s price level (\(P_{t-1}\)) by \(P_{t-1}\), then multiplying by 100 %. This captures the overall price change associated with the new currency.
