What is Polish crisis 1956 Poznan protests?

Workers in Poznań, Poland protested poor working conditions and political repression in June 1956, triggering violent clashes with authorities that exposed regime weakness and led to limited liberalization under Władysław Gomułka, marking the first major challenge to Stalinist orthodoxy in Eastern Europe.
