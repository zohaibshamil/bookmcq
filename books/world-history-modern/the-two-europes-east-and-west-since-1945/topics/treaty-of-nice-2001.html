What is Treaty of Nice 2001?

Signed in 2001 and effective in 2003, the Treaty of Nice reformed EU institutions to accommodate Eastern enlargement by adjusting voting weights, Commission size, and parliamentary seats, though critics argued it preserved excessive national vetoes and failed to adequately address democratic deficit ahead of massive expansion.
