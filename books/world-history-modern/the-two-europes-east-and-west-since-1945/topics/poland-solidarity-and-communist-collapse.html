Q1. In what year did General Jaruzelski become the leader of Poland?

🔹 A. 1979
🔹 B. 1981
🔹 C. 1983
🔹 D. 1985

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

General Wojciech Jaruzelski assumed the top political position in Poland in \(1981\). His rise came amid mounting social unrest and the emergence of the Solidarity movement, setting the stage for the later declaration of martial law and the complex negotiations that followed.

Q2. Which organization demanded a referendum to demonstrate its support before martial law was declared?

🔹 A. The Roman Catholic Church
🔹 B. The Polish Workers' Party
🔹 C. Solidarity
🔹 D. The National Front

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Solidarity, the independent trade‑union movement founded in 1980, pressed for a popular referendum to gauge its backing. The government’s refusal to accommodate this demand contributed to the decision to impose martial law, illustrating the clash between labor activism and authoritarian control.

Q3. What percentage of seats in the lower house were guaranteed to the communists after the April 1989 constitutional changes?

🔹 A. 0.5
🔹 B. 0.6
🔹 C. 0.65
🔹 D. 0.7

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The April 1989 reforms stipulated that communists would automatically occupy \(65\%\) of the seats in the Sejm, the lower house. This arrangement was intended to preserve the party’s influence while allowing limited opposition participation, a compromise that later proved unsustainable as Solidarity’s popularity surged.

This mechanism was designed to blend communist control with emerging democratic elements, though the guaranteed communist seats still heavily influenced the outcome.
