What is Serb-Croat War 1991-1995?

Fighting between Croatian forces and Serb rebels backed by Belgrade erupted after Croatia’s independence declaration in 1991, featuring ethnic cleansing, siege of Vukovar, and territorial disputes over Krajina region until Operation Storm and Erdut Agreement restored Croatian sovereignty over disputed areas.
