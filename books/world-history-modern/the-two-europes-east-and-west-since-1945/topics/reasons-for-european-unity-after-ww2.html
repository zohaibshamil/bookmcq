What is Reasons for European unity after WW2?

European nations pursued unity after World War II primarily to prevent future wars through economic interdependence, rebuild devastated economies collectively, counter Soviet expansion during the Cold War, and restore Europe’s global influence by pooling sovereignty in shared institutions.
