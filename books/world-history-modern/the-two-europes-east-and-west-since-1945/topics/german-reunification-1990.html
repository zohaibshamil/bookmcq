What is German reunification 1990?

Following the Berlin Wall’s fall in November 1989, East and West Germany rapidly negotiated reunification through the Two Plus Four Treaty involving Allied powers, formally uniting on October 3, 1990, ending Europe’s division and integrating the former GDR into the Federal Republic and European Communities.
