📖 From World History: Modern • 10. The Two Europes: East and West since 1945 • 33 questions available

What is Britain EC budget contribution and rebate?

Britain negotiated a significant budget rebate in 1984 because it contributed disproportionately to the EC budget while receiving minimal agricultural subsidies, resolving a major source of tension but creating lasting resentment among other member states who viewed it as unfair special treatment.
