What is Yugoslav civil war 1991-1995?

Yugoslavia disintegrated violently after Slovenia and Croatia declared independence in 1991, sparking multi-ethnic conflicts fueled by nationalist leaders exploiting economic crisis and weakening federal institutions, resulting in widespread atrocities and international intervention before Dayton Accords ended major hostilities.
