What is West Germany 1949-1990 history?

West Germany, officially the Federal Republic of Germany, was founded in 1949 as a democratic capitalist state that experienced an economic miracle and became a key NATO member, eventually achieving peaceful reunification with communist East Germany in 1990 following the fall of the Berlin Wall.

Q1. In which year was the German Federal Republic (West Germany) officially established?

A. 1945
B. 1949 ✅
C. 1950
D. 1955

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Federal Republic of Germany was founded on 23 May 1949, marking the creation of West Germany as a sovereign state separate from the Soviet‑occupied zone. This date is central to understanding post‑war German political development and the beginning of the Wirtschaftswunder, the period of rapid economic growth.

Q2. Who served as the first Chancellor of the German Federal Republic (West Germany)?

A. KonradAdenauer ✅
B. Ludwig Erhard
C. Helmut Kohl
D. Willy Brandt

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Konrad Adenauer became the inaugural Chancellor in 1949, leading the new republic through reconstruction and the early stages of the economic miracle. His conservative policies, close ties with the United States, and support for European integration set the foundation for West Germany’s post‑war success.

Q3. Which post‑war aid program supplied substantial American investment to West Germany?

A. MarshallPlan ✅
B. Truman Doctrine
C. Eisenhower Doctrine
D. Marshall Aid

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Marshall Plan, formally the European Recovery Program, provided West Germany with critical financial assistance, facilitating the import of machinery, raw materials, and technology. This influx of capital helped rebuild industrial capacity, accelerate modernization, and lay the groundwork for the country’s rapid economic expansion in the 1950s.

Q21. If steel production increased from 10 million tonnes in 1938 to 15 million tonnes in 1960, what is the absolute increase?

B. 10 million tonnes
C. 15 million tonnes
D. 20 million tonnes

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The absolute growth equals the 1960 figure minus the 1938 baseline: 15 million t − 10 million t = 5 million tonnes. This straightforward calculation underscores the tangible scale of industrial expansion that underpinned West Germany’s broader economic resurgence.

B. A5 ✅
