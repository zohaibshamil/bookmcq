Q1. Which plan was the first Russian-sponsored step toward an economically united Eastern bloc?

🔹 A. Marshall Plan
🔹 B. Molotov Plan
🔹 C. Truman Doctrine
🔹 D. Warsaw Pact

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Molotov Plan, announced in 1947, was the Soviet Union’s direct response to the American Marshall Aid program. It established trade agreements with satellite states, aiming to integrate their economies with the USSR and provide an alternative source of assistance, thereby cementing Soviet influence in Eastern Europe.

📖 Explanation:

COMECON was created by the Soviet Union in 1949 to coordinate economic planning among its satellite states. The organization sought to promote industrial and agricultural development through centralized directives, reflecting the broader strategy of integrating the economies of the Eastern bloc under Soviet guidance.

Q3. Which organization was created to ensure that all communist governments followed the same political line as Moscow?

The Communist Information Bureau, known as Cominform, was established in 1947 to align the political policies of communist parties across the Soviet sphere. Membership was mandatory for all satellite states, and the body served as a mechanism for enforcing Moscow‑directed ideological conformity.

Q12. Compare the doctor‑to‑population ratios of the USSR and Britain in 1980. How many times more doctors did the USSR have per capita?

🔹 B. 2.4 times
🔹 C. 3.0 times
🔹 D. 4.0 times

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The USSR’s ratio of one doctor per 258 people versus Britain’s one per 618 people yields a comparative factor of \( \frac{618}{258}\approx2.4\). Hence, the Soviet Union had roughly 2.4 times more physicians per capita than the United Kingdom in 1980.
