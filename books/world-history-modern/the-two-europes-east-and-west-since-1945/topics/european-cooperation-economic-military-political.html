What is European cooperation economic military political?

Post-war European cooperation evolved across three pillars: economically through the Common Market and customs union, militarily via NATO and later EU defense initiatives, and politically through supranational institutions like the European Parliament that fostered integration beyond mere trade agreements.

Q1. In which year was the Organization for European Economic Co‑operation (OEEC) officially established?

D.1950
