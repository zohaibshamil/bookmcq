What is Collapse of communism in Eastern Europe?

In 1989, communist regimes across Eastern Europe fell in rapid succession through mostly peaceful revolutions driven by popular demands for democracy, economic failure, loss of Soviet support, and civil society activism, ending four decades of Soviet domination and reshaping Europe’s political map.

Q1. Which Polish leader was forced to resign after the riots of \(1970\)?

A.Władysław Gomułka ✅
B.Edward Gierek
C.Lech Wałęsa

📖 Explanation: Gomułka's resignation followed the violent protests sparked by sudden food price increases in \(1970\). The unrest forced the party to replace him, marking a clear sign of growing dissent within the communist system.
