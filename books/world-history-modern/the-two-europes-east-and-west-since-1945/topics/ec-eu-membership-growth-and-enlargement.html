📖 From World History: Modern • 10. The Two Europes: East and West since 1945 • 33 questions available

What is EC EU membership growth and enlargement?

From its original six members, the EC/EU expanded through multiple enlargements to include Southern European democracies in the 1980s, Nordic and Austrian states in 1995, Central and Eastern European nations after communism’s collapse, and Balkan countries, transforming it into a continent-wide union of 27 members.

Q15. Imagine the Community’s total population grew to \(350\) million after a hypothetical 2000 enlargement. If the original \(320\) million represented a 10% increase from the pre‑1986 total, what was the pre‑1986 population?
