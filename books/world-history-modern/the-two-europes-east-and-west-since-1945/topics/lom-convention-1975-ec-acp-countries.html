Q1. What was the primary objective of the Lomé Convention signed in 1975?

🔹 A. To establish a customs union between Europe and Africa
🔹 B. To promote trade and aid cooperation between the European Community and ACP countries
🔹 C. To create a common monetary policy for member states
🔹 D. To unify the legal systems of signatory states

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The convention aimed to foster trade, development assistance, and economic cooperation between the European Community and the African, Caribbean and Pacific (ACP) states, providing preferential access and aid without creating a customs union.

The convention specifically targeted ACP countries, offering them preferential trade terms and development aid from the European Community, distinguishing them from other global groupings.
