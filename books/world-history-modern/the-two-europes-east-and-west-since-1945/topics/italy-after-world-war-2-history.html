What is Italy after World War 2 history?

Post-war Italy transitioned from monarchy to republic in 1946 and joined NATO and the European Community, experiencing rapid economic growth known as the Italian Miracle despite chronic political instability caused by frequent government changes and regional disparities between the industrial north and agricultural south.

Q1. During which years did Alcide de Gasperi serve as Prime Minister of Italy?

A.1946–1953 ✅
B.1950–1957
C.1948–1955
D.1952–1959
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Alcide de Gasperi led the country from the immediate post‑war period through the early 1950s, specifically from \(1946\) to \(1953\). His tenure helped stabilize Italy’s institutions and set the foundation for the new Republic, making his dates a cornerstone of modern Italian political history.

Q23. If inflation fell from 12 % to 8 % and unemployment from 10 % to 7 % under Craxi, calculate the average annual percentage point reduction assuming a four‑year term.

A.1 % inflation, 0.75 % unemployment per year ✅
B.1 % inflation, 1 % unemployment per year
C.0.5 % inflation, 0.75 % unemployment per year
D.2 % inflation, 1.5 % unemployment per year
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Inflation dropped \(12\% - 8\% = 4\%\) over four years, yielding \(4\% ÷ 4 = 1\%\) per year. Unemployment fell \(10\% - 7\% = 3\%\) over the same period, giving \(3\% ÷ 4 = 0.75\%\) per year. These averages illustrate the modest but steady improvements during his administration.
