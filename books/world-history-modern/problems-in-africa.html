About 25. Problems in Africa

Post-independence Africa has faced persistent challenges including authoritarian rule, civil wars, economic mismanagement, and the lingering effects of colonial boundaries that grouped diverse peoples together artificially. Resource wealth often fueled corruption and conflict rather than development, while external interference and debt burdens constrained policy autonomy and perpetuated dependency.

Despite these difficulties, African nations have also demonstrated resilience through regional cooperation, democratic progress in some countries, and vibrant civil societies pushing for accountability. Contemporary issues like climate change, youth unemployment, and migration intersect with historical legacies, requiring both internal reforms and fairer international partnerships to achieve sustainable peace and prosperity.
