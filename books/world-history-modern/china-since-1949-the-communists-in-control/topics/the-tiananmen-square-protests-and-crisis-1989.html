What is The Tiananmen Square Protests and Crisis (1989)?

The Tiananmen Square protests began in 1989 as students and other citizens demonstrated in Beijing for political reform, greater freedom, less corruption, and greater government accountability. After weeks of demonstrations, the Chinese government declared martial law and sent troops into Beijing. On June 3–4, soldiers used force to clear the protest areas, resulting in many deaths and injuries. The crackdown ended the movement and strengthened the government's control while attracting widespread international criticism.
