What is Leadership Changes in China Since 2000?

Since 2000, China has experienced several changes in national leadership while remaining under the control of the Chinese Communist Party. Jiang Zemin was followed by Hu Jintao in 2002, and Hu was followed by Xi Jinping in 2012. Each leadership period continued China's economic development while emphasizing political stability and Party authority. Under Xi Jinping, power has become more centralized, and the government has placed strong emphasis on national security, Party discipline, technological development, and China's position as a major global power.
