What is Deng Xiaoping's Economic Reforms and Modernization Policies?

Deng Xiaoping introduced major economic reforms after 1978 to modernize China while keeping Communist Party rule. His policies reduced strict central planning and gave farmers, businesses, and local governments greater economic freedom. Special Economic Zones were established to attract foreign investment, technology, and trade. Deng promoted the Four Modernizations in agriculture, industry, science and technology, and defense. These reforms helped produce rapid economic growth, although they also increased inequality and created new social and economic problems.
