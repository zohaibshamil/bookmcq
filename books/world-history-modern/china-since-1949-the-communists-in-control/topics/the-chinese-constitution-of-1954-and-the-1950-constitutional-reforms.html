What is The Chinese Constitution of 1954 and the 1950 Constitutional Reforms?

The Chinese Communist government introduced major political reforms after coming to power. The 1954 Constitution formally established the People's Republic of China as a socialist state and created government institutions such as the National People's Congress. Earlier reforms during the 1950s strengthened Communist Party control and changed China's political and legal system. Although the Constitution described rights and responsibilities for citizens, real political power remained strongly controlled by the Communist Party.

📝 All The Chinese Constitution of 1954 and the 1950 Constitutional Reforms MCQs

Q1. According to the 1950 constitution, how long is the term for members of the National People’s Congress?

A.2 years
B.3 years
C.4 years ✅
D.5 years
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The constitution stipulated that delegates to the National People’s Congress serve a four‑year term, allowing for periodic renewal while maintaining continuity in legislative work. This four‑year cycle was intended to balance stability with the opportunity for new representation, reflecting the early PRC’s emphasis on structured governance.

Q2. If a 20‑year‑old citizen wishes to vote under the 1950 Constitution, is he eligible?

A.Yes, he can vote ✅
B.No, he cannot vote
C.Only if he is a Communist Party member
D.Only if he is over 25
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The constitution granted voting rights to all citizens aged 18 and above, regardless of party affiliation. Therefore a 20‑year‑old meets the age requirement and may participate in elections, illustrating the broad suffrage principle adopted during the early years of the People’s Republic of China.

Q3. A party member wants to run for a seat in the National People’s Congress but is not nominated by any constituency. According to the constitution, what is the most likely outcome?

A.He will be automatically elected
B.He may be elected if the Congress approves
C.He cannot stand for election ✅
D.He can stand but must win a local vote
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The 1950 constitution limited candidacy to Communist Party members, but it also required a formal nomination process. Without a constituency nomination, the individual lacks the procedural legitimacy to appear on the ballot, making him ineligible to stand despite party membership.

Q4. Which body was responsible for selecting the Political Bureau (Politburo) according to the 1950 constitution?

A.National People’s Congress
B.State Council ✅
C.Chairman of the Republic
D.Central Committee of the Communist Party
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The State Council, acting as the executive organ, held the authority to choose members of the Politburo. This arrangement centralized decision‑making power within the executive branch while still reflecting the Communist Party’s dominance, ensuring that key political directions were coordinated by the highest state officials.

Q5. Under the 1950 constitution, which of the following statements about the Chairman of the Republic is correct?

A.Elected by the public in a direct vote
B.Appointed by the Communist Party Central Committee
C.Elected by the National People’s Congress ✅
D.Rotates annually among provincial leaders
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The Chairman of the Republic was elected by the National People’s Congress, the supreme legislative body. This indirect election reinforced the principle that the state’s highest office derived its legitimacy from the people’s representatives, while still operating within the party‑controlled political framework.

Q6. A local committee proposes a law that conflicts with an existing national statute. According to the constitutional hierarchy, what should happen?

A.The local law supersedes the national law
B.Both laws are enforced simultaneously
C.The national law prevails and the local law is voided ✅
D.The conflict is resolved by a popular referendum
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The constitution establishes the National People’s Congress as the ultimate legislative authority, meaning national statutes outrank local regulations. In case of conflict, the higher‑level law automatically nullifies the lower‑level one, preserving legal uniformity across the country and preventing fragmented governance.

Q7. During a crisis, the State Council decides to suspend certain legislative sessions. Which constitutional provision justifies this action?

A.Article granting emergency powers to the State Council ✅
B.Clause allowing the Chairman to dissolve the Congress
C.Section permitting the Politburo to override legislation
D.No provision permits suspension; it is unconstitutional
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The constitution includes an article that endows the State Council with emergency powers to ensure continuity of governance during extraordinary circumstances. This clause authorizes temporary suspension of legislative activities to address urgent matters, reflecting the designers’ intent to maintain stability while still respecting the hierarchy of state organs.

Q8. Which of the following best describes the role of the National People’s Congress in the 1950 constitution?

A.Executive enforcement of laws
B.Final authority for legislation ✅
C.Judicial review of statutes
D.Advisory body without real power
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The National People’s Congress was designated as the supreme legislative organ, holding the final authority to enact, amend, and repeal laws. Its central position in the constitutional framework underscores the emphasis on a single, unified legislative body guiding the nation’s legal and policy direction.

Q9. If a non‑party citizen wishes to become a member of the State Council, what does the constitution stipulate?

A.He can be appointed if he has a university degree
B.He may be elected by the National People’s Congress
C.He cannot serve because only party members may hold the position ✅
D.He can serve after passing a loyalty oath

📖 Explanation: The constitution explicitly limited all high‑level state positions, including the State Council, to Communist Party members. This restriction ensured that the party maintained absolute control over the executive branch, preventing non‑party individuals from accessing senior governmental roles.

📖 Explanation: The passage emphasizes that the 1950 constitution was pivotal because it created a strong central government after a prolonged period without one. This centralization was crucial for political stability and unified administration, marking a significant shift in China’s governance structure.

Q11. Which statement accurately reflects the election eligibility for National People’s Congress members?

📖 Explanation: The constitution granted voting rights to all citizens aged 18 and above, yet it restricted candidacy to Communist Party members. This dual approach expanded the electorate while maintaining party dominance, ensuring that elected representatives were aligned with the ruling ideology.

This approach reinforced the party’s monopoly on power and streamlined decision‑making.

Q14. A newly drafted law aims to grant regional autonomy. Based on the 1950 constitution’s emphasis on central authority, what is the most probable legislative outcome?

📖 Explanation: Given the constitution’s focus on a strong central government, any proposal that expands regional autonomy would likely be viewed as contradictory to the foundational principle of centralized authority, leading to its rejection or substantial alteration to preserve national unity.
