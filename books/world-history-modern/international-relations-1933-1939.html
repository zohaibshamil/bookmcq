About 5. International Relations 1933-1939

From 1933 to 1939, international relations deteriorated rapidly as Adolf Hitler’s Nazi Germany openly challenged the Versailles settlement and pursued aggressive expansionism. The policy of appeasement adopted by Britain and France, hoping to avoid another war by conceding to limited demands, ultimately failed as Axis powers tested and broke international norms through remilitarization and territorial annexations.

This era saw the complete breakdown of collective security as the League of Nations proved powerless and alliances shifted toward defensive blocs. By 1939, diplomacy had been replaced by brute force and ideological polarization, with the invasion of Poland marking the definitive end of peace efforts and the beginning of the Second World War.
