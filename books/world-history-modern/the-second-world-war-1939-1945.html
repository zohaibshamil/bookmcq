About 6. The Second World War 1939-1945

The Second World War was the deadliest conflict in human history, fought on a truly global scale across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Pacific with unprecedented levels of civilian suffering and destruction. It involved total mobilization of societies and economies, witnessed atrocities like the Holocaust and the use of atomic weapons, and fundamentally reshaped the moral and political landscape of the modern world.

The war ended with the decisive defeat of the Axis powers and the emergence of the United States and Soviet Union as rival superpowers. Rather than restoring the pre-war order, the conflict’s conclusion led directly to the division of Europe, the beginning of decolonization, and the establishment of new international institutions designed to prevent such a catastrophe from happening again.
