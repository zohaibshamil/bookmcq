What is HIV AIDS prevention and treatment efforts?

Global efforts against HIV/AIDS have combined prevention strategies like condom promotion, needle exchanges, and education with life-saving antiretroviral therapy, significantly reducing transmission and mortality since the 2000s, though stigma, funding gaps, and inequitable access remain challenges.
