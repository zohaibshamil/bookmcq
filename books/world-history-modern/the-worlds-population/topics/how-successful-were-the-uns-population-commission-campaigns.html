What is How successful were the UN's Population Commission campaigns??

UN population campaigns achieved mixed success; they significantly raised awareness and expanded access to family planning and reproductive health services globally, but faced political opposition, cultural resistance, and funding limitations that prevented uniform achievement of demographic goals.
