What is Eastern Front 1915 World War 1?

The Eastern Front in 1915 saw Central Powers launch major successful offensives that pushed Russian forces hundreds of miles back through Poland and Lithuania in what became known as the Great Retreat, causing enormous Russian losses but failing to knock Russia out of the war entirely.
