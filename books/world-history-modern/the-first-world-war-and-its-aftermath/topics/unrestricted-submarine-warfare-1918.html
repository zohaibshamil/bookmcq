Q1. Analytical: Evaluate the economic effect of unrestricted submarine warfare on British wartime production in 1917‑1918. Which statement best reflects the outcome?

🔹 A. Production increased due to scarcity‑driven efficiency
🔹 B. Production remained unchanged despite shipping losses
🔹 C. Production declined sharply because raw material imports were curtailed
🔹 D. Production shifted entirely to colonial sources

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The sinking of merchant vessels limited the flow of essential raw materials like steel and coal, causing a marked decline in British industrial output. Although some efficiencies emerged, the overall effect was a contraction of wartime production capacity.
