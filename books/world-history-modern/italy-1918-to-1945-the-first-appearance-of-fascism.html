About 13. Italy (1918 to 1945): The First Appearance of Fascism

Italy after World War I faced economic chaos, social unrest, and nationalist frustration over perceived betrayals at the peace table, creating fertile ground for Benito Mussolini’s fascist movement. Fascism promised national rebirth through authoritarian rule, violent suppression of socialism, and glorification of the state, becoming the world’s first totalitarian regime and a model for others.

Mussolini’s regime dismantled democracy, controlled media and education, and pursued aggressive foreign policies including the invasion of Ethiopia and alliance with Nazi Germany. Though initially popular for restoring order, fascism ultimately led Italy into disastrous wars, internal resistance, and national humiliation, demonstrating the dangers of ultranationalism and dictatorial rule.
