What is The Warlord Era in China (1916–1928)?

The Warlord Era was a period of chaos after the fall of the Qing dynasty when regional military leaders controlled different parts of China, fought each other for power, ignored the central government, and caused suffering for ordinary people through taxation and violence, preventing national unity and effective governance.

📝 All The Warlord Era in China (1916–1928) MCQs

Q1. In which year did the first Opium War begin?

A.1839 ✅
B.1845
C.1850
D.1860
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Opium War began in 1839 when British forces confronted the Qing dynasty over the illegal opium trade, leading to a series of conflicts that forced China to concede Hong Kong and open several ports to foreign trade.

Q2. Which foreign power first seized Hong Kong as a result of the Opium Wars?

A.Britain
B.France ✅
C.United States
D.Japan
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Britain secured Hong Kong after the Treaty of Nanking in 1842, establishing it as a strategic trading hub and a foothold for further colonial expansion in East Asia.

Q3. The Taiping Rebellion lasted from which years?

A.1850–1864
B.1840–1855
C.1860–1875 ✅
D.1870–1885
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The massive civil war known as the Taiping Rebellion unfolded between 1850 and 1864, challenging Qing authority and causing widespread devastation before being suppressed by regional forces.

Q4. What name did the Taiping rebels give to the state they tried to establish?

A.Heavenly Kingdom of Great Peace
B.Qing Empire
C.Republic of China
D.Ming Restoration ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The rebels called their envisioned regime the \Heavenly Kingdom of Great Peace\ (Taiping Tianguo), reflecting a blend of Christian millenarianism and radical social reforms.

Q5. Between which years did China's population rise from 140 million to 410 million?

A.1741‑1841 ✅
B.1800‑1900
C.1600‑1700
D.1700‑1800
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Rapid demographic growth between 1741 and 1841 increased the population from roughly 140 million to 410 million, straining agricultural output and prompting social unrest.

Q6. Which territory did China cede to Japan after the 1894‑95 war?

A.Taiwan
B.Manchuria ✅
C.Hong Kong
D.Macau
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Following defeat in the First Sino‑Japanese War, China was forced to relinquish Taiwan (Formosa) to Japan, marking a significant loss of overseas territory.

Q7. Which era directly preceded the Warlord Era?

A.Late Qing ✅
B.Republican
C.Ming
D.Sui
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Late Qing dynasty, weakened by internal rebellions and foreign pressure, gave way to the fragmented Warlord Era after the 1911 revolution toppled imperial rule.

Q8. In which city was the central government located during most of the Warlord Era?

A.Beijing
B.Shanghai
C.Nanjing
D.Guangzhou ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Beijing (Peking) remained the nominal capital, though actual control frequently shifted among competing warlords and their factions.

Q9. What term describes the regional armies the Qing dynasty relied on after the Taiping Rebellion?

A.Beiyang Army ✅
B.Green Standard Army
C.Regional Militias
D.Imperial Guard
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Beiyang Army, originally a modernized regional force, became the backbone of Qing military power after the failure of traditional troops during the Taiping uprising.

Q10. In which year did the Warlord Era officially end?

A.1928
B.1930 ✅
C.1916
D.1949
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Northern Expedition led by the Nationalist Party in 1928 effectively ended the Warlord Era by reunifying much of China under a central government.

Q11. If China's population grew by 190 million over 100 years, what was the average annual increase?

A.1.0 million per year
B.1.9 million per year ✅
C.2.5 million per year
D.3.0 million per year
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Dividing the total increase (190 million) by the time span (100 years) yields an average growth of \( \frac{190\,\text{million}}{100}=1.9\,\text{million per year} \), illustrating the demographic pressure on resources.

Q12. How did foreign concessions in Chinese ports affect Qing sovereignty?

A.They strengthened central control
B.They had little impact
C.They eroded sovereignty by granting extraterritorial rights ✅
D.They unified regional armies
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Concessions gave foreigners legal immunity and control over trade, undermining Qing authority and fostering resentment that later fueled warlord ambitions and nationalist movements.

Q13. Which warlord controlled the Beiyang Government after Yuan Shikai's death?

A.Duan Qirui ✅
B.Zhang Zuolin
C.Feng Yuxiang
D.Cao Kun
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: After Yuan Shikai died in 1916, Duan Qirui, a leading figure in the Beiyang clique, assumed nominal control of the central government, though his authority was constantly contested by rival warlords.

Q14. A province has five regional armies each contributing 10,000 troops. If they all defect to a warlord, how many troops does he gain?

A.30000
B.40000
C.45000
D.50000 ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Summing the forces gives \(5 \times 10{,}000 = 50{,}000\) soldiers, a sizable reinforcement that could shift the balance of power in a contested region.

Q15. What was the primary cause of central authority fragmentation after 1911?

A.Overpopulation
B.Foreign pressure ✅
C.Weak imperial army
D.Regional warlords' ambitions
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The collapse of the Qing dynasty left a power vacuum that regional commanders exploited; their personal ambitions, rather than external forces, drove the splintering of central authority.

Q16. Which foreign power obtained a concession in Tianjin in 1900?

A.Britain
B.Germany
C.Russia ✅
D.Japan
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Russia secured a significant concession in Tianjin after the Boxer Rebellion, using it to project influence in North China and protect its commercial interests.

Q17. If a warlord levied a 5 % tax on a province producing 20 million taels of rice, what revenue does he collect?

A.500,000 taels
B.1,000,000 taels ✅
C.1,500,000 taels
D.2,000,000 taels
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The calculation is \(0.05 \times 20{,}000{,}000 = 1{,}000{,}000\) taels, illustrating how modest tax rates could still generate substantial income for a warlord.

Q18. Which reform movement created the New Army that later became a warlord power base?

A.Self‑Strengthening Movement ✅
B.Hundred Days' Reform
C.May Fourth Movement
D.Boxer Rebellion
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Self‑Strengthening Movement of the late 19th century emphasized modern military training, leading to the formation of the New Army, which later fragmented into competing warlord forces.

Q19. An army grew from 30,000 to 80,000 troops over 8 years. What was the average annual increase?

A.5,000 per year
B.6,250 per year
C.7,500 per year ✅
D.10,000 per year
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The net increase is 50,000; dividing by 8 years gives \( \frac{80{,}000-30{,}000}{8}=6{,}250\) soldiers added each year, reflecting rapid militarization.

Q20. Which treaty forced China to cede Hong Kong to Britain?

A.Treaty of Nanking ✅
B.Treaty of Tientsin
C.Treaty of Shimonoseki
D.Treaty of Beijing
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Treaty of Nanking (1842) concluded the First Opium War, requiring China to hand over Hong Kong and open several ports to British commerce.

Q21. What ideology most influenced early Republican warlords?

A.Marxism
B.Confucianism
C.Nationalism ✅
D.Anarchism

📖 Explanation: Nationalist ideas, emphasizing a strong centralized state and modernization, shaped many warlords' political rhetoric even as they pursued personal power.

Q22. If China had concessions in 80 ports each generating 0.8 million taels annually, what was total revenue?

A.40 million taels
B.48 million taels
C.56 million taels
D.64 million taels ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Multiplying the number of ports by revenue per port yields \(80 \times 0.8 = 64\) million taels, a substantial income that foreign powers extracted from China.

Q23. Which warlord was known as the \Beijing Warlord\?

B.Zhang Zuolin

📖 Explanation: Wu Peifu earned the nickname because his forces dominated the political and military scene around Beijing during the early 1920s.

Q24. During the Warlord Era, which entity claimed legitimacy as the successor of the Qing?

A.Republic of China
B.Manchukuo
C.Beiyang Government ✅
D.Southern Government
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The Beiyang Government, based in Beijing, asserted continuity with the Qing dynasty, positioning itself as the legitimate central authority despite lacking effective control.

Q25. Evaluate how the failure of the Qing central army contributed to the rise of regional warlords, using evidence from the Taiping Rebellion.

A.It showed that centralized forces were superior
B.It demonstrated that regional armies could defeat large rebellions, encouraging local commanders to assert autonomy
C.It had no impact on later developments ✅
D.It caused immediate foreign intervention

📖 Explanation: The Qing’s reliance on regional militias to suppress the Taiping uprising revealed the central army’s weakness, prompting provincial leaders to retain independent forces that later evolved into autonomous warlord factions.

Q26. If a warlord’s customs revenue fell by 12 % after a foreign concession, and his original revenue was 5 million taels, what is the new revenue?

A.4,400,000 taels ✅
B.5,000,000 taels
C.5,600,000 taels
D.6,000,000 taels
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Applying a 12 % reduction: \(5{,}000{,}000 \times (1-0.12)=4{,}400{,}000\) taels, indicating a significant fiscal loss that could weaken a warlord’s capacity to maintain troops.

Q27. Why did the Beiyang Army fragment after Yuan Shikai’s death?

A.Because of foreign pressure
B.Because of a popular uprising
C.Because of economic collapse
D.Because competing cliques vied for power, leading to splintering ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Yuan’s death unleashed rival factions within the Beiyang Army; personal ambitions and lack of a unifying leader caused the force to split into multiple warlord armies.

Q28. Britain demanded 3 % of Chinese export value from 80 ports, with total exports worth 200 million taels. How much did Britain claim?

A.4,000,000 taels
B.6,000,000 taels ✅
C.8,000,000 taels
D.10,000,000 taels

📖 Explanation: The claim equals \(0.03 \times 200{,}000{,}000 = 6{,}000{,}000\) taels, illustrating the financial pressure foreign powers placed on China through unequal trade agreements.

A.It accelerated industrial growth through competition
B.It had minimal effect on industry
C.It hindered modernization by fragmenting investment and creating instability ✅
D.It led to foreign takeover of all factories
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Continuous conflict diverted resources away from infrastructure, discouraged foreign investment, and prevented coordinated economic planning, thereby slowing the development of a modern industrial sector.
