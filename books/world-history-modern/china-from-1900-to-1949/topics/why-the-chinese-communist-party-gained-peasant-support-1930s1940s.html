What is Why the Chinese Communist Party Gained Peasant Support (1930s–1940s)?

The Chinese Communist Party gained peasant support in the 1930s and 1940s by redistributing land from landlords to poor farmers, reducing rents and taxes, treating peasants with respect, and presenting themselves as defenders of rural communities against both warlords and Japanese invaders.
