What is Battle of Midway 1942?

The Battle of Midway in June 1942 was a decisive naval engagement where the US Navy sank four Japanese aircraft carriers, halting Japanese expansion in the Pacific and shifting the balance of naval power to the Allies.

Q1. When did the Battle of Midway begin?

D.May 4 1942

📖 Explanation: The battle commenced on June 4, 1942, when United States carrier forces intercepted the Japanese fleet near Midway Atoll. This date is widely recognized as the start of the engagement that turned the tide in the Pacific, leading to a decisive American victory.

Q9. Assuming each US carrier launched 12 aircraft with a 10% chance of sinking a Japanese carrier, what is the expected number of carriers sunk by three US carriers?

A.2.4
B.3.6 ✅
C.4.8
D.6
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: With three carriers each launching 12 aircraft, the total number of aircraft is 3×12=363 \times 12 = 363×12=36. Multiplying by the 10% success probability gives an expected value of 36×0.10=3.636 \times 0.10 = 3.636×0.10=3.6 carriers sunk. This statistical estimate reflects the average outcome over many similar engagements.
