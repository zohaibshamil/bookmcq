Q1. When did Operation Barbarossa commence?

🔹 C. 7 December 1941
🔹 D. 6 June 1944

📖 Explanation:

The invasion began on 22 June 1941, when German forces crossed the Soviet border in a massive surprise attack, marking the start of the Eastern Front in World II.

Q2. Which country signed a non‑aggression pact with Germany shortly before the invasion?

🔹 A. Soviet Union
🔹 B. Italy
🔹 C. Japan
🔹 D. Spain

📖 Explanation:

The Molotov‑Ribbentrop Pact was concluded between Nazi Germany and the Soviet Union in August 1939, providing a temporary diplomatic cover before Germany broke it with the launch of Barbarossa.

Q3. What was the primary strategic aim of the German invasion of the Soviet Union?

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Hitler intended a rapid, decisive campaign to cripple the Soviet state, knock out its army, and seize key economic regions, hoping to avoid a prolonged war of attrition.

Q11. Given that Germans advanced roughly \(300\) km per month in summer 1941, what total distance did they cover after three months?

Multiplying the monthly advance (\(300\) km) by three months yields \(300\times3 = 900\) km, which matches the historical depth of the initial German penetration.

Q15. If German supply lines were stretched to \(500\) km and each truck could travel \(400\) km per day, how many days for a round‑trip convoy?
