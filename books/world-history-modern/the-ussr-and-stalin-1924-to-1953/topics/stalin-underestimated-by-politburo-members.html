What is Stalin underestimated by Politburo members?

Q11. Assuming each Politburo member has a thirty percent chance to detect Stalin’s move and two independent moves occur what is the probability that at least one detection happens expressed using probability notation

A. \(0.51\)
B. \(0.7\)
C. \(0.6\)
D. \(0.9\) ✅

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Each member has a seventy percent chance to notice Stalin’s action. The chance that a single member fails is \(1-0.7=0.3\). For two independent moves the probability that neither detection occurs is \(0.3^{2}=0.09\). Therefore the probability that at least one detection happens is \(1-0.09=0.91\) which rounds to \(0.9\). This matches option D.
