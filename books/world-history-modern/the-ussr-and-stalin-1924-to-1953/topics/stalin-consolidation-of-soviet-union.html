What is Stalin consolidation of Soviet Union?

Stalin unified the diverse republics under centralized Russian-dominated control by suppressing national identities, enforcing linguistic uniformity, and eliminating regional autonomy to create a single obedient Soviet state.

Q1. Which republic was NOT part of the six Soviet republics by 1925?

A. Russia
B. Ukraine
C. Belorussia
D. Kazakhstan ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Kazakhstan never became one of the six founding Soviet republics; the six were Russia, Transcaucasia (Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan), Ukraine, Belorussia, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan, leaving Kazakhstan outside that initial group.

Q6. Why did Stalin view the independent Transcaucasian states as a threat during the civil war?

Q8. How did Stalin’s two‑handed approach differ from the tsarist policy of ‘Russianization’?

Q11. Assess the long‑term impact of encouraging national cultures while retaining central authority on the stability of the USSR.

A. It prevented any nationalist movements
B. It created a fragile balance that eventually contributed to dissolution ✅
C. It had no effect on stability
D. It strengthened Moscow’s absolute control

📖 Explanation:

The limited cultural autonomy placated minority groups temporarily, but the underlying central‑dominant structure left unresolved grievances, making the union vulnerable to later nationalist resurgence and ultimately playing a role in its breakup.
