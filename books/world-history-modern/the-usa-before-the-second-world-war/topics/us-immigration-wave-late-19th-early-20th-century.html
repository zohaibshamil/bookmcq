Q1. Approximately how many immigrants arrived in the United States between 1860 and 1930?

🔹 A. 10 million
🔹 B. 20 million
🔹 C. 30 million
🔹 D. 40 million

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The period from 1860 to 1930 saw a massive influx of people seeking new opportunities. Historical records estimate that over thirty million individuals crossed the Atlantic and settled in the United States during these seven decades. This figure far exceeds the earlier centuries' totals and illustrates the scale of the nineteenth‑century immigration wave.

Q2. Which immigrant group was predominant in the United States between 1840 and 1870?

🔹 A. Germans
🔹 B. Irish
🔹 C. Italians
🔹 D. Russians

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

During the mid‑nineteenth century, the Irish constituted the largest contingent of newcomers to America. Economic hardship, famine, and political unrest in Ireland drove millions to seek a better life across the Atlantic. Consequently, Irish immigrants dominated the flow of people entering the United States from 1840 through 1870.

Q4. If immigration had been reduced by 12 % the total U.S. population in 1930 would have been what fraction of the actual population? Use expression \(0.88 \times \text{actual}\).

A twelve‑percent reduction in immigration would directly translate into a twelve‑percent decrease in the overall population, assuming other factors remained constant. Mathematically this is expressed as \(0.88 \times\) the actual 1930 population. Therefore the correct choice reflects a 12 % lower population relative to the historical figure.

Q6. Economic historian William Ashworth estimated that without immigration the labor force in 1920 would have been 14 % lower. Assuming per‑capita productivity remained constant, what would be the most direct effect on total output?

A smaller workforce directly reduces the total goods and services produced, making a proportional decrease in total output the most immediate consequence.

Q12. In which year did the United States implement annual immigration quotas that sharply reduced the flow of newcomers?

🔹 A. 1912
🔹 B. 1924
🔹 C. 1930
🔹 D. 1945

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

This legislation marked a decisive turn toward isolationist policies and dramatically curbed the massive influx of newcomers that had characterized the previous decades.

🔹 C. 0.05
🔹 D. 0.07

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Computing \(\ln(30/5)=\ln(6)\approx1.79\) and dividing by 50 yields roughly 0.0358, or about 3.5 % per year. This rate reflects the rapid settlement of the western territories.
