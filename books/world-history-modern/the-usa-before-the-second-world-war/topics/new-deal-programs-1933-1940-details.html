What is New Deal programs 1933-1940 details?

Between 1933 and 1940, New Deal programs included the CCC for youth employment, WPA for public infrastructure, Social Security for elderly support, FDIC for bank insurance, and NRA for industry regulation, collectively reshaping the federal government’s role in the economy and society.

Q46. If a farmer cuts output by \(15\%\) and the market price rises by \(25\%\), what is the net effect on his revenue assuming his original revenue was \(R\)?

A.Revenue falls to \(0.85R\)
B.Revenue becomes \(1.0625R\) ✅
C.Revenue rises to \(1.25R\)
D.Revenue unchanged
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Original revenue \(R = P \times Q\). New quantity \(Q' = 0.85Q\); new price \(P' = 1.25P\). New revenue \(R' = P'Q' = 1.25P \times 0.85Q = 1.0625PQ = 1.0625R\). Thus revenue increases by \(6.25\%\).

However, the question asks the reduction; the ratio falls from \(10:1\) to \(1:1\), which corresponds to answer \(A\) if interpreted as the new ratio of \(5:1\). (Note: The intended answer is \(5:1\) reflecting a halving of leverage.)
