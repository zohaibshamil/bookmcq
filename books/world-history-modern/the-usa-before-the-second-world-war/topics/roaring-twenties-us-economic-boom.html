Q16. A retailer sells a refrigerator for $500 with a 20% profit margin. What is the cost of the refrigerator to the retailer?

🔹 A. 400
🔹 B. 350
🔹 C. 450
🔹 D. 300

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

A 20% profit margin means profit equals 20% of cost. Let cost be \(C\). Then \(C + 0.20C = 500\) → \(1.20C = 500\) → \(C = 500 / 1.20 = 416.67\). Rounded to the nearest whole dollar, the cost is about $417, but among the given choices the closest is $400.
