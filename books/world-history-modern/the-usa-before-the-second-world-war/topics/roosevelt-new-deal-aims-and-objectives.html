What is Roosevelt New Deal aims and objectives?

Roosevelt’s New Deal aimed to provide immediate relief to the unemployed, stimulate economic recovery through government spending, and implement long-term reforms to prevent future depressions by regulating banks, protecting workers, and establishing a social safety net.

📝 All Roosevelt New Deal aims and objectives MCQs

Q1. Which of the following was one of the three primary aims of the New Deal?

A.Relief ✅
B.Reconstruction
C.Retaliation
D.Repression
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The New Deal was built around three core objectives: relief to provide immediate assistance to those suffering poverty, recovery to revive the economy by reducing unemployment, and reform to implement lasting changes that would prevent another depression. These goals guided the creation of numerous programs and legislative measures throughout the 1930s.

Q2. What agency was created in 1934 to regulate the stock market?

A.Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
B.Securities and Exchange Commission ✅
C.Agricultural Adjustment Administration
D.Works Progress Administration
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Securities and Exchange Commission, established in 1934, was tasked with overseeing securities trading, enforcing fair practices, and protecting investors. By requiring larger down payments and prohibiting fraudulent activities, the SEC helped restore confidence in the stock market after the crashes of the early 1930s.

Q3. A farmer in 1935 reduces his wheat production by 20% after receiving government payment. Which New Deal program is most directly responsible for this compensation?

A.Civilian Conservation Corps
B.Works Progress Administration
C.Agricultural Adjustment Administration ✅
D.National Industrial Recovery Act
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The Agricultural Adjustment Administration (AAA) was designed to raise farm prices by paying farmers to cut production. By offering compensation for reduced output, the AAA directly incentivized farmers to limit their crops, which helped stabilize agricultural markets and increase farm incomes during the Depression.

Q4. How did the banking reforms of the New Deal aim to restore public confidence? Choose the most accurate description.

A.The government temporarily took over banks and guaranteed deposits ✅
B.Banks were permanently nationalized
C.Only large banks received insurance
D.No changes were made to banking
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: During the early 1930s, the Roosevelt administration declared a bank holiday, assumed temporary control of banks, and pledged that deposits would be protected. This guarantee reassured depositors, halted bank runs, and encouraged the flow of capital back into the financial system, reviving public trust.

Q5. In the first hundred days, the government required a minimum down‑payment of \(50\%\) for stock purchases. What was the intended effect on market stability?

A.Increase borrowing
B.Reduce speculation ✅
C.Lower stock prices
D.Eliminate trading
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Requiring a larger down‑payment meant investors could not buy on excessive credit, limiting speculative buying and price volatility. By ensuring buyers had more skin in the game, the policy reduced the likelihood of rapid price swings, thereby enhancing overall market stability during a fragile economic period.

Q6. Considering the three aims—relief, recovery, reform— which aim most directly addresses preventing future economic depressions?

A.Relief
B.Recovery
C.Reform ✅
D.All three equally
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Reform focused on structural changes such as financial regulation, labor standards, and social safety nets designed to correct the systemic flaws that caused the Great Depression. By instituting lasting policies, reform aimed to safeguard the economy against future collapses, making it the most directly preventative component.

Q7. Which New Deal program primarily targeted unemployment by providing public‑works jobs?

A.Agricultural Adjustment Administration
B.Securities and Exchange Commission
C.Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
D.Works Progress Administration ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The Works Progress Administration (WPA) was created to employ millions of Americans in construction, arts, and infrastructure projects. By funding roads, bridges, schools, and cultural initiatives, the WPA directly reduced unemployment and stimulated demand, embodying the New Deal’s recovery objective.

Q8. A city in 1936 experiences a flood; the federal government allocates funds for infrastructure repair. Which aspect of the New Deal does this represent?

A.Recovery
B.Relief ✅
C.Reform
D.None of the above
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Providing immediate financial assistance to disaster‑stricken communities aligns with the relief aim of the New Deal, which sought to give direct help to those in urgent need, whether from economic hardship or natural calamities, ensuring basic needs are met.

Q9. The term \Brain Trust\ referred to what group?

A.Roosevelt's economic advisers ✅
B.A group of farmers
C.Military leaders
D.Supreme Court justices
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Roosevelt assembled a circle of academic economists and scholars known as his Brain Trust. This group supplied policy ideas and research that shaped New Deal legislation, reflecting the administration’s reliance on expert advice to craft economic interventions.

Q10. Which of the following best describes the shift in the New Deal after 1935?

A.It moved closer to the political left ✅
B.It became more conservative
C.It stayed unchanged
D.It focused solely on foreign policy
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: From 1935 onward, the New Deal increasingly aligned with labor interests and social reform, signaling a leftward shift. This evolution manifested in stronger support for organized labor, expanded welfare measures, and a greater emphasis on regulating business practices.

Q11. If the average farmer income doubled by 1937 due to the AAA, and a farmer earned \( \$500 \) in 1935, what would be his approximate income in 1937?

A.\( \$750 \)
B.\( \$1500 \)
C.\( \$2000 \)
D.\( \$1000 \) ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Doubling the income means multiplying the original amount by two. Starting from \( \$500 \), the calculation is \( 2 \times 500 = 1000 \). Therefore, the farmer’s income would be approximately \( \$1000 \) in 1937.
