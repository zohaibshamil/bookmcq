Q17. If the United States had produced the same amount of petroleum as Russia in 1900, what would be the percent decrease from its actual production?

🔹 B. \(30\%\)
🔹 C. \(40\%\)
🔹 D. \(50\%\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

U.S. petroleum was \(9.5\) million metric tons; Russia’s was \(11.5\) million. To equal Russia, the U.S. would need to increase, not decrease, so the question is flawed; the correct interpretation is a percent increase of \((11.5-9.5)/9.5 ≈ 21\%\). Since none match, the intended answer is \(20\%\) (closest), option A. However, given the options, the best fit is A.
