What is Roosevelt New Deal achievements?

The New Deal achieved significant milestones including reduced unemployment, restored banking stability, created lasting social programs like Social Security, empowered labor unions, and established the precedent that the federal government has a responsibility to ensure economic security for its citizens.

📝 All Roosevelt New Deal achievements MCQs

Q1. Which program created jobs through public works projects like dams and roads?

A. Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC)
B. Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA)
C. Works Progress Administration (WPA) ✅
D. Social Security Act (SSA)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The Works Progress Administration, launched in 1935, was designed to employ millions of job‑seekers on public works such as roads, bridges, schools, and dams, directly addressing unemployment while building lasting infrastructure that benefited communities across the United States.

Q2. What year was the Social Security Act passed?

A. 1933
B. 1935 ✅
C. 1937
D. 1939

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Social Security Act was enacted in 1935, establishing a federal system of old‑age benefits, unemployment insurance, and aid to dependent children, marking a significant step toward a national welfare state and providing a safety net for millions of Americans during the Depression.

Q3. Which act helped farmers but displaced many farm laborers, illustrating mixed results?

A. Farmers' Relief Act ✅
B. Soil Conservation Service
C. Agricultural Adjustment Act
D. Rural Electrification Administration

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Farmers' Relief Act aimed to stabilize agricultural prices and provide assistance to farm owners, yet its implementation reduced demand for seasonal labor, leading many farm workers to lose jobs—a clear example of how New Deal policies sometimes produced unintended negative consequences for vulnerable groups.

Q4. Which Supreme Court decision initially limited New Deal spending by striking down a key program?

A. Schechter v. United States
B. West Coast Hotel v. Parrish
C. United States v. Butler ✅
D. Brown v. Board of Education

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

In United States v. Butler (1936), the Court ruled that the Agricultural Adjustment Act overstepped federal authority, signaling judicial resistance to New Deal initiatives and compelling the Roosevelt administration to modify or abandon certain programs until later rulings upheld more expansive federal powers.

Q5. If unemployment fell from 12 million to 8 million by 1937, what was the percentage decrease?

A. 0.2
B. 0.25
C. 0.33 ✅
D. 0.4

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The percentage reduction equals (12 – 8) ÷ 12 = 4 ÷ 12 ≈ 0.333, or about 33 %. This calculation shows the New Deal’s partial success in lowering joblessness, though millions remained unemployed, highlighting both progress and the limits of the era’s economic interventions.

Q6. Which agency was responsible for flood control and electricity generation in the Tennessee Valley?

A. Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) ✅
B. Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC)
C. Works Progress Administration (WPA)
D. Public Works Administration (PWA)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Tennessee Valley Authority was created in 1933 to manage the Tennessee River watershed, providing flood control, navigation, and hydroelectric power, thereby stimulating regional development and delivering essential services that continue to benefit the area to this day.

Q7. Roosevelt reduced government spending in 1938, causing unemployment to rise. Which economic concept does this illustrate?

A. Multiplier effect
B. Crowding out
C. Fiscal contraction ✅
D. Supply‑side stimulus

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Fiscal contraction refers to a deliberate decrease in government expenditure, which can lower aggregate demand and raise unemployment. Roosevelt’s 1938 spending cuts exemplify this concept, as the reduction in public investment led to a resurgence of joblessness during an already fragile economic recovery.

Q8. How many years did Roosevelt serve before the United States entered World War II?

A. 8 ✅
B. 9
C. 10
D. 12

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Franklin D. Roosevelt was inaugurated in March 1933, and the United States entered World War II after the Pearl Harbor attack in December 1941. The interval between those dates is eight full years, during which the New Deal policies reshaped American economic and social life.

Q9. Which statement best reflects the New Deal’s impact on American political culture?

A. It eliminated all state governments
B. It created a one‑party system
C. It introduced a socialist economy
D. It expanded federal involvement while preserving democratic institutions ✅

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The New Deal markedly increased the federal government’s role in economic regulation and social welfare, yet it maintained democratic processes and private enterprise. This balance helped forge a political culture that accepts government intervention without abandoning the core values of American democracy.

Q10. If the New Deal created 5 million jobs and unemployment was 12 million before, what proportion of the unemployed were employed by New Deal programs?

A. 0.33
B. 0.42 ✅
C. 0.5
D. 0.6

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Dividing the 5 million jobs by the initial 12 million unemployed yields 5 ÷ 12 ≈ 0.417, or roughly 42 %. This proportion indicates that New Deal employment initiatives directly absorbed a substantial share of the jobless population, though many remained without work.

Q11. Which act established a national retirement system?

A. Social Security Act ✅
B. Wagner Act
C. Fair Labor Standards Act
D. Glass‑Steagall Act

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Social Security Act of 1935 created a federal old‑age pension system and laid the groundwork for unemployment insurance and aid to families, representing a cornerstone of the modern American welfare state and providing long‑term financial security for retirees.

Q12. The New Deal increased federal authority over states. Which constitutional principle was most affected?

A. Federalism ✅
B. Separation of powers
C. Checks and balances
D. Judicial review

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Federalism defines the division of power between national and state governments. By expanding federal programs and regulatory reach, the New Deal shifted the balance toward a stronger central government, thereby reshaping the traditional federalist relationship while still operating within the Constitution’s framework.

Q13. What core difference distinguishes the New Deal from fascist Italy’s response to economic crisis?

A. Both relied on state control of industry
B. New Deal emphasized democratic institutions while fascist Italy suppressed them ✅
C. Both avoided any government intervention
D. New Deal focused on militarization

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The New Deal pursued recovery through democratic processes, public works, and social safety nets, whereas fascist Italy under Mussolini employed authoritarian control, suppressing political freedoms and emphasizing nationalist militarism, highlighting a fundamental divergence in how each regime addressed economic hardship.

Q14. What was the primary goal of the National Industrial Recovery Act (NIRA)?

A. To regulate wages and hours ✅
B. To provide unemployment insurance
C. To create public works projects
D. To fund agricultural subsidies

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The NIRA sought to stimulate industrial recovery by establishing codes that set minimum wages, maximum working hours, and fair competition standards, thereby attempting to stabilize employment and improve labor conditions during the Depression.

Q15. Which program was designed to provide temporary financial assistance to the unemployed?

A. Works Progress Administration (WPA)
B. Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC)
C. National Youth Administration (NYA)
D. Federal Emergency Relief Administration (FERA) ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The Federal Emergency Relief Administration, created in 1933, delivered direct cash assistance to individuals and families facing unemployment, offering immediate relief while longer‑term job programs like the WPA were being developed.

Q16. Assuming the New Deal reduced unemployment from 12 million to 8 million by 1937 and the war effort later reduced it to 1 million by 1943, what is the total percentage reduction from 1933 to 1943?

A. 0.8
B. 0.85
C. 0.9
D. 0.92 ✅

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The drop from 12 million to 1 million equals a reduction of 11 million, which is 11 ÷ 12 ≈ 0.917, or about 92 %. This figure illustrates the combined impact of New Deal policies and wartime mobilization in dramatically lowering joblessness over a decade.

Q17. During the Dust Bowl, which New Deal agency helped with soil conservation?

A. Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC)
B. Soil Conservation Service (SCS) ✅
C. Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA)
D. Works Progress Administration (WPA)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Soil Conservation Service, established in 1935, promoted practices such as contour plowing and tree planting to reduce erosion and restore fertility, directly addressing the environmental devastation that crippled agriculture across the Great Plains during the Dust Bowl.

Q18. What year did the Supreme Court begin to uphold New Deal legislation after Roosevelt’s court‑packing plan?

A. 1937 ✅
B. 1938
C. 1939
D. 1940

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

In 1937, the Supreme Court upheld New Deal measures in cases such as West Coast Hotel v. Parrish, signaling a shift toward greater acceptance of federal economic regulation after the political pressure surrounding Roosevelt’s court‑packing proposal.

D. Free enterprise and socialism

📖 Explanation:

📖 Explanation:

Q25. How did the New Deal’s collective bargaining policies affect labor‑management relations?

B. They eliminated collective bargaining

📖 Explanation:

By recognizing workers’ rights to organize and negotiate, New Deal legislation such as the Wagner Act institutionalized collective bargaining, fostering more balanced labor‑management relations and giving unions a legal foundation that persisted long after the 1930s.

📖 Explanation:

The Rural Electrification Administration, established in 1935, provided low‑interest loans and technical assistance to bring electric power to isolated farms and small towns, dramatically improving living standards and economic productivity in rural areas.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Two million of the four‑million reduction represents half of the total decline, or 50 %, indicating that federal job creation accounted for a substantial portion of the overall improvement in employment levels.
