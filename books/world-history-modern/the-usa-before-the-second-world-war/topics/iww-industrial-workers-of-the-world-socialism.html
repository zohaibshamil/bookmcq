What is IWW Industrial Workers of the World socialism?

The Industrial Workers of the World (IWW) was a radical socialist union founded in 1905 that advocated for worker solidarity across industries, direct action, and the abolition of capitalism, promoting revolutionary change rather than incremental reform within the existing system.
