Q1. In which year were ten members of the Molly Maguires hanged after a miners’ strike in Pennsylvania?

🔹 B. \(1876\)

📖 Explanation:

The miners’ strike occurred in \(1876\) and the ten leaders, members of the Irish secret society known as the Molly Maguires, were executed that same year for alleged violence.

Q3. Which federal force was ultimately called in to end the 1877 railway strikes?
