What is Ku Klux Klan and Jim Crow laws history?

The Ku Klux Klan used terror and intimidation to enforce white supremacy, while Jim Crow laws institutionalized racial segregation and denied Black citizens voting rights and equal treatment, creating a rigid caste system in the American South until the mid-twentieth century.

Q1. When was the original Ku Klux Klan founded?

A. December 24, 1865 ✅
B. April 12, 1861
C. July 4, 1867
D. November 11, 1868

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Klan was created as a secret society on Christmas Eve 1865 in Tennessee, shortly after the Civil War ended. Its formation was a direct reaction to Reconstruction policies and aimed to restore white dominance through intimidation and violence.

Q2. What does the Greek word \kuklos\ (from which \Ku Klux\ is derived) mean?

A. Circle ✅
B. Sword
C. Shield
D. Bow

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The term \kuklos\ translates to \circle\ or \drinking bowl\ in Greek, reflecting the secretive, closed nature of the organization. The founders chose the name to emphasize the group's clandestine meetings and the sense of unity among its members.

Q3. Which president’s administration saw the initial passage of Jim Crow laws after 1877?

B. Ulysses S. Grant
C. James A. Garfield
D. Benjamin Harrison

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Rutherford B. Hayes took office in 1877, and his administration coincided with the end of Reconstruction. Southern state legislatures quickly enacted Jim Crow statutes to restrict African American rights, marking the start of the segregation era.

Q4. Which of the following tactics was most commonly used by the KKK to intimidate African Americans?

B. Public debates in newspapers
C. Legal lawsuits
D. Peaceful protests

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Klan conducted night raids, dressing in white hoods and masks, burning crosses and committing acts such as lynchings, beatings, and tarring. These violent, covert actions spread fear and discouraged blacks from asserting their civil rights.

Q10. Which constitutional amendment originally granted African American men the right to vote?
