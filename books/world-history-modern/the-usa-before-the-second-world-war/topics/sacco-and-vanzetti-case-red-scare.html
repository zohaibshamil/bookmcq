What is Sacco and Vanzetti case Red Scare?

The Sacco and Vanzetti case exemplified Red Scare hysteria when two Italian immigrant anarchists were controversially convicted and executed for murder in 1927, widely seen as a miscarriage of justice driven by anti-immigrant sentiment and fear of radical politics rather than solid evidence.
