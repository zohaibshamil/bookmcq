About 9. The United Nations Organization

The United Nations was founded in 1945 to replace the failed League of Nations and provide a more effective platform for maintaining international peace, security, and human rights. Unlike its predecessor, the UN included all major powers and established the Security Council with permanent members holding veto power, aiming to balance idealistic goals with political realism.

While often hamstrung by Cold War vetoes and national sovereignty concerns, the UN has played vital roles in peacekeeping, humanitarian aid, decolonization, and setting international legal standards. It remains the central forum for global diplomacy, even as its effectiveness continues to be debated and reformed in response to changing world dynamics.
