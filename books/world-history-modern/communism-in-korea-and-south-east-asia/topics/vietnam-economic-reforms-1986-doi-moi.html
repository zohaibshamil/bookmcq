What is Vietnam economic reforms 1986 Doi Moi?

Facing economic collapse, Vietnam launched the Doi Moi reforms in 1986 to transition from a centrally planned economy to a socialist-oriented market system, which opened the country to foreign investment, boosted agricultural production, and dramatically improved living standards while maintaining one-party communist rule.
