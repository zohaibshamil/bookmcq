What is Cambodia after Pol Pot Sihanouk return?

After Vietnamese forces toppled the Khmer Rouge in 1979, Cambodia endured years of continued civil war before Prince Sihanouk returned to lead a fragile peace process in the 1990s, restoring the monarchy and facilitating UN-supervised elections to end decades of violence.
