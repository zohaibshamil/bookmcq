What is North Korea US nuclear confrontation?

Tensions between North Korea and the United States escalated as Pyongyang developed nuclear weapons and ballistic missiles to guarantee regime survival against perceived American aggression, resulting in decades of failed diplomacy, international sanctions, and recurring threats of military conflict.
