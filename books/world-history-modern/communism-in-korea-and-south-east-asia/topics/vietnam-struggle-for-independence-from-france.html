What is Vietnam struggle for independence from France?

Led by Ho Chi Minh and the Viet Minh, Vietnamese nationalists fought a prolonged war against French colonial rule that culminated in the decisive 1954 Battle of Dien Bien Phu, ending centuries of foreign domination but setting the stage for future conflict over the country's political direction.

Q1. When did Ho Chi Minh proclaim the Democratic Republic of Vietnam?

A. April 30, \(1945\)
B. September 2, \(1945\)
C. September 2, \(1945\) ✅
D. August 15, \(1945\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The proclamation was made on September 2, \(1945\), after the Vietminh seized Hanoi and other major towns following Japan’s surrender. This event marked the formal declaration of Vietnamese independence, even though the Allies had already arranged for French and British administration of the region.

Q2. Which colonial power administered Indochina before World War II?

A. Britain
B. France ✅
C. Netherlands
D. Portugal

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

France created the Indochinese Union in \(1887\) and administered Vietnam, Laos, and Cambodia as a French colony. Although the French built infrastructure such as roads, railways, and schools, they provided little industrial development, leaving most of the population as impoverished peasants.

Q3. What was the primary ideological influence on Ho Chi Minh after his travels abroad?

A. Pan‑Asianism
B. Buddhism
C. Communism ✅
D. Nationalism

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Ho Chi Minh’s experiences in France, China, and the USSR exposed him to Marxist‑Leninist ideas, leading him to adopt communism while retaining his nationalist goal of an independent, united Vietnam. This dual commitment shaped the Vietminh’s political program.

Q4. Which city housed the university established by the French in northern Vietnam?

A. Saigon
B. Hue
C. Da Nang
D. Hanoi ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The French founded a university in Hanoi, the capital of northern Vietnam, as part of their efforts to develop a limited elite class. This institution contributed to the emergence of educated Vietnamese leaders who later participated in anti‑colonial movements.

Q5. In which decade did protest movements first emerge in Vietnam against French rule?

A. 1910s
B. 1920s
C. 1930s ✅
D. 1940s

📖 Explanation:

During the 1930s, Vietnamese peasants and intellectuals organized protests and strikes against the exploitative French colonial system. These movements were soon suppressed, but they laid the groundwork for later revolutionary activity, including the formation of the Vietminh.
