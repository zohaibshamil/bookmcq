What is Cambodia Khmer Rouge regime 1975-1979?

The Khmer Rouge seized power in 1975 and implemented a radical agrarian utopia that resulted in the genocide of nearly two million Cambodians through forced labor, mass executions, starvation, and the systematic destruction of education, religion, and urban life.

Q1. In which year did the Khmer Rouge capture Phnom Penh and declare Democratic Kampuchea?

A. 1975 ✅
B. 1976
C. 1977
D. 1978

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Khmer Rouge seized Phnom Penh on 17 April 1975, after which they proclaimed the Democratic Kampuchea regime. This marked the beginning of a four‑year period of extreme social engineering, mass killings, and forced relocations that devastated the Cambodian population.
