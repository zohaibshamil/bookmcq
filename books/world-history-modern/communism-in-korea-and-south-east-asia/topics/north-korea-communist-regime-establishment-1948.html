What is North Korea communist regime establishment 1948?

In 1948, the Democratic People's Republic of Korea was officially founded under Kim Il-sung with Soviet backing, creating a totalitarian communist state in the northern half of the Korean peninsula that rejected democratic elections and solidified the permanent division of Korea.
