🔹 C. \(1977\)

📖 Explanation:

The proclamation took place in July \(1976\), marking the formal unification of North and South Vietnam under a single socialist state, and establishing Hanoi as the capital of the new nation. This event ended decades of conflict and set the stage for the challenges that followed.

📖 Explanation:

Hanoi was designated the capital of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam after unification in July \(1976\). The choice reflected the political dominance of the northern government and symbolized the shift of power from the former South Vietnamese administration in Saigon.
