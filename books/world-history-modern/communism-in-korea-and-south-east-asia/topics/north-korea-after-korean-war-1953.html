Q1. What year did Kim Il-sung die, ending his 40-year rule?

🔹 A. 1990
🔹 B. 1991
🔹 C. 1994
🔹 D. 1995

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Kim Il-sung passed away in 1994, marking the end of his four‑decade leadership that began after the Korean War. His death led to a succession that continued the regime’s policies, but also introduced subtle shifts in domestic and foreign strategies.

Q2. Which two communist allies provided aid to North Korea after the war?

🔹 A. USSR and China
🔹 B. USSR and Vietnam
🔹 C. China and Cuba
🔹 D. USSR and Japan

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Soviet Union and the People’s Republic of China were the primary sources of economic and technical assistance to North Korea in the first decade after the war, enabling rapid industrial expansion despite the regime’s rhetoric of self‑sufficiency.

Q3. What term describes Kim's policy of self‑reliance in defence?

🔹 A. Juche
🔹 B. Songun
🔹 C. Self‑reliance in defence
🔹 D. Marxism‑Leninism

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The fourth theme of the mid‑1960s self‑sufficiency doctrine specifically emphasized “self‑reliance in defence,” reflecting Kim’s belief that a strong, autonomous military was essential for preserving sovereignty without reliance on external powers.

Q4. If North Korea allocated \(15\%\) of its GDP to military spending in the 1960s, and its GDP was \(100\) billion won, how much was spent on the military?

🔹 A. \(10\) billion
🔹 B. \(15\) billion
🔹 C. \(20\) billion
🔹 D. \(25\) billion

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

\(15\%\) of \(100\) billion won equals \(0.15 \times 100 = 15\) billion won. This allocation illustrates the regime’s prioritization of defence spending, consistent with the self‑reliance principle and the extensive militarization pursued after the war.

Q6. Why did Kim’s regime, despite seeking self‑sufficiency, still rely heavily on aid from USSR and China?

The reliance on Soviet and Chinese aid stemmed largely from inefficient central planning, which hampered domestic production and forced the regime to import essential inputs. Although ideology promoted autonomy, practical constraints and mismanagement made external assistance indispensable for economic growth.

Q7. What were the four themes of self‑sufficiency defined in the mid‑1960s?

📖 Explanation:

The official doctrine articulated four pillars: ideological autonomy, political independence, economic self‑sufficiency, and defence self‑reliance. This framework guided domestic policies and justified the regime’s isolationist stance while attempting to project an image of comprehensive national strength.

Q18. If North Korea's industrial output grew by \(8\%\) annually for the first ten years after the war, what factor approximates the total growth after a decade? (Use compound growth)
