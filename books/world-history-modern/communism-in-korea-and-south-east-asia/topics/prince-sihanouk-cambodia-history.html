📝 All Prince Sihanouk Cambodia history MCQs

Q1. In which year did Prince Sihanouk first become king of Cambodia?

🔹 A. \(1941\)
🔹 B. \(1945\)
🔹 C. \(1955\)
🔹 D. \(1960\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Prince Sihanouk ascended to the throne in \(1941\) after King Monivong's death, becoming the youngest monarch in Cambodian history. His early reign was constrained by French colonial authority, providing him with limited power but positioning him for future political strategies toward full independence.

Q2. Which foreign power occupied Cambodia from 1941 to 1945?

🔹 A. France
🔹 B. Japan
🔹 C. United States
🔹 D. Thailand

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

During World War II, Japan seized control of Cambodia, replacing the French colonial administration from 1941 until Japan's surrender in \(1945\). This occupation disrupted the existing protectorate arrangement, creating an environment where Cambodian leaders, including Sihanouk, could contemplate assertions of sovereignty.

Q3. Who succeeded Prince Sihanouk as king after his abdication?

🔹 A. Norodom Monivong
🔹 B. Norodom Ranariddh
🔹 C. Norodom Sihamoni
🔹 D. Norodom Suramarit

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

After Prince Sihanouk abdicated in 1955 to engage directly in politics, his father, Norodom Suramarit, assumed the throne. Suramarit's reign provided a constitutional framework that allowed Sihanouk to operate as a civilian leader while retaining the royal family's symbolic influence over the nation.

Q4. In what year did Prince Sihanouk abdicate to pursue a political career?

🔹 A. \(1955\)
🔹 B. \(1960\)
🔹 C. \(1953\)
🔹 D. \(1949\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Prince Sihanouk abdicated the throne in \(1955\) to enter the political arena as a private citizen. This strategic move enabled him to found the People’s Socialist Community and contest elections, ultimately securing a landslide victory that solidified his dominance over Cambodian political life.

Q5. Which political party did Sihanouk establish before the 1955 elections?

🔹 A. People's Socialist Community
🔹 B. Khmer Rouge
🔹 C. Democratic Party
🔹 D. National United Front

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Sihanouk founded the People’s Socialist Community, a nationalist party that appealed to a broad cross‑section of society. The party’s platform emphasized peace, independence, and social development, allowing it to dominate the 1955 electoral contest and capture every seat in the National Assembly.

Q6. What major international agreement recognized Cambodia’s independence in 1954?

The 1954 Geneva Conference concluded the First Indochina War and formally recognized Cambodia as an independent nation. This diplomatic milestone ended formal French colonial rule, granting Sihanouk’s government legitimacy on the world stage and enabling the country to pursue its own foreign‑policy objectives.
