What is Cambodia civil war 1970-1975 Sihanouk overthrow?

The 1970 coup that ousted Prince Sihanouk plunged Cambodia into civil war as the deposed leader allied with the communist Khmer Rouge against the U.S.-backed Lon Nol government, drawing the country deeper into the Vietnam War and causing massive civilian suffering and displacement.

Q1. In what year did Prince Sihanouk first proclaim Cambodia’s independence under Japanese occupation?

A. 1941
B. 1943
C. 1945 ✅
D. 1947

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The Japanese occupation began in 1941, but as their defeat became inevitable in March 1945, Sihanouk declared Cambodia independent. This brief assertion of sovereignty was quickly reversed when French forces returned after World War II, restoring the pre‑war colonial arrangement.

Q2. Which political party did Prince Sihanouk found after returning from abdication?

A. People's Socialist Community ✅
B. Khmer National Party
C. Democratic Kampuchea
D. Royalist Front

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Sihanouk created the People’s Socialist Community (PSC) to contest the 1955 elections. By forming this party, he avoided traditional party structures and positioned himself as a unifying figure, leading to a landslide victory that secured his political dominance while maintaining his royal prestige.

Q3. What was the primary reason the intelligentsia resented Sihanouk’s rule?

B. Growing authoritarianism ✅
C. Foreign policy failures
D. Military incompetence

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Although Sihanouk was celebrated for achieving independence, many intellectuals grew uneasy with his concentration of power, perceiving his governance as increasingly authoritarian. This tension contributed to the rise of opposition groups, including pro‑democracy factions and the nascent communist movement.
