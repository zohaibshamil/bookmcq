What is Life in North Korea under Kim Jong-il?

During Kim Jong-il's rule, citizens endured severe famine, economic collapse, and extreme political repression as the regime prioritized military spending over public welfare, forcing people to rely on illegal black markets for survival while facing constant state surveillance and propaganda.

Q1. In what year did Kim Jong-il become the official successor after Kim Il-sung's death?

A.1994 ✅
B.1995
C.1993
D.1992
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Kim Il-sung announced his intention for his son to succeed him, and after his death in 1994 Kim Jong‑il assumed the role of successor. This transition marked the formal handover of power, consolidating Kim Jong‑il's position as the new leader of North Korea.

Q2. Which organization agreed to provide two light‑water reactors to North Korea?

A.IAEA
B.KEDO ✅
C.UNDP
D.WHO
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Korean Peninsula Energy Development Organization (KEDO) was formed by the United States, South Korea, and Japan to supply North Korea with two light‑water nuclear reactors. This arrangement was part of the 1994 agreement that aimed to replace the plutonium‑producing reactors with safer, civilian‑use facilities.

Q4. What was the per‑capita Gross National Product (GNP) in 2000?

D.457 ✅

This steep decline reflects the combined effects of economic mismanagement, loss of Soviet support, and the continued allocation of resources to the nuclear weapons program despite widespread poverty.
