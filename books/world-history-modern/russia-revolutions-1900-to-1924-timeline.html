About 16. Russia revolutions 1900 to 1924 timeline

Between 1900 and 1924, Russia underwent a series of upheavals starting with the failed 1905 revolution against Tsarist autocracy, followed by the February 1917 revolution that ended imperial rule and established a provisional government. Widespread discontent over war, poverty, and political exclusion created revolutionary conditions that moderate reformers could not satisfy.

The Bolshevik seizure of power in October 1917 initiated a radical transformation through civil war, war communism, and the eventual establishment of the USSR by 1924. Lenin’s regime consolidated control through repression and ideological commitment, replacing tsarist autocracy with a communist party-state that would shape global history for decades.
