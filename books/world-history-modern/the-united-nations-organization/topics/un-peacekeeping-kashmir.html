What is UN peacekeeping Kashmir?

UNMOGIP has monitored the ceasefire line in Jammu and Kashmir since 1949 following the first Indo-Pakistani war, observing violations and reporting to the Security Council, though both India and Pakistan have disputed its mandate over time.
