📝 All UN General Assembly functions and powers MCQs

Q1. How many representatives may each member nation send to the General Assembly?

🔹 A. Three
🔹 B. Four
🔹 C. Five
🔹 D. Six

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Each UN member state is permitted to send up to five representatives, but regardless of the number of delegates, each nation retains a single vote in the General Assembly, ensuring equal sovereign influence.

Q2. In which month does the regular session of the General Assembly begin each year?

🔹 A. January
🔹 B. April
🔹 C. September
🔹 D. December

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The General Assembly convenes its annual session each September, marking the start of a three‑month period during which member states discuss global issues and adopt resolutions.

Q3. How many official languages are used for translation of speeches and debates in the General Assembly?

🔹 A. 5
🔹 B. 6
🔹 C. 7
🔹 D. 8

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The United Nations recognizes six official languages—English, French, Russian, Chinese, Spanish, and Arabic—ensuring that all delegates can understand and participate regardless of linguistic background.

Q4. What determines the voting power of each member nation in the General Assembly?

🔹 A. One vote per representative
🔹 B. One vote per nation
🔹 C. Two votes per nation
🔹 D. No voting

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

In the General Assembly, each member state holds exactly one vote, independent of the number of representatives it sends, reflecting the principle of sovereign equality among nations.

Q5. Who may call a special session of the General Assembly?

🔹 A. Only the Security Council
🔹 B. Only member nations
🔹 C. Both member nations and the Security Council
🔹 D. Only the Secretary‑General

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Special sessions can be convened either by a request from member states or by a decision of the Security Council when urgent matters require immediate attention.

Q6. Which of the following decisions requires a two‑thirds majority in the General Assembly?

🔹 A. Admitting new members
🔹 B. Discussing the UN budget
🔹 C. Electing non‑permanent Security Council members
🔹 D. Scheduling the meeting dates

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Admission of new members, as well as expulsion and major peace‑maintaining actions, demand a super‑majority of two‑thirds of the votes cast, reflecting the high stakes of such decisions.

Q7. If the General Assembly has 190 member states, how many votes are needed for a two‑thirds majority?

🔹 A. 126
🔹 B. 127
🔹 C. 128
🔹 D. 129

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A two‑thirds majority of 190 members equals \( \frac{2}{3}\times190 = 126.\overline{6} \); therefore at least 127 votes are required to meet the super‑majority threshold.

Q8. A member nation proposes expelling a member for violating peace. What majority is required?

🔹 A. Simple majority
🔹 B. Unanimous
🔹 C. Three‑fourths majority
🔹 D. Two‑thirds majority

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Expulsion of a member is a serious measure, and the UN Charter stipulates that such actions need a two‑thirds majority of the votes cast in the General Assembly.

Q9. If the total UN budget is $100 million and each of the 190 members pays an equal share, how much does each pay?

🔹 A. 500000
🔹 B. 525000
🔹 C. 600000
🔹 D. 1000000

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Dividing $100 million by 190 yields approximately $526,315.79; the closest listed amount is $525,000, which represents the equal contribution each member would make.

Q10. During a crisis, the Security Council calls a special session. What is true about the session's duration?

🔹 A. It lasts exactly three months
🔹 B. It can be extended indefinitely
🔹 C. Its length is determined by the issue at hand and is not fixed
🔹 D. It follows the same three‑month schedule as the regular session

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Special sessions are convened to address urgent matters; their length is flexible and decided based on the specific crisis rather than adhering to a preset calendar.

Q11. A resolution on maintaining peace receives 120 votes in favor, 30 against, and 40 abstentions out of 190 members. Does it pass?

🔹 A. Yes, because it meets a simple majority
🔹 B. Yes, because it meets two‑thirds of votes cast
🔹 C. No, because abstentions count toward the total
🔹 D. No, because it is not unanimous

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Only votes cast (120 + 30 = 150) are counted for the two‑thirds test; \( \frac{2}{3}\times150 = 100 \). Since 120 > 100, the resolution satisfies the super‑majority requirement and passes.

Q12. A member wants to increase its UN budget contribution by 10%. If its current share is $525,000, what will be the new contribution?

🔹 A. 525000
🔹 B. 550000
🔹 C. 577500
🔹 D. 600000

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

A 10 % increase on $525,000 yields $525,000 × 1.10 = $577,500, representing the new financial commitment of that member state.

Q13. To elect a non‑permanent Security Council member, what is the minimum number of votes a candidate must receive if 190 members vote?

🔹 A. 100
🔹 B. 120
🔹 C. 127
🔹 D. 150

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

A two‑thirds majority of 190 members equals \( \frac{2}{3}\times190 = 126.\overline{6} \); thus a candidate needs at least 127 votes to be elected.

Q20. If a resolution on the budget gets 80 votes for, 30 against, and 80 abstentions, does it pass?

🔹 A. Yes, simple majority
🔹 B. Yes, two‑thirds majority
🔹 C. No, because abstentions count
🔹 D. No, because not two‑thirds

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Only votes cast (80 + 30 = 110) are considered; a simple majority requires more than 55 votes. Since 80 > 55, the resolution passes.

Q23. If the General Assembly has 193 member states and each may send up to five representatives, what is the maximum possible number of representatives?

🔹 A. 850

🔹 D. 1050

Q24. During a vote, 150 members are present, 10 are absent, and 33 abstain. How many votes are needed for a two‑thirds majority?

🔹 A. 100
🔹 B. 101
🔹 C. 110
🔹 D. 120

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Only votes cast count for the super‑majority. With 150 present and 33 abstentions, 117 votes are cast; two‑thirds of 150 equals 100, so at least 100 votes are required.

Q25. If a resolution requires a simple majority and receives 95 votes in favor, 94 against, and 1 abstention, does it pass?

🔹 A. Yes
🔹 B. No
🔹 C. Depends on abstention
🔹 D. Cannot determine

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Excluding the abstention, 95 + 94 = 189 votes are cast. A simple majority needs more than half, i.e., at least 95 votes, which is met, so the resolution passes.

Q28. During a vote on expelling a member, 180 members are present, 5 abstain, and 5 are absent. How many votes are needed for a two‑thirds majority?

🔹 A. 115
🔹 B. 116
🔹 C. 117
🔹 D. 118

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Votes cast equal 180 − 5 = 175. Two‑thirds of 175 is \( \frac{2}{3}\times175 = 116.\overline{6} \); therefore at least 117 affirmative votes are required to achieve the super‑majority.

Q29. The GA's budget contributions are based on a scale of assessment. If a country's gross national income is \( \$2 \) trillion and its assessed contribution rate is 0.027 %, what is its contribution?

🔹 A. 540000
🔹 B. $5.4 million
🔹 C. $540 million
🔹 D. $5.4 billion

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Multiplying \( 2\times10^{12} \) by 0.00027 (0.027 %) yields \( 5.4\times10^{8} \), i.e., $540 million, which is the amount the country would contribute under the assessment scale.

Q30. A proposal to admit a new member receives 140 votes in favor, 30 against, and 20 abstentions. Does it meet the two‑thirds requirement?

🔹 A. Yes, passes
🔹 B. No, fails
🔹 C. Passes only if unanimous
🔹 D. Cannot be determined

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Votes cast equal 140 + 30 = 170. Two‑thirds of 170 is 113.33, so at least 114 votes are needed. With 140 affirmative votes, the proposal exceeds the threshold and is adopted.

Q31. If the GA decides to increase the number of non‑permanent Security Council seats from 10 to 12, what amendment process is required?

🔹 A. Simple majority of the GA
🔹 B. Two‑thirds majority of the GA
🔹 C. Unanimous consent of all members
🔹 D. Approval by the Security Council

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Amending the composition of the Security Council is a substantive change to the Charter, which necessitates a two‑thirds majority vote in the General Assembly followed by ratification by member states.
