What is UNESCO United Nations Educational Scientific Cultural Organization?

UNESCO promotes international collaboration in education, science, culture, and communication to build peace, eradicate poverty, and foster sustainable development through protecting heritage, advancing literacy, and supporting scientific cooperation.
