What is ECOSOC Economic and Social Council UN?

The Economic and Social Council coordinates the UN’s economic, social, humanitarian, and cultural activities, serving as a central forum for discussing sustainable development and overseeing specialized agencies and functional commissions.

Q1. How many members does ECOSOC have?

A. 272727 members ✅
B. 303030 members
C. 242424 members
D. 212121 members

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

ECOSOC is composed of twenty‑seven member states, a number fixed by the UN Charter. This relatively small size allows for manageable deliberations while still representing a broad geographic cross‑section of the international community, facilitating diverse input on economic and social issues.

Q2. Who elects the members of ECOSOC?

A. The Security Council
B. The General Assembly ✅
C. The Secretary‑General
D. The Economic Commission for Africa

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The General Assembly, consisting of all UN member states, conducts the election of ECOSOC members. This democratic process ensures that the council reflects the collective will of the international community, providing legitimacy and broad-based support for its economic and social initiatives.

Q3. What fraction of ECOSOC members retire each year?

A. One‑half
B. One‑quarter
C. One‑third ✅
D. One‑fifth

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

ECOSOC operates on a staggered renewal system where one‑third of its twenty‑seven members complete their terms annually. This rotation promotes continuity while allowing regular infusion of fresh perspectives, balancing institutional memory with evolving global priorities.

Q4. How many regional commissions does ECOSOC have?

A. Three
B. Five
C. Six
D. Four ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

ECOSOC has established four regional commissions—Europe, Latin America, Africa, and Asia and the Far East—to decentralize its work and address region‑specific challenges. These bodies enable more focused coordination of development programmes and facilitate dialogue among member states within each geographic area.

Q5. Which specialized agency is primarily concerned with labour standards?

📖 Explanation:

The International Labour Organization (ILO) is the UN‑affiliated agency dedicated to promoting decent work and establishing international labour standards. It works closely with ECOSOC to integrate employment policies into broader socioeconomic strategies, ensuring that labour rights are part of the development agenda.
