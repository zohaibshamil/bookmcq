What is UN agency resource wastage?

UN agencies face criticism for resource wastage due to bureaucratic inefficiencies, duplication across entities, high administrative costs, and poor accountability, reducing effectiveness despite substantial funding and undermining donor confidence in multilateral system.

Q1. Which two agencies are most frequently criticized for overlapping their mandates in international health and agriculture?

A.World Health Organization (WHO) and Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) ✅
B.International Monetary Fund (IMF) and World Bank
C.UNESCO and UNICEF
D.World Trade Organization (WTO) and United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Both the WHO and FAO operate in sectors where health and agriculture intersect, leading to duplicated programs and reporting structures. Critics argue that this overlap creates inefficiencies, diverting resources that could be better allocated to distinct, complementary initiatives. Recognizing and addressing these redundancies can improve overall effectiveness.
