📝 All UNICEF United Nations Children's Fund MCQs

Q1. In which year was UNICEF established as a part of the United Nations?

🔹 A. 1945
🔹 B. 1946
🔹 C. 1947
🔹 D. 1948

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

UNICEF was created in the aftermath of World II to address the urgent needs of children worldwide, and it formally began operations in \(1946\) as the United Nations International Children’s Emergency Fund, later shortened to UNICEF.

Q2. A low‑income country reports a rise in child malnutrition. Which UNICEF intervention would most directly address immediate nutritional needs?

🔹 A. Long‑term school feeding programs
🔹 B. Emergency therapeutic feeding kits
🔹 C. Construction of new hospitals
🔹 D. Policy advocacy for agricultural subsidies

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Emergency therapeutic feeding kits are designed to provide rapid, life‑saving nutrition to severely malnourished children, making them the most direct response to an acute rise in malnutrition, whereas the other options target longer‑term or indirect solutions.

Q3. What is UNICEF's primary source of funding?

🔹 A. Member state contributions
🔹 B. Private donations
🔹 C. UN regular budget
🔹 D. Revenue from commercial activities

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Member state contributions form the backbone of UNICEF’s budget, providing predictable and sizable resources that enable the agency to plan and implement large‑scale programmes, while private donations and other streams supplement but do not dominate the overall financial structure.

Q4. Evaluate the impact of UNICEF's Conditional Cash Transfer (CCT) programs on school attendance in rural Kenya. Which statement best reflects findings from the 2021 evaluation?

🔹 A. Attendance increased by less than 5 % and was not statistically significant.
🔹 B. Attendance rose by about 12 % with modest gender gaps.
🔹 C. Attendance improved by over 20 % with notable gains for girls.
🔹 D. Attendance showed no measurable change due to implementation challenges.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The 2021 evaluation demonstrated that CCTs led to a more than 20 % increase in school attendance, especially among girls, highlighting the program’s effectiveness in reducing gender disparities and encouraging consistent enrollment in rural Kenyan schools.

Q22. A longitudinal study examines the impact of early childhood nutrition programs on adult earnings. Which statistical method would best isolate causal effects?

🔹 B. Difference‑in‑differences
🔹 C. Propensity score matching
🔹 D. Randomized controlled trial
