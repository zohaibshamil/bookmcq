What is UN Trusteeship Council purpose?

The UN Trusteeship Council was established to supervise the administration of trust territories and ensure their advancement toward self-government or independence, successfully completing its mandate when the last territory achieved sovereignty in 1994.

Q1. The Trusteeship Council was established to replace which earlier body?

A.League of Nations Mandates Commission ✅
B.UN General Assembly
C.Security Council
D.Economic and Social Council
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The League of Nations Mandates Commission, created in 1919, oversaw territories taken from Germany and Turkey after WWI. The Trusteeship Council succeeded this commission, continuing the oversight of trust territories under the United Nations framework.

Q2. In what year did the majority of trust territories achieve independence according to the passage?

A.1965
B.1970 ✅
C.1975
D.1980
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: By 1970 most of the mandated territories under the Trusteeship Council’s supervision had attained independence, marking a significant milestone in decolonization and reflecting the Council’s effectiveness in guiding these regions toward self‑governance.

D.Belgium
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: South Africa, governed by a white minority regime, resisted granting independence to Namibia, creating a prolonged dispute that tested the Trusteeship Council’s capacity to enforce decolonization principles and maintain international consensus.

Q4. What was the primary purpose of the mandates system after World War I?

D.To establish new trade routes
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The mandates system was designed to place former German and Ottoman territories under the administration of victorious powers, with the ultimate goal of guiding these areas toward self‑determination and sovereign statehood.
