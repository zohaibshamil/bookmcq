Q12. If the UN’s total annual budget is \( \$10\) billion and member contributions total \( \$7.5\) billion, what is the shortfall?

🔹 C. \( \$1.5\) billion
🔹 D. \( \$2\) billion

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The shortfall equals the budget minus contributions: \( \$10\) billion − \( \$7.5\) billion = \( \$2.5\) billion, a gap that would require emergency funding or reduced operations to avoid jeopardizing UN activities.

Q30. If a shortfall of \( \$500\) million exists and middle‑income countries collectively contribute \( \$100\) million, how much must each of five such countries add to cover the gap?

🔹 A. \( \$80\) million
🔹 B. \( \$100\) million
🔹 C. \( \$60\) million
🔹 D. \( \$50\) million

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The remaining gap is \( \$500\) million − \( \$100\) million = \( \$400\) million; divided among five countries, each must provide \( \$400\) million ÷ 5 = \( \$80\) million, demonstrating the additional burden required to bridge the deficit.
