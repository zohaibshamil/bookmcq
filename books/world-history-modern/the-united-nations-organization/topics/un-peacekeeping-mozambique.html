Q1. In which year did Mozambique gain independence from Portugal?

Mozambique's independence came after a prolonged liberation struggle against Portuguese colonial rule, culminating in 1975. The year marks a pivotal moment in the nation's history, establishing sovereign governance and setting the stage for subsequent political developments, including later conflicts and reforms.

Q2. The ceasefire agreement was signed in Rome during which month?

🔹 D. December

Q3. The first multiparty elections in Mozambique were held in which month and year?
