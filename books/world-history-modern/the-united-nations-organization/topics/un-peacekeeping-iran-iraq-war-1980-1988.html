What is UN peacekeeping Iran-Iraq War 1980-1988?

UNIIMOG monitored the ceasefire between Iran and Iraq from 1988 to 1991 following eight years of war, verifying troop withdrawals and border compliance as part of UN-mediated settlement efforts ending the devastating conflict.

Q1. In what year did the Iran‑Iraq War officially begin?

A. 1979
B. 1980 ✅
C. 1981
D. 1982

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The conflict started when Iraq launched a full‑scale invasion of Iran on 22 September 19801980. This date marks the official beginning of the war, which lasted eight years and resulted in massive casualties on both sides.

Q2. Which Iraqi leader announced the decision to go to war with Iran?

A. Saddam Hussein
B. Ahmed Hassan al‑Bakr
C. Izzat Ibrahim al‑Douri
D. Tariq Aziz ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Tariq Aziz, Iraq’s foreign minister, publicly announced the war in a televised speech on 22 September 19801980. Although Saddam Hussein was the ultimate decision‑maker, Aziz was the face of the declaration to the world.

Q3. What was the primary justification Iraq gave for invading Iran?

A. Territorial disputes over the Shatt al‑Arab ✅
B. Support for Kurdish rebels
C. Desire to spread Arab nationalism
D. Retribution for Iranian support of Iraq’s opposition groups

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Iraq claimed the war was necessary to resolve the longstanding border dispute over the Shatt al‑Arab waterway, arguing that Iran’s refusal to negotiate threatened Iraqi sovereignty.

Q4. Which of the following best describes the UN’s role during the early phase of the Iran‑Iraq War?

B. Mediation and cease‑fire proposals
C. Providing humanitarian aid only ✅
D. No involvement at all

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

During the initial years, the UN mainly focused on delivering humanitarian assistance to civilians, while direct military action and mediation were limited due to the conflict’s complexity and the interests of the great powers.

Q5. How did the United States initially view the Iran‑Iraq conflict?

📖 Explanation:

Q6. Which city was the first major target of the Iraqi offensive?

Q26. Which factor most significantly limited the UN’s ability to enforce its resolutions during the conflict?

C. Absence of clear legal authority for sanctions

The UN does not possess its own permanent military force, relying on member contributions; during the Iran‑Iraq War, this limitation hampered its capacity to compel compliance with cease‑fire or disarmament demands.

Q27. A peace analyst argues that a UN‑led observer mission could have shortened the war. Which logical flaw undermines this argument?

Q33. How did the war influence the design of future UN peacekeeping mandates?

Q34. Considering the UN’s experience, what lesson can be drawn about peacekeeping in conflicts where great‑power interests are at stake?
