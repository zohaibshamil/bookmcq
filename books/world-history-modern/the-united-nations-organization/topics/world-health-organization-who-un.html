What is World Health Organization WHO UN?

The World Health Organization directs and coordinates international health efforts within the UN system, setting norms, responding to emergencies, and promoting universal health coverage to achieve highest attainable standard of health for all peoples.

📝 All World Health Organization WHO UN MCQs

Q1. When was the World Health Organization officially established?

A. 1948 ✅
B. 1950
C. 1960
D. 1975

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The WHO was officially founded on 7 April 1948 as a specialized agency of the United Nations, tasked with directing and coordinating international health within the UN system. Its creation marked a major step toward global cooperation on health issues.

Q2. Which of the following is NOT a core function of the WHO?

A. Setting international health standards
B. Conducting disease surveillance
C. Issuing travel visas ✅
D. Providing technical assistance to countries

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The WHO focuses on health standards, surveillance, and technical support, but it does not have authority to issue travel visas. Visa issuance is managed by individual governments or immigration agencies, not by the WHO.

Q3. How many member states does the WHO currently have?

A. 193 ✅
B. 190
C. 150
D. 200

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

As of the most recent data, the WHO comprises 193 member states, reflecting its broad global reach. This membership allows the organization to coordinate health policies and initiatives across virtually every recognized sovereign nation.

Q4. Which WHO program focuses on combating a specific infectious disease that caused a pandemic in 2020?

A. Global Polio Eradication Initiative
B. COVID‑19 Solidarity Response Fund ✅
C. Malaria Elimination Programme
D. Tuberculosis Control Programme

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The COVID‑19 Solidarity Response Fund was launched by the WHO to support research, medical supplies, and coordination during the pandemic that began in 2020. It exemplifies the WHO’s rapid response capacity to emerging global health threats.

Q5. A low‑income country reports a sudden rise in malaria cases after heavy rains. Which WHO tool should the country prioritize to manage the outbreak?

A. The International Health Regulations (IHR)
B. The Global Health Observatory data repository
C. The WHO Malaria Toolkit ✅
D. The WHO Framework Convention on Tobacco Control

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The WHO Malaria Toolkit provides guidelines, treatment protocols, vector control strategies, and monitoring tools specifically designed for malaria outbreaks. While the IHR addresses broader health emergencies, the Malaria Toolkit offers targeted, actionable resources for the situation described.

Q6. Which document outlines the WHO’s strategic priorities for the next decade?

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The WHO 2023‑2030 Strategic Plan details the organization’s priorities, including universal health coverage, health emergencies, and promoting healthier populations. This plan guides resource allocation and policy development for the upcoming ten years.

Q7. What is the primary purpose of the International Health Regulations (IHR) adopted by the WHO?

A. To regulate pharmaceutical pricing globally
B. To provide a legal framework for preventing and responding to public health emergencies ✅
C. To standardize medical education curricula
D. To manage international medical research funding

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The IHR create a legally binding framework that obliges member states to detect, assess, report, and respond to public health threats, enhancing global health security and preventing the spread of disease across borders.

📖 Explanation:

The WHO Global Action Plan for Non‑Communicable Diseases recommends fiscal policies such as sugar taxes to curb consumption of unhealthy foods, thereby reducing the burden of diseases like diabetes and cardiovascular conditions.

C. Discouraged due to insufficient evidence ✅
D. Neutral, leaving decisions to individual countries
