What is UN peacekeeping West New Guinea 1946?

There was no UN peacekeeping mission in West New Guinea in 1946; the UN Temporary Executive Authority administered the territory only in 1962–1963 to facilitate transfer from Dutch to Indonesian control following a dispute resolution agreement.
