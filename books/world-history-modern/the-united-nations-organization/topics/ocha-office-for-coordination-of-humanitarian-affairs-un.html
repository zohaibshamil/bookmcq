What is OCHA Office for Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs UN?

OCHA coordinates international humanitarian response to emergencies, mobilizes funding through appeals, advocates for affected populations, and ensures coherent delivery of aid by bringing together UN agencies, NGOs, and donors.

Q1. In which year was the Rome Statute, the agreement that created the International Criminal Court, signed?

A.1998 ✅
B.1999
C.2000
D.2001
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Rome Statute was adopted on 17 July 1998 when 120 United Nations member states signed the treaty. This date marks the formal commitment to establish a permanent court for prosecuting war crimes, genocide, and crimes against humanity, providing essential context for OCHA’s coordination with legal mechanisms during humanitarian emergencies.

B.Geneva

D.Vienna

📖 Explanation: The International Criminal Court is based in The Hague, Netherlands. Locating the court there facilitates interaction with other major international legal institutions and enables OCHA to coordinate effectively with the ICC when addressing accountability issues arising from humanitarian crises.

Q3. What primary concern led the United States to withdraw its signature from the Rome Statute in May 2002?

📖 Explanation: The United States withdrew its signature primarily because of concerns that its citizens, especially peacekeepers, could be subject to ICC jurisdiction and politically motivated prosecutions. This stance influences how OCHA negotiates access and cooperation with US‑led missions in conflict zones.

Q16. Given that the ICC has 18 judges, what is the minimum number of judges that must agree to authorize an investigation under the Rome Statute?

B.12
C.13 ✅
D.15
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Article 36 of the Rome Statute requires a majority of 13 of the 18 judges to agree before an investigation can be authorized. This threshold ensures broad consensus among the judges, balancing efficiency with the need for careful deliberation, a factor OCHA must consider when anticipating legal actions.

Q17. Which UN organ is responsible for appointing the judges of the ICC?

📖 Explanation: Judges of the ICC are elected by the Assembly of States Parties, which consists of all states that have ratified the Rome Statute. This body functions through a collaborative process involving both the General Assembly and the Security Council, ensuring balanced representation.
