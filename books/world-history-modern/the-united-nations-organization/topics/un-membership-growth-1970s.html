Q1. By 1970, which regions together formed a clear majority of UN members?

🔹 A. Europe and North America
🔹 B. Latin America
🔹 C. Africa and Asia
🔹 D. Oceania

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

By 1970, decolonization in Africa and the emergence of newly independent Asian states dramatically increased their representation, resulting in more than half of the General Assembly seats being held by countries from these two continents, establishing a clear numerical majority.

Q2. What major shift occurred in the balance of power within the UN General Assembly during the 1970s?

🔹 B. Communist bloc gained dominance
🔹 C. Third World bloc gained dominance
🔹 D. No significant shift occurred

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The influx of newly independent nations from Africa and Asia altered the voting dynamics, allowing the Third World bloc to command a majority and making it difficult for either the Western or Communist blocs to secure passage of their preferred resolutions without broader coalition support.
