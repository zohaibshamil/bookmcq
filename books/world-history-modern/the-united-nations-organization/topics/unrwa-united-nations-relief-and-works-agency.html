What is UNRWA United Nations Relief and Works Agency?

UNRWA provides education, healthcare, social services, and emergency assistance specifically to registered Palestine refugees in Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, West Bank, and Gaza Strip, operating separately from other UN refugee agencies due to unique mandate.

Q1. In which year was UNRWA formally established by the UN General Assembly?

A. 1945
B. 1947
C. 1949 ✅
D. 1950

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

UNRWA was created in 1949 by the United Nations General Assembly through resolution 302 (IV) to provide relief and works programmes for Palestinian refugees displaced by the 1948 Arab‑Israeli conflict.
