📝 All League of Nations Secretariat MCQs

Q1. On what date did the League of Nations Secretariat officially begin its work?

🔹 A. 10 January \(1920\)
🔹 B. 24 April \(1919\)
🔹 C. 1 March \(1921\)
🔹 D. 15 June \(1918\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Secretariat was created simultaneously with the League of Nations, commencing operations on \(10\) January \(1920\). This date marks the formal start of its administrative duties, coinciding with the activation of the Versailles Treaty and the League’s charter.

Q2. The Secretariat reported directly to which organ of the League?

🔹 A. The Assembly
🔹 B. The Council
🔹 C. The Permanent Court of International Justice
🔹 D. The Economic and Financial Committee

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Although the Secretariat worked for both the Assembly and the Council, its primary accountability was to the Council, which was the executive body responsible for implementing League decisions and overseeing the Secretariat’s daily operations.

Q3. Which of these individuals served as a notable Deputy Secretary‑General?

🔹 A. Sir Eric Drummond
🔹 B. Julius Streicher
🔹 C. Charles Seymour
🔹 D. James McDonald

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Charles Seymour, a British diplomat, held the position of Deputy Secretary‑General and assisted Sir Eric Drummond in managing the League’s extensive administrative tasks, particularly during the interwar years when the Secretariat’s workload increased.

Q4. Who was the first Secretary‑General of the League of Nations?

🔹 A. Sir Eric Drummond
🔹 B. Julius Streicher
🔹 C. Léon Bourgeois
🔹 D. Jan Smuts

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Sir Eric Drummond, a seasoned British civil servant, was appointed as the inaugural Secretary‑General in \(1919\) and served until \(1937\), guiding the Secretariat through its formative decades and establishing many of its operating procedures.

Q5. In which year did the Secretariat relocate its headquarters within Geneva?

🔹 A. \(1920\)
🔹 B. \(1922\)
🔹 C. \(1925\)
🔹 D. \(1930\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Secretariat moved to a larger building in Geneva in \(1922\) to accommodate its growing staff and expanding responsibilities, reflecting the League’s increasing administrative demands during the early 1920s.

Q6. The Secretariat was funded primarily through contributions from member states. What proportion of the total League budget did the Secretariat's expenses represent in the 1920s?

🔹 A. \(5\%\)
🔹 B. \(15\%\)
🔹 C. \(25\%\)
🔹 D. \(35\%\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Financial records from the 1920s show that the Secretariat’s operating costs accounted for roughly \(15\%\) of the League’s overall budget, a share that allowed it to maintain staff, publish reports, and support diplomatic activities without overwhelming the organization’s finances.

Q7. Which city housed the League of Nations Secretariat?

🔹 A. Geneva
🔹 B. Paris
🔹 C. London
🔹 D. New York

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Geneva, Switzerland, was selected as the permanent seat of the League of Nations, providing a neutral location for the Secretariat and other organs, and it remained the headquarters until the League’s dissolution after the Second World War.

Q8. Imagine a dispute arises between two member nations over a border. How would the Secretariat facilitate resolution according to League procedures?

🔹 A. Dispatch a neutral mediator to the parties
🔹 B. Issue a binding arbitration ruling
🔹 C. Organize a conference of the Council and Assembly
🔹 D. Apply economic sanctions

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Secretariat’s role in border disputes involved arranging a neutral mediation process, sending a qualified envoy to both parties, and preparing a report for the Council. This approach emphasized dialogue and aimed to prevent escalation without imposing immediate punitive measures.

Q9. During the Manchuria crisis of \(1931\), the Secretariat prepared a report for the Council. Which of the following actions would best reflect its role?

🔹 A. Drafting a military response plan
🔹 B. Compiling diplomatic communications and recommendations
🔹 C. Imposing trade embargoes
🔹 D. Calling for immediate League dissolution

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

In the Manchuria crisis, the Secretariat’s primary function was to gather all relevant diplomatic correspondence, assess the situation, and provide the Council with a concise set of recommendations, thereby supporting informed decision‑making without directly initiating military or economic actions.

Q10. Which language was NOT an official working language of the Secretariat?

🔹 A. English
🔹 B. French
🔹 C. German
🔹 D. Spanish

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

During the League’s existence, English, French, and German were the three official languages used in Secretariat documents and meetings. Spanish, while spoken by many members, was never adopted as an official working language of the organization.

Q11. What was the primary function of the League Secretariat?

🔹 A. Administrative support and coordination
🔹 B. Military enforcement
🔹 C. Economic sanctions
🔹 D. Judicial rulings

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Secretariat acted as the League’s bureaucratic backbone, handling correspondence, organizing meetings, publishing reports, and ensuring that the decisions of the Assembly and Council were implemented, thereby enabling the organization to operate smoothly on a daily basis.

Q12. A member state requests assistance for repatriating prisoners of war. How should the Secretariat prioritize this request among its other duties?

🔹 A. Place it at the top of the agenda due to humanitarian priority
🔹 B. Delay until after economic meetings
🔹 C. Refer to the Council for decision
🔹 D. Ignore as outside its mandate

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Humanitarian concerns, such as the return of prisoners of war, were central to the League’s mission. The Secretariat would therefore elevate the request, coordinate with relevant agencies, and expedite the necessary paperwork to address the urgent humanitarian need.

Q13. How many staff members did the Secretariat have at its inception?

🔹 A. Approximately \(150\)
🔹 B. Around \(500\)
🔹 C. Close to \(1,000\)
🔹 D. Nearly \(2,000\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

When the League began, the Secretariat was staffed by roughly \(150\) employees, a modest number that reflected the organization’s initial scope and the expectation that its administrative workload would grow gradually as more members joined.

Q14. A new member joins the League. What procedural step must the Secretariat complete before the member can participate in the Assembly?

🔹 A. Verify the ratification of the Covenant
🔹 B. Arrange a ceremonial oath
🔹 C. Assign a seat in the Secretariat staff
🔹 D. Issue a security clearance

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Before a new nation could take its seat in the Assembly, the Secretariat was responsible for confirming that the state had formally ratified the League Covenant, ensuring that it accepted the organization’s rules and obligations.

Q15. Which of the following documents defined the duties of the Secretariat?

🔹 A. League Covenant
🔹 B. Treaty of Versailles
🔹 C. Charter of the United Nations
🔹 D. Munich Agreement

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The League Covenant, incorporated into the peace treaties after World War I, outlined the structure, functions, and responsibilities of the Secretariat, providing the legal framework that guided its operations throughout the League’s existence.

Q16. The Secretariat produced an annual statistical report. Which sector did it NOT cover?

🔹 A. Health
🔹 B. Education
🔹 C. Military expenditures
🔹 D. Agriculture

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

While the Secretariat collected data on health, education, and agriculture to support social and economic initiatives, it deliberately excluded detailed statistics on military spending, as such information was considered sensitive and beyond its civilian mandate.

Q17. During the Abyssinia crisis of \(1935\), the Secretariat was asked to mediate. Which diplomatic tool would it most likely employ?

🔹 A. Economic sanctions
🔹 B. Mediation and fact‑finding mission
🔹 C. Military escort
🔹 D. Public condemnation

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Secretariat’s preferred method in the Abyssinia dispute was to send a fact‑finding mission and offer mediation services, aiming to clarify the facts on the ground and facilitate dialogue between Italy and Ethiopia before any punitive measures were considered.

Q18. Who succeeded Sir Eric Drummond as Secretary‑General in \(1937\)?

🔹 A. Joseph Avenol
🔹 B. Harcourt Butler
🔹 C. John Foster Dulles
🔹 D. Émile Vandervelde

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

After Drummond’s retirement, the French diplomat Joseph Avenol was appointed Secretary‑General in \(1937\). His tenure coincided with the League’s most challenging period, marked by aggressive actions from Japan and Italy that the organization struggled to curb.

Q19. Suppose the Secretariat discovers that a member state has secretly re‑armed in violation of League agreements. According to League protocol, what is the most appropriate course of action?

🔹 A. Publicly expose the violation
🔹 B. Report to the Council for possible sanctions
🔹 C. Ignore to preserve unity
🔹 D. Seek UN intervention

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Secretariat’s duty was to inform the Council of any breach of League provisions. The Council could then deliberate on appropriate measures, such as sanctions or diplomatic pressure, ensuring that the issue was handled through the League’s established mechanisms rather than unilateral publicity.

Q20. If the Secretariat's budget is cut by \(30\%\) in a given year, which function would most likely be reduced first?

🔹 A. Publication of reports
🔹 B. Staff salaries
🔹 C. Diplomatic missions
🔹 D. Maintenance of headquarters

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

When faced with severe budget reductions, the Secretariat would prioritize core administrative activities and likely curtail the production and distribution of its extensive reports, as these are costly and can be temporarily suspended without immediate impact on day‑to‑day operations.

Q21. The Secretariat maintained a specialized department for economic affairs. What was it called?

🔹 A. International Labour Office
🔹 B. Economic and Financial Committee Secretariat
🔹 C. League Bureau of Trade
🔹 D. World Bank

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Economic and Financial Committee Secretariat was responsible for analyzing global economic trends, advising the League on fiscal policies, and coordinating cooperation among member states, thereby playing a crucial role in the organization’s broader economic mission.

Q22. A member state proposes a new amendment to the Covenant. What role does the Secretariat play in processing this amendment?

🔹 A. Drafting the legal text and circulating it to all members
🔹 B. Voting on its adoption
🔹 C. Enforcing its immediate implementation
🔹 D. Rejecting it if it conflicts with existing rules

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Secretariat’s procedural duties included preparing the precise legal language of any proposed amendment, distributing the draft to every member for review, and managing the subsequent voting process, ensuring that all states had equal opportunity to consider the change.

Q23. When the League of Nations dissolved in \(1946\), what became of its Secretariat's archives?

🔹 A. Transferred to the United Nations archives
🔹 B. Destroyed
🔹 C. Returned to member states
🔹 D. Stored in the Swiss National Museum

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Upon dissolution, the League’s extensive records and documents were handed over to the newly formed United Nations, which incorporated them into its own archival system, preserving the historical legacy of the League for future research.
