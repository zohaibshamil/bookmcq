Q1. In which year did the League of Nations officially begin its operations?

🔹 A. 1919
🔹 B. 1920
🔹 C. 1921
🔹 D. 1922

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The League was formally established on 10 January 1920, the same day the Treaty of Versailles entered into force. Its headquarters were set up in Geneva, Switzerland, and the inaugural meeting convened shortly thereafter, marking the launch of an unprecedented international body aimed at preserving peace.

Q3. Which treaty came into operation on the same day as the League's founding?

🔹 D. Treaty of Locarno

This simultaneity underscored the intention of the post‑war settlement to create both a legal framework for peace and an organization to enforce it.

Q4. If the League had 58 member states in 1930 and each contributed an average of \(5\) million dollars to its budget, what was the total budget?

🔹 A. \(250\) million
🔹 B. \(290\) million
🔹 C. \(300\) million
🔹 D. \(350\) million

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Multiplying the number of members (58) by the average contribution (\(5\) million dollars) yields a total of \(58 \times 5 = 290\) million dollars. This figure illustrates the financial scale of the League’s operations in the early 1930s, providing resources for diplomatic missions, humanitarian aid, and administrative costs.

Q5. During the Japanese invasion of Manchuria (1931), the League required a unanimous vote of its 58 members to pass sanctions, but only \(45\) voted in favor. What percentage of members supported the resolution?

🔹 A. 0.7
🔹 B. 0.75
🔹 C. 0.78
🔹 D. 0.8

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The proportion supporting the resolution is calculated as \(45 \div 58 \approx 0.7759\), which rounds to 78%. This shortfall demonstrated the League’s inability to achieve unanimity on critical issues, weakening its authority and foreshadowing its limited capacity to enforce collective security measures.

Q7. Analyze why the League failed to prevent the Italian attack on Abyssinia in 1935 despite having a covenant that required collective action. Which factor most undermined its effectiveness?

This combination rendered the League powerless to compel Italy to withdraw, exposing a fundamental flaw in its collective security model.

Q23. Which of the following best explains why the United States never joined the League of Nations?

🔹 D. All of the above
