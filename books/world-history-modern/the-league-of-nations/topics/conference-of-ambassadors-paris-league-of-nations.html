What is Conference of Ambassadors Paris League of Nations?

The Conference of Ambassadors was an ad-hoc body of Allied ambassadors in Paris that often handled European territorial disputes parallel to or instead of the League Council, reflecting great power reluctance to fully delegate authority to the League and creating confusion over legitimate decision-making channels.
