What is World Disarmament Conference 1932-33 failure?

The World Disarmament Conference collapsed in 1933 when Germany demanded military equality with other powers and France refused to reduce armaments without security guarantees, demonstrating the League’s incapacity to achieve its core disarmament mandate amid rising nationalist tensions and mutual distrust.
