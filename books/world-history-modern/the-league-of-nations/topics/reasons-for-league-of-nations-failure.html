What is Reasons for League of Nations failure?

The League failed primarily because the United States never joined, key aggressor nations withdrew or were expelled, decision-making required unanimity which paralyzed action, and it lacked its own military force to enforce sanctions against powerful countries like Japan, Italy, and Germany.
