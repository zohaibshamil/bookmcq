Q1. Which treaty did France sign with the USSR in May 1935?

🔹 A. Treaty of Versailles
🔹 B. Treaty of Mutual Assistance
🔹 C. Molotov–Ribbentrop Pact
🔹 D. Anglo-German Naval Agreement

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The French government, alarmed by Germany's rearmament, concluded a Treaty of Mutual Assistance with the Soviet Union in May 1935. This agreement pledged mutual military support against aggression, aiming to contain Hitler’s ambitions, though it was short‑lived and later undermined by shifting alliances.

Q2. What was the primary purpose of the Anti‑Comintern Pact signed in 1936?

🔹 A. To promote trade between Germany and Japan
🔹 B. To oppose the Communist International
🔹 C. To establish a defensive alliance against the USSR
🔹 D. To coordinate naval construction

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Anti‑Comintern Pact, concluded between Germany and Japan in 1936, was designed to counteract the influence of the Communist International (Comintern). By targeting the organization founded by Lenin, the pact aligned the two powers against Soviet ideological expansion, reinforcing their anti‑communist stance.

Q3. During the Spanish Civil War, which side received Soviet assistance?

🔹 A. Nationalists
🔹 B. Republicans
🔹 C. Both sides equally
🔹 D. No side received help

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Soviet aid was directed to the Republican government in Spain, which faced the Nationalist rebellion led by Franco. The USSR supplied weapons, advisors, and material support, aiming to prevent a fascist victory and to protect left‑wing interests, while Germany and Italy backed the Nationalists.

Q4. How did the Anglo‑German Naval Agreement of June 1935 affect the Stresa Front?

🔹 A. It strengthened the alliance
🔹 B. It caused Britain to leave the front
🔹 C. It led to a joint naval buildup with Italy
🔹 D. It had no impact

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The Anglo‑German Naval Agreement allowed Germany to expand its submarine fleet, breaching Versailles. Britain signed it without consulting France or Italy, alienating its partners and effectively dismantling the Stresa Front. This unilateral move eroded trust among the three nations, weakening collective opposition to German aggression.

Q5. Which event most directly prompted the USSR to intervene in the Spanish conflict?

🔹 A. The invasion of Manchuria
🔹 B. The Anschluss of Austria
🔹 C. The outbreak of the Spanish Civil War
🔹 D. The Munich Agreement

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Although the USSR was wary of direct confrontation, the rise of fascist powers in Europe, exemplified by Germany’s annexation of Austria (Anschluss) in 1938, heightened Soviet concerns. This climate motivated Soviet support for the Spanish Republicans as part of a broader strategy to counteract fascist expansion.

Q6. What was a key consequence of Italy’s invasion of Abyssinia for Soviet foreign policy?

🔹 D. It caused the USSR to withdraw from Europe

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Italy’s unpunished aggression in Abyssinia exposed the League of Nations’ impotence, which the USSR interpreted as a warning. Recognizing the need for a stronger collective response, the Soviet Union intensified its diplomatic efforts to build coalitions against unchecked fascist aggression, though practical cooperation remained limited.

Q7. Why did Britain and France adopt a non‑intervention stance during the Spanish Civil War?

🔹 A. They feared Soviet domination
🔹 B. They were sympathetic to the Nationalists
🔹 C. They wanted to avoid another war
🔹 D. They lacked military capability

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Both Britain and France, still recovering from World War I and confronting domestic economic woes, chose a policy of non‑intervention to prevent escalation into a broader European conflict. This reluctance left the Republicans reliant on Soviet aid while the Nationalists benefited from German and Italian support, influencing the war’s outcome.

Q8. In what way did the Rome‑Berlin Axis of October 1936 influence Soviet strategic calculations?

🔹 D. It forced the USSR to support Italy’s policies

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The formal alliance between Italy and Germany signaled a growing bloc of fascist powers, prompting the Soviet Union to reassess its security posture. Anticipating a possible coalition against communism, the USSR accelerated its own rearmament and sought new diplomatic ties to counterbalance the emerging Axis threat.

Q9. How did the Soviet Union justify its intervention in the Spanish Republic?

🔹 A. To spread communism across Europe
🔹 B. To defend democratic governments
🔹 C. To protect Soviet borders
🔹 D. To retaliate against Germany

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The USSR framed its assistance to the Spanish Republic as a defense of democratic and anti‑fascist forces, aligning with its broader ideological opposition to the rising fascist powers. By presenting the intervention as support for legitimate governance, the Soviets aimed to legitimize their involvement while countering Axis influence.

Q10. Analyze the impact of the Soviet‑German non‑aggression pact (Molotov‑Ribbentrop) on the onset of World War II.

🔹 A. It delayed the war by two years
🔹 B. It facilitated the division of Eastern Europe
🔹 C. It had no strategic effect
🔹 D. It forced Britain to declare war earlier

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The Molotov‑Ribbentrop Pact, signed in August 1939, surprised many analysts because it neutralized Soviet opposition to German expansion. While it temporarily secured Soviet borders, the secret protocol dividing Eastern Europe set the stage for the joint invasion of Poland, directly triggering World War II. Thus, the pact’s strategic effect was pivotal rather than negligible.

Q11. Evaluate why the Soviet Union’s aid to the Spanish Republicans failed to prevent a Nationalist victory.

🔹 A. Insufficient quantity of weapons
🔹 B. Lack of coordination with Britain
🔹 C. Overwhelming German‑Italian support
🔹 D. All of the above

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Soviet assistance, though crucial, was limited by logistical constraints, delayed deliveries, and the absence of coordinated Allied support. Simultaneously, the Nationalists received extensive German and Italian military aid, tipping the balance. The combination of these factors, along with internal Republican disunity, culminated in the Nationalist triumph.

Q12. What role did the Comintern play in Soviet foreign policy during the 1930s?

🔹 A. It directed Soviet economic policy
🔹 B. It organized espionage networks
🔹 C. It facilitated support for communist movements abroad
🔹 D. It negotiated peace treaties

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Established in 1919, the Comintern served as a conduit for Soviet influence by coordinating communist parties worldwide. During the 1930s, it enabled the USSR to provide ideological and material support to aligned movements, such as the Spanish Republicans, thereby extending Soviet geopolitical reach without direct military confrontation.

Q13. Which factor most directly weakened the Stresa Front after its formation?

🔹 D. The Munich Agreement

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Anglo‑German Naval Agreement of June 1935 allowed Germany to expand its submarine fleet, breaching Versailles and undermining the mutual trust among Britain, France, and Italy. This unilateral British action eroded the cohesion of the Stresa Front, causing France and Italy to question the alliance’s reliability.

Q14. How did the Soviet Union view the failure of the League of Nations to curb Japanese aggression in Manchuria?

🔹 B. As a justification for Soviet expansion
🔹 C. As a reason to withdraw from international affairs
🔹 D. As an opportunity to ally with Japan

📖 Explanation:

The League’s inability to stop Japan’s 1931 invasion of Manchuria exposed the impotence of collective security mechanisms. The USSR interpreted this failure as a sign of Western reluctance to enforce international law, reinforcing its belief that stronger bilateral or bloc arrangements were necessary to counteract aggression.

Q15. Scenario: If the USSR had not supplied aid to the Spanish Republicans, which outcome would have been most likely?

📖 Explanation:

Without Soviet material support, the Republicans would have lacked critical arms and advisors, accelerating their defeat. The Nationalists, already bolstered by German and Italian assistance, would have faced reduced resistance, leading to a swifter collapse of Republican forces and a definitive Nationalist triumph.

Q16. What strategic advantage did the Soviet Union gain by signing the Anti‑Comintern Pact with Germany?

🔹 A. Access to German naval technology
🔹 B. A formal alliance against Britain
🔹 C. No advantage; it was purely symbolic
🔹 D. Improved intelligence sharing

📖 Explanation:

The Anti‑Comintern Pact was primarily an anti‑communist declaration rather than a mutual defense treaty. The USSR did not join the pact; instead, Germany and Japan signed it. Therefore, the Soviet Union derived no direct strategic benefit, and its stance remained opposed to the pact’s objectives.

This limited response reflected both strategic caution and the absence of a formal alliance with France at that time.
