What is Hitler's Responsibility: The Architect of Global Conflict?

Despite contributing factors like appeasement and Soviet opportunism, Hitler bears ultimate responsibility because his racist ideology, explicit war aims outlined in Mein Kampf, and deliberate sequencing of aggressive acts demonstrate intentional pursuit of continental domination rather than mere reaction to external circumstances.

Q1. In which year did Japan invade Manchuria, marking its first major aggression in the 1930s?

A. 1929
B. 1931 ✅
C. 1933
D. 1935

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Japanese invasion of Manchuria occurred in \(1931\), establishing a precedent for aggressive expansion that later influenced the actions of Germany and Italy, and highlighted the League of Nations' inability to enforce collective security.

Q2. Which treaty did Hitler breach by announcing the reintroduction of conscription in March 1935?

A. Treaty of Versailles ✅
B. Locarno Pact
C. Kellogg-Briand Pact
D. Munich Agreement

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The reintroduction of conscription violated the military restrictions imposed by the Treaty of Versailles, which limited the size and capabilities of the German armed forces, signaling a direct challenge to the post‑World War I order.

Q3. What was the primary consequence of the Stresa Front's collapse in June 1935 for Germany's diplomatic standing?

A. Strengthened alliance with Italy
B. Increased trust from Britain
C. Isolation of Germany
D. Loss of naval restrictions ✅

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The breakdown of the Stresa Front, caused by Britain signing the Anglo‑German Naval Agreement without consulting its partners, removed a key diplomatic check on Germany and enabled it to expand its navy, undermining the collective effort to contain German rearmament.

Q4. The Anti‑Comintern Pact of 1936 primarily aimed to:

A. Coordinate military actions against the Soviet Union ✅
B. Promote trade between Germany and Japan
C. Support the League of Nations
D. Establish a joint economic zone

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Established by Germany and Japan to oppose the Communist International, the pact aligned the two powers against the Soviet Union and signaled a shared anti‑communist stance that would shape future alliances.

Q5. Analyze how Britain's decision to sign the Anglo‑German Naval Agreement affected its relationship with France and Italy.

B. It caused distrust and weakened the Stresa Front ✅
C. It had no impact
D. It led to a new alliance with Italy

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

By unilaterally agreeing to allow Germany to expand its submarine fleet, Britain alienated France and Italy, eroding the trust that underpinned the Stresa Front. This move signaled a willingness to accommodate German rearmament, encouraging further aggression and destabilizing European diplomacy.
