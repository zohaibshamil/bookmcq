What is Mussolini foreign policy 1923-1934?

During this earlier phase, Mussolini balanced between cooperation with Western democracies and assertive nationalism, signing treaties like the Locarno Pact while simultaneously cultivating fascist alliances and preparing the ideological groundwork for later imperial adventures.

Q1. What year did Japan invade Manchuria?

A. 1931 ✅
B. 1932
C. 1933
D. 1934

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The invasion occurred in \(1931\) when Japanese forces occupied Manchuria marking the first major aggression of the era. This action shocked the international community and exposed the weakness of the League of Nations in enforcing collective security measures.

Q2. Which treaty allowed Britain to sign a naval agreement with Germany without consulting France?

A. Treaty of Versailles
B. Locarno Pact
C. Stresa Front
D. Anglo‑German Naval Agreement ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The Anglo‑German Naval Agreement was signed in \(1935\) and permitted Germany to build submarines. Britain acted independently of France and Italy

Q3. What was the primary purpose of the Anti‑Comintern Pact signed in \(1936\)?

A. To promote trade between Germany and Japan
B. To oppose communist international activity ✅
C. To establish a military alliance against Italy
D. To create a cultural exchange program

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Anti‑Comintern Pact was a political agreement between Germany and Japan aimed at countering the influence of the Communist International. It reflected growing ideological cooperation against perceived Soviet threats.

Q4. Which country signed a mutual assistance treaty with the USSR in May \(1935\)?

A. Germany
B. Italy
C. France ✅
D. Japan

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

France signed a mutual assistance treaty with the Soviet Union in May \(1935\) as a response to German rearmament. The pact was intended to deter further German aggression but ultimately failed to prevent the escalation of tensions in Europe.

Q5. Who led the Nationalist forces in the Spanish Civil War?

Q6. What was the main result of the Stresa Front collapse?

B. Increased trust between France and Italy
C. Creation of the Rome‑Berlin Axis

The breakdown of the Stresa Front after Britain signed the Anglo‑German Naval Agreement eroded trust between France and Italy and paved the way for Germany to pursue its rearmament agenda unchecked.

Q8. Which event directly preceded Italy’s invasion of Abyssinia?

B. Hitler’s reintroduction of conscription ✅

Italy’s decision to invade Abyssinia in October \(1935\) was influenced by earlier German rearmament actions such as the reintroduction of conscription and the Anglo‑German Naval Agreement

Q9. How did the League of Nations respond to Italy’s invasion of Abyssinia?

A. It imposed a full economic embargo
B. It declared war on Italy
C. It offered limited sanctions ✅
D. It took no action

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

The League of Nations issued limited sanctions that were half‑hearted and ineffective. This tepid response demonstrated the League’s inability to enforce its own principles and contributed to the perception that aggressive powers could act with impunity.

Q10. Why did Britain choose to sign the Anglo‑German Naval Agreement despite French objections?
