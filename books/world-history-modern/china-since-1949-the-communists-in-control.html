About 20. China Since 1949: The Communists in Control

After 1949, the Chinese Communist Party implemented sweeping land reform, industrialization, and social campaigns like the Great Leap Forward and Cultural Revolution that caused massive disruption and loss of life. Mao’s era emphasized ideological purity and self-reliance but left China impoverished and isolated until his death opened the door to pragmatic reforms.

Deng Xiaoping’s market-oriented policies from the late 1970s unleashed unprecedented economic growth and lifted hundreds of millions out of poverty while maintaining strict party control. Today’s China blends state capitalism with authoritarian governance, emerging as a global superpower that challenges Western-led international order while grappling with inequality, demographic shifts, and demands for political freedom.
