What is World in 1914 before World War 1?

In 1914, the world was dominated by European great powers engaged in an arms race and colonial expansion, with society appearing stable on the surface but deeply fractured by militarism, secret alliances, and unresolved territorial disputes that made peace increasingly fragile.
