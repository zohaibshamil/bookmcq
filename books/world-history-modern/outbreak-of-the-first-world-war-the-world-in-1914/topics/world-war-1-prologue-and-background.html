What is World War 1 prologue and background?

The period leading up to 1914 was characterized by intense imperial competition, complex alliance systems, and rising nationalism that created a volatile international environment where regional conflicts could easily escalate into a global war due to interconnected political and military obligations.

Q1. What date did German assault troops enter Belgium during the First World War?

A. 03-Aug-14 ✅
B. 05-Aug-14
C. 13-Aug-14
D. 16-Aug-14

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

German troops entered Belgium on 3 August 1914, two days before converging on Liège on 5 August. The other dates represent later events: 5 August was the convergence on Liège, 13 August was the first fort's surrender, and 16 August was Liège's capture.
