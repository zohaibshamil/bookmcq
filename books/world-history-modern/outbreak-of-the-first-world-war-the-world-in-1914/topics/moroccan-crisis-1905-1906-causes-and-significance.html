Q1. In what year was the Entente Cordiale signed between Britain and France?

📖 Explanation:

The Entente Cordiale was signed in 1904. This agreement formed the basis for Anglo-French cooperation that would be tested during the Moroccan Crisis of 1905-6, with Britain supporting France against German ambitions in Morocco.

Q2. What was the primary German objective in the Moroccan Crisis?

🔹 C. To establish naval bases in Africa
🔹 D. To support Moroccan independence

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Germany's primary objectives were to expand their empire and to test the recently signed Anglo-French Entente Cordiale. By challenging French interests in Morocco, Germany hoped to determine whether the new understanding between Britain and France would hold under diplomatic pressure.

Q3. What did France agree to recognize in the Entente Cordiale in return for British approval of a French takeover of Morocco?

France agreed to recognize Britain's position in Egypt in return for British approval of a possible French takeover of Morocco. This mutual recognition of spheres of influence was the foundation of the Entente Cordiale, removing colonial friction between the two powers.
