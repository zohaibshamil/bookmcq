What is Anglo-Russian Agreement 1907 explained?

This settlement resolved long-standing colonial disputes between Britain and Russia in Persia, Afghanistan, and Tibet, effectively completing the Triple Entente alliance system and isolating Germany diplomatically by uniting its three potential adversaries into a cohesive bloc.

📝 All Anglo-Russian Agreement 1907 explained MCQs

Q1. In which year was the British agreement with Russia signed?

A.1905
B.1906
C.1907 ✅
D.1908
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The British agreement with Russia was signed in 1907. This agreement completed the Triple Entente by resolving Anglo-Russian differences in Persia, Afghanistan, and Tibet, creating a diplomatic alignment that Germany viewed as hostile encirclement.

Q2. How did Germany view the Anglo-Russian agreement of 1907?

A.As a friendly gesture
B.As confirmation of encirclement fears ✅
C.As a trade agreement
D.As a military alliance
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Germany viewed the Anglo-Russian agreement as confirmation of their fears that Britain, France, and Russia were planning to encircle them. This perception reflected German anxieties about being surrounded by hostile powers despite the agreement not being explicitly anti-German.

Q3. Which alliance had Russia signed in 1894 that made an Anglo-Russian agreement logical?

A.The Triple Alliance
B.The Triple Entente
C.An alliance with France ✅
D.An alliance with Germany
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Russia had signed an alliance with France in 1894. Since France was Britain's new partner in the Entente Cordiale, an Anglo-Russian agreement was a logical step to create a coherent alignment among the three powers.

Q4. What was Britain's historical view of Russia before 1905?

C.Russia was economically advanced
D.Russia was a naval power

📖 Explanation: For many years, Britain viewed Russia as a disgraceful example of corrupt, anti-democratic aristocratic government. This negative perception, combined with Russian threats to British interests, had prevented Anglo-Russian cooperation for decades before the 1907 agreement.
