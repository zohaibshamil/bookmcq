What is McCarthyism and anti-communist hysteria?

McCarthyism was a period of intense fear of communist infiltration in the early Cold War era where Senator Joseph McCarthy and others made unsubstantiated accusations against government employees and citizens, leading to blacklists, loyalty oaths, and suppression of dissent without due process.
