What is Nixon foreign policy and realpolitik?

President Nixon’s foreign policy embraced realpolitik by prioritizing practical national interests over ideological purity, pursuing détente with the Soviet Union, opening diplomatic relations with China, and seeking to balance global power through strategic negotiation rather than moral confrontation.

Q1. In which month and year did President Nixon travel to Beijing to meet Chairman Mao?

A.January \(1972\) ✅
B.February \(1972\)
C.March \(1972\)
D.April \(1972\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The correct answer is February 1972 because Nixon’s historic visit occurred in that month, opening diplomatic channels with China. Option A and D are wrong dates, and March is also incorrect. Understanding the timing helps illustrate how the administration used a specific diplomatic event to shift Cold‑War dynamics. Recognizing the exact date reinforces the broader pattern of Nixon’s outreach to Communist powers.
