What is Military industrial complex and anti-communism?

The military-industrial complex describes the powerful alliance between defense contractors, the Pentagon, and Congress that perpetuated high military spending driven by anti-communist ideology, creating economic dependence on war production and influencing foreign policy decisions throughout the Cold War.
