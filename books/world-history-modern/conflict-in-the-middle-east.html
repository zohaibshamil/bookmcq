About 11. Conflict in the Middle East

Conflict in the Middle East since 1945 has been driven by the legacy of colonial border-drawing, the creation of Israel, competition over oil resources, and deep sectarian and ideological divisions. The region became a major Cold War battleground and later a focal point for terrorism, foreign intervention, and civil wars that have caused immense human suffering and regional instability.

Multiple Arab-Israeli wars, the Iranian Revolution, Gulf Wars, and ongoing crises in Syria, Yemen, and Palestine reflect unresolved tensions between nationalism, religion, and external power interests. Despite numerous peace initiatives, the region remains volatile due to governance failures, resource competition, and the enduring impact of historical grievances and foreign meddling.
