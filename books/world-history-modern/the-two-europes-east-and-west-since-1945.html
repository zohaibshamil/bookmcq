About 10. The Two Europes: East and West since 1945

Post-war Europe was physically and ideologically divided by the Iron Curtain, with Western Europe integrating under American security guarantees and market economies while Eastern Europe fell under Soviet domination and centrally planned systems. This division shaped every aspect of life, from political freedoms and economic prosperity to cultural exchange and military alignment, creating two distinct civilizations on one continent.

Over time, Western Europe achieved remarkable recovery and unity through institutions like the EEC and NATO, while Eastern Europe stagnated under authoritarian rule until the revolutions of 1989. The eventual fall of communism and reunification of Europe marked one of the most significant geopolitical transformations of the late 20th century, though legacies of division still influence contemporary politics.
