What is US domination challenges in Nicaragua?

Nicaragua directly challenged U.S. domination through the Sandinista revolution which rejected American intervention, implemented socialist reforms, and accepted aid from the Soviet Union and Cuba, provoking a prolonged U.S.-funded counterinsurgency campaign.

Q1. In which year did Daniel Ortega first assume the presidency of Nicaragua in the 21st century?

A. 2005 ✅
B. 2006
C. 2007
D. 2008

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The correct answer is 2007 because Ortega won the election that took place in November 2006 and was inaugurated in January 2007. Option A (2005) and Option B (2006) refer to years before his inauguration, while Option D (2008) is after he had already begun his term. Knowing the inauguration year helps place his early reforms in context and shows how his policies developed over the following decade.

Q2. What proportion of Nicaraguans was reported to be living below the poverty line in 2011?

A. 0.35 ✅
B. 0.46
C. 0.55
D. 0.62

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The correct answer is 46% because surveys from 2011 indicated that nearly half of the population fell below the national poverty threshold. Option A (35%) understates the severity, Option C (55%) exaggerates it, and Option D (62%) is far higher than documented. Recognizing the scale of poverty is essential for evaluating why social programs such as scholarships and housing subsidies were prioritized during Ortega’s administration.

Q3. What primary factor motivated Ortega to replace overt Marxist language with references to Christian principles during his presidency?
