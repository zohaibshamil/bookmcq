What is US domination challenges in Venezuela?

U.S. domination in Venezuela was challenged by leaders like Hugo Chávez who promoted anti-imperialist policies, nationalized the oil industry, and forged alliances with other leftist governments to reduce American political and economic influence in the region.

Q1. If the world price of crude oil falls by 20%20\%20% and oil accounts for about 90%90\%90% of Venezuela's export revenue, what is the most likely immediate impact on the government's fiscal budget?

A.The budget deficit will increase sharply
B.The budget surplus will grow ✅
C.No change in the budget
D.Government revenue will rise
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A drop of 20%20\%20% in oil prices cuts the bulk of export earnings because oil makes up roughly 90%90\%90% of revenue. This reduction directly shrinks total government income, widening the deficit. Option B is opposite because a deficit, not a surplus, widens. Option C ignores the heavy reliance on oil, and Option D is incorrect because lower prices reduce, not raise, revenue.

Q2. Why did Hugo Chávez move to fully nationalize the oil industry in 2007?

C.To comply with WTO regulations
D.To lower domestic fuel prices
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Chávez aimed to diminish U.S. and other foreign control over strategic resources, ensuring that profits stayed in the state treasury. Nationalization was a political tool to assert sovereignty. Option B contradicts the goal of limiting private competition. Option C is unrelated; WTO rules do not require nationalization. Option D may be a secondary effect but was not the primary motive.

Q4. A policy maker wants to diversify Venezuela's economy after repeated oil price shocks. Which sector, already under state control, would be most logical to expand for export growth?

D.Tourism
