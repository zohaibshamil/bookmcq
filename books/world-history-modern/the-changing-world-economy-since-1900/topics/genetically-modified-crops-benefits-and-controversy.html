What is Genetically modified crops benefits and controversy?

Genetically modified crops offer benefits like higher yields, pest resistance, and improved nutrition to address food security, but they remain controversial due to concerns about corporate control of seeds, potential environmental risks, unknown long-term health effects, and impacts on small farmers.

Q1. In which year did at least 6 million farmers in 16 countries grow GM crops?

B.2000
C.2004 ✅

📖 Explanation: The correct answer is 2004 because that is the year reported when 6 million farmers across 16 nations were cultivating GM crops. Knowing the timeline helps understand how quickly GM technology spread worldwide, which is essential for assessing its economic impact and policy development.

Q6. A farmer applies glyphosate at 1.5 L/ha and reduces weed density from 30 weeds/m² to 5 weeds/m². What percentage reduction did he achieve?

A.0.75
B.0.83 ✅
C.0.5
D.0.25
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The reduction is calculated as \((30-5)/30 \times 100 = 83\%\). Option B matches this result. Option A (75%) would arise from a different initial density, while C and D are far lower than the actual decrease. Knowing how to compute percentage change lets agronomists evaluate the effectiveness of herbicide‑tolerant GM crops in real‑world field conditions.
