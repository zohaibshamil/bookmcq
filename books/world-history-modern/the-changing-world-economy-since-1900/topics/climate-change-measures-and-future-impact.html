What is Climate change measures and future impact?

Global efforts to combat climate change include transitioning to renewable energy, improving efficiency, and implementing carbon pricing, but inadequate action risks severe future impacts including rising sea levels, extreme weather, food insecurity, and economic disruption that will disproportionately affect vulnerable populations.
