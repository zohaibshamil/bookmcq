What is Capitalist vs communist blocs Cold War economy?

During the Cold War, the global economy was split into two opposing systems where the capitalist bloc led by the US emphasized private ownership and free markets, while the communist bloc led by the Soviet Union relied on state-controlled planning, resulting in separate trade networks and limited economic interaction between the two sides.
