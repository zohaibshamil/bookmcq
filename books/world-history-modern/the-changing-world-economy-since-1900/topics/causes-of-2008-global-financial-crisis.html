What is Causes of 2008 global financial crisis?

Key causes of the 2008 crisis included deregulation of financial markets, proliferation of subprime mortgages, securitization of toxic assets, inadequate risk management by banks, rating agency failures, and a housing bubble fueled by cheap credit and speculative investment.
