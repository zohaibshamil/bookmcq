What is Cold War thaw after Stalin death 1953?

Following Stalin’s death, new Soviet leadership under Khrushchev pursued limited diplomatic engagement with the West, reducing immediate tensions through summit meetings and cultural exchanges while maintaining underlying ideological rivalry.

Q1. In which year did Stalin die, prompting a shift toward peaceful coexistence?

📖 Explanation: Stalin’s death in 195319531953 created a power vacuum and allowed new Soviet leaders to reconsider policy. The new leadership, seeking economic relief, introduced the concept of peaceful coexistence, signalling a move away from direct confrontation with the West and setting the stage for later diplomatic thawing.

Q2. What was the primary purpose of the 1955 Austrian State Treaty?

A.To reunify Austria with Germany
B.To remove all occupying troops from Austria ✅
C.To create a neutral Baltic state
D.To establish a Soviet military base in Vienna
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The 1955 Austrian State Treaty aimed to end the post‑war occupation by withdrawing Soviet, American, British, and French forces, thereby restoring Austria’s full sovereignty and neutrality. This withdrawal was a symbolic gesture of easing East‑West tensions and demonstrated that the USSR could cooperate on specific security arrangements without compromising its core interests.

C.To support Turkey’s entry into the European Union
D.To encourage Turkish migration to the United States

📖 Explanation: American aid to Turkey, especially through the Truman Doctrine, was motivated by the desire to contain Soviet expansion into strategically vital regions such as the Mediterranean and the Middle East. By strengthening Turkey’s economy and military, the United States aimed to create a reliable anti‑communist ally on the USSR’s southern flank.

Q5. Which event directly renewed Cold War tensions after the 1950s thaw?

Q6. How did the 1972 Strategic Arms Limitation Treaty (SALT I) affect the arms race?

A.It eliminated all nuclear weapons
B.It halted the development of new missile technology
C.It placed limits on certain categories of strategic weapons ✅
D.It mandated the destruction of existing arsenals
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: SALT I introduced ceilings on the number of intercontinental ballistic missiles and submarine‑launched ballistic missiles each side could possess, thereby curbing the unchecked expansion of nuclear arsenals. While not ending the arms race, the treaty created a framework for verification and dialogue, reducing the immediate risk of a destabilizing buildup of strategic weapons.

Q14. Scenario: A Western diplomat in 197219721972 is tasked with explaining SALT I to a skeptical audience. Which point would be most persuasive?
