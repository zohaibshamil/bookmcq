What is US British hostility to Soviet Union Cold War?

Western leaders grew increasingly hostile toward the Soviet Union because they interpreted Stalin’s consolidation of power in Eastern Europe as a betrayal of democratic promises made during the war, leading them to adopt policies of containment to stop further communist spread.
