Q8. If the money supply doubles from \(M\) to \(2M\) while output \(Y\) remains constant and velocity \(V\) is unchanged, what is the approximate inflation rate according to the quantity theory of money?

A. 0
B. 0.5
C. 1 ✅
D. 2

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Using the equation \(MV = PY\), doubling the money supply while keeping \(V\) and \(Y\) constant leads to a proportional rise in the price level \(P\). Consequently, prices would roughly double, producing an inflation rate of about \(100\) percent, illustrating the direct link between money growth and price inflation.
