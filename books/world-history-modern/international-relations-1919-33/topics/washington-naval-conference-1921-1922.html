What is Washington Naval Conference 1921-1922?

This landmark diplomatic meeting produced treaties that limited naval armaments among major powers and established agreements regarding Pacific territories, representing a successful early attempt at arms control and reducing tensions in East Asia outside the League of Nations framework.

📝 All Washington Naval Conference 1921-1922 MCQs

Q1. What was the primary purpose of the Washington Naval Conference?

A.To limit naval armaments ✅
B.To discuss trade tariffs
C.To create the League of Nations
D.To settle border disputes
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The conference was convened to address the post‑World War I naval arms race, seeking to set quantitative limits on battleships and other warships. By doing so, the participating powers hoped to reduce the risk of future wars and the economic strain of maintaining ever‑larger fleets.

Q2. Which treaty emerged from the Washington Conference that set a 5:5:3 ratio for capital ships among the US, Britain, and Japan?

A.Treaty of Versailles
B.Four‑Power Treaty
C.Five‑Power Treaty ✅
D.London Naval Treaty
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The Five‑Power Treaty, also known as the Washington Naval Treaty, established the 5:5:3 tonnage ratio for capital ships, limiting the United States and Britain each to 525,000 tons and Japan to 315,000 tons. This ratio was intended to maintain a balance of power while curbing naval expansion.

Q3. Which nation was NOT a signatory of the Five‑Power Treaty?

A.United States
B.Italy ✅
C.Japan
D.Britain
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Italy was excluded from the Five‑Power Treaty because it was not considered a major naval power at the time. The treaty was signed by the United States, Britain, Japan, France, and China, reflecting the main Pacific and Atlantic naval powers that sought to limit battleship construction.

Q4. Why did the United States advocate for the 5:5:3 ratio in the Five‑Power Treaty?

A.To ensure parity with Britain
B.To limit Japanese naval expansion
C.To protect American commercial interests
D.All of the above ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: American policymakers wanted a ratio that would keep the United States competitive with Britain while preventing Japan from achieving a fleet capable of challenging US dominance in the Pacific. The balance also served U.S. trade goals by preserving open sea routes and reducing the cost of an unchecked naval buildup.

Q5. If the United States limited its battleship tonnage to 525,000 tons, what maximum tonnage was allowed for Japan under the 5:5:3 ratio?

A.315,000 tons ✅
B.425,000 tons
C.525,000 tons
D.600,000 tons
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Using the ratio, Japan’s limit is three‑fifths of the U.S. tonnage: \( \frac{3}{5}\times525,000 = 315,000 \) tons. This restriction was designed to keep Japanese naval strength below that of the United States and Britain while still allowing Japan a modest fleet.

Q6. Which of the following was a major outcome of the Four‑Power Treaty?

A.A mutual defense pact
B.An agreement to consult on Pacific issues ✅
C.Disarmament of air forces
D.Creation of a joint navy
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Four‑Power Treaty, signed by the United States, Britain, Japan, and France, established a framework for regular consultations on Pacific matters. It was intended to reduce tensions by encouraging diplomatic dialogue rather than military confrontation, though it did not create a formal alliance or joint naval forces.

Q7. How did the Washington Conference aim to reduce naval expenditures during the early 1920s?

A.By imposing tax increases
B.By setting construction limits
C.By encouraging ship scrapping
D.Both B and C ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The conference introduced quantitative caps on capital ships and also promoted the scrapping of older vessels to meet those limits. By restricting new construction and incentivizing the disposal of surplus ships, the participating nations could lower defense spending and avoid a costly arms race.

Q8. Britain had 540,000 tons of capital ships before the conference. To meet the 5:5:3 ratio with the United States (525,000 tons), what reduction was required?

A.Reduce by 15,000 tons ✅
B.Increase by 15,000 tons
C.No change needed
D.Reduce by 30,000 tons
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Since the United States was limited to 525,000 tons, Britain needed to match that figure. The required reduction is \(525,000 - 15,000 = 510,000\) tons, meaning Britain must cut 15,000 tons from its fleet to comply with the treaty’s equal‑tonnage provision.

Q9. What impact did the Washington Naval Conference have on Japan’s subsequent naval policies?

A.It led to increased naval buildup ✅
B.It caused Japan to focus on air power
C.It resulted in Japan withdrawing from naval treaties
D.It had no significant effect
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Although the treaty imposed limits, Japan perceived the restrictions as humiliating and responded by accelerating its own shipbuilding program within the allowed tonnage, later seeking to expand its navy beyond the treaty limits, which contributed to rising tensions in the 1930s.

Q10. Which participant proposed the inclusion of submarine limits in the treaties?

A.United Kingdom
B.United States ✅
C.Japan
D.France
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The United States, recognizing the strategic importance and growing threat of submarines, pushed for their inclusion in the treaty framework. This led to specific tonnage caps for submarines, ensuring that no signatory could gain a disproportionate advantage through undersea warfare.

Q11. In which city was the Washington Naval Conference held?

A.New York
B.Washington, D.C. ✅
C.London
D.Paris
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The conference took place in Washington, D.C., the capital of the United States, from November 1921 to February 1922. Hosting the meeting in the U.S. capital underscored the country's leadership role in shaping post‑war naval policy and international diplomacy.

Q12. Besides battleships, which other type of warship did the Washington Conference address?

A.Aircraft carriers
B.Submarines
C.Destroyers
D.All of the above ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The treaty provisions covered a range of warship classes, including battleships, aircraft carriers, submarines, and destroyers. By limiting each category, the conference sought a comprehensive reduction in naval armaments, reflecting the varied ways in which modern fleets could threaten one another.

Q13. If the treaty limited destroyer tonnage to 150,000 tons for each of the US, Britain, and Japan, what total tonnage were the three nations collectively allowed?

A.300,000 tons
B.450,000 tons ✅
C.600,000 tons
D.750,000 tons
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The combined allowance is calculated as \(3 \times 150,000 = 450,000\) tons. This aggregate cap ensured that even the smallest of the major powers could not amass a destroyer fleet that would destabilize the overall balance of naval forces.

Q14. Which nation withdrew from the Washington Naval Treaty in 1936?

A.United States
B.United Kingdom
C.Japan ✅
D.Italy
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Japan formally withdrew from the treaty in 1936 after deciding to pursue a larger navy beyond the imposed limits. This move signaled its rejection of the constraints and foreshadowed the aggressive naval expansion that would later contribute to tensions leading up to World War II.

Q15. During which years did the Washington Naval Conference take place?

A.1919‑1920
B.1921‑1922 ✅
C.1923‑1924
D.1925‑1926
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The conference was convened from November 1921 to February 1922, bringing together the major naval powers of the time to negotiate limits on warship construction and to promote stability in the Pacific and Atlantic regions.

Q16. How did domestic politics in the United States influence its stance at the Washington Conference?

A.Isolationist sentiment led to minimal involvement
B.Economic interests favored naval reduction
C.Public pressure for peace encouraged disarmament
D.All of the above ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: American public opinion after World War I favored peace and reduced military spending. Business interests also preferred a smaller navy to cut costs, while isolationist tendencies meant the U.S. sought diplomatic rather than military solutions, all of which shaped its advocacy for disarmament.

Q17. Assuming each capital ship displaced 30,000 tons, how many such ships could Japan build under its 315,000‑ton limit?

A.8
B.10 ✅
C.12
D.14
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Dividing the total allowed tonnage by the displacement of a single ship gives \( \frac{315,000}{30,000}=10.5\). Since a ship cannot exceed the limit, Japan could construct up to 10 capital ships within the treaty’s constraints.

Q18. Which treaty from the Washington Conference specifically addressed United States rights in the Pacific?

A.Four‑Power Treaty
B.Nine‑Power Treaty ✅
C.Five‑Power Treaty
D.Treaty of Versailles
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Nine‑Power Treaty affirmed the sovereignty and territorial integrity of China and recognized the United States' interests in the Pacific. It complemented the naval limitations by providing a diplomatic framework for Pacific security and trade.

Q19. What was the significance of the Nine‑Power Treaty for Chinese sovereignty?

A.It affirmed Chinese territorial integrity ✅
B.It allowed foreign concessions
C.It imposed economic sanctions
D.It mandated Chinese disarmament
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Nine‑Power Treaty explicitly recognized China's territorial integrity and opposed any further territorial acquisitions. By doing so, it aimed to protect Chinese sovereignty against colonial ambitions, though enforcement proved weak and subsequent aggression by Japan challenged its effectiveness.

Q20. Which U.S. President convened the Washington Naval Conference?

A.Woodrow Wilson
B.Warren G. Harding ✅
C.Calvin Coolidge
D.Herbert Hoover
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: President Warren G. Harding called the conference in November 1921 as part of his administration’s effort to promote peace and limit naval armaments, reflecting a broader desire for stability in the post‑war international order.

Q21. The Washington Conference limited which class of warships to 150,000 tons for each signatory?

A.Battleships
B.Cruisers
C.Submarines ✅
D.Aircraft carriers
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The treaty set a specific tonnage ceiling for submarines, capping each nation's submarine fleet at 150,000 tons. This restriction was intended to curb the growing threat of undersea warfare and to keep submarine numbers in proportion with surface fleet capabilities.

Q22. If the United Kingdom wanted a cruiser fleet of 180,000 tons but the treaty limited cruisers to 150,000 tons, what options were available?

A.Increase ship displacement
B.Reduce number of cruisers
C.Seek treaty amendment
D.Both B and C ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Britain could either cut the number of cruisers to stay within the 150,000‑ton limit or pursue diplomatic negotiations to amend the treaty’s terms. Increasing displacement would violate the treaty, so the viable choices were reduction or diplomatic amendment.

Q23. During the Washington Conference, which issue was NOT discussed?

A.Naval armament limits
B.Pacific security
C.Economic sanctions on Germany ✅
D.Territorial rights in the Pacific
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The conference focused on naval disarmament, Pacific stability, and related territorial concerns, but it did not address economic sanctions against Germany. Issues concerning German reparations and sanctions were handled in separate post‑war forums such as the Dawes Plan and later the Young Plan.
