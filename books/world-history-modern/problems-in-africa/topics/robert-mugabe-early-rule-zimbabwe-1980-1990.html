What is Robert Mugabe early rule Zimbabwe 1980-1990?

Robert Mugabe’s early rule in Zimbabwe from 1980 to 1990 was characterized by reconciliation, investment in education and healthcare, and relative stability after independence, earning him praise as a nationalist hero who prioritized social development and racial harmony.

Q1. In what year was the Unity Accord that merged ZANU and ZAPU signed?

A. 1985
B. 1987 ✅
C. 1989
D. 1990

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Unity Accord was signed in 1987, ending the rivalry between ZANU and ZAPU. Option B correctly names that year. Option A (1985) predates the agreement and no formal merger occurred then. Option C (1989) is after the accord, and Option D (1990) is even later, missing the key event. Knowing the correct year helps students place the political consolidation in the broader timeline of Zimbabwe’s early independence, showing how the merger shaped the country’s single‑party dominance.

C. Eliminating all foreign investment to promote self‑reliance.
D. Reducing public spending on health and education to cut deficits.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Option A reflects Mugabe’s early strategy of assuring the white minority a guaranteed share of parliamentary seats while simultaneously improving living standards through wage hikes and food subsidies. This combination eased economic fears and kept commercial agriculture productive. Option B would have disrupted output, Option C would have cut needed capital, and Option D would have harmed social services, all likely damaging growth. The correct policy shows how political inclusion can stabilize an economy during transition.
