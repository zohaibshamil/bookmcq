Q1. A small African nation decides to divert 15% of its military spending to build water reservoirs. Which immediate effect is most likely?

A. Increase in food production
B. Decrease in defense capability ✅
C. Rise in foreign debt
D. Higher unemployment

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Diverting money from the military means fewer funds for weapons, training, and equipment, so the country's ability to defend itself drops right away. The other options describe longer‑term or indirect results, not the direct and quickest impact of cutting the budget. Understanding this helps students see how budget choices can instantly change a nation's security posture.

Q2. Why do recurring droughts exacerbate conflict over land in East Africa?

A. Scarcity of water and arable land increases competition ✅
B. Droughts lower the price of land making it easier to acquire
C. Governments use drought as a pretext for expanding territory
D. Droughts improve soil fertility, reducing tension

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

When rain fails, both farmers and pastoralists have less water and fewer crops, so they compete harder for the limited fertile plots. This competition often turns into disputes or violent clashes. The other choices either misrepresent economic effects or describe outcomes that do not follow from a drought. Recognizing scarcity‑driven tension is key to grasping the root of many African land conflicts.

Q4. If the World Food Programme can feed 3.5 million Malawians, representing one‑third of the population, what is the estimated total population of Malawi?

A. 9 million
B. 10.5 million ✅
C. 12 million
D. 14 million

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

One‑third equals 3.5 million, so the whole population is three times that amount: 3 × 3.5 = 10.5 million. The other numbers come from simple mis‑multiplications. This calculation shows how aid figures can be used to infer demographic size, a useful skill for evaluating humanitarian data.

Q7. A refugee camp in Tanzania receives 200,000 new arrivals each year. If the camp’s capacity is 500,000 and currently hosts 350,000, after how many years will it exceed capacity assuming no departures?

A. 1 year ✅
B. 2 years
C. 3 years
D. 4 years

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Current occupancy is 350,000. Adding 200,000 in the first year gives 550,000, which already exceeds the 500,000 limit. Therefore the breach happens after one year. The other choices assume additional years before overflow, which is inaccurate when the inflow is larger than the remaining space.

Q8. What is the primary reason that border armament spending reduces agricultural productivity in Eritrea and Ethiopia?

A. It creates inflation
B. It diverts labor from farms ✅
C. It improves infrastructure
D. It attracts foreign investment

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Money spent on weapons and soldiers pulls both funds and able-bodied men away from planting, ploughing, and irrigation work. Inflation and infrastructure improvements are indirect effects, while foreign investment is not a typical result of increased armaments. Understanding this trade‑off highlights how militarisation can undermine food security.

Q9. If a drought reduces Malawi’s maize harvest to 10 % of normal, and normal production is 2 million tons, what is the actual harvest?

A. 100,000 tons
B. 200,000 tons ✅
C. 300,000 tons
D. 400,000 tons

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Ten percent of 2 million is 0.10 × 2,000,000 = 200,000 tons. The other figures come from mis‑reading the percentage or moving the decimal incorrectly. This straightforward calculation shows how drought severity translates into concrete food‑loss numbers.

B. 950,000 tons
C. 1,000,000 tons ✅

A. 5 million
B. 6 million
C. 7 million ✅
D. 8 million

Q20. If the World Food Programme’s budget for Malawi is increased by 20% from $150 million to cover a projected 6 million people needing aid, what is the new per‑person allocation assuming equal distribution?

A. 20
B. 25
C. 30 ✅
D. 35

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

A 20% raise gives 150 million × 1.20 = 150 million × 1.20 = 150 million × 1.20 = 180 million. Dividing by 6 million people yields 180 million ÷ 6 million = 180 million ÷ 6 million = 180 million ÷ 6 million = 30 per person. The other options arise from using the original budget or mis‑calculating the increase. This exercise shows how budget changes translate into individual assistance levels.
