What is Nelson Mandela and Thabo Mbeki South Africa?

Nelson Mandela symbolized reconciliation and moral leadership during South Africa’s transition, while Thabo Mbeki focused on economic policy and African diplomacy as his successor, together shaping post-apartheid governance though facing criticism over HIV/AIDS response and inequality.
