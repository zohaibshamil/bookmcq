What is End of apartheid South Africa 1994?

Apartheid ended in 1994 through negotiated settlements between the ANC and National Party, culminating in South Africa’s first democratic elections where Nelson Mandela became president, marking a peaceful transition to majority rule and the establishment of a new constitutional democracy.
