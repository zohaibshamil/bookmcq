What is Nyerere's approach was Success or failure??

Nyerere’s approach is viewed as both success and failure; it succeeded in fostering national identity, expanding education and healthcare, and maintaining peace, but failed economically due to inefficient collective farming, declining productivity, and dependence on foreign aid, leading to eventual policy reversal.
