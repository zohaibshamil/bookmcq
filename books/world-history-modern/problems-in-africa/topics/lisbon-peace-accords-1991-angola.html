📝 All Lisbon Peace Accords 1991 Angola MCQs

Q1. In what year did Namibia achieve independence?

🔹 A. 1988
🔹 B. 1990
🔹 C. 1992
🔹 D. 1994

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Namibia became a sovereign nation in 1990, ending South African administration. The transition was led by Sam Nujoma, who headed the independence movement. This date is a factual milestone that marks the successful outcome of the diplomatic negotiations linked to the Lisbon Peace Accords. The other years are either before the negotiations began or after the independence process was already complete, making them incorrect.

Q2. Why did the end of the Cold War make peace talks between MPLA and UNITA more feasible?

🔹 A. Soviet support to MPLA stopped
🔹 B. The UN lost funding for the mission
🔹 C. South Africa wanted more colonies
🔹 D. Cuba increased troops in Angola

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

When the Cold War ended, the Soviet Union withdrew its political and material assistance to the MPLA, removing a major source of external backing. Without Soviet aid, the MPLA lost leverage, and both sides faced pressure to negotiate. The other options either describe unrelated financial changes, unrealistic colonial ambitions, or a contradictory increase in Cuban forces, which did not occur after 1991.

Q3. How did the withdrawal of Cuban troops affect the military balance in Angola?

🔹 A. It strengthened the MPLA
🔹 B. It had no significant impact
🔹 C. It weakened the MPLA
🔹 D. It allowed UNITA to dominate

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Cuban forces had bolstered the MPLA’s capacity to fight UNITA. Their departure in 1991 removed that advantage, reducing the MPLA’s combat strength and creating a more even military stalemate. The option stating that the MPLA was strengthened is the opposite of what happened, and claiming no impact ignores the strategic loss. UNITA did not instantly dominate because other factors, such as terrain and resources, still mattered.

Q4. A neighboring country wants to mediate a civil conflict by arranging a ceasefire followed by UN‑monitored elections. Which lesson from the Lisbon Peace Accords suggests this plan is most likely to succeed?

🔹 A. All external powers must withdraw first
🔹 B. One side must control natural resources
🔹 C. A pre‑existing peace treaty is required
🔹 D. UN must deploy ground troops

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Lisbon talks succeeded because South Africa, Cuba, and other external actors agreed to pull out, creating a neutral environment for internal parties to negotiate. This demonstrates that removing foreign interference is a prerequisite for a credible ceasefire and election process. The other choices either impose unrealistic conditions, such as resource control or mandatory UN troops, which were not decisive factors in the historic agreement.

Q5. If South Africa had demanded that Cuban troops remain in Angola as a condition for leaving Namibia, what would most likely have happened?

🔹 A. The civil war would have continued longer
🔹 B. An immediate peace settlement would have been reached
🔹 C. The UN would have imposed sanctions on South Africa
🔹 D. Angola would have become a Soviet satellite

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

South Africa’s willingness to leave Namibia was tied to the removal of Cuban forces. Had it insisted on keeping those troops, the balance of power in Angola would have stayed skewed, giving the MPLA a continued advantage and reducing UNITA’s incentive to negotiate. This would likely have prolonged the conflict, whereas the other options describe outcomes that did not fit the political realities of the early 1990s.

Q6. What role did the Organization of African Unity (OAU) primarily play in the 1991 peace process?

🔹 A. Provided military forces to enforce the ceasefire
🔹 B. Funded the post‑war elections
🔹 C. Acted as a neutral facilitator of talks
🔹 D. Ignored the conflict altogether

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The OAU contributed diplomatic support by offering a platform for African leaders to discuss the Angolan crisis and by helping to convene the Lisbon negotiations. It did not send troops, nor did it finance the elections directly. Its neutral stance helped build trust among the parties. The option that claims the OAU ignored the conflict contradicts its active involvement in encouraging dialogue.

Q7. Why did the 1991 agreement specify elections rather than a power‑sharing arrangement for Angola’s future government?

🔹 A. Elections were viewed as more legitimate by the international community
🔹 B. Power‑sharing was prohibited by the UN charter
🔹 C. Elections would guarantee an MPLA victory
🔹 D. Power‑sharing would increase foreign intervention

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

International actors, especially the UN, favored elections because they provide a transparent mechanism for citizens to choose their leaders, enhancing legitimacy and reducing the risk of future disputes. Power‑sharing can entrench divisions and was not prohibited by any charter, making options B and D inaccurate. Option C misrepresents the intent, as elections were meant to be free and fair, not a guaranteed win for any party.

Q8. After a change in leadership, both former warring parties claim the 1991 accords are no longer valid. Which diplomatic move aligns best with the principles used in the original peace process?

🔹 A. Reopen negotiations under UN auspices
🔹 B. Impose economic sanctions on both sides
🔹 C. Withdraw all foreign aid until one side concedes
🔹 D. Recognize the party that controls the capital city

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The original accords were built on dialogue facilitated by the United Nations, so returning to that framework respects the established process and offers a neutral arena for renegotiation. Sanctions or aid cuts would heighten tensions, while recognizing the de facto ruler would undermine the principle of a negotiated settlement. Therefore, resuming UN‑led talks is the most consistent and constructive approach.

Q9. Which method would be most effective for the United Nations to monitor compliance with the ceasefire in Angola?

🔹 A. Use satellite imagery to detect troop movements
🔹 B. Deploy ground troops to both former combat zones
🔹 C. Rely on reports from local NGOs
🔹 D. Depend on international media coverage

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Deploying UN peacekeepers to both sides provides direct, on‑the‑ground verification of ceasefire terms, allowing immediate reporting of violations. While satellite images are useful, they cannot always distinguish between military and civilian movements. NGO reports and media coverage can be delayed or biased, making them less reliable for real‑time monitoring. Ground troops therefore offer the most comprehensive and timely oversight.
