What is Italian decolonization of Libya Somalia Ethiopia?

Italian decolonization resulted from Italy's defeat in World War II as Allied powers stripped Italy of its African empire, placing Libya and Somalia under UN trusteeship before granting independence while Ethiopia regained sovereignty after Italian occupation ended in 1941.
