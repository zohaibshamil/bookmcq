What is Tunisia and Morocco independence from France?

Tunisia and Morocco achieved independence from France in 1956 through diplomatic negotiations and moderate nationalist pressure rather than full-scale war, as France recognized that maintaining control was unsustainable and chose to preserve economic and cultural ties through peaceful transfer of sovereignty.

Q1. In which year did Tunisia achieve full independence under Habib Bourghiba?

A.1954
B.1955
C.1956 ✅
D.1957
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The correct answer is 1956 because Tunisia officially became independent in March 1956 after Bourghiba’s release and the formation of his government. Answers 1954 and 1955 are incorrect as they precede the actual declaration, and 1957 is after the fact. This date anchors the timeline of decolonization, showing how the end of French protectorate status fits into the broader post‑World War II independence wave.

Q2. Which political party led the Tunisian nationalist movement known as New Destour?

A.Istiqlal
B.Neo Destour
C.New Destour ✅
D.Democratic Constitutional
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: New Destour itself was the organization directing the Tunisian nationalist struggle, so option C is correct. Istiqlal is a Moroccan party, Neo Destour was a later Tunisian party after 1954, and Democratic Constitutional is unrelated. Knowing the correct party clarifies the actors involved, helping students differentiate between the parallel movements in Tunisia and Morocco.

Q13. Given 250,000 European settlers in Tunisia and 300,000 in Morocco in 1945, and assuming each colony had 5 million total inhabitants, which colony had the higher settler‑to‑population percentage?

B.Morocco ✅
C.Both equal
D.Not enough data
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The correct answer is B: \( \frac{250,000}{5,000,000}=5\% \) for Tunisia and \( \frac{300,000}{5,000,000}=6\% \) for Morocco, so Morocco’s settler proportion is higher. Options A and C are mathematically incorrect, and D is irrelevant because the needed data are provided. This calculation underscores how demographic ratios influenced French colonial policy.
