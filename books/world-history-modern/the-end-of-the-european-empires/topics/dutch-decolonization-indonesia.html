What is Dutch decolonization Indonesia?

Dutch decolonization of Indonesia followed four years of armed conflict and diplomatic pressure after Indonesian nationalists declared independence in 1945, ending when international condemnation and military stalemate forced the Netherlands to formally recognize Indonesian sovereignty in 1949.

Q1. In which year did Sukarno proclaim the independence of the Republic of Indonesia?

A.1945 ✅
B.1949
C.1950
D.1963
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The correct answer is 1945 because Sukarno announced independence on August 17, 1945, immediately after Japan's surrender. 1949 marks the year the Netherlands formally recognized Indonesian sovereignty, 1950 is when the United States of Indonesia became a republic, and 1963 is when West Irian was transferred. Understanding the exact year helps students see how quickly the nationalist movement moved from declaration to international recognition.
