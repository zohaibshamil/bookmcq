What is British Cyprus independence 1960?

British Cyprus gained independence in 1960 following years of Greek Cypriot demands for union with Greece and Turkish Cypriot opposition, resulting in a power-sharing constitution guaranteed by Britain, Greece, and Turkey that attempted to balance ethnic interests but left underlying tensions unresolved.
