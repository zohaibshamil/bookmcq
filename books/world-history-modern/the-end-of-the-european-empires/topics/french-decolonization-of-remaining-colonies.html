What is French decolonization of remaining colonies?

French decolonization of remaining sub-Saharan African colonies occurred rapidly in 1960 when France granted independence to fourteen territories simultaneously, choosing to maintain influence through economic agreements, military bases, and cultural ties rather than risk costly wars like Algeria.
