What is Korean War background and causes?

The Korean War began because Korea was divided at the 38th parallel after World War II into a Soviet-backed communist North and a US-backed capitalist South, creating intense ideological tension and border clashes that escalated when both sides claimed to be the legitimate government of the entire peninsula.
