What is US intervention in Southeast Asia Cold War?

US intervention in Southeast Asia during the Cold War was primarily motivated by containment and the domino theory, leading to direct wars in Korea and Vietnam as well as support for anti-communist regimes to prevent Soviet or Chinese influence from dominating the region.
