Q1. What year was the National Liberation Front (NLF) formed in South Vietnam?

🔹 A. \(1955\)
🔹 B. \(1960\)
🔹 C. \(1965\)
🔹 D. \(1970\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The NLF, also known as the Viet Cong, was established in \(1960\) as a political-military organization opposing the South Vietnamese government. This date is pivotal because it marks the formalization of organized resistance that later escalated into a full‑scale insurgency, influencing U.S. policy decisions throughout the conflict.

Q2. Which foreign power provided the most critical military aid to the Vietcong after \(1970\)?

🔹 A. China
🔹 B. Soviet Union
🔹 C. France
🔹 D. United Kingdom

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

After \(1970\), the Soviet Union became the primary supplier of sophisticated weaponry to the Vietcong, delivering rifles, machine guns, long‑range artillery, anti‑aircraft missiles, and tanks. This support bolstered the insurgents’ capabilities, allowing them to withstand intensified U.S. bombing campaigns and maintain operational effectiveness.

Q3. What type of warfare were the Vietcong experts in?

🔹 B. Guerrilla warfare
🔹 C. Naval warfare
🔹 D. Aerial bombing

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Vietcong mastered guerrilla warfare, leveraging small, mobile units that could blend into civilian populations and strike unpredictably. Their tactics contrasted sharply with the conventional, large‑scale battles familiar to U.S. forces, making it difficult for American troops to engage an enemy lacking a distinct uniform or fixed positions.

Q11. If the Vietcong received \(500{,}000\) rifles from the Soviet Union and each unit of 10 soldiers required 2 rifles, how many full units could be equipped?

🔹 A. \(25{,}000\)
🔹 B. \(50{,}000\)
🔹 C. \(100{,}000\)
🔹 D. \(250{,}000\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Dividing the total rifles by the requirement per unit (\(500{,}000 ÷ 2 = 250{,}000\) rifles for units of 10), we find that \(250{,}000 ÷ 10 = 25{,}000\) complete units could be equipped. However, the question asks for the number of full units, so the correct answer is \(25{,}000\) units, corresponding to option A.
