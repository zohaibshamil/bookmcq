What is Salvador Allende policies in Chile 1970-1973?

Salvador Allende pursued a democratic socialist agenda known as the Chilean Way to Socialism, which included nationalizing copper mines, redistributing land, and expanding social welfare programs, but faced severe economic instability and fierce opposition from domestic elites and the United States.

📝 All Salvador Allende policies in Chile 1970-1973 MCQs

Q1. What percentage of the popular vote did Salvador Allende obtain in the September 1970 election?

A.0.34
B.0.36 ✅
C.0.38
D.0.4
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Allende won a narrow victory with \(36\%\) of the votes, edging out his nearest rival who received \(35\%\). This modest plurality was enough to make him president, marking the first democratic election of a Marxist leader in modern history.

Q2. Which political parties comprised the Unidad Popular coalition that supported Allende?

A.Communist Party, Socialist Party, Radical Party, Christian Democrats
B.Communist Party, Socialist Party, Radical Party, Social Democrats
C.Communist Party, Socialist Party, Radical Party, Social Democrats ✅
D.Communist Party, Socialist Party, Radical Party, Green Party
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The Unidad Popular was a left‑wing alliance that brought together the Communist Party, the Socialist Party, the Radical Party, and a group of Social Democrats. This coalition enabled Allende to secure a parliamentary majority despite his modest vote share.

Q3. Under the Chilean constitution of the 1960s, what restriction was placed on the president regarding re‑election?

A.The president could not stand for immediate re‑election ✅
B.The president could serve unlimited consecutive terms
C.The president could be re‑elected only after a five‑year gap
D.There were no restrictions on re‑election
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The constitution mirrored the U.S. system in many ways but added a clause preventing a president from seeking immediate re‑election. This rule meant that any leader, including Allende, had only a single term to implement his agenda before stepping down or being replaced.

Q6. Approximately how many private land holdings were confiscated and redistributed under Frei’s land reform?

Q11. If inflation fell from \(38\%\) to \(25\%\) over three years under Frei, what was the average annual reduction assuming a linear trend?

B.\(4.33\%\) per year
C.\(5\%\) per year
D.\(6\%\) per year ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The total decline is \(38\%-25\%=13\%\). Dividing by three years yields an average decrease of approximately \(13/3 \approx 4.33\%\) per year. Rounded to two decimal places, the answer is \(4.33\%\) per year, which corresponds to option D.

Q15. If Allende imposed price ceilings on staple foods, which economic outcome would most likely occur?

📖 Explanation: Price ceilings set below market equilibrium create excess demand, leading to shortages as producers cannot profitably supply the required quantities. This classic result of a price ceiling aligns with option A, indicating likely scarcity of basic items.

Q21. Evaluating the nationalization of half of American‑owned copper mines, how would this affect Chile’s balance of payments if copper exports constitute \(30\%\) of GDP and previously remitted profits were \(10\%\) of GDP?

Q23. If public spending rose by \(15\%\) of GDP and tax‑collection efficiency increased from \(60\%\) to \(70\%\) of a baseline revenue of \(50\%\) of GDP, what is the net fiscal gap?

A.\(5\%\) of GDP deficit
B.\(10\%\) of GDP deficit
C.\(15\%\) of GDP deficit ✅
D.Balanced budget
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Baseline revenue = \(0.5\) GDP. Improved efficiency yields \(0.7 \times 0.5 = 0.35\) GDP, a gain of \(0.05\) GDP. Adding spending of \(0.15\) GDP creates a net deficit of \(0.15 - 0.05 = 0.10\) GDP, i.e., a \(10\%\) of GDP shortfall, matching option C.

Q31. If a fiscal plan cuts taxes on the wealthy by \(10\%\) of GDP while raising social spending by \(12\%\) of GDP, what is the likely effect on the budget balance?

B.\(2\%\) of GDP deficit
C.Balanced budget ✅
D.\(5\%\) of GDP deficit
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The net fiscal impact equals the increase in spending minus the revenue loss: \(12\% - 10\% = 2\%\) of GDP deficit. Therefore, the budget would move into a shortfall of roughly \(2\%\) of GDP, matching option C.
