What is US involvement in Vietnam War?

US involvement in the Vietnam War escalated from providing financial aid to France to deploying over half a million combat troops, driven by the domino theory which held that preventing a communist takeover in Vietnam was essential to stopping the spread of communism across Southeast Asia.
