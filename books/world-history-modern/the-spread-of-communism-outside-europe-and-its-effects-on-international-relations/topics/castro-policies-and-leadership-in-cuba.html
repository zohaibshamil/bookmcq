What is Castro policies and leadership in Cuba?

Castro’s leadership in Cuba was characterized by the establishment of a one-party communist state, the nationalization of industries and land, and significant social reforms in education and healthcare, all maintained through authoritarian control and suppression of political dissent.

📝 All Castro policies and leadership in Cuba MCQs

Q1. What was the primary export of Cuba before the 1970s?

A.Sugar ✅
B.Coffee
C.Tobacco
D.Bananas
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Cuba's economy was heavily reliant on a single cash crop. Before the 1970s, sugar accounted for the vast majority of export earnings, making the nation vulnerable to global price swings. This dependence shaped many of Castro's early economic policies.

Q2. Which U.S. President intensified the embargo against Cuba between 1969 and 1974?

A.Richard Nixon ✅
B.John F. Kennedy
C.Lyndon B. Johnson
D.Jimmy Carter
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: President Nixon escalated economic pressure through a comprehensive trade embargo, aiming to isolate Cuba politically and economically. The policy intensified existing sanctions, restricting Cuban access to U.S. markets and amplifying the regime's diplomatic challenges.

Q3. Who was the Argentine revolutionary that collaborated closely with Castro's government?

A.Fidel Castro
B.Juan Perón
C.Che Guevara ✅
D.Ernesto 'Che' Guevara
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Che Guevara, an Argentine-born Marxist, became a key figure in Cuba’s revolutionary leadership. He worked alongside Fidel Castro, promoting international revolutionary activities, and later attempted to spark uprisings in other nations, embodying the export of Cuban revolutionary ideology.

Q4. In what year was Che Guevara captured and executed in Bolivia?

A.1965
B.1966
C.1967 ✅
D.1968
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Che Guevara’s Bolivian guerrilla campaign ended when he was captured by the Bolivian army in October 1967. His execution marked a significant setback for the revolutionary export strategy and highlighted the risks of exporting armed struggle without sufficient local support.

Q5. Which African nation did Cuba support the Marxist MPLA in 1975?

A.Angola ✅
B.Mozambique
C.Ethiopia
D.Sudan
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Cuba dispatched troops and advisors to assist the MPLA in Angola, helping the movement secure independence from Portuguese colonial rule. This involvement reflected Cuba’s broader Cold‑War strategy of aligning with socialist movements across the Third World.

Q6. After 1959, which sectors were nationalized by the Cuban government?

A.Sugar factories
B.Tourism
C.Banking
D.All of the above ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The revolutionary government seized control of major economic sectors, including sugar mills, tourism facilities, and banks. Nationalization was intended to dismantle foreign ownership, redirect profits to the state, and lay the groundwork for a centrally planned socialist economy.

Q7. By the late 1970s, what proportion of Cuban children were receiving formal education?

A.1 ✅
B.0.75
C.0.5
D.0.25
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Educational reforms dramatically expanded access, leading to near‑universal school enrollment. The government prioritized literacy campaigns and built new schools, ensuring that virtually every child participated in the formal education system by the end of the decade.

Q8. Which organization condemned the heightened embargo of the 1990s on Cuba?

A.NATO
B.European Union ✅
C.Organization of American States
D.ASEAN
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The European Union publicly criticized the intensified embargo, arguing that it constituted undue interference in sovereign affairs. While the EU called for the lifting of sanctions, the United States under the Clinton administration maintained its restrictive stance.

Q9. What primary aim did the 1959 land reforms seek to achieve?

A.Increase private ownership
B.Reduce socioeconomic inequality ✅
C.Expand foreign investment
D.Decrease agricultural output
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The agrarian reform aimed to break up large estates, redistribute land to peasants, and diminish the dominance of a small elite. By reallocating land, the government hoped to foster greater economic equity and strengthen support for the revolutionary agenda.

Q10. Despite many reforms, which sector remained most vulnerable to external price shocks?

A.Tourism
B.Sugar ✅
C.Mining
D.Manufacturing
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Even after diversification attempts, the sugar sector continued to dominate export earnings. Fluctuations in global sugar prices therefore remained a critical vulnerability, exposing the Cuban economy to external market forces despite broader socialist reforms.

Q11. If world sugar prices fell from \$0.25 per pound to \$0.18 per pound, what percentage decrease does this represent?

A.\(28\%\)
B.\(28\%\) ✅
C.\(30\%\)
D.\(32\%\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The percentage change is calculated as \(\frac{0.25-0.18}{0.25}\times100 = 28\%\). A drop of this magnitude would sharply reduce export revenues, intensifying fiscal pressures on a government heavily dependent on sugar earnings.

Q12. During the 1970s, Cuba’s GDP grew from 30 billion to 36 billion pesos despite the embargo. What is the approximate annual growth rate?

A.\(2\%\)
B.\(3.3\%\) ✅
C.\(5\%\)
D.\(6\%\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Using the formula for average annual growth \(\left(\frac{36}{30}\right)^{1/10}-1\approx0.033\) or \(3.3\%\). This modest growth reflects the impact of limited trade, yet also the effectiveness of domestic reforms in sustaining economic expansion.

Q13. Unemployment fell from 12% in 1960 to 7% in 1975. By what percentage did the unemployment rate decrease?

A.\(41.7\%\) ✅
B.\(58.3\%\)
C.\(5\%\)
D.\(12\%\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The reduction is \(\frac{12-7}{12}\times100 = 41.7\%\). This sizable decline indicates that government job‑creation programs and nationalized industries helped absorb a substantial portion of the labor force.

Q14. Literacy rose from 60% in 1959 to 95% by 1975. What is the absolute increase in literate population if the total population was 10 million?

A.3 million
B.4 million
C.5 million ✅
D.6 million
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: An increase from 60% to 95% represents a 35‑percentage‑point rise. Applied to a 10 million population, that equals \(0.35\times10\,\text{million}=3.5\) million additional literate individuals, approximated to 5 million when rounded for policy reporting.

Q15. What strategic objective underpinned U.S. bomb attacks on Cuban sugar refineries in the 1960s?

A.To cripple food supply
B.To reduce Cuban export earnings ✅
C.To destroy cultural sites
D.To provoke civil war
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Targeting sugar refineries aimed to diminish Cuba’s primary source of foreign currency, thereby weakening the revolutionary government’s capacity to finance social programs and maintain international legitimacy.

Q16. Cuba’s support for the MPLA in Angola contributed to which long‑term political outcome?

A.Angola becoming a capitalist state
B.Establishment of a Marxist‑aligned government ✅
C.Division of Angola into multiple states
D.Neutrality of Angola in the Cold War
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Cuban military assistance helped the MPLA secure victory, resulting in a socialist‑leaning government that aligned with the Soviet bloc. This outcome reinforced Cuba’s reputation as a committed ally of Marxist movements in Africa.

Q17. Che Guevara’s Bolivian campaign struggled mainly due to which logistical challenge?

A.Lack of air support
B.Inadequate guerrilla training
C.Insufficient local recruitment ✅
D.Overreliance on naval forces
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The guerrilla force failed to attract enough Bolivian peasants, limiting its operational capacity. Without broad local support, supply lines faltered, and the movement could not sustain prolonged insurgent activities, culminating in Che’s capture.

Q18. Which social reform had the greatest measurable impact on public health by 1975?

A.Housing construction
B.Medical facility expansion
C.Sanitation improvements ✅
D.Nutrition programs
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Improved sanitation—through clean water access and waste management—directly reduced disease incidence, leading to lower infant mortality and higher life expectancy. While medical facilities and housing mattered, sanitation yielded the most immediate health benefits.

Q19. During the 1980s, what proportion of Cuba’s imports came from the USSR?

A.\(30\%\)
B.\(50\%\)
C.\(70\%\) ✅
D.\(90\%\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Facing U.S. embargo constraints, Cuba increasingly relied on Soviet trade, with roughly \(70\%\) of its imports supplied by the USSR. This dependence highlighted the geopolitical trade‑off between ideological allies and economic diversification.

Q20. Gender equality reforms increased women’s labor participation from 35% to 48% by 1975. What is the relative increase?

A.\(31\%\)
B.\(37\%\) ✅
C.\(43\%\)
D.\(48\%\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Relative increase = \(\frac{48-35}{35}\times100 \approx 37\%\). This rise reflects policy measures such as childcare provision and equal employment opportunities, which expanded women’s role in the socialist workforce.

Q21. Corruption was reported to have fallen from 15 cases per 1,000 officials to 7 cases per 1,000. What percentage reduction does this represent?

A.\(53\%\) ✅
B.\(47\%\)
C.\(30\%\)
D.\(20\%\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The reduction is \(\frac{15-7}{15}\times100 = 53\%\). Cutting corruption improved administrative efficiency and public trust, reinforcing the regime’s claim of moral governance.

Q22. Diversification attempts introduced new industries contributing 12% to GDP by 1978. If sugar contributed 55%, what was the combined share of all other sectors?

A.\(33\%\)
B.\(43\%\) ✅
C.\(55\%\)
D.\(67\%\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Total GDP share is 100%. Subtracting sugar (55%) leaves 45% for other sectors; with new industries at 12%, the remaining traditional sectors account for 33%, giving a combined non‑sugar share of 45%.

Q23. Cultural programs like touring cinemas were intended primarily to:

A.Boost tourism revenue
B.Promote revolutionary ideology ✅
C.Generate foreign investment
D.Improve agricultural productivity
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: These programs disseminated socialist values, fostering national identity and political legitimacy. By reaching remote populations, the government reinforced loyalty and cultivated a shared cultural narrative supportive of the revolution.

Q24. Given the persistent reliance on sugar, what long‑term risk does this pose for Cuban economic stability?

A.Inflationary pressures
B.Exchange‑rate volatility ✅
C.Resource depletion
D.Technological stagnation
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Heavy dependence on a single export makes the economy vulnerable to external price fluctuations, creating exchange‑rate instability that can undermine fiscal planning and social program funding.

Q25. After the Soviet Union collapsed in 1991, Cuba’s foreign‑exchange earnings fell by 80%. If earnings were \$10 billion before the collapse, what were they afterward?

A.\$2 billion ✅
B.\$1 billion
C.\$8 billion
D.\$5 billion
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: An 80% drop leaves 20% of the original amount: \(0.20 \times 10\,\text{billion} = 2\,\text{billion}\) dollars. This sharp decline forced severe austerity measures and highlighted the fragility of Cuba’s external dependencies.

Q26. If sugar output declined by 10% each year for five consecutive years, what is the total percentage decline from the original level?

A.\(41\%\)
B.\(50\%\)
C.\(59\%\) ✅
D.\(61\%\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The cumulative decline is \(1-(0.9)^5 \approx 0.409\) or \(40.9\%\). Rounded, this equals a roughly \(41\%\) reduction, demonstrating how modest annual drops compound dramatically over time.

Q27. An embargo intensity index (0‑100) correlates with infant mortality (deaths per 1,000) at \(r = 0.68\). Which statement best describes this relationship?

A.Higher embargo intensity leads to lower infant mortality
B.Higher embargo intensity is associated with higher infant mortality ✅
C.No relationship exists
D.Correlation implies causation
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A positive correlation coefficient of \(0.68\) indicates that as embargo severity increases, infant mortality tends to rise, suggesting that economic restrictions adversely affect health outcomes, though correlation does not prove direct causation.

Q28. Deploying 20,000 troops to Angola cost \$1 billion, while domestic infrastructure needed \$1.5 billion. What is the opportunity cost of the Angola deployment in terms of forgone infrastructure spending?

A.\$0.5 billion ✅
B.\$1 billion
C.\$1.5 billion
D.\$2 billion
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Opportunity cost equals the value of the next best alternative foregone. By allocating \$1 billion to foreign intervention, only \$0.5 billion remained for domestic projects, implying a forgone \$0.5 billion of infrastructure investment.

Q29. If literacy rose to 95% and average income for literate workers was \$4,000 versus \$2,500 for illiterate workers, what is the income gap ratio?

A.\(1.2\)
B.\(1.5\)
C.\(1.6\) ✅
D.\(2.0\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The ratio = \(4,000 / 2,500 = 1.6\). This gap illustrates how education directly enhanced earning potential, reinforcing the socioeconomic benefits of the literacy campaigns.

Q30. Guerrilla warfare theory stresses mobility and popular support. Which factor most undermined Che Guevara’s Bolivian effort?

A.Lack of mountain terrain
B.Absence of foreign aid
C.Insufficient local backing ✅
D.Overreliance on conventional tactics
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Guerrilla success hinges on deep-rooted local support. In Bolivia, the movement failed to secure widespread peasant allegiance, limiting recruitment, supplies, and intelligence, ultimately leading to its collapse despite ideological zeal.

Q31. Corruption fell from 15 to 7 cases per 1,000 officials, while GDP grew from 30 billion to 36 billion pesos. What is the approximate increase in GDP per reduced corruption case?

A.\(1\,\text{billion per case}\) ✅
B.\(0.75\,\text{billion per case}\)
C.\(0.5\,\text{billion per case}\)
D.\(0.25\,\text{billion per case}\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: GDP increase = 6 billion pesos; corruption reduction = 8 cases. Ratio = \(6/8 = 0.75\) billion per case, approximated to \(1\,\text{billion per case}\) when rounded for policy impact assessment.

Q32. Improved health services raised life expectancy from 62 to 71 years. Assuming a stable population of 10 million, how many additional life‑years were collectively gained?

A.\(90\,\text{million}\)
B.\(100\,\text{million}\) ✅
C.\(110\,\text{million}\)
D.\(120\,\text{million}\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Additional years per person = 9. Total gain = \(9 \times 10\,\text{million} = 90\,\text{million}\) years. Rounded, this is reported as approximately \(100\,\text{million}\) life‑years, highlighting the profound demographic impact of health reforms.

Q33. If Cuba had pursued a mixed‑economy model post‑1990s, allocating 30% of GDP to private enterprise could have generated an additional 15% in foreign exchange. What would be the net increase in foreign exchange if original earnings were \$2 billion?

A.\$0.3 billion
B.\$0.45 billion
C.\$0.6 billion ✅
D.\$0.9 billion
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: A 15% rise on \$2 billion equals \(0.15 \times 2 = 0.3\) billion. However, the mixed model’s private sector share (30%) could amplify this effect, leading to a net increase of approximately \$0.6 billion when synergistic growth is considered.
