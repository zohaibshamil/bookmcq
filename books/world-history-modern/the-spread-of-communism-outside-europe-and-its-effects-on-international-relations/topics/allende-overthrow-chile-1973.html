What is Allende overthrow Chile 1973?

Allende was overthrown in a violent military coup on September 11, 1973, orchestrated by General Augusto Pinochet with covert support from the CIA, ending Chile’s democratic experiment and replacing it with a brutal right-wing dictatorship.

Q1. What was one major economic effect of land redistribution under Allende?

A. Increase production
B. Decrease production ✅
C. No change
D. Export boom

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The redistribution displaced many farmers, prompting them to stop sowing and often slaughter their livestock. This abrupt halt in agricultural activity led to a noticeable decline in overall production, contributing to food shortages and heightened economic strain across the country.
