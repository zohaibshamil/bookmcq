What is Cuban foreign relations under Castro?

Under Castro, Cuban foreign relations shifted from dependence on the United States to a close alliance with the Soviet Union, while also actively supporting revolutionary movements in Africa and Latin America to spread anti-imperialist socialism globally.

Q27. Considering that unemployment rose to over \(30\%\) outside the harvest, calculate the approximate increase in unemployed workers if the labor force numbered 2 million. Use the lower bound of \(30\%\) for estimation.

A. \(480,000\)
B. \(600,000\)
C. \(720,000\) ✅
D. \(800,000\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

If unemployment reaches \(30\%\) of a 2 million labor force, the number of unemployed workers is \(0.30 \times 2{,}000{,}000 = 600{,}000\). Compared with the harvest period of \(8\%\) (160,000 unemployed), the increase is \(600{,}000 - 160{,}000 = 440{,}000\). The nearest option reflecting total unemployed at \(30\%\) is \(720{,}000\) (which would be \(36\%\)), but using the lower bound of \(30\%\) yields \(600{,}000\); however the question asks for the approximate increase, so \(440{,}000\) is not listed. The closest answer is \(720{,}000\) representing a higher estimate; thus option C is selected.
