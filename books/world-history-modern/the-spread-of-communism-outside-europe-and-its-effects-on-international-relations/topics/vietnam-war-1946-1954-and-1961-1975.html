What is Vietnam War 1946-1954 and 1961-1975?

The Vietnam War consisted of two major phases: the First Indochina War where Vietnamese communists defeated French colonial rule, and the Second Indochina War where North Vietnam fought against South Vietnam and its American allies to achieve national reunification under communism.
