What is US Soviet relations 1970-1990?

US-Soviet relations from 1970 to 1990 fluctuated between periods of détente featuring arms control agreements and renewed tensions caused by proxy wars and human rights disputes, ultimately ending with Soviet collapse and the conclusion of the Cold War.

Q1. In which year was the “hotline” telephone link between the USSR and the USA first established?

A.1960
B.1961
C.1963 ✅
D.1965
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The direct communication line, called the “hotline,” was created after the Cuban Missile Crisis to allow rapid contact between the superpowers. It became operational in 1963, providing a channel that could prevent accidental escalation by enabling immediate dialogue between leaders during tense moments.

Q2. Which treaty, signed in 1967, prohibited the placement of nuclear weapons in outer space?

A.Treaty of Moscow
B.Outer Space Nuclear Ban Treaty
C.Outer Space Treaty ✅
D.Space Weapons Prohibition Act
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The 1967 agreement that banned the deployment of nuclear weapons in outer space is commonly referred to as the Outer Space Treaty. It was a key element of détente, preventing the militarization of space and limiting both superpowers from placing nuclear arms beyond Earth’s atmosphere.

Q3. The Strategic Arms Limitation Treaty (SALT I) was signed in which year?

A.1969
B.1970
C.1972 ✅
D.1974
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: SALT I was concluded in 1972 after lengthy negotiations between the United States and the Soviet Union. The treaty set limits on the number of ABMs, ICBMs, and SLBMs each side could possess, marking a milestone in arms control even though it did not curb the overall size of each arsenal.

Q4. The Helsinki Agreement of 1975 recognized the post‑World War II division of which European country?

A.Poland
B.Czechoslovakia
C.Germany ✅
D.Austria
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The Helsinki Final Act, signed in 1975, included a provision that accepted the existing borders in Europe, notably confirming the division of Germany into East and West. This acknowledgment helped stabilize the continent and reduced a source of potential conflict between the blocs.

Q5. Which Soviet leader met President Reagan in Geneva in November 1985?

Q6. What was the popular nickname for President Reagan’s Strategic Defence Initiative?

Q11. How did SALT I influence the overall pace of the arms race?

A.It eliminated all nuclear weapons
B.It slowed the growth of arsenals without reducing existing stockpiles ✅
C.It increased the number of deployed warheads

By limiting expansion, the treaty slowed the rapid growth of each side’s nuclear arsenal, contributing to a more measured arms race.

Q23. Gorbachev’s 1985 joint statement with Reagan that ‘nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought’ primarily reflects which diplomatic principle?

A.Realpolitik
B.Deterrence theory
C.Mutual assured destruction (MAD) ✅

By publicly acknowledging this, the leaders reinforced the logic that restraint and arms‑control were essential for survival.
