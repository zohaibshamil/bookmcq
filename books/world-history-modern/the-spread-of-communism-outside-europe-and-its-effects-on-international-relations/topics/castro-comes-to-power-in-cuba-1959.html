What is Castro comes to power in Cuba 1959?

Fidel Castro came to power in Cuba in 1959 by leading a guerrilla revolution that overthrew the US-backed dictator Fulgencio Batista, initially promising democratic reform but eventually aligning with Marxism-Leninism due to domestic opposition and hostile US policies.

Q1. In which year did Fidel Castro finally assume power in Cuba?

A. 1956
B. 1957
C. 1958
D. 1959 ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Fidel Castro's guerrilla forces entered Havana on January 1, 1959, and Batista fled the country on January 2. By early January, the revolutionary junta led by Castro had taken control of the government, marking the official start of his rule. This date is widely recognized as the moment when Castro assumed power.

Q2. Which American‑owned sector held a controlling interest in Cuba’s sugar industry before 1959?

A. Oil
B. Banking
C. Sugar ✅
D. Textile

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

American companies dominated the Cuban sugar sector, owning the plantations, processing mills, and export facilities. Their control meant that the majority of profits from sugar production flowed to U.S. interests, reinforcing Cuban dependence on the United States and fueling nationalist resentment that later motivated revolutionary action.

Q4. Fulgencio Batista seized power in a coup in which year?
