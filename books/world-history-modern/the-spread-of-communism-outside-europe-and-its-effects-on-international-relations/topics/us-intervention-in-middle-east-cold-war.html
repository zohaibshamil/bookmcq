What is US intervention in Middle East Cold War?

US intervention in the Middle East during the Cold War focused on securing oil supplies and containing Soviet expansion by supporting authoritarian allies like Iran and Saudi Arabia, intervening in regional conflicts, and promoting pro-Western stability over democratic development.
