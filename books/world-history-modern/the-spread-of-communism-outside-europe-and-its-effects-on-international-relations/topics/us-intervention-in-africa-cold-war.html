What is US intervention in Africa Cold War?

US intervention in Africa during the Cold War involved supporting anti-communist governments and rebel groups in proxy conflicts like those in Angola and Ethiopia, often prioritizing geopolitical alignment over human rights or democratic governance to counter Soviet and Cuban influence.

Q1. In which year did the Congo gain independence from Belgium?

A.1955
B.1960 ✅
C.1965
D.1970
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Congo became independent in June 1960, marking a pivotal moment in African decolonization. This event followed growing nationalist pressures and set the stage for subsequent Cold War involvement, as both the United States and Belgium sought to influence the new nation’s political trajectory.

Q2. Who was the first Prime Minister of the independent Congo?

A.Joseph Mobutu
B.Patrice Lumumba ✅
C.Laurent Kabila
D.Moïse Tshombe
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Patrice Lumumba served as the inaugural Prime Minister after independence. His advocacy for economic independence and appeal to the UN and USSR alarmed Western powers, leading to CIA and Belgian actions that ultimately resulted in his removal and assassination in early 1961.

Q3. Which African country gained independence in 1957 under Kwame Nkrumah?

A.Nigeria
B.Kenya
C.Ghana ✅
D.Uganda
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Ghana achieved independence in 1957 with Kwame Nkrumah as its first Prime Minister. Nkrumah pursued a non-aligned stance, seeking balanced relations with both Western and communist blocs, a policy that later provoked covert CIA involvement and a military coup in 1966.

Q4. What was the main mineral export of the Congo that attracted foreign interest?

A.Gold
B.Diamond
C.Copper ✅
D.Oil
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Copper was the Congo’s primary export, especially from the mineral‑rich Katanga province. Control over copper mining became a strategic economic concern for Belgium and American interests, influencing their support for secessionist movements and shaping Cold War dynamics in Central Africa.

Q5. Which Cold War superpower did Lumumba appeal to after the UN failed to help?

C.China
D.France
