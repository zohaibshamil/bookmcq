What is Why did North Korea invade South Korea 1950?

North Korea invaded South Korea in June 1950 because leader Kim Il-sung believed he could quickly reunify the country under communism with the approval of Stalin and Mao, mistakenly assuming the United States would not intervene to defend the South.
