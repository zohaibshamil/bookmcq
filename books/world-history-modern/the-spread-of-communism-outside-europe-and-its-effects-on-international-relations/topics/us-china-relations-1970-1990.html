What is US China relations 1970-1990?

US-China relations transformed from hostility to strategic partnership beginning with Nixon’s 1972 visit, as both nations sought to counterbalance Soviet power, leading to diplomatic normalization in 1979 and growing economic ties despite ongoing ideological differences.
