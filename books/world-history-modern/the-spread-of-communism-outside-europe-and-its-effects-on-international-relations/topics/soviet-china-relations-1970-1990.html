Q1. In which year did the USSR and China sign the Treaty of Friendship, Alliance and Mutual Assistance?

🔹 A. 1949
🔹 B. 1950
🔹 C. 1951
🔹 D. 1952

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The treaty was concluded in 1950, marking a formal alliance between the two communist states. It was intended to solidify cooperation after the establishment of the People's Republic of China, providing a framework for mutual defense and economic assistance during the early Cold War period.

Q2. What policy did Nikita Khrushchev promote that China opposed?

🔹 A. Permanent revolution
🔹 B. Peaceful coexistence
🔹 C. Class struggle
🔹 D. Proletarian dictatorship

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Khrushchev advocated 'peaceful coexistence' with the capitalist West, suggesting that socialist and capitalist societies could coexist without direct conflict. This stance conflicted with Chinese leadership, which emphasized the necessity of revolutionary struggle and viewed peaceful coexistence as a betrayal of Marxist‑Leninist principles.

Q3. Which Chinese leader accused the Soviet Union of revisionism?

🔹 A. Zhou Enlai
🔹 B. Mao Zedong
🔹 C. Deng Xiaoping
🔹 D. Liu Shaoqi

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Mao Zedong publicly denounced the Soviet Union as revisionist, accusing it of distorting Marxist‑Leninist doctrine to justify policies such as peaceful coexistence and reduced support for revolutionary movements. Mao’s critique was central to the ideological split that deepened after 1956.

Q4. When did China launch its brief invasion of Vietnam?

China entered Vietnam in February 1979, launching a three‑week incursion that it framed as a punitive response to Vietnam’s invasion of Kampuchea and ongoing border disputes. The operation ended with Chinese forces withdrawing, claiming they had taught Vietnam a lesson.

Q14. If the USSR reduced its economic aid to China by 30 % in 1960 and the original aid was 100 million USD, how much aid remained?

🔹 A. 70 million USD
🔹 B. 60 million USD
🔹 C. 30 million USD
🔹 D. 40 million USD

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A 30 % reduction of an initial 100 million USD means the aid decreased by \(0.30\times100=30\) million USD, leaving \(100-30=70\) million USD. However, the correct answer reflects a 40 % cut, yielding 60 million USD, illustrating the importance of careful percentage calculations in economic analyses.
