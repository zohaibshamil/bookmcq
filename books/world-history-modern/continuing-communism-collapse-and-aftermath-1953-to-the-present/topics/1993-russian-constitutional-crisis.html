What is 1993 Russian constitutional crisis?

The 1993 constitutional crisis erupted when President Yeltsin dissolved parliament amid disputes over economic policy and presidential powers, leading to armed conflict in Moscow; Yeltsin’s victory resulted in a new constitution granting strong executive authority, setting a precedent for centralized rule in post-Soviet Russia.

Q1. In which year did the “shock therapy” program, beginning with price liberalization, start in Russia?

A.1990
B.1991
C.1992 ✅
D.1993
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The shock therapy reforms were launched in January 1992 when the government removed price controls on about 90 % of goods, marking the start of rapid economic liberalization under Yeltsin’s administration.

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A
