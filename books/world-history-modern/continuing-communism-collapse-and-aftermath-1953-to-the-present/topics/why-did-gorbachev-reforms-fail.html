Q1. In which year did Mikhail Gorbachev become the leader of the Soviet Union?

🔹 A. 1985
🔹 B. 1983
🔹 C. 1990
🔹 D. 1979

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Gorbachev was appointed General Secretary of the Communist Party in March \(1985\), marking the start of his reform agenda that included glasnost and perestroika, aimed at revitalizing the Soviet system.

Q2. What does the term “glasnost” refer to in Gorbachev’s reform program?

🔹 A. Openness and transparency
🔹 B. Economic restructuring
🔹 C. Military expansion
🔹 D. Cultural isolation

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Glasnost, meaning “openness,” was introduced to increase freedom of information, encourage public debate, and reduce censorship, allowing Soviet citizens to discuss political and social issues more openly.

Q3. Perestroika primarily sought to reform which aspect of Soviet society?

🔹 A. Political ideology
🔹 B. Economic management
🔹 C. Military doctrine
🔹 D. Religious policy

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Perestroika, meaning “restructuring,” focused on changing the centrally planned economy by introducing market mechanisms, reducing state control, and encouraging efficiency and productivity.
