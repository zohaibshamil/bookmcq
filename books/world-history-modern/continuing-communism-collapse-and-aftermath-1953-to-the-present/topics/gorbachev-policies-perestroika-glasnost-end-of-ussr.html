What is Gorbachev policies Perestroika Glasnost end of USSR?

Mikhail Gorbachev introduced perestroika (economic restructuring) and glasnost (political openness) to revitalize socialism, but these reforms unintentionally unleashed nationalist movements, exposed systemic failures, and weakened central authority, culminating in the dissolution of the Soviet Union in December 1991.

Q2. What does the term glasnost refer to in Gorbachev's reform agenda?

A.A policy of economic liberalization
B.A policy of political openness ✅
C.A policy of military expansion
D.A policy of cultural isolation
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Glasnost, meaning “openness,” was introduced by Gorbachev to increase transparency and freedom of information within Soviet society. It allowed previously censored topics such as human rights, cultural affairs, and criticism of the government to be discussed publicly. This shift aimed to foster a more informed citizenry and reduce the climate of fear that had prevailed under earlier regimes.

Q3. Perestroika primarily targeted which aspect of Soviet life?

A.Cultural expression
B.Military strategy
C.Economic restructuring ✅
D.Foreign diplomacy
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Perestroika, translating to “restructuring,” was Gorbachev’s initiative to overhaul the Soviet economic system. It sought to introduce market-like mechanisms, reduce central planning rigidity, and improve efficiency in production. While political reforms accompanied it, the core focus remained on revitalizing the economy, encouraging limited private enterprise, and addressing chronic shortages of consumer goods.
