What is Brezhnev era Soviet Union 1964-1982?

The Brezhnev era was characterized by political stability, economic stagnation, military buildup, and suppression of dissent, as the leadership prioritized maintaining the status quo over reform, leading to declining productivity, corruption, and growing public disillusionment despite superficial prosperity and détente with the West.

Q1. Who had solidified personal control over the Soviet Union by 1977?

A.Kosygin
B.Brezhnev ✅
C.Podgorny
D.Gorbachev
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Brezhnev’s gradual accumulation of power after Khrushchev’s ouster, combined with his ability to outmaneuver rivals like Kosygin and Podgorny, allowed him to become the undisputed leader by 1977, marking the start of the period commonly referred to as the Brezhnev era.

Q2. Which economic reform did Alexei Kosygin champion that later fell out of favor?

A.Centralization of planning
B.Decentralization of decision‑making ✅
C.Nationalization of farms
D.Collectivization of industry
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Kosygin pushed for a more decentralized economic system, arguing that local enterprises should have greater autonomy. This stance clashed with the prevailing belief among senior officials that too much independence could foster dissent, especially after the Prague Spring, leading to the abandonment of his proposals.

Q3. What was Brezhnev’s primary concern regarding the Soviet ruling elite?

B.Keeping the nomenklatura satisfied ✅
C.Reducing military expenditure
D.Improving agricultural output
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Brezhnev focused on maintaining the loyalty of the nomenklatura, the entrenched bureaucracy that managed party and state affairs. By ensuring their material interests and privileges remained intact, he aimed to preserve political stability and prevent challenges to his authority.
