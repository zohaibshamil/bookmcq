Q1. On what date was the joint US‑UK operation against Afghanistan launched?

The operation began on \(7\) October \(2001\), marking the start of the military response to the September 11 attacks. This date set the timeline for subsequent air strikes and ground offensives that eventually led to the Taliban’s displacement from key cities.
