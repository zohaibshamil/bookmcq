What is US invasion of Afghanistan 2001 background?

The U.S. invasion of Afghanistan in 2001 was launched in response to the 9/11 attacks after the Taliban regime refused to extradite Osama bin Laden, marking the beginning of Operation Enduring Freedom with the goal of destroying Al-Qaeda’s safe haven and removing the Taliban from power.
