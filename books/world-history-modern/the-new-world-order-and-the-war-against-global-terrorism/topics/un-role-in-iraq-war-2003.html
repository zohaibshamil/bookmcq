What is UN role in Iraq War 2003?

The United Nations played a limited and contested role in the 2003 Iraq War, as the U.S. and UK proceeded without explicit Security Council authorization after failing to secure a second resolution, though the UN later assisted with humanitarian aid, elections, and reconstruction efforts in post-invasion Iraq.
