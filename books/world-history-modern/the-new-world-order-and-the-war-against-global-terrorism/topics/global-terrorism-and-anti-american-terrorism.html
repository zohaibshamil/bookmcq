What is Global terrorism and anti-American terrorism?

Global terrorism refers to transnational violent attacks that cross borders and target international interests, while anti-American terrorism specifically focuses on hostility toward U.S. foreign policy, military presence, and cultural influence, often stemming from grievances over American interventions in the Middle East and support for authoritarian regimes.
