Q1. In which year did Colonel Muammar Gaddafi seize power in Libya?

🔹 A. 1969
🔹 B. 1971
🔹 C. 1973
🔹 D. 1975

📖 Explanation:

The coup occurred in 1969 when Gaddafi and a group of junior officers took advantage of King Idris's absence, establishing the Libyan Arab Republic without armed conflict.

Q2. Who was the monarch of Libya before Gaddafi's coup?

🔹 A. King Hassan
🔹 B. King Idris
🔹 C. King Abdullah
🔹 D. King Faisal

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

King Idris was the reigning monarch whose pro‑Western stance and absence for medical treatment created the opening for Gaddafi's bloodless takeover.

Q3. What type of coup did Gaddafi lead in 1969?

🔹 A. Violent
🔹 B. Partial
🔹 C. Foreign
🔹 D. Bloodless

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The 1969 takeover was characterized by a lack of armed confrontation, allowing the new regime to assume control without casualties, which is why it is described as a bloodless coup.
