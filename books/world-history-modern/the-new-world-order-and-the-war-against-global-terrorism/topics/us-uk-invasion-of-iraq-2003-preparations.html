What is US UK invasion of Iraq 2003 preparations?

Preparations for the 2003 Iraq invasion involved extensive U.S. and UK military buildup in the Persian Gulf, intelligence assessments claiming Iraqi WMD programs, diplomatic efforts to secure UN backing that ultimately failed, and domestic campaigns to build public support based on the threat of imminent danger from Saddam Hussein’s alleged weapons.
