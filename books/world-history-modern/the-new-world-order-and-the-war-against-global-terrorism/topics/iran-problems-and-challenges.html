Q1. Who became the supreme leader of Iran after Ayatollah Khomeini's death in 1989?

🔹 A. Ayatollah Ali Khamenei
🔹 B. Ali Akbar Rafsanjani
🔹 C. Muhammad Khatami
🔹 D. Hassan Rouhani

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Ayatollah Ali Khamenei was appointed as Khomeini's successor, consolidating the religious authority of the Islamic Republic and continuing the system of Velayat-e Faqih, where a senior cleric holds ultimate political power.

Q2. The US Embassy hostage crisis began in which month and year?

🔹 A. Nov-79
🔹 B. Dec-79
🔹 C. Jan-80
🔹 D. Feb-80

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The crisis started in November 1979 when student militants seized the US Embassy in Tehran, holding 66 Americans hostage, an event that dramatically heightened anti‑American sentiment and shaped Iran’s foreign policy for years.

Q3. Which war dominated Khomeini’s tenure and lasted from 1980 to 1989?

🔹 A. Iran‑Iraq War
🔹 B. Afghan War
🔹 C. Gulf War
🔹 D. Yemen Civil War

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Iran‑Iraq War, initiated by Iraq’s invasion in 1980, consumed Iran’s resources, caused massive casualties, and left the economy in ruins, influencing subsequent reconstruction policies under Rafsanjani.

Q4. What was the approximate inflation rate in Iran after the Iran‑Iraq War?

Q11. If Iran’s inflation remained at \(30\%\) for three consecutive years, what would be the approximate cumulative price increase?

🔹 A. \(~90\%\)
🔹 B. \(~100\%\)
🔹 C. \(~120\%\)
🔹 D. \(~150\%\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Compounding a \(30\%\) annual inflation rate over three years yields a cumulative increase of \((1.30)^3-1 ≈ 1.197\) or about \(119.7\%\), close to the \(120\%\) option, illustrating severe erosion of purchasing power.

Q12. Analyze how the Iran‑Iraq War’s destruction of oil infrastructure could have impacted Iran’s GDP growth in the 1980s. Which effect is most plausible?

🔹 A. A sharp decline in GDP due to reduced oil exports
🔹 B. A modest increase in GDP because of war‑time production
🔹 C. No impact because agriculture compensated fully
🔹 D. GDP doubled due to reconstruction contracts

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The war decimated oil fields and export capacity, cutting the primary revenue source; consequently, GDP contracted sharply, as other sectors could not offset the loss, leading to a prolonged economic downturn.

Q29. Assess the impact of high inflation (\(30\%\)) on real wages if nominal wages rose by \(10\%\) in a given year. What happens to real wages?

🔹 B. They stay the same
🔹 C. They increase slightly
🔹 D. They increase significantly
