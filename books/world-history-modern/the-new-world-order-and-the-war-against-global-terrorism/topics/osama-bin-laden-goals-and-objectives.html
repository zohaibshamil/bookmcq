What is Osama bin Laden goals and objectives?

Osama bin Laden’s primary goals included expelling U.S. military forces from Muslim lands, ending Western support for Israel and authoritarian Arab regimes, establishing a pan-Islamic caliphate governed by strict Sharia law, and inspiring global jihad through high-profile attacks to mobilize Muslims against perceived Western imperialism.

Q1. Which of the following was one of bin Laden’s primary objectives regarding US military presence in Saudi Arabia?

A.Remove US troops from Saudi soil ✅
B.Increase US military bases in the Gulf
C.Invite US forces to protect holy sites
D.Maintain the status quo
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Bin Laden viewed the presence of American forces as a violation of Islamic sovereignty. He repeatedly called for the withdrawal of U.S. troops, believing their removal would restore religious purity and reduce Western influence in the Arabian Peninsula, a central tenet of his campaign.

Q2. What political system did bin Laden aim to replace the Saudi monarchy with?

A.A secular democracy
B.A socialist republic ✅
C.An Islamic regime governed by Sharia
D.A constitutional monarchy
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Bin Laden’s rhetoric emphasized the need for a government that adhered strictly to Islamic law. He sought to overthrow the existing monarchy, which he saw as too Western‑aligned, and replace it with a regime that would implement Sharia in all aspects of governance.

📖 Explanation: The text explains that bin Laden believed the presence of foreign troops on Saudi soil breached the holy status of the region, which houses Islam’s two most sacred cities. He argued that allowing such deployment conflicted with the religious duty to protect these sites.
