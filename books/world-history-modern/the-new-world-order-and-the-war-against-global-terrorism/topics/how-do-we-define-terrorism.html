What is How do we define 'terrorism'??

Terrorism is generally defined as the deliberate use of violence or the threat of violence against civilians or non-combatants by non-state actors to create fear, coerce governments, and achieve political, religious, or ideological goals, though the exact definition remains debated due to differing perspectives on who qualifies as a terrorist versus a freedom fighter.
