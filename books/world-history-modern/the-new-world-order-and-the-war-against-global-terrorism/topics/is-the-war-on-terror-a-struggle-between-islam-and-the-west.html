What is Is the 'war on terror' a struggle between Islam and the West??

Most historians and scholars argue that the War on Terror is not inherently a clash between Islam and the West but rather a conflict involving specific extremist ideologies and geopolitical grievances, emphasizing that the vast majority of Muslims reject terrorism and that framing it as a civilizational war oversimplifies complex local, political, and historical factors.
