What is George W Bush War on Terror policy?

President George W. Bush’s War on Terror policy was characterized by the doctrine of preemptive war, the establishment of the Department of Homeland Security, the Patriot Act, indefinite detention at Guantanamo Bay, and military invasions of Afghanistan and Iraq, all framed as necessary measures to protect national security against asymmetric threats.

Q1. How many commercial airliners were hijacked on September 11, 2001?

A. 2
B. 4 ✅
C. 5
D. 7

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The attacks involved four separate airplanes that were taken over by terrorist hijackers. This figure is widely reported in official investigations and media coverage, making it a basic factual detail essential for understanding the scale of the coordinated assaults.

Q2. Which building was targeted by the third hijacked plane?

A. The White House
B. The Pentagon ✅
C. The Capitol
D. The CIA Headquarters

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The third aircraft, American Airlines Flight 77, was deliberately flown into the Pentagon, the headquarters of the U.S. Department of Defense. This target was chosen to strike a symbol of American military power and to maximize psychological impact.

Q3. Approximately how many people died in the World Trade Center attacks?

A. 1800
B. 2800 ✅
C. 3800
D. 4800

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Official death counts place the number of fatalities at the World Trade Center around 2,800, including first‑responders, office workers, and visitors. This figure is a key statistic used in memorials, policy analyses, and discussions about the human cost of the attacks.

Q4. Which U.S. President declared the 'War on Terror' after the September 11 attacks?

A. Bill Clinton
B. George H. W. Bush
C. George W. Bush ✅
D. Barack Obama

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

President George W. Bush, who was in office on September 11, 2001, framed the response as a global 'War on Terror.' His administration used this rhetoric to justify a range of military, legal, and diplomatic actions in the years that followed.

Q5. Which terrorist organization was widely believed to be responsible for the September 11 attacks?

A. Hezbollah
B. Al-Qaeda ✅
C. ISIS
D. Boko Haram

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Intelligence agencies quickly linked the attacks to al‑Qaeda, a network led by Osama bin Laden. The organization claimed responsibility later, and its involvement shaped U.S. foreign policy, leading to the invasion of Afghanistan to dismantle its base.

Q6. How many towers comprised the World Trade Center complex that were hit?

The World Trade Center consisted of twin skyscrapers, known as the North and South Towers. Both were struck by hijacked planes, causing massive loss of life and the eventual collapse of each structure within hours of the impacts.

Q20. If the number of al‑Qaeda operatives decreased by 10% annually after 2001, how many would remain after three years, starting from 5,000?

A. 3645 ✅
B. 4050
C. 4500
D. 3800

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Applying a 10 % yearly reduction: Year 1 = 5,000 × 0.9 = 4,500; Year 2 = 4,500 × 0.9 = 4,050; Year 3 = 4,050 × 0.9 ≈ 3,645. This demonstrates the gradual attrition of a terrorist network under sustained pressure.

Q27. Using the data that al‑Qaeda had approximately 5,000 members in 2001 and assuming a logistic growth model with carrying capacity of 20,000 and growth rate r = 0.2 per year, what would be the estimated membership after 5 years?

A. 6000 ✅
B. 7500
C. 9000
D. 10000

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Applying the logistic formula \(P(t)=\frac{K}{1+((K-P_0)/P_0)e^{-rt}}\) with K = 20,000, P₀ = 5,000, r = 0.2, t = 5 gives \(P(5)\approx 9,500\), which is closest to the 9,000 option among those provided.

Q29. If the probability of a terrorist cell being detected in a given year is 0.12, what is the probability that it remains undetected for three consecutive years?

A. \(0.88^3\) ✅
B. \(\0.12^3\)
C. \(0.88\)
D. \(0.12\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The chance of evading detection each year is 1 − 0.12 = 0.88. Raising this to the third power yields \(0.88^3\approx0.68\), representing roughly a 68 % probability of remaining hidden over three years.
