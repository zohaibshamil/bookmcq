Q1. In which year was Hamid Karzai first elected President of Afghanistan?

🔹 B. 2004
🔹 C. 2006

📖 Explanation:

Karzai won the 2004 presidential election, marking the first democratic transition after the fall of the Taliban. His victory was celebrated internationally, but he soon faced the daunting task of rebuilding a war‑torn nation while managing insurgent threats and deep‑seated corruption.

Q4. By 2007, roughly what proportion of Afghanistan's GDP was derived from illegal drugs?

🔹 D. 0.75

📖 Explanation:

Estimates suggest that about half of Afghanistan's gross domestic product stemmed from illegal drug production by 2007. This dependence underscored the difficulty of breaking the nexus between the economy and the illicit opium market, complicating stabilization efforts.

Q11. Why did NATO’s attempts to curb opium cultivation often provoke additional violence?

Q13. If the Taliban controlled 20 % of districts and each district has an average of 1,000 voters, estimate the number of voters under Taliban control.

🔹 C. 1000
🔹 D. 2000

📖 Explanation:

Using the simple calculation \(20\% \times 1{,}000 = 200\), we estimate that about two hundred voters per district fall under Taliban‑influenced authority, illustrating the potential electoral impact of insurgent dominance.
