What is Aftermath of Iraq War 2003-2011?

The aftermath of the Iraq War from 2003 to 2011 was marked by prolonged insurgency, sectarian civil war, the rise of extremist groups like ISIS, massive civilian casualties, infrastructure destruction, and political instability, culminating in the U.S. withdrawal in 2011 without achieving lasting peace or democratic consolidation.
