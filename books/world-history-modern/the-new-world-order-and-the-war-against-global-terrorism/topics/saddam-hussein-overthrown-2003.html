What is Saddam Hussein overthrown 2003?

Saddam Hussein was captured in December 2003 near Tikrit after months in hiding following the fall of Baghdad, leading to his trial by an Iraqi tribunal for crimes against humanity and eventual execution in 2006, symbolizing the end of Ba’athist rule but also triggering sectarian violence and power vacuums.
