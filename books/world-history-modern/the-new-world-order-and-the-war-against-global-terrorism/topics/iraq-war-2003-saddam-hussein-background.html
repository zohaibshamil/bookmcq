What is Iraq War 2003 Saddam Hussein background?

Saddam Hussein was the authoritarian president of Iraq from 1979 to 2003 whose regime was accused of possessing weapons of mass destruction, supporting terrorism, and committing human rights abuses, providing the stated justification for the 2003 U.S.-led invasion despite lacking conclusive evidence and UN authorization.

Q1. When did the first Gulf War officially end?

A. 1990
B. 1991 ✅
C. 1992
D. 1993

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The first Gulf War, also known as Operation Desert Storm, concluded with a cease‑fire in February 1991. Although combat operations ceased earlier, the formal end is recognized as 1991, marking the beginning of the post‑war sanctions regime on Iraq.

Q2. Which UN agency reported that over half a million Iraqi children died due to sanctions?

A. WHO
B. UNICEF ✅
C. UNDP
D. UNESCO

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

UNICEF released a report in 1999 documenting that more than 500,000 children had died since 1990 as a direct consequence of prolonged sanctions. The agency highlighted severe malnutrition and lack of medical supplies, underscoring the humanitarian cost of the embargo.

Q3. If sanctions reduced Iraq's food imports by \(30\%\) each year for five consecutive years, what is the approximate total reduction percentage?

A. 0.7
B. 0.83 ✅
C. 0.9
D. 0.95

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A yearly reduction of \(30\%\) compounds: the remaining proportion after five years is \((0.7)^5 \approx 0.17\). Therefore the total reduction is \(1-0.17 = 0.83\) or about \(83\%\). This illustrates the severe cumulative impact of sustained embargoes.

Q4. What was the primary reason the USA and UK were reluctant to remove Saddam Hussein before 2003?

A. Fear of regional chaos ✅
B. Desire for oil
C. Alliance with Iraq
D. Domestic politics

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Policymakers feared that ousting Saddam could unleash sectarian violence, destabilize the delicate balance between Kurds and Shia, and create a power vacuum that neighboring states might exploit. This strategic caution often outweighed the desire for regime change.

Q9. If Iraq possessed all components for one nuclear warhead and each component cost \( \$15\) million, what would be the total cost for a single warhead assuming five components?

Q15. If the sanctions lasted for ten years and each year reduced medical imports by \(8\%\), what cumulative reduction percentage would result (rounded to nearest whole number)?

A. \( \approx 56\% \)
B. \( \approx 63\% \) ✅
C. \( \approx 72\% \)
D. \( \approx 80\% \)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The cumulative effect is \(1-(0.92)^{10}\). Calculating \((0.92)^{10}\approx0.37\); thus the reduction is \(1-0.37=0.63\) or about \(63\%\).
