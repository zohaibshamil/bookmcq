What is Tunisia Arab Spring 2010-2011?

Tunisia’s Arab Spring began in December 2010 when street vendor Mohamed Bouazizi set himself on fire to protest police harassment and economic despair, sparking nationwide protests that forced President Ben Ali to flee in January 2011 and initiated the only relatively successful democratic transition among Arab Spring uprisings.

Q1. What was the name of the Tunisian youth whose self‑immolation sparked the 2010 protests?

A.Mohammed Bouazizi ✅
B.Youssef Chahed
C.Habib Bourguiba
D.Zine El Abidine
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Mohammed Bouazizi, a recent college graduate, set himself on fire on 17 December 2010 after police confiscated his fruit‑stand goods. His act ignited widespread protests that grew into the Tunisian Revolution, marking him as the catalyst of the movement.

📖 Explanation: Bouazizi’s stall was located in the inland town of Sidi Bouzid. The lack of a permit and the police seizure of his merchandise there led directly to his desperate act of self‑immolation, which then triggered nationwide unrest.

Q30. If the overall unemployment rate is 15 % and youth unemployment is 30 %, assuming youths constitute 40 % of the labor force, what is the unemployment rate for those over 30?

B.0.07
C.0.1
D.0.12
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A
