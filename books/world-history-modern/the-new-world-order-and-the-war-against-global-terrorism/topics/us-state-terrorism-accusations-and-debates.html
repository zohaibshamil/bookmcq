What is US state terrorism accusations and debates?

Accusations of U.S. state terrorism involve criticisms that American military interventions, drone strikes, sanctions, and support for repressive regimes have caused civilian casualties and instability, sparking debates over whether these actions constitute legitimate national security measures or violate international law and human rights standards.
