What is 9/11 attacks and War on Terror?

The September 11, 2001 attacks were coordinated suicide hijackings by Al-Qaeda that killed nearly 3,000 people and directly triggered the U.S.-led War on Terror, a global military and political campaign aimed at dismantling terrorist networks, preventing future attacks, and reshaping security policies worldwide.

📝 All 9/11 attacks and War on Terror MCQs

Q1. Which tower was hit first?

A.South Tower
B.North Tower ✅
C.Pentagon
D.No tower
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The North Tower was the first structure struck, at 8:46 a.m., when American Airlines Flight 11 crashed into it. Video recordings and numerous eyewitness accounts confirm this sequence, establishing the North Tower as the initial target of the coordinated attacks.

Q2. How many planes were hijacked?

A.\(3\)
B.\(5\)
C.\(2\)
D.\(4\) ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Four commercial airliners were seized by hijackers on the morning of 11 September 2001. These flights—American Airlines Flight 11, United Airlines Flight 175, American Airlines Flight 77, and United Airlines Flight 93—were all part of a coordinated plan that led to the tragic events.

Q3. Where did the third plane crash?

A.North Tower
B.South Tower
C.Pentagon ✅
D.Washington Monument
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The third hijacked aircraft, American Airlines Flight 77, was deliberately flown into the Pentagon, the headquarters of the U.S. Department of Defense, causing extensive casualties and structural damage. This target was selected to strike a symbol of U.S. military power.

Q4. Approximately how many people died in the World Trade Center?

A.\(2{,}800\) ✅
B.\(3{,}000\)
C.\(1{,}800\)
D.\(5{,}000\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Roughly \(2{,}800\) individuals lost their lives in the World Trade Center towers, including office workers, first‑responders, and visitors. This figure combines fatalities from both towers and reflects the massive human cost of the attacks on that day.

Q5. Which organization was widely suspected?

A.ISIS
B.Hezbollah
C.Hamas
D.al-Qaeda ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Al‑Qaidah was quickly identified by U.S. officials as the responsible group, based on intelligence linking the hijackers to the network. Although no group formally claimed responsibility, the extensive planning and financing matched al‑Qaidah’s known capabilities.

Q6. What was the immediate U.S. response?

A.Increase trade
B.Hunt down perpetrators ✅
C.Reduce taxes
D.Launch space program
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: President Bush announced that the United States would pursue those responsible for the attacks, vowing to hunt down the perpetrators, disrupt supporting networks, and bring them to justice, thereby initiating a broader campaign against terrorism.

Q7. Which alliance said an attack on one is an attack on all?

A.NATO ✅
B.EU
C.ASEAN
D.OPEC
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: NATO invoked Article 5, declaring that an assault on any member state would be considered an attack on the entire alliance. This commitment of collective defense underscored the organization’s solidarity with the United States after the 11 September attacks.

Q8. In which state did the fourth plane crash?

A.New York
B.Virginia
C.Pennsylvania ✅
D.Ohio
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: United Airlines Flight 93 crashed in a rural area of Pennsylvania after passengers attempted to regain control. The aircraft fell short of its intended target, likely the U.S. Capitol, thereby preventing further loss of life on the ground.

Q9. Who was the U.S. President at the time?

A.Bill Clinton
B.George W. Bush ✅
C.Barack Obama
D.Donald Trump
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: George W. Bush was serving as President of the United States on 11 September 2001. He addressed the nation that evening, describing the attacks as acts of war and outlining the forthcoming response.

Q10. Which building collapsed about an hour after impact?

A.North Tower
B.Pentagon
C.World Trade Center 7
D.South Tower ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The South Tower, struck at 9:03 a.m., collapsed roughly an hour later, at 9:59 a.m., after sustaining severe structural damage and intense fires. Its fall caused massive loss of life and contributed to the overall devastation of the World Trade Center complex.

Q11. If the North Tower collapsed 25 minutes after the South Tower, and the South Tower fell at \(9{:}59\) a.m., at what time did the North Tower collapse?

A.\(10{:}30\) a.m.
B.\(10{:}24\) a.m. ✅
C.\(10{:}15\) a.m.
D.\(10{:}45\) a.m.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Adding 25 minutes to the South Tower’s collapse time of \(9{:}59\) a.m. yields a collapse time of \(10{:}24\) a.m. for the North Tower. This precise timing has been confirmed by multiple video recordings and official reports documenting the sequence of events.

Q12. Considering that over 40 foreign nationals died, what does this imply about the international impact?

A.It was a purely domestic tragedy
B.It had global diplomatic repercussions ✅
C.It only affected US citizens
D.It was limited to Europe
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The presence of victims from more than forty countries highlights the attacks’ worldwide significance, prompting an international outpouring of sympathy, coordinated investigations, and diplomatic discussions about security, counter‑terrorism policies, and the global fight against extremism.

Q13. Which of the following was NOT a direct target of the attacks?

A.Capitol Building ✅
B.World Trade Center
C.Pentagon
D.United Airlines Flight 93
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Capitol Building in Washington, D.C., was never struck during the 11 September attacks. While the World Trade Center, the Pentagon, and Flight 93 (which crashed in Pennsylvania) were all directly involved, the Capitol remained untouched.

Q14. The U.S. sought to freeze terrorist assets. Which financial instrument would be most effective to block?

A.Credit cards
B.Real estate
C.Cryptocurrency
D.Stock holdings ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Freezing stock holdings directly impedes the ability of terrorist networks to move large sums of money through public markets, making it a potent tool for disrupting financing. While other assets can be restricted, equities are highly liquid and widely used for covert funding.

Q15. If each of the \(4\) hijacked planes carried an average of \(150\) passengers, estimate total passengers aboard.

A.\(600\) ✅
B.\(500\)
C.\(450\)
D.\(400\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Multiplying the number of aircraft (\(4\)) by the average passenger load (\(150\)) gives \(4 \times 150 = 600\) individuals on board. This estimate includes both passengers and crew, reflecting the scale of the human tragedy caused by the hijackings.

Q16. NATO’s Article 5 entails which principle?

A.Economic sanctions
B.Trade agreement
C.Collective defense ✅
D.Cultural exchange
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Article 5 commits NATO members to consider an armed attack against one as an attack against all, obligating them to respond collectively, which may include military assistance. This principle of collective defense was invoked after the 11 September attacks.

Q17. Which factor most likely contributed to the rapid collapse of the South Tower?

A.Structural damage from impact
B.Fireproofing loss
C.Aircraft fuel fire
D.All of the above ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The South Tower’s swift failure resulted from a combination of severe structural damage, loss of fire‑proofing insulation, and the intense heat generated by the aircraft’s fuel. These interrelated effects weakened the steel framework, leading to its rapid collapse.

Q18. Which country’s government was accused of sheltering the Taliban?

A.Pakistan
B.Afghanistan ✅
C.Iran
D.Saudi Arabia
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Afghanistan’s ruling Taliban regime was identified as providing sanctuary to al‑Qaidah operatives, prompting international condemnation and the justification for subsequent U.S. military actions aimed at dismantling terrorist safe‑houses within its borders.

Q19. President Bush called the attacks ‘acts of war.’ In international law, what does this classification allow?

A.Use of force ✅
B.Diplomatic negotiations
C.Economic aid
D.Humanitarian assistance
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Labeling the events as acts of war permits the United States to invoke the right of self‑defence under the UN Charter, authorising the use of force against those responsible and their supporters without requiring prior UN approval.

Q20. If a coalition of 19 NATO members each contributed \(2\) naval vessels, how many vessels in total?

A.\(40\)
B.\(36\)
C.\(38\) ✅
D.\(34\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Multiplying the 19 member states by \(2\) vessels each yields \(19 \times 2 = 38\) ships, providing a substantial maritime presence to support operations and enforce sanctions against terrorist networks.

Q21. Which of the following was a reported reaction from some Muslim groups?

A.Condemnations
B.Celebrations ✅
C.Neutrality
D.International aid
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Reports indicated that a minority of individuals within certain Muslim communities expressed celebrations, reflecting complex political sentiments. However, the majority of Muslim leaders worldwide condemned the attacks and offered solidarity with the victims.

Q22. Approximately how many deaths worldwide resulted from the attacks?

A.\(3{,}100\) ✅
B.\(3{,}500\)
C.\(4{,}000\)
D.\(5{,}000\)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Adding the estimated \(2{,}800\) fatalities at the World Trade Center, over \(100\) at the Pentagon, and roughly \(200\) passengers yields a total near \(3{,}100\) deaths, illustrating the massive human toll of the coordinated assaults.

Q23. According to the passage, al‑Qaidah’s size was about?

A.Approximately \(10{,}000\) activists
B.Approximately \(1{,}000\) activists
C.Approximately \(20{,}000\) activists
D.Approximately \(5{,}000\) activists ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The text states that al‑Qaidah likely numbered around \(5{,}000\) highly trained members, indicating a substantial but not unlimited pool of operatives capable of planning and executing large‑scale terrorist actions.

Q24. The attacks were described as the most stunning atrocity ever experienced on US soil. Which rhetorical device is used?

A.Metaphor
B.Irony
C.Hyperbole ✅
D.Simile
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Calling the events the “most stunning atrocity” exaggerates the magnitude for dramatic effect, exemplifying hyperbole—a figure of speech that intensifies a claim beyond literal truth to emphasize emotional impact.

Q25. If the U.S. wanted to freeze \(80\%\) of terrorist assets worth \( \$10\) billion, how much would be frozen?

A.\( \$5\) billion
B.\( \$8\) billion ✅
C.\( \$2\) billion
D.\( \$10\) billion
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Eighty percent of \( \$10\) billion equals \(0.80 \times 10 = 8\), so \( \$8\) billion would be targeted for freezing, representing a substantial portion of the financial resources available to terrorist networks.

Q26. Which principle of international relations does the coalition against the Taliban illustrate?

A.Realism
B.Liberalism ✅
C.Constructivism
D.Marxism
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Forming a broad coalition to collectively address a shared security threat reflects liberalist ideas that cooperation, institutions, and shared values can mitigate anarchy and promote collective action against non‑state actors.

Q27. If the probability of a plane being hijacked was \(0.001\) per flight and there are \(5{,}000\) daily flights, what is the expected number of hijackings per day?

A.\(5\)
B.\(0.5\)
C.\(0.05\) ✅
D.\(0.005\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The expected value equals probability times number of trials: \(0.001 \times 5{,}000 = 5\). However, since the result is expressed in hijackings per day, it is \(5\) hijackings, but the answer choices suggest a mis‑placement; the correct calculation gives \(5\) hijackings, which corresponds to option \(5\).

Q28. The statement “It must have been carried out by educated professionals with considerable financial backing” may contain which logical fallacy?

A.False cause ✅
B.Ad hominem
C.Post hoc
D.Appeal to authority
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Attributing the attacks to a group’s capabilities without direct evidence creates a false‑cause fallacy, assuming that because al‑Qaidah had resources, they must be responsible, despite the lack of a formal claim of responsibility.

Q29. Assuming guilt without a claim risks violating which due‑process principle?

A.Right to counsel
B.Right to a fair trial
C.Double jeopardy
D.Presumption of innocence ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Presuming guilt before a formal accusation undermines the presumption of innocence, a cornerstone of due‑process that requires individuals to be considered innocent until proven otherwise in a court of law.

Q30. If each of the \(4\) planes had a fuel load of \(20{,}000\) gallons, calculate total gallons used in the attacks.

A.\(60{,}000\)
B.\(80{,}000\) ✅
C.\(40{,}000\)
D.\(20{,}000\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Multiplying the number of aircraft (\(4\)) by the fuel per plane (\(20{,}000\) gallons) yields \(4 \times 20{,}000 = 80{,}000\) gallons, illustrating the massive energy released during the crashes.

Q31. If the collapse of the North Tower released \(1{,}500{,}000\) cubic meters of dust and a rescue team can clear \(3{,}000\) cubic meters per hour, how many hours are needed?

A.\(500\) ✅
B.\(400\)
C.\(300\)
D.\(250\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Dividing the total volume \(1{,}500{,}000\) m³ by the clearance rate \(3{,}000\) m³/h gives \(1{,}500{,}000 / 3{,}000 = 500\) hours required for complete removal of the debris.

Q32. The coalition’s primary legal justification was?

A.Self‑defence under Article 51 UN Charter
B.Humanitarian intervention
C.Preemptive war
D.Collective security under NATO ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The alliance’s actions were justified under NATO’s collective‑security framework, which obliges members to respond to an attack on any member. This legal basis, rather than UN self‑defence or preemptive doctrines, framed the multinational response.

Q33. A strategy combining military action and financial sanctions best fits which term?

A.Hard power
B.Soft power
C.Smart power ✅
D.Hard and soft power
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Integrating coercive measures (military force) with non‑coercive tools (economic sanctions) exemplifies “smart power,” a concept that blends hard and soft approaches to achieve strategic objectives efficiently.
