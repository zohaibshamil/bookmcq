What is Why did Russian Provisional Government fall 1917?

The Provisional Government collapsed because it continued Russia’s unpopular involvement in World War I, delayed land reform and elections, lost support from soldiers and peasants, and could not compete with the Bolsheviks’ clear promises of peace, land, and bread.

Q1. What unpopular decision did the provisional government make regarding Russia’s involvement in World War I?

A. Continue the war ✅
B. Withdraw from the war
C. Sign a peace treaty with Germany
D. Remain neutral

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The provisional government chose to keep Russia in the conflict, hoping to honor its allies and maintain prestige. This decision alienated war‑weary soldiers and civilians, eroding confidence in the new regime and creating fertile ground for revolutionary opposition.
