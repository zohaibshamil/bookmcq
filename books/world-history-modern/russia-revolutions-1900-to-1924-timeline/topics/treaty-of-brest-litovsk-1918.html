Q1. In which year did Lenin remark that the Bolsheviks would not use the sort of terror employed by the French revolutionaries?

🔹 A. 1915
🔹 B. 1916
🔹 C. 1917
🔹 D. 1918

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Lenin made the statement in November \(1917\), expressing a desire to avoid the mass executions that characterized the Reign of Terror during the French Revolution. He hoped that the Soviet regime could maintain order without resorting to guillotine‑style killings, although later events showed a shift toward harsher measures.

Q2. Which treaty is described in the passage as humiliating for Russia?

🔹 A. Treaty of Versailles
🔹 B. Treaty of Brest–Litovsk
🔹 C. Treaty of Paris
🔹 D. Treaty of Trianon

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Treaty of Brest–Litovsk, signed in March \(1918\), forced Russia to cede large territories, including Ukraine, to the Central Powers. Historians label it humiliating because it represented a severe diplomatic defeat and contributed to internal unrest, setting the stage for increased Bolshevik coercion.

Fanya Kaplan, a left‑wing Social Revolutionary, shot and killed the head of the Petrograd Cheka, Alexander Kolchak, on that date.
