Q1. In the 'Battle for the Lira', Mussolini revalued the lira from \(150\) to \(90\) per pound. Which industry benefited most from cheaper imported raw materials?

🔹 A. Steel
🔹 B. Textiles
🔹 C. Agriculture
🔹 D. Tourism

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The steel sector relied heavily on imported iron ore and coal; the revaluation lowered the cost of these inputs, making production more profitable. While other sectors also saw price changes, steel manufacturers experienced the most direct advantage, leading to increased output and greater competitiveness within Italy's industrial base.

Q2. By 1930, Italy's iron and steel production had ____ compared to 1925.

🔹 A. Halved
🔹 B. Doubled
🔹 C. Tripled
🔹 D. Remained the same

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Government subsidies and a push for autarky spurred rapid expansion of heavy industry. Official statistics reported that output of iron and steel had roughly doubled in five years, a figure used in propaganda to showcase the regime's economic achievements despite broader international downturns.

📖 Explanation:

Q6. Mussolini revalued the lira from \(150\) to \(90\) per pound. What percentage change does this represent?

🔹 A. 40% increase
🔹 B. 40% decrease
🔹 C. 60% increase
🔹 D. 60% decrease

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The percentage change is calculated as \((90-150)/150 \times 100 = -40\%\). This 40 % decrease in the exchange rate meant the lira became stronger relative to the pound, affecting both import prices and export competitiveness in the Italian economy.

Q7. How did the 'Battle for Wheat' affect wealthy northern cereal growers versus overall wheat self‑sufficiency?

🔹 C. It hurt both groups equally
🔹 D. It had no measurable impact

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The policy raised tariffs on foreign wheat, protecting domestic producers, especially affluent cereal farmers in the north. At the same time, the push for increased domestic cultivation succeeded in cutting imports dramatically, moving Italy nearer to self‑sufficiency despite regional disparities in benefit.

Q8. The three‑day week reduced weekly work hours from 48 to 24. What percentage reduction does this represent?

🔹 A. 0.25
🔹 B. 0.33
🔹 C. 0.5
🔹 D. 0.75

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Original work time was six days × 8 hours = 48 hours. The new schedule of three days × 8 hours equals 24 hours. The reduction is \((48-24)/48 \times 100 = 50\%\), indicating a halving of scheduled labor time, which combined with wage cuts to further strain workers' livelihoods.

The net result was a deterioration in the trade balance, as export revenues fell faster than import costs were reduced.

Q24. If a wheat tariff of 30 % is applied to an imported price of \(1.5\) units, what is the effective price after the tariff?

🔹 A. \(1.5\) units

🔹 D. \(2.1\) units

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C
