What is Why did Mussolini gain support in Italy?

Mussolini gained support because he promised to crush socialism, restore national greatness, and bring stability to a chaotic post-war society, attracting funding from wealthy elites who feared communism and appealing to nationalists and veterans who felt abandoned by the liberal government.

Q2. Which newspaper did Mussolini edit before founding his own publication?

B. Il Popolo d'Italia

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

Q3. In which year did Mussolini establish the Fascist Party?

A. 1918
B. 1919 ✅
C. 1920
D. 1921

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Mussolini founded the Fasci di Combattimento, later known as the Fascist Party, in 1919. This date marks the formal organization of his movement, which initially combined elements of socialism, nationalism, and republicanism before evolving toward authoritarianism.

Q4. What ancient Roman symbol did the Fascist Party adopt to represent authority?
