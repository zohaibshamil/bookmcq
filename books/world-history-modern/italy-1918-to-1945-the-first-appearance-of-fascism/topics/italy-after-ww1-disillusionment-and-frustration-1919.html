What is Italy after WW1 disillusionment and frustration 1919?

After World War I, many Italians felt betrayed because the peace treaties did not grant Italy all the territories it had been promised, leading to widespread anger over economic hardship, high unemployment, and a sense that the democratic government was too weak to restore national pride or order.

Q1. Which of the following territories was actually granted to Italy by the Versailles settlement?

A.Trentino, South Tyrol, Istria, Trieste ✅
B.Albania, Dalmatia, Aegean islands, Fiume
C.All promised territories
D.None of the above
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Italy received the first four areas—Trentino, South Tyrol, Istria, and Trieste—while the remaining promises such as Albania, Dalmatia, and the Aegean islands were awarded to other states, primarily Yugoslavia. This partial fulfillment sparked widespread disappointment among Italians.

Q3. Who was the prime minister who ordered the army to remove d'Annunzio from Fiume?

A.Benito Mussolini
B.Giovanni Giolitti ✅
C.Victor Emmanuel III
D.Alessandro Salvi
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Giovanni Giolitti, serving as Italy's prime minister in 1920, decided that restoring governmental authority required a decisive military action against Gabriele d'Annunzio's occupation of Fiume. Giolitti's order led to the peaceful surrender of d'Annunzio but damaged the government's popularity.

📖 Explanation: The post‑war period left about 2.5 million former soldiers without jobs, as heavy industry reduced wartime production and the economy could not absorb the large pool of veterans. This unemployment contributed heavily to social unrest and the feeling of national frustration.

Q6. In which month and year did d'Annunzio surrender his hold on Fiume?

The surrender occurred without armed conflict, but it underscored the government's weakened authority in the eyes of many Italians.

Q8. What primary financial action contributed to Italy's post‑war economic crisis?

Q9. Which electoral reform was introduced in the 1919 Italian elections?
