About 3. The League of Nations

The League of Nations was established after World War I as the first international organization dedicated to maintaining world peace through collective security and diplomacy rather than military force. Its primary goal was to prevent future wars by providing a forum for resolving disputes, promoting disarmament, and improving global welfare through agencies focused on health, labor, and refugees.

Despite its noble intentions, the League ultimately failed to stop aggression in the 1930s due to structural weaknesses and the absence of key powers like the United States. It lacked its own military force and required unanimous decisions, which made it ineffective against determined aggressors like Japan, Italy, and Germany, eventually undermining its credibility and leading to its dissolution.
