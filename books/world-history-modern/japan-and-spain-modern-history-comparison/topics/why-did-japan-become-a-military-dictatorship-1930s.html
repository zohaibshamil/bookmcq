What is Why did Japan become a military dictatorship 1930s?

Japan shifted toward military dictatorship in the 1930s because severe economic depression, political corruption, and perceived Western hostility convinced ultranationalist army officers that only direct military rule could restore national strength and secure vital resources through aggressive expansion.
