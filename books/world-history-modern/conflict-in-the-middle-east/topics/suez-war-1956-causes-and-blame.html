What is Suez War 1956 causes and blame?

The Suez War of 1956 was caused by Egyptian President Nasser’s nationalization of the Suez Canal, prompting Britain, France, and Israel to secretly collude in a military invasion to regain control and remove Nasser, but international pressure from the U.S. and USSR forced their withdrawal, marking a decline in European colonial power.

Q7. An Arab historian argues that the British decision to renew the 1936 treaty would have prevented the war. Which analytical flaw does this claim contain?

C. Ignoring the role of Soviet arms sales
D. Assuming causality without evidence

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The claim assumes that renewing the treaty would automatically avert conflict, a classic post‑hoc fallacy. It overlooks other variables like Nasser's nationalism, regional tensions, and Cold War dynamics, thereby simplifying a complex causal chain.
