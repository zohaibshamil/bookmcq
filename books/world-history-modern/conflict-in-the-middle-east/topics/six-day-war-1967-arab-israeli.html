What is Six Day War 1967 Arab-Israeli?

The Six-Day War of 1967 erupted when Israel launched preemptive airstrikes against Egypt amid rising tensions, border clashes, and Egyptian mobilization, quickly defeating Egypt, Jordan, and Syria in six days and capturing the Sinai Peninsula, Gaza Strip, West Bank, East Jerusalem, and Golan Heights.
