What is Israel Lebanon War 2006?

The 2006 Israel-Lebanon War began when Hezbollah captured two Israeli soldiers, prompting a massive Israeli military campaign that devastated southern Lebanon and infrastructure, while Hezbollah fired rockets into northern Israel, ending in a UN-brokered ceasefire with no clear victor and significant civilian casualties on both sides.

Q1. In which year did Israel finally withdraw its troops from southern Lebanon?

Israel completed its withdrawal from the southern Lebanese territory in the year \(2000\). This marked the end of a decade‑long occupation that began in 1990, and it is a basic factual point often cited in discussions of post‑war geopolitics in the region.
