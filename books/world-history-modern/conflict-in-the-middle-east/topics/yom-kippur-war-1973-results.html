What is Yom Kippur War 1973 results?

The Yom Kippur War ended without major territorial changes but restored Arab pride, led to U.S.-brokered disengagement agreements, triggered an oil embargo that caused a global energy crisis, and paved the way for eventual Egyptian-Israeli peace talks by demonstrating that military stalemate required diplomatic solutions.
