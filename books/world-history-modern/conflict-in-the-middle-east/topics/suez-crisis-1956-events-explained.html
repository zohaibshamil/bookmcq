Q1. On what date did the Israeli invasion of Egypt begin?

📖 Explanation:

The invasion began on \(29\) October 1956, marking the opening of the Suez Crisis. This date is recorded in diplomatic archives and indicates Israel’s first major military action against Egyptian territory, which quickly escalated into a broader international conflict involving Britain, France, and the United Nations.
