What is Israeli attitude change after Gulf War?

After the Gulf War, many Israelis shifted toward supporting territorial compromise because witnessing U.S. power, recognizing Arab military limitations, and experiencing Scud missile attacks without retaliation made them believe land-for-peace deals could enhance security rather than threaten it, enabling Oslo negotiations.
