Q1. In which month and year was the peace treaty signed?

🔹 A. A) March 1978
🔹 B. B) March 1979
🔹 C. C) March 1980
🔹 D. D) March 1981

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The treaty was concluded in Washington in March 1979, ending the formal state of war that had existed between Egypt and Israel since 1948 and establishing a new diplomatic framework for the two nations.

Q2. Which Arab states did NOT condemn the 1979 peace treaty?

🔹 A. A) Egypt and Jordan
🔹 B. B) Saudi Arabia and Iraq
🔹 C. C) Libya and Algeria
🔹 D. D) Sudan and Morocco

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Only Sudan and Morocco refrained from condemning the treaty; the majority of Arab states viewed the agreement as a betrayal of Arab solidarity, leading to widespread denunciation despite the historic peace between Egypt and Israel.

Q3. Under the treaty, Israel agreed to withdraw its troops from which territory?

📖 Explanation:

Israel pledged to pull its forces out of the Sinai Peninsula, a key demand of Egypt that would later be completed, symbolizing a major concession and a step toward normalizing relations between the two countries.
