What is Iran-Iraq War 1980-1988 stalemate?

The Iran-Iraq War resulted in a brutal eight-year stalemate featuring trench warfare, chemical weapons, and massive casualties exceeding one million, with neither side achieving decisive victory despite foreign support, ending only through a UN-mediated ceasefire that left borders unchanged and economies devastated.

B.0.4
C.2
D.4
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Each tanker faces a 0.020.020.02 chance of sinking per mine; with 100 mines, the per‑tanker sinking probability is roughly 0.020.020.02.
