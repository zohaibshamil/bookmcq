What is Middle East peace process stall 1990s?

The Middle East peace process stalled in the late 1990s due to Netanyahu’s election reversing Oslo momentum, accelerated settlement construction, Palestinian suicide bombings eroding Israeli public support, failed summit diplomacy at Camp David in 2000, and the outbreak of the Second Intifada that shattered remaining optimism for negotiated settlement.

Q14. If 60 settlements built after March 2001 were dismantled, reducing the total settlement count by 5%, what was the original total number of settlements?

A. 1200
B. 1100
C. 1000 ✅
D. 900

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Let \(x\) be the original number of settlements. Dismantling 60 settlements reduces the total by 5%, so \(0.05x = 60\). Solving gives \(x = 60 / 0.05 = 1,200\). Therefore, the original number was 1,200 settlements, illustrating the scale of settlement expansion.
