What is Results of 1948 Arab-Israeli War?

The 1948 Arab-Israeli War resulted in Israel controlling more territory than the UN partition plan allotted, the permanent displacement of approximately 700,000 Palestinians known as the Nakba, the division of Jerusalem, and armistice agreements that left no formal peace but established de facto borders lasting until 1967.
