What is Iran Iraq War 1980 to 1988 and Iraq's motives?

The Iran-Iraq War from 1980 to 1988 was initiated by Saddam Hussein who invaded Iran seeking to seize oil-rich Khuzestan, exploit post-revolutionary chaos, assert regional dominance, and prevent Shia revolutionary ideology from spreading to Iraq’s majority-Shia population, but the war became a costly stalemate.

Q1. What was Iraq's primary stated reason for invading Kuwait in August 1990?

A. To gain control of Kuwait's oil fields
B. To correct a perceived border dispute ✅
C. To spread democracy
D. To support UN sanctions

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Iraq announced that the invasion was meant to rectify an alleged historical border dispute dating back to the 1930s, framing it as a legal correction rather than an act of aggression. This narrative was used to justify the military action domestically and internationally, despite widespread condemnation.

Q2. Which Iraqi leader ordered the invasion of Kuwait in 1990?

A. Saddam Hussein ✅
B. Ahmed Hassan al-Bakr
C. Tariq Aziz
D. Saddam al-Mahdi

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The invasion was directed by President Saddam Hussein, who sought to consolidate regional power and address economic grievances. His personal authority and decision‑making style meant that the order came directly from the top, reflecting his broader ambitions for Iraq’s position in the Gulf.

Q3. In which year did Iraqi forces withdraw from Kuwait following the coalition's military campaign?

A. 1991 ✅
B. 1992
C. 1993
D. 1994

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

After a swift coalition offensive known as Operation Desert Storm, Iraqi troops retreated from Kuwait in February 1991, ending the occupation. The withdrawal was formalized in the cease‑fire agreement, though the regime retained many of its military assets in the region.

Q4. During the 1980s, Iraq supported Palestinian militant groups mainly to:

📖 Explanation:

Iraq’s backing of Palestinian factions was a strategic move to bolster its standing among Arab nations, projecting leadership in the Arab‑Israeli conflict and countering rival states’ influence, especially after the Iran‑Iraq war.

Q5. One of the major economic motives behind Iraq's 1990 invasion of Kuwait was:
