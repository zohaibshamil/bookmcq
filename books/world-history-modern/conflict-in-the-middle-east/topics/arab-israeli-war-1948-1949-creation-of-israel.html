What is Arab-Israeli War 1948-1949 creation of Israel?

The 1948-1949 Arab-Israeli War began immediately after the United Nations partitioned Palestine and Israel declared independence, leading neighboring Arab states to invade in opposition, resulting in Israel’s establishment, the displacement of hundreds of thousands of Palestinians, and armistice lines that defined new borders.

Q1. What year did the World Zionist Organization get founded in Basel, Switzerland?

A. 1895
B. 1897 ✅
C. 1901
D. 1910

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The World Zionist Organization was founded in 1897 at the First Zionist Congress in Basel, Switzerland. This organization marked the formal beginning of modern political Zionism, aiming to establish a Jewish national home in Palestine, which later contributed to tensions with the Arab population already residing there.

Q2. Which British official issued the declaration supporting a Jewish national home in Palestine?

C. David Lloyd George
D. Winston Churchill

📖 Explanation:

Arthur Balfour, the British Foreign Secretary in 1917, authored the Balfour Declaration, stating British support for the establishment of a “national home for the Jewish people” in Palestine. This declaration fueled Jewish immigration and heightened Arab opposition, setting the stage for later conflict.
