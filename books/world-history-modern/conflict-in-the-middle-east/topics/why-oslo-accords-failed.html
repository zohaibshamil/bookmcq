What is Why Oslo Accords failed?

The Oslo Accords failed due to unresolved core issues like Jerusalem, refugees, settlements, and borders, continued settlement expansion, terrorist attacks by Hamas and Islamic Jihad, Israeli security concerns, leadership assassinations including Rabin’s, loss of public trust on both sides, and absence of enforcement mechanisms for commitments.
