What is The Gulf War and Saddam Hussein's motives?

Saddam Hussein invaded Kuwait in 1990 primarily to erase Iraq’s massive war debt owed to Kuwait, seize its vast oil reserves, accuse Kuwait of slant-drilling Iraqi oil fields, and assert Iraq’s regional hegemony, miscalculating international response and triggering a U.S.-led coalition intervention.
