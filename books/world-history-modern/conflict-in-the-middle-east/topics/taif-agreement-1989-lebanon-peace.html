What is Taif Agreement 1989 Lebanon peace?

The Taif Agreement of 1989 ended the Lebanese Civil War by restructuring the government to give Muslims equal parliamentary representation with Christians, reducing presidential powers, mandating Syrian troop withdrawal (later delayed), and establishing a power-sharing system that restored stability but entrenched sectarian politics.
