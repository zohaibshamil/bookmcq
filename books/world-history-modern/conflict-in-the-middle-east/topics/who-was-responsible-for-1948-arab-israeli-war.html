What is Who was responsible for 1948 Arab-Israeli War?

Responsibility for the 1948 Arab-Israeli War is debated among historians, with some blaming Arab leaders for rejecting the UN partition plan and invading, others citing Zionist militias for expelling Palestinians before statehood, and many concluding that mutual distrust, competing nationalisms, and British withdrawal created an inevitable conflict.
