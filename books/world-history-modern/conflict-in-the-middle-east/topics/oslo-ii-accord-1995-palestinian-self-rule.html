What is Oslo II Accord 1995 Palestinian self-rule?

The Oslo II Accord of 1995 divided the West Bank into Areas A, B, and C with varying degrees of Palestinian and Israeli control, expanded Palestinian self-governance in civilian matters, scheduled further Israeli withdrawals, and set timelines for permanent status negotiations, though implementation stalled due to violence and political disagreements.
