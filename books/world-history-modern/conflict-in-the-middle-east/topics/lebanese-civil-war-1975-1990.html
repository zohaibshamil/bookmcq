What is Lebanese Civil War 1975-1990?

The Lebanese Civil War from 1975 to 1990 was a complex sectarian conflict involving Christian militias, Muslim factions, Palestinian refugees, and foreign interveners like Syria and Israel, fueled by political imbalance, regional proxy struggles, and social inequality, resulting in over 100,000 deaths and widespread destruction.
