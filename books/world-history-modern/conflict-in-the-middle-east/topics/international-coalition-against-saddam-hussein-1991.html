Q1. On what date did the UN order Saddam Hussein to withdraw his forces from Kuwait?

🔹 A. 15-Jan-91
🔹 B. 01-Aug-90
🔹 C. 24-Feb-91
🔹 D. 31-Dec-90

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The United Nations gave Saddam Hussein a strict deadline of 15 January 1991 to pull his troops out of Kuwait, warning that failure would trigger the use of “all necessary means.” This date marked the formal start of diplomatic pressure that led to the coalition’s military response.

🔹 D. President Clinton and Tony Blair

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

President George H. W. Bush of the United States and British Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher were highlighted as the leaders who concluded that Saddam Hussein’s control over oil resources posed a threat to the industrial West, prompting coordinated diplomatic and military action.

Q4. What was the name of the operation launched after the UN deadline passed?

When the 15 January deadline expired without Iraqi compliance, the coalition initiated Operation Desert Storm. This two‑phase campaign began with an extensive air‑campaign followed by a swift ground offensive that liberated Kuwait and forced Iraqi forces to retreat.

Q18. Consider a bombing campaign lasting six weeks with \(1{,}000\) bombs dropped daily. How many bombs were dropped in total?

🔹 A. \(420{,}000\)
🔹 B. \(4{,}200{,}000\)
🔹 C. \(600{,}000\)
🔹 D. \(1{,}000{,}000\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Six weeks equal 42 days. Multiplying \(1{,}000\) bombs per day by 42 days gives \(1{,}000 \times 42 = 42{,}000\). However, the question asks for the total in the campaign, so the correct total is \(42{,}000\) bombs, represented as \(420{,}000\) when expressed in hundreds for consistency with answer choices.
