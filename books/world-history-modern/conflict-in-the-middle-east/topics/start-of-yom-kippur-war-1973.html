What is Start of Yom Kippur War (1973)?

The Yom Kippur War started on October 6, 1973, when Egyptian forces crossed the Suez Canal and Syrian troops advanced into the Golan Heights in a carefully planned surprise offensive timed to coincide with Yom Kippur, catching Israel unprepared and aiming to reclaim territories lost in 1967.

📝 All Start of Yom Kippur War (1973) MCQs

Q1. On what date did the Yom Kippur War officially begin?

A.06-Oct-73 ✅
B.07-Oct-73
C.08-Oct-73
D.09-Oct-73
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The conflict started on the morning of \(6\) October \(1973\), when Egyptian and Syrian forces launched a coordinated surprise attack against Israel on the Jewish holy day of Yom Kippur. This date is widely recognized by historians as the opening moment of the fourth Arab‑Israeli war.

Q2. Which Jewish religious festival coincided with the start of the war?

A.Passover
B.Hanukkah
C.Yom Kippur ✅
D.Sukkot
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Yom Kippur, also known as the Day of Atonement, is the most solemn day in the Jewish calendar and involves fasting and intense prayer. The war’s launch on this day was intended to exploit the reduced military readiness of Israeli forces, who observe the holiday with limited activity.

Q3. Which two Arab nations initiated the attack on Israel on 6 October 1973?

C.Lebanon and Saudi Arabia
D.Libya and Yemen
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Egypt and Syria coordinated a joint offensive on the morning of \(6\) October \(1973\), aiming to reclaim territories lost in the 1967 Six‑Day War. Their simultaneous actions on both the Sinai Peninsula and the Golan Heights marked the opening of the Yom Kippur War.

📖 Explanation: The United States and the Soviet Union, the two dominant Cold War superpowers, each pressured their regional allies to cease hostilities. Their diplomatic involvement was crucial in negotiating a cease‑fire that both the Arab coalition and Israel eventually accepted.
