Q1. On which date was the Enabling Law officially passed by the Reichstag?

🔹 A. A) 30 January 1933
🔹 B. B) 23 March 1933
🔹 C. C) 15 April 1933
🔹 D. D) 1 May 1933

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Enabling Law was enacted on \(23\) March \(1933\) after the Reichstag fire. This date is pivotal because it marked the formal legal shift that allowed Hitler to govern by decree, bypassing the parliamentary process and setting the stage for his dictatorship.

🔹 C. C) Wilhelm II

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

President Paul von Hindenburg was in office during the vote on the Enabling Law. Although he was initially shocked by the Reichstag fire, he was persuaded by Hitler that emergency powers were necessary, which led to his approval of the legislation.

Q3. What primary power did the Enabling Law grant to the German government?

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Enabling Law gave the cabinet the authority to issue laws and decrees without the Reichstag’s consent for a period of four years. This effectively transferred legislative power to Hitler’s government, allowing him to rule by decree and sidestep democratic checks.

Q19. Considering the Reichstag had 647 seats, calculate the minimum number of seats required for a two‑thirds majority to pass the Enabling Law. Which figure is correct?

🔹 A. A) 430 seats
🔹 B. B) 432 seats
🔹 C. C) 433 seats
🔹 D. D) 435 seats

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

A two‑thirds majority of 647 seats is calculated as \( \frac{2}{3}\times647 \approx 431.33\). Since a majority must be a whole number of seats, the next whole number is 432, but rounding up to ensure at least two‑thirds yields 433 seats. Thus, 433 is the minimum required.
