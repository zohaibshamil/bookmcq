What is Hitler's ideas and aims?

Hitler's core ideas and aims were extreme and ruthless: he believed in racial supremacy, insisting that the German 'Aryan' race was superior and had a right to living space (Lebensraum) in Eastern Europe; he was violently antisemitic, blaming Jews for all of Germany's problems and aiming to eliminate them; he hated democracy, communism, and the Treaty of Versailles, and his ultimate goal was to create a vast, racially pure German empire that would dominate the world.
