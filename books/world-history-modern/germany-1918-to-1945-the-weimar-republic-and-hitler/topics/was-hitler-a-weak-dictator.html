What is Was Hitler a weak dictator?

The question of whether Hitler was a weak dictator is debated, as his leadership style was chaotic and he often deliberately gave vague orders, allowing his underlings like Himmler, Goebbels, and Göring to compete and fight over responsibilities, which suggests he was a 'lazy' or 'weak' dictator who didn't want to do detailed administrative work; however, this 'weakness' actually strengthened his absolute power, as it made everyone scramble to please him, and all major, radical decisions like the Holocaust still originated from and were fully supported by his ultimate authority.
