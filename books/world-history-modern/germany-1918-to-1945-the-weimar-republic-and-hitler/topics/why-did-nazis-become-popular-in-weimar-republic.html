What is Why did Nazis become popular in Weimar Republic?

The Nazis became popular in the Weimar Republic because they promised desperate people simple, powerful solutions to every crisis: they swore to tear up the Treaty of Versailles, restore German pride, fix the economy and provide jobs, blame all problems on scapegoats like Jews and communists, and offer a strong, single leader (the Führer) who would end the chaos of democracy, which appealed to farmers, workers, business owners, and the middle class who felt betrayed by their government.

Q1. Which of the following groups was NOT blamed by Nazi propaganda for Germany’s problems?

A.Marxists
B.Jesuits
C.Freemasons
D.Farmers ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The Nazis singled out Marxists, Jesuits, and Freemasons as part of a conspiratorial elite, but they never targeted farmers as a primary scapegoat. Their propaganda focused on urban and intellectual groups, leaving the agricultural population largely unmentioned in the blame narrative, which is why \Farmers\ is the correct answer.

Q2. What was the name of the Nazi private army that attracted many unemployed youths?

A.SS
B.SA ✅
C.Gestapo
D.Wehrmacht
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Sturmabteilung, abbreviated as SA, was the Nazi Party’s paramilitary wing that offered a uniform, a small wage, and a sense of belonging to young men without jobs. This organization became a key recruitment tool for the Nazis, distinguishing it from the other listed forces, which served different purposes.

Q3. Which treaty did the Nazis promise to overturn as part of their appeal to German nationalism?

A.Treaty of Brest-Litovsk
B.Treaty of Versailles ✅
C.Treaty of Trianon
D.Treaty of Saint-Germain
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Treaty of Versailles, signed after World War I, imposed heavy reparations and territorial losses on Germany, making it deeply unpopular. Nazi rhetoric centered on revoking this treaty to restore German pride and power, whereas the other treaties involved different nations and were not the primary focus of Nazi propaganda.

Q4. Which book, written by Hitler while imprisoned, outlines his ideological goals and plans for Germany?

B.Mein Kampf ✅
C.The Communist Manifesto
D.The Prince
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Hitler authored \Mein Kampf\ during his incarceration after the Beer Hall Putsch, detailing his worldview, anti‑Jewish sentiment, and political strategy. The other titles belong to different authors and ideologies, making \Mein Kampf\ the definitive source of Nazi doctrine and the correct answer.
