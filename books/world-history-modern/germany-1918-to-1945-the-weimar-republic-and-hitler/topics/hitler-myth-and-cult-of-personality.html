What is Hitler myth and cult of personality?

The Hitler myth was a carefully manufactured cult of personality that portrayed Hitler as a brilliant, infallible, and almost god-like leader who had personally saved Germany from collapse, and this was spread through relentless propaganda, mass rallies, and the media, making him seem like a selfless father figure to the nation, which led millions of ordinary Germans to trust him completely, support his policies, and believe that he was always working for the good of the country.

Q1. According to the passage, which early foreign policy success contributed most to Hitler’s popular image?

A.Rearmament announcement
B.Remilitarization of the Rhineland ✅
C.Anschluss with Austria
D.Incorporation of Czechoslovakia
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The remilitarization of the Rhineland in March 1936 was portrayed as a bold reversal of the Versailles constraints, signaling Germany’s resurgence. Goebbels’s propaganda highlighted this move as evidence of Hitler’s decisive leadership, reinforcing the perception of a strong, revitalized nation and thereby bolstering his popular image among many Germans.
