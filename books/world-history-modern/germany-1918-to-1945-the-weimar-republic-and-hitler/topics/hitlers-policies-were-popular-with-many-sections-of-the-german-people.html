What is Hitler's policies were popular with many sections of the German people?

Many German people genuinely supported Hitler's policies because they brought visible improvements: the economy recovered from the Great Depression, providing jobs through massive public works like the autobahns; Germany regained its international status and military strength; the Nazi regime appeared to restore law and order; and many people, especially in the middle class and rural areas, agreed with its traditional values and promises to restore national pride after the humiliations of Versailles.
