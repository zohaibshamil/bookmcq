Q1. What does the term \Gleichschaltung\ refer to in the context of Nazi Germany?

🔹 A. Voluntary cooperation of citizens
🔹 B. Forcible coordination of institutions
🔹 C. Economic liberalization
🔹 D. Military alliance

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The word Gleichschaltung literally means “forced coordination.” In Nazi practice it described the systematic process by which the regime brought all political, social, and cultural institutions under the direct control of the NSDAP, eliminating independent authority and creating a totalitarian state.

Q2. Which organization replaced independent trade unions after they were abolished?

🔹 A. German Labour Front
🔹 B. National Socialist Women’s League
🔹 C. Reich Ministry of Labor
🔹 D. German Workers' Association

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The German Labour Front (Deutsche Arbeitsfront, DAF) was created to take over the functions of trade unions. It forced all workers to become members, controlled wages and working conditions, and ensured that no independent labor organization could challenge Nazi economic policy.

Q3. Which political party dissolved itself a week before the official ban on opposition parties?

🔹 B. Catholic Centre Party
🔹 C. German National People's Party
🔹 D. Communist Party of Germany

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The Catholic Centre Party chose to dissolve itself voluntarily on 29 July 1933, anticipating the forthcoming ban on all parties except the NSDAP. This self‑dissolution removed a major centrist force from the political arena, smoothing the path toward a one‑party state.
