What is Reichstag fire 1933 and election campaign?

The Reichstag fire in February 1933, which burned down the German parliament building, was blamed on a young communist and used by Hitler as a brilliant excuse to claim that a communist uprising was imminent, leading him to push through an emergency decree that suspended civil liberties and allowed him to arrest communist leaders, which he then used during the election campaign to terrify voters into supporting the Nazis as the only party that could restore order.

Q1. Who was arrested and later executed for starting the Reichstag fire?

A. Marinus van der Lubbe ✅
B. Ernst Röhm
C. Heinrich Himmler
D. Joseph Goebbels

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Marinus van der Lubbe, a Dutch communist, was apprehended at the scene of the fire, tried, and executed. Contemporary accounts and trial records consistently identify him as the sole arsonist, despite later debates about possible conspiracies involving other Nazi elements.
