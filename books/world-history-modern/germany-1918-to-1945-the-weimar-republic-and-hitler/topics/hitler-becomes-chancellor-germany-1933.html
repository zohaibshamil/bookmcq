Q1. How many seats did the Nationalist Party hold in the Reichstag after the July 1932 election?

🔹 A. \(37\)
🔹 B. \(45\)
🔹 C. \(58\)
🔹 D. \(72\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Nationalist Party, also known as the Deutschnationale Volkspartei, secured only \(37\) seats in the Reichstag after the July 1932 election, far below a majority. This small representation forced them to seek alliances with larger parties, notably the Nazis, to increase their influence in parliament.

Q2. Which two right‑wing politicians were the main conspirators in bringing Hitler into the government?

🔹 D. Gustav Stresemann and Friedrich Ebert

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Franz von Papen and General Kurt von Schleicher orchestrated the political maneuvering that led to Hitler’s appointment as Chancellor. Their collaboration with conservative elites and the Reichswehr created the conditions for a coalition that appeared to control the Nazi leader, though it ultimately backfired.

Q3. What role did President Hindenburg ultimately assign to Hitler in January 1933?

🔹 B. Vice‑Chancellor
🔹 C. Minister of Defense
🔹 D. President of the Reichstag

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

President Paul von Hindenburg, persuaded by Papen and Schleicher, appointed Adolf Hitler as Chancellor on 30 January 1933. Although the original plan was to keep Hitler subordinate, he insisted on the top post, which he obtained, marking the legal start of Nazi rule.
