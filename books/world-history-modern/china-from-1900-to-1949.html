About 19. China from 1900 to 1949

China’s early 20th century was marked by the collapse of the Qing dynasty, warlord fragmentation, foreign imperialism, and failed republican experiments that left the nation weak and divided. Intellectuals and reformers searched for ways to restore national strength, leading to the rise of both the Nationalist Kuomintang and the Communist Party amid Japanese invasion and civil war.

The prolonged conflict devastated the country but also radicalized peasants and discredited the Nationalists’ corruption and inefficiency. By 1949, Mao Zedong’s communists emerged victorious through rural mobilization and nationalist appeal, ending decades of chaos and establishing a new revolutionary state poised to transform Chinese society.
