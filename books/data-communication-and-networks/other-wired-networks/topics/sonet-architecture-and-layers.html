What is SONET architecture and layers?

SONET architecture consists of four hierarchical layers—photonic, section, line, and path—that define signal framing, error monitoring, multiplexing, and end-to-end payload transport, enabling interoperable high-capacity optical networking with precise synchronization across network elements.

Q1. What is the bandwidth allocated to each individual TV channel within the video band?

A. 6 MHz ✅
B. 5 MHz
C. 8 MHz
D. 10 MHz

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Each TV channel in the traditional broadcast video band is assigned a fixed width of 6 MHz. This spacing allows the signal to fit within the allocated spectrum while providing enough guard band to avoid interference between adjacent channels.
