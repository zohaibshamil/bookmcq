Q1. What is the bandwidth allocated to each individual TV channel within the video band?

🔹 A. 6 MHz
🔹 B. 5 MHz
🔹 C. 8 MHz
🔹 D. 10 MHz

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Each TV channel in the traditional broadcast video band is assigned a fixed width of 6 MHz. This spacing allows the signal to fit within the allocated spectrum while providing enough guard band to avoid interference between adjacent channels.
