What is SONET (Synchronous Optical Network)?

SONET is a standardized optical fiber transmission protocol used primarily in North America for high-speed synchronous data transport over fiber optics, providing reliable, scalable backbone infrastructure for telephone and data networks with built-in redundancy and management capabilities.

Q1. If the payload capacity of a SONET STS‑1 frame is increased while keeping the overhead constant, what is the most likely effect on the overall network throughput?

A.Increase proportionally ✅
B.Decrease due to overhead dominance
C.Remain unchanged
D.Cause synchronization errors
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Increasing the payload directly raises the amount of user data that can be carried per frame. Because the overhead remains fixed, the ratio of payload to total bits grows, so the effective throughput of the link rises in proportion to the payload increase.

Q2. A network engineer observes that a SONET mesh topology continues to deliver traffic after a single fiber cut, whereas a linear SONET topology loses connectivity. What can be inferred about the mesh topology’s fault tolerance?

A.It has lower fault tolerance
B.It provides redundant paths ✅
C.It relies on higher bandwidth
D.It uses ATM cells for recovery
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The continued operation after a fiber failure indicates that alternative routes exist within the mesh. Redundant physical links allow traffic to be rerouted, demonstrating higher fault tolerance compared with a linear arrangement that has no alternate paths.

Q3. An STS‑3c SONET signal has a raw line rate of \(155.52\ \text{Mbps}\). After accounting for SONET overhead of \(9.6\ \text{Mbps}\), what is the maximum payload rate available for user data?

A.\(150.00\ \text{Mbps}\)
B.\(145.92\ \text{Mbps}\) (correct) ✅
C.\(144.00\ \text{Mbps}\)
D.\(155.52\ \text{Mbps}\)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Subtracting the overhead from the total line rate gives the usable payload: \(155.52 - 9.6 = 145.92\ \text{Mbps}\). This value represents the maximum bandwidth that can be allocated to user data, assuming no additional protocol overhead.

Q4. Which of the following best distinguishes SONET from traditional Ethernet regarding clock synchronization?

A.Uses external GPS for timing
B.SONET embeds its own synchronous timing (correct) ✅
C.Both use the same asynchronous method
D.Ethernet embeds synchronous timing
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: SONET frames are generated based on an internal, network‑wide clock, providing a globally synchronized timing reference. Ethernet, by contrast, relies on asynchronous packet timing, making SONET inherently synchronized while Ethernet is not.

Q5. When integrating ATM over a SONET backbone, which characteristic of SONET most directly supports the fixed‑size 53‑byte ATM cell transmission?

A.Variable frame length
B.Constant bit‑rate framing (correct) ✅
C.Packet switching
D.Dynamic bandwidth allocation
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: SONET’s fixed‑size, constantly transmitted frames ensure a steady bit rate, which aligns well with the uniform 53‑byte ATM cell size. This predictable timing allows ATM cells to be mapped into the SONET payload without requiring variable‑rate adjustments.

Q6. In a SONET linear topology, latency is primarily a function of distance, whereas in a mesh topology, additional latency arises from what factor?

A.Increased overhead bytes ✅
B.Multiple hop processing (correct)
C.Higher line rates
D.ATM cell size
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Mesh networks often route traffic through several intermediate nodes. Each hop introduces processing delay for framing, error checking, and forwarding, adding to the total latency beyond the propagation delay that dominates in a simple linear link.

Q7. If each STS‑3c channel provides \(112.5\ \text{Mbps}\) of payload, how many such channels must be aggregated to support a 10 Gbps IP backbone?

A.90 (correct) ✅
B.64
C.80
D.100
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Dividing the required 10 Gbps (10,000 Mbps) by the per‑channel payload of 112.5 Mbps yields \(10,000 / 112.5 ≈ 88.9\). Since a fractional channel is impossible, at least 90 full STS‑3c channels are needed to meet or exceed the 10 Gbps target.

Q8. How does the SONET frame structure facilitate the encapsulation of ATM cells for transport across a WAN?

A.By inserting cells into the synchronous payload envelope (correct) ✅
B.By mapping each ATM cell into a dedicated payload slot
C.By using pointer adjustments to align cells
D.By converting cells into variable‑length packets
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: SONET frames contain a synchronous payload envelope that can carry a continuous stream of bits. ATM cells are placed sequentially within this envelope, preserving their fixed size while benefiting from SONET’s timing and error‑checking mechanisms.

Q9. Design a hybrid network where a SONET backbone carries ATM traffic for voice and IP packets for data. Which bandwidth allocation strategy best ensures QoS for voice while maximizing data throughput?

A.Dynamic allocation using priority queuing (correct) ✅
B.Fixed 20 % for voice, 80 % data
C.Equal split 50/50
D.Reserve full STS‑1 for voice only
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: A dynamic, priority‑based queuing system can reserve sufficient bandwidth for time‑critical voice cells while allowing data packets to use any remaining capacity. This approach adapts to traffic variations, guaranteeing voice quality without permanently sacrificing data throughput.
