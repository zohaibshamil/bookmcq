What is Line Coding Schemes converting digital data to digital signals?

Line coding schemes are specific standardized methods like NRZ, Manchester, or AMI that define exactly how binary bits are mapped to signal levels to ensure synchronization, minimize DC components, and reduce errors during transmission.
