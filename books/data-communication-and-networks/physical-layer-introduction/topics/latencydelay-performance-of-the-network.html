📝 Latency(Delay) performance of the network

📖 From Data Communication and Networks • 3. Physical Layer Introduction • 19 questions available

What is Latency(Delay) performance of the network?

Latency is the total time elapsed from when a packet leaves the source until it arrives at the destination, comprising propagation delay, transmission delay, queuing delay, and processing delay, and is critical for interactive applications because even high throughput cannot compensate for excessive delay in real-time communications like video conferencing or online gaming where timely arrival matters more than raw speed.

Q1. If the simulation of case 000 uses a signal with frequency \(f = 0\) and phase 180°, what effect does changing the phase to 0° have on the simulated digital pattern?

A. Inverts the pattern
B. Shifts the pattern by half a period ✅
C. No change
D. Doubles the amplitude

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Changing the phase from 180° to 0° introduces a half‑cycle offset for a zero‑frequency (DC) component. This offset moves the entire waveform by half a period, effectively shifting the simulated digital pattern while preserving its shape, which matches the definition of a half‑period shift.

Q4. What does the term \first harmonic\ refer to in this context?
