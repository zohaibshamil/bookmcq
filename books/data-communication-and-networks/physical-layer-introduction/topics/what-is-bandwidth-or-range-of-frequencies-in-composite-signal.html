What is What is Bandwidth or Range of Frequencies in Composite Signal?

Bandwidth is the difference between the highest and lowest frequencies contained in a composite signal, measured in Hertz, and determines the signal's capacity to carry information because a wider bandwidth allows more data to be transmitted per second, making it a critical resource that network designers must allocate efficiently to support desired communication speeds and quality.

Q1. A signal has a period of 200 ms. If the period is doubled, what is the new frequency in Hz?

A.2.5 Hz ✅
B.5 Hz
C.10 Hz
D.20 Hz
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The original period of 200 ms (0.2 s) gives a frequency of 5 Hz. Doubling the period to 0.4 s halves the frequency, yielding 2.5 Hz. Option A matches this calculation, making it the correct choice.

Q2. A system transmits a 3 GHz carrier and a 5 GHz carrier simultaneously. What bandwidth is required if bandwidth equals the difference between highest and lowest frequencies?

A.8 GHz
B.2 GHz ✅
C.5 GHz
D.3 GHz
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Bandwidth is defined as the span from the lowest to the highest frequency. Subtracting 3 GHz from 5 GHz gives 2 GHz. Therefore the required bandwidth is 2 GHz, which corresponds to option B.

Q3. A pulse has a rise time of 0.5 ns. Using the approximation \\\text{Bandwidth}\\approx\\frac{0.35}{\\text{rise time}}\, what is the approximate bandwidth in GHz?

A.0.35 GHz
B.0.5 GHz
C.0.7 GHz ✅
D.1.0 GHz
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Insert the rise time (0.5 ns = 0.5×10⁻⁹ s) into the formula: \0.35 / 0.5×10^{-9}=0.7×10^{9}\ Hz, which is 0.7 GHz. Option C provides this value.

Q4. A signal’s period approaches zero as its frequency approaches infinity. If a signal’s period is reduced by a factor of 10, by what factor does its frequency increase?

A.0.1
B.0.01
C.100
D.10 ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Frequency is the inverse of period (\f=1/T\). Reducing the period by a factor of 10 multiplies the frequency by the same factor, i.e., a ten‑fold increase. Hence the correct factor is 10, represented by option D.

Q5. In a communication link, the observed phase shift between transmitted and received signals is 45°. If the frequency is 12 GHz, what is the approximate time delay corresponding to this phase shift? Use \\\Delta t=\\frac{\\phi}{2\\pi f}\.

A.10 ps ✅
B.6 ps
C.60 ps
D.600 ps
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Convert 45° to radians (π/4). Plug into \\\Delta t = (\\pi/4)/(2\\pi\\times12\\times10^{9}) = 1/(96\\times10^{9})\ s ≈ 1.04×10⁻¹¹ s, or about 10 ps. Option A matches this result.

Q6. Two digital signals have identical rise times of 0.2 ns. One operates at 50 MHz, the other at 150 MHz. Which statement is true regarding the required bandwidth?

B.Both require the same bandwidth
C.The 50 MHz signal requires more bandwidth ✅
D.Bandwidth cannot be determined
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Bandwidth for a digital edge is mainly set by rise time via \B\\approx0.35/T_r\. Since both signals share the same rise time, they need identical bandwidth regardless of carrier frequency. Thus option C is correct.

Q7. If bandwidth doubles, what happens to rise time according to \\\text{rise time}\\approx\\frac{0.35}{\\text{Bandwidth}}\?

A.It doubles
B.It remains unchanged

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

Q8. Which of the following periods corresponds to a frequency of 1 kHz?
