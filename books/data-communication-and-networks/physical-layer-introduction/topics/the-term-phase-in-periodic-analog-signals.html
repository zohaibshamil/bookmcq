What is The Term Phase in Periodic Analog Signals?

Phase describes the position of a sine wave relative to a reference point at time zero, measured in degrees or radians, and indicates how much the waveform has been shifted horizontally from its standard starting position, which is crucial for modulation techniques and signal synchronization because phase differences allow multiple signals to coexist or encode additional information without changing frequency or amplitude.

B.The frequency of a waveform
C.The relative position of a point in the waveform cycle ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Phase refers to the angular position of a specific point within one cycle of a periodic signal, typically measured in degrees or radians. It indicates where the waveform is at a given instant relative to a reference point, distinguishing it from amplitude, frequency, or power.
