What is Sine Wave in Periodic Analog Signals?

The sine wave is the most fundamental form of a periodic analog signal because it represents a single frequency with smooth oscillation and cannot be decomposed into simpler components, serving as the basic building block for understanding more complex signals since any other periodic waveform can be constructed by combining multiple sine waves of different frequencies, amplitudes, and phases.
