What is Attenuation and Transmission Impairment?

Attenuation is the loss of signal energy as it travels through a transmission medium due to resistance, absorption, or scattering, measured in decibels, and represents the most common transmission impairment because all media absorb some energy, requiring amplifiers or repeaters to restore signal strength at regular intervals to maintain reliable communication over long distances.

Q1. What does the term 'attenuation' refer to in the context of signal transmission?

📖 Explanation: Attenuation is defined as the reduction in signal strength as it propagates through a medium. It primarily manifests as a loss of amplitude, meaning the received signal is weaker than the transmitted one. The other options describe different phenomena that are not attenuation.
