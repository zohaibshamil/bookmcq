Q1. What is the minimum number of bits required to represent all integer values from 0 to 255 inclusive?

🔹 A. 7
🔹 B. 8
🔹 C. 9
🔹 D. 10

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A value of 255 requires the binary pattern \(11111111\), which is eight bits long. Anything fewer than eight bits cannot reach 255, so the smallest bit length that can represent the full range 0–255 is eight bits.

Q2. If a 12‑bit unsigned integer reaches its maximum value and 1 is added, what value appears after overflow?

🔹 A. 0
🔹 B. 4095
🔹 C. 2048
🔹 D. 1

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

When a 12‑bit unsigned number hits its maximum \(2^{12}-1 = 4095\) and 1 is added, the result wraps around modulo \(2^{12}\), yielding 0. This is the standard overflow behavior for unsigned binary arithmetic.

Q3. Compare the numeric ranges of a 10‑bit signed‑magnitude integer and a 10‑bit two’s‑complement integer. Which statement is correct?

🔹 A. Both representations cover the same range
🔹 B. Signed‑magnitude can represent a larger positive value
🔹 C. Two’s‑complement can represent a larger negative value
🔹 D. Two’s‑complement has one extra negative value

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Signed‑magnitude spans \(-511\) to \(+511\). Two’s‑complement spans \(-512\) to \(+511\). The extra negative value (\(-512\)) gives two’s‑complement one more representable negative integer, making statement D correct.

Q4. A sensor outputs voltages between –0.5 V and 4.5 V. If the desired resolution is 0.01 V, how many bits are required for a uniform quantizer?

🔹 A. 8
🔹 B. 9
🔹 C. 10
🔹 D. 11

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The total range is \(4.5-(-0.5)=5\) V. Dividing by the resolution \(0.01\) V gives 500 quantization steps. The smallest integer \(n\) with \(2^{n}\ge 500\) is \(n=9\), so nine bits are needed.

Q5. What decimal value does the 16‑bit two’s‑complement pattern \(1111 1111 1111 1010\) represent?

🔹 A. -6
🔹 B. -5
🔹 C. -10
🔹 D. -12

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Invert the bits to get \(0000 0000 0000 0101\), add 1 to obtain \(0000 0000 0000 0110\) (decimal 6), then apply the negative sign because the original MSB was 1, resulting in –6.

Q6. When replacing a 16‑bit integer audio format with a 24‑bit fixed‑point format, which effect is most accurate?

🔹 A. Higher dynamic range but larger storage
🔹 B. Higher dynamic range and smaller storage
🔹 C. Same dynamic range
🔹 D. Reduces quantization noise but needs more processing

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

A 24‑bit representation can represent \(2^{24}\) distinct levels versus \(2^{16}\) for 16‑bit, giving a substantially larger dynamic range. The trade‑off is increased storage size for each sample.

Q7. A 12‑bit ADC must sample a 1 kHz sinusoid with at least 8 samples per cycle. What minimum sampling rate is required, and does it satisfy the Nyquist criterion?

🔹 A. 8 kHz, does not satisfy Nyquist
🔹 B. 4 kHz, satisfies Nyquist
🔹 C. 8 kHz, satisfies Nyquist
🔹 D. 16 kHz, exceeds Nyquist

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Eight samples per period of a 1 kHz signal require a sampling interval of \(1/8000\) s, i.e., a rate of 8 kHz. Nyquist demands >2 kHz, so 8 kHz comfortably meets the criterion.

Q8. If two n‑bit numbers A = \(2^{n-1}\) and B = \(2^{n-1}-1\) are concatenated to form a 2n‑bit number C, what is the decimal value of C?

🔹 A. \(2^{2n-1}+2^{n-1}-1\)
🔹 B. \(2^{2n-1}+2^{n-1}\)
🔹 C. \(2^{2n-1}-1\)
🔹 D. \(2^{2n}-1\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Concatenation shifts A left by n bits: \(A\cdot2^{n}=2^{n-1}\cdot2^{n}=2^{2n-1}\). Adding B yields \(2^{2n-1}+2^{n-1}-1\).

Q9. For n‑bit two’s‑complement addition, which condition correctly detects overflow?

🔹 A. Result MSB = 1
🔹 B. Carry out of MSB = 1
🔹 C. Operands have opposite signs
🔹 D. Operands have same sign and result sign differs

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Overflow occurs only when the two addends share the same sign but the sum’s sign differs, i.e., \((\text{sign}(x)=\text{sign}(y))\land(\text{sign}(x)\neq\text{sign}(z))\).

Q10. An 8b/10b line code adds two extra bits for every eight payload bits. How many bits are transmitted for a 500‑byte message?

🔹 A. 4000
🔹 B. 5000
🔹 C. 6000
🔹 D. 8000

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

500 bytes = 500 × 8 = 4000 payload bits. The 8b/10b scheme expands this to \(4000\times\frac{10}{8}=5000\) bits total.

Q11. A register is widened from 14 bits to 18 bits. By what factor does its maximum unsigned value increase?

🔹 A. 4
🔹 B. 8
🔹 C. 16
🔹 D. 32

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The maximum unsigned value grows from \(2^{14}-1\) to \(2^{18}-1\). The ratio is \(2^{18}/2^{14}=2^{4}=16\).

Q12. In digital representation, what does the term “bit length” refer to?

🔹 A. Number of bits needed to encode a character
🔹 B. Total number of bits used to represent a numeric value
🔹 C. Length of a binary file
🔹 D. Frequency of a digital signal

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Bit length denotes how many binary digits are allocated to store a particular numeric quantity, determining the range and precision of the representation.
