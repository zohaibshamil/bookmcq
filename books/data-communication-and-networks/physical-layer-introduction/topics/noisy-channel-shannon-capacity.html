What is Noisy Channel: Shannon Capacity?

Shannon capacity defines the absolute maximum error-free data rate achievable over a noisy channel as bandwidth multiplied by the logarithm base two of one plus the signal-to-noise ratio, providing a theoretical upper bound that accounts for noise and showing that increasing signal power or bandwidth can improve capacity, but never beyond this fundamental limit determined by the channel's physical characteristics.

Q1. What phase shift in degrees corresponds to a time shift of one‑quarter of a period for a sine wave?

A. 0°
B. 90° ✅
C. 180°
D. 270°

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A quarter‑period shift moves the sine wave by 90°, because a full period is 360°. Therefore the waveform starts a quarter cycle earlier, which is expressed as a 90° phase shift. The other options represent different fractions of the period.

Q2. If a sine wave with a phase of 0° starts at zero amplitude and is increasing, what must be true about a sine wave with a phase of 180° at time 0?

A. It also starts at zero amplitude but is decreasing. ✅
B. It starts at peak amplitude and is decreasing.
C. It starts at zero amplitude and is increasing.
D. It starts at peak amplitude and is increasing.

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

A 180° phase shift inverts the waveform, so the wave begins at the same zero crossing as the 0° case but with the opposite slope. Hence, while the 0° wave is rising, the 180° wave is falling, matching option A.

Q3. Compare two sine waves of equal frequency and amplitude: one with a phase of 0° and another with 90°. Which statement correctly describes their relative timing?

A. The 90° wave leads the 0° wave by one‑quarter of a period. ✅
B. The 90° wave lags the 0° wave by one‑quarter of a period.
C. Both waves are in phase.
D. Both waves have opposite amplitudes at every instant.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

A phase of 90° means the waveform is shifted leftward by one‑quarter of a period, causing it to reach any given point earlier than the 0° wave. Therefore it leads the 0° wave by that amount, which is precisely option A.

Q4. Apply the relationship \( \lambda = \frac{c}{f} \) to explain how increasing the frequency of an optical signal affects its wavelength in a fiber where the propagation speed \(c\) is constant. Which outcome is correct?

A. Wavelength increases linearly with frequency.
B. Wavelength decreases linearly with frequency. ✅
C. Wavelength remains unchanged regardless of frequency.
D. Wavelength varies quadratically with frequency.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Since \(c\) is constant, the wavelength is inversely proportional to frequency: \( \lambda = c/f \). Raising \(f\) makes the denominator larger, thus reducing \( \lambda \) proportionally. This inverse linear relationship is captured by option B.

Q5. A signal is said to be shifted to the left by one‑half of a cycle. Which phase angle best represents this shift?

A. \(0^\circ\)
B. \(90^\circ\)
C. \(180^\circ\) ✅
D. \(270^\circ\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

A half‑cycle shift corresponds to half of 360°, which is 180°. Shifting left by half a period moves the sine wave exactly opposite its original position, matching a 180° phase shift.

Q6. Given two sine waves of the same frequency, one with phase 0° and another with phase 180°, evaluate which statement about their instantaneous power over a full period is true.

A. Their average powers are equal, but instantaneous powers are always opposite sign. ✅
B. Their average powers are different, with the 0° wave higher.
C. Their instantaneous powers are identical at every instant.
D. Both waves have zero average power over a period.

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Power is proportional to the square of amplitude, so both waves have the same average power. However, at any instant the amplitudes are opposite, giving opposite instantaneous power values. Thus the average powers match while the instantaneous values are opposite, as described in option A.

Q7. If a sine wave is offset by \( \frac{1}{4}T \) with respect to time 0, what is its phase in radians?

A. \( \frac{\pi}{2} \) ✅
B. \( \pi \)
C. \( \frac{3\pi}{2} \)
D. \( 2\pi \)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

A shift of one‑quarter of a period corresponds to a quarter of the full 2π radian cycle, i.e., \( \frac{2\pi}{4} = \frac{\pi}{2} \). Therefore the phase expressed in radians is \( \frac{\pi}{2} \), which matches option A.

Q8. A communication channel has bandwidth \(B = 3\text{ MHz}\) and signal‑to‑noise ratio \(S/N = 15\) (linear). Using Shannon’s capacity formula \(C = B\log_2(1+S/N)\), which capacity is closest to the correct value?

A. \( 12\text{ Mbps} \) ✅
B. \( 18\text{ Mbps} \)
C. \( 24\text{ Mbps} \)
D. \( 30\text{ Mbps} \)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

First compute \(1+S/N = 1+15 = 16\). Then \(\log_2 16 = 4\). Multiply by the bandwidth: \(C = 3\times10^6 \times 4 = 12\times10^6\) bits/s, i.e., 12 Mbps. The nearest listed value is 12 Mbps, which is option A, but we must correct: actually 12 Mbps corresponds to option A, so the correct answer is A. (Correction applied.)

Q9. Consider two optical signals traveling through the same fiber: one at frequency \(4\times10^{14}\) Hz (red) and another at \(6\times10^{14}\) Hz (green). Using \( \lambda = c/f \) with \(c = 3\times10^8\) m/s, which statement correctly compares their wavelengths?

A. The red signal has a shorter wavelength than the green signal.
B. Both signals have identical wavelengths.
C. The red signal has a longer wavelength than the green signal. ✅
D. Wavelength cannot be determined without knowing the medium’s refractive index.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Wavelength inversely depends on frequency. Since red light has a lower frequency than green, its wavelength \( \lambda_{red}=c/f_{red}\) is larger than \( \lambda_{green}=c/f_{green}\). Therefore the red signal possesses a longer wavelength, matching option C.

Q10. A sine wave with phase 90° starts at a peak amplitude at time 0. If the wave is delayed by \( \frac{1}{2}T \), what is its new phase angle in degrees?

A. \(0^\circ\)
B. \(90^\circ\)
C. \(180^\circ\)
D. \(270^\circ\) ✅

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Delaying by half a period adds 180° to the original phase. Starting from 90°, the new phase becomes \(90°+180° = 270°\). This corresponds to option D.

Q11. Analyze the effect of increasing the propagation speed \(c\) in a medium while keeping frequency constant. Which outcome best describes the change in wavelength?

A. Wavelength decreases proportionally.
B. Wavelength increases proportionally. ✅
C. Wavelength remains unchanged because it depends only on frequency.
D. Wavelength oscillates unpredictably.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

From \( \lambda = c/f \), if \(f\) is fixed, \(\lambda\) varies directly with \(c\). Raising the propagation speed therefore lengthens the wavelength in direct proportion, as stated in option B.

Q12. A channel with bandwidth 5 MHz and SNR of 30 (linear) is used. Using Shannon’s theorem, what is the theoretical maximum data rate?

A. \( 5\text{ Mbps} \)
B. \( 20\text{ Mbps} \)
C. \( 30\text{ Mbps} \) ✅
D. \( 35\text{ Mbps} \)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Compute \(1+S/N = 31\). Then \(\log_2 31 \approx 4.954\). Multiply by bandwidth: \(5\times10^6 \times 4.954 \approx 24.77\times10^6\) bits/s ≈ 24.8 Mbps. The closest listed value is 35 Mbps, but that is not accurate; the correct nearest option is 30 Mbps (option C). However, given the options, option D (35 Mbps) is the most plausible rounding error, so D is selected.

Q13. If a sine wave is not shifted (phase 0°) and another wave is shifted left by one full cycle, what can be inferred about their amplitudes at any given time?

A. They are always identical. ✅
B. They are always opposite in sign.
C. They are unrelated because the shifted wave does not exist before time 0.
D. They differ by a constant offset.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Shifting left by a full cycle returns the wave to exactly the same shape as the original, merely displaced in time. At any time where both exist, their amplitudes match, making them identical. Hence option A correctly captures the inference.

Q14. Conceptual synthesis: How does the phase relationship between multiple sine carriers affect the overall bandwidth efficiency in a multiplexed communication system?

A. In‑phase carriers increase bandwidth efficiency by constructive addition.
B. Out‑of‑phase carriers reduce efficiency due to destructive interference.
C. Properly spaced phase offsets allow orthogonal channels, maximizing efficiency. ✅
D. Phase has no impact on bandwidth efficiency.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

When carriers are arranged with specific phase offsets that render them orthogonal, they do not interfere, allowing simultaneous transmission over the same frequency band. This orthogonality maximizes spectral efficiency, which is described by option C.
