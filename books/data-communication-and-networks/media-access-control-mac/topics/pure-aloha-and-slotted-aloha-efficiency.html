What is Pure ALOHA and Slotted ALOHA efficiency?

Pure ALOHA allows stations to transmit frames at any time with a maximum theoretical efficiency of 18.4%, while Slotted ALOHA improves this to 36.8% by synchronizing transmissions into discrete time slots, thereby reducing the vulnerable period during which collisions can occur.

Q1. What does the acronym SABM stand for in the ALOHA protocol?

📖 Explanation: The acronym SABM expands to Set Asynchronous Balanced Mode. It is the frame used by a node to request a balanced‑mode connection in LAPB. The other options mix unrelated terms, making A the only correct expansion.

Q2. Which unnumbered frame does node B send to acknowledge a DISC frame from node A?

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: When node A issues a DISC, node B replies with a UA frame to confirm the disconnection. RR and REJ are supervisory frames, while DM signals a permanent disconnection, so UA is the appropriate acknowledgment.

📖 Explanation: A missing UA after the timeout usually indicates that the SABM frame was corrupted or lost on the medium, preventing node B from receiving it. Busy links would still eventually reply, and silent rejection is not part of the protocol.
