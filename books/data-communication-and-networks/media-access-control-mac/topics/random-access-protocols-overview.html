What is Random Access Protocols overview?

Random access protocols are media access control methods where stations transmit data whenever they have information to send without a central coordinator, relying on collision detection or avoidance mechanisms to handle conflicts when multiple devices attempt to use the shared channel simultaneously.

Q3. According to CSMA/CD, after a station senses the medium idle and begins transmitting, what should it do if it detects a collision?

A.Continue transmitting the corrupted frame
B.Immediately cease transmission and send a jam signal ✅
C.Wait until the end of the frame and retransmit
D.Increase transmission power
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: When a collision is detected, CSMA/CD requires the transmitting station to stop sending the current frame, broadcast a jam signal to ensure all stations recognize the collision, and then defer retransmission using a backoff algorithm. This rapid response minimizes wasted channel time, making option B correct.

Q7. Why is CSMA/CA preferred over CSMA/CD in wireless LANs?

A.Wireless signals cannot be detected while transmitting, making collision detection impractical ✅

Q9. Which of the following best describes the impact of using binary exponential backoff versus linear backoff on collision resolution in Ethernet?

A.Binary exponential backoff reduces average delay under heavy load compared to linear backoff ✅
B.Linear backoff provides faster recovery after a collision than binary exponential backoff
C.Both have identical performance regardless of network load
D.Binary exponential backoff increases the probability of repeated collisions
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Binary exponential backoff doubles the contention window after each collision, quickly spreading retransmission attempts over a larger time span. This adaptive behavior reduces the chance of repeated collisions under high traffic, lowering average delay compared with a fixed‑size linear backoff, making option A correct.

Q10. Explain the hidden node problem in wireless CSMA/CA and identify a mechanism that mitigates it.

A.Hidden nodes cause increased collisions; RTS/CTS handshake mitigates it ✅
B.Hidden nodes improve throughput; ACK frames mitigate it
C.Hidden nodes have no effect; CSMA/CA inherently solves it
D.Hidden nodes cause signal attenuation; power control mitigates it
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: A hidden node cannot hear another transmitter but can interfere at a common receiver, leading to undetected collisions. The RTS/CTS exchange reserves the channel by having both sender and receiver announce their intent, allowing nearby hidden nodes to defer transmission, thereby reducing collisions. Option A captures this solution.
