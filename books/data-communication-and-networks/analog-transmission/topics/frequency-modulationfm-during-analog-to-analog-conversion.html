What is Frequency Modulation(FM) during Analog to Analog conversion?

Frequency Modulation (FM) is an analog-to-analog conversion method where the frequency of a carrier wave is varied in accordance with the amplitude of the input analog signal while maintaining a constant amplitude, resulting in superior noise immunity and higher fidelity compared to AM because most environmental noise affects amplitude rather than frequency, making FM ideal for high-quality audio broadcasting.

Q1. What does the abbreviation FM stand for in communication systems?

B.Frequency Modulation ✅
C.Fading Modulation
D.Fixed Modulation
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Frequency Modulation (FM) refers to the technique where the instantaneous frequency of a carrier wave varies in proportion to the amplitude of the input (modulating) signal. This definition distinguishes FM from other schemes such as amplitude modulation, and is the standard meaning used in radio and communication contexts.
