What is Phase Shift Keying during Analog Transmission?

Phase Shift Keying (PSK) is a digital-to-analog modulation technique that conveys data by changing the phase of a carrier wave while keeping its amplitude and frequency constant, where specific phase shifts (such as 0° and 180° in binary PSK) represent different bit values, providing greater spectral efficiency and noise resistance compared to ASK and FSK for analog transmission channels.
