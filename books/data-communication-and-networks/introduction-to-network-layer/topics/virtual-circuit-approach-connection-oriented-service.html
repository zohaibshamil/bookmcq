What is Virtual Circuit approach connection oriented service?

The virtual circuit approach is a connection-oriented network service that establishes a predefined path with assigned identifiers before data transfer begins, ensuring ordered delivery and consistent routing similar to a dedicated circuit without reserving physical resources.
