Q2. If a router detects a change in its vector, what immediate action does the algorithm prescribe?

🔹 A. Update the routing table locally
🔹 B. Discard the path
🔹 C. Send the vector \(\{Path[1],Path[2],\dots,Path[y]\}\) to all neighbors

🔹 A. \(Path_1\)
🔹 B. \(Path_2\)
🔹 C. Both are equally preferred
🔹 D. Neither is selected
