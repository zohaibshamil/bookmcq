What is Virtual Circuit Networks in Packet Switching?

Virtual circuit networks combine features of circuit and packet switching by establishing a logical path with predefined routing tables before data transfer, ensuring all packets follow the same route and arrive in sequence while still sharing physical links through statistical multiplexing.
