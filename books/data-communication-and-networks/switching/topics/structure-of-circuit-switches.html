What is Structure of Circuit Switches?

Circuit switches are typically constructed using crossbar or multistage interconnection networks of physical contacts or electronic gates that create direct point-to-point electrical paths between input and output ports, designed for low-latency, dedicated connections rather than intelligent packet processing.
