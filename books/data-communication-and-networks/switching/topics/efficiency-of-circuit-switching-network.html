What is Efficiency of Circuit Switching Network?

Circuit switching is generally inefficient for bursty data traffic because it reserves fixed bandwidth for the entire duration of a connection regardless of actual usage, leading to wasted capacity during silent periods, though it remains highly efficient for constant-rate streams like traditional voice calls.

📝 All Efficiency of Circuit Switching Network MCQs

Q1. What is the primary definition of efficiency in the context of switched networks?

A. Ratio of useful time to total time ✅
B. Number of packets transmitted per second
C. Maximum bandwidth achievable
D. Average hop count per packet

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Efficiency measures how much of the total time is spent doing useful work. In switched networks it is expressed as the ratio of useful transmission time to the overall time, reflecting the proportion of resources that contribute directly to data delivery.

Q2. If a packet‑switched network reduces average queuing delay, what is the most likely effect on overall network efficiency?

A. Efficiency decreases due to higher overhead
B. Efficiency remains unchanged
C. Efficiency increases because more time is spent transmitting data ✅
D. Efficiency becomes unpredictable

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

When queuing delay drops, packets spend less time waiting and more time being forwarded. This raises the proportion of time used for actual data transmission, thereby improving the efficiency metric, which is the ratio of useful transmission time to total time.

Q3. Consider a network carrying bursty traffic with long idle periods. Which switching method is likely to achieve higher efficiency?

A. Circuit switching
B. Packet switching ✅
C. Both achieve the same efficiency
D. It depends on packet size

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Burstiness means the network is often idle, so a method that releases resources when not in use—packet switching—avoids reserving bandwidth unnecessarily. Circuit switching would keep a dedicated path even during idle periods, wasting capacity and lowering overall efficiency.

Q4. Which statement correctly describes resource utilization when comparing circuit switching with the datagram approach?

A. Circuit reserves resources only when transmitting
B. Both allocate fixed bandwidth
C. Datagram uses resources continuously
D. Circuit uses resources continuously; datagram uses resources only when transmitting ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Circuit switching establishes a dedicated path that remains reserved for the duration of the connection, regardless of actual data flow. In contrast, the datagram approach forwards each packet independently, consuming network resources only while a packet is being transmitted, leading to more opportunistic utilization.

Q5. Evaluate the trade‑offs between virtual‑circuit and datagram approaches. Which statement is most accurate?

A. Virtual‑circuit offers higher efficiency but lower scalability ✅
B. Datagram offers higher efficiency and scalability
C. Virtual‑circuit provides lower efficiency but better scalability
D. Both have equal efficiency and scalability

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

A virtual‑circuit sets up a path before data transfer, allowing routers to allocate resources efficiently along a fixed route, which boosts per‑packet efficiency. However, maintaining many simultaneous circuits limits scalability. Datagram routing makes independent forwarding decisions, scaling well but incurring per‑packet overhead that reduces efficiency.

Q6. If the useful transmission time is \(80\ \text{ms}\) and the total time is \(100\ \text{ms}\), what is the efficiency of the network?

A. \(80\%\)
B. \(0.8\) ✅
C. \(20\%\)
D. \(0.2\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Efficiency is calculated as the ratio of useful time to total time: \(\frac{80}{100}=0.8\). Expressed as a decimal it is 0.8, which corresponds to 80 % when converted to a percentage. The option showing the decimal form directly matches the definition.

Q7. To improve efficiency for a mix of bulk‑data and interactive traffic, which hybrid switching strategy is most appropriate?

A. Use circuit switching for bulk data and packet switching for interactive traffic ✅
B. Use datagram for all traffic
C. Use virtual‑circuit for all traffic
D. Use only message switching

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Bulk data benefits from a reserved path that minimizes per‑packet overhead, while interactive traffic requires low latency and dynamic routing. Combining circuit switching for the former and packet switching for the latter leverages the strengths of each method, yielding higher overall efficiency than a single‑mode solution.
