What is Characteristics of wireless LANs?

Wireless LANs are characterized by their use of unlicensed radio frequency bands, limited coverage range due to signal attenuation, susceptibility to environmental interference and eavesdropping, variable data rates depending on distance and obstacles, and support for mobile device connectivity without physical tethers, making them ideal for flexible access but requiring careful planning for security and performance.

Q1. What is the line rate of an STS‑3 (OC‑3) signal as listed in the SONET/SDH table?

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The SONET/SDH specification lists the line rate for an STS‑3 (also called OC‑3) as 155.520155.520155.520 Mbps. This value is exactly three times the basic STS‑1 rate of 51.84051.84051.840 Mbps, confirming the scaling rule presented in the table. Therefore option B is the correct choice.

Q2. If eighteen STS‑1 channels can be multiplexed into one STS‑18, how many STS‑3 channels can be multiplexed into the same STS‑18?

A.A) 3
B.B) 9
C.C) 12
D.D) 6 ✅
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: An STS‑18 container holds eighteen STS‑1 channels. Since each STS‑3 is built from three STS‑1 channels, the number of STS‑3 channels that fit into an STS‑18 is 18÷3=618 ÷ 3 = 618÷3=6. This division directly yields six STS‑3 channels, making option D correct.

D.D) STS‑9 is half the rate of STS‑18
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: According to the table, STS‑9 transports 466.560466.560466.560 Mbps while STS‑18 transports 933.120933.120933.120 Mbps. Observing the numbers shows that 933.120=2×466.560933.120 = 2 × 466.560933.120=2×466.560, meaning the STS‑18 rate is exactly twice that of STS‑9. Consequently, option B correctly describes the relationship.

Q4. Given that an STS‑12 signal transports 622.080622.080622.080 Mbps, what is the expected rate of an STS‑24 signal assuming linear scaling?

A.A) 1244.1601244.1601244.160 Mbps ✅
B.B) 933.120933.120933.120 Mbps
C.C) 1555.2001555.2001555.200 Mbps
D.D) 622.080622.080622.080 Mbps
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: SONET rates increase linearly with the multiplexing factor. Doubling the factor from 12 to 24 therefore doubles the bandwidth, so the expected rate for STS‑24 is 2×622.080=1244.1602 × 622.080 = 1244.1602×622.080=1244.160 Mbps. This matches the value given in the table, confirming option A.

Q5. Each STS‑3 channel carries three times the bandwidth of an STS‑1. What total bandwidth do four STS‑3 channels provide?

A.A) 311.040311.040311.040 Mbps

D.D) 933.120933.120933.120 Mbps
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: One STS‑3 equals three STS‑1 channels, giving a bandwidth of 3×51.840=155.5203 × 51.840 = 155.5203×51.840=155.520 Mbps. Multiplying this by four yields 4×155.520=622.0804 × 155.520 = 622.0804×155.520=622.080 Mbps, which coincides with the rate listed for STS‑12. Therefore the correct total is option C.

Q8. Which statement accurately describes the relationship between STS‑9 and OC‑9?
