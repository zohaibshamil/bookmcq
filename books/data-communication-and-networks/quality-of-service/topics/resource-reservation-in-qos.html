What is Resource reservation in QoS?

Resource reservation is the process of explicitly allocating network resources such as bandwidth and buffer space along a path before data transmission begins, ensuring that sufficient capacity exists to meet the quality of service requirements of a specific flow.

Q1. What is the primary purpose of resource reservation in network communication?

A.To guarantee a predefined quality of service ✅
B.To minimize latency at all costs
C.To increase total bandwidth
D.To enhance security
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Resource reservation is used to ensure that a flow receives the bandwidth, latency, and jitter characteristics required for its application, effectively providing a predefined quality of service. Without reservation, competing traffic could degrade performance, making QoS guarantees impossible.

Q5. Which statement best distinguishes static reservation from dynamic reservation in the context of the given values?

A.Static reserves fixed amounts like the repeated 255255255 entries, while dynamic adjusts based on current demand

C.Static allows values such as 454545 and 909090 to change

📖 Explanation: Static reservation pre‑allocates a fixed amount of resources, often represented by constant values such as 255255255. In contrast, dynamic reservation monitors ongoing traffic and modifies allocations in real time, allowing the system to adapt to fluctuating demand patterns.
