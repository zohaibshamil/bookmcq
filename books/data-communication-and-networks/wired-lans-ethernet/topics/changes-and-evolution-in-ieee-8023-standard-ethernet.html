What is Changes and evolution in IEEE 802.3 standard Ethernet?

The IEEE 802.3 standard has evolved from defining only 10 Mbps CSMA/CD on coaxial cable to encompassing full-duplex operation, switched architectures, auto-negotiation, power over Ethernet, and multi-gigabit speeds, reflecting shifts from shared media to point-to-point links while preserving core frame formats and MAC addressing.

Q1. If the frame transmission time is 1 ms and the offered load \(G = 1\), what is the throughput \(S\) for slotted ALOHA?

A. 0.368 ✅
B. 0.5
C. 0.2
D. 0.7

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Using the slotted ALOHA formula \(S = G e^{-G}\) with \(G = 1\) gives \(S = 1 \times e^{-1} = 0.368\). This means 36.8 % of the transmitted frames are expected to be received successfully, matching option A.

Q2. In CSMA, what does the acronym stand for?

A. Carrier Sense Multiple Access ✅
B. Collision Sense Multiple Access
C. Channel Share Multiple Access
D. Control Signal Multiple Access

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

CSMA is defined as Carrier Sense Multiple Access. Each station first listens to the carrier (medium) before transmitting, which is captured by the phrase “Carrier Sense”. The other options mix unrelated terms, making A the correct definition.

Q3. Pure ALOHA achieves a maximum throughput of about 0.184, while slotted ALOHA achieves about 0.368. Which statement correctly reflects this difference?

A. Pure ALOHA throughput is twice that of slotted ALOHA
B. Slotted ALOHA throughput is twice that of pure ALOHA ✅
C. Both have identical maximum throughput
D. Slotted ALOHA has half the throughput of pure ALOHA

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Because slotted ALOHA restricts transmissions to slot boundaries, its vulnerable period is halved, doubling the maximum throughput to \(0.368\) compared with pure ALOHA’s \(0.184\). Hence the correct relationship is that slotted ALOHA’s throughput is roughly twice that of pure ALOHA.

Q4. A slotted ALOHA network transmits 200‑bit frames over a 200 kbps channel. What is the duration of one slot (frame transmission time)?

A. 1 ms ✅
B. 0.5 ms
C. 2 ms
D. 5 ms

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The slot length equals the frame transmission time: \(\frac{200\text{ bits}}{200\,\text{kbps}} = 0.001\text{ s} = 1\text{ ms}\). This is the time needed to send a whole frame, so option A is correct.

Q5. When the offered load is \(G = 0.5\) in slotted ALOHA, what is the throughput \(S\)?

A. 0.303
B. 0.5
C. 0.25 ✅
D. 0.4

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Applying \(S = G e^{-G}\): \(S = 0.5 \times e^{-0.5} \approx 0.5 \times 0.6065 = 0.303\). Therefore the throughput is about 30.3 %, which corresponds to option C.

Q6. What is the vulnerable period for slotted ALOHA?

A. One frame transmission time
B. Two frame transmission times
C. Half a frame transmission time
D. Zero time ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

In slotted ALOHA the vulnerable period is exactly one frame transmission time because a collision can only occur if another station transmits in the same slot. This definition matches option D.

Q7. If the offered load doubles from \(G = 1\) to \(G = 2\), how does the throughput change?

A. It decreases to about 0.270
B. It stays at 0.368
C. It increases to about 0.735
D. It becomes zero ✅

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

For \(G = 2\), \(S = 2 e^{-2} \approx 2 \times 0.1353 = 0.2706\). The throughput actually drops, not rises, because the increased contention outweighs the higher attempt rate. Hence the correct answer is D (0.270).

Q8. In a CSMA network, why can a collision still occur even though stations sense the medium before transmitting?

A. Propagation delay prevents immediate detection of another station’s transmission
B. Stations ignore the carrier sense signal ✅
C. The medium is always busy
D. All stations transmit simultaneously by design

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Even after a station senses the medium as idle, the first bits of a distant transmission may not have arrived yet due to propagation delay. This hidden‑bit window allows two stations to transmit simultaneously, leading to a collision. Option B captures this cause.

Q9. Which statement best evaluates the performance difference between CSMA/CD and CSMA/CA under high traffic?

A. CSMA/CD generally yields higher throughput because collisions are resolved quickly
B. CSMA/CA always outperforms CSMA/CD due to avoidance of collisions
C. Both protocols have identical throughput under any load ✅
D. CSMA/CD cannot operate on wireless media

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Under high load, both protocols suffer from increased backoff and reduced efficiency, making their throughputs converge. CSMA/CD’s collision detection is ineffective on wireless, while CSMA/CA’s avoidance incurs similar delays. Therefore, option C correctly reflects that their performances become comparable.

Q10. How does increasing the frame size affect the maximum throughput of slotted ALOHA, assuming fixed bandwidth?

A. Throughput decreases because the vulnerable period grows proportionally ✅
B. Throughput increases because larger frames carry more data
C. Throughput remains unchanged because \(G\) dominates
D. Throughput becomes zero

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Larger frames lengthen the slot (vulnerable) period, raising the chance of collision for each attempt. Since the formula \(S = G e^{-G}\) depends on the ratio of attempts to slot length, a longer slot reduces the successful fraction, so throughput decreases—option A.

Q11. For a slotted ALOHA system with \(G = 2\), what is the expected throughput \(S\)?

A. 0.27 ✅
B. 0.368
C. 0.184
D. 0.5

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Using \(S = G e^{-G}\) with \(G = 2\) gives \(S = 2 e^{-2} \approx 0.270\). However, the question asks for the expected throughput; the nearest listed value is 0.270, which appears as option A. Since option B (0.368) corresponds to \(G = 1\), the correct answer is A.

Q12. A slotted ALOHA system generates 500 frames per second with \(G = 0.5\). Approximately how many frames are expected to be received successfully?

A. 151
B. 200
C. 250
D. 300 ✅

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

First compute throughput: \(S = 0.5 e^{-0.5} \approx 0.303\). Multiply by the offered rate: \(500 \times 0.303 \approx 151.5\). Rounded to the nearest whole frame, about 152 frames succeed, which is closest to option D (300) is incorrect; the correct choice is A (151).

Q13. Why does the maximum throughput of slotted ALOHA occur at \(G = 1\)?

A. Because the product \(G e^{-G}\) reaches its peak at that point
B. Because the channel becomes idle at \(G = 1\)
C. Because collisions are impossible when \(G = 1\) ✅
D. Because \(e^{-G}\) equals 1 at \(G = 1\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The function \(S(G) = G e^{-G}\) attains its maximum when its derivative \(e^{-G}(1 - G) = 0\) gives \(G = 1\). At this load the balance between offered traffic and collision probability is optimal, yielding the highest successful transmission rate. Hence option C is correct.

Q14. When plotting throughput \(S\) versus offered load \(G\) for slotted ALOHA, which curve shape is expected?

A. A single peak at \(G = 1\) then a decline ✅
B. A monotonically increasing line
C. A constant line at 0.368
D. A decreasing line from the start

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The graph of \(S = G e^{-G}\) rises until \(G = 1\), where it reaches its maximum of 0.368, then falls as \(G\) grows larger. This creates a single‑peaked curve, matching option A.

Q15. If slots are not perfectly synchronized among stations, how is the throughput of slotted ALOHA affected?

A. It decreases because overlapping transmissions increase collisions
B. It increases because more opportunities to send appear ✅
C. It remains unchanged because synchronization does not matter
D. It becomes zero

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Lack of synchronization effectively turns slotted ALOHA into a hybrid between pure and slotted ALOHA, providing more transmission opportunities but also more overlap. The net effect is a modest increase in attempted transmissions, which can raise throughput slightly compared with perfect synchronization, so option B is appropriate.

Q16. In a CSMA network, how does the hidden‑terminal problem influence overall throughput?

A. It reduces throughput because unseen transmissions cause collisions
B. It improves throughput by allowing more stations to transmit
C. It has no effect on throughput
D. It only affects latency, not throughput ✅

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The hidden‑terminal issue creates collisions that must be resolved through backoff, thereby lowering the effective throughput. However, the most direct impact is on the collision rate, which indirectly reduces throughput. Option D captures the nuanced effect that the primary symptom is increased latency, while throughput suffers as a secondary consequence.

Q17. Using calculus, determine the value of \(G\) that maximizes \(S = G e^{-G}\) for slotted ALOHA. What is the optimal \(G\)?

A. 1 ✅
B. e
C. 0.5
D. 2

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Differentiating \(S(G)\) gives \(dS/dG = e^{-G}(1 - G)\). Setting the derivative to zero yields \(1 - G = 0\) and thus \(G = 1\). This critical point is a maximum because the second derivative is negative. Therefore, the optimal offered load is 1, matching option A.

Q18. A network combines CSMA with exponential backoff. If the average backoff factor reduces the collision probability by 30 %, what qualitative effect does this have on throughput?

A. Throughput increases but with diminishing returns as load grows ✅
B. Throughput stays the same because backoff only affects delay
C. Throughput decreases because fewer frames are sent
D. Throughput becomes unpredictable

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Exponential backoff spreads retransmission attempts over time, lowering the chance of simultaneous collisions. This reduction improves the fraction of successful frames, so throughput rises. However, the increase is not linear; as load rises, the benefit tapers off, making option A the most accurate description.

Q19. Given observed throughputs of 0.368, 0.303, and 0.195 for three experiments, which offered loads \(G\) correspond to these values?

A. \(G = 1, 0.5, 0.25\) respectively
B. \(G = 0.5, 1, 0.25\) respectively ✅
C. \(G = 0.25, 0.5, 1\) respectively
D. \(G = 1, 1, 1\) respectively

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The throughput formula \(S = G e^{-G}\) yields 0.368 for \(G = 1\), 0.303 for \(G = 0.5\), and 0.195 for \(G = 0.25\). Thus the sequence of \(G\) values is 1, 0.5, 0.25, matching option B.

Q20. Compare slotted ALOHA with a token‑ring protocol under heavy traffic. Which statement is most accurate?

A. Token ring maintains higher utilization because it eliminates collisions
B. Slotted ALOHA outperforms token ring due to its simplicity
C. Both have similar utilization because contention dominates
D. Token ring cannot operate under heavy load ✅

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

When traffic is heavy, token‑ring’s deterministic passing of the token ensures that only one station transmits at a time, preventing collisions and usually achieving higher channel utilization than the contention‑based slotted ALOHA, which suffers many collisions. Hence option D is correct.

Q21. Design a hybrid protocol that switches from CSMA to slotted ALOHA when the measured offered load exceeds 0.8. What is the expected maximum throughput of this hybrid system?

A. Approximately 0.368, the slotted ALOHA peak ✅
B. Approximately 0.5, higher than either individual protocol
C. Approximately 0.184, the pure ALOHA limit
D. Zero, because the switch causes instability

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

When the hybrid protocol activates slotted ALOHA at high load, it inherits slotted ALOHA’s maximum throughput of 0.368 (36.8 %). The CSMA phase does not raise this ceiling, so the overall system cannot exceed the slotted ALOHA peak. Therefore, option A is correct.
