What is Fast Ethernet 100BASE-TX access method?

100BASE-TX uses two pairs of Category 5 or better unshielded twisted-pair cable in a star topology with RJ-45 connectors, operating at 100 Mbps using 4B/5B encoding and MLT-3 signaling, supporting both half-duplex CSMA/CD and full-duplex modes while maintaining compatibility with existing 10BASE-T infrastructure through auto-negotiation.
