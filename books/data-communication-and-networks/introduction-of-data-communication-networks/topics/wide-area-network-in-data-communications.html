What is Wide Area Network in data communications?

A Wide Area Network (WAN) spans large geographical areas ranging from cities to entire countries or continents, utilizing leased telecommunication lines or satellite links to connect multiple LANs and enable long-distance data exchange across vast regions.

📝 All Wide Area Network in data communications MCQs

Q1. What is the primary definition of a Wide Area Network (WAN)?

A.A network that interconnects devices over a large geographic area ✅
B.An internal network limited to a single building
C.A network that uses only wireless links
D.A local network confined to a single floor
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: A WAN is defined as a network that interconnects devices over a large geographic area, spanning towns, states, or even countries. This distinguishes it from LANs, which are confined to smaller, localized environments.

Q2. Which device most commonly interconnects LANs within a WAN architecture?

A.A hub
B.B switch
C.C router ✅
D.D repeater
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Routers are the primary devices used to interconnect LANs across a WAN. They examine network-layer information and forward packets between distinct networks, whereas switches operate at the data-link layer within a LAN.

Q3. If a company adopts a point-to-point WAN, what can be logically inferred about the number of endpoints involved?

A.Exactly two endpoints ✅
B.At least three endpoints
C.Exactly one endpoint
D.An undefined number of endpoints
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: A point-to-point WAN by definition connects exactly two communicating devices, establishing a direct link between two endpoints.

Q4. In a switched WAN, if one of the intermediate switches fails, which outcome is most likely?

A.The entire WAN becomes inoperable
B.Traffic can be rerouted through alternate switches ✅
C.Latency drops to zero
D.Encryption is automatically enabled
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Switched WANs are designed with redundancy; when a switch fails, traffic can often be rerouted through other available switches, preserving connectivity albeit with possible increased latency.

Q5. When a new router is added to one end of an existing point-to-point WAN, what is the most probable effect on routing decisions?

A.All routes remain unchanged
B.Existing routes are recalculated to include the new router ✅
C.Packets will be dropped until the router is removed
D.The WAN automatically converts to a switched WAN
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Adding a router introduces a new routing decision point; the network will recalculate paths to incorporate the new device, potentially altering packet forwarding while still maintaining the point-to-point nature.

Q6. Which statement best compares ownership of LANs and WANs?

A.LANs are privately owned while WANs are typically owned by communication service providers ✅
B.Both LANs and WANs are always privately owned
C.LANs are owned by service providers and WANs by enterprises
D.B
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

Q7. What is a key advantage of a switched WAN compared to a point-to-point WAN?

A.Higher latency
B.Scalability to many endpoints ✅
C.Lower security
D.Reduced cost
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Switched WANs can connect multiple endpoints through a network of switches, offering greater scalability than the two‑endpoint limitation of point-to-point WANs.

Q8. How do routers differ from switches in the context of WAN connectivity?

A.Routers operate at Layer 2 while switches operate at Layer 3 ✅
B.Routers make forwarding decisions based on IP addresses
C.Switches forward packets based on MAC addresses
D.Routers amplify signals
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Routers work at the network layer (Layer 3) using IP addresses to determine the best path, whereas switches operate at the data-link layer (Layer 2) forwarding frames based on MAC addresses.

Q9. Why might an organization prefer leasing a WAN service rather than building its own private WAN?

A.Leasing provides higher bandwidth
B.Leasing reduces capital expenditure and maintenance responsibilities ✅
C.Leasing eliminates all latency
D.Leasing allows use of proprietary protocols
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Leasing a WAN shifts the capital cost of infrastructure to an operational expense and transfers maintenance and upgrade responsibilities to the service provider, making it financially and operationally attractive for many organizations.

Q10. How does a router direct packets between two LANs across a WAN?

A.By broadcasting to all devices
B.By examining the destination IP address and forwarding based on its routing table ✅
C.By changing packet sizes
D.By converting packets to analog signals
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Routers inspect the destination IP address, consult their routing tables, and forward packets toward the appropriate next hop, enabling communication between LANs over a WAN.

Q11. In what way does a switched WAN function as a combination of several point-to-point WANs?

A.It uses wireless signals exclusively
B.It connects multiple point-to-point links via switches ✅
C.It eliminates the need for routers
D.It operates only within a single building
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A switched WAN interconnects multiple point-to-point links through switches, effectively creating a mesh where each switch acts as a hub for several dedicated links, enabling many‑to‑many communication.

Q12. Designing a small internetwork with two LANs and a switched WAN containing three switches, which topology best ensures redundancy?

A.Star topology
B.Ring topology
C.Mesh topology ✅
D.Bus topology
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: A mesh topology, where each switch is interconnected with the others, provides multiple paths for data, ensuring that if one link fails, alternate routes maintain connectivity, which is essential for redundancy.

Q13. If a point-to-point WAN has a latency of ms and a switched WAN adds ms per hop, what is the total latency for a packet traversing two hops in the switched WAN compared to the point-to-point link?

A.Switched WAN is slower by ms
B.Switched WAN is faster by ms
C.Switched WAN latency equals point-to-point latency
D.Switched WAN is slower by ms ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: Two hops in the switched WAN add ms. Compared to the point-to-point latency of ms, the switched WAN is slower by ms, making answer D correct.

Q14. When upgrading a WAN from a T1 line (1.544 Mbps) to a 100 Mbps Ethernet link while retaining the same number of switches, what is the expected impact on overall bandwidth?

A.Bandwidth decreases
B.Bandwidth remains unchanged
C.Bandwidth increases dramatically ✅
D.Latency becomes zero
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Replacing a 1.544 Mbps T1 line with a 100 Mbps Ethernet link raises the raw transmission capacity by roughly 65 times, dramatically increasing the WAN’s bandwidth while the number of switches does not affect this raw capacity.

Q15. In an internetwork containing multiple WANs, if router R1 blocks a packet, which inference is most reasonable?

A.The destination is unreachable
B.The packet originated from an unauthorized source ✅
C.The packet has a malformed header
D.All routers will drop the packet
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: When a router blocks a packet, it typically indicates the packet failed a security or policy check, such as originating from an unauthorized source, rather than a simple reachability issue.

Q16. How do security considerations differ between using a public switched WAN and a private point-to-point WAN?

A.Public switched WAN offers higher encryption by default
B.Private point-to-point WAN reduces exposure to external threats ✅
C.Public switched WAN eliminates the need for firewalls
D.Private point-to-point WAN cannot be monitored
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A private point-to-point WAN limits exposure to only the two endpoints involved, reducing the attack surface compared to a public switched WAN that traverses shared infrastructure, thereby offering enhanced security.
