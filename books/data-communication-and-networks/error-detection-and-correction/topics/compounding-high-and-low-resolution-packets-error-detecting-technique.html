What is Compounding High and Low Resolution Packets Error Detecting Technique?

Compounding high and low resolution packets is an advanced strategy that sends redundant versions of data at different fidelity levels, ensuring that even if high-resolution packets are corrupted beyond recovery, the low-resolution versions provide sufficient baseline information for partial reconstruction.
