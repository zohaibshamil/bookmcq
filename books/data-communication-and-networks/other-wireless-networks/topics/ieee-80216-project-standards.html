What is IEEE 802.16 project standards?

The IEEE 802.16 project defines the technical specifications and air interface standards for WiMAX networks to ensure different devices and base stations can communicate effectively and interoperate globally.

Q1. What is the effective bandwidth of a telephone line used for data transmission as mentioned in the passage?

A.3000 Hz ✅
B.3300 Hz
C.1500 Hz
D.3500 Hz
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The passage states that a line with a nominal frequency of 3300 Hz provides an effective bandwidth of 3000 Hz for data. This value is derived by excluding the edge portions reserved for voice, leaving the central 3000 Hz for reliable data transmission.

Q2. If a data signal requires a bandwidth of 2500 Hz, can it be transmitted without occupying the edge frequencies of the 3000 Hz telephone channel?

A.Yes, but only if modulation reduces bandwidth
B.No, because edge frequencies are needed for a guard band
C.No, because data always uses the full channel
D.Yes, because 2500 Hz fits within the central portion ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: A 2500 Hz signal can be placed entirely within the central 3000 Hz channel, leaving 250 Hz on each side as a natural guard band. Since the edges are reserved for voice tolerance, the data does not need to use them, making transmission feasible without compromising integrity.

Q3. Assuming the edge frequencies are suddenly made available for data, what is the most likely effect on the error rate of a 2800 Hz data stream?

A.Decrease error rate due to more bandwidth
B.Increase error rate due to higher interference ✅
C.No change in error rate
D.Error rate becomes unpredictable
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Using the edge frequencies introduces portions of the spectrum that were originally chosen for voice because they tolerate more interference. When data occupies these noisy edges, the signal experiences additional distortion, leading to a higher probability of bit errors and thus an increased error rate for the 2800 Hz stream.

Q4. Which of the following best contrasts the tolerance to interference between voice transmission and data transmission over the same telephone line?

A.Voice can tolerate high distortion; data cannot
B.Voice requires low distortion; data tolerates high distortion
C.Voice tolerates moderate interference; data requires low interference ✅
D.Both voice and data have identical tolerance levels
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Voice signals are designed to remain intelligible even with noticeable interference, allowing the use of edge frequencies. In contrast, data signals need precise integrity; any distortion can corrupt bits, so they must avoid the noisy edges and occupy only the clean central portion of the spectrum.

Q5. Given a cable bandwidth of 3500 Hz, evaluate how the effective data bandwidth changes if the safety margin of 300 Hz at each edge is maintained?

A.Remains 3000 Hz
B.Decreases to 2900 Hz ✅
C.Increases to 3200 Hz
D.Becomes 3400 Hz
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Subtracting the safety margins (300 Hz on each side) from the total 3500 Hz yields an effective data band of 3500 − 600 = 2900 Hz. This reduction ensures that the edges, which are prone to interference, are kept free for voice or protective purposes, preserving data integrity.

Q6. Differentiate between the raw cable bandwidth and the effective bandwidth for a line with a nominal bandwidth of 3300 Hz when a 10 % guard band is required at each edge?

A.3300 Hz raw, 3000 Hz effective
B.3300 Hz raw, 2640 Hz effective ✅
C.3000 Hz raw, 2700 Hz effective
D.3500 Hz raw, 3000 Hz effective
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A 10 % guard band of 330 Hz on each side consumes 660 Hz total. Removing this from the nominal 3300 Hz leaves 3300 − 660 = 2640 Hz as the usable bandwidth for data. The raw bandwidth remains the full 3300 Hz, while the effective bandwidth reflects the portion free from edge interference.

Q7. Applying the principle that signal bandwidth must be smaller than cable bandwidth, which configuration ensures compliance for an IEEE 802.16 link requiring 2800 Hz?

A.Use 3500 Hz cable but reserve 600 Hz for voice
B.Use 2500 Hz cable, allocate 2800 Hz
C.Use 3000 Hz cable, allocate 2800 Hz to data ✅
D.Use 2800 Hz cable with no guard band
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: With a 3000 Hz cable, the 2800 Hz data signal fits comfortably, leaving 200 Hz on each side as a natural guard band. This satisfies the rule that the signal bandwidth must be smaller than the cable bandwidth, while also providing margin for potential interference.

Q8. Synthesize a bandwidth allocation strategy for a mixed voice‑data service where voice needs 500 Hz at each edge for tolerance, and data requires 2000 Hz centrally. What total cable bandwidth is minimally required?

A.2500 Hz
B.3500 Hz
C.4000 Hz
D.3000 Hz ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: The voice guard bands require 500 Hz on each side (total 1000 Hz). Adding the central 2000 Hz data requirement gives 1000 + 2000 = 3000 Hz. Therefore, a cable bandwidth of at least 3000 Hz is needed to accommodate both services without overlap.
