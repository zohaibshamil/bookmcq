📝 All 4G LTE and advanced cellular networks MCQs

Q1. What is the typical bandwidth allocated for a voice channel in a 4G network?

🔹 A. 4 kHz
🔹 B. 8 kHz
🔹 C. 12 kHz
🔹 D. 16 kHz

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

A voice channel in legacy telephony and early 4G voice services is allocated a narrow band of about 4 kHz. This bandwidth accommodates the human speech frequency range while minimizing spectrum usage, making it the standard allocation for circuit‑switched voice calls.

Q2. If a trunk can multiplex 200 voice channels each occupying 4 kHz, what is the minimum aggregate bandwidth the trunk must support?

🔹 A. 0.4 MHz
🔹 B. 0.8 MHz
🔹 C. 0.2 MHz
🔹 D. 1.6 MHz

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Multiplying 200 channels by 4 kHz gives 800 kHz, i.e., 0.8 MHz. This is the theoretical minimum bandwidth required before accounting for guard bands or overhead, illustrating how multiplexing aggregates many narrow channels into a wider pipe.

Q3. Which latency range best represents typical 4G performance compared with 3G?

🔹 A. 150 ms
🔹 B. 30 ms
🔹 C. 50 ms
🔹 D. 100 ms

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

4G LTE generally achieves end‑to‑end latency around 30–50 ms, whereas 3G networks often exhibit about 100 ms. The reduction stems from flatter network architecture and more efficient radio protocols, making 4G noticeably faster for interactive applications.

Q4. Applying the principle of frequency reuse, how many distinct cells can share the same 20 MHz spectrum if the reuse factor is 4?

🔹 A. 8
🔹 B. 12
🔹 C. 4
🔹 D. 16

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

With a reuse factor of 4, the total number of cells that can reuse the same spectrum is the inverse of the factor multiplied by the total cells in a cluster. For a large network this yields 4 × 4 = 16 cells sharing the 20 MHz band.

Q5. A 4G base station uses OFDM with 1024 subcarriers each spaced at 15 kHz. What is the total occupied bandwidth?

🔹 A. 12.8 MHz
🔹 B. 15.36 MHz
🔹 C. 10.24 MHz
🔹 D. 8.192 MHz

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The total bandwidth equals the number of subcarriers times the spacing: 1024 × 15 kHz = 15 360 kHz, or 15.36 MHz. This bandwidth includes the useful data portion of the OFDM signal before adding guard bands.

Q6. Evaluate the impact on spectral efficiency when moving from 16‑QAM to 64‑QAM modulation in LTE. Which statement is most accurate?

🔹 A. Decreases
🔹 B. Increases by 33%
🔹 C. Remains same
🔹 D. Spectral efficiency doubles

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Going from 16‑QAM (4 bits per symbol) to 64‑QAM (6 bits per symbol) raises the bits per symbol by 2/6 ≈ 33 %. Assuming the same symbol rate, spectral efficiency improves by roughly one‑third, provided the channel quality supports the higher constellation.

Q7. Which component in a 4G network is responsible for converting digital baseband signals to RF for transmission over the air?

🔹 A. Power Amplifier
🔹 B. Mixer
🔹 C. Digital Front End
🔹 D. Antenna

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The power amplifier takes the digitally processed baseband signal, up‑converts it to the carrier frequency, and boosts its power for over‑the‑air transmission. It is the critical link between the baseband processor and the antenna system.

Q8. If a subscriber's local loop number is 5678 and the office code is 345, what is the full 7‑digit telephone number?

🔹 A. 345-5678
🔹 B. 345-8765
🔹 C. 543-5678
🔹 D. 345-5679

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Telephone numbers in this scheme consist of the three‑digit office prefix followed by the four‑digit local loop. Concatenating 345 with 5678 yields the standard format 345‑5678.

Q9. Compare the handover mechanisms in 4G (X2 handover) vs 5G (NG handover). Which key difference is correct?

🔹 A. X2 handover is faster
🔹 B. NG handover requires core network involvement
🔹 C. Both are identical
🔹 D. X2 uses satellite links

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

In 4G, X2 handover operates between eNodeBs without contacting the core, allowing rapid transitions. 5G NG handover, however, involves the core network (AMF/SMF), adding signaling steps and making the process more involved.

Q10. What principle allows multiple users to share the same time slot in LTE's TDMA scheme?

🔹 A. Code Division
🔹 B. Frequency Division
🔹 C. Time Division
🔹 D. Spatial Multiplexing

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA) allocates distinct time intervals within a shared frequency channel, enabling different users to transmit in separate slots while reusing the same spectral resources.

Q11. A trunk carries 1000 voice channels, each 4 kHz. If the trunk uses statistical multiplexing achieving 80 % utilization, what is the required bandwidth?

🔹 A. 3.2 MHz
🔹 B. 4.0 MHz
🔹 C. 2.0 MHz
🔹 D. 5.0 MHz

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The nominal bandwidth is 1000 × 4 kHz = 4 MHz. With 80 % utilization, the effective needed bandwidth is 0.8 × 4 MHz = 3.2 MHz, reflecting the savings from statistical sharing of idle channels.

Q12. Which of the following best differentiates 4G LTE‑Advanced from standard LTE in terms of carrier aggregation?

🔹 A. Allows up to 2 component carriers
🔹 B. Supports up to 5 component carriers
🔹 C. No carrier aggregation
🔹 D. Only uses single carrier

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

LTE‑Advanced extends LTE by permitting aggregation of up to five component carriers, boosting peak data rates and improving spectral efficiency. Standard LTE typically aggregates only two carriers or none at all.

Q13. Applying the Shannon‑Hartley theorem, if the signal‑to‑noise ratio is 20 dB and bandwidth is 10 MHz, what is the theoretical maximum data rate?

🔹 A. ≈66 Mbps
🔹 B. 100 Mbps
🔹 C. ≈132 Mbps
🔹 D. 200 Mbps

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Convert 20 dB to linear SNR: 10^(20/10)=100. Capacity = B·log₂(1+SNR)=10 MHz·log₂(101)≈10 MHz·6.658≈66.6 Mbps, giving roughly 66 Mbps as the upper bound.

Q14. In 4G networks, which technology enables simultaneous transmission and reception on the same frequency band?

🔹 A. FDMA
🔹 B. TDD
🔹 C. FDD
🔹 D. OFDM

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Frequency Division Duplex (FDD) allocates separate uplink and downlink channels on paired frequencies, allowing simultaneous two‑way communication. This contrasts with Time Division Duplex (TDD), which alternates transmission direction in time.)
