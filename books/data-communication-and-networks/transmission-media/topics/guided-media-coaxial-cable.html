What is Guided media: Coaxial Cable?

Coaxial cable is a guided transmission medium featuring a central copper conductor surrounded by insulation, a metallic shield, and an outer jacket, which provides better noise immunity and higher bandwidth than twisted pair for cable television and broadband internet.
