What is Interfacing of D/A Converter in 8085 Microprocessor?

Interfacing of a D/A converter with the 8085 involves connecting the microprocessor’s data bus to the DAC’s digital inputs via a latch (e.g., 74LS373) to hold stable data during conversion, using address decoding to generate chip select signals, and employing control lines like to trigger the conversion, ensuring the analog output accurately reflects the latched digital value without glitches.
