What is Binary Ladder D/A Converter in 8085 Microprocessor?

A Binary Ladder D/A converter, commonly known as an R-2R ladder DAC, overcomes the limitations of weighted resistors by using only two resistor values R R R and 2R 2R 2R arranged in a repeating ladder network, ensuring that each bit contributes a current proportional to its binary weight such that Vout=Vref⋅D/2n V_{out} = V_{ref} \cdot D / 2^n Vout​=Vref​⋅D/2n, providing better accuracy and easier integration for 8085-based systems.

Q17. If the most‑significant byte of the terminal count is loaded before the least‑significant byte, how will the DMA controller interpret the transfer length?
