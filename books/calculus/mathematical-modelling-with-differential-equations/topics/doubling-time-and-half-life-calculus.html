Example:
Bacteria with k=0.05 k=0.05 k=0.05/hr has doubling time Td=ln⁡20.05≈13.86 T_d = \frac{\ln 2}{0.05} \approx 13.86 Td​=0.05ln2​≈13.86 hours before population doubles.

Reason:
These metrics provide intuitive understanding of exponential rates, facilitating comparisons across different growth or decay scenarios.
