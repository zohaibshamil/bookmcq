What is Modeling with differential equations applications?

Definition:
Differential equations model real-world phenomena by relating a function to its derivatives, allowing us to predict system behavior over time using mathematical relationships like dtdy​=f(t,y).

Example:
If a bacteria population grows at rate dtdP​=0.1P with P(0)=100, then P(t)=100e0.1t.

Reason:
This approach transforms physical laws into solvable mathematical forms, enabling precise predictions of dynamic systems in science and engineering.

📖 Explanation: The uninhibited model dtdy​=ky assumes unlimited resources, leading to exponential growth without bound. Using the uninhibited model ignores these density-dependent factors, making it invalid near equilibrium.
