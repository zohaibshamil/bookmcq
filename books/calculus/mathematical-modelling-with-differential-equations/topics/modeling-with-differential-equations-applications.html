What is Modeling with differential equations applications?

Definition:
Differential equations model real-world phenomena by relating a function to its derivatives, allowing us to predict system behavior over time using mathematical relationships like dydt=f(t,y)\frac{dy}{dt} = f(t,y)dtdy​=f(t,y).

Example:
If a bacteria population grows at rate dPdt=0.1P\frac{dP}{dt} = 0.1PdtdP​=0.1P with P(0)=100P(0)=100P(0)=100, then P(t)=100e0.1tP(t) = 100e^{0.1t}P(t)=100e0.1t.

Reason:
This approach transforms physical laws into solvable mathematical forms, enabling precise predictions of dynamic systems in science and engineering.
