What is Inhibited / Logistic population growth model?

Definition:
Logistic growth accounts for limited resources by introducing carrying capacity KKK, modeled as dPdt=kP(1−PK)\frac{dP}{dt} = kP(1 - \frac{P}{K})dtdP​=kP(1−KP​) with S-shaped curve approaching KKK.

Example:
With K=1000K=1000K=1000, k=0.1k=0.1k=0.1, P0=100P_0=100P0​=100, population grows rapidly then slows, approaching 1000 as t→∞t \to \inftyt→∞.

Reason:
Real populations face resource limits; this model captures saturation effects, providing more realistic long-term predictions than exponential growth.
