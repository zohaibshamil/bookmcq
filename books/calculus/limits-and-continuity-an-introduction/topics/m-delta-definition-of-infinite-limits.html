What is M delta definition of infinite limits?

The formal definition for infinite limits states that \( \lim_{x \to c} f(x) = \infty \) if for every \( M > 0 \) there exists a \( \delta > 0 \) such that \( f(x) > M \) whenever \( 0 < |x - c| < \delta \).
