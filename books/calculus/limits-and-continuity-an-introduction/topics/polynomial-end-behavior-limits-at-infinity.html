📝 Polynomial end behavior limits at infinity

📖 From Calculus • 2. Limits and Continuity an Introduction • 14 questions available

What is Polynomial end behavior limits at infinity?

Definition:
For a polynomial \( f(x) = a_n x^n + \cdots + a_0 \), the end behavior as \( x \to \infty \) or \( x \to -\infty \) is dominated by the leading term \( a_n x^n \), so \( \lim_{x \to \infty} f(x) \) is \( \infty \) if \( a_n > 0 \), \( -\infty \) if \( a_n < 0 \), and similarly for \( -\infty \) depending on the parity of \( n \). This means only the highest degree term matters for large \( |x| \), as lower terms become negligible.

Example:
Find \( \lim_{x \to \infty} (-2x^3 + 5x - 1) \).
Solution: Leading term \( -2x^3 \to -\infty \), so limit is \( -\infty \).

Reason:
This simplifies analysis of polynomial growth, which is important for curve sketching and for comparing polynomial rates in optimization and approximation problems where higher-degree terms dictate long-term trends.

Q1. What is \\(\\displaystyle\\lim_{x\\to +\\infty}\\left(1+\\frac{1}{x}\\right)^{x}\\) ?

A. e ✅
B. 1
C. 0
D. ∞

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Using the definition of e as the limit of the compound‑interest expression, we evaluate \\(\\left(1+\\frac{1}{x}\\right)^{x}\\) as \\(x\\) grows without bound. The base approaches 1 while the exponent grows large, and the expression converges to the constant e≈2.71828. Hence the limit equals e.

Q3. Given that \\(\\displaystyle\\lim_{x\\to +\\infty}\\left(1+\\frac{1}{x}\\right)^{x}=e\\), which of the following statements about the sequence \\(a_n = \\left(1+\\frac{1}{n}\\right)^{n}\\) is true?

Q5. Evaluate \\(\\displaystyle\\lim_{x\\to +\\infty}\\left(1+\\frac{k}{x}\\right)^{x}\\) for a constant \\(k>0\\). Which expression correctly represents the limit?

Q7. Using the definition of limit, justify that \\(\\displaystyle\\lim_{x\\to -\\infty}\\left(1+\\frac{1}{x}\\right)^{x}=e\\) despite the base \\((1+1/x)\\) being less than 1 for large negative x.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Write \\(L=\\lim_{x\\to-\\infty}\\left(1+\\frac{1}{x}\\right)^{x}\\) and consider \\(\\ln L=\\lim_{x\\to-\\infty}x\\ln\\left(1+\\frac{1}{x}\\right)\\).
