What is Limit of 1 - cos x over x as x approaches 0?

This important trigonometric limit evaluates to \( \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1 - \cos x}{x} = 0 \), often derived by multiplying by the conjugate and applying the \( \frac{\sin x}{x} \) limit, essential for differentiating cosine.

Q1. What is \\(\\displaystyle\\lim_{x\\to 0}\\frac{1-\\cos x}{x}\\)?

A. 0 ✅
B. 1
C. undefined
D. 2

📖 Explanation:
