What is Logistic growth curves calculus?

Definition:
Logistic growth models population dynamics using P(t)=K1+Ae−ktP(t) = \frac{K}{1 + Ae^{-kt}}P(t)=1+Ae−ktK​, where KKK is carrying capacity. The derivative P′(t)P'(t)P′(t) shows growth rate slows as population approaches KKK, creating an S-shaped curve distinct from exponential growth patterns.

Example:
If P(t)=1001+9e−tP(t) = \frac{100}{1 + 9e^{-t}}P(t)=1+9e−t100​, then P(0)=10P(0) = 10P(0)=10. As t→∞t \to \inftyt→∞, P(t)→100P(t) \to 100P(t)→100, the limiting capacity.

Reason:
This model accounts for resource limitations, unlike exponential growth, making it realistic for biological populations constrained by environmental factors.
