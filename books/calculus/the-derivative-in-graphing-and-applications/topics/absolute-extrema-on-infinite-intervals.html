What is Absolute extrema on infinite intervals?

Definition:
On infinite intervals, absolute extrema may not exist. Analysis involves checking critical points and evaluating limits as x→±∞x→±∞. If limits exceed critical values, no absolute extremum exists; otherwise, the largest or smallest critical value may be the global extremum.

Example:
For f(x)=1x2+1f(x)=x2+11​ on (−∞,∞)(−∞,∞), max is 11 at x=0x=0, but no min exists as f(x)→0f(x)→0 without reaching it.

Reason:
Infinite domains require limit analysis to determine if function values boundedly approach a supremum or infimum without necessarily attaining them.
