Q1. Which of the following correctly defines the profit function \(P(x)\) in terms of revenue \(R(x)\) and cost \(C(x)\)?

🔹 A. \(P(x)=C(x)-R(x)\)
🔹 B. \(P(x)=R(x)+C(x)\)
🔹 C. \(P(x)=R(x)-C(x)\)
🔹 D. \(P(x)=R(x)\times C(x)\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Profit is calculated as revenue minus cost, so the correct expression is \(P(x)=R(x)-C(x)\). The other choices either reverse the order or combine the quantities incorrectly.

Q2. In the cost function \(C(x)=a+bx+cx^{2}\), what does the constant \(a\) represent?

🔹 A. Variable manufacturing cost per unit
🔹 B. Fixed overhead cost independent of output
🔹 C. Quadratic cost coefficient
🔹 D. Price per unit

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The constant \(a\) captures overhead costs such as rent and insurance that must be paid regardless of how many units are produced.

Q3. If each unit sells for \(p\) dollars, which expression gives the total revenue for \(x\) units?

🔹 A. \(R(x)=p+x\)
🔹 B. \(R(x)=px\)
🔹 C. \(R(x)=p-x\)
🔹 D. \(R(x)=p x^{2}\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Revenue equals price per unit times quantity sold, so \(R(x)=px\). The other formulas do not represent total sales revenue.

Q4. Given a production capacity limit \(l\), the feasible values of \(x\) satisfy which interval?

🔹 A. \(0 < x < l\)
🔹 B. \(-l \le x \le l\)
🔹 C. \(0 \le x \le l\)
🔹 D. \(x \ge l\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The firm can produce any non‑negative amount up to its capacity, so the admissible interval is \(0 \le x \le l\).

Q5. For the profit function \(P(x)=200x-(500000+80x+0.003x^{2})\), what is the derivative \(P'(x)\) equal to?

🔹 A. \(200-80-0.006x\)
🔹 B. \(200-80+0.006x\)
🔹 C. \(200+80-0.006x\)
🔹 D. \(200-0.003x\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Differentiating gives \(P'(x)=200-(80+0.006x)=200-80-0.006x\). This represents marginal profit.

Q6. Which cost function has a higher fixed overhead than \(C(x)=500000+80x+0.003x^{2}\)?

🔹 A. \(C(x)=600000+80x+0.003x^{2}\)
🔹 B. \(C(x)=500000+70x+0.003x^{2}\)
🔹 C. \(C(x)=500000+80x+0.002x^{2}\)
🔹 D. \(C(x)=500000+80x\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The first option adds \$100,000 to the constant term, raising the overhead while leaving the variable parts unchanged.

Q7. If the selling price per unit increases from \$200 to \$250, what is the immediate effect on the profit function \(P(x)=px-(a+bx+cx^{2})\)?

🔹 A. The linear term coefficient increases, shifting the profit curve upward.
🔹 B. The quadratic term coefficient increases.
🔹 C. The overhead \(a\) decreases.
🔹 D. The profit function becomes independent of \(x\).

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Raising \(p\) raises the coefficient of the linear term \(px\), lifting the entire profit curve without altering its shape.

Q8. If the overhead \(a\) rises while \(b, c,\) and \(p\) stay unchanged, how does the profit‑maximizing output \(x^{*}\) change?

🔹 A. \(x^{*}\) increases.
🔹 B. \(x^{*}\) decreases.
🔹 C. \(x^{*}\) stays the same.
🔹 D. Cannot determine without numbers.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The optimal output is found from \(P'(x)=p-b-2cx=0\), which does not involve \(a\). Therefore changes in overhead do not affect the maximizing \(x\).

Q9. Consider \(P_{1}(x)=200x-(500000+80x+0.003x^{2})\) and \(P_{2}(x)=210x-(500000+90x+0.003x^{2})\). Which statement is true about their profit‑maximizing outputs?

🔹 A. \(x^{*}_{1}=x^{*}_{2}\)
🔹 B. \(x^{*}_{1}>x^{*}_{2}\)
🔹 C. \(x^{*}_{1}<x^{*}_{2}\)
🔹 D. No maximum exists for either.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Both give \(x^{*}=(p-b)/(2c)\). For each, \(p-b=200-80=120\) and \(210-90=120\); with the same \(c\), the optimal \(x\) is identical.

Q10. Using \(P(x)=200x-(500000+80x+0.003x^{2})\), what is the profit‑maximizing production level \(x^{*}\) (ignore capacity limit)?

🔹 A. 10,000 units
🔹 B. 15,000 units
🔹 C. 20,000 units
🔹 D. 25,000 units

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Set \(P'(x)=0\): \(200-80-0.006x=0\) ⇒ \(x=120/0.006=20,000\).

Q11. What is the marginal profit \(P'(x)\) when \(x=10,000\) for the profit function in the example?

🔹 A. \$60
🔹 B. \$80
🔹 C. \$100
🔹 D. \$120

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

\(P'(x)=200-80-0.006x\). Substituting \(x=10,000\) gives \(120-60=60\) dollars.

Q12. If the optimal \(x^{*}=20,000\) exceeds the capacity limit \(l=15,000\), what production level will the firm actually choose to maximize profit?

🔹 A. 0
🔹 B. 15000
🔹 C. 20000
🔹 D. 30000

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

When the unconstrained optimum lies beyond capacity, the firm produces at the highest feasible level, i.e., the capacity limit \(l=15,000\).

Q13. In the cost function \(C(x)=a+bx+cx^{2}\), a positive \(c\) indicates what about marginal cost as output increases?

🔹 A. Marginal cost decreases.
🔹 B. Marginal cost remains constant.
🔹 C. Marginal cost increases.
🔹 D. Marginal cost becomes negative.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The marginal cost is \(C'(x)=b+2cx\); with \(c>0\), this derivative grows as \(x\) grows, so marginal cost rises.

Q14. If the selling price \(p\) increases by 10%, how does the optimal output \(x^{*}=\frac{p-b}{2c}\) change, assuming \(b\) and \(c\) are unchanged?

🔹 A. Increases by 10%
🔹 B. Increases by less than 10%
🔹 C. Decreases
🔹 D. Remains unchanged

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Because \(x^{*}\) is proportional to the numerator \(p-b\), a 10% rise in \(p\) raises \(x^{*}\) but not by the full 10% unless \(b=0\).

Q15. Given the capacity limit of 30,000 units, what is the profit \(P(30,000)\) for the example function?

🔹 A. \$400,000
🔹 B. \$500,000
🔹 C. \$300,000
🔹 D. \$600,000

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

\(P(30,000)=200(30,000)-(500,000+80(30,000)+0.003(30,000)^2)=6,000,000-5,600,000=400,000\).

Q16. The break‑even output occurs when profit equals zero. For the example, which equation must be solved to find the break‑even points?

🔹 A. \(200x = 500000+80x+0.003x^{2}\)
🔹 B. \(200x = 0\)
🔹 C. \(500000+80x+0.003x^{2}=0\)
🔹 D. \(200 = 500000+80+0.003x\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Setting \(P(x)=0\) gives \(200x-(500000+80x+0.003x^{2})=0\), which simplifies to the listed equation.

Q17. If the quadratic cost coefficient \(c\) is reduced, what is the expected effect on the shape of the profit function \(P(x)=px-(a+bx+cx^{2})\)?

🔹 A. The profit curve becomes more concave (steeper downwards).
🔹 B. The profit curve becomes less concave (flatter).
🔹 C. The profit curve becomes convex.
🔹 D. No change.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A smaller \(c\) reduces the magnitude of the negative quadratic term, making the profit curve flatter (less sharply concave).

Q18. For \(P(x)=200x-(500000+80x+0.003x^{2})\) with capacity \(l=30,000\), what is the profit‑maximizing output \(x^{*}\) and corresponding profit?

🔹 A. \(x^{*}=20,000,\; P=200,000\)
🔹 B. \(x^{*}=20,000,\; P=400,000\)
🔹 C. \(x^{*}=30,000,\; P=400,000\)
🔹 D. \(x^{*}=15,000,\; P=600,000\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Setting \(P'(x)=0\) gives \(x^{*}=20,000\). Substituting back yields profit \(200(20,000)-[500,000+80(20,000)+0.003(20,000)^2]=200,000\).

Q19. If the linear cost coefficient \(b\) increases by 20 while the price \(p\) stays at 200, how does the optimal output \(x^{*}=\frac{p-b}{2c}\) change?

🔹 A. Decreases by \(\frac{20}{2c}\)
🔹 B. Increases by \(\frac{20}{2c}\)
🔹 C. Remains unchanged
🔹 D. Becomes negative

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

An increase in \(b\) reduces the numerator \(p-b\); the optimal output falls by exactly \(\frac{20}{2c}\).

Q20. Given that the profit function \(P(x)=px-(a+bx+cx^{2})\) is concave downwards for all \(x\), what must be true about the coefficient \(c\)?

🔹 A. \(c>0\)
🔹 B. \(c<0\)
🔹 C. \(c=0\)
🔹 D. No restriction on \(c\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The second derivative is \(P''(x)=-2c\). For the function to be concave (negative second derivative), \(-2c<0\) ⇒ \(c>0\).

Q21. Why does the profit function \(P(x)=px-(a+bx+cx^{2})\) with \(c>0\) guarantee a single global maximum on \([0,l]\)?

🔹 A. Because \(P(x)\) is a linear function.
🔹 B. Because the second derivative \(P''(x)=-2c\) is negative, making \(P\) strictly concave.
🔹 C. Because the first derivative is always positive.
🔹 D. Because the cost function dominates revenue.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A negative constant second derivative means the function is strictly concave, so any critical point is the unique global maximum within the interval.

Q22. If the overhead \(a\) increases by \$100,000, how does the maximum profit change, assuming the optimal output remains at \(x^{*}=20,000\)?

🔹 A. Decreases by \$100,000
🔹 B. Increases by \$100,000
🔹 C. Remains unchanged
🔹 D. Decreases by less than \$100,000

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Overhead appears additively in the profit expression, so a \$100,000 rise reduces profit by the same amount, regardless of output level.

Q23. A firm wants a profit of at least \$500,000 with price \(p=200\) and capacity \(l=30,000\). Which choice of overhead \(a\) (keeping \(b=80, c=0.003\)) ensures this target is met at the optimal output?

🔹 A. \$500,000
🔹 B. \$600,000
🔹 C. \$700,000
🔹 D. \$800,000

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

At the optimal \(x=20,000\), profit equals \(1,200,000-a\). To achieve ≥\$500,000, \(a\) must be ≤\$700,000; the highest permissible choice listed is \$700,000.

Q24. Given \(p=200\), \(b=80\), and \(c=0.003\), which of the following best describes the behavior of profit as \(x\) approaches the capacity limit 30,000?

🔹 A. Profit continues to increase beyond 30,000.
🔹 B. Profit reaches its maximum at \(x=20,000\) then declines.
🔹 C. Profit is constant for all \(x\) in [0,30,000].
🔹 D. Profit is negative for all \(x\).

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The profit function is concave with a peak at \(x^{*}=20,000\); beyond this point, additional units reduce profit, so at the capacity limit profit has already begun to fall.
