What is Graphing with calculus and graphing calculators?

Definition:
Combining calculus analysis with graphing calculators verifies theoretical sketches. Calculus provides exact critical points and asymptotes, while calculators offer visual confirmation and numerical approximations, helping identify viewing window issues or subtle behaviors like near-asymptotic trends.

Example:
After calculating asymptotes for f(x)=xx2−1f(x) = \frac{x}{x^2-1}f(x)=x2−1x​, use a calculator to zoom in near x=1x=1x=1 to confirm the vertical asymptote behavior visually.

Reason:
Technology complements analytical methods by providing immediate visual feedback, reducing errors in manual sketching and allowing exploration of complex functions efficiently.
