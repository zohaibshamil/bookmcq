Q1. Which of the following is a necessary condition for applying the formula \( \int_a^b [f(x) - g(x)] \, dx \) to find the area between two curves over an interval \([a,b]\)?

🔹 A. The functions \( f \) and \( g \) must both be nonnegative on \([a,b]\).
🔹 B. The curve \( y = f(x) \) must lie above the curve \( y = g(x) \) for all \( x \) in \([a,b]\).
🔹 C. The functions \( f \) and \( g \) must intersect at \( x = a \) and \( x = b \).
🔹 D. The functions \( f \) and \( g \) must be linear.

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

Q2. Let \( R \) be the region bounded by \( y = x^2 \) and \( y = 2x - x^2 \). Find the area of \( R \).

🔹 A. \( \frac{1}{3} \)
🔹 B. \( 1 \)
🔹 C. \( \frac{4}{3} \)
🔹 D. \( \frac{2}{3} \)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

Q3. If the area between \( y = x^2 - 4x + 3 \) and the x-axis is \( A \), what is the area between \( y = x^2 - 4x + 3 \) and the line \( y = 1 \)?

🔹 A. \( A \)
🔹 B. \( A + \int_a^b 1 \, dx \)
🔹 C. \( A - \int_a^b 1 \, dx \)
🔹 D. \( \int_a^b [(x^2 - 4x + 3) - 1] \, dx \)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B
