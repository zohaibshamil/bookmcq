What is Trig substitution for sqrt(x² - a²)?

Definition:
For integrals involving \( \sqrt{x^2 - a^2} \), use the substitution \( x = a \sec \theta \), which simplifies the radical to \( a \tan \theta \) since \( \sec^2 \theta - 1 = \tan^2 \theta \).

Example:
For \( \int \frac{\sqrt{x^2-1}}{x} dx \), let \( x = \sec \theta \). Then \( dx = \sec \theta \tan \theta d\theta \) and the integral becomes \( \int \tan^2 \theta \, d\theta = \int (\sec^2 \theta - 1) d\theta \).

Reason:
It utilizes the identity \( \sec^2 \theta - 1 = \tan^2 \theta \) to remove the radical, making the integral solvable using basic trigonometric integration techniques.
