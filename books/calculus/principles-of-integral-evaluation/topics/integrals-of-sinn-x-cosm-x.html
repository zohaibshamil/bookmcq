What is Integrals of sin^n x cos^m x?

Definition:
Integrals of powers of sine and cosine are solved by using trigonometric identities to reduce powers, typically splitting off one factor if the power is odd or using half-angle formulas if both are even.

Example:
For ∫sin3xcos2xdx, write sin3x=sin2xsinx=(1−cos2x)sinx, then substitute u=cosx to integrate −(1−u2)u2du.

Reason:
Trigonometric identities convert higher powers into linear combinations of simpler functions, enabling standard substitution methods to find the antiderivative efficiently.
