What is Products of tangents and secants integrals?

Definition:
When integrating products of tangents and secants, if the power of secant is even, save a sec⁡2x\sec^2 xsec2x factor for dududu; if tangent is odd, save a sec⁡xtan⁡x\sec x \tan xsecxtanx factor, converting the rest using identities.

Example:

Reason:
This strategy leverages the derivative ddx(tan⁡x)=sec⁡2x\frac{d}{dx}(\tan x) = \sec^2 xdxd​(tanx)=sec2x or ddx(sec⁡x)=sec⁡xtan⁡x\frac{d}{dx}(\sec x) = \sec x \tan xdxd​(secx)=secxtanx to create perfect differentials for substitution.
