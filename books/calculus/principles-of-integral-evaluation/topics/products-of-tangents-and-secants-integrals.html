What is Products of tangents and secants integrals?

Definition:
When integrating products of tangents and secants, if the power of secant is even, save a sec⁡2x \sec^2 x sec2x factor for du du du; if tangent is odd, save a sec⁡xtan⁡x \sec x \tan x secxtanx factor, converting the rest using identities.

Example:
For ∫tan⁡2xsec⁡4x dx \int \tan^2 x \sec^4 x \, dx ∫tan2xsec4xdx, save sec⁡2x \sec^2 x sec2x, rewrite rest as (tan⁡2x)(1+tan⁡2x) (\tan^2 x)(1+\tan^2 x) (tan2x)(1+tan2x), and substitute u=tan⁡x u=\tan x u=tanx to integrate u2(1+u2)du u^2(1+u^2) du u2(1+u2)du.

Reason:
This strategy leverages the derivative ddx(tan⁡x)=sec⁡2x \frac{d}{dx}(\tan x) = \sec^2 x dxd​(tanx)=sec2x or ddx(sec⁡x)=sec⁡xtan⁡x \frac{d}{dx}(\sec x) = \sec x \tan x dxd​(secx)=secxtanx to create perfect differentials for substitution.
