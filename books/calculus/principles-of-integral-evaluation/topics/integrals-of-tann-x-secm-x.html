What is Integrals of tan^n x sec^m x?

Definition:
Integrals of tangent and secant powers are handled by exploiting the identity sec⁡2x=1+tan⁡2x \sec^2 x = 1 + \tan^2 x sec2x=1+tan2x or tan⁡2x=sec⁡2x−1 \tan^2 x = \sec^2 x - 1 tan2x=sec2x−1, separating factors to facilitate substitution based on odd or even powers.

Example:
For ∫tan⁡3xsec⁡x dx \int \tan^3 x \sec x \, dx ∫tan3xsecxdx, rewrite as ∫(sec⁡2x−1)sec⁡xtan⁡x dx \int (\sec^2 x - 1) \sec x \tan x \, dx ∫(sec2x−1)secxtanxdx, then substitute u=sec⁡x u=\sec x u=secx to get ∫(u2−1)du \int (u^2 - 1) du ∫(u2−1)du.

Reason:
The derivative relationships between tan and sec allow strategic separation of terms, transforming the integral into a polynomial form via substitution for straightforward solution.
