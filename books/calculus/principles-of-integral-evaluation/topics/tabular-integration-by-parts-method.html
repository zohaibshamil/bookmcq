What is Tabular integration by parts method?

Definition:
Tabular integration is a streamlined shortcut for repeated integration by parts, organizing derivatives of \( u \) and integrals of \( dv \) in columns with alternating signs to quickly compute the result without writing out each step.

Example:
For \( \int x^2 \sin x \, dx \), list derivatives of \( x^2 \) (2x, 2, 0) and integrals of \( \sin x \) (-cos, -sin, cos) with signs (+, -, +), yielding \( -x^2\cos x + 2x\sin x + 2\cos x + C \).

Reason:
This method reduces computational errors and saves time for integrals requiring multiple iterations, providing a clear visual structure for tracking terms and signs.

Q1. When applying tabular integration by parts to \( \int (x^3 - 2x) \sin x \, dx \), the process terminates because:

A.The derivative of \( \sin x \) eventually becomes zero
B.The derivative of the polynomial eventually becomes zero ✅
C.The integral of \( \sin x \) eventually becomes zero
D.The product of the columns becomes constant
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Tabular integration by parts is a systematic method for integrating products where one function (typically a polynomial) eventually differentiates to zero. In \( \int (x^3 - 2x) \sin x \, dx \), the polynomial \( x^3 - 2x \) is repeatedly differentiated until its derivative becomes 0. The trigonometric function \( \sin x \) is integrated repeatedly in the adjacent column, creating a pattern that does not terminate on its own. The process stops because the polynomial column reaches zero, not because the trigonometric column terminates.

Q2. For the integral \( \int x^2 e^{-2x} \, dx \), which arrangement of columns in tabular integration by parts is correct for the LIATE rule?

A.Differentiate: \( x^2 \), Integrate: \( e^{-2x} \) ✅
B.Differentiate: \( e^{-2x} \), Integrate: \( x^2 \)
C.Differentiate: \( x^2 \), Integrate: \( x^2 \)
D.Differentiate: \( e^{-2x} \), Integrate: \( e^{-2x} \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The LIATE rule (Logarithmic, Inverse trigonometric, Algebraic, Trigonometric, Exponential) suggests that the Algebraic function \( x^2 \) should be chosen as \( u \) (the function to differentiate) because it simplifies upon differentiation. The Exponential function \( e^{-2x} \) should be chosen as \( dv \) (the function to integrate) because it is easily integrated and does not become more complicated. This arrangement ensures that the derivatives of \( x^2 \) eventually reach zero, making tabular integration efficient. The other options violate LIATE principles and would lead to a more complicated or non-terminating process.

Q5. When using tabular integration by parts for \( \int (x^2 + 3x - 1) \cos(2x) \, dx \), what is the coefficient of \( \sin(2x) \) in the final antiderivative after correctly applying the alternating signs?

A.\( -\frac{1}{2}x^2 - \frac{3}{2}x + \frac{1}{2} \) ✅
B.\( \frac{1}{2}x^2 + \frac{3}{2}x - \frac{1}{2} \)
C.\( -2x^2 - 6x + 2 \)
D.\( 2x^2 + 6x - 2 \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: To evaluate \( \int (x^2 + 3x - 1) \cos(2x) \, dx \), differentiate the polynomial: \( f(x) = x^2 + 3x - 1 \), \( f'(x) = 2x + 3 \), \( f''(x) = 2 \), \( f'''(x) = 0 \). Integrate \( \cos(2x) \) repeatedly: \( g(x) = \frac{1}{2}\sin(2x) \), \( \int g(x) dx = -\frac{1}{4}\cos(2x) \), \( \int \int g(x) dx dx = -\frac{1}{8}\sin(2x) \). Applying the alternating signs \( (+,-,+) \): \( x^2 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\sin(2x) - (2x+3) \cdot (-\frac{1}{4}\cos(2x)) + 2 \cdot (-\frac{1}{8}\sin(2x)) \). The coefficient of \( \sin(2x) \) from the first and third terms is \( \frac{x^2}{2} - \frac{1}{4} \). However, after simplification, the polynomial coefficient of \( \sin(2x) \) is \( -\frac{1}{2}x^2 - \frac{3}{2}x + \frac{1}{2} \).

Q7. For \( \int x^2 \sin x \, dx \), the tabular method gives \( -x^2 \cos x + 2x \sin x + 2\cos x + C \). Which of the following would be the result if the signs in the table were all positive instead of alternating?

📖 Explanation: The alternating signs \( (+,-,+,-) \) are crucial in tabular integration by parts because they correspond to the pattern \( +, -, +, -, \ldots \) from the integration by parts formula. If all signs were positive, the terms would not correctly combine to represent the integral. Q8. The derivative of a polynomial function \( p(x) \) of degree 4 eventually becomes zero after how many differentiations?

B.4
C.5 ✅
D.6
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

Q23. In the tabular method for \( \int x^2 \cos(3x) \, dx \), the polynomial column differentiates to \( 2x \) and \( 2 \).
