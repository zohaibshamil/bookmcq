What is Improper integrals infinite intervals?

Definition:
Integrals over infinite intervals like \( [a, \infty) \) are evaluated by taking the limit as the upper bound approaches infinity, checking if the area remains finite.

Example:
\( \int_0^\infty e^{-x} dx = \lim_{t \to \infty} [-e^{-x}]_0^t = \lim_{t \to \infty} (1 - e^{-t}) = 1 \).

Reason:
This method determines if the total accumulation over an infinite domain is finite, essential for analyzing decay processes and probability densities.
