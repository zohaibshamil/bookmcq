What is Rational functions domain asymptotes?

Definition:
A rational function is a ratio of two polynomials f(x)=P(x)/Q(x)f(x) = P(x)/Q(x)f(x)=P(x)/Q(x), with domain all real numbers except zeros of Q(x)Q(x)Q(x), and it may have vertical asymptotes at those zeros, and horizontal or oblique asymptotes based on degrees of PPP and QQQ.

Example:
For f(x)=2x+1x−3f(x) = \frac{2x+1}{x-3}f(x)=x−32x+1​, domain is x≠3x \neq 3x=3, vertical asymptote at x=3x=3x=3, and horizontal asymptote at y=2y=2y=2 (since degrees equal).

Reason:
Rational functions model rates, proportions, and many physical phenomena like concentration or velocity, where asymptotes indicate limits.
