What is Rational functions domain asymptotes?

Definition:
A rational function is a ratio of two polynomials f(x)=P(x)/Q(x), with domain all real numbers except zeros of Q(x), and it may have vertical asymptotes at those zeros, and horizontal or oblique asymptotes based on degrees of P and Q.

Example:
For f(x)=x−32x+1​, domain is x=3, vertical asymptote at x=3, and horizontal asymptote at y=2 (since degrees equal).

Reason:
Rational functions model rates, proportions, and many physical phenomena like concentration or velocity, where asymptotes indicate limits.

Q1. Which of the following best defines a rational function?

A.A function that can be written as a sum of two polynomials.
B.A function that can be expressed as the ratio of two polynomials. ✅
C.A function whose graph is always continuous.
D.A function that has only horizontal asymptotes.
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A rational function is precisely a quotient of two polynomial expressions, written as P(x)/Q(x) where Q(x)=0. This definition matches option B, whereas the other options describe unrelated properties such as continuity or asymptotic behavior.

Q2. Consider f(x)=x−2x2−4​. After simplifying, what type of discontinuity does the function have at x=2?

A.Vertical asymptote
B.No discontinuity
C.Removable hole ✅
D.Jump discontinuity
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Factoring the numerator gives (x−2)(x+2). Cancelling the common factor with the denominator leaves f(x)=x+2 for all x=2. The point x=2 is omitted, creating a removable hole rather than an infinite break, so option C is correct.

Q3. What is the domain of g(x)=x2−9x+3​?

A.All real numbers except x=−3 and x=3 ✅
B.All real numbers
C.All real numbers except x=0
D.All real numbers except x=9
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The denominator x2−9 factors to (x−3)(x+3). The function is undefined wherever this product equals zero, i.e., at x=3 and x=−3. All other real numbers are allowed, making option A the correct description of the domain.

Q4. If h(x)=x−2x+1​, what is the sign of h(x) just to the right of its vertical asymptote at x=2?

A.Positive ✅
B.Negative
C.Zero
D.Undefined
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Near the vertical asymptote x=2, evaluate the sign of the numerator and denominator. For x just larger than 2, the numerator x+1 is positive, and the denominator x−2 is a small positive number, giving a positive quotient. Hence the function is positive to the right of the asymptote, matching option A.

Q5. Given k(x)=2x2−73x2+5​, which of the following statements about its horizontal asymptote is true?

A.The horizontal asymptote is y=23​. ✅
B.The function has no horizontal asymptote.
C.The horizontal asymptote is y=0.
D.The horizontal asymptote is y=1.
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The degrees of numerator and denominator are both 2, so the horizontal asymptote is the ratio of leading coefficients: 23​. Therefore the statement that the horizontal asymptote is y=23​ is true, which corresponds to option A.

Q6. Which of the following rational functions shares the same horizontal asymptote as f(x)=x2−52x2+3​?

A.x2+42x2+1​ ✅
B.x2+4x2+1​
C.2x2+52x2+3​
D.2x2+3x2+2​
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Horizontal asymptotes depend on the leading terms. The original function has leading coefficient ratio 2/1=2. Among the choices, only x2+42x2+1​ retains that ratio, giving the same horizontal asymptote y=2. Thus option A is correct.

Q7. For the rational function p(x)=x−3x2−9​, which statement correctly describes its discontinuity at x=3?

A.There is a vertical asymptote at x=3.
B.The function is continuous at x=3.
C.There is a removable hole at x=3. ✅
D.The function has a jump discontinuity at x=3.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The numerator x2−9 factors as (x−3)(x+3). Cancelling the factor (x−3) with the denominator leaves p(x)=x+3 for all x=3. The point at x=3 is removed, producing a hole rather than a vertical asymptote, so option C is correct.

Q8. What is x→∞lim​2x3+5x23x3−x​?

A.23​ ✅
B.0
C.∞
D.-23​
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: When x grows without bound, the highest-degree terms dominate. The ratio of the leading coefficients 23​ determines the limit, because lower-degree terms become negligible. Hence x→∞lim​2x3+5x23x3−x​=23​, which matches option A.

Q9. Find all real numbers a such that the rational function r(x)=x−ax2−a​ has a removable discontinuity (hole) at x=a.

A.All real numbers
B.No real numbers
C.Only a=0 or a=1 ✅
D.Only a=1
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: A removable discontinuity occurs when both numerator and denominator vanish at the same point, allowing cancellation. Setting the numerator zero at x=a gives a2−a=0, factoring to a(a−1)=0. Thus a must be 0 or 1. Option C lists exactly these values, making it the correct choice.

Q10. For s(x)=x2−9x2−4​, on which interval is the function increasing?

A.(−∞,−3) ✅
B.(−3,−2)
C.(−2,2)
D.(2,∞)
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Differentiating s(x)=x2−9x2−4​ yields s&#039;(x)=\frac{-10x}{(x^{2}-9)^{2}}. The denominator is always positive except at the undefined points x=±3. Therefore the sign of s&#039;(x) is opposite to the sign of x. The function is increasing where x&lt;0; the interval (−∞,−3) satisfies this, so option A is correct.

Q11. Why can a rational function have a horizontal asymptote even though it is not a polynomial?

A.Because the numerator eventually dominates the denominator.
B.Because the degrees of numerator and denominator determine the end behavior. ✅
C.Because rational functions are always bounded.
D.Because they have vertical asymptotes.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The end behavior of a rational function is governed by the degrees of its numerator and denominator. When these degrees are equal, the ratio of the leading coefficients determines a constant value approached as x tends to ±∞. This explains why such functions have a horizontal asymptote, matching option B.

Q12. When sketching the graph of f(x)=x−1x2−1​, which of the following steps is essential?

A.Identify the hole at x=1 and the vertical asymptote at x=0.
B.Cancel the common factor to find the simplified function and locate the hole. ✅
C.Find the horizontal asymptote y=0.
D.Determine the intercepts without simplifying.
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The original expression simplifies by factoring x2−1=(x−1)(x+1) and cancelling the common factor (x−1). This reveals a hole at x=1 and reduces the function to f(x)=x+1 elsewhere. Recognizing and removing the factor is essential for accurate graphing, so option B is correct.

Q13. What is the vertical asymptote of the rational function f(x)=x−52​?

A.x = 0
B.x = 5 ✅
C.x = -5
D.No vertical asymptote
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A vertical asymptote occurs where the denominator equals zero while the numerator remains non‑zero. For f(x)=x−52​, the denominator vanishes at x=5, producing an infinite blow‑up there. Hence the vertical asymptote is the line x=5, which is option B.
