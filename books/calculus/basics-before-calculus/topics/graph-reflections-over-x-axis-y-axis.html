What is Graph reflections over x axis y axis?

Reflecting a graph transforms \( y = f(x) \) across an axis, where multiplying the function by \( -1 \) to get \( -f(x) \) reflects it over the x-axis, and replacing \( x \) with \( -x \) to get \( f(-x) \) reflects it over the y-axis, effectively mirroring the original curve.

Q1. What is the equation of the graph obtained by reflecting \(y = |x|\) across the x‑axis?

A. \(y = -|x|\) ✅
B. \(y = |x|\)
C. \(y = -x\)
D. \(y = x\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Reflecting across the x‑axis changes the sign of every y‑coordinate while leaving x unchanged. The absolute‑value function \(y = |x|\) becomes its vertical mirror, giving \(y = -|x|\). The other options either leave the graph unchanged or represent different transformations, so A is correct.

Q2. Starting with \(y = f(x)\), the graph is first shifted 3 units left and then reflected about the y‑axis. Which expression represents the resulting function?

A. \(y = f(x+3)\)
B. \(y = f(-x-3)\) ✅
C. \(y = f(-x+3)\)
D. \(y = f(-x)\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A left shift replaces x by \(x+3\); a subsequent reflection about the y‑axis replaces x by \(-x\). Applying both operations yields \(f(-(x+3)) = f(-x-3)\). Options A, C, and D omit one of the transformations, leaving B as the only correct representation.

Q3. Which sequence of transformations yields the graph of \(y = 4 - |x-2|\) from the basic graph \(y = |x|\)?

A. Translate right 2, reflect across y‑axis, translate up 4
B. Translate left 2, reflect across x‑axis, translate down 4
C. Translate right 2, reflect across x‑axis, translate up 4 ✅
D. Translate left 2, reflect across y‑axis, translate up 4

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

First move the parent graph right by 2, giving \(|x-2|\). Reflecting this across the x‑axis changes the sign, producing \(-|x-2|\). Finally, shifting the result upward by 4 units adds 4 to the y‑value, resulting in \(4-|x-2|\). Only option C follows this exact order.

Q4. How does the graph of \(y = -\sqrt{x}\) compare to the graph of \(y = \sqrt{x}\)?

A. It is a horizontal reflection across the y‑axis.
B. It is a translation 1 unit upward.
C. It coincides with \(y = \sqrt{x}\). ✅
D. It is a vertical reflection of \(y = \sqrt{x}\) across the x‑axis.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The function \(\sqrt{x}\) lies above the x‑axis for \(x\ge0\). Multiplying the entire output by \(-1\) flips every point vertically, producing a mirror image below the axis. This is precisely a reflection across the x‑axis. Options A and B describe different transformations, and option C is false because the graphs are distinct.

Q5. What are the coordinates of the vertex of the graph of \(y = 4 - |x-2|\)?

A. \((0,4)\)
B. \((2,4)\) ✅
C. \((2,0)\)
D. \((4,2)\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The expression \(|x-2|\) attains its minimum value 0 when \(x=2\). Substituting this into the outer expression gives \(y = 4 - 0 = 4\). Thus the point \((2,4)\) is the vertex, the highest point of this V‑shaped graph. The other choices either misplace the x‑coordinate or the y‑coordinate.

Q6. Reflect the parabola \(y = (x-1)^2\) about the line \(y = x\). Which equation represents the reflected curve?

A. \(y = (x+1)^2\)
B. \(x = (y+1)^2\)
C. \(x = (y-1)^2\) ✅
D. \(y = (x-1)^2\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Reflecting about \(y = x\) swaps the roles of x and y. Starting with \(y = (x-1)^2\), interchange the variables to obtain \(x = (y-1)^2\). This equation describes the same set of points after the reflection. Options A and D leave the equation unchanged, and B uses the wrong sign inside the square.

Q7. If the graph of \(y = |2x+4| + 2\) is first reflected about the x‑axis and then about the y‑axis, what is the resulting equation?

A. \(y = -|2x-4| - 2\) ✅
B. \(y = -|2x+4| - 2\)
C. \(y = |2x-4| + 2\)
D. \(y = -|2x-4| + 2\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Reflecting across the x‑axis changes the sign of y, giving \(y = -|2x+4| - 2\). A subsequent reflection across the y‑axis replaces x by \(-x\), turning \(|2(-x)+4|\) into \(|-2x+4| = |2x-4|\). The final expression is therefore \(y = -|2x-4| - 2\), which matches option A.

Q8. Which of the following describes the transformation sequence that produces \(y = -|2x+4| + 5\) from the parent graph \(y = |x|\)?

A. Horizontal stretch by factor 2, shift left 2, reflect across y‑axis, shift up 5
B. Horizontal compression by factor 1/2, shift left 2, reflect across x‑axis, shift up 5 ✅
C. Horizontal stretch by factor 2, shift right 2, reflect across y‑axis, shift up 5
D. Horizontal stretch by factor 2, shift left 2, reflect across x‑axis, shift up 5

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Inside the absolute value, \(2x+4 = 2(x+2)\) indicates a horizontal compression by a factor of \(1/2\) followed by a left shift of 2 units. The outer negative sign reflects the graph across the x‑axis, and the final \(+5\) translates it upward. Option B lists these steps in the correct order.

Q9. Reflect the graph of \(y = \sqrt{x}\) about the line \(y = -x\). Which expression gives the resulting curve?

A. \(y = -x^2\) ✅
B. \(y = -\sqrt{-x}\)
C. \(x = -\sqrt{-y}\)
D. \(y = \sqrt{-x}\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

A reflection across \(y = -x\) swaps coordinates and changes both signs, turning \((x,\sqrt{x})\) into \((-\,\sqrt{x},-x)\). Eliminating the parameter leads to the relation \(y = -x^2\), a downward‑opening parabola. The other options either retain a square‑root form or mix variables incorrectly.

Q10. Which statement correctly compares the graphs of \(y = |x|\) reflected about the x‑axis and about the y‑axis?

A. Both reflections produce the same graph.
B. Reflection about the x‑axis yields \(y = -|x|\); about the y‑axis yields \(y = |x|\). ✅
C. Reflection about the x‑axis yields \(y = -|x|\); about the y‑axis yields \(y = -|x|\).
D. Reflection about the x‑axis yields \(y = |x|\); about the y‑axis yields \(y = -|x|\).

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Reflecting across the x‑axis changes the sign of the output, giving \(y = -|x|\), a V opening downward. Reflecting across the y‑axis replaces x by \(-x\), but because the absolute value eliminates the sign, the graph remains \(y = |x|\). Hence the two reflected graphs are different, matching option B.
