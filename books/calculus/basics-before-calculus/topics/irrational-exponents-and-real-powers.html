What is Irrational exponents and real powers?

Definition:
Irrational exponents define real powers ar a^r ar for any real r r r, extending rational exponent rules via limits of rational approximations, ensuring continuity and consistency for exponential functions with real exponents.

Example:
22 2^{\sqrt{2}} 22​ is defined as the limit of 21.414,21.4142,… 2^{1.414}, 2^{1.4142}, \ldots 21.414,21.4142,…, approximately equal to 2.665 2.665 2.665, using rational approximations of 2 \sqrt{2} 2​.

Reason:
This extension allows exponential functions to be defined on all real numbers, enabling continuous growth models (like compound interest continuously) and natural logarithms.
