What is Inverse proportion functions?

Definition:
Inverse proportion functions express a relationship where one variable is inversely proportional to another, typically written as \( y = k/x \) or \( y = kx^{-1} \), meaning as \( x \) increases, \( y \) decreases, and vice versa, with constant product \( xy = k \).

Example:
If \( y = 12/x \), then when \( x=3 \), \( y=4 \); when \( x=6 \), \( y=2 \), showing the product \( 3 \cdot 4 = 6 \cdot 2 = 12 \).

Reason:
This models real-world situations like speed and time for a fixed distance, or pressure and volume in Boyle's law, highlighting trade-offs.

📝 All Inverse proportion functions MCQs

Q1. Which of the following best defines an inverse proportion between variables \(x\) and \(y\)?

A.\(y = kx\) for some constant \(k\)
B.\(y = \dfrac{k}{x}\) for some positive constant \(k\) ✅
C.\(y = k - x\)
D.\(y = k + x\)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The definition of inverse proportionality states that the product \(xy\) is a constant \(k>0\), which can be written as \(y = k/x\). Option B captures this relationship, while the other options describe direct or linear relations, not the characteristic reciprocal behavior of inverse proportion.

Q2. In a data set the product \(x y\) is constantly \(20\). If the value of \(x\) is changed from \(4\) to \(8\), what is the new value of \(y\)?

A.10
B.5
C.2.5 ✅
D.20
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Since the product \(xy\) remains \(20\), we solve \(8y = 20\) giving \(y = 20/8 = 2.5\). This demonstrates how the inverse relationship forces the second variable to adjust inversely to maintain the constant product, confirming the answer choice C.

Q3. Suppose \(y\) is inversely proportional to \(x\) and \(y=3\) when \(x=5\). Which statement correctly describes the effect on \(y\) if \(x\) is increased by 50%?

A.y becomes 2 ✅
B.y becomes 2.5
C.y becomes 2.0
D.y becomes 2.4
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: First find the constant: \(k = xy = 3 \times 5 = 15\). Increasing \(x\) by 50% gives \(x' = 1.5 \times 5 = 7.5\). The new \(y\) is \(k/x' = 15/7.5 = 2\). Thus \(y\) decreases to 2, matching option A.

Q4. Which of the following graphs represents an inverse proportional relationship between \(x\) and \(y\)?

A.A straight line through the origin
B.A hyperbola in the first quadrant ✅
C.A parabola opening upward
D.A horizontal line
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: An inverse proportional relationship \(y = k/x\) produces a rectangular hyperbola that lies in the first quadrant for positive \(k\). The hyperbolic shape distinguishes it from linear, parabolic, or constant graphs, making option B the correct representation.

Q5. Given two relationships: \(y = \dfrac{6}{x}\) and \(y = 2x\). Which statement correctly compares the behavior of \(y\) as \(x\) grows large?

A.Both \(y\) values approach zero
B.The first \(y\) approaches zero while the second \(y\) grows without bound ✅
C.Both \(y\) values grow without bound
D.The first \(y\) grows without bound while the second \(y\) approaches zero
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: For \(y = 6/x\), as \(x \to \infty\) the term \(6/x\) tends to zero. Conversely, \(y = 2x\) increases linearly without bound. Therefore the first function approaches zero and the second diverges, which is described by option B.

Q6. Consider the function \(y = \frac{k}{x}\) with \(k>0\). If the area under the curve between \(x = a\) and \(x = b\) (where \(0<a<b\)) is equal to the area of a rectangle with sides \(k\) and \(\ln(b/a)\), which of the following statements is true?

A.The statement holds for any positive \(k\) ✅
B.It holds only when \(k=1\)
C.It holds only when \(ab = 1\)
D.It never holds
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The integral \(\int_a^b \frac{k}{x}\,dx = k\ln(b/a)\) gives the exact area under the curve. A rectangle with sides \(k\) and \(\ln(b/a)\) also has area \(k\ln(b/a)\). Hence the equality is true for every positive constant \(k\), confirming option A.

Q7. If the speed \(v\) of a car is inversely proportional to the time \(t\) it takes to travel a fixed distance, what happens to the speed when the travel time is halved?

A.Speed doubles ✅
B.Speed halves
C.Speed remains unchanged
D.Speed triples
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The relationship \(v = k/t\) implies that halving \(t\) multiplies \(v\) by two, because the product \(vt\) stays constant. Therefore the speed doubles, which corresponds to option A.

Q8. A photographer uses the relation \( \text{Exposure} = \dfrac{k}{\text{Aperture}} \). If the aperture is increased from f/2 to f/4, how does the exposure change?

A.It doubles
B.It halves ✅
C.It remains the same
D.It quarters
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Increasing the denominator from 2 to 4 doubles the denominator, so the overall fraction is halved. Consequently the exposure is reduced to one‑half of its original value, matching option B.

Q9. In Boyle’s law, pressure \(P\) and volume \(V\) are inversely proportional. If a gas initially at \(P = 2 \text{ atm}\) and \(V = 5 \text{ L}\) is compressed to \(V = 3 \text{ L}\), what is the new pressure (assuming temperature constant)?

A.1.2 atm
B.3.33 atm ✅
C.2.5 atm
D.4 atm
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Boyle’s law gives the constant \(k = PV = 2 \times 5 = 10\). With the new volume \(V' = 3\) L, the pressure becomes \(P' = k/V' = 10/3 \approx 3.33\) atm, which is option B.

Q10. A certain biological reaction rate \(R\) is inversely proportional to the concentration \(C\) of an inhibitor, i.e., \(R = \frac{k}{C}\). Given that when \(C = 0.5\) mol/L the rate is 2 units, what is the highest concentration of inhibitor allowed while still keeping the reaction rate at least 0.8 units?

A.0.5 mol/L
B.0.625 mol/L
C.0.8 mol/L
D.1.25 mol/L ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: From the known condition, \(k = R C = 2 \times 0.5 = 1\). To maintain \(R \ge 0.8\), we need \(1/C \ge 0.8\) → \(C \le 1/0.8 = 1.25\) mol/L. Therefore the greatest permissible concentration is 1.25 mol/L, corresponding to option D.

Q11. If a set of measurements obeys \(xy = 15\) and one measurement is \((x, y) = (3, 5)\), which of the following pairs could NOT be part of the same data set?

A.(5, 3)
B.(1.5, 10)
C.(2, 7.5)
D.(4, 4) ✅
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: All listed pairs except (4,4) satisfy the product condition \(xy = 15\). The pair (4,4) yields a product of 16, violating the constant‑product rule, so it cannot belong to the same data set. Hence option D is the correct choice.

Q12. Two variables \(x\) and \(y\) satisfy \(y = \dfrac{9}{x}\). If we plot \(y\) against \(x\) on a log‑log graph, what is the slope of the line?

A.-1 ✅
B.0
C.1
D.9
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Taking logarithms gives \(\log y = \log 9 - \log x\). This is a straight line with slope \(-1\) (the coefficient of \(\log x\)). Therefore the log‑log plot has slope \(-1\), which corresponds to option A.
