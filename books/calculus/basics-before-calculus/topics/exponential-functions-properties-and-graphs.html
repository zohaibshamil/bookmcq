Q1. If \(b^2 = 9\) for an exponential function \(f(x)=b^x\) with \(b>0\), what is \(f(-3)\)?

🔹 A. \( \frac{1}{27} \)
🔹 B. 27
🔹 C. \( \frac{1}{9} \)
🔹 D. 9

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Since \(b^2=9\) gives \(b=3\). The value at \(-3\) is \(b^{-3}=3^{-3}=1/27\). The other options correspond to incorrect manipulations of the exponent or base. Therefore the correct choice is \(\frac{1}{27}\).

Q2. Reflecting the graph of \(y=(\tfrac12)^x\) about the y‑axis yields which function, and how does its growth for positive \(x\) compare to the original?

🔹 A. \(y=(\tfrac12)^{-x}\)
🔹 B. \(y=2^{x}\)
🔹 C. \(y=(\tfrac12)^{x}\)
🔹 D. \(y=2^{-x}\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Replacing \(x\) by \(-x\) gives \((\tfrac12)^{-x}=2^{x}\). The new function \(2^{x}\) increases as \(x\) increases, whereas the original \((\tfrac12)^{x}\) decreases. Hence the reflected graph grows faster for positive \(x\). The other choices either leave the function unchanged or give a decreasing version.

Q3. For the exponential function \(f(x)=b^{x}\) with \(b=3\), what is the horizontal asymptote of its graph?

🔹 A. \(y=1\)
🔹 B. \(y=3\)
🔹 C. \(y=0\)
🔹 D. \(y=\infty\)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Exponential graphs of the form \(b^{x}\) approach zero as \(x\to -\infty\) and never cross the x‑axis, making \(y=0\) the horizontal asymptote regardless of the base \(b\) (provided \(b>0\) and \(b\neq1\)). The other options describe lines that are not asymptotes for this function.

Q4. The point \((4,16)\) lies on the graph of \(y=b^{x}\). What is the value of the base \(b\)?

🔹 A. 4
🔹 B. 16
🔹 C. \(\\sqrt{2}\)
🔹 D. 2

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

Substituting the point gives \(b^{4}=16\). Taking the fourth root yields \(b=16^{1/4}=2\). The other options either misinterpret the exponent or give incorrect roots. Thus the base is \(2\).

Q5. If an exponential function \(q(x)=b^{x}\) satisfies \(q(-1)=0.5\), what is the base \(b\)?

🔹 A. 0.5
🔹 B. 2
🔹 C. \(\tfrac12\)
🔹 D. 4

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The equation \(b^{-1}=0.5\) implies \(1/b=0.5\), so \(b=2\). Option B correctly reflects this value. Options A and C are the reciprocals, and D is unrelated.

Q6. Which statement correctly describes the relative growth of \(y=2^{x}\) and \(y=3^{x}\) for \(x>0\)?

🔹 A. \(2^{x}\) grows faster than \(3^{x}\)
🔹 B. \(3^{x}\) grows faster than \(2^{x}\)
🔹 C. Both grow at the same rate
🔹 D. Both decrease as \(x\) increases

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Because the base 3 is larger than the base 2, the function \(3^{x}\) increases more rapidly for positive \(x\). This is evident from comparing values such as \(2^{2}=4\) versus \(3^{2}=9\). The other options either reverse the relationship or incorrectly claim decreasing behavior.

Q7. Evaluate \\(\\displaystyle\\lim_{x\\to\\infty}\\left(\\tfrac12\\right)^{x}\\).

🔹 A. 1
🔹 B. \\(\\infty\\)
🔹 C. \\(-\\infty\\)
🔹 D. 0

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The base \\(\\tfrac12\\) lies between 0 and 1, so raising it to larger and larger exponents drives the value toward zero. Formally, \\(\\left(\\tfrac12\\right)^{x}=e^{x\\ln(\\tfrac12)}\\) with \\(\\ln(\\tfrac12)<0\\), yielding a limit of 0 as \\(x\\to\\infty\\). The other choices misinterpret the behavior of a decaying exponential.

Q8. Which expression represents a vertical stretch of the graph of \\(y=b^{x}\\) by a factor of 3?

🔹 A. \(y=b^{3x}\)
🔹 B. \(y=(3b)^{x}\)
🔹 C. \(y=3\\,b^{x}\)
🔹 D. \(y=b^{x+3}\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Multiplying the entire function by 3 scales every output value by 3, which is a vertical stretch. The expression \\(3\\,b^{x}\\) captures this effect. Options A and D change the exponent, altering the shape differently, while option B changes the base, also producing a different graph.

Q9. Replacing \\(x\\) by \\(x-2\\) in the equation \\(y=b^{x}\\) results in which transformation of the graph?

🔹 A. Shift right by 2 units
🔹 B. Shift left by 2 units
🔹 C. Shift up by 2 units
🔹 D. Shift down by 2 units

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The substitution \\(x\\rightarrow x-2\\) moves every point of the original graph two units to the right because the function now reaches the same output value at an \\(x\\) that is two larger than before. Horizontal translations affect the input variable, not the output, so the vertical shift options are incorrect.

Q10. Let \\(f(x)=b^{x}\\) and \\(g(x)=c^{x}\\) with \\(b>c>1\\). Which inequality correctly compares their derivatives at \\(x=0\\)?

🔹 A. \(f'(0)<g'(0)\)
🔹 B. \(f'(0)>g'(0)\)
🔹 C. \(f'(0)=g'(0)\)
🔹 D. Cannot be determined without specific values

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

The derivative of \\(b^{x}\\) is \\(\\ln(b)\\,b^{x}\\); at \\(x=0\\) this simplifies to \\(\\ln(b)\\). Since \\(b>c\\) and the natural logarithm is increasing, \\(\\ln(b)>\\ln(c)\\). Therefore \\(f'(0)>\\!g'(0)\\). The other options either reverse the inequality or claim equality without justification.

Q11. In the decay model \\(N(t)=N_{0}\\left(\\tfrac12\\right)^{t/5}\\), what is the half‑life of the quantity?

🔹 A. 2.5
🔹 B. 10
🔹 C. 1
🔹 D. 5

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The expression \\(\\left(\\tfrac12\\right)^{t/5}\\) indicates that every increase of 5 time units halves the quantity. Therefore the half‑life, the time required for the amount to reduce to one‑half its initial value, is 5 units. The other numbers correspond to different intervals but not the half‑life.

Q12. Solve for \\(x\\) in the equation \\(4^{x}=8\\).

🔹 A. 2
🔹 B. 0.5
🔹 C. 1.5
🔹 D. 3

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Rewrite both sides with base 2: \\(4^{x}=(2^{2})^{x}=2^{2x}\\) and \\(8=2^{3}\\). Equating exponents gives \\(2x=3\\), so \\(x=\\tfrac{3}{2}=1.5\\). Option C matches this value; the other options arise from incorrect exponent handling.
