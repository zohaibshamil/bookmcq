Example:
f(x)=2xf(x)=2^xf(x)=2x passes through (0,1), (1,2), (2,4), and is increasing; g(x)=(1/2)xg(x)=(1/2)^xg(x)=(1/2)x is decreasing but also passes through (0,1).
