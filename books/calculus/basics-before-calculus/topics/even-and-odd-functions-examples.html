📝 Even and odd functions examples

📖 From Calculus • 1. Basics before calculus • 15 questions available

What is Even and odd functions examples?

Definition:
Even functions satisfy \( f(-x)=f(x) \) and are symmetric about the y-axis, while odd functions satisfy \( f(-x)=-f(x) \) and are symmetric about the origin, with examples including polynomials with only even/odd powers.

Example:
Even: \( f(x)=x^2+1 \), since \( f(-x)=(-x)^2+1=x^2+1=f(x) \); Odd: \( f(x)=x^3-x \), since \( f(-x)=(-x)^3-(-x)=-x^3+x=-(x^3-x)=-f(x) \).

Reason:
Classifying functions as even or odd helps in integration (e.g., integrals over symmetric intervals vanish for odd functions), and in understanding physical symmetries.

📝 All Even and odd functions examples MCQs

Q1. Consider \(f(x)=x^{4}-5x^{2}+3\). Which statement is true about its parity?

A. Even ✅
B. Odd
C. Neither
D. Both even and odd

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

Because an even function satisfies f(-x)=f(x) for all x, and each term of the polynomial \(x^{4}\) and \(-5x^{2}\) is even while the constant 3 is also even, the whole expression is unchanged by replacing x with -x, so the function is even.

Q2. If \(f\) is an even function and \(g\) is any function, which of the following must be true about the composition \(h(x)=f(g(x))\)?

A. h is even regardless of g.
B. h is odd regardless of g.
C. h has the same parity as g.
D. No parity can be guaranteed. ✅

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

The outer function f is even, meaning f(-y)=f(y). For the composition h(x)=f(g(x)), the value h(-x)=f(g(-x)). Since we have no information about g, the parity of h depends entirely on g, and no definite parity can be asserted. Hence option D is correct.

Q3. Let \(f\) be odd and \(g\) be even. What is the parity of the composition \(h(x)=f(g(x))\)?

A. Even
B. Odd
C. Neither ✅
D. Both even and odd

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Since g is even, g(-x)=g(x). Substituting into h gives h(-x)=f(g(x)). Because f is odd, f(g(x)) is generally not equal to h(x) nor its negative, unless g(x)=0. Therefore the composition does not possess a guaranteed even or odd symmetry; it is classified as neither.

Q4. Define \(h(x)=f(x)+g(x)\) where \(f\) is even and \(g\) is odd. Which statement about the parity of \(h\) is correct?

A. h is even
B. h is odd
C. h is neither even nor odd ✅
D. h is both even and odd

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

An even function satisfies f(-x)=f(x) while an odd function satisfies g(-x)=-g(x). Adding them yields h(-x)=f(x)-g(x), which is not equal to h(x) nor to -h(x) for most x. Hence the sum is typically neither even nor odd.

Q5. Which of the following graphs must represent an odd function?

A. Symmetric about the y‑axis
B. Symmetric about the origin ✅
C. Neither symmetry
D. Symmetric about both axes

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

A graph symmetric about the origin means that rotating the graph 180° leaves it unchanged, which is the defining geometric property of odd functions. Symmetry about the y‑axis corresponds to even functions. Therefore the correct description is symmetry about the origin.

Q6. The derivative of an even function is always:

A. Even
B. Odd ✅
C. Neither
D. Both

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

Differentiating an even function f yields f'(-x)= -f'(x) because the derivative of f(-x) equals -f'(x). This relationship shows that the derivative of an even function is odd, making option B the correct choice.

Q7. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2} x^{3}\,dx\). Which statement is correct?

A. Integral equals 0 ✅
B. Integral equals 8
C. Integral equals -8
D. Integral is undefined

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The integrand \(x^{3}\) is an odd function because \((-x)^{3} = -x^{3}\). Integrating an odd function over a symmetric interval \([-a,a]\) always yields zero, since the contributions from negative and positive halves cancel each other.

Q8. If \(p(x)=f(x)g(x)\) where \(f\) is even and \(g\) is odd, then \(p(x)\) is:

A. Neither
B. Even
C. Both
D. Odd ✅

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation:

When multiplying an even function by an odd function, the product satisfies p(-x)=f(-x)g(-x)=f(x)(-g(x))=-f(x)g(x)=-p(x). Hence the product is odd, which corresponds to option D in the reordered list.

Q9. Let \(f(x)=\sin x\) (odd). Consider \(k(x)=f(x- \pi)\). What is the parity of \(k\)?

A. Even
B. Odd
C. Neither ✅
D. Both

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Shifting \(\sin x\) by \(\pi\) gives \(k(x)=\sin(x-\pi) = -\sin x\). Evaluating \(k(-x)=\sin(-x-\pi)= -\sin(x+\pi)=\sin x\). Since \(k(-x)\neq \pm k(x)\), the function is neither even nor odd.

Q10. Apply absolute value to an odd function: \(h(x)=|f(x)|\) where \(f\) is odd and non‑zero for x≠0. What can be said about the parity of \(h\)?

A. Even ✅
B. Odd
C. Neither
D. Both

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

For any x, \(|f(-x)| = |-f(x)| = |f(x)|\). Thus the absolute‑value transformation removes the sign change that characterizes odd functions, resulting in a function that satisfies the even‑function condition. Hence the correct answer is 'Even'.

Q11. The composition of an even function with another even function, \(h(x)=f(g(x))\), is always:

A. Even ✅
B. Odd
C. Neither
D. Both

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

If both f and g are even, then g(-x)=g(x) and f(-y)=f(y). Substituting, h(-x)=f(g(-x))=f(g(x))=h(x). Therefore the composition of two even functions remains even.

Q12. If a function is both even and odd, which of the following must be true?

A. It is identically zero ✅
B. It is constant non‑zero
C. It is linear
D. No such function exists

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

A function that is both even and odd must satisfy f(x)=f(-x) and f(x)=-f(-x) simultaneously, which forces f(x)=0 for every x. The only function meeting both criteria is the zero function.

Q13. Using the Taylor series, explain why \(\cos x\) is even while \(\sin x\) is odd.

A. Cosine series contains only even powers of x, while sine series contains only odd powers, giving the respective symmetries. ✅
B. Cosine has zero coefficients for odd terms, sine has zero coefficients for even terms, but both are periodic.
C. Both series have mixed powers, but parity is defined by coefficients.
D. Parity cannot be deduced from series.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The Taylor series of \(\cos x\) contains only even powers of x ( \(1 - x^{2}/2! + x^{4}/4! -\dots\) ), while the series of \(\sin x\) contains only odd powers ( \(x - x^{3}/3! + x^{5}/5! -\dots\) ). Because replacing x with -x leaves the even‑power terms unchanged and flips the sign of odd‑power terms, cosine is even and sine is odd.

Q14. Consider the piecewise function \(f(x)=\begin{cases}x+2,& x\le -1\\-\,x,& -1< x<1\\x-2,& x\ge 1\end{cases}\). Which statement about its parity is correct?

A. f is even
B. f is odd
C. f is neither ✅
D. f is both

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

Testing the definition, for x=2 we have f(2)=0 and f(-2)=0? Actually f(-2)=(-2)+2=0, while -f(2)=0, but checking other points such as x=0.5 gives f(0.5) = -0.5 and f(-0.5)= -0.5? The values do not satisfy either even or odd conditions consistently, so the function is neither even nor odd.

Q15. In Fourier analysis, which terms appear in the series of an even function defined on \([-L,L]\)?

A. Only sine terms
B. Only cosine terms ✅
C. Both sine and cosine
D. No trigonometric terms

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

When a function is even on the interval \([-L,L]\), its Fourier expansion contains only cosine terms because cosine functions are even and sine functions are odd. The orthogonality of sine terms forces their coefficients to be zero, leaving a pure cosine series.
