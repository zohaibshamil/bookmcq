What is Ratio test for convergence?

For a series ∑an\sum a_n∑an​ with nonzero terms, compute L=lim⁡n→∞∣an+1an∣L = \lim_{n\to\infty} \left| \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} \right|L=limn→∞​​an​an+1​​​; if L<1L < 1L<1, the series converges absolutely; if L>1L > 1L>1 or L=∞L = \inftyL=∞, it diverges; if L=1L = 1L=1, the test is inconclusive, often used for factorials like ∑n!nn\sum \frac{n!}{n^n}∑nnn!​.
