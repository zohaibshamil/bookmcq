What is Taylor series multiplication division?

You can multiply and divide Taylor series term-by-term (within their convergence intervals) to find new series; for multiplication, use the Cauchy product ∑cn​xn⋅∑dn​xn=∑en​xn where en​=∑k=0n​ck​dn−k​, and division is done by long division of series, often used in physics for approximations.
