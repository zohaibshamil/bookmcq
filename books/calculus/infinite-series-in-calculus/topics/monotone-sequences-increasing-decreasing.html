What is Monotone sequences increasing decreasing?

A sequence is monotone if it is either increasing (each term is ≤\le≤ the next, an≤an+1a_n \le a_{n+1}an​≤an+1​) or decreasing (each term is ≥\ge≥ the next, an≥an+1a_n \ge a_{n+1}an​≥an+1​); for example, an=na_n = nan​=n is increasing, while an=1na_n = \frac{1}{n}an​=n1​ is decreasing, and monotone sequences are easier to analyze for convergence.
