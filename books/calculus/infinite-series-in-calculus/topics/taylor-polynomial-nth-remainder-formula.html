What is Taylor polynomial nth Remainder formula?

The remainder (error) after the n-th Taylor polynomial is Rn​(x)=f(x)−Pn​(x), and Lagrange's form is Rn​(x)=(n+1)!f(n+1)(c)​(x−a)n+1 for some c between a and x, which bounds the error if you know the maximum of the (n+1)-th derivative.
