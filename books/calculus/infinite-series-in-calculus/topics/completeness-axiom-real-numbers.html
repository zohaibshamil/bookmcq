What is Completeness axiom real numbers?

The completeness axiom says that every nonempty set of real numbers that is bounded above has a least upper bound (supremum) in RR, which is the foundation for the monotone convergence theorem because it guarantees that the supremum of the sequence terms is the actual limit.
