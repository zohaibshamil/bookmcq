What is Alternating series approximation?

For an alternating series ∑(−1)n+1bn\sum (-1)^{n+1} b_n∑(−1)n+1bn​ that satisfies the test, the error in using the first NNN terms to approximate the sum is at most the next term bN+1b_{N+1}bN+1​, so ∣S−SN∣≤bN+1|S - S_N| \le b_{N+1}∣S−SN​∣≤bN+1​, which gives a simple bound for numerical estimation.
