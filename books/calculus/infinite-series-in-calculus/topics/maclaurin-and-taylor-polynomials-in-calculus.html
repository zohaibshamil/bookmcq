What is Maclaurin and Taylor Polynomials in calculus?

Taylor polynomials are polynomial approximations of a function f(x) near a point a, using derivatives; the n-th Taylor polynomial is Pn​(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a)+2!f′′(a)​(x−a)2+⋯+n!f(n)(a)​(x−a)n. If a=0, it's called a Maclaurin polynomial.
