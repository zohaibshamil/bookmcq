What is Limit of a sequence calculus?

The limit of a sequence is the single number LLL that the terms ana_nan​ get arbitrarily close to as nnn becomes very large, written as lim⁡n→∞an=L\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n = Llimn→∞​an​=L; for example, lim⁡n→∞1n=0\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} = 0limn→∞​n1​=0 because 1n\frac{1}{n}n1​ shrinks to zero, and if no such LLL exists, the sequence diverges.
