What is Limit of a sequence calculus?

The limit of a sequence is the single number LL that the terms anan​ get arbitrarily close to as nn becomes very large, written as lim⁡n→∞an=Llimn→∞​an​=L; for example, lim⁡n→∞1n=0limn→∞​n1​=0 because 1nn1​ shrinks to zero, and if no such LL exists, the sequence diverges.
