What is Monotone convergence theorem?

This theorem states that every bounded monotone sequence converges: if a sequence is increasing and bounded above (there is M with an​≤M for all n), then it has a finite limit; similarly, if it is decreasing and bounded below, it converges, which is a powerful way to prove convergence without knowing the limit explicitly.
