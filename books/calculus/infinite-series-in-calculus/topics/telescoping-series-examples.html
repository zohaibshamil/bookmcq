What is Telescoping series examples?

A telescoping series has terms that cancel in a chain, like ∑n=1∞(1n−1n+1)∑n=1∞​(n1​−n+11​), whose partial sum is SN=1−1N+1SN​=1−N+11​, so the infinite sum is lim⁡N→∞SN=1limN→∞​SN​=1, making it easy to evaluate by writing out terms.
