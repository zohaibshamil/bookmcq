What is Convergence test for series?

A convergence test is a rule that helps decide whether an infinite series ∑an \sum a_n ∑an​ converges or diverges; common tests include the divergence test, integral test, comparison tests, ratio test, root test, and alternating series test, each suited for different types of terms.
