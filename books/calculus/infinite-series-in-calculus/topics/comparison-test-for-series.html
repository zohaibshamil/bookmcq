What is Comparison test for series?

If 0≤an≤bn0 \le a_n \le b_n0≤an​≤bn​ for all large nnn, and ∑bn\sum b_n∑bn​ converges, then ∑an\sum a_n∑an​ converges; if an≥bn≥0a_n \ge b_n \ge 0an​≥bn​≥0 and ∑bn\sum b_n∑bn​ diverges, then ∑an\sum a_n∑an​ diverges; for example, ∑1n2+1\sum \frac{1}{n^2+1}∑n2+11​ converges by comparing to ∑1n2\sum \frac{1}{n^2}∑n21​.
