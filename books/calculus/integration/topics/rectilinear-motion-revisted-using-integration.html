What is Rectilinear motion revisted using integration?

Definition:
In rectilinear motion, integrating acceleration \( a(t) \) gives velocity \( v(t) \), and integrating velocity gives position \( s(t) \). Initial conditions determine constants. \( v(t) = \int a(t) dt \) and \( s(t) = \int v(t) dt \).

Example:
If \( a(t) = 6t \) and \( v(0)=2 \), then \( v(t) = \int 6t \, dt = 3t^2 + C \). \( 2 = 0 + C \Rightarrow C=2 \). So \( v(t) = 3t^2 + 2 \).

Reason:
This application demonstrates the physical significance of integration in kinematics, enabling prediction of future position and velocity from acceleration data.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: For constant acceleration, displacement in nth interval: \( s_n = uT + \frac{1}{2} a (2n-1)T^2 \). So \( s_2 - s_1 = [uT + \frac{3}{2} a T^2] - [uT + \frac{1}{2} a T^2] = a T^2 \). Thus \( a = \frac{s_2 - s_1}{T^2} \). Option B has 1/2 factor, C has 2 factor, D has dimension of velocity. This is a classic problem requiring understanding of kinematics with constant acceleration and the relationship between displacements in equal time intervals.

Q25. A particle moves with velocity \( v(t) = t^2 - 1 \). What is the total distance traveled from \( t=0 \) to \( t=3 \)?

Option B (14/3) is from integrating without absolute value; C (16/3) is a common error; D (20/3) is just the positive part. So correct is A.
