What is Sigma notation summation explained?

Definition:
Sigma notation ∑i=1nai∑i=1n​ai​ compactly represents the sum of a sequence of terms. Here, ii is the index, 1 is the lower limit, nn is the upper limit, and aiai​ is the formula for the ith term, streamlining lengthy addition expressions.

Example:
Expand ∑k=14k2∑k=14​k2. Terms are 12,22,32,4212,22,32,42. Sum =1+4+9+16=30=1+4+9+16=30. This notation avoids writing out every term individually.

Reason:
Mastering sigma notation is essential for writing Riemann sums clearly and concisely, facilitating the transition from finite sums to infinite limits in integral calculus definitions.
