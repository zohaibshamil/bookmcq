What is Irrational exponents real powers?

Definition:
Irrational exponents are defined via limits of rational exponents or using bx=exln⁡bb^x = e^{x \ln b}bx=exlnb. This extends power functions to all real numbers, ensuring continuity and differentiability for bases b>0b>0b>0.

Example:

Reason:
This definition allows calculus operations on general exponential functions, enabling modeling of phenomena with non-integer growth rates.
