What is e^x definition exponential function?

Definition:
The number e is defined such that ln(e)=1. The exponential function ex is the inverse of ln(x). It is the unique function equal to its own derivative: dxd​ex=ex.

Example:
Solve ex=5. Take ln: ln(ex)=ln(5)⇒x=ln(5)≈1.609.

Reason:
The function ex models continuous growth and decay, central to finance, biology, and physics, due to its unique calculus properties.
