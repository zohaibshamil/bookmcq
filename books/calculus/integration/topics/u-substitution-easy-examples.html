What is U substitution easy examples?

Definition:
Easy u-substitution cases involve linear inner functions or obvious derivatives. The goal is to identify uuu such that dududu matches the remaining terms in the integrand, allowing direct replacement and integration using power or exponential rules.

Example:
Integrate ∫e5x dx\int e^{5x} \, dx∫e5xdx. Let u=5xu = 5xu=5x, du=5 dx⇒dx=du/5du = 5 \, dx \Rightarrow dx = du/5du=5dx⇒dx=du/5. Integral: ∫eudu5=15eu+C=15e5x+C\int e^u \frac{du}{5} = \frac{1}{5}e^u + C = \frac{1}{5}e^{5x} + C∫eu5du​=51​eu+C=51​e5x+C.

Reason:
Practicing simple substitutions builds confidence and pattern recognition skills, preparing students for more complex nonlinear substitutions required in advanced calculus problems.
