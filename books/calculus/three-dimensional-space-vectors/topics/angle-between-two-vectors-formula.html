What is Angle between two vectors formula?

Definition:
The angle θ \theta θ between nonzero vectors u⃗ \vec{u} u and v⃗ \vec{v} v satisfies cos⁡θ=u⃗⋅v⃗∥u⃗∥∥v⃗∥ \cos\theta = \frac{\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}}{\|\vec{u}\|\|\vec{v}\|} cosθ=∥u∥∥v∥u⋅v​, with θ∈[0,π] \theta \in [0, \pi] θ∈[0,π] determined uniquely by the inverse cosine.

Example:
For u⃗=⟨1,0⟩ \vec{u} = \langle 1,0 \rangle u=⟨1,0⟩ and v⃗=⟨1,1⟩ \vec{v} = \langle 1,1 \rangle v=⟨1,1⟩, cos⁡θ=12 \cos\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} cosθ=2​1​ so θ=45∘ \theta = 45^\circ θ=45∘.

Reason:
This formula translates algebraic dot product into measurable geometric angle, crucial for determining molecular bond angles, antenna alignment, or surface incidence in optics.
