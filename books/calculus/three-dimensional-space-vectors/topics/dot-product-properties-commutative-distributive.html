What is Dot product properties commutative distributive?

Definition:
Dot product is commutative u⋅v=v⋅u, distributive u⋅(v+w)=u⋅v+u⋅w, and compatible with scalars (cu)⋅v=c(u⋅v), but not associative.

Example:
Expanding (a+b)⋅(a−b) uses distributivity and commutativity to yield ∥a∥2−∥b∥2, analogous to difference of squares.

Reason:
These properties enable algebraic manipulation of inner products in proofs, derivations of laws of cosines, and simplification of energy expressions in physics.
