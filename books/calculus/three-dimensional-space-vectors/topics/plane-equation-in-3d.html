What is Plane equation in 3D?

Definition:
A plane with normal vector n⃗=⟨a,b,c⟩ \vec{n} = \langle a,b,c \rangle n=⟨a,b,c⟩ passing through (x0,y0,z0) (x_0,y_0,z_0) (x0​,y0​,z0​) satisfies a(x−x0)+b(y−y0)+c(z−z0)=0 a(x-x_0) + b(y-y_0) + c(z-z_0) = 0 a(x−x0​)+b(y−y0​)+c(z−z0​)=0, or generally ax+by+cz+d=0 ax+by+cz+d=0 ax+by+cz+d=0 where d=−(ax0+by0+cz0) d = -(ax_0+by_0+cz_0) d=−(ax0​+by0​+cz0​).

Example:
Plane through (1,2,3) (1,2,3) (1,2,3) with normal ⟨2,−1,4⟩ \langle 2,-1,4 \rangle ⟨2,−1,4⟩ has equation 2(x−1)−(y−2)+4(z−3)=0 2(x-1) - (y-2) + 4(z-3) = 0 2(x−1)−(y−2)+4(z−3)=0, simplifying to 2x−y+4z=12 2x - y + 4z = 12 2x−y+4z=12.

Reason:
Normal vector form encodes orientation intrinsically, enabling distance calculations, reflection laws, and defining tangent planes to surfaces in multivariable optimization.
