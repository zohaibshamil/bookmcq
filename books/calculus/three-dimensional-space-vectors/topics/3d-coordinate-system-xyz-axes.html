What is 3D coordinate system xyz axes?

Definition:
The three-dimensional Cartesian coordinate system consists of three mutually perpendicular axes labeled x x x, y y y, and z z z that intersect at the origin (0,0,0) (0,0,0) (0,0,0) to locate any point in space as an ordered triple (x,y,z) (x,y,z) (x,y,z).

Example:
The point P(2,−1,4) P(2, -1, 4) P(2,−1,4) is located 2 units along the positive x x x-axis, 1 unit along the negative y y y-axis, and 4 units up the positive z z z-axis.

Reason:
This orthogonal framework extends 2D graphing into space, allowing precise description of physical phenomena like fluid flow or electromagnetic fields where three independent variables are required.
