What is Intersection of two planes?

Definition:
Two nonparallel planes a1x+b1y+c1z=d1a_1x+b_1y+c_1z=d_1a1​x+b1​y+c1​z=d1​ and a2x+b2y+c2z=d2a_2x+b_2y+c_2z=d_2a2​x+b2​y+c2​z=d2​ intersect in a line whose direction vector is n⃗1×n⃗2\vec{n}_1 \times \vec{n}_2n1​×n2​, found by solving the linear system.

Example:
Planes x+y+z=1x+y+z=1x+y+z=1 and x−y+z=0x-y+z=0x−y+z=0 have normals ⟨1,1,1⟩\langle 1,1,1 \rangle⟨1,1,1⟩ and ⟨1,−1,1⟩\langle 1,-1,1 \rangle⟨1,−1,1⟩; cross product ⟨2,0,−2⟩\langle 2,0,-2 \rangle⟨2,0,−2⟩ gives line direction, and solving yields parametric line.

Reason:
Line of intersection defines edges in polyhedra, solution sets in linear systems, and paths of constrained motion, vital in solid modeling and solving overdetermined systems.
