What is Cross product definition and formula?

Definition:
Cross product \( \vec{u} \times \vec{v} \) is vector with magnitude \( \|\vec{u}\|\|\vec{v}\|\sin\theta \) and direction perpendicular to plane of \( \vec{u},\vec{v} \) following right-hand rule, defined only in \( \mathbb{R}^3 \) (and \( \mathbb{R}^7 \)).

Example:
If \( \|\vec{u}\| = 3 \), \( \|\vec{v}\| = 4 \), and \( \theta = 30^\circ \), then \( \|\vec{u} \times \vec{v}\| = 3 \cdot 4 \cdot \sin 30^\circ = 6 \), pointing normal to their plane.

Reason:
Geometric definition emphasizes area interpretation and handedness, critical for defining oriented surfaces, magnetic fields, and rotational dynamics where direction matters as much as magnitude.

Q4. Which of the following best explains why \( \mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) \neq (\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}) \times \mathbf{c} \) in general?

B. The cross product results in a vector outside the original span.

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

This tests deep conceptual understanding of vector algebra structure. The vector triple product expansion shows \( \mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c})\mathbf{b} - (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b})\mathbf{c} \), which is linear combination of \( \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{c} \). Different subspaces imply non-associativity beyond simple commutativity.
