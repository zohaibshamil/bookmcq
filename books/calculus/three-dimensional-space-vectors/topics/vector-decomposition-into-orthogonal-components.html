What is Vector decomposition into orthogonal components?

Definition:
Any vector v can be decomposed relative to unit vector u^ as v=proju^​v+v⊥​, where parallel component is (v⋅u^)u^ and perpendicular component is v−(v⋅u^)u^.

Example:
Decomposing v=⟨3,4⟩ along u^=⟨1,0⟩ yields parallel part ⟨3,0⟩ and perpendicular part ⟨0,4⟩.

Reason:
Orthogonal decomposition isolates effects along specific directions, enabling analysis of inclined planes, signal filtering, and separation of variables in PDEs.
