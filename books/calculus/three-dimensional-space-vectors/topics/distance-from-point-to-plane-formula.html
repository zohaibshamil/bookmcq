What is Distance from point to plane formula?

Definition:
Distance from point (x0,y0,z0) (x_0,y_0,z_0) (x0​,y0​,z0​) to plane ax+by+cz+d=0 ax+by+cz+d=0 ax+by+cz+d=0 is D=∣ax0+by0+cz0+d∣a2+b2+c2 D = \frac{|ax_0+by_0+cz_0+d|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}} D=a2+b2+c2​∣ax0​+by0​+cz0​+d∣​, derived from projection of point-to-plane vector onto unit normal.

Example:
Distance from (1,2,3) (1,2,3) (1,2,3) to plane 2x−y+2z−4=0 2x-y+2z-4=0 2x−y+2z−4=0 is ∣2−2+6−4∣4+1+4=23 \frac{|2-2+6-4|}{\sqrt{4+1+4}} = \frac{2}{3} 4+1+4​∣2−2+6−4∣​=32​.

Reason:
This formula quantifies shortest separation, used in collision avoidance, tolerance checking in manufacturing, and defining level sets in optimization and machine learning classifiers.
