What is Line segment in 3D?

Definition:
A line segment between endpoints \( A \) and \( B \) is parameterized as \( \vec{r}(t) = (1-t)\vec{A} + t\vec{B} \) for \( t \in [0,1] \), restricting infinite line to finite interval.

Example:
Segment from \( (0,0,0) \) to \( (2,4,6) \) at \( t=0.5 \) gives midpoint \( (1,2,3) \); values outside \( [0,1] \) lie beyond endpoints.

Reason:
Bounded parameterization models physical edges, wires, or trajectories with start/end conditions, crucial for CAD modeling, animation keyframes, and finite element meshing.
