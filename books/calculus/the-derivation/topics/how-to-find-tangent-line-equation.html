What is How to find tangent line equation?

Definition:
To find the tangent line equation at a point, calculate the derivative to get the slope at that specific x-value, then use the point-slope form y−y1=m(x−x1) y - y_1 = m(x - x_1) y−y1​=m(x−x1​) with the known point coordinates to construct the linear equation.

Example:
For f(x)=x2 f(x) = x^2 f(x)=x2 at (1,1) (1,1) (1,1), slope m=2 m=2 m=2, so equation is y−1=2(x−1) y - 1 = 2(x - 1) y−1=2(x−1) or y=2x−1 y = 2x - 1 y=2x−1.

Reason:
This procedure combines differentiation with algebra, enabling students to approximate functions locally and solve optimization problems by identifying critical points where tangents are horizontal.
