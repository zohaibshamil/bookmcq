What is Derivative notations dy/dx f'(x) D?

Definition:
Derivative notations include Leibniz notation dydx \frac{dy}{dx} dxdy​ emphasizing ratios of changes, Lagrange notation f′(x) f'(x) f′(x) for compactness, and operator notation Dxf D_x f Dx​f highlighting the differentiation operation, each serving different contextual purposes in mathematical communication.

Example:
For y=x3 y = x^3 y=x3, we write dydx=3x2 \frac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 dxdy​=3x2, or f′(x)=3x2 f'(x) = 3x^2 f′(x)=3x2, or Dx(x3)=3x2 D_x(x^3) = 3x^2 Dx​(x3)=3x2, all representing the same derivative result.

Reason:
Familiarity with multiple notations enhances flexibility in problem-solving, as different fields prefer specific styles, and understanding them prevents confusion when reading diverse mathematical texts and scientific literature.
