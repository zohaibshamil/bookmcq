What is Parametric representation of surfaces of revolution?

Definition:
A surface generated by rotating a curve y=f(x)y=f(x) around the x-axis can be parametrized as r(x,θ)=⟨x,f(x)cos⁡θ,f(x)sin⁡θ⟩r(x,θ)=⟨x,f(x)cosθ,f(x)sinθ⟩.

Example:
Rotating y=xy=x​ around the x-axis gives r(x,θ)=⟨x,xcos⁡θ,xsin⁡θ⟩r(x,θ)=⟨x,x​cosθ,x​sinθ⟩.

Reason:
This representation exploits symmetry, reducing the description of a 3D object to a 2D curve and an angle, simplifying analysis and integration.
