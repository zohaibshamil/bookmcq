What is Convert double integral to polar coordinates?

Definition:
To convert, substitute x=rcos⁡θ x=r\cos\theta x=rcosθ, y=rsin⁡θ y=r\sin\theta y=rsinθ, replace dA dA dA with r dr dθ r \, dr \, d\theta rdrdθ, and adjust the limits to describe the region in terms of r r r and θ \theta θ.

Example:
The integral ∫−11∫01−x2f(x,y) dy dx \int_{-1}^1 \int_0^{\sqrt{1-x^2}} f(x,y) \, dy \, dx ∫−11​∫01−x2​​f(x,y)dydx over the upper semicircle becomes ∫0π∫01f(rcos⁡θ,rsin⁡θ) r dr dθ \int_0^\pi \int_0^1 f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) \, r \, dr \, d\theta ∫0π​∫01​f(rcosθ,rsinθ)rdrdθ.

Reason:
Conversion is necessary when the region or integrand exhibits radial symmetry, drastically simplifying the algebraic complexity of the integral.
