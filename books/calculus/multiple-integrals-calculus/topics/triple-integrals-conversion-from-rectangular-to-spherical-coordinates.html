What is Triple Integrals Conversion from Rectangular to Spherical Coordinates?

Definition:
Substitute the spherical expressions for x,y,zx,y,z, replace dVdV with ρ2sin⁡ϕ dρ dϕ dθρ2sinϕdρdϕdθ, and adjust limits. Note ρ≥0,0≤ϕ≤π,0≤θ≤2πρ≥0,0≤ϕ≤π,0≤θ≤2π.

Example:
The cone z=x2+y2z=x2+y2​ becomes ϕ=π/4ϕ=π/4 in spherical coordinates.

Reason:
Conversion leverages symmetry, turning complex Cartesian bounds into constant limits in spherical coordinates, greatly simplifying integration.
