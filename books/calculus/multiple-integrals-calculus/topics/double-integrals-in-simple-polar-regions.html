What is Double integrals in Simple Polar Regions?

Definition:
A simple polar region is defined by α≤θ≤β \alpha \le \theta \le \beta α≤θ≤β and g1(θ)≤r≤g2(θ) g_1(\theta) \le r \le g_2(\theta) g1​(θ)≤r≤g2​(θ). These regions are bounded by rays from the origin and curves defined in polar form.

Example:
The region inside the cardioid r=1+cos⁡θ r = 1+\cos\theta r=1+cosθ is described by 0≤θ≤2π 0 \le \theta \le 2\pi 0≤θ≤2π and 0≤r≤1+cos⁡θ 0 \le r \le 1+\cos\theta 0≤r≤1+cosθ.

Reason:
Many natural shapes (petals, spirals, circles off-center) are naturally described in polar form, making this coordinate system essential for accurate modeling and integration.
