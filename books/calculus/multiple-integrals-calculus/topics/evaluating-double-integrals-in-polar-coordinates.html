What is Evaluating Double integrals in polar coordinates?

Definition:
Evaluation follows the same iterated process as Cartesian, but with the extra factor of rrr in the integrand. We integrate with respect to rrr first (usually), then θ\thetaθ.

Example:
For ∬Ry dA\iint_R y \, dA∬R​ydA over the upper half-disk, we compute ∫0π∫01(rsin⁡θ) r dr dθ=∫0πsin⁡θ dθ∫01r2 dr=2⋅13=23\int_0^\pi \int_0^1 (r\sin\theta) \, r \, dr \, d\theta = \int_0^\pi \sin\theta \, d\theta \int_0^1 r^2 \, dr = 2 \cdot \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}∫0π​∫01​(rsinθ)rdrdθ=∫0π​sinθdθ∫01​r2dr=2⋅31​=32​.

Reason:
The Jacobian factor rrr accounts for the stretching of area elements as we move away from the origin, ensuring accurate accumulation of values across the polar grid.
