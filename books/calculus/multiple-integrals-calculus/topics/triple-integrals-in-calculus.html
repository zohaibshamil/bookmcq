What is Triple Integrals in Calculus?

Definition:
A triple integral extends the concept of double integrals to three dimensions, integrating a function f(x,y,z)f(x,y,z) over a 3D region EE: ∭Ef(x,y,z) dV∭E​f(x,y,z)dV.

Example:
The mass of a solid with density ρ(x,y,z)ρ(x,y,z) is M=∭Eρ(x,y,z) dVM=∭E​ρ(x,y,z)dV.

Reason:
Triple integrals allow us to calculate physical quantities like mass, charge, and average value over 3D volumes, essential for physics and engineering applications.
