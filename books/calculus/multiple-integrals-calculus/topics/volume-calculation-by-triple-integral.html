What is Volume Calculation by Triple Integral?

Definition:
The volume of a 3D region E E E is given by V=∭E1 dV V = \iiint_E 1 \, dV V=∭E​1dV.

Example:
The volume of the unit cube is ∫01∫01∫011 dz dy dx=1 \int_0^1 \int_0^1 \int_0^1 1 \, dz \, dy \, dx = 1 ∫01​∫01​∫01​1dzdydx=1.

Reason:
This generalizes the area calculation to 3D, providing a unified method for finding volumes of arbitrary solids, including those with complex boundaries.
