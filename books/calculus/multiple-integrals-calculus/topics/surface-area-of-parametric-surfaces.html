What is Surface area of parametric surfaces?

Definition:
The surface area is computed by integrating the magnitude of the cross product of partial derivatives: A=∬D∥ru×rv∥ du dvA = \iint_D \| \mathbf{r}_u \times \mathbf{r}_v \| \, du \, dvA=∬D​∥ru​×rv​∥dudv.

Example:
For the cone z=x2+y2z = \sqrt{x^2+y^2}z=x2+y2​ parametrized appropriately, the cross product magnitude yields the slant factor needed for correct area calculation.

Reason:
The cross product magnitude represents the area of the parallelogram spanned by tangent vectors, which approximates the surface area element, ensuring accurate total area computation.
