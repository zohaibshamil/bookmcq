What is Parametric surfaces representation?

Definition:
A parametric surface is represented by a vector function r(u,v)=x(u,v)i+y(u,v)j+z(u,v)k\mathbf{r}(u,v) = x(u,v)\mathbf{i} + y(u,v)\mathbf{j} + z(u,v)\mathbf{k}r(u,v)=x(u,v)i+y(u,v)j+z(u,v)k, where (u,v)(u,v)(u,v) vary over a domain DDD in the uv-plane.

Example:
A cylinder of radius RRR can be parametrized as r(u,v)=⟨Rcos⁡u,Rsin⁡u,v⟩\mathbf{r}(u,v) = \langle R\cos u, R\sin u, v \rangler(u,v)=⟨Rcosu,Rsinu,v⟩ for 0≤u≤2π0 \le u \le 2\pi0≤u≤2π and 0≤v≤h0 \le v \le h0≤v≤h.

Reason:
Parametrization allows us to describe complex 3D surfaces using two parameters, facilitating the calculation of tangent planes, normal vectors, and surface integrals.
