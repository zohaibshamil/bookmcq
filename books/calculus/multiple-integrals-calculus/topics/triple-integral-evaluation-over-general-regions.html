What is Triple Integral Evaluation over General Regions?

Definition:
For general regions, limits become functions. Type 1: zz between surfaces, (x,y)(x,y) in projection. Type 2: yy between surfaces, (x,z)(x,z) in projection. Type 3: xx between surfaces, (y,z)(y,z) in projection.

Example:
For a tetrahedron bounded by planes, we might use ∫01∫01−x∫01−x−yf dz dy dx∫01​∫01−x​∫01−x−y​fdzdydx.

Reason:
Real-world solids have irregular shapes; adapting limits to the bounding surfaces allows accurate integration over any 3D region.

Which principle gives the better basis for choosing between them?
