What is Differentials of multivariable functions?

Definition:
The total differential df=fxdx+fydy df = f_x dx + f_y dy df=fx​dx+fy​dy represents linear change in f f f resulting from infinitesimal changes dx,dy dx, dy dx,dy.

Example:
For volume V=πr2h V = \pi r^2 h V=πr2h, dV=2πrh dr+πr2 dh dV = 2\pi rh \, dr + \pi r^2 \, dh dV=2πrhdr+πr2dh estimates volume change from small radius/height errors.

Reason:
Differentials enable error propagation analysis, sensitivity studies, and change-of-variables in integration through linear approximation of nonlinear relationships.
