What is Equality of mixed partial derivatives theorem?

Definition:
Clairaut's Theorem states fxy=fyx f_{xy} = f_{yx} fxy​=fyx​ at points where both mixed partials are continuous, ensuring order independence.

Example:
For smooth f(x,y)=x2y3+sin⁡(xy) f(x,y) = x^2y^3 + \sin(xy) f(x,y)=x2y3+sin(xy), mixed partials equal everywhere; pathological counterexamples exist only where continuity fails.

Reason:
This symmetry simplifies computation and theoretical proofs, reducing independent second derivatives from four to three for two-variable functions.
