What is Finding relative extrema of two variable functions?

Definition:
Procedure: find critical points (∇f=0\nabla f=\mathbf{0}∇f=0), apply second derivative test D=fxxfyy−fxy2D=f_{xx}f_{yy}-f_{xy}^2D=fxx​fyy​−fxy2​, classify based on sign of DDD and fxxf_{xx}fxx​.

Example:
For f=x3−3xy+y3f=x^3-3xy+y^3f=x3−3xy+y3, critical point (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) has D=9>0,fxx=6>0D=9>0, f_{xx}=6>0D=9>0,fxx​=6>0 ⇒ local min; (0,0)(0,0)(0,0) has D=0D=0D=0 ⇒ test inconclusive.

Reason:
Systematic classification replaces guesswork; inconclusive cases require alternative methods like completing square or path analysis.
