What is Partial derivatives of three variable functions?

Definition:
Computing fx,fy,fzfx​,fy​,fz​ for w=f(x,y,z)w=f(x,y,z) by differentiating with respect to one variable while fixing the other two as constants.

Example:
For f(x,y,z)=xyz+ezf(x,y,z)=xyz+ez, fz=xy+ezfz​=xy+ez treats x,yx,y as constants.

Reason:
Three-variable partials extend gradient concepts to R3R3, enabling analysis of volumetric fields, thermodynamic potentials, and spatial dynamics.
