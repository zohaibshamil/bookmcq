What is Partial Derivatives in calculus?

Definition:
Partial derivative fx(a,b)=lim⁡h→0f(a+h,b)−f(a,b)hfx​(a,b)=limh→0​hf(a+h,b)−f(a,b)​ measures rate of change with respect to one variable while holding others constant.

Example:
For f(x,y)=x2y+y3f(x,y)=x2y+y3, fx=2xyfx​=2xy treats yy as constant coefficient during differentiation.

Reason:
Isolating individual variable effects decomposes complex multivariable behavior into manageable single-variable components for analysis and approximation.
