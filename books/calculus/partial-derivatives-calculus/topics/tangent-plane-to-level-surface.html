What is Tangent plane to level surface?

Definition:
For implicit surface F(x,y,z)=kF(x,y,z)=k, tangent plane at PP is ∇F(P)⋅⟨x−x0,y−y0,z−z0⟩=0∇F(P)⋅⟨x−x0​,y−y0​,z−z0​⟩=0 with normal ∇F(P)∇F(P).

Example:
Tangent plane to sphere x2+y2+z2=9x2+y2+z2=9 at (1,2,2)(1,2,2) uses normal ⟨2,4,4⟩⟨2,4,4⟩: 2(x−1)+4(y−2)+4(z−2)=02(x−1)+4(y−2)+4(z−2)=0.

Reason:
Implicit formulation handles non-functional surfaces; gradient-as-normal unifies explicit and implicit tangent plane derivations elegantly.
