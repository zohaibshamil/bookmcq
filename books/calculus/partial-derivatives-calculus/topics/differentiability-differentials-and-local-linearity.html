What is Differentiability Differentials and Local Linearity?

Definition:
Multivariable differentiability means ff can be approximated by linear map L(x,y)=f(a,b)+fx(a,b)(x−a)+fy(a,b)(y−b)L(x,y)=f(a,b)+fx​(a,b)(x−a)+fy​(a,b)(y−b) with error vanishing faster than distance.

Example:
Near (1,2)(1,2), f(x,y)=x2+yf(x,y)=x2+y is approximated by L(x,y)=3+2(x−1)+1(y−2)L(x,y)=3+2(x−1)+1(y−2).

Reason:
Differentiability justifies linear approximation, enabling error analysis, numerical methods, and defining tangent planes rigorously beyond mere partial existence.
