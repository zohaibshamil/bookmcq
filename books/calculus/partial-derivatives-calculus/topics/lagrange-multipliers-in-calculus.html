What is Lagrange Multipliers in calculus?

Definition:
Method finds extrema of f(x,y) f(x,y) f(x,y) subject to constraint g(x,y)=k g(x,y)=k g(x,y)=k by solving ∇f=λ∇g \nabla f = \lambda \nabla g ∇f=λ∇g and g=k g=k g=k simultaneously.

Example:
Maximize area A=xy A=xy A=xy subject to perimeter 2x+2y=20 2x+2y=20 2x+2y=20; solve ⟨y,x⟩=λ⟨2,2⟩ \langle y,x \rangle = \lambda \langle 2,2 \rangle ⟨y,x⟩=λ⟨2,2⟩ ⇒ x=y=5 x=y=5 x=y=5.

Reason:
Transforms constrained optimization into system of equations; geometric insight: optimum occurs where objective and constraint contours are tangent.
