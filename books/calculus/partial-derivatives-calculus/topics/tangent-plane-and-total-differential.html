What is Tangent plane and total differential?

Definition:
Total differential dz=fxdx+fydy dz = f_x dx + f_y dy dz=fx​dx+fy​dy gives vertical change along tangent plane; Δz≈dz \Delta z \approx dz Δz≈dz for small displacements.

Example:
On z=x2+y z=x^2+y z=x2+y at (1,2) (1,2) (1,2), dz=2dx+dy dz = 2dx + dy dz=2dx+dy; moving dx=0.1,dy=−0.1 dx=0.1, dy=-0.1 dx=0.1,dy=−0.1 gives dz=0.1 dz=0.1 dz=0.1, approximating actual Δz \Delta z Δz.

Reason:
Links geometric tangent plane to analytic differential, providing error bounds and justifying linear models in measurement and computation.
