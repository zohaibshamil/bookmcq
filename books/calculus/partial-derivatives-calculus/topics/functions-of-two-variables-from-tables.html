What is Functions of two variables from tables?

Definition:
A tabular representation lists discrete pairs (xi,yj) (x_i, y_j) (xi​,yj​) alongside corresponding function values f(xi,yj) f(x_i, y_j) f(xi​,yj​) without an explicit algebraic formula.

Example:
A weather table showing wind chill W(v,T) W(v, T) W(v,T) for specific wind speeds v v v and temperatures T T T at grid intersections.

Reason:
Tables provide empirical data for functions derived from experiments where analytical expressions are unknown or too complex to formulate.
