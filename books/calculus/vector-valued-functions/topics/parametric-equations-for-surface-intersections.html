What is Parametric equations for surface intersections?

Definition:
Parametric equations for surface intersections are derived by finding a vector function \( \vec{r}(t) \) that satisfies the equations of two surfaces simultaneously to describe their common curve.

Example:
The intersection of the cylinder \( x^2 + y^2 = 4 \) and plane \( z = x \) is parameterized as \( \vec{r}(t) = \langle 2\cos t, 2\sin t, 2\cos t \rangle \).

Reason:
Finding this parametrization converts a geometric intersection problem into a single-variable calculus problem suitable for integration or differentiation.
