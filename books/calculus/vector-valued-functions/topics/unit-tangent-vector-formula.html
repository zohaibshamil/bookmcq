What is Unit tangent vector formula?

Definition:
The unit tangent vector is T⃗(t)=r⃗′(t)∥r⃗′(t)∥T(t)=∥r′(t)∥r′(t)​, indicating direction of motion normalized to length one.

Example:
For r⃗(t)=⟨t,t2⟩r(t)=⟨t,t2⟩, T⃗(t)=⟨1,2t⟩1+4t2T(t)=1+4t2​⟨1,2t⟩​.

Reason:
Normalization isolates directional information from speed, enabling consistent comparison of curve orientation regardless of parametrization.
