What is Central forces in orbital motion?

Definition:
Central forces act along the radial direction F⃗=f(r)r^\vec{F} = f(r)\hat{r}F=f(r)r^, conserving angular momentum and confining motion to a plane.

Example:
Gravitational and electrostatic forces are central, leading to planar orbits.

Reason:
Conservation laws from central symmetry reduce 3D problems to 2D effective potentials, simplifying orbital analysis.
