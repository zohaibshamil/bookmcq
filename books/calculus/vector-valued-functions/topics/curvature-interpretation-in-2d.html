What is Curvature interpretation in 2D?

Definition:
In 2D, signed curvature kkk indicates bending direction: positive for counterclockwise turning, negative for clockwise.

Example:
y=x2y = x^2y=x2 has positive curvature everywhere; y=−x2y = -x^2y=−x2 has negative curvature.

Reason:
Sign distinguishes convexity from concavity, aiding in graph analysis and determining stability in mechanical systems.
