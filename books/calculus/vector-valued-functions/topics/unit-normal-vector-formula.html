What is Unit normal vector formula?

Definition:
The principal unit normal vector is N⃗(t)=T⃗′(t)∥T⃗′(t)∥\vec{N}(t) = \frac{\vec{T}'(t)}{\| \vec{T}'(t) \|}N(t)=∥T′(t)∥T′(t)​, pointing toward the center of curvature.

Example:
For uniform circular motion, N⃗\vec{N}N always points radially inward toward the circle's center.

Reason:
It quantifies the direction in which the curve is turning, directly relating to centripetal acceleration and bending.
