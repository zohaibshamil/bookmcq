What is Curvature in calculus?

Definition:
Curvature κκ measures how sharply a curve bends, defined as κ=∥dT⃗ds∥κ=∥dsdT​∥.

Example:
A straight line has κ=0κ=0, while a circle of radius RR has constant κ=1/Rκ=1/R.

Reason:
It provides a scalar invariant quantifying deviation from linearity, fundamental in road design, optics, and relativity.
