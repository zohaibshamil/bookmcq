What is Inward unit normal vector in 2D?

Definition:
In 2D, the inward unit normal is obtained by rotating T⃗=⟨a,b⟩\vec{T} = \langle a, b \rangleT=⟨a,b⟩ clockwise to ⟨b,−a⟩\langle b, -a \rangle⟨b,−a⟩ or selecting the sign pointing toward concavity.

Example:
For parabola y=x2y=x^2y=x2 at origin, inward normal is ⟨0,1⟩\langle 0, 1 \rangle⟨0,1⟩ pointing up into the cup.

Reason:
Distinguishing inward from outward normals is critical for flux integrals, pressure forces, and defining enclosed regions.
