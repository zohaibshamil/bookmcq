What is Unit Tangent Normal and Binormal Vectors?

Definition:
The TNB frame consists of orthogonal unit vectors: Tangent T⃗ \vec{T} T, Normal N⃗ \vec{N} N, and Binormal B⃗=T⃗×N⃗ \vec{B} = \vec{T} \times \vec{N} B=T×N.

Example:
For a circle, T⃗ \vec{T} T points along motion, N⃗ \vec{N} N toward center, and B⃗ \vec{B} B perpendicular to the plane.

Reason:
This moving reference frame decomposes acceleration and describes local orientation, essential for Frenet-Serret formulas.
