What is Conic sections definition parabola ellipse hyperbola?

Definition: A parabola is set of points equidistant from a focus and directrix (e=1). An ellipse is set of points with sum of distances to two foci constant (e<1). A hyperbola is set with absolute difference of distances to two foci constant (e>1).
Example: Parabola y2=4px y^2=4px y2=4px focus (p,0) (p,0) (p,0), directrix x=−p x=-p x=−p. Ellipse x2/a2+y2/b2=1 x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 =1 x2/a2+y2/b2=1 with a>b a>b a>b, foci at (±c,0) (\pm c,0) (±c,0), c2=a2−b2 c^2=a^2-b^2 c2=a2−b2. Hyperbola x2/a2−y2/b2=1 x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2=1 x2/a2−y2/b2=1, foci (±c,0) (\pm c,0) (±c,0), c2=a2+b2 c^2=a^2+b^2 c2=a2+b2.
Reason: These definitions unify conics via distance properties, leading to reflective and orbital applications.
