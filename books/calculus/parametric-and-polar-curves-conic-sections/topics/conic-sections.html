What is Conic Sections?

Definition: Conic sections are curves formed by intersecting a plane with a double cone. They include parabolas (eccentricity e=1), ellipses (e<1), hyperbolas (e>1), and circles (e=0, special ellipse). General second-degree equation: Ax2+Bxy+Cy2+Dx+Ey+F=0Ax^2+Bxy+Cy^2+Dx+Ey+F=0Ax2+Bxy+Cy2+Dx+Ey+F=0.
Example: Circle: x2+y2=25x^2+y^2=25x2+y2=25, ellipse: x2/9+y2/4=1x^2/9 + y^2/4 = 1x2/9+y2/4=1, parabola: y=x2y=x^2y=x2, hyperbola: x2/9−y2/4=1x^2/9 - y^2/4 = 1x2/9−y2/4=1.
Reason: Conics are fundamental in physics (orbits), optics (reflection), and engineering (suspension bridges, telescopes).
