What is Rose curves polar equations r = a sin(nθ)?

Example: r=3sin⁡(2θ)r = 3\sin(2\theta)r=3sin(2θ) has 2n=42n = 42n=4 petals, each length 3. r=2cos⁡(3θ)r = 2\cos(3\theta)r=2cos(3θ) has 3 petals (since n odd) of length 2.
Reason: Rose curves are beautiful symmetric patterns used in mathematics and art; they illustrate how trigonometric functions create radial periodicity.
