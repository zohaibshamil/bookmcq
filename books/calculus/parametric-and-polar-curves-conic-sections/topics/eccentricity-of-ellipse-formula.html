What is Eccentricity of ellipse formula?

Definition: For an ellipse x2/a2+y2/b2=1x2/a2+y2/b2=1 with a>ba>b, eccentricity e=c/a=a2−b2/ae=c/a=a2−b2​/a, where 0<e<10<e<1. For circle, e=0. For hyperbola, e>1.
Example: For ellipse x2/9+y2/4=1x2/9+y2/4=1, a=3,b=2 → c=√(9-4)=√5, so e=5/3≈0.745e=5​/3≈0.745. For a circle radius 5, e=0.
Reason: Eccentricity measures how much an ellipse deviates from a circle; it's crucial in orbital mechanics (e.g., planetary orbits).
