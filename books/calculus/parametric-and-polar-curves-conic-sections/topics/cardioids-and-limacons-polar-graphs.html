What is Cardioids and limacons polar graphs?

Definition: Limacons have equation r=a±bcos⁡θr=a±bcosθ or r=a±bsin⁡θr=a±bsinθ. If a=ba=b, it's a cardioid (heart-shaped). If a<ba<b, it has an inner loop. If a>ba>b, it's a convex limacon. Cardioid: r=a(1+cos⁡θ)r=a(1+cosθ).
Example: r=2+2cos⁡θr=2+2cosθ is a cardioid (cusp at origin). r=1+2cos⁡θr=1+2cosθ has an inner loop because a<ba<b (1<2). r=3+cos⁡θr=3+cosθ is a convex limacon.
Reason: These curves model orbits, radiation patterns, and mechanical cams; their polar form directly shows shape based on ratio a/ba/b.
