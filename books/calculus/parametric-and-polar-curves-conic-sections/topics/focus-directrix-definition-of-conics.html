What is Focus directrix definition of conics?

Definition: A conic is the set of points P such that distance to a focus (point) divided by distance to a directrix (line) equals eccentricity e. For parabola e=1, ellipse e<1, hyperbola e>1.
Example: Parabola y^2=4px: focus (p,0), directrix x=-p, and for any point, distance to focus = distance to directrix. Ellipse x^2/25+y^2/9=1: e=c/a=4/5<1.
Reason: This unified definition connects all conics and leads to polar equations and reflection properties.
