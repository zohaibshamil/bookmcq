What is Converting rectangular to parametric equations?

Definition: To convert a rectangular equation y=f(x) y = f(x) y=f(x) or F(x,y)=0 F(x,y)=0 F(x,y)=0 to parametric form, choose a parameter (often t t t) and set x=t x = t x=t (or x=g(t) x = g(t) x=g(t)), then solve for y y y in terms of t t t.

Example: For y=x2 y = x^2 y=x2, let x=t x = t x=t, then y=t2 y = t^2 y=t2, so parametric equations are x=t,y=t2 x=t, y=t^2 x=t,y=t2. For ellipse x2/4+y2/9=1 x^2/4 + y^2/9 = 1 x2/4+y2/9=1, use x=2cos⁡t,y=3sin⁡t x=2\cos t, y=3\sin t x=2cost,y=3sint.

Reason: Conversion gives flexibility for graphing, calculus, and modeling motion, especially when the rectangular form is difficult to integrate or differentiate.
