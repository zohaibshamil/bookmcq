What is Rotation of Axes?

Definition: To eliminate the xyxy-term in Ax2+Bxy+Cy2+Dx+Ey+F=0Ax2+Bxy+Cy2+Dx+Ey+F=0, rotate axes by angle θθ where cot⁡(2θ)=(A−C)/Bcot(2θ)=(A−C)/B. New coordinates: x=Xcos⁡θ−Ysin⁡θ,y=Xsin⁡θ+Ycos⁡θx=Xcosθ−Ysinθ,y=Xsinθ+Ycosθ.
Example: For x2+xy+y2=1x2+xy+y2=1, A=1,C=1,B=1 → cot⁡(2θ)=0cot(2θ)=0 → 2θ=π/22θ=π/2 → θ=45∘θ=45∘. Rotating gives 3X2/2+Y2/2=13X2/2+Y2/2=1, an ellipse.
Reason: Rotation simplifies the equation to standard conic form, making identification and graphing easier.
