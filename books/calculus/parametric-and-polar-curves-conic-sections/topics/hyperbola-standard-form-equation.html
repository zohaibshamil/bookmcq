What is Hyperbola standard form equation?

Definition: Hyperbola centered at origin, horizontal transverse axis: x2/a2−y2/b2=1 x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1 x2/a2−y2/b2=1. Vertical: y2/a2−x2/b2=1 y^2/a^2 - x^2/b^2 = 1 y2/a2−x2/b2=1. Foci at (±c,0) (\pm c,0) (±c,0) or (0,±c) (0,\pm c) (0,±c) with c2=a2+b2 c^2 = a^2+b^2 c2=a2+b2. Vertices at (±a,0) (\pm a,0) (±a,0) or (0,±a) (0,\pm a) (0,±a).
Example: x2/9−y2/16=1 x^2/9 - y^2/16 = 1 x2/9−y2/16=1 → a=3, b=4, c=5, vertices (±3,0), foci (±5,0). Vertical: y2/4−x2/25=1 y^2/4 - x^2/25 = 1 y2/4−x2/25=1 → a=2, b=5, c=√29, vertices (0,±2), foci (0,±√29).
Reason: Standard form gives asymptotes and orientation, key for sketching and understanding hyperbola's open branches.
