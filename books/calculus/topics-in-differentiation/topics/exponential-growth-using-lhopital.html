What is Exponential growth using L'Hopital?

Definition:
When analyzing limits involving exponential growth, L'Hôpital's Rule helps determine the dominance of exponential functions over polynomials or logarithms. For instance, in forms like xnex\frac{x^n}{e^x}exxn​ as x→∞x \to \inftyx→∞, repeated application of the rule shows that the exponential term in the denominator grows much faster, driving the limit to zero, illustrating the superior growth rate of exponentials.

Reason:
Understanding exponential dominance is vital in physics and computer science. It explains why certain processes stabilize or diverge rapidly. L'Hôpital's Rule provides a rigorous proof of these hierarchical growth rates, confirming intuitive observations about the speed of exponential expansion.
