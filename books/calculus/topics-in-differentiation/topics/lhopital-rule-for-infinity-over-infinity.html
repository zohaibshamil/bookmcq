What is L'Hopital rule for infinity over infinity?

Definition:

This allows for the comparison of growth rates between functions by examining their derivatives rather than their raw values.

Reason:
Comparing infinite growth rates is crucial in algorithm analysis and asymptotic behavior studies. This form of L'Hôpital's Rule helps determine which function dominates as xxx approaches infinity, aiding in understanding long-term trends in mathematical models.
