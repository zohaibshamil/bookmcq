What is Derivative of ln x and log base b?

Definition:

For a logarithm with base bbb, denoted log⁡b(x)\log_b(x)logb​(x), the derivative is 1xln⁡(b)\frac{1}{x \ln(b)}xln(b)1​. This relationship arises because log⁡b(x)\log_b(x)logb​(x) can be rewritten as ln⁡(x)ln⁡(b)\frac{\ln(x)}{\ln(b)}ln(b)ln(x)​, making 1ln⁡(b)\frac{1}{\ln(b)}ln(b)1​ a constant multiplier in the differentiation process.

Reason:
