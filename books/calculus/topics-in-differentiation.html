Q1. Which of the following best describes an explicit definition of a function y in terms of x?

🔹 A. y appears alone on one side of the equation
🔹 B. y appears with x on both sides
🔹 C. The equation cannot be solved for y
🔹 D. y is defined implicitly

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

Q2. Given the implicit equation \( x^2 + y^2 = 1 \), solving for y yields \( y = \pm\sqrt{1 - x^2} \). What logical inference follows regarding the domain of each implicit function?

🔹 A. Both functions are defined for all real x
🔹 B. Both functions are defined only where \( 1 - x^2 \ge 0 \)
🔹 C. Only the positive branch is defined for \( x \ge 0 \)
🔹 D. The domain is unrestricted because the circle is closed

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

Q3. Suppose an implicit equation \( \sin(xy) = y \) has a solution \( y = g(x) \) near \( x = 0 \). Using the Implicit Function Theorem, which condition must be satisfied at \( (0,0) \) for \( g \) to exist?

🔹 A. \( \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\sin(xy) \neq 0 \) at (0,0)
🔹 B. \( \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\sin(xy) \neq 0 \) at (0,0)
🔹 C. Both partial derivatives must be zero
🔹 D. No condition is needed

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A
