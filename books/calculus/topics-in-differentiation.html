About 4. Topics in Differentiation

This chapter expands beyond basic differentiation rules to cover specialized techniques needed for more complex functions and relationships. Key topics include implicit differentiation for equations where y is not isolated, logarithmic differentiation for variables in exponents, and the derivatives of inverse trigonometric and hyperbolic functions. These advanced methods ensure that students can find rates of change for virtually any standard mathematical function they encounter in science and engineering.

Another critical topic in this chapter is higher-order derivatives and their physical interpretations, particularly the relationship between position, velocity, and acceleration. Students also explore parametric and polar differentiation, which are necessary for analyzing curves that cannot be easily expressed as simple y = f(x) functions. By mastering these specialized topics, learners gain the versatility to tackle sophisticated problems in physics, biology, and economics where standard differentiation rules alone are insufficient.
