What is Shell method volume revolving about y-axis?

Definition:
The shell method calculates volume by integrating cylindrical shells parallel to the axis of rotation. For rotation about the y-axis, shells have radius \( x \) and height \( h(x) \). The formula is \( V = 2\pi \int_{a}^{b} x \cdot h(x) \, dx \). This avoids solving for x in terms of y.

Example:
Rotate region under \( y = x^2 \) from \( x=0 \) to \( x=2 \) about y-axis. Height \( h(x) = x^2 \). Solution: \( V = 2\pi \int_{0}^{2} x(x^2) \, dx = 2\pi \int_{0}^{2} x^3 \, dx = 2\pi [\frac{x^4}{4}]_0^2 = 8\pi \).

Reason:
The shell method is often simpler than the washer method when rotating around the y-axis because it allows integration with respect to x, avoiding the need to invert functions or split integrals for complex regions.

Q1. A region bounded by \( y = \sqrt{x} \), the x-axis, \( x = 1 \), and \( x = 4 \) is revolved about the y-axis. Which integral correctly computes the volume using cylindrical shells?

A. \( 2\pi \int_{1}^{4} x \sqrt{x} \, dx \) ✅
B. \( 2\pi \int_{1}^{4} \sqrt{x} \, dx \)
C. \( \pi \int_{1}^{4} x^2 \, dx \)
D. \( 2\pi \int_{1}^{4} x^2 \sqrt{x} \, dx \)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The cylindrical shell method requires integrating the surface area of each shell, which is \( 2\pi \cdot (\text{radius}) \cdot (\text{height}) \). Here, the radius is \( x \) and the height is \( f(x) = \sqrt{x} \), so the integrand is \( 2\pi x \sqrt{x} \). Options B and C are incorrect because they omit the radius factor or use the wrong height. Option D incorrectly squares the radius component.

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The cylindrical shell method for revolving about the y-axis requires the region to be defined by \( y = f(x) \) with \( f(x) \ge 0 \) on \( [a,b] \) and \( 0 \le a < b \). This ensures the radius \( x \) is nonnegative. The method doesn't require the function to be monotonic. Options C and D are incorrect because they describe different scenarios or conditions.

Q4. A region is bounded by \( y = x \) and \( y = x^2 \) in the first quadrant. A solid is generated by revolving this region about the y-axis. Which of the following represents the correct integral for the volume?

A. \( 2\pi \int_{0}^{1} x (x - x^2) \, dx \) ✅
B. \( 2\pi \int_{0}^{1} x (x^2 - x) \, dx \)
C. \( \pi \int_{0}^{1} (x^2 - x^4) \, dx \)
D. \( 2\pi \int_{0}^{1} (x - x^2) \, dx \)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

For the shell method, the height of the shell at radius \( x \) is the difference between the upper and lower curves, \( x - x^2 \). The radius is \( x \), so the integrand is \( 2\pi x (x - x^2) \). Option B has the height reversed, giving a negative volume. Option C is the disk/washer method about the x-axis. Option D misses the radius factor \( x \), which is essential in the shell method.

📖 Explanation:

For the shell method, the radius of each shell is the horizontal distance from the y-axis to the shell, which is \( x \). To ensure the radius is nonnegative, the region must lie in the domain \( x \ge 0 \), i.e., entirely to the right of the y-axis. The method does not require the function to be one-to-one (B) or for the region to be bounded by two curves (C). While the axis is the y-axis in this specific case, the method can be adapted to other axes, so D is not a necessary condition for the method itself, but for this specific Medium.

📖 Explanation:

The shell method requires the radius \( x \) and the height \( f(x) = 1/x \). The integrand is \( 2\pi x \cdot \frac{1}{x} = 2\pi \). So the integral is \( 2\pi \int_{1}^{3} dx \). Option A misses the radius \( x \), which would incorrectly give the area of a rectangle. Option C is the disk method about the x-axis. Option D incorrectly simplifies the height. This demonstrates that the shell method can sometimes simplify dramatically, but it's important to correctly identify the radius and height.

Q14. If a region is revolved about the y-axis, and the shell method is used, what does the differential \( dx \) represent in the integral?

C. The radius of the cylindrical shell

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

In the cylindrical shell method, the variable of integration corresponds to the axis perpendicular to the shells. When revolving about the y-axis, the shells are formed by vertical strips of thickness \( dx \). Thus, \( dx \) represents the thickness of each shell. The height is given by \( f(x) \), the radius by \( x \), and the circumference by \( 2\pi x \). This is a fundamental concept for setting up shell integrals.

Q17. What is the volume of the solid generated by revolving the region bounded by \( y = x^2 \) and \( y = 2x \) about the y-axis?

A. \( \frac{8\pi}{3} \) ✅
B. \( \frac{4\pi}{3} \)
C. \( \frac{16\pi}{3} \)
D. \( \frac{32\pi}{3} \)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The curves intersect at \( x=0 \) and \( x=2 \). For \( 0 \le x \le 2 \), the upper curve is \( y=2x \) and the lower is \( y=x^2 \). Using the shell method, the height is \( 2x - x^2 \), and the radius is \( x \). The volume is \( 2\pi \int_{0}^{2} x(2x - x^2) \, dx = 2\pi \int_{0}^{2} (2x^2 - x^3) \, dx = 2\pi \left[ \frac{2x^3}{3} - \frac{x^4}{4} \right]_{0}^{2} = 2\pi \left( \frac{16}{3} - 4 \right) = 2\pi \cdot \frac{4}{3} = \frac{8\pi}{3} \). Option B is half of this, option C is double, and option D is four times. This requires careful integration and correct identification of the region.

Q18. A region is bounded by \( y = \sin x \) and the x-axis from \( x=0 \) to \( x=\pi \). If this region is revolved about the y-axis, which method would require splitting the integral into two parts?

A. Neither; shells works with a single integral
B. Washers about the y-axis ✅
C. Shells about the y-axis
D. Disks about the x-axis

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation:

When revolving about the y-axis, the washer method would require solving \( y = \sin x \) for \( x \), which gives \( x = \arcsin y \) for \( 0 \le y \le 1 \). However, since \( \sin x \) is not one-to-one on \( [0,\pi] \), the washer method would need to split the region into two parts: one where \( x = \arcsin y \) and another where \( x = \pi - \arcsin y \). The shell method integrates \( 2\pi x \sin x \, dx \) from 0 to \( \pi \), which is a single integral. Thus, washers would require splitting, while shells would not. This highlights the advantage of shells in this case.

Q20. A solid is formed by revolving the region in the first quadrant enclosed by \( y = x^3 \), \( y = 8 \), and \( x = 0 \) about the y-axis. Which of the following integrals gives the volume using cylindrical shells?

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation:

The region is bounded by \( y = x^3 \), \( y = 8 \), and \( x = 0 \). When using shells about the y-axis, it's easier to integrate with respect to \( x \). The radius is \( x \), and the height is the vertical distance from the lower boundary \( y = x^3 \) to the upper boundary \( y = 8 \), so the height is \( 8 - x^3 \). The bounds for \( x \) are from 0 to 2 (since \( 2^3 = 8 \)). Thus, the integral is \( 2\pi \int_{0}^{2} x(8 - x^3) \, dx \). Option A and B are incorrect because they use \( y \) as the radius and \( y^{1/3} \) as height, which would be the washer method about the y-axis. Option D has the wrong height.

Q24. Which of the following scenarios would make the shell method more difficult to apply than the washer method?
