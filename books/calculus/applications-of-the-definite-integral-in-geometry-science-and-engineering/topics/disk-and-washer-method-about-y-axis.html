What is Disk and washer method about y-axis?

When applying the disk or washer method about the y-axis, integration is performed with respect to \( y \) using horizontal slices, where the volume is \( V = \pi \int_c^d ([R(y)]^2 - [r(y)]^2) \, dy \) with radii measured horizontally from the y-axis to the boundary curves.

Q1. Which integral represents the volume of the solid formed by revolving the region bounded by x = y^2, y = 0, and y = 2 about the y-axis?

A. \( \int_{0}^{2} \pi y^4 \, dy \) ✅
B. \( \int_{0}^{2} 2\pi y^3 \, dy \)
C. \( \int_{0}^{2} \pi y^2 \, dy \)
D. \( \int_{0}^{2} 2\pi y \, dy \)

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The disk method about the y-axis uses the radius as a function of y, \( r(y) \). For \( x = y^2 \), the radius is \( y^2 \), so the area of each disk is \( \pi (y^2)^2 = \pi y^4 \). Integrating this from \( y = 0 \) to \( y = 2 \) gives the volume. This tests the ability to correctly identify the radius and set up the integral using the disk method.

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The integral represents the washer method: \( \pi[(R(y))^2 - (r(y))^2] \). Here, \( R(y) = \sqrt{y} \) is the outer radius and \( r(y) = y^2 \) is the inner radius. This indicates the region is bounded on the right by \( x = \sqrt{y} \) and on the left by \( x = y^2 \), revolved about the y-axis. This tests if the student can correctly interpret the radii in terms of y.

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

The region is bounded by \( x = y^2 + 1 \), \( x = 0 \), \( y = 0 \), and \( y = 2 \). Since \( x = y^2 + 1 > 0 \) for all y in [0,2], the radius is \( r(y) = y^2 + 1 \). The volume using disks is \( V = \int_{0}^{2} \pi (y^2 + 1)^2 dy = \pi \int_{0}^{2} (y^4 + 2y^2 + 1) dy = \pi [\frac{y^5}{5} + \frac{2y^3}{3} + y]_0^2 = \pi (\frac{32}{5} + \frac{16}{3} + 2) = \frac{124\pi}{5} \).

Q18. A washer has an inner radius of \( r(y) = y \) and an outer radius of \( R(y) = y^2 \). For what interval of y does this represent a valid washer (R >= r)?

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation:

For a washer, the outer radius must be greater than or equal to the inner radius: R(y) >= r(y). Here, y^2 >= y. This inequality holds when y >= 1 or y <= 0. Since we are typically dealing with positive y in these problems, the interval is y >= 1. However, if y is between 0 and 1, y^2 < y, so the radii would be reversed, which is not valid for a washer unless the outer and inner radii are swapped. If the region is in the first quadrant, y must be between 1 and something. So the valid interval for R=y^2 and r=y is y>=1.
