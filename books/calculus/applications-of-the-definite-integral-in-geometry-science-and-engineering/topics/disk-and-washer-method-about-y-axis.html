What is Disk and washer method about y-axis?

Definition:
When rotating around the y-axis, we integrate with respect to y. For the disk method, \( V = \pi \int_{c}^{d} [g(y)]^2 \, dy \). For washers, \( V = \pi \int_{c}^{d} ([R(y)]^2 - [r(y)]^2) \, dy \). Radii are horizontal distances from the y-axis to the curve boundaries.

Example:
Rotate \( x = \sqrt{y} \) from \( y=0 \) to \( y=4 \) about y-axis. Disk method: \( V = \pi \int_{0}^{4} (\sqrt{y})^2 \, dy = \pi \int_{0}^{4} y \, dy = \pi [\frac{y^2}{2}]_0^4 = 8\pi \).

Reason:
Using the y-axis as the axis of rotation requires expressing functions in terms of y, which is crucial for objects that are naturally described vertically, such as certain types of towers or silos in architectural modeling.

Q1. Which integral represents the volume of the solid formed by revolving the region bounded by x = y^2, y = 0, and y = 2 about the y-axis?

A.\( \int_{0}^{2} \pi y^4 \, dy \) ✅
B.\( \int_{0}^{2} 2\pi y^3 \, dy \)
C.\( \int_{0}^{2} \pi y^2 \, dy \)
D.\( \int_{0}^{2} 2\pi y \, dy \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The disk method about the y-axis uses the radius as a function of y, \( r(y) \). For \( x = y^2 \), the radius is \( y^2 \), so the area of each disk is \( \pi (y^2)^2 = \pi y^4 \). Integrating this from \( y = 0 \) to \( y = 2 \) gives the volume. This tests the ability to correctly identify the radius and set up the integral using the disk method.
