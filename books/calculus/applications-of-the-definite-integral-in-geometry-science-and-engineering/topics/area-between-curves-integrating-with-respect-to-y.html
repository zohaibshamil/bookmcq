What is Area between curves integrating with respect to y?

Definition:
Integrating with respect to y involves slicing the region horizontally. Here, the right function minus the left function gives the width, and the height is dydy. The formula is A=∫cd[xright−xleft] dyA=∫cd​[xright​−xleft​]dy. This is useful when functions are better expressed as x=g(y)x=g(y).

Example:
Area bounded by x=y2x=y2 and x=y+2x=y+2. Intersection at y=−1,2y=−1,2. Solution: A=∫−12[(y+2)−y2] dy=[y22+2y−y33]−12=92A=∫−12​[(y+2)−y2]dy=[2y2​+2y−3y3​]−12​=29​.

Reason:
Horizontal integration simplifies calculations when vertical slices would require splitting the integral into multiple parts due to changing boundary functions, thus reducing computational complexity significantly.
