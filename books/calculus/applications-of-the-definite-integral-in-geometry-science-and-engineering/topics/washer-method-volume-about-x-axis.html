What is Washer method volume about x-axis?

Definition:
The washer method is used when the region rotated around the x-axis has a hole, creating a hollow solid. It subtracts the inner disk from the outer disk. The formula is \( V = \pi \int_{a}^{b} ([R(x)]^2 - [r(x)]^2) \, dx \), where \( R \) is outer radius and \( r \) is inner radius.

Example:
Rotate region between \( y=x \) and \( y=x^2 \) about x-axis from 0 to 1. Outer \( R=x \), Inner \( r=x^2 \). Solution: \( V = \pi \int_{0}^{1} (x^2 - x^4) \, dx = \pi [\frac{x^3}{3} - \frac{x^5}{5}]_0^1 = \frac{2\pi}{15} \).

Reason:
This method extends the disk method to handle hollow shapes, such as pipes or rings, by accounting for the empty space in the center, ensuring accurate volume calculations for complex mechanical parts.
