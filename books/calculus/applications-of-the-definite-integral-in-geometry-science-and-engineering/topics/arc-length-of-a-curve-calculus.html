What is Arc length of a curve calculus?

Definition:
Arc length measures the distance along a curved path. For a function y=f(x)y=f(x)y=f(x) from aaa to bbb, the formula is L=∫ab1+[f′(x)]2 dxL = \int_{a}^{b} \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} \, dxL=∫ab​1+[f′(x)]2​dx. This derives from the Pythagorean theorem applied to infinitesimal segments of the curve.
