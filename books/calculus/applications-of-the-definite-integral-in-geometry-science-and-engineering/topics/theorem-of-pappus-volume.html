What is Theorem of Pappus volume?

Definition:
Pappus's Theorem states that the volume of a solid of revolution is the product of the area of the region being rotated and the distance traveled by its centroid. \( V = 2\pi R A \), where R is the distance from the axis to the centroid, and A is the area.

Example:
Rotate a circle of radius r centered at (R,0) about y-axis (Torus). Area \( A=\pi r^2 \). Centroid distance \( R \). Solution: \( V = 2\pi R (\pi r^2) = 2\pi^2 R r^2 \).

Reason:
This theorem simplifies volume calculations for complex shapes by avoiding direct integration, leveraging geometric properties of the centroid, which is particularly useful in mechanical engineering for designing gears and rings.

Q1. A circular region of radius \( r \) is revolved about a line in its plane at a distance \( 4r \) from its center to form a torus. What is the volume of the torus?

A.\( 4\pi^2 r^3 \)
B.\( 8\pi^2 r^3 \) ✅
C.\( 16\pi^2 r^3 \)
D.\( 2\pi^2 r^3 \)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The Theorem of Pappus states the volume is the product of the area of the region and the distance traveled by its centroid. The area is \( \pi r^2 \). The centroid (center) travels a distance \( 2\pi (4r) = 8\pi r \). Thus, \( V = (\pi r^2)(8\pi r) = 8\pi^2 r^3 \). Option B is correct. The other options result from arithmetic errors in calculating the circumference of the centroid's path or from incorrectly using the formula for the area of a circle.

Q2. A plane region with area \( A \) has its centroid at a distance \( d \) from the axis of revolution. If the volume of the resulting solid is \( V \), which equation correctly represents the Theorem of Pappus?

A.\( V = 2\pi d A \) ✅
B.\( V = \pi d^2 A \)
C.\( V = \frac{2}{3}\pi d A \)
D.\( V = 4\pi d A \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The Theorem of Pappus is mathematically stated as \( V = A \times (\text{distance traveled by centroid}) \). Since the centroid travels in a circular path of radius \( d \), the distance is \( 2\pi d \). Therefore, \( V = 2\pi d A \). Option A is the correct formula. Options B, C, and D represent common misconceptions, such as confusing the formula with that of a cylinder or incorrectly using the circumference formula.

Q4. A right triangle with legs 3 and 4 is revolved about its hypotenuse. What is the volume of the resulting solid?

A.\( 12\pi \)
B.\( 24\pi \)
C.\( 48\pi \)
D.\( \frac{48\pi}{5} \) ✅
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: D

📖 Explanation: This requires multi-step reasoning. First, find the area of the triangle \( A = \frac{1}{2}(3)(4) = 6 \). The distance from the right angle to the hypotenuse is \( h = \frac{3\times4}{5} = \frac{12}{5} \). The centroid of a triangle is located one-third of the distance from the base (hypotenuse) to the opposite vertex. The distance from the centroid to the hypotenuse is \( d = \frac{1}{3}h = \frac{4}{5} \). The path length is \( 2\pi d = \frac{8\pi}{5} \). Volume \( V = A \times (2\pi d) = 6 \times \frac{8\pi}{5} = \frac{48\pi}{5} \). Option D is correct.

Q8. Which of the following is a key limitation of the Theorem of Pappus?

Q9. A triangular lamina with vertices (0,0), (2,0), and (0,4) is revolved about the y-axis. What is the volume of the solid generated?

A.\( \frac{16\pi}{3} \) ✅
B.\( 8\pi \)
C.\( \frac{32\pi}{3} \)
D.\( 4\pi \)
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The area of the triangle is \( A = \frac{1}{2}(2)(4) = 4 \). The centroid of a triangle is the average of its vertices: \( \bar{x} = \frac{0+2+0}{3} = \frac{2}{3} \). The distance from the centroid to the y-axis (the axis of revolution) is \( d = \frac{2}{3} \). The distance traveled by the centroid is \( 2\pi d = \frac{4\pi}{3} \). The volume \( V = A \times (2\pi d) = 4 \times \frac{4\pi}{3} = \frac{16\pi}{3} \). Option A is correct. Option B is a common mistake of using \( d=1 \) or miscalculating the centroid.

Q11. A semicircular region of radius \( r \) is revolved about its diameter. What is the volume of the resulting sphere?

A.\( \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \) ✅
B.\( \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3 \)
C.\( \frac{1}{2}\pi r^3 \)
D.\( \pi r^3 \)
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The area of the semicircle is \( A = \frac{1}{2}\pi r^2 \). The centroid of a semicircle is located at a distance \( \frac{4r}{3\pi} \) from its diameter. When revolved about its diameter (the axis), the centroid travels a distance \( 2\pi \times \frac{4r}{3\pi} = \frac{8r}{3} \). The volume is \( V = A \times \text{dist} = \frac{1}{2}\pi r^2 \times \frac{8r}{3} = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \). This is the well-known formula for the volume of a sphere. Option A is correct. Option B is the area of the semicircle, and C and D are common geometric errors.

Q29. A lamina is formed by the region bounded by \( y = x^2 \) and \( y = 4 \). If this lamina is revolved about the y-axis, what is the volume of the resulting solid?
