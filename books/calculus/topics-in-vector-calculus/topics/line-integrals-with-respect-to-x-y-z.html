What is Line integrals with respect to x y z?

Line integrals with respect to x, y, z:
Line integrals can be expressed as ∫CP dx+Q dy+R dz∫C​Pdx+Qdy+Rdz, evaluated by substituting parameterization and integrating each term separately.

Example:
∫Cx dx+y dy∫C​xdx+ydy along r(t)=⟨t,t⟩r(t)=⟨t,t⟩, 0≤t≤10≤t≤1, becomes ∫01(t⋅1+t⋅1)dt=1∫01​(t⋅1+t⋅1)dt=1.

Reason:
This differential form is useful in physics for work and in complex analysis, linking to exact differentials and path independence.
