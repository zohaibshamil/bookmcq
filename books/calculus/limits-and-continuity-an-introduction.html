Q1. Given \(f(x)=x^{2}\) and the point \(P(3,9)\) on its graph, which statement correctly relates the slope of the tangent line at \(P\) to the derivative \(f'(3)\)?

🔹 A. The slope equals \(f'(3)\).
🔹 B. The slope is twice \(f'(3)\).
🔹 C. The slope is half of \(f'(3)\).
🔹 D. The slope is unrelated to \(f'(3)\).

💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

Q2. If a function \(g\) satisfies \(g'(x)=3x^{2}-4\) and its tangent line at \(x=2\) passes through \((2,g(2))\), which expression gives the y‑intercept of that tangent line?

🔹 A. \(g(2)-8\)
🔹 B. \(g(2)-12\)
🔹 C. \(g(2)-16\)
🔹 D. \(g(2)-20\)

💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: D

Q3. Consider \(f(x)=\sin x\). Which \(x_{0}\) in \((0,\pi)\) makes the tangent line at \((x_{0},\sin x_{0})\) pass through \((\pi,0)\)?

🔹 A. \(x_{0}\approx 0.86\)
🔹 B. \(x_{0}\approx 1.57\)
🔹 C. \(x_{0}\approx 2.00\)
🔹 D. \(x_{0}\approx 2.68\)

💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B
