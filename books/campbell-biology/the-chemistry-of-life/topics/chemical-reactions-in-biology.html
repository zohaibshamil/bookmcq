What is Chemical reactions in biology?

Definition:
Chemical reactions in biology are the chemical processes that occur within living organisms to maintain life, including metabolism, digestion, respiration, photosynthesis, and synthesis of biomolecules. These reactions are often catalyzed by enzymes and are essential for growth, reproduction, and homeostasis.

Working:
Reactants called substrates bind to enzyme active sites, where bonds are broken and new bonds form to produce products. Enzymes lower activation energy, speeding up reactions without being consumed. Energy may be released (exergonic) or consumed (endergonic).

Example:
Cellular respiration: C6H12O6+6O2→6CO2+6H2O+EnergyC_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 \rightarrow 6CO_2 + 6H_2O + \text{Energy}C6​H12​O6​+6O2​→6CO2​+6H2​O+Energy. Solution: glucose is broken down to release energy in the form of ATP for cellular work.

Reason:
Understanding biological chemical reactions is important because they explain how organisms obtain energy, build structures, and respond to their environment.

📝 All Chemical reactions in biology MCQs

Q1. A cell combines two substances to form a new molecule with properties different from either starting substance. Which conclusion best explains what happened?

A.The substances were physically mixed without changing their identities
B.Atoms were rearranged as existing bonds were broken and new bonds formed ✅
C.The atoms disappeared and were replaced by newly created atoms
D.Only the temperature changed, while the molecular structures remained identical
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A chemical reaction changes molecular identity because atoms are rearranged. Some bonds in the reactants must be broken while new bonds form, producing molecules with different structures and properties. The atoms themselves are conserved.

Q2. A student claims that if a reaction produces a gas, the atoms of that gas must have been created during the reaction. Which reasoning best corrects the claim?

A.Gas formation proves that atoms can be generated when enough energy is supplied
B.The gas must already have existed as a complete molecule before the reaction
C.The gas contains atoms that were rearranged from the original reactants ✅
D.Only oxygen and hydrogen atoms can participate in chemical reactions
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: Chemical reactions do not create or destroy ordinary atoms. If a gaseous product forms, atoms originally present in the reactants have been reorganized into different molecules. The new arrangement can produce properties unlike those of the starting substances.

Q3. A researcher compares a reaction before and after increasing reactant concentration. The reaction occurs more rapidly after the increase. Which interpretation is most scientifically justified?

A.The increase necessarily changed which atoms were present
B.Higher concentration can increase productive encounters between reacting particles ✅
C.Higher concentration guarantees that every collision forms a product
D.The reaction must have changed from chemical to physical
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Increasing concentration places more reactant particles in the same volume, making productive encounters more frequent under suitable conditions. It does not change the identity of the atoms or guarantee that every collision produces products.

Q4. A reaction starts with molecules containing three carbon atoms each and ends with molecules containing two carbon atoms each. A student concludes that one carbon atom was destroyed from every molecule. What is the strongest objection?

A.Carbon atoms cannot participate in reactions
B.The missing carbon atoms must have changed into energy
C.Products and other reactants must be examined because atoms are rearranged rather than destroyed ✅
D.Carbon atoms can only form bonds with oxygen
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: C

📖 Explanation: The conclusion ignores conservation of matter. A carbon atom apparently absent from one product may occur in another product or combine with another reactant. Determining atom conservation requires accounting for all substances involved.

Q5. A reaction is monitored by measuring product concentration over time. The measured values are 0, 4, 7, 9, and 10 units at equal time intervals. What does the pattern most strongly suggest?

A.Product formation is continuing but becoming slower ✅
B.Product formation is accelerating at a constant rate
C.No chemical reaction is occurring after the first measurement
D.The product is being converted completely back into reactants
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: The product concentration rises throughout the measurements, showing that product formation continues. However, the increases become smaller, indicating a decreasing rate of net product accumulation as the reaction progresses.

Q6. Two reactions use the same reactants. Reaction X requires breaking strong bonds before weaker new bonds form, while Reaction Y involves less bond rearrangement. Which prediction is most reasonable about energy requirements?

A.X may require more energy to initiate because stronger bonds are harder to break ✅
B.Y must always release more energy regardless of its products
C.X cannot occur because strong bonds never break
D.Bond strength has no relationship to chemical energy
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Breaking chemical bonds requires energy, and stronger bonds generally require more energy input. Therefore, a reaction requiring substantial disruption of strong bonds may have a larger activation requirement, although overall energy change also depends on bonds formed.

Q7. A student says, 'Because a reaction releases energy, every bond involved in the reaction must have released energy when it broke.' Which evaluation is correct?

A.Correct, because breaking bonds always releases energy
B.Incorrect, because breaking bonds requires energy while forming bonds can release energy ✅
C.Correct, because atoms release energy whenever they separate
D.Incorrect, because chemical reactions never involve energy changes
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The student's reasoning reverses the energy roles of bond breaking and bond formation. Breaking bonds requires energy input, whereas forming bonds can release energy. The overall energy change depends on the balance between these opposing processes.

Q8. A sealed container contains reactants that gradually form products. A graph of reactant amount versus time decreases steeply at first and then approaches a nearly constant value. Which explanation best fits the graph?

A.Reactants are being created faster than they are consumed
B.Reactant consumption is rapid initially and then slows as conditions change during the reaction ✅
C.Atoms are gradually disappearing from the container
D.The reaction rate must remain identical throughout the experiment
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The steep initial decline indicates rapid reactant consumption. As the curve becomes flatter, the rate of reactant loss decreases. This can occur as reactant availability changes and the reaction approaches a state where net changes become smaller.

Q9. A food-processing reaction produces a desired molecule but also an unwanted by-product. Engineers modify the starting materials so that atoms are rearranged into more of the desired product. Why is this a chemical strategy rather than simple mixing?

A.The modification changes which molecular structures are produced ✅
B.Mixing always creates new atoms
C.Physical mixing automatically changes bond energies permanently
D.The unwanted product proves that no reaction occurred
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Changing the starting materials to favor formation of a different molecular product changes the chemical structures produced. This involves rearrangement of atoms and chemical bonds, unlike simple mixing, where substances retain their molecular identities.

Q10. A reaction can be represented by reactants becoming products. If the products contain exactly the same numbers of each type of atom as the reactants but have different molecular arrangements, what does this demonstrate?

A.Chemical reactions conserve atoms while changing how those atoms are bonded ✅
B.Chemical reactions conserve molecules without changing their structures
C.Only the physical state of matter has changed
D.Atoms can change into different elements during ordinary reactions
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Matching atom counts before and after a reaction demonstrates conservation of atoms. The molecules can nevertheless be completely different because atoms have been redistributed and connected through new bonding arrangements.
