What is How do chemical reactions work?

Definition:
Chemical reactions work by breaking existing chemical bonds in reactant molecules, rearranging the atoms, and forming new chemical bonds to produce product molecules with different properties. The total number of atoms is conserved, and energy is either released or absorbed during the process.

Working:
Reactant molecules collide with sufficient energy and correct orientation to overcome the activation energy \( E_a \). Bonds break, atoms separate, and new bonds form. The energy change is \( \Delta H = H_{\text{products}} - H_{\text{reactants}} \). Exergonic reactions release energy, while endergonic reactions absorb energy.

Example:
In the reaction \( 2H_2 + O_2 \rightarrow 2H_2O \), bonds in \( H_2 \) and \( O_2 \) break, and new bonds form in \( H_2O \). Solution: the products have different properties than the reactants, and energy is released.

Reason:
Understanding how chemical reactions work is important because it is the basis of all chemical processes, including metabolism, digestion, and energy production in living organisms.

📝 All How do chemical reactions work MCQs

Q1. A molecule of hydrogen reacts with oxygen to form water. Which description best explains what happens to the chemical bonds during this reaction?

A.Existing bonds disappear without forming any new bonds
B.Bonds in the reactants are rearranged, breaking some bonds and forming new bonds in water ✅
C.Only the oxygen bonds change because hydrogen atoms remain unchanged
D.The atoms change into different elements before forming water
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Chemical reactions rearrange atoms rather than transform one element into another. Bonds holding hydrogen and oxygen molecules together must be disrupted, while new bonds form between hydrogen and oxygen atoms. The resulting water molecules therefore have a different bonding arrangement.

Q2. A student claims that forming a chemical bond always requires energy, while breaking a chemical bond always releases energy. Which evaluation is most accurate?

A.Both statements are correct because bonds store no chemical energy
B.The first statement is generally correct, but the second reverses the usual energy relationship ✅
C.The first statement is incorrect, but the second is always correct
D.Both statements are incorrect because chemical bonds never involve energy changes
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Breaking a chemical bond generally requires an input of energy because attractive interactions must be overcome. Forming a bond generally releases energy as atoms move into a more stable arrangement. Overall reactions can still release or absorb energy depending on the balance.

Q3. In a laboratory model, two molecules collide but their atoms simply bounce apart without rearranging. A researcher increases temperature and observes more successful product formation. Which explanation best accounts for the change?

A.Higher temperature changes the atoms into different elements
B.Higher temperature increases the frequency and energy of collisions, making productive rearrangements more likely ✅
C.Higher temperature prevents reactant bonds from vibrating
D.Higher temperature guarantees that every collision forms products
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Increasing temperature raises the average kinetic energy of particles and can increase the number of collisions with enough energy and suitable orientation for bonds to break and form. It does not guarantee successful reactions, because molecular arrangement and collision conditions still matter.

Q4. Consider the reaction model AB+CD→AC+BDAB + CD \rightarrow AC + BD. A student says no chemical reaction occurred because the same four atoms appear before and after the reaction. What is the best response?

A.The student is correct because chemical reactions require atoms to disappear
B.The student is incorrect because the atoms have been rearranged into new bonding relationships ✅
C.The student is correct because only changes in temperature count as reactions
D.The student is incorrect only if the total number of atoms increases
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A chemical reaction can occur without changing the number or identities of atoms. In this model, the atoms are conserved, but their bonding partners change. Breaking the A−BA-B and C−DC-D bonds and forming A−CA-C and B−DB-D creates new substances.

Q5. A reaction has an energy profile in which the reactants begin at 40 energy units, the highest point is 95 units, and the products finish at 15 units. Which conclusion is best supported by the graph?

A.The reaction absorbs 25 energy units overall
B.The reaction releases 25 energy units overall, while the activation barrier is 55 energy units ✅
C.The reaction releases 80 energy units and has no activation barrier
D.The reaction absorbs 55 energy units overall
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The overall energy change compares products with reactants: 15−40=−2515-40=-25 energy units, indicating net energy release. The activation barrier compares the peak with the reactants: 95−40=5595-40=55 energy units, showing that an energy input is still required to initiate the reaction.

Q6. Two reactions are compared. Reaction X requires 30 energy units to reach its highest point and releases 10 energy units overall. Reaction Y requires 70 energy units to reach its highest point and releases 25 energy units overall. Which statement is most reasonable?

A.Reaction Y necessarily occurs faster because it releases more energy
B.Reaction X has the smaller activation barrier, so it may be easier to initiate even though it releases less energy overall ✅
C.Reaction X cannot occur because it releases less energy
D.Reaction Y must require less energy because its products are more stable
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Activation energy and overall energy change describe different aspects of a reaction. Reaction X has a smaller activation barrier, so fewer energetic collisions may be needed to initiate it. Reaction Y releases more energy overall, but that does not by itself determine reaction speed.

Q7. A cell uses a reaction in which one molecule donates a group to another molecule. During the process, one bond in the donor is broken and a new bond forms with the receiving molecule. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

A.The reaction destroys the atoms involved
B.The reaction is a rearrangement of atoms involving both bond breaking and bond formation ✅
C.The reaction must involve nuclear changes
D.The reaction cannot occur because breaking a bond prevents new bonds from forming
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: Chemical reactions commonly involve coordinated changes in bonding. A bond in one molecule can be broken while another bond forms, transferring atoms or groups without destroying the atoms themselves. The products differ because their atoms have new bonding relationships.

Q8. A student draws the products of a reaction but gives one atom five bonds even though that atom is normally stable with fewer bonds in the given model. Which checking strategy would best identify the error?

A.Ignore bonding because only the number of molecules matters
B.Count the atoms before and after, then check whether the proposed bonding arrangement is chemically plausible ✅
C.Change every coefficient until the drawing looks symmetrical
D.Assume the products must contain fewer atoms than the reactants
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: A reliable reaction model should conserve the identities and numbers of atoms while also representing chemically reasonable bonding. Checking atom counts can reveal conservation errors, while examining bonding patterns can expose an implausible product structure that arithmetic alone may miss.

Q9. A graph shows that as reaction progress increases, the energy first rises sharply from 20 to 85 units and then falls to 5 units. Which sequence correctly describes the major energy events?

A.Energy is released first and then absorbed permanently
B.Energy is required to reach the high-energy transition region, followed by net energy release as products form ✅
C.No energy change occurs because the graph ends below the starting point
D.Energy is absorbed overall because the highest point is greater than the starting point
💡 Difficulty: hard | ✅ Correct: B

📖 Explanation: The rising portion represents the energy needed to reach the high-energy transition region where bonds can be rearranged. After that point, the system moves toward lower-energy products. Because the products are at 5 units compared with 20 initially, the overall process releases energy.

Q10. A researcher compares two possible models for a reaction. Model 1 preserves every atom but changes which atoms are bonded. Model 2 produces an extra oxygen atom that was absent from the reactants. Which model is scientifically stronger?

A.Model 1, because chemical reactions rearrange existing atoms rather than create new atoms of an element ✅
B.Model 2, because successful reactions always create additional atoms
C.Model 2, because more atoms make products more stable
D.Both models are equally valid because atom conservation is optional
💡 Difficulty: easy | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Chemical reactions rearrange existing atoms into new combinations. Model 1 respects conservation of matter by keeping every atom accounted for while changing bonding relationships. Model 2 introduces an oxygen atom without a source, so it fails to represent a valid chemical transformation.

Q11. A reaction between two substances produces a compound with lower chemical potential energy than either starting arrangement. A student concludes that no energy was involved because the products contain the same atoms as the reactants. What is the strongest correction?

A.Energy changes can accompany bond rearrangements even when the atoms themselves remain unchanged ✅
B.Energy can change only when atoms are converted into other elements
C.The student is correct because conserved atoms imply conserved energy in every reaction
D.Energy changes only when the number of molecules increases
💡 Difficulty: medium | ✅ Correct: A

📖 Explanation: Conservation of atoms does not mean that chemical energy remains unchanged. Rearranging atoms changes their bonding relationships and therefore their chemical potential energy. A lower-energy product arrangement can result in net energy release even though every atom present initially is still present afterward.
