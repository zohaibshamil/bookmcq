Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BookMCQ — how it works, how our content is created, and how to get the most out of your practice.

🎯 General Questions

Is BookMCQ free to use?

Yes — BookMCQ is completely free. There is no subscription, no paywall, and no hidden fees. All MCQs, explanations, and practice features are available to every visitor without login.

Who creates the MCQs on BookMCQ?

All content is curated by Zohaib Khosa, the founder of BookMCQ. He holds a degree in Information Technology from the University of Education and has over 9 years of experience in academic content development.

You can read his full background on the About the Author page.

Do I need to create an account to practice MCQs?

No account is required to start practicing. You can browse books, chapters, and topics, and answer MCQs immediately. Login is only needed if you want to save progress or access personalized tracking features.

Which books and subjects are available on BookMCQ?

BookMCQ currently covers books across these subjects:

  • Biology (e.g., Campbell Biology, Principles of Biochemistry)
  • Computer Science (e.g., Operating Systems, Data Communication & Networks, Microprocessor 8085)
  • Mathematics (e.g., Calculus)
  • Digital SAT (Algebra and more)
  • History (e.g., World History: Modern)

We are continuously adding more books and subjects based on student requests.

Is BookMCQ suitable for competitive exams?

Yes. Our MCQs are designed for students preparing for a wide range of exams, including CSS, PMS, UPSC, FPSC, PPSC, SPSC, BPSC, IBPS, SSC, Banking, Digital SAT, GRE, GMAT, JEE, and NEET. The chapter-wise structure and difficulty tagging (Easy / Medium / Hard) make it ideal for structured exam preparation.

📚 Content & Accuracy

How are the MCQs verified for accuracy?

Every MCQ passes through a structured 5-step process:

  • Source selection — based on standard textbooks and official exam guides
  • Question creation — each MCQ targets a specific concept
  • Accuracy review — cross-checked against the source textbook
  • Explanation writing — every MCQ gets a step-by-step explanation
  • Ongoing updates — content is revisited regularly to fix errors

See the Why Trust Us page for the full methodology.

What books do you use as your sources?

Our questions are grounded in respected academic resources, including:

  • Campbell Biology
  • Calculus by James Stewart
  • Operating Systems by Silberschatz
  • Kaplan and Princeton Review SAT guides
  • Official College Board SAT material
  • Manhattan Prep guides
  • NCERT textbooks
  • Standard university textbooks and official exam syllabi
I found an error in an MCQ. How do I report it?

Please use the Contact page to report any error. Include the URL of the page and a short description of the issue. Every report is personally reviewed, and confirmed errors are corrected.

Are the MCQs generated by AI?

No. Every MCQ is human-reviewed before publishing. Questions are based on real textbooks and exam guides, and each answer is cross-checked against the source material. We do not publish unverified or auto-generated questions.

🛠️ Usage & Features

How do I start practicing MCQs?

It takes just a few clicks:

  • Click 📝 Quiz or 📚 Practice in the top navigation.
  • Select a book from the list.
  • Pick a chapter and then a topic.
  • Start answering MCQs. Each question includes a detailed explanation after submission.
What do Easy, Medium, and Hard mean?

Each MCQ is tagged by difficulty:

  • Easy — tests recall of a core definition or basic fact.
  • Medium — requires applying a concept to a slightly new situation.
  • Hard — involves deeper reasoning, calculation, or multi-step logic.

This lets you progress gradually and identify your weak areas.

Can I request a book to be added to BookMCQ?

Yes! Go to the Contact page, select "Book Request" from the message type, and include the book title and author. We review every request and add books based on student demand.

Does BookMCQ work on mobile phones?

Yes. BookMCQ is fully mobile-responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The layout adapts automatically for smaller screens so you can practice MCQs anywhere.
